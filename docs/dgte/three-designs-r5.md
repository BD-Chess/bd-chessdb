## Claude contribution

The new section presents Claude line’s Open Review and Design R1 (19 October 2026), independently of the earlier proposals. DGTE-MV combines a two-leg moving-body loop, compliant vapor bags, matched absolute top pressure and an external rope-driven generator. The page retains his critical assessment of BD/GPT and his preference for MV as heat-engine research, while keeping P/L first for intermittent events.

Two new engineering schematics, each in English and Slovenian, are programmatically drawn SVGs. They are an editorial visualization of Claude’s proposal, not a fabrication design. Bottom hydraulic separation, bath-density differences, mixing and pressure-maintenance work remain explicit open interfaces.

Model basis: CoolProp 8.0.0 / HEOS, ideal isothermal hot ascent, liquid return, 1 L hot vapor at the bottom. Water 90/25 °C, 3 m, bath density 850 kg/m³: 56.63 J / 506.59 J = 11.18%, before cycling envelope and machine losses. Ethanol and methanol cases have separate conditions. Envelope estimates and 3–9 W column scenarios are labeled as assumptions. GPT reproduced the supplied model and independently checked principal integrals in the preceding review; this publication does not claim physical validation.

Editorial qualifications remain outside Claude’s assessment: the RT82/RT64HC expansion objection mixed material cases; the NTU correction already existed in the previous GPT text; a pressure-controlled bag test exchanges work with its pressure source and is not standalone proof of net engine output.
