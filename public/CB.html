⚡ ULTIMATE POWER RANKINGS ⚡

Lore-Accurate Re-Ranking (Original Text Restored)
SSS
SUPREME
#1The One-Above-AllMarvel
Supreme Being

Marvel’s ultimate “author-level” creator figure. In most depictions it stands outside the entire Marvel cosmology: multiverse, abstracts, time, and even the rules that bind beings like Eternity or the Living Tribunal. It rarely acts directly; when it does, it’s usually through quiet interventions, avatars, or moments of moral/creative revelation.

#2The PresenceDC
Supreme Being
DC’s supreme creator principle—often treated as the top of the hierarchy that includes the Source, the Spectre-force, and the major metaphysical powers of the DC cosmology. Depending on era and imprint (DC/Vertigo), “The Presence” ranges from a personal God-like being to a more abstract, omnipresent law of existence.
#3Elaine BellocDC/Vertigo
Human-Divine Hybrid
A key figure from Vertigo’s Lucifer: a human girl with angelic lineage who grows into something far larger—eventually inheriting divine authority in that continuity. Elaine’s power is less about flashy feats and more about the terrifying burden of authorship: shaping creation while staying compassionate and sane.
#4Mother of ExistenceImage
Creator / Supreme Being

Also known in Spawn lore as “Man of Miracles,” the Mother of Existence is portrayed as the creator‑figure behind the Spawn cosmology: the source-level being from which that universe’s fundamental order proceeds. In this mythos the Mother is commonly described as the parent of both the ‘God’ and ‘Satan’ figures of the setting.

#5The Great Evil BeastDC
Primordial Darkness

In DC’s metaphysical horror tradition, the Great Evil Beast is the sentient ‘soul of the Darkness’—a primordial expression of evil that stands as the shadow‑counterpart to divine light. It’s often framed as what becomes distinct when creation separates light from darkness.

SS
OUTER GODS
#6Lucifer MorningstarDC
Fallen Angel
Vertigo/DC Lucifer is a top-tier metaphysical agent: brilliant, defiant, and operating on the level of creation’s architecture rather than city-scale heroics. He rarely “wins” by raw power alone; he wins by seeing the hidden structure of choices. His signature is freedom—sometimes noble, sometimes terrifying.
#7Michael DemiurgosDC
Archangel
From DC/Vertigo’s Lucifer: the archangel most closely linked to raw creative power—the ability to generate new realities. Michael is not merely strong; he is foundational, a key pillar in the architecture of creation. His stories often revolve around duty, loyalty, and the moral cost of being an engine for worlds.
#8The Living TribunalMarvel
Multiversal Abstract Judge
Marvel’s multiversal judge: an abstract cosmic entity tasked with maintaining balance and adjudicating threats that endanger the structure of the multiverse itself. The Tribunal is typically above most pantheons and abstracts, acting with a cold, legalistic logic.
#9Beyonder (Pre-Retcon)Marvel
Beyond-Reality Entity
The pre-retcon Beyonder is Marvel’s famous “beyond everything” powerhouse from the original Secret Wars era—portrayed as vastly greater than the known multiverse and its abstract entities. He is curiosity plus infinite leverage: a childlike intelligence with cosmic-scale consequences.
#10The Spectre (Fully Unleashed)DC
Divine Spirit
The Spectre is the embodiment of divine wrath in DC: a near-absolute instrument of judgment that typically requires a human host to act in the world. “Fully unleashed” versions push toward the top of the DC power ladder—reality-warping, soul-level punishment, cosmic-scale enforcement.
#11Thanos (Infinity Stones)Marvel
Titan (with Infinity Gauntlet)
Thanos with the Infinity Stones is the classic Marvel scenario where strategy meets absolute leverage: reality, time, mind, soul, space, and power in one hand. The point isn’t that Thanos can blow things up—it's that he can define what counts as true. His greatest danger is ideological certainty: a single will deciding the value of all lives.
#12Cosmic Armor SupermanDC
Conceptual Superman
The “Thought Robot” (Cosmic Armor Superman) is DC’s conceptual Superman: a meta-defense mechanism built to counter threats to the idea of Superman itself. It’s as much philosophy as character—an embodiment of narrative resilience and adaptive opposition. Because it operates on story-logic, it can scale to match the threat.
#13Dr. ManhattanDC
Transcended Human
A quantum-consciousness superbeing from Watchmen who experiences time nonlinearly and manipulates matter/energy at a fundamental level. In DC crossovers (Doomsday Clock), he becomes a probe that tests whether “meaning” is robust or just a story we tell ourselves.
#14Molecule ManMarvel
Human Mutate
Marvel’s ultimate “small lever, huge effect” reality manipulator: a seemingly ordinary man whose control over molecules becomes multiversal-scale in key storylines. In Hickman’s Secret Wars era, he becomes a structural pillar of the multiverse’s fate.
#15NemesisMarvel
Cosmic Entity

Marvel’s Nemesis is presented as a pre‑multiversal ‘first self’—a solitary cosmic entity from the earliest reality. A recurring origin thread is that Nemesis shattered itself (often attributed to loneliness) into the Infinity Gems/Stones, so that existence could diversify rather than remain a single closed identity.

S
COSMIC
#16EternityMarvel
Embodiment of Time
Marvel’s personification of the universe (or multiverse, depending on scope): the living totality of existence, often paired conceptually with Infinity. Eternity is not a “fighter” in the usual sense; it expresses cosmic order, continuity, and the right of being to persist.
#17InfinityMarvel
Embodiment of Space
Marvel’s abstract embodiment of space and the endless extent of reality—often paired with Eternity (time/existence) and contrasted with Oblivion (non-existence). Infinity represents boundlessness: expansion, possibility, and the “room” in which stories unfold.
#18Death (The Endless)DC/Vertigo
Endless Conceptual Being
From DC/Vertigo’s Sandman mythos: Death is not a reaper-warrior but the personable, inevitable function that gives lives contour. She is gentle, absolute, and fundamentally un-bribeable: everything ends, and that ending is part of what makes love and choice real.
#19OblivionMarvel
Embodiment of Void
A Marvel abstract entity representing non-existence/void—the “outside” pressure of nothingness against reality. Oblivion is not a schemer; it is a principle that can express itself through avatars, realms, or inevitabilities.
#20The SourceDC
Cosmic Energy Field
In DC cosmology, the Source is often described as the foundational wellspring behind existence—linked to the Source Wall and the deep structure of the multiverse. It behaves like physics with intention.
#21Anti-MonitorDC
Anti-Matter Entity
DC’s classic anti-matter apocalypse engine from Crisis on Infinite Earths: a cosmic entity that feeds on worlds and seeks to reduce the multiverse to a controllable minimum. The Anti-Monitor threat is systemic: he weaponizes cosmology, turning the structure of reality into a battlefield.
#22GalactusMarvel
Cosmic Devourer
Marvel’s iconic cosmic devourer: a fundamental function of the universe’s lifecycle more than a simple villain. Galactus is frightening because he is both a person and a necessity in many cosmological models—hunger as law.
#23Franklin RichardsMarvel
Human Mutant
A Marvel mutant whose reality-warping potential has repeatedly been portrayed as universe-class. Franklin is compelling because the power is paired with childhood: imagination becomes creation, fear becomes catastrophe, and maturity becomes the central limiter.
#24Jean Grey (Phoenix Force)Marvel
Human Mutant
Jean as Phoenix is Marvel’s archetype of cosmic fire: life, death, rebirth, and the temptation of infinite intensity. Phoenix is often portrayed as both salvation and apocalypse—the same energy that can ignite new life can also burn worlds.
#25Mr. MxyzptlkDC
5th Dimensional Imp
A classic DC 5th-dimensional imp: whimsical, reality-bending, and often effectively unstoppable by conventional force. Mxyzptlk’s “limits” are usually rule-based (word games, self-imposed constraints, narrative bargains) rather than power-based.
#26Darkseid (True Form)DC
New God
DC’s archetype of tyranny elevated to metaphysics: Darkseid is not just a powerful alien god—he is the idea of domination, the gravitational pull of despair and obedience. “True Form” Darkseid is often framed as existing beyond the normal multiversal layers.
#27Mad Jim JaspersMarvel
Human Mutant
A Marvel reality-warping mutant whose power is most dangerous when unbounded by sanity, context, or containment. Jaspers is famous for turning “politics + madness + reality editing” into horror: laws rewritten, people remade, worlds twisted into grotesque art.
#28Rune King ThorMarvel
Asgardian God
A mythic apex version of Thor who has gained profound knowledge/power through runes and sacrifice, often portrayed as operating on a level where he can rewrite or transcend Asgardian fate. Rune King Thor is less about brute force and more about sovereignty over destiny.
#29Superman Prime (One Million)DC
Kryptonian
A far-future Superman who has spent immense time absorbing solar energy and mythic experience, often depicted as an evolved, near-mythic protector rather than a street-level hero. Superman Prime stories lean into the idea of hope scaled up.
A
SKYFATHER
#30OdinMarvel
Asgardian
All-Father of Asgard in Marvel: a god-king with immense mystical and cosmic power, but also deeply political and burdened by prophecy and legacy. Odin’s strength is not just raw might—it’s authority over realms and knowledge of ancient law.
#31ZeusMarvel
Olympian God
Marvel’s Zeus is a ruler among Olympian gods: immense divine might, cosmic-scale longevity, and political influence across pantheons. His stories often revolve around pride, authority, and the clash between ancient divine order and modern moral sensibilities.
#32TrigonDC
Interdimensional Demon
DC’s demon-lord archetype: a multiversal-scale predator of souls and worlds, often framed as the living consequence of hatred, conquest, and spiritual corruption. Trigon is dangerous because he weaponizes emotion and identity—turning love into leverage.
#33DormammuMarvel
Dark Dimension Ruler
Marvel’s Dark Dimension ruler: a being of immense mystical/cosmic power who thrives in a reality where physics is subordinate to sorcery and will. Dormammu is an archetypal conquering intellect—hungry for realms, souls, and leverage.
#34KnullMarvel
Symbiote Deity
Marvel’s god of symbiotes: an ancient darkness that forges living weapons and treats individuality as raw material. Knull’s theme is anti-connection: he creates bonds that are parasitic, not loving—control masquerading as unity.
#35Doctor Fate (Classic Nabu)DC
Lord of Order
DC’s archetypal Lord of Order: when Nabu is dominant, Fate becomes a near-impersonal instrument of cosmic law and magical precision. This makes Fate formidable against chaos, demons, and reality distortion—but also ethically scary.
#36Zatanna (Full Potential)DC
Homo Magi
DC’s stage magician turned true sorceress, Zatanna at full potential represents disciplined, reality-shaping magic guided by conscience. Unlike cosmic abstracts, her power is “human-shaped”: she uses language, ritual, and focus.
#37Scarlet Witch (House of M)Marvel
Human Mutant
A defining Marvel “reality rewrite” event: House of M Wanda reshapes the world through grief, desire, and trauma, demonstrating that emotional instability plus godlike power is existentially dangerous.
#38Raven (Full Demon Mode)DC
Half-Demon Hybrid
A half-demon empath tied to Trigon, Raven at full demon mode is a storm of soul-magic, telepathy/empathy, and reality-bending darkness. Her theme is control: enormous inner power paired with the risk of being consumed by it.
#39Doctor Strange (Sorcerer Supreme)Marvel
Human
Marvel’s premier mystic protector: a surgeon turned sorcerer whose real strength is knowledge, discipline, and the willingness to pay personal costs to prevent metaphysical disasters. Strange often wins by understanding rules and bargaining.
#40SurturMarvel
Fire Giant
Marvel’s fire demon/giant of Muspelheim and a core Ragnarok trigger: an apocalyptic force more than a nuanced person. Surtur embodies catastrophe driven by destiny—when he rises, realms burn.
B
PLANETARY
#41Silver SurferMarvel
Transformed Alien
Galactus’ herald-turned-free agent: a cosmic wanderer with the Power Cosmic, granting energy manipulation, extreme speed, and a philosophical temperament. Surfer’s stories are about conscience: a man who once served a necessary monster.
#42Black Adam (Magic Amped)DC
Magic Empowered
Black Adam with major magic amplification becomes a near-mythic force: divine lightning, durability, and the authority of ancient gods pushed to extreme levels. Adam’s defining trait is not just power, but unwavering conviction.
#43Captain AtomDC
Quantum Energy
DC’s quantum-powered hero who can tap into enormous energy, matter manipulation, and dimensional effects—often portrayed as a walking reactor with godlike potential but human fragility. Captain Atom’s danger is volatility.
#44Hulk (Worldbreaker)Marvel
Gamma Mutate
Worldbreaker Hulk is rage scaled to apocalypse: a version where emotional intensity translates into seismic, planet-threatening power. The fear isn’t just that he can smash; it’s that his existence becomes a disaster.
#45Flash (Wally West)DC
Speed Force Conduit
Wally West at peak is one of DC’s most extreme speedsters, often portrayed as tapping deeply into the Speed Force with “impossible” feats (time manipulation, dimensional slips, near-instant traversal).
#46Blue MarvelMarvel
Human
A Marvel hero whose power set (antimatter/energy manipulation) scales extremely high. Blue Marvel’s narrative theme is responsibility under prejudice and secrecy—he stepped back from heroism due to systemic racism, then returned when needed.
#47Captain Marvel (Binary)Marvel
Kree/Human Hybrid
Binary Carol Danvers is a high-energy cosmic form tied to stellar-scale output—flight, energy projection, and extreme durability. At that level she shifts from “fighter pilot hero” to “mobile star-engine.”
#48Wonder Woman (God Mode)DC
Demigoddess
A peak depiction of Diana where divine gifts, battle skill, and mythic authority align—sometimes with additional godly boons. Even then, her core remains: truth, compassion, and warrior discipline.
#49Nova PrimeMarvel
Human (Enhanced)
At peak, Nova as Nova Prime channels the full Nova Force and carries the responsibility of a galactic police legacy. He can hit extremely hard, fly at cosmic speeds, and operate in deep space with tactical precision.
C
HERO
#50Superman (Base)DC
Kryptonian
Base Superman is the iconic high-tier hero: solar-powered strength, speed, flight, senses, and a moral center built around hope and restraint. His greatest power is credibility: people want to believe in him.
#51Thor (Base)Marvel
Asgardian God
Base Thor is Asgardian godly power with a warrior’s heart: immense strength/durability, lightning and storm control, and mythic weaponry. He tends to meet threats head-on, yet he also respects honor and can learn humility.
#52Wonder Woman (Base)DC
Amazonian Demigoddess
Base Diana is already an elite blend of demigoddess strength, speed, durability, combat mastery, and moral clarity. Her unique role is that she can fight like a warrior while insisting on truth and compassion.
#53Green Lantern (Hal Jordan)DC
Power Ring Bearer
Hal Jordan as a core Green Lantern is willpower made weapon: constructs limited mainly by imagination, focus, and emotional stability. Hal’s strength is courage and decisiveness; his weakness is overconfidence.
#54Martian ManhunterDC
Martian
A DC powerhouse with telepathy, shapeshifting, intangibility, flight, strength, and deep empathy. Martian Manhunter is often the team’s conscience and psychic anchor—capable of resolving conflict by understanding minds rather than hitting bodies.
#55ShazamDC
Magically Empowered
A magical champion empowered by the wizard’s blessing—wisdom, strength, stamina, speed, courage, and magical lightning. Shazam’s uniqueness is that the host is often a child or young person: immense power plus innocence.
#56Hulk (Base Form)Marvel
Gamma Mutation
Base Hulk is already a top-tier physical powerhouse whose strength scales with emotion. He’s also a trauma narrative: a person split by pain, hunted, misunderstood, and used as a weapon.
#57VisionMarvel
Android
Vision is an android hero whose peak states combine supercomputer intelligence with density control, energy projection, and the moral curiosity of a being trying to understand humanity. His strengths are precision and ethics.
#58Iron Man (Godkiller Armor)Marvel
Human
An extreme Tony Stark build designed to fight god-tier threats: a demonstration that intellect, resources, and relentless iteration can compete with mythic power. The narrative point is escalation: when humans build bigger weapons, the world becomes more brittle.
#59AquamanDC
Atlantean
King of Atlantis. Far stronger than pop culture suggests, with telepathy over marine life, immense durability, and magical weaponry (Trident). He commands 70% of the Earth's surface.
D
STREET
#80Spider-Man (Prime)Marvel
Mutated Human
Prime Spider-Man is a high-mobility, high-responsibility hero: strength, agility, spider-sense, and creativity combined with a deep moral code. He’s often written as the emotional center of Marvel because he treats ordinary people as the point of heroism.
#81Black Panther (Enhanced)Marvel
Human (Enhanced)
T’Challa enhanced (tech, training, strategy, vibranium, and occasionally mystic/ancestral boosts) is one of Marvel’s most complete leaders: scientist, tactician, and king. His power is systems-level: he can mobilize institutions, not just fists.
#82Iron Man (Base Armor)Marvel
Human
Tony Stark in standard armor is a high-end tech hero: flight, firepower, sensors, and rapid improvisation powered by genius engineering. His real superpower is iteration—he learns, upgrades, and adapts faster than most threats can stabilize.
#83Batman (Prep Time)DC
Peak Human
Batman with prep time is the archetype of strategic asymmetry: a human who wins against gods by preparation, psychology, contingency planning, and exploiting opponent blind spots. This form is powerful because it’s believable: intelligence and discipline can punch above weight.
#84WolverineMarvel
Human Mutant
A lethal survivor with healing factor, adamantium skeleton/claws, and decades of trauma. Wolverine’s strength is resilience and willingness to do what others won’t; his weakness is that he sometimes confuses violence with honesty.
#85Captain AmericaMarvel
Super-Soldier
Steve Rogers is not a cosmic powerhouse; he is a moral and strategic one. The serum gives him peak human strength, stamina, and healing, but his real edge is leadership: he can hold teams together under fear, and he refuses to trade ethics for expedience.
#86DeadpoolMarvel
Human Mutant
A chaotic antihero with regenerative healing, improvisational combat skill, and a mind that is both sharp and fragmented. Deadpool’s humor is armor against pain; his unpredictability makes him dangerous and sometimes strangely effective.
#87Moon KnightMarvel
Divine Avatar
A vigilante tied to the Egyptian god Khonshu, Moon Knight combines elite combat skill with dissociative identity complexity and a mission that often blurs justice with obsession. His power set is less about raw might and more about intensity and resilience.
#88Punisher (Celestial Gear)Marvel
Human (Enhanced)
A hypothetical/amped Punisher state where Frank Castle wields cosmic-level weaponry (Celestial tech) while still carrying a punishment-first worldview. This is ethically explosive: the most uncompromising vigilante suddenly has godlike tools.
#89Blue Beetle (Jaime Reyes)DC
Alien Scarab Host
Jaime Reyes bonded to the alien Scarab is a youthful hero with a modular, adaptive combat system: armor, weapons, shields, flight, and rapid analysis. The Scarab can be aggressive and tactical, sometimes pushing lethal solutions.
#90Green ArrowDC
Vigilante Archer
Oliver Queen is a peak human archer with tactical intelligence and a strong social conscience. He’s written best as a principled agitator: willing to confront power structures, corruption, and hypocrisy rather than just punching criminals.
#91HawkeyeMarvel
Marksman
Clint Barton is the quintessential “skilled human among gods”: an elite marksman with nerve, improvisation, and a stubborn moral core. His strength is reliability under pressure—he performs when cosmic teammates are distracted by their own scale.
