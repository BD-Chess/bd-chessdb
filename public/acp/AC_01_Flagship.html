This flagship paper is intentionally narrower than the companions. Its job is to make the strongest ontological move cleanly.

One-sentence spine

Current science may describe mechanisms extraordinarily well while still failing to explain why any mechanism is present at all. AC is offered as a less miraculous ground of that presence.

I · The pressure point

1. The problem AC is trying to solve

The pressure point is not whether physics and neuroscience have learned real things. They plainly have. The pressure point is whether their most successful descriptions of mechanism finish the job of explaining presence.

Rebuild principle. This paper does not discard standard science. It preserves causal structure, measurement, neural dynamics, and physical law as real achievements. What it challenges is the reflex that a complete description of mechanism automatically becomes a complete description of why anything is experientially present.

Claim type. The central move here is ontological, not laboratory-final. It is a proposal about what kind of ground makes experience least mysterious while preserving the empirical success of current science.

If a framework can describe signal flow, memory, integration, causal organization, and behavior, that matters. But if it still leaves us saying, “and somehow this became felt presence,” then a real explanatory remainder remains.

AC begins exactly there.

II · Preservation

2. What AC keeps from current science

AC is strongest when it behaves like a disciplined upgrade, not a theatrical overthrow. It keeps at least five major achievements of current science intact:

  • Physics keeps its lawful structure. AC does not ask us to throw away conservation laws, field dynamics, or successful low-level models.
  • Neuroscience keeps its mechanism. Brains still have rhythms, lesions, integration patterns, global coordination problems, and clinically observable state gradients.
  • Information theory keeps its usefulness. Complexity, integration, and structure remain powerful descriptive tools.
  • Computation keeps its place. Computation can still describe state evolution, control, and information processing.
  • Engineering keeps its approximations. Continuum descriptions, algorithmic models, and standard mathematics still work where they work.

Upgrade claim. AC says the success of those descriptions should be preserved, then placed inside a deeper ontology in which presence is not a late miracle but a primitive fact.

III · Least-miraculous move

3. The ontological move

The flagship move is simple:

If experience is real, a world in which presence is primitive may be less miraculous than a world in which wholly non-experiential mechanism somehow becomes experience.

This does not prove AC. It changes the burden structure. Instead of asking AC to justify why presence exists at all, it asks matter-only views why a non-experiential base should ever yield felt presence in the first place.

That is why AC is best presented as a least-miraculous ontology. It is not “mysticism added on top of science.” It is a proposal that science may already describe the machinery correctly while still grounding that machinery in an ontologically thinner way than reality warrants.

Strictness point. AC does not erase mechanism. It says mechanism may be derivative description, while presence is more basic.

IV · Relation, not lab equation

4. Resonance and relative existence

The rebuilt flagship keeps the resonance move, but with stricter labeling. Expressions such as RE = AC · R are treated here as ontological relations or compressed philosophical notation, not as established physics equations.

The intended meaning is that finite being is not outside the ground, but modulated within it: relative existence is grounded manifestation, not independent self-subsistence.

VI · Discipline and pressure tests

6. What this paper claims, and what would weaken it

claimsnon-claimsnext-step bridge

Claims. Experience is a datum. Mechanism may be insufficient to ground it. AC is a serious least-miraculous candidate for that ground.

Does not claim. That AC is experimentally proven, that physics has already been rederived from it, or that the companions are already complete scientific replacements for existing frameworks.

  • Weakening condition: a rival ontology explains presence equally well with less machinery.
  • Weakening condition: the bridge papers add no real discriminating power beyond current theories.
  • Failure aspiration: if mechanism alone one day explains presence without residue, AC loses much of its reason for being.

That is the right tone: preserve current science where it works, then demand a deeper answer where it still gestures instead of explains.
