AC Whole-First Co-Emergent Physicalization Hypothesis v0.1

Status: exploratory research seed, not an established physical theory Date: 2026-06-20 Authors: BD × AI Primary use: source attachment for AIm³ MentalArena QM–GR brainstorming

Abstract

This paper sharpens a whole-first ontology in which elementary particles, physical locality and spacetime are not fundamental building blocks. The candidate ground is AC — Absolute Consciousness; the neutral scientific symbol is Ω, so the mathematical programme can be tested without assuming the AC interpretation.

The central revision is that the proposal should not be represented as a simple chronological production line. Before physical time exists, “first” and “then” cannot mean temporal succession. The arrows instead express ontological or explanatory dependence. Several stages may be mutually constitutive:

AC / Ω
→ [self-distinction ↔ relation ↔ proto-information ↔ proto-perspective?]
→ [extended relational modes / loops / proto-strings ↔ causal ordering / proto-dynamics]
→ [physical information ↔ spacetime ↔ energy–momentum ↔ fields / matter]
→ complex resonant organisms
→ persistent embodied RC

The least mysterious transition is distinction-plus-relation into proto-information. The largest unresolved leaps are: why an undivided Whole differentiates at all; why differentiation yields a local subject or perspective; how a prephysical relational structure becomes this particular quantum-gravitational world; and how embodied organisation relates to persistent relative consciousness.

The paper proposes a neutral mathematical skeleton, separates proto-information from physical information, treats proto-strings as pre-geometric relational modes rather than tiny objects moving in prior space, and defines kill tests for use in MAL.

1. Core proposition

The ordinary bottom-up picture begins with elementary local objects and attempts to build larger structures from them. The whole-first reversal is:

The Whole does not emerge from elementary parts. Effective parts emerge when the Whole differentiates.

“Whole” does not mean spatially gigantic. If spacetime is emergent, AC/Ω is not large in metres and is not located anywhere. “Whole” means maximally inclusive and ontologically prior: no external container, no independent outside and no already-given separation.

The strongest defensible neutral version is:

A globally constrained pre-geometric structure Ω differentiates into relatively autonomous centres and relations. Stable relational modes become information-bearing physical degrees of freedom; spacetime, physical information, energy–momentum and fields/matter arise as a self-consistent cluster.

The AC interpretation adds:

The ultimate ground is not wholly non-present or inert; presence/consciousness is primitive, and local conscious centres are relative expressions or resonances of that ground.

These two formulations must be kept distinct. A successful Ω-model may support whole-first physics without establishing AC.

2. Not a timeline before time

A chain such as

AC → information → spacetime → energy → strings → matter

looks temporal. But if physical time emerges within the model, the early arrows cannot mean “this event happened earlier.” They mean:

  • logical dependence;
  • definitional dependence;
  • constraint dependence;
  • one structure being necessary for another to be meaningful;
  • or a jointly solved consistency relation.

Therefore the preferred architecture is a dependency graph with co-emergent clusters, not an assembly line.

Three broad emergence events are proposed:

Cluster A — primordial differentiation

AC / Ω
→ [distinction ↔ relation ↔ proto-information ↔ possible proto-perspective]

Distinction, relation and minimal information are nearly inseparable. A distinction with no relation is operationally inaccessible. A relation with nothing distinguishable at its ends is empty. Proto-information is the structured difference among related alternatives.

The question mark after proto-perspective is essential. A bounded distinction does not automatically imply subjectivity.

Cluster B — pre-geometric organisation

[extended relational modes / loops / proto-strings ↔ causal ordering / proto-dynamics]

Once relations can form recurrent chains, loops, spectra or modes, an ordering of possible transformations may become meaningful. This is not yet a string vibrating in metres per second. It is an extended pattern in relation-space and an order structure over possible changes.

Cluster C — physicalization

[physical information ↔ spacetime ↔ energy–momentum ↔ fields / matter]

These may be mutually constitutive. Geometry defines physical neighbourhood and causal accessibility. Fields provide distinguishable states. Energy–momentum characterises and generates physical change. Energy–momentum also shapes geometry. Physical information is embodied in these causally accessible states.

The physical cluster may therefore arise as a fixed point rather than a one-way chain.

4. Definitions

AC

Absolute Consciousness: BD’s candidate ultimate ground of presence and existence. It is not a spatial object, a cosmic human personality or a field located inside spacetime.

Ω

A neutral placeholder for the global pre-geometric ground. Use Ω whenever testing mathematics that does not require the AC interpretation.

Self-distinction-with-relation

An internal differentiation that creates distinguishable poles, sectors, centres or states while preserving a relation within the Whole. It is distinction without absolute disconnection.

Proto-information, I₀

Structured distinguishability before physical encoding. It may be represented by state-space alternatives, algebraic relations, constraints or correlations. It is not automatically Shannon information and need not yet be measured in physical bits.

Proto-local perspective / proto-RC

A candidate relative centre of access or standpoint. This is the most controversial early step because a partition or algebraic restriction does not by itself prove there is “something it is like” to be that restriction.

Proto-string

An extended, recurrent or loop-like relational mode before ordinary geometry. It is not necessarily a literal perturbative string in a target spacetime. Three branches must be compared:

  • A. Literal fundamental strings in a spacetime or target-space formulation.
  • B. Emergent relational loops whose later effective description is string-like.
  • C. Abstract modes for which string language is one dual representation among others.

For this worldview, B or C is presently cleaner than A.

Proto-dynamics

An order, transition rule, automorphism, path weight, constraint propagation or update structure before physical clock time is derived.

Physical information, Iphys

Distinguishable states encoded in and accessible through physical fields, causal relations, energy distributions and geometry.

Physicalization

The transition or fixed-point solution through which spacetime, physical information, energy–momentum and fields/matter become jointly well-defined.

RC

Relative Consciousness: a locally bounded conscious centre. “Persistent embodied RC” includes memory, agency, self-models, sensory boundaries and continuity through an organism.

6. Two births of information

The phrase “information creates spacetime” is ambiguous because information appears at two levels.

Proto-information

I₀ = structured distinguishability within Ω

It appears with distinction and relation. It does not require coordinates, particles, joules or a physical clock.

Physical information

Iphys = operationally accessible distinctions embodied in physical states

It may arise jointly with the physical cluster:

Iphys ↔ causal order ↔ spacetime ↔ energy–momentum ↔ fields/matter

Thus information can be both earlier and co-emergent, depending on which meaning is intended. MAL must reject any proposal that slides between I₀ and Iphys without defining the map between them.

7. Why spacetime was previously placed too late

Placing matter before spacetime used “matter” loosely to mean stable pre-geometric patterns. That wording was misleading.

If matter means physical particles and fields with location, momentum and mass, then it cannot cleanly precede spacetime. Conversely, spacetime without any physical relational or field content may be an empty formal scaffold. General relativity already makes geometry and energy–momentum mutually constraining.

The cleaner distinction is:

stable pre-geometric modes
        ↓
[spacetime ↔ energy–momentum ↔ physical fields/matter]

Matter and spacetime may form together as two aspects of one consistent physical regime.

8. Proto-strings as the distinction–relation bridge

The useful string-theory insight is not necessarily that reality contains literal tiny cords in hidden spatial dimensions. It is that one underlying extended structure can possess many modes that appear as different particles and can include gravitational behaviour.

A whole-first translation is:

AC/Ω does not fragment into isolated point-particles. It supports internally extended relational modes. Closed or recurrent modes are proto-strings; their spectra become stable physical species when the physical cluster forms.

This avoids “AC mixing with nothingness.” Boundaries can be phase, relation, mode or access distinctions inside one Whole. No second substance called nothingness is required.

However, “AC starts vibrating” must be used carefully. Literal vibration presupposes an ordering parameter and usually a metric or configuration space. Before physical time, the safer statement is:

AC/Ω admits nontrivial self-relational eigenmodes and transitions. After an effective time parameter emerges, some of these can be represented as oscillations with physical frequencies and energies.

9. Neutral mathematical skeleton

Let

Ω = (A, S, C)

where:

  • A is a global relational/observable algebra or combinatorial structure;
  • S is the allowed state set;
  • C is a set of global consistency constraints.

A differentiation map

D : Ω → {Aᵢ, Rᵢⱼ}

produces effective centres Aᵢ and relations Rᵢⱼ without assuming coordinates.

A relational operator may define modes:

KΩ ψₙ = λₙ ψₙ

Candidate interpretations:

  • stable ψₙ → particle/field modes;
  • protected/topological sectors → conserved charges;
  • oriented zero modes → chirality candidates;
  • loop-like support of ψₙ → proto-string candidates.

An effective distance may be inferred from correlation or transition strength:

d(i,j) = -ℓ log[(|Cᵢⱼ| + ε) / C₀]

No coordinate is supplied to the learner.

The physical cluster can be written as a fixed-point problem:

P = {Iphys, CausalOrder, g, T, Φ}
P = FΩ,D(P)

where g is effective geometry, T energy–momentum and Φ fields/modes. A successful physicalization map must have stable, nontrivial solutions and recover known limits.

The AC-specific branch must define an additional admissibility, selection or dynamics rule. Its neutral ablation must be explicit:

λAC = 0  → neutral Ω model
λAC ≠ 0 → AC-specific model

If the outputs are unchanged, AC has not yet added physics.

10. QM–GR interpretation

A possible division of explanatory labour is:

  • Quantum mechanics: the rules governing conditional local descriptions and transformations of the global relational state.
  • General relativity: the large-scale self-consistent geometry of causal relations among stable physical modes.
  • Physicalization: the bridge that jointly establishes local observables, causal order, geometry, energy–momentum and fields.

This may explain why simply quantising a pre-existing classical geometry is difficult: QM and GR could be two effective projections of a deeper common generator.

But this is only a research orientation. It must still recover:

  • Hilbert/operator structure or a precise alternative;
  • Born probabilities and no-signalling;
  • classical records/decoherence;
  • Lorentzian causality;
  • effective 3+1 dimensionality;
  • equivalence principle and Einstein limit;
  • chiral matter and anomaly cancellation;
  • black-hole information accounting;
  • cosmological observables.

11. Competing architectures MAL must compare

Sequential model

I₀ → modes → causal order → geometry → fields → matter

Advantage: easier to implement and diagnose. Risk: inserts hidden background assumptions and may force an artificial order.

Co-emergent fixed-point model

{Iphys, causality, geometry, energy–momentum, fields} = F(...)

Advantage: matches mutual dependence. Risk: can become circular unless the fixed-point equation and independent primitive are explicit.

Phase-transition model

A pre-geometric system crosses a control threshold and a stable physical phase appears with joint geometry and matter excitations.

Advantage: supplies a dynamical mechanism and critical signatures. Risk: “phase transition” itself normally assumes an order parameter and dynamics that must be defined pre-geometrically.

Dual-description model

Physical reality admits equivalent geometric, algebraic and string-like descriptions; none is uniquely fundamental.

Advantage: compatible with duality. Risk: may evade ontology rather than explain emergence.

MAL should not force a winner before toy tests.

12. Kill tests

  1. Pre-time chronology test: Does the model use “before,” “vibration” or “energy” in a way that already assumes physical time?
  2. Distinction mechanism test: Is self-distinction derived from a rule or merely declared?
  3. Subjectivity test: Does proto-RC add a defined variable or prediction, or is subsystem restriction renamed consciousness?
  4. Information-level test: Are I₀ and Iphys distinguished mathematically?
  5. Proto-string rename test: Do loops/modes explain anything beyond generic graph cycles?
  6. Co-emergence circularity test: Does P = F(P) have an independent primitive, existence/uniqueness conditions and stable solutions?
  7. Coordinate-leak test: Can dimension and locality emerge without hidden coordinates or lattice labels?
  8. Energy derivation test: Does physical energy arise only after a time-like generator exists?
  9. Chirality test: Does handedness emerge rather than being assigned?
  10. Anomaly test: Is the complete mode spectrum globally consistent?
  11. Einstein-limit test: Does any controlled gravitational response approximate known GR?
  12. AC-neutral twin test: Does AC-specific structure change a derivation, compression or prediction?
  13. RC embodiment test: Can the model distinguish generation, realisation and resonance accounts of consciousness?
  14. Held-out test: Do gains survive unseen systems and full model-cost accounting?

Failure does not kill the seed globally. It identifies the highest rung earned.

13. Smallest MAL research objective

The first serious objective should not be a complete theory of everything. It should compare sequential and co-emergent models in a coordinate-free toy system.

Minimum build

  • Generate a global algebra, directed hypergraph or incidence operator with no coordinates.
  • Search differentiation maps D that produce stable centres.
  • Detect extended loop-supported modes.
  • infer causal order and effective dimension from transitions/correlations.
  • Compare sequential emergence with a jointly optimised physicalization fixed point.
  • Add an orientation bifurcation and measure chirality.
  • Include neutral, random, hidden-lattice, literal-compact-dimension and AC-ablation controls.

Metrics

  • held-out spectral/causal dimension stability;
  • propagation locality and approximate light-cone score;
  • mode persistence and dispersion;
  • fixed-point stability and uniqueness;
  • chirality index and anomaly proxy;
  • description length and parameter count;
  • sensitivity to λAC;
  • failure class: NO_SIGNAL, STRUCTURE_ONLY, TOY_MECHANISM, PHYSICS_BRIDGE_CANDIDATE.

14. Research verdict

The refined proposal is more coherent than a literal chain in which AC splits into particles, mixes with nothingness and later manufactures space.

The strongest current seed is:

AC/Ω differentiates through relation rather than fragmentation. Distinction, relation and proto-information arise together. Extended relational modes provide a proto-string-like bridge. Causal ordering develops with those modes. Physical information, spacetime, energy–momentum and fields/matter then become mutually defined as one physicalization regime. Complex organisms stabilise persistent embodied relative consciousness.

Its strongest part is the distinction–relation–information connection. Its weakest parts are the first differentiation, the appearance of subjectivity, and the derivation of the exact physical laws and constants.

That is not a reason to discard it. It tells MAL exactly where to concentrate discovery pressure.
