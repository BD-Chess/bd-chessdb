  • Complexity alone is not consciousness.
  • Hysteresis is not a controller signature.

  • Claustrum evidence is mixed and supports candidate-modulator status only.

What a result would — and would not — mean

Positive state-model result
Supports a frozen local neural representation under the declared conditions. It does not establish phenomenality or ontology.
Positive adaptive-control result
Establishes controllability only unless endogenous error coding, temporal precedence, mediation, selective disruption, rescue, and penalized superiority all survive.
Positive claustrum result
May support a pathway-specific controller component. It does not establish a seat, switch, necessary node, or complete controller.
Negative CCH result
Must count against the relevant hypothesis without moving X(t), ΩCCH, thresholds, or target regions after the result.
Engineering success
Supports the tested DCC/MDL×DCC/AI8 system only. It does not prove biological CCH, AC, CFH, or artificial phenomenality.

Reviewer questions worth answering

Gap: does the prospectively cross-condition-validated state-and-intervention gap genuinely remain?
Rival: can thalamocortical + brainstem + PK/PD + recurrent dynamics explain the same observations more simply?
Identification: are X(t) and ΩCCH frozen independently of the desired outcome?
Controller: is there signed error information and causal mediation beyond controllability?
Falsification: can a negative result kill H2 or H3 without redefining the target?
Ontology: does AC/CFH make any preregistered prediction that differs from the same no-AC/no-CF model?
