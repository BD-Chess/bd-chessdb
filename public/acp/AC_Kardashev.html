MDL · The Compression Criterion

Minimum Description Length as Civilizational Fitness

If better solutions are more compressible — as demonstrated across TSP (ρ = 0.80), Sudoku (ρ = 0.85), and DNA (Z = 38) — then a civilization's quality can be measured by how succinctly it describes itself without sacrificing functional richness.

A Type‑I civilization requiring millions of pages of legal code, contradictory narratives, and wasteful bureaucracy has high MDL — it is not a "good solution." A mature Type‑II or Type‑III civilization compresses its governance, knowledge base, and self‑description to the minimum needed to preserve Cₙ and Ψ(I). MDL is not simplicity — it is elegance: maximal compression without loss of essential complexity.

MDL = Ldescr + Lresid

Lresid = residual unpredictability (unexplained variance, societal noise, conflict)
