Papers on consciousness, structure, and scientific refinement

Absolute Consciousness / CCH Papers

Six dark-theme papers built for public reading: keep what science already measures well, refine the ontology where it still seems shallow, and keep speculative extensions clearly labeled.
preserve empirical successesadd better ontological structurespeculation status kept explicit

Bundle logic. This series no longer frames itself as “science overturned.” It treats current science as a set of genuine empirical achievements that may still be ontologically under-described. The goal is to keep the successes, sharpen the categories, and add better bridge questions.

Paper 1 · Flagship

Absolute Consciousness as the Least-Miraculous Ground of Experience

The ontological center: what AC is, what problem it addresses, what it keeps from current science, and what it refuses to overclaim.
Paper 2 · Bridge

From AC to Testable Structure

The operational bridge: coherence, complexity, S, coupling language, measurement strategy, and failure conditions.
Paper 3 · Companion

Discrete Physics and Non-Computational Consciousness

How a discrete substrate can coexist with a non-computational consciousness ground without collapsing back into reductionism.
Paper 4 · Companion

Zero, Absence, and Ill-Posed Operations

A semantic and ontological refinement paper kept explicitly modest about its status.
Paper 5 · Companion

Massless Carriers, Grid Speed, and Ontological Absence

A speculative but structured note on photons, gravitons, propagation ceilings, and coherence-building roles.
Paper 6 · Method

Seed, Formalization, Test, Result

How the program was actually built through human seed generation and AI formalization, without pretending method success is proof of ontology.

Reading order. Read 1 → 2 first. Papers 3–5 extend and stress-test the stack. Paper 6 explains the method and why the package aims to refine science rather than posture above it.

Editorial stance. This rebuild frames the stack as an attempt to preserve the empirical successes of current science while refining its ontology, tightening its category boundaries, and adding sharper discriminating questions where current interpretations feel shallow.
