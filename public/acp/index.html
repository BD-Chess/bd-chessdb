Epistemic firewall

ACP contains two connected but independent research lanes. The ontology lane asks what phenomenal presence may ultimately be. The science lane asks which measurable brain dynamics and control mechanisms can be causally tested. Neither lane proves the other.

CCH does not require AC or CFH. A successful CCH result would not prove AC or CFH. A successful DCC, MDL×DCC, or AI8 result would support only its domain-bounded engineering claim—not AC, CFH, biological CCH, or biological or artificial consciousness.

From first-person inquiry to a working engineering kernel.

Soul Voyage and the later AC/CFH/CCH work are not presented as proof of DCC or MDL×DCC. They are the documented intellectual lineage from which the control problem was abstracted. DCC was then operationalized and combined with MDL in code, solvers, arenas, and measured experiments across materially different domains.

Those engineering results do not validate AC, CFH, or biological CCH. They do establish that the originating research path produced a functioning technical method with cross-domain evidence, and that MDL×DCC must be evaluated on its own results.

One may reject the ontology and still have to evaluate the invention.
