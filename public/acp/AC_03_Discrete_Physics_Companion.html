This paper protects the stack from one recurring confusion: that any appeal to discrete physics must secretly collapse back into computationalism.

I · Why keep this paper

1. Why a discrete-physics companion is still useful

The value of this companion is not that it replaces physics. Its value is that it clarifies one conceptual junction: a physically computational substrate need not mean consciousness is computational.

That is already a scientific refinement. It says current physics may describe state evolution correctly while still leaving the ontological question of presence open.

II · Layering without confusion

2. Two layers, two jobs

The cleaned framing is:

  • Physical layer: state evolution, lawful dynamics, substrate transitions, possibly discrete at some bottom scale.
  • Consciousness ground: phenomenal presence, not reducible merely to state transition description.
  • Bridge: structured systems may stand in differing coupling relation to that ground.

Precision gain. This does not reverse current science. It says science may already capture the mechanism of evolution while missing the best ontological reading of what that mechanism is evolution within.

III · Discreteness without collapse

3. Why a discrete substrate does not collapse the framework

Even if physical reality is discrete at bottom scale, what follows immediately is a claim about state updates, not a completed claim about phenomenal identity. A frame-update picture can describe how the world evolves without exhausting why any evolved structure is present at all.

That matters because it preserves a real distinction:

QuestionHandled by
How do physical configurations evolve?Physics, computation, update laws
Why is any eligible configuration phenomenally present?Not settled merely by update law description

That is not anti-scientific. It is just category discipline.

IV · Grid-speed as interpretive bridge

4. The speed of light as an interpretive bridge

Reading the speed of light as a substrate propagation ceiling rather than just a familiar velocity is a useful conceptual move. It is useful because it gives intuitive shape to the difference between ordinary matter-like dynamics within a substrate and propagation-limited carriers that mark its ceiling behavior.

Good use. As an interpretive bridge, this helps unify discrete intuition and relativistic ceiling intuition.

Not yet enough. It still does not derive relativity in full. Lorentz invariance, low-energy recovery, dispersion constraints, and preferred-frame absence remain mandatory if this path wants to become more than interpretation.

V · Limits and value

5. What this paper adds — and what it still does not finish

This companion adds scientific value if it makes one confusion harder to commit: that mechanism-level discreteness automatically settles the consciousness question. It does not.

What it still does not finish:

  • full relativity recovery
  • a complete derivation of bridge variables from first principles
  • a final account of substrate consciousness criteria

That is fine. As part of the rebuilt stack, its job is refinement: preserve physics, clarify categories, and remove false forced choices.
