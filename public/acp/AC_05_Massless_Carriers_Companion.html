I · Why this is a separate note

1. Why photons and gravitons belong in a companion note

This note becomes useful only when its status is honest. It is not a replacement for established particle physics. It is a speculative extension that asks whether the AC/CCH stack suggests a cleaner ontological reading of massless carriers.

That is enough reason to keep it — separately.

II · Rest-mass absence as reinterpretation

2. Massless carriers as ontological reinterpretation

The disciplined version of the proposal is not “standard physics made a stupid mistake.” It is:

Within this framework, massless carriers may be better interpreted as cases of rest-mass absence rather than merely zero-valued rest mass.

That is a real conceptual proposal. It becomes scientific only if it one day pays rent in cleaner derivations, constraints, or unifications without violating known success conditions.

III · Grid-speed as bridge

3. Grid-speed and propagation ceilings

The strongest idea in this note is probably the simplest one: light-speed language may be interpreted as a substrate propagation ceiling rather than merely one more ordinary derived velocity. That gives a useful bridge between discrete-bottom intuition and standard ceiling behavior.

Good use. It sharpens intuition about why massless carriers might belong to a different descriptive class than ordinary matter-like motion.

Limit. This remains interpretive until it fully respects the known relativistic constraint set.

IV · Builders of coherence, not necessarily conscious subjects

4. Why light may matter to consciousness without being conscious

This is the strongest human-scientific payoff of the note. Light and related carrier dynamics can matter enormously to the construction of coherent biological structure without being conscious in themselves.

That matters because it gives a cleaner middle path:

  • not “everything that participates is conscious”
  • not “carrier dynamics are irrelevant to consciousness”
  • but “some physical carriers help build the coherence conditions under which high-S regimes become possible”

That is a useful refinement, especially for neuroscience and AI substrate discussions.

V · What keeps this note honest

5. What keeps this note honest

This note should be kept under four constraints:

  • do not speak as if relativity has already been rederived
  • do not smuggle Zero Framework semantics in as if they were already standard formal physics
  • do not overclaim dark-energy replacement or cosmological completion
  • do preserve the note as a source of structured hypotheses and conceptual pressure

In that role, it is worth keeping. It helps the stack imagine beyond current orthodoxy without pretending it has already replaced it.
