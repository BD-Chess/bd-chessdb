1 · Firewall

Two connected but independent lanes

Core rule

The ontology lane asks what phenomenal presence may ultimately be. The science lane asks which measurable brain dynamics and control mechanisms can be causally tested. Neither lane proves the other.

CCH does not require AC or CFH.

A successful CCH result would not prove AC or CFH.

A successful DCC or AI8 result would prove only its engineering result, not biological or artificial consciousness.
