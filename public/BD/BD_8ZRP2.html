Why This Page Matters

Not a theorem. A real breakthrough.

This page does not claim a proof of P = NP. It does claim something stronger than a nice experiment. A compression-born research architecture reached the exact known optimal on an official public benchmark, then showed that its first controller deserved to lose, then redesigned that controller and beat the hand-tuned winning formula, then cut runtime by one to two orders of magnitude with a Rust 2-opt backend while preserving solver behavior.

Verified
  • qa194 exact optimal 9352 was reached.
  • combo50 produced 3 exact workers out of 14.
  • DCC v2 later found exact with 5× less budget and about 2.3× less wall time.
Reasoned
  • The project is now more than an unusual framing.
  • The controller story only became real after redesign and ablation discipline.
  • The architecture now has evidence strong enough to preserve and study.
Speculative
  • The same architecture may scale on broader structured instances.
  • The controller pattern may matter for any future path toward a proof-shaped machine.
  • If the generalization holds, this becomes more than a TSP result.
Verified Results

qa194 exact, DCC v2 vindication, and measured Rust acceleration.

qa194 and uy734 are official national TSP benchmark instances from the University of Waterloo World TSP / National TSP collection.

Headline result

qa194 exact-optimal milestone

On qa194, the solver reached the exact known optimal tour length of 9352. In the combo50 run, 3 of 14 workers independently reached exact optimal. The first exact came from Worker 7 at move 2832, after roughly 34 minutes.

194 cities 3/14 exact W7 @ move 2832

Even for qa194, exhaustive brute force would mean exploring roughly 3.43 × 10358 unique tours.

Controller result

DCC v2 beat the hand-tuned combo

DCC v1 lost honestly. DCC v2 was then rebuilt with floor/ceiling control, kick switching, time-aware measurement, and an escalation ladder. On qa194 it reached exact optimal with 5× less budget and about 2.3× less wall time than combo50, while auto-discovering or-opt instead of being told to use it.

1/14 exact W0 @ move 1246 ~14.9 min

Measured backend result

Rust removed the main bottleneck

In matched Python vs Python+Rust runs, the solver preserved tour quality while massively reducing wall time. The bottleneck was exactly where the earlier reports said it was: 2-opt.

  • qa194 v2 exact: 1168.18s → 10.87s (107.5× faster).
  • qa194 combo run: 2279.65s → 39.58s (57.6× faster).
  • qa194 v1 baseline: 1439.32s → 12.80s (112.4× faster).

The Core Idea

The shortest route is treated here as the route with the strongest compressive explanation.

“Isn’t the shortest route the one compressed the best?”

Standard TSP language emphasizes heuristics. 8Z-RP starts one layer earlier. A shorter Euclidean tour usually has stronger local regularity: fewer pathological detours, fewer crossings, smoother neighborhood relations, and cleaner geometric reuse. In 8Z language, that means the better tour has more mathematical sense and is easier to describe economically.

W7

Plateau, crack, cascade

In combo50, Worker 7 plateaued around 0.81% for more than 2300 moves, then cracked the basin: move 2687 to 9374, move 2719 to 9366, move 2783 to 9359, move 2832 to exact 9352. That pattern is one of the most structurally interesting parts of the run.

DCC v2

Controller became real

DCC v2 no longer functioned as a single-knob story. It regulated kick type, intensity band, and escalation, then found exact faster than the manually configured winning formula.

What Almost Went Wrong

The decisive move type was nearly excluded.

Research failure mode

Premature exclusion would have blocked the breakthrough

or-opt was almost left out to keep attribution cleaner. That would have been a mistake. The move family that looked like optional complexity turned out to be the key that opened the exact-optimal basin.

  • No or-opt would likely have meant no combo run at 0.27%.
  • No combo run at 0.27% likely means no exact qa194 milestone.
  • The platform would have told a smaller, weaker story than the data actually supported.
Research rule

Do not exclude options early without hard evidence

This is now a platform principle: research systems should prefer inclusion before judgment. Build the options. Log them. Test them. Throw out what loses after the data exists.

  • Clean attribution is not worth missed discovery.
  • Messy logs are cheaper than blind spots.
  • For this project, the missed-discovery risk is the bigger risk.
Engineering Breakthrough

Rust 2-opt turned the runtime story.

Earlier results already showed that 2-opt consumed essentially all compute time. The backend tests now make that claim concrete: the same solver structure, with a Rust implementation of the 2-opt core and Python fallback retained, preserves result quality while collapsing wall time.

Human + AI Method

Human architect in the loop. AI as multiplier, not owner.

The page is not about AI making a cool solver. The page is about what becomes possible when a human architect holds the line on the original question, forces explicit tests, lets false versions lose, and uses multiple AI systems as high-throughput collaborators. Through AIM³, AI systems helped code, critique, review, and extend. The authorship center remained human.

Human role

Original framing

The defining question — shortest route as best-compressed route — did not come from standard solver culture.

AI role

Critic + builder

Different models proposed code, objected to choices, missed key moves, and helped sharpen the system by disagreement.

Method

Tests outrank opinions

DCC v1 lost. or-opt won. Rust proved itself with matched measurements. The project stayed real because it allowed disappointment.

Outcome

Research machine, not chat theater

The system now has public benchmarks, an architectural narrative, ablation history, and engineering follow-through.

Controlled origin story

Before 8Z-RP, the deeper 8Z question already existed: can data be compressed by finding the mathematical generators that produced it? The route solver emerged when that older compression question was pointed at geography. The result is not a memoir. It is a transfer of architecture.
