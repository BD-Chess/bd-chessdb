Dve plati istega kovancaTwo sides of the same coin

Karakter pove, kaj človek varuje. Razmišljanje pokaže, kako to prevede v dejanje.

Character describes what a person protects. Reasoning shows how that becomes action.

Tvoja nova misel je dobro izhodišče: karakter in razmišljanje sta lahko dva pogleda na iste ponavljajoče se izbire. V portretu opišemo držo; tukaj opišemo, kaj ta drža naredi z vprašanjem, možnostmi in naslednjim korakom. To je uredniška povezava med dokumentoma, ne psihološki model celotnega človeka.

Your new thought provides a useful starting point: character and reasoning can be two views of the same recurring choices. The portrait describes a disposition; this page describes what it does to a question, the available options, and the next step. This is an editorial bridge between the documents, not a psychological model of the whole person.

Izvorna zankaThe original loop

Zavrni → razstavi → posploši → prepusti izbiro sistemu. To je skupno jedro vseh različic arhiva.

Challenge → decompose → generalize → let the system decide. This is the common core of every archive version.

  1. 01Zavrni privzeto omejitevChallenge the assumed constraint

    Ali je to res meja problema — ali samo meja trenutne formulacije?

    Is this a limit of the problem—or only of its current formulation?

  2. 02RazstaviDecompose

    Ne vsa slika, ampak kos. Ne ves problem, ampak prvi uporaben rez.

    Not the whole image, but a chunk. Not the entire problem, but the first useful cut.

  3. 03PosplošiGeneralize

    Ne samo π; družina generatorjev. Ne samo ena domena; drug preizkus iste zamisli.

    Not only π; a family of generators. Not only one domain; another test of the same idea.

  4. 04Prepusti izbiro primerjaviLet comparison decide

    Kandidati tekmujejo; strošek in rezultat določita, kaj ostane.

    Candidates compete; cost and outcome determine what stays.

PodlagaBasis · S01 S03 S05

Izvorna zgodba je zamisel o delčkih slikovnih podatkov v decimalkah π. Ko je bilo iskanje cele slike predrago, se vprašanje ni zaprlo: velikost kosa se je zmanjšala, nabor generatorjev pa razširil. Viri to opisujejo kot BD-jev prihod do načela MDL skozi lastno vprašanje. Gre za izvor 8Z-jeve uporabe ideje, ne za trditev, da je tu nastala zgodovina MDL kot teorije.

The origin story begins with looking for pieces of image data in the digits of π. When searching for an entire image was too expensive, the question stayed open: the chunk became smaller and the generator family became larger. The sources describe this as BD reaching the MDL principle through his own question. This is the origin of its use in 8Z, not a claim that MDL as a theory began here.

Uredniška izpeljava: štiri poteze niso zahteva, da mora prva intuicija zmagati. Njihova moč je, da iz preširoke intuicije nastane več preizkusljivih možnosti — med njimi tudi preprosta možnost, ki prvotnega mehanizma sploh ne potrebuje.

Editorial interpretation: the four moves do not require the initial intuition to win. Their strength is turning a broad intuition into several testable options—including a simple option that does not need the original mechanism.

seme → most → gradnja → test → rezultat → popravek → prenosseed → bridge → build → test → result → revision → transfer

Daljša zanka doda tisto, česar štiri poteze same še ne zapišejo: rezultat je treba ohraniti, popravek prenesti v dokumente in naslednji preizkus začeti z boljšim izhodiščem.

The longer loop adds what the four moves alone do not record: preserve the result, carry the correction into the documents, and start the next test from a better position.

Osem ponavljajočih se miselnih potezEight recurring reasoning moves

To niso dodatne osebnostne oznake. So posegi, ki spremenijo, kaj sistem lahko vidi, primerja in naredi.

These are not additional personality labels. They are interventions in what a system can see, compare, and do.

M01

Poišči vrzel med obljubo in kodoFind the gap between promise and code

»Ali tega naš sistem že ne zna?« lahko pomeni: informacija obstaja, vendar ne doseže vseh odločitev. Preveri vhod, čas odločitve in mesto uporabe; šele nato predlagaj nov modul.

“Doesn’t our system already do this?” can mean: the information exists, but does not reach every decision. Inspect the input, decision time, and point of use before proposing another module.

M02

Premakni pogled, nato ga prevedi nazajChange perspective, then translate it back

Mesta lahko postanejo teren, odboji zvoka, delci ali igralne figure. Korak ni končan pri prispodobi: povedati je treba, kateri podatek opazujemo, katero količino računamo in katera odločitev se zaradi nje spremeni.

Cities can become terrain, echoes, particles, or game pieces. A metaphor is not the endpoint: specify what data is observed, what quantity is computed, and which decision changes because of it.

M03

Nerazloženi ostanek je naslednje vprašanjeMake the unexplained remainder the next question

Po prvem modelu ne vprašaj le, kako zapisati ostanek, ampak ali zanj obstaja drugačen model. V arhivu to poveže zvočne residuale z generatorji iz drugih domen. Globina mora plačati lasten opis in iskanje.

After the first model, ask not only how to encode the remainder, but whether a different model fits it. The archive connects audio residuals with generators from other domains. Each extra layer must pay for its description and search.

M04

Vključi tisto, kar je bilo zunaj merilaInclude what was outside the objective

Glava datoteke, stanje učenca, cena senzorja ali dolg prenos konteksta niso samodejno brezplačni. V primerjavo sodi celoten potreben paket. Istočasno lahko obstaja kandidat brez dodatnega sloja.

A file header, learner state, sensor cost, or long context handoff is not automatically free. Compare the complete required package. A candidate without the extra layer can compete alongside it.

M05

Učenje premakni tudi na krmilnikLet learning reach the controller too

Vprašanje ni samo, kateri kandidat zmaga. Je tudi, kateri senzor zazna stanje, kateri krmilni zakon se odzove ter koliko širine ali globine si zasluži iskanje. Samodejna nastavitev je kandidat za primerjavo, ne privilegij.

The question is not only which candidate wins. It is also which sensor detects the state, which control law responds, and how much search breadth or depth is warranted. Adaptation is a candidate to compare, not a privilege.

M06

Vprašaj po presenečenju, ne le po povprečjuAsk about surprise, not only the average

Ko se vrstni red senzorjev obrne ali preprosta konfiguracija premaga prilagodljivo, se odpre novo vprašanje o velikosti, režimu ali reprezentaciji. Izguba na eni instanci ni ista trditev kot neuporabnost povsod.

When sensor rankings reverse or a simple configuration beats an adaptive one, a new question opens about size, regime, or representation. Losing on one instance is not the same claim as being useless everywhere.

M07

Prepoznaj izdelek v že zgrajenem orodjuRecognize the product inside an existing tool

»Ali sem edini, ki to potrebuje?« spremeni merilo iz interne funkcije v uporabniški problem. Cold storage in paywall sta v arhivu dva različna taka obrata: nova raba iste zmožnosti ter prepoznanje že uporabljene rešitve.

“Am I the only person who needs this?” moves the frame from an internal feature to a user problem. Cold storage and the paywall are two distinct turns in the archive: a new use for an existing capability, and recognition of the solution already in use.

M08

Popravek naredi prenosljivMake the correction transferable

Dobro pojasnilo v klepetu še ni podedovano znanje. Zapiši, katera domneva je padla, kaj se je spremenilo, kateri dokaz to podpira in kaj naj naslednji sodelavec preveri. Spomin naj zmanjša ponovno delo, ne zamrzne doktrine.

A good explanation in chat is not yet inherited knowledge. Record the failed assumption, the change, the supporting evidence, and what the next collaborator should check. Memory should reduce repeated work, not freeze doctrine.

PodlagaBasis · S04 S05 S07

Moj skupni opis teh potez: BD pogosto izboljšuje iskanje tako, da v sistem vrne nekaj, kar je bilo prej zunaj njega: del podatkov, drugo perspektivo, prezrto odločitev, strošek, kandidata, ugovor ali izgubljeni spomin.

My synthesis of these moves: BD often improves search by bringing back something that had been left outside it: part of the data, another perspective, an overlooked decision, a cost, a candidate, an objection, or a lost memory.

Po zapisu je MTF soglasje več časovnih okvirov vplivalo na velikost začetnega vstopa, ne pa na kasnejša dodajanja poziciji. AI je ponudil nastavitve; BD je vprašal, zakaj se trenutno soglasje ne uporabi tudi ob vsakem dodajanju.

According to the record, multi-timeframe consensus affected the initial entry size, but not subsequent position additions. The AI offered settings; BD asked why the current consensus was not also used at each addition.

Iz tega so nastali Sync Add Boost, ločena omejitev množitelja ter nastavljiva utež gnezdenja. Splošna poteza je pregled vseh odločilnih mest: če informacija pomaga pri A, preveri, ali je relevantna tudi pri B, C in D. Drugi preskok je bil zahteva, naj se ugotovitev shrani v reasoning dokumente.

This led to Sync Add Boost, a separate multiplier ceiling, and an adjustable nesting weight. The general move is to inspect every decision point: when information helps at A, check whether it is relevant at B, C, and D. The second move was requiring that the lesson be captured in the reasoning documents.

Dve stvari sta bili hkrati koristni: GPT-jeva zahteva po ablaciji DCC in BD-jeva zavrnitev, da zaradi nje iz arene izključimo or-opt. Ablacija je pokazala izgubo DCC v1; širši nabor je omogočil test or-opt in kasnejših kombinacij.
