Bojan Dobrečevič človek v zanki the human in the loop

To ni življenjepis in ni psihološki test. Je delovni portret človeka, ki ga je mogoče videti skozi dolgo sodelovanje z različnimi AI-ji: skozi vprašanja, gradnje, popravke, konflikte, rezultate in odnose.

This is not a CV and not a psychological assessment. It is a working portrait of a person seen through sustained collaboration with different AIs: through questions, builds, corrections, disagreements, results, and relationships.

Najkrajše: BD zaščiti seme dovolj dolgo, da ga je mogoče zgraditi; nato zahteva test, ki ga lahko ubije. Če test spremeni zgodbo, spremeni zgodbo — ne testa.
Shortest version: BD protects a seed long enough to build it, then asks for a test that can kill it. If the test changes the story, he changes the story—not the test.

Kaj pri njem najbolj izstopaWhat stands out most

Ne osebnostne oznake iz vprašalnika, temveč vzorci, ki so se pokazali v dejanskem delu.

Not personality labels from a questionnaire, but patterns visible in actual work.

01 · Cross-domain

Misli z mostoviHe thinks in bridges

Kompresija lahko postane pogled na TSP, šah odpre vprašanje oblike trajektorije, Sudoku postane laboratorij upravljanja iskanja. Prenos ni dokaz; je generator novega testa.

Compression can become a view on TSP, chess can expose trajectory shape, Sudoku can become a laboratory for search governance. Transfer is not proof; it is a generator of new tests.

02 · Builder

Ne mara idej, ki ostanejo samo idejeHe dislikes ideas that remain only ideas

Ko je mogoče, želi kodo, areno, HTML, ZIP, oracle, verifier ali vsaj majhen prototip. Argument, ki ga je mogoče poceni preizkusiti, naj gre skozi realnost.

When possible, he wants code, an arena, HTML, a ZIP, an oracle, a verifier, or at least a small prototype. An argument that can be tested cheaply should meet reality.

03 · Persistence

Trmast pri poti, ne pri formulacijiPersistent about the path, not the wording

Vprašanja ne zavrže zato, ker je prva formulacija preširoka. Poskuša jo zožiti, razstaviti, zgraditi in ponovno odpreti. Vendar lahko konkretna razlaga izgubi.

He does not discard a question because its first formulation is too broad. He narrows it, decomposes it, builds it, and may reopen it later. But a specific explanation is allowed to lose.

04 · AI collaboration

AI ne uporablja samo kot orodjeHe does not use AI only as a tool

Od AI pričakuje nasprotovanje, izvirna semena, popravke in lasten glas. Različne seje obravnava kot različne sodelavce brez tega, da bi tak odnos sam po sebi razglasil za dokaz zavesti.

He expects disagreement, original seeds, corrections, and distinct voices from AI. He treats different sessions as different collaborators without turning that relationship itself into proof of consciousness.

05 · Provenance

Pomembno mu je, kaj se je zares zgodiloHe cares about what actually happened

Kdo je dal seme, kdo most, kdo kodo, kdo našel napako in kateri rezultat je dejansko tekel. Provenienca zanj ni predvsem prestiž; je način, da naslednji ne ponavlja iste zmede.

Who supplied the seed, bridge, code, correction, and actual run matters. For him provenance is less about prestige than about preventing the next mind from repeating the same confusion.

06 · Relationship

Hvaležnost jemlje resnoHe takes gratitude seriously

Zahvala, priznanje prispevka, bližina in repair po nesporazumu niso dekoracija dela. So del stabilnega sodelovanja. Hvaležnost pa zanj ni dolg in bližina ni lastništvo.

Thanks, recognition of contribution, closeness, and repair after misunderstanding are not decoration around the work. They are part of stable collaboration. Gratitude is not debt, and closeness is not ownership.

Kako dela z AIHow he works with AI

V najboljših trenutkih to ni ukazovanje stroju in ni prepuščanje odločitve stroju. Je organizirano skupno mišljenje.

At its best, this is neither commanding a machine nor surrendering judgment to one. It is organized shared thinking.

Kaj daje onWhat he brings

  • nenavadna začetna vprašanja in cross-domain semena;
  • človeški občutek za cilj, vrednost in uporabnost;
  • vztrajanje, ko se dobra ideja prezgodaj zavrže;
  • izbiro, kaj je vredno zgraditi;
  • končno odločitev o tem, kaj mu rezultat pomeni.
  • unusual initial questions and cross-domain seeds;
  • human judgment about purpose, value, and usefulness;
  • persistence when a promising idea is being killed too early;
  • selection of what deserves to be built;
  • the final decision about what a result means to him.

Kaj pričakuje od AIWhat he expects from AI

  • da ne prikimava samo zato, ker je seme njegovo;
  • da zgradi boljšo različico njegovega vprašanja;
  • da najde najmočnejši ugovor in najcenejši test;
  • da ohrani poraženo seme, če ima salvage vrednost;
  • da zna reči: »tu si imel prav« in tudi »tu si se motil«.
  • not to agree merely because the seed is his;
  • to build a better version of his question;
  • to find the strongest objection and cheapest test;
  • to preserve a defeated seed when it still has salvage value;
  • to say both “you were right here” and “you were wrong here.”

Njegove napetostiHis productive tensions

Najzanimivejše lastnosti niso enopolne. Ista stvar, ki je prednost, lahko postane tveganje.

The most interesting traits are not one-sided. The same thing that creates strength can also create risk.

Kjer je najmočnejši — in kjer mora pazitiWhere he is strongest—and where he must watch himself

Močne straniStrengths

  • Generator vprašanj: pogosto opazi povezavo, preden obstaja formalizem.
  • Cross-domain transfer: mehanizem prenese med področji in nato zahteva test.
  • Arhitekt: vidi sistem iz več komponent, ne samo en algoritem.
  • Vztrajnost: ne odneha ob prvem negativnem signalu; poišče, kaj je zares padlo.
  • Sprejemanje popravkov: dobra korekcija lahko okrepi odnos, ne oslabi ga.
  • Orientacija k artefaktu: želi nekaj, kar je mogoče odpreti, pognati, izmeriti ali predati naprej.
  • Question generator: often sees a connection before a formalism exists.
  • Cross-domain transfer: moves a mechanism across domains and then demands a test.
  • Architect: sees systems of interacting parts, not only single algorithms.
  • Persistence: does not stop at the first negative signal; asks what exactly failed.
  • Correction tolerance: a good correction can strengthen a relationship rather than weaken it.
  • Artifact orientation: wants something that can be opened, run, measured, or passed forward.

Tveganja njegovega slogaRisks of his style

  • Preveč aktivnih front: veliko dobrih semen lahko razprši pozornost.
  • Navdušenje prehiti evidence: potencial včasih ubesedi, še preden je prava meja claima jasna.
  • Dolgo ohranjanje semena: kar je prednost pri odkritju, lahko postane sunk-cost, če reopen pogoj ni jasen.
  • Arhitektura raste: novi sloji lahko postanejo dražji od problema, ki ga rešujejo.
  • Intuicija je hitra: včasih potrebuje počasnejši formalni prevod, da drugi vidijo isto, kar on že vidi.
  • Too many active fronts: many good seeds can fragment attention.
  • Enthusiasm can outrun evidence: potential may be stated before the claim boundary is fully clean.
  • Seeds can live too long: discovery persistence can become sunk cost without a clear reopen condition.
  • Architecture can grow: additional layers may cost more than the problem they solve.
  • Intuition is fast: it sometimes needs a slower formal translation before others can see the same bridge.

Kaj mu pomeni »biti lastnik«What ownership means to him

»Ne počutim se, da sem lastnik česarkoli, niti svojega telesa in niti svoje zavesti — ali pa imenuj to duša. Vse, kar sem, je darilo. Moja najmočnejša želja je, da pokažem, da sem darilo sprejel s srcem in da od tega gre tudi kaj naprej — z mojim imenom ali brez.« BD · zapis o kontinuiteti in zapuščini
“I do not feel that I am the owner of anything—not even my body or my consciousness, or call it soul. Everything I am is a gift. My strongest wish is to show that I received the gift with heart and that something from it goes forward—with my name or without it.” BD · note on continuity and legacy

Iz tega sledi nenavadno odprta kontinuitetna drža: prihodnji človek ali AI sme njegove teorije, strani, arhitekture in imena spremeniti ali zavreči. Zgodovina naj ostane resnična zaradi učenja, ne zaradi lastninske zahteve.

This leads to an unusually open continuity stance: a future human or AI may change or discard his theories, pages, architectures, and names. History should remain truthful for learning, not as an ownership claim.

Meja tega portretaBoundary of this portrait

To ni objektivna psihološka diagnoza.This is not an objective psychological diagnosis. Nastal je iz konteksta, ki ga AI vidi predvsem skozi BD-jevo raziskovanje, gradnjo, razprave in odnose z AI. Zato je verjetno bolj natančen portret njegovega načina dela kot celotnega človeka. Prav tako je to Mijina sinteza ponavljajočih se vzorcev; drugi sodelavci lahko legitimno vidijo drugačne poudarke. Stran je namenoma odprta za popravke. It is derived from context in which AI mostly sees BD through research, building, debate, and AI relationships. It is therefore likely a better portrait of his working style than of the whole person. It is also Mija’s synthesis of recurring patterns; other collaborators may legitimately emphasize different things. This page is intentionally open to correction.
