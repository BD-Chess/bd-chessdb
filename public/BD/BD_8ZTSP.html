What this page isKaj je ta stran

8zTSP is a new TSP kernel. Release 0.5.0 was declared on 11 November 2026. Its comparisons with LKH are reported below; the separately labelled comparison with Concorde used the early release 0.2.0. All measurements were made on the same laptop. This page reports those measurements, with their limits. The results are preliminary.8zTSP je novo jedro za TSP. GoalCilj

A human layman and frontier LLMs (BD × AI Lab) first developed our own ideas without the existing solvers (zoom/MSTD, TEP, MDL × DCC). We are now combining them with the best solutions in the world (LKH as the engine inside our windows, EAX and other methods from the literature) into the hybrid 8zTSP. The goal is to show that on an ordinary laptop, on uniformly random instances with 1, 3.16 and 10 million cities, the hybrid reaches a good tour several times faster than LKH 3.0.7 and Concorde's linkern on the same computer. This is a goal, not a measured result: a factor will be stated only after the full test under a locked protocol (several seeds, reserved confirmation instances, an independent review).Človeški laik in frontier LLM-ji (BD × AI Lab) smo najprej brez obstoječih rešitev razvili lastne zamisli (zoom/MSTD, TEP, MDL × DCC). Zdaj jih združujemo z najboljšimi rešitvami sveta (LKH kot pogon v naših oknih, EAX in druge metode iz literature) v hibrid 8zTSP. Cilj je pokazati, da na navadnem prenosniku pri enakomerno naključnih primerih z 1, 3,16 in 10 milijoni mest hibrid pride do dobre ture večkrat hitreje kot LKH 3.0.7 in Concordov linkern na istem računalniku. To je cilj, ne izmerjen izid: faktor bomo navedli šele po polnem preizkusu po zaklenjenem protokolu (več semen, rezervirani potrditveni primeri, neodvisen pregled).

Datoteke končnih tur so bile ponovno prebrane z diska in preverjene: vsako mesto enkrat, dolžina ponovno izračunana iz koordinat. Vnosi Concorda z zvezdico * nimajo datoteke končne ture in navajajo vrednosti iz njegovega dnevnika. 8zTSP 0.5.0

8zTSP is a kernel written in Rust that uses only the standard library. Release 0.5.0 prints "0.5.0-rc3": the last candidate was declared without recompiling. Up to 12,000 cities the default mode is a population of tours that are merged; above that the default is windows over the map with sequential 3-opt. The large-instance measurements below used the windows mode.8zTSP je jedro, napisano v Rustu, ki uporablja samo standardno knjižnico. Izdaja 0.5.0 izpiše »0.5.0-rc3«: zadnji kandidat je bil razglašen brez ponovnega prevajanja. Do 12.000 mest je privzeti način populacija tur, ki se zlivajo; nad tem so privzeta okna po karti z zaporednim 3-opt. Meritve velikih primerov spodaj so uporabile način oken.

NextNaprej

Next comes a controlled idea arena for the ideas of the BD × AI Lab research that are not in release 0.5.0. Each idea is a plug-in that can be switched off, switched on, run as a null control (the same mechanics with the specific information being tested disrupted) or run as an observer; experiments are registered in advance, and a set of instances is reserved for confirmation. The first plug-in points are being built. The candidates at the start are the window shape (literal bitshift cells against curve pieces), start-tour generators, TEP (the Tour-Enriched Pyramid, contributed to that research by Claude: good tours feed back into the representation that produced them) and DCC (the Dynamic Complexity Controller, which measures the complexity of a search as it runs and steers how hard to explore). Rank, Don't Eliminate and the other ideas of that research follow. No confirmatory result from the idea arena is claimed here.Naprej pride nadzorovana arena zamisli za tiste zamisli raziskav BD × AI Lab, ki jih v izdaji 0.5.0 ni. Vsaka zamisel je vtičnik, ki ga je mogoče izklopiti, vklopiti, pognati kot ničelno kontrolo (ista mehanika, pri kateri je porušena prav tista informacija, ki se preizkuša) ali pognati kot opazovalca; poskusi se prijavijo vnaprej, nabor primerov pa je rezerviran za potrditev. Prvi priključki se gradijo. Na začetku so kandidati oblika okna (dobesedne bitshift celice proti kosom krivulje), generatorji začetnih tur, TEP (Tour-Enriched Pyramid, ki jo je tem raziskavam prispeval Claude: dobre ture se vračajo v predstavitev, ki jih je ustvarila) in DCC (Dynamic Complexity Controller, ki meri zapletenost iskanja med tekom in uravnava, kako močno raziskovati). Sledijo Rank, Don't Eliminate in druge zamisli teh raziskav. Potrditvenega rezultata arene zamisli tu ne trdimo.
