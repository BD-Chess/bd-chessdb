GoalCilj

A human layman and frontier LLMs (BD × AI Lab) first developed our own ideas without the existing solvers (zoom/MSTD, TEP, MDL × DCC). We are now combining them with the best solutions in the world (LKH as the local search inside our windows, EAX and other methods from the literature) into the hybrid 8zTSP. The goal is to show that on an ordinary laptop, on uniformly random instances with 1, 3.16 and 10 million cities, the hybrid reaches a good tour several times faster than LKH 3.0.7 and Concorde's linkern on the same computer. This is a goal, not a measured result: a factor will be stated only after the full test under a locked protocol (several seeds, reserved confirmation instances, an independent review).Človeški laik in frontier LLM-ji (BD × AI Lab) smo najprej brez obstoječih rešitev razvili lastne zamisli (zoom/MSTD, TEP, MDL × DCC). 8zTSP is a kernel written in Rust that uses only the standard library. Release 0.5.0 prints "0.5.0-rc3": the last candidate was declared without recompiling. Up to 12,000 cities the default mode is a population of tours that are merged; above that the default is windows over the map with sequential 3-opt. How it was built and checkedKako je nastalo in bilo preverjeno

  • Six rounds of independent review by a second AI session (a GPT model) of frozen releases and candidates: 0.2.0, 0.3.0-rc1, the windows mode, 0.4.0-rc1, 0.5.0-rc1 and 0.5.0-rc3. The reviewer is not a human referee.Šest krogov neodvisnega pregleda zamrznjenih izdaj in kandidatov, ki jih je opravila druga seja UI (model GPT): 0.2.0, 0.3.0-rc1, način oken, 0.4.0-rc1, 0.5.0-rc1 in 0.5.0-rc3. Pregledovalec ni človeški recenzent.
  • Release-blocking defects identified in the reviews were corrected, and the failure cases supplied by the reviewer were added as regression tests. The review of the final candidate recommended release for the default paths. Known limitations remain and are listed below.Napake, ki so po pregledih ovirale izdajo, so bile odpravljene, primeri odpovedi, ki jih je dal pregledovalec, pa dodani kot regresijski preizkusi.   • Examples from the first review of the pre-release kernel: city numbers in the input were discarded; a new best tour was announced even when writing the tour failed; arithmetic overflow at extreme coordinates; crashes with zero-valued numerical options. The same review checked as clean, by proof and test, the cycle decomposition, the validity of children and the segment moves (3,888 crossovers exhaustively on small instances, 23,226 moves against an independent reference, neighbours against brute force). These were bounded checks on the stated small cases, not a proof of correctness for every input.Primeri iz prvega pregleda jedra pred izdajo: številke mest iz vhoda so se zavrgle; nova najboljša tura je bila objavljena, tudi ko zapis ture ni uspel; aritmetična prekoračitev pri skrajnih koordinatah; sesutja pri številskih možnostih z vrednostjo nič.
