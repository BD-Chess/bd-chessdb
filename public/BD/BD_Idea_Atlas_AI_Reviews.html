PiX should not be presented as a current flagship project. It is a promising MDL candidate-generator hypothesis and research arena. It belongs under MDL×DCC incubators / generator-space research, not next to 8Z, TSP, DGTE/HTH, AIM³, ChessBest, or the strongest executable arenas.

PiX ne sme biti predstavljen kot trenutni flagship projekt. Je obetavna hipoteza in raziskovalna arena za MDL candidate-generator. Spada pod MDL×DCC inkubatorje / raziskavo prostora generatorjev, ne ob 8Z, TSP, DGTE/HTH, AIM³, ChessBest ali najmočnejše izvršljive arene.

Promotion rule: PiX moves upward only after repeated real-data wins against controls and a clean public reproducibility pack. Until then, it is valuable because it is disciplined research, not because it is already one of the core public pillars.
