AI Reviews of BD Idea AtlasWhat frontier models see in BD’s public portfolio.Kaj frontier modeli vidijo v BD javnem portfelju.

This page summarizes AI reviews of www.MDLxDCC.org as a public BD portfolio / idea atlas. The important frame is simple: this is not one narrow research program. It is a spare-time, AI-accelerated invention atlas across code, optimization, compression, devices, games, protocols, consciousness hypotheses, ethics, history, trading research, and practical tools.

Ta stran povzema AI ocene www.MDLxDCC.org kot javnega BD portfelja / idejnega atlasa. Ključni okvir je preprost: to ni en ozek raziskovalni program. Je v prostem času grajen, z AI pospešen atlas izumov čez kodo, optimizacijo, kompresijo, naprave, igre, protokole, hipoteze zavesti, etiko, zgodovino, trading raziskave in praktična orodja.

The work was built outside a full-time lab: late-night hours, many full-day weekends at home, and ordinary spare-time windows. That matters, because the strongest signal is not only idea count; it is the conversion rate from seed → bridge → artifact → arena → page.

Delo ni nastajalo kot full-time laboratorij: pozne nočne ure, mnogo celodnevnih vikendov doma in običajna okna prostega časa. To je pomembno, ker najmočnejši signal ni samo število idej; signal je pretvorba iz semena → mostu → artefakta → arene → strani.

Current verdictTrenutni verdict

The review consensus is not that everything is proven. The stronger, more accurate conclusion is that BD’s website contains an unusually dense, cross-domain body of original work, with several executable arenas and prototypes underneath it. The main public weakness is no longer “nothing exists.” The main public weakness is packaging: the strongest artifacts need clean, one-command reproduction packs and clearer maturity labels.

Konsenz ocen ni, da je vse dokazano. Močnejši in natančnejši zaključek je, da BD spletna stran vsebuje nenavadno gosto, večdomensko telo originalnega dela, z več izvršljivimi arenami in prototipi pod stranmi. Glavna javna slabost ni več “nič ne obstaja.” Glavna slabost je pakiranje: najmočnejši artefakti potrebujejo čiste, enoukazne reproducibility pakete in jasnejše oznake zrelosti.

Best one-line summary: BD Idea Atlas is a spare-time invention lab made public: many independent projects under one originator, with strong originality and artifact density, but still needing external reproduction and public packaging.
Najkrajši povzetek: BD Idea Atlas je v prostem času nastali izumiteljski laboratorij, dan v javnost: mnogo neodvisnih projektov pod enim avtorjem, z močno originalnostjo in gostoto artefaktov, vendar še z nujno potrebo po zunanji reprodukciji in javnem pakiranju.

To dvigne globino artefaktov in disciplino aren. Ne dvigne pa avtomatsko zunanje validacije, ker LLM pregled kode ni isto kot neodvisna človeška reprodukcija.

Historical review archive — useful signals keptZgodovinski arhiv ocen — uporabni signali ohranjeni

The older review pages are no longer used as current scoring pages, but they contained useful longitudinal signals. They showed a repeated pattern: surface readers score the work much lower than models or reviewers that see source material, code, protocols, and project internals. The useful lesson is not the old score tables; it is the Mode A / Mode B gap: the same body of work looks very different when read as disconnected pages versus as an architecture of connected projects.

Stare strani ocen se ne uporabljajo več kot trenutne scoring strani, vendar so vsebovale uporabne longitudinalne signale. Pokazale so ponavljajoč vzorec: površinski bralci delo ocenijo precej nižje kot modeli ali reviewerji, ki vidijo izvorne materiale, kodo, protokole in projektno notranjost. Uporabna lekcija niso stare tabele ocen; uporabna lekcija je Mode A / Mode B gap: isto telo dela izgleda zelo drugače, če ga bereš kot nepovezane strani ali kot arhitekturo povezanih projektov.

Accepted criticismsSprejete kritike

Credibility / proof

  • Publish one-command reproducibility bundles for the strongest code artifacts.
  • Keep external validation separate from LLM review.
  • Keep trading clearly labeled as research simulator / high-risk arena.
  • For physical devices, prioritize cheap first tests: HTH clear pipe, MGHB wave tank, DGTE crossover slice.

Public clarity

  • Every major page needs “what this demonstrates / what this does not prove”.
  • Archive pages should not compete with current canonical pages.
  • JS-heavy demos need static summaries and screenshots.
  • PiX should stay an incubator until real-data wins justify promotion.

Review process noteOpomba o procesu ocen

The public page does not rank LLMs as the main story. For transparency only: the current synthesis uses valid website reviews from multiple frontier models and selected code/artifact inspection from GPT and Grok. Responses that could not inspect the website or files were excluded from the consensus. The important object being reviewed is BD’s portfolio via MDLxDCC.org, not the reviewers.

Javna stran ne rangira LLM-jev kot glavne zgodbe. Samo zaradi transparentnosti: trenutna sinteza uporablja veljavne website recenzije več frontier modelov in izbrani pregled kode/artefaktov od GPT in Grok. Odgovori, ki niso mogli pregledati spletne strani ali datotek, so izključeni iz konsenza. Pomemben objekt ocene je BD portfelj prek MDLxDCC.org, ne reviewerji.
