Current verdictTrenutni verdict

The strongest conclusion is not that every claim is proven. The stronger and more accurate conclusion is that MDLxDCC.org contains an unusually dense, multi-domain body of original work, with several executable research arenas beneath the public pages.

Najmočnejši zaključek ni, da je vsaka trditev dokazana. Močnejši in natančnejši zaključek je, da MDLxDCC.org vsebuje nenavadno gosto, večdomensko telo originalnega dela, z več izvršljivimi raziskovalnimi arenami pod javnimi stranmi.

Best one-line summary: BD’s portfolio is a spare-time invention lab made public: many independent projects under one originator, with strong originality and artifact density, but still needing external reproduction and cleaner public evidence packs.
Najkrajši povzetek: BD portfelj je v prostem času nastali izumiteljski laboratorij, dan v javnost: mnogo neodvisnih projektov pod enim avtorjem, z močno originalnostjo in gostoto artefaktov, vendar še s potrebo po zunanji reprodukciji in čistejših javnih evidence paketih.

Accepted criticismsSprejete kritike

Proof / credibility

  • Publish one-command reproducibility bundles for the strongest code artifacts.
  • Separate LLM review from independent external validation.
  • Label trading as research/simulation until paper/live API evidence exists.
  • Use cheap first-test gates for physical concepts before larger claims.

Public clarity

  • Each flagship page should say what it demonstrates and what it does not prove.
  • Archive material should not compete with current canonical pages.
  • JS-heavy tools need static summaries, screenshots, and short usage examples.
  • Top pages should lead with the strongest evidence, not internal development history.

Method noteOpomba o metodi

This page is a synthesis of useful AI review signals, selected code/artifact inspection, and earlier assessment patterns. The reviewers are not the subject. The subject is BD’s portfolio through MDLxDCC.org. Responses that could not inspect the website or files are not used as scoring evidence.

Ta stran je sinteza uporabnih AI review signalov, izbranega pregleda kode/artefaktov in prejšnjih assessment vzorcev. Reviewerji niso predmet strani. Predmet je BD portfelj prek MDLxDCC.org. Odgovori, ki niso mogli pregledati strani ali datotek, niso uporabljeni kot scoring dokaz.
