Core operating cycle

  • Open equal rounded base quantities LONG and SHORT.
  • Manage the profitable and losing books independently.
  • First losing-side add is commonly considered near −200% ROE; later thresholds and sizes may roughly double.
  • Close rescue excess around +2% on that added clip and keep the intended core.
  • Re-anchor a badly displaced loser and use a large rescue only after stabilization.

Winner and large-rescue handling

  • Progressively protect/realize the winner around +200%, +400% and +500% ROE.
  • On a rapid confirmed continuation, add roughly 2–3× to the winning leg; retained cores may grow through cycles, for example 2 → 4 → 8 SOL.
  • A stabilized SOL rescue may add 10–20 SOL, then close most at +1–2% and retain about 2 SOL.
  • Delayed-add is an experimental risk governor, not yet promoted as alpha.
  • Funding, spread, slippage and liquidation risk remain even on zero-fee pairs.
