Profit lenses — how to read the dollar columns

1. Median MTM $

The cleanest single-scenario result. MTM = realized PnL + ending unrealized PnL. This avoids the trap where realized profit hides an open losing leg.

2. Σ test MTM $

Sum across all test replays/scenarios for that strategy. This often reaches thousands, but it is not a live account-equity curve because scenarios are test cases, not sequential trading days.

3. Est. $ @ $10k risk

Older v0.6.4 tables scaled strategies to a chosen risk budget. This is why you remember several-thousand-dollar results. It is useful, but only if survival and capital-at-risk are shown beside it.
