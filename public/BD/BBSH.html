+04 · Signal → intent → next-open revalidation → fill
  1. Ob phone-check closeu setup samo ustvari intent.
  2. Na naslednjem 1m open ponovno preveri: kdo je še loser, ali je setup še svež, ali je drift sprejemljiv, ali je impulse že preklical thesis, ali je account še equal-core in brez drugega rescuea.
  3. Količino ponovno izračunaj na dejanski fill ceni. Stale qty se ne sme slepo izvršiti.
  4. Če je minimum-sufficient add večji od risk/margin cap-a → NO ACTION.
  5. Če fill nastane, takoj deaktiviraj nezdružljiv winner conditional in takoj postavi exact-excess TP.
NO CHASE: če se je cena med signalom in naslednjim openom že vrnila, če loser ni več loser ali če je setup potekel, intent umre. Nova akcija zahteva nov dogodek.

Modelni fill in ročni klik nista isti dogodek

close[t] → open[t+1] je vzročni raziskovalni execution model. Ročni klik se zgodi po opazovanju, odzivu in dejanski potrditvi; naslednjega open-a ne zagotavlja. Na njem ne uporabljaj še neznanega high/low/close novega bara.

Pred potrjenim addom: ponovno preveri tudi naročila, ki so se medtem že izvršila. Nezdružljiva winner zaščita ne sme sobivati z novim rescue excessom; samo zavrnjen intent pa ne pomeni, da je treba prej veljavno zaščito tiho izbrisati.

Potrditev šteje: iz dejanskega add filla izračunaj novo povprečje in redukcijsko količino; potrdi pravilen leg, količino, ceno in aktivnost TP. Oddano naročilo ni enako potrjenemu naročilu. Model idealizira ta prehod; stran ne dokazuje atomarnosti na borzi.

+05 · Matematika Neutral-Locka: equal core · reanchor · pair trim

Za enako količino q SOL na LONG in SHORT:

PairPnL(P) = q(P − L) + q(S − P) = q(S − L)

Trenutna cena P se izniči. Če sta oba corea enako velika, samo gibanje cene ne ustvarja novega directional PnL. Če je po prejšnjih reanchorjih S > L, ima par zaklenjen pozitiven spread, neodvisen od trenutne cene.

WAIT stanjePomen
NEUTRAL_WAITEnaki količini; ni novega directional PnL iz premika cene.
ARMED_NEUTRALŠe vedno enaki količini, toda conditional order lahko samodejno spremeni stanje.
DIRECTIONAL_WAITWinner SL ali loser add se je že izvršil; količini nista več enaki in adverse tail postane pomemben.

To je idealni linearni model. Funding, slippage, mark-price liquidation, maintenance margin in execution failure niso zajeti v tej identiteti.

To je trenutno najmočnejši matematični mehanizem Reanchor Lock logike.

A = (q·P0 + d·P1) / (q + d)

Če loserju pri ceni P1 dodaš d, dobiš novi aggregate average A. Ko se retrace zgodi in zapreš samo temporary excess d, ostaneta:

q LONG  @ P0
q SHORT @ A

Njuna skupna vrednost je q(A − P0) in je neodvisna od trenutne cene.

Ključ: uspešen loser reanchor lahko velik del prejšnjega winner displacementa spremeni v zaklenjen average spread, ne da winner core zapreš in ne da izgubiš equal hedge.

Po majhnem retraceu zapreš 36-SOL excess v profitu. Ostane 4 LONG @100 + 4 SHORT @104.50, kar vsebuje približno $18 zaklenjenega pair PnL pri katerikoli poznejši ceni.

Če imaš enak core z različnima averageoma:

q LONG  @ L
q SHORT @ S, kjer S > L

in zapreš enako količino h na obeh straneh:

realized = h(P − L) + h(S − P) = h(S − L)

Tako lahko realiziraš del zaklenjenega profita brez napovedovanja smeri. Po želji lahko nato ponovno odpreš svež equal LONG+SHORT core.

Celoten cikel: kam dejansko pride dobiček?

Naj bo s=+1 za LONG rescue, s=−1 za SHORT rescue; E0 stari average loserja, Ew average nasprotnega corea, P1 dejanski add fill in T poznejša cena zapiranja. Predhodni zaklenjeni spread je B0=s·q·(Ew−E0).

A = (q·E0 + d·P1)/(q+d)
excess realized = s·d·(T−A)
locked core spread = s·q·(Ew−A)
skupaj = B0 + s·d·(T−P1)

Dodajanje samo ne ustvari profita. Ob fillu T=P1 ostane skupni PnL B0. Dobiček novega cikla prihaja iz ugodnega premika cene na dodatni količini; novi average ta rezultat le razporedi med realizacijo in preostali core spread.

+07 · Reanchor sizing in target geometrija

Če je loserjev stari gap D bp in želiš po reanchorju doseči target po ugodnem premiku R bp, z majhnim profit-lockom g velja približno:

Total book multiple M ≈ D / (R − g)    ·    add multiple ≈ M − 1

Manjši target pomeni hitrejši možni rescue, vendar eksplozivno večji add. Zato je cilj najmanjši add, ki daje razumno dosegljiv target in prestane risk budget, ne najmanjši možni retrace.

Risk po času odsotnosti

max net-excess / wallet ≈ loss_budget × 10,000 / adverse_planning_bps

v0.1.2 uporablja kot raziskovalne planning proxyje približno 110 bp (5 min), 160 bp (15 min) in 285 bp (60 min). To niso garantirani tail boundi. Daljši možni čas brez telefona mora praviloma znižati dovoljen excess.

+10 · Exact-excess harvest, locked spread in pair reset

Po loser addu pri ceni P1 se weighted average premakne proti trgu. Ko retrace doseže eksplicitni target, zapri samo excess. Originalni core ostane.

A = (q·P0 + d·P1)/(q+d)    →    po TP ostane q LONG @ P0 + q SHORT @ A

Ko sta quantity spet enaki, se price delta spet zaklene. Če je spread med averageoma dovolj velik, lahko realiziraš del profita brez smerne napovedi:

pair trim realized = h(P−L) + h(S−P) = h(S−L)
Current evidence podpira več zaporednih zaključenih ciklov: najboljši vidni STALL cell ima 14 reanchorjev, 14 exact-excess TP in 14 pair resetov ter konča kot COMPLETE_EQUAL_CORE.

Delni TP in neuspešen prehod

Ob delnem fillu zmanjšaš samo dejansko zaprt excess. Preostali d−m ostane directional; TP prilagodi na preostanek, ne znova na izvorni d. Simetrični pair trim mainline pride šele po potrjeni enakosti količin. Reduce-only opisuje omejitev zapiranja, ne obljube izvršitve.

Dvostranski trim v matematični identiteti predpostavi isto ceno za oba filla. Različni ceni dasta h·[(S−L)+(PcloseL−PcloseS)] pred stroški. Če se zapre samo ena stran, izhod začasno ni nevtralen. Vsi delni filli in neznane potrditve sodijo v stanje za uskladitev, ne v »končano«.

+14 · Celoten phone-check decision protocol

A. Najprej stanje

Če RESCUE_ACTIVE ali DIRECTIONAL_HANDOFF:
  ne odpiraj novega nepovezanega setupa.
  Upravljaj obstoječi tail / TP / repair.

Če EQUAL_CORE in brez pending orderjev:
  nadaljuj na setup gate.

B. Setup gate

NO ACTION, če:
- samo outer-BB touch ali samo reentry
- impulse še pospešuje
- ni completed large outside-BB displacementa
- ni fresh extreme + stall/reversal/selektivnega potrjevanja
- setup je zamujen
- minimum-sufficient add presega risk/margin cap

REANCHOR INTENT, če:
- completed displacement je dovolj velik
- svež extreme je prisoten
- STALL / REVERSAL / SELECTIVE gate je veljaven
- continuation veto ne zahteva zavrnitve ali manjšega sizea

C. Na naslednjem open

REVALIDATE EVERYTHING.
Če loser/setup/size/risk ne držijo več → CANCEL.
Če držijo → fill minimum-sufficient qty → takoj exact-excess TP.

D. Po TP

Če qty spet equal:
  meri locked spread.
  pair trim/reset samo, če je spread dovolj velik.
  sicer NO ACTION.
Sleep rule: za dolg nenadzorovan interval ciljaj na equal qty, brez rescue excessa, brez pending reanchorja in brez winner stopa, ki bi lahko sam ustvaril velik naked book.
BBSH1: prejšnja kartica z winner gate-om — NI trenutna mainline

Korak 1 — Je account equal-core neutral?

DA: premik trga sam ni ustvaril nove directional pair izgube. NE: najprej upravljaj obstoječi excess ali directional state; ne začenjaj nove nepovezane akcije.

Korak 2 — Winner gate

NO ACTION:
- samo outer BB touch
- samo reentry v BB
- 1m in 5m kanal še močno podpirata winner smer
- uporaben stop bi bil znotraj noise-a
- trigger bi pustil prevelik naked loser

TIGHTEN / ARM:
- profit je dovolj velik za strukturiran pozitiven stop
- 1m kanal je flatten/turn
- ali completed middle-BB close proti winner smeri
- resulting directional exposure je sprejemljiv

Preferiraj partial winner action ali neutral pair trim pred full winner-only exitom.

Korak 3 — Loser reanchor gate

NO ACTION:
- samo BB touch
- impulse še pospešuje
- setup okno je zamujeno
- minimum-sufficient add presega wallet/horizon cap
- po prejšnjem rescueu ni novega adverse displacementa

ARM, ne avtomatski fill:
- velik completed outside-BB displacement
- svež local extreme / stall / reversal structure je viden
- 1m + 5m ne zahtevata continuation veto-a za full size
- candidate size je matematično uporaben in wallet-feasible

PO FILLU:
- takoj preračunaj aggregate average
- takoj postavi partial TP za exact excess
- target naj bo explicit price, ne stale pre-add ROE
- nov add samo ob novem adverse displacementu in novem dogodku
Korak 4: če noben gate jasno ne velja → DO NOTHING.

+04 · Signal → intent → next-open revalidation → fill
  1. At the phone-check close, a setup only creates an intent.
  2. At the next 1m open, recheck who is still the loser, setup freshness, drift, impulse invalidation, equal-core state and absence of another rescue.
  3. Recompute quantity at the actual fill price. Never blindly execute stale quantity.
  4. If the minimum-sufficient add exceeds risk/margin caps → NO ACTION.
  5. If a fill occurs, disable any incompatible winner conditional and immediately place the exact-excess TP.
NO CHASE: if price already reverted before the next open, if the leg is no longer the loser, or if the setup expired, the intent dies. A new action requires a new event.

A model fill and a manual click are different events

close[t] → open[t+1] is a causal research execution model. A manual click follows observation, response and actual acknowledgement; it does not guarantee the next open. Never use the new bar’s still-unknown high/low/close at its open.

Before committing the add: recheck orders that may have filled in the meantime. Incompatible winner protection must not coexist with new rescue excess; a rejected intent alone is not a reason to silently delete previously valid protection.

Acknowledgements matter: use actual add fills to compute the average and reducing quantity; confirm the correct leg, quantity, price and active TP. Submitted is not acknowledged. The model idealizes this transition; this page does not prove exchange atomicity.

+05 · Neutral-Lock mathematics: equal core · reanchor · pair trim

For equal SOL quantity q on LONG and SHORT:

PairPnL(P) = q(P − L) + q(S − P) = q(S − L)

Current price P cancels. If quantities remain equal, price movement alone does not create new directional pair PnL. If previous reanchors produced S > L, the pair contains a positive locked spread independent of current price.

WAIT stateMeaning
NEUTRAL_WAITEqual quantities; no new directional PnL from price movement.
ARMED_NEUTRALEqual now, but a conditional order can automatically change the state.
DIRECTIONAL_WAITA winner stop or loser add has filled; quantities are unequal and adverse-tail exposure matters.

This is the ideal linear model. Funding, slippage, mark-price liquidation, maintenance margin and execution failure sit outside the invariant.

A = (q·P0 + d·P1) / (q + d)

Add d to the loser at P1, creating new aggregate average A. After a retrace, close only the temporary excess d. The remaining equal cores are:

q LONG  @ P0
q SHORT @ A

Their combined PnL is q(A − P0), independent of current price.

Key point: a successful loser reanchor can transform much of the previous winner displacement into a locked average spread without closing the winner and without abandoning the equal hedge.

With equal cores at different averages:

q LONG  @ L
q SHORT @ S, where S > L

closing the same amount h from each side realizes:

realized = h(P − L) + h(S − P) = h(S − L)

This realizes locked profit without predicting the next SOL direction. You can then optionally reopen a fresh equal LONG+SHORT core.

The whole cycle: where does profit actually come from?

Let s=+1 for a LONG rescue and s=−1 for SHORT; E0 is the old loser average, Ew the opposite-core average, P1 the actual add fill and T the later closing price. The prior locked spread is B0=s·q·(Ew−E0).

A = (q·E0 + d·P1)/(q+d)
excess realized = s·d·(T−A)
locked core spread = s·q·(Ew−A)
total = B0 + s·d·(T−P1)

The add itself creates no profit. At the fill, T=P1, total PnL remains B0. Incremental cycle profit comes from a favorable price move on the added quantity; the new average only allocates it between realization and retained core spread.

+07 · Reanchor sizing and target geometry

If the old loser gap is D bp and the post-reanchor target requires an adverse-to-favorable move of R bp with small lock g:

Total book multiple M ≈ D / (R − g)    ·    add multiple ≈ M − 1

A smaller target can make rescue faster but makes the add explode. The objective is the smallest add that gives a reasonably reachable target and passes the risk budget, not the smallest possible retrace.

Risk by unattended horizon

max net-excess / wallet ≈ loss_budget × 10,000 / adverse_planning_bps

v0.1.2 uses research planning proxies around 110 bp (5 min), 160 bp (15 min), and 285 bp (60 min). These are not guaranteed tail bounds. A longer unattended horizon should generally reduce permitted excess.

Current live leaders at W250k are not a W250k recommendation, and G5/G10/G20 labels are not net leverage. The live summary ranks absolute PnL and therefore favors large wallets. The final wallet frontier is still unknown.

+10 · Exact-excess harvest, locked spread and pair reset

After adding to the loser at P1, weighted average moves toward market. When retrace reaches the explicit target, close only the excess. Original core stays.

A = (q·P0 + d·P1)/(q+d)    →    after TP: q LONG @ P0 + q SHORT @ A

Once quantities are equal again, price delta re-locks. If spread between averages is large enough, realize part of it without directional prediction:

pair trim realized = h(P−L) + h(S−P) = h(S−L)
Current evidence includes repeated completed cycles: the strongest visible STALL cell has 14 reanchors, 14 exact-excess TPs and 14 pair resets, ending COMPLETE_EQUAL_CORE.

Partial TP and failed transitions

A partial fill reduces only the quantity actually closed. Remaining d−m is still directional; amend the TP to the remainder rather than the original d. Mainline symmetric trim follows confirmed equality. Reduce-only describes a closing constraint, not a fill guarantee.

The symmetric-trim identity assumes both legs fill at the same price. Different fill prices yield h·[(S−L)+(PcloseL−PcloseS)] before costs. If only one side closes, the exit is temporarily non-neutral. Partial fills and unknown acknowledgements require reconciliation, not a “complete” label.

A. State first

If RESCUE_ACTIVE or DIRECTIONAL_HANDOFF:
  do not start a new unrelated setup.
  Manage existing tail / TP / repair first.

If EQUAL_CORE and no pending orders:
  proceed to the setup gate.

B. Setup gate

NO ACTION if:
- only outer-BB touch or only reentry
- impulse is still accelerating
- no completed large outside-BB displacement
- no fresh extreme + stall/reversal/selective confirmation
- setup was missed
- minimum-sufficient add exceeds risk/margin cap

REANCHOR INTENT if:
- completed displacement is large enough
- fresh extreme exists
- STALL / REVERSAL / SELECTIVE gate is valid
- continuation veto does not require rejection or smaller size

C. At the next open

REVALIDATE EVERYTHING.
If loser/setup/size/risk no longer hold → CANCEL.
If they hold → fill minimum-sufficient quantity → immediately place exact-excess TP.

D. After TP

If quantity is equal again:
  measure locked spread.
  pair trim/reset only when spread is large enough.
  otherwise NO ACTION.
Sleep rule: for a long unattended interval, aim for equal quantities, no rescue excess, no pending reanchor and no winner stop that can autonomously create a large naked book.
