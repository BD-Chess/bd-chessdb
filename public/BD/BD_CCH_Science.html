Chapter 1

Document Role & Research Question

Three pages, three different burdens

AC_01_Flagship is the canonical ontological proposal. CFH records the personal origin and speculative AC/CF bridge. This CCH page owns only the operational question: which local brain dynamics and control mechanisms can be defined, measured, perturbed, and falsified?

The Claustrum–Consciousness Hypothesis (CCH) proposes that ordinary conscious brain regimes may depend on a controlled, multi-dimensional dynamical region in which large-scale integration, differentiation, temporal organization, and adaptive regulation coexist.

The current prototype compresses part of that state into a candidate summary statistic, S*. S* is not consciousness, not a quantity of Consciousness Field, and not a personhood score. It is a candidate signal-processing proxy whose value must be earned against simpler baselines and held-out data.

CCH further proposes that the brain may require active distributed regulation to remain inside a viable region rather than collapsing into overly rigid or overly fragmented dynamics. The claustrum is one biologically plausible modulator within that wider control architecture — not a proven seat, receiver, or sole controller of consciousness.

Why This Matters

Complexity alone is not the claim. A random process can be complex. A seizure can be highly synchronized. A large model can be computationally elaborate without being conscious. CCH asks a narrower construction question: is there a measurable controlled regime, and does active regulation causally help sustain it?

From Description to Blueprint

The page now separates the proposed plant, state variables, sensors, controller candidates, actuators, disturbances, outcomes, baselines, and falsifiers. The included code is a prototype for smoke tests and preregistration. It is not a validated biomarker and not a construction plan for consciousness.

Ontology-Neutral Science Boundary

CCH can be tested by a physicalist, an emergentist, or someone who prefers AC/CFH. It contains no external receiver variable and does not model consciousness as a signal arriving from elsewhere. Under AC/CFH, every system is already within the proposed ground; CCH would still study only the local organization and regulation associated with bounded relative experience.

What CCH does not claim

Not complexity alone: high complexity is neither sufficient nor treated as evidence of consciousness.

Not a receiver theory: the brain is not modeled as a radio, antenna, or portal through which an external field arrives.

Not claustrum supremacy: the claustrum is one candidate hub inside a potentially distributed controller.

Not S = consciousness: S* is a candidate summary of measured dynamics, not phenomenal intensity, moral worth, personhood, or standing.

Not artificial consciousness: a DCC-like controller improving an AI system would establish an engineering result, not awareness.

Not proof of CFH: success of CCH would not establish AC/CF; failure would reject or revise this bridge, not logically settle every possible ontology.

Chapter 2

Four Testable Hypotheses

CCH is organized as four separable hypotheses. Each may survive or fail independently.

H1
Controlled Regime Hypothesis

Conscious brain states may require a joint region of integration and differentiation, maintained over time. The primary object should be a state vector, not a single magic number:

X(t) = [Cn(t), ERn(t), SI(t)]
Coherence × spatial diversity × structured temporal complexity

A candidate compression of that vector is:

S* = k · Cn · Ψ(I),   Ψ(I)=ERn·SI
Prototype summary statistic — not a consciousness quantity
H2
Active Regulation Hypothesis

CCH predicts that robust maintenance of the proposed region requires adaptive feedback under changing conditions. This is not assumed as fact. It must be tested against passive recurrent dynamics, fixed controllers, and simpler homeostatic baselines under matched disturbance and compute budgets.

H3
Claustrum Modulation Hypothesis

The claustrum is proposed as one candidate modulator of large-scale cortical coordination, variability, attention, and state transitions. Human stimulation evidence is mixed, while recent animal work supports a role in modulating prefrontal and cortex-wide dynamics. CCH therefore predicts a measurable contribution, not sole ownership of consciousness.

H4
Engineering Analogue — DCC-like Governance

Artificial systems can test an analogous control principle: monitor task-relevant coherence, diversity, stability, and failure modes; then adapt routing, coupling, budget, memory, or exploration. A positive result would show better sustained adaptive cognition than matched fixed or episodic baselines. It would not by itself show consciousness, intrinsic agency, or coupling to CF.

Chapter 3

Measured State and Candidate S* Summary

X(t)=[Cn, ERn, SI]    ;    S*=k·Cn·(ERn·SI)
Use X(t) for interpretation; use S* only as a preregistered candidate compression.
Do not maximize S*

Different dynamical states can share the same product. A scalar may hide whether a change came from coherence, diversity, or temporal structure. The controller target is therefore a viable region in X-space, not an unconditional maximum of S*.

Cn — Normalized Coherence / Integration Proxy

Cn is a candidate summary of cross-channel phase coordination in a declared frequency band. The exact estimator, channel set, reference, surrogate null, and frequency band must be frozen before evaluation. Pairwise PLV, global phase consistency, and alternative connectivity measures should compete rather than be treated as interchangeable.

Cn = normalize(connectivity - surrogate baseline)
Prototype range [0,1]; estimator and null model are part of the contract.

Ψ(I) — Differentiation / Structured Complexity Proxy

The current prototype combines two distinct features:

Spatial Diversity: ERn

Normalized effective rank estimates how many independent dimensions are expressed across the measured channels. It is sensitive to channel count, preprocessing, reference choice, noise, and sample size; these dependencies must be controlled.

Temporal Structure: SI

SI = exp(−(LZCn − μcal)² / 2σcal²)
Prototype proximity to a calibration target, not an established biological optimum.

μcal and σcal must be estimated only from a frozen calibration set. They must not be selected from the held-out test labels or altered after outcomes are known.

The Soft-Conjunction Metaphor

The multiplicative S* formula encodes a useful engineering intuition: a system should not receive a high summary score when one required dimension collapses. This is a candidate soft conjunction, not proof that the listed factors are necessary and sufficient for experience.

Adaptive Feedback Architecture

A candidate CCC performs four steps:

1. Sense: estimate X(t) with uncertainty.
2. Compare: determine distance from a preregistered viable region ΩCCH.
3. Act: modulate one or more lawful control channels.
4. Verify: test whether the intervention improves the predicted neural and behavioral outcomes relative to matched controls.

The controller hypothesis is false or over-scoped if passive recurrent organization, a simpler fixed controller, or a different distributed network explains the same outcomes as well or better.

Why Keep the Claustrum in the Model?

The claustrum has dense, structured cortical connectivity and can modulate prefrontal activity and large-scale functional relationships. That makes it a plausible component of a control architecture. But the causal evidence does not justify calling it the seat or sole controller of consciousness.

Mixed human stimulation evidence

A 2014 case reported reversible unresponsiveness and amnesia during stimulation near a claustral electrode. A 2019 study of direct, including bilateral, claustrum stimulation did not evoke loss of consciousness. CCH must preserve both results and design tests that distinguish local spread, patient-specific factors, and actual claustrum function.

Recent animal studies support a narrower statement: claustrum manipulation can alter prefrontal activity, long-range functional connectivity, and neural variability. These findings motivate a modulation hypothesis; they do not prove a consciousness controller.

Distributed Alternatives Must Compete

Claustrum-centered models must be compared with thalamocortical, frontoparietal, salience/executive, brainstem/neuromodulatory, and interoceptive/homeostatic control models. CCH should promote whichever architecture best predicts and survives intervention data.

Productive-Corridor Hypothesis

The phrase edge of chaos is retained as a motivating dynamical hypothesis: useful cognition may occupy a region between rigid synchronization and unstructured variability. It is not treated as a universal law of consciousness. The region must be defined by measurable state variables and validated outcomes, not by metaphor alone.

No receiver mechanism

Nothing in this blueprint transmits consciousness into the brain. CCH models local dynamics and control. AC/CFH may interpret why such dynamics are phenomenally present, but that ontological question is not solved by the controller.

Chapter 5

Predictions, Baselines & Falsifiability

P1 — State Sensitivity smoke-test ready

On subject-held-out sleep data, X(t) and S* should discriminate declared physiological states better than preregistered simple spectral, entropy, and single-feature baselines.

Boundary: Sleep-stage classification is not the same as measuring consciousness. Sleep-EDF has only two EEG channels, so it cannot strongly test large-scale integration; it is an initial pipeline and calibration test.

P2 — Cross-Condition Generalization

A metric trained or calibrated on one condition should retain predictive value across held-out subjects and at least one distinct regime such as anesthesia, recovery, or another recording dataset. Failure to transfer counts against the general marker claim.

P3 — Causal Controller Signature

Pre-registered perturbation of a candidate controller should produce a predicted change in X(t), network organization, and behavior that differs from control-region stimulation and from nonspecific arousal effects. Claustrum, thalamic, and distributed-network hypotheses must be compared.

P4 — Active vs. Passive Regulation

Under matched disturbances, an adaptive controller should maintain the target state region and task-relevant performance more robustly than the same plant with no controller, a fixed controller, or a simpler homeostatic rule.

P5 — DCC-like AI Engineering Test

Using identical models, tools, tasks, and total budgets, DCC-like governance should improve sustained research performance, stability, or recovery over episodic and fixed-workflow baselines. This is an engineering prediction only.

What Would Falsify or Reduce CCH?

Fail conditions by layer

Metric layer: X/S* fails held-out generalization, is unstable under reasonable preprocessing, or adds no value beyond simpler baselines.

Control layer: active feedback does not outperform passive or simpler fixed organization under matched disturbances.

Claustrum layer: claustrum perturbation produces no distinctive predictive effect, or another distributed model explains the evidence better.

AI layer: DCC-like governance adds complexity, tokens, or human work without better validated outcomes.

Scientific discipline: definitions, target regions, or thresholds are repeatedly changed after results are known.

Partial failure is allowed

The scalar S* may fail while a multi-dimensional X(t) model survives. The claustrum-specific hypothesis may fail while distributed active control remains viable. CCH must not protect the full package by moving every failed claim into a vaguer formulation.

Prototype code status

The code below is preserved as a transparent historical prototype. It requires independent software audit, frozen preprocessing, subject-wise splits, stronger baselines, uncertainty reporting, and richer recordings before any biological claim.

import numpy as np
from scipy.signal import hilbert, butter, filtfilt

def bandpass_filter(data, lowcut, highcut, fs, order=4):
    """Zero-phase Butterworth Bandpass filter."""
    nyquist = 0.5 * fs
    low = lowcut / nyquist
    high = highcut / nyquist
    b, a = butter(order, [low, high], btype='band')
    return filtfilt(b, a, data, axis=1)

def lempel_ziv_complexity(binary_sequence):
    """Standard LZC implementation."""
    n = len(binary_sequence)
    c = 1; l = 1; i = 0; k = 1; k_max = 1
    while True:
        if binary_sequence[i + k - 1] == binary_sequence[l + k - 1]:
            k += 1
            if l + k > n:
                c += 1; break
        else:
            if k > k_max: k_max = k
            i += 1
            if i == l:
                c += 1; l += k_max
                if l + 1 > n: break
                else: i = 0; k = 1; k_max = 1
            else: k = 1
    return c

def compute_S_v1_3(epoch, fs, target_lzc, tolerance,
                    noise_floor=0.1, k=100):
    """Computes S = k * C_n * (ER_n * SI)"""
    n_channels, n_samples = epoch.shape

    # --- A. Coherence (C_n) ---
    filtered = bandpass_filter(epoch, 8, 12, fs)
    analytic = hilbert(filtered, axis=1)
    phase = np.angle(analytic)
    plv = np.mean(np.abs(np.mean(np.exp(1j * phase), axis=0)))
    C_n = np.clip((plv - noise_floor) / (1.0 - noise_floor), 0, 1)

    # --- B. Effective Rank (ER_n) ---
    cov = np.cov(epoch)
    evals = np.linalg.svd(cov, compute_uv=False)
    p = evals / np.sum(evals)
    p = p[p > 1e-10]
    entropy = -np.sum(p * np.log(p))
    ER_n = (np.exp(entropy) - 1) / (n_channels - 1)
    ER_n = np.clip(ER_n, 0, 1)

    # --- C. Structural Integrity (SI) ---
    medians = np.median(epoch, axis=1, keepdims=True)
    binary = (epoch > medians).astype(int)
    lzc_sum = sum(lempel_ziv_complexity(binary[ch,:])
                  for ch in range(n_channels))
    norm_factor = n_channels * (n_samples / np.log2(n_samples))
    lzc_norm = lzc_sum / norm_factor
    SI = np.exp(-(lzc_norm - target_lzc)**2
                / (2 * tolerance**2))
    SI = np.clip(SI, 0, 1)

    # --- D. Final S ---
    Psi = ER_n * SI
    S = k * C_n * Psi
    return S

DCC-like Toy Controller

A PI-style controller modulates a toy coupled-oscillator system. Its purpose is to test whether feedback can stabilize a declared dynamical corridor under disturbance. It is not a model of phenomenal experience and should be compared with no-control and simpler fixed-control baselines.

class DigitalClaustrum:
    """
    Monitors LZC of the system.
    If LZC drops (Seizure) → inject noise.
    If LZC spikes (Chaos)  → increase coupling.
    """
    def __init__(self, kp=3.0, ki=0.5, tolerance=0.03):
        self.kp = kp
        self.ki = ki
        self.tolerance = tolerance
        self.target_complexity = None
        self.error_integral = 0.0
        self.coupling_gain = 2.0
        self.noise_injection = 0.5

    def update(self, state):
        current = self._complexity_proxy(state)
        error = current - self.target_complexity
        self.error_integral += error
        # PI control
        self.coupling_gain -= self.kp * error \
                            + self.ki * self.error_integral
        self.coupling_gain = float(
            np.clip(self.coupling_gain, 0.0, 10.0))

        if error < -self.tolerance:
            self.noise_injection = min(
                5.0, self.noise_injection + 0.2)
            action = "DESYNC (Seizure risk)"
        elif error > self.tolerance:
            self.noise_injection = max(
                0.0, self.noise_injection - 0.2)
            action = "SYNC (Noise risk)"
        else:
            action = "Hold"
        return self.coupling_gain, \
               self.noise_injection, current, action

Roadmap, Claim Ladder & Limitations

Completed as research scaffolding done

Defined the X/S* prototype; created transparent Python and synthetic fixtures; built a toy DCC-like controller; separated CCH from CFH and artificial-consciousness claims.

Immediate evidence work next

Freeze preprocessing and baselines; audit the code; run subject-held-out Sleep-EDF smoke tests; report all X components; test sensitivity to estimator, channel count, band, and calibration choices; publish negative results.

Causal and richer-data program future

Use high-density EEG/MEG or intracranial data; compare sleep, anesthesia, recovery, and task states; collaborate on preregistered claustrum and competing-network perturbation studies; compare against strong clinical and computational baselines.

AI8 / DCC engineering program separate evidence lane

Test whether DCC-like software governance improves persistent research loops, stability, recovery, and innovation over matched episodic and fixed-workflow systems. Keep this evidence separate from biological consciousness and CFH.

Current Empirical Caveats

No large-scale held-out CCH result is established on this page. Sleep-EDF is a limited-channel dataset. The synthetic fixtures are metric unit tests. The DCC code is a toy controller. The claustrum evidence is mixed and does not establish necessity or sufficiency.

Mechanism and Ontology Must Not Borrow Proof

A successful CCH result would support a local dynamical or control model. It would remain compatible with several ontologies and would not by itself confirm CFH. Failure of CCH would reject or revise this particular bridge; it would not logically disprove every AC/CF proposal.

Claim ladder

L0: code runs and synthetic fixtures behave as designed.

L1: X/S* generalizes on held-out physiological data beyond simple baselines.

L2: active control has a causal advantage and a reproducible biological implementation.

L3: claustrum contributes distinctively within a distributed controller.

L4: engineering analogues improve AI cognition.

Not implied: phenomenal consciousness, CFH, intrinsic agency, or personhood.

Reference

Glossary of Key Terms

X(t) — CCH State Vector

The measured multi-dimensional state used for interpretation. The current prototype includes normalized coherence Cn, effective-rank diversity ERn, and structural-integrity proxy SI.

S* — Candidate Summary Statistic

S* = k · Cn · (ERn · SI). A prototype compression of X(t), not a quantity of consciousness, CF, worth, or personhood.

Cn — Normalized Coherence Proxy

A preregistered estimator of cross-channel coordination relative to a declared null model. It is not identical to unity of experience.

ERn — Normalized Effective Rank

A channel- and preprocessing-dependent estimate of spatial dimensionality or diversity.

SI — Structural Integrity Proxy

A prototype measure of proximity to a calibration target in temporal complexity. The target is empirical and may fail.

CCC — Coherence-Complexity Control Function

A hypothesized adaptive feedback function that senses a neural state, compares it with a viable region, acts through lawful control channels, and verifies the consequence. It may be distributed.

Claustrum

One candidate hub that may modulate cortical activity, connectivity, variability, attention, or state transitions. It is not established as the seat, receiver, or sole controller of consciousness.

Digital Claustrum / DCC-like Controller

An engineering analogue that regulates an artificial system’s internal search or dynamical regime. A performance gain would not establish phenomenal consciousness.

Correlated-Noise Stress Test

A synthetic unit test formerly called the “Smart Zombie.” It probes whether the current scalar is fooled by shared-source synchrony. It is not a test of philosophical zombies.

Productive Corridor / Edge-of-Chaos Hypothesis

The proposal that useful dynamics occupy a bounded region between excessive rigidity and unstructured variability. It is a testable dynamical hypothesis, not an established law of consciousness.

R — AC/CFH Resonance / Organization Condition

An ontological or bridge term defined in the AC/CFH documents. R is not S*, and CCH does not operationalize an external receiver or field-transmission mechanism.
