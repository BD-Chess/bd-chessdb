Scout first, Advisor later

The problem

Problem

Routing data is noisy. The hard question is often not “is there an alert?” but “which ASN/prefix/window deserves expert attention first?”

Routing podatki so šumni. Težko vprašanje pogosto ni “ali obstaja alert?”, ampak “kateri ASN/prefix/časovno okno naj strokovnjak pogleda najprej?”

The Scout layer

Scout sloj

Scout builds stable review cases from public BGP/routing evidence: repeated, parsed-first, source-quality-aware, and safe to show to a human reviewer.

Scout iz javnih BGP/routing dokazov zgradi stabilne review case-e: ponovljive, parsed-first, source-quality-aware in varne za človeški pregled.

Current decision

Trenutna odločitev

Do not jump to Advisor yet. First make Scout “tip-top”: stronger top-case view, better casebook, clearer metadata, and easier Jan/operator review.

Še ne skočiti na Advisor. Najprej naredimo Scout “tip-top”: boljši top-case pogled, casebook, jasnejši metapodatki in lažji Jan/operator review.

Boundary: RouteSignal does not configure routers, does not issue production routing advice, and does not classify an event as attack, hijack, leak, outage, or misconfiguration. It produces a review queue, not a verdict.

Meja: RouteSignal ne konfigurira routerjev, ne daje produkcijskih routing navodil in ne razglasi dogodka kot attack, hijack, leak, outage ali misconfiguration. Rezultat je review queue, ne razsodba.

Plain-language explanation for non-specialistsPoljudna razlaga za ne-specialiste

RouteSignal is not a speed booster. It does not change traffic. Its possible value is indirect: if it helps operators find routing oddities faster, then real problems may be corrected sooner.

RouteSignal ni “speed booster”. Ne spreminja prometa. Njegova možna vrednost je posredna: če operaterjem pomaga hitreje najti routing čudnosti, se lahko realne težave prej popravijo.

For ordinary users that could eventually mean fewer long slowdowns, fewer strange reachability issues, and faster recovery from routing problems — but only after operator validation and better data coverage.

Za navadne uporabnike bi to lahko sčasoma pomenilo manj dolgih upočasnitev, manj čudnih težav z dosegljivostjo in hitrejše okrevanje po routing problemih — ampak šele po operaterski validaciji in boljši pokritosti podatkov.
