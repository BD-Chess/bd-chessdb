What is 8Z Shield?

One tool to protect, read, and trace your digital content. No server, no account, no dependencies. Just a browser and a passphrase. Everything runs locally — nothing leaves your machine.

Protecting your files (3 steps)

  1. Drop files into the box below — .html, .js, .css files, or a .zip
  2. Review findings — the scanner identifies API keys, algorithms, and sensitive code. Check what to protect
  3. Set a passphrase and click Shield. You get an encrypted .8zv bundle plus a trace manifest

Reading protected content

Drop any .8zv file into the box. It appears on your bookshelf. Click it, enter the passphrase, view full-screen. Your library lives as long as the tab is open.

Tracing a leak

Click Trace in the top right. Load the .trace.json manifest, paste the leaked content, click Analyze. 4 forensic channels identify who leaked it.

Download options

After shielding, download either a single .8zv bundle (one file with everything), or the individual encrypted HTML files separately — useful for hosting each page as a standalone protected file.
