How BD works with AIKako BD dela z AI

The recurring production loop.Ponavljajoči produkcijski krog.

seed → bridge → AI build → arena → result → page

This is the core workflow behind the Atlas. BD provides the seed, direction, taste, cross-domain transfer, and continuity. AI systems help build, test, write, refactor, score, and challenge. The output is not just text; it becomes code, demos, protocols, papers, tables, arenas, and pages.

To je jedrni workflow za Atlasom. BD daje seme, smer, okus, cross-domain transfer in kontinuiteto. AI sistemi pomagajo graditi, testirati, pisati, refaktorirati, ocenjevati in izzivati. Output ni samo tekst; postane koda, demosi, protokoli, papirji, tabele, arene in strani.
