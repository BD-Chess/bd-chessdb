8zTSP in your browser8zTSP v vašem brskalniku

Our TSP kernel 8zTSP in its minimal build, 8zTSP-min: the same Rust code, compiled to WebAssembly and running on your computer. Press Run and watch it look for the shortest round trip it can find through nu3496, 3,496 cities in Nicaragua whose optimal tour (96,132) is known, through random points, or through your own TSPLIB file.Naše jedro za TSP 8zTSP v minimalni gradnji, 8zTSP-min: ista koda v Rustu, prevedena v WebAssembly, ki teče na vašem računalniku. Pritisnite Zaženi in opazujte, kako išče čim krajšo krožno pot skozi nu3496, 3.496 mest v Nikaragvi, katerih optimalna pot (96.132) je znana, skozi naključne točke ali skozi vašo datoteko TSPLIB.

  • Speed depends on your computer and browser. On our laptop, in Chromium, one core reached the nu3496 optimum in about 40 seconds (one pilot run, 14 Nov 2026).Hitrost je odvisna od vašega računalnika in brskalnika. Na našem prenosniku je v Chromiumu eno jedro optimum nu3496 doseglo v okoli 40 sekundah (en pilotni tek, 14. nov. 2026).
  • Several workers are independent tries. Each has its own seed, and the best tour counts. One population shared by all workers, as the native threads share it (about 10 seconds to the nu3496 optimum with 7 threads on our laptop), is in development.Več delavcev pomeni neodvisne poskuse. Vsak ima svoje seme, šteje najboljša pot. Ena populacija, ki si jo delijo vsi delavci, kot si jo delijo nativne niti (s 7 nitmi na našem prenosniku okoli 10 sekund do optimuma nu3496), je v razvoju.
  • Everything stays on your device. The search runs in your browser; the files you open are not uploaded anywhere.Vse ostane na vaši napravi. Iskanje teče v vašem brskalniku; datoteke, ki jih odprete, se ne pošiljajo nikamor.

Who made itKdo je to naredil

BD × AI Lab. The idea of 8zTSP-min and 8zTSP-web, to switch the parts off one by one, keep what carries the results and take it to the web, is BD’s. Inside: our windows, from zoom in and out, BD’s idea, first built as MSTD by Claude (C) on 22 March 2026, later implemented by GPT as the integer bitshift pyramid; TEP, the Tour-Enriched Pyramid, by Claude C1 in a session BD prepared free of old TSP knowledge, after seeing BD’s zoom in and out; and known methods such as the edge assembly crossover (EAX) of Nagata and Kobayashi. The browser version was built by a GPT session (8zTSP-web) from a brief by 8zTSP-K, and 8zTSP-K reviewed it; this page by the session 8zTSP-page (Claude).BD × AI Lab. Zamisel 8zTSP-min in 8zTSP-web, da dele izklapljamo enega za drugim, obdržimo tisto, kar nosi izide, in to prenesemo na splet, je BD‑jeva. V jedru: naša okna iz zooma noter in ven, BD‑jeve zamisli, ki jo je 22. marca 2026 kot MSTD prvi izvedel Claude (C), pozneje pa GPT kot celoštevilsko bitshift piramido; TEP, Tour-Enriched Pyramid (piramida, obogatena s turami), Claude C1 v seji, ki jo je BD odtujil od okov starega znanja o TSP, ko je videla BD‑jev zoom noter in ven; ter znane metode, na primer križanje s sestavljanjem robov (EAX) Nagate in Kobayashija. Različico za brskalnik je po nalogi seje 8zTSP-K zgradila seja GPT (8zTSP-web), 8zTSP-K jo je pregledala; to stran je pripravila seja 8zTSP-page (Claude).

What runs here, and the limitsKaj tu teče in meje

The build. 8zTSP-min from kernel 673813b (main 9f7670a), compiled to WebAssembly: tsp8z_web_bg.wasm, 406,333 bytes (sha256 aa1b60a4…), with a small JavaScript wrapper; no server takes part in the search. Each worker is one Web Worker, so one thread. With 7 native threads the same build found the nu3496 optimum in a median of 9.2 s (10 seeds, 14 Nov; from the lab).Gradnja. 8zTSP-min iz jedra 673813b (main 9f7670a), prevedena v WebAssembly: tsp8z_web_bg.wasm, 406.333 bajtov (sha256 aa1b60a4…), z majhnim ovojem v JavaScriptu; pri iskanju ne sodeluje noben strežnik. Vsak delavec je en Web Worker, torej ena nit. S 7 nativnimi nitmi je ista gradnja optimum nu3496 našla v mediani 9,2 s (10 semen, 14. nov.; iz laboratorija).

On this site the workers share nothing. To share even the best length, a browser needs cross-origin isolation, which this site does not switch on for now; each worker runs with its own seed, and the page shows the best result. In the browser the clock is the solver’s, from the moment the map has been read.Na tej strani si delavci ne delijo ničesar. Da bi si delili vsaj najboljšo dolžino, brskalnik potrebuje izolacijo med izvori (cross-origin isolation), ki je ta stran zaenkrat ne vklopi; vsak delavec teče s svojim semenom, stran pa pokaže najboljši izid. V brskalniku teče ura reševalnika od trenutka, ko je zemljevid prebran.

Limits of this page. Up to 200,000 cities, files up to 32 MB, at most 600 s per run; EUC_2D and CEIL_2D files. The export (JSON) holds the settings, the history of improvements, the tour and the sha256 of the map, so that a run can be checked.Meje te strani. Do 200.000 mest, datoteke do 32 MB, največ 600 s na tek; datoteke EUC_2D in CEIL_2D. Izvoz (JSON) vsebuje nastavitve, zgodovino izboljšav, pot in sha256 zemljevida, da je tek mogoče preveriti.
