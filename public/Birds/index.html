The widening horror

The threat changes before the audience can name it.

01 · Vulnerability

We entered the sky without becoming animals of the sky.

A bird does not need to overpower a human near a wall. It only needs to make the human miss.

02 · Strategy

The most frightening sign of intelligence is restraint.

Not the attack. The decision to wait, observe, abort and return when the conditions are better.

03 · Agency

Intelligence does not create agreement. It creates choices.

Predation can become culture. So can warning, rescue, memory and distrust.

Creative rule

The animals never become people in animal bodies.

Every species expresses higher cognition through its existing senses, ecology, social structure and physical limits. No animal dialogue is needed. Intelligence is inferred entirely from behaviour.

No hive mindNo supernatural evilNo easy leader

Tone rule

Wonder must come first.

The opening should make the audience want to fly with the birds. The same image must later mean proximity, measurement and target assessment.

Progression: wonder → curiosity → humour → unease → pattern recognition → terror.
