CHAPTER 01

WONDER

We entered the sky and mistook proximity for friendship.

01 / 36

Before the Sky Changed

At dawn, the Alps belonged to scale.

The valleys were still blue with night. Limestone walls caught the first light. Above them, invisible columns of warm air were already beginning to rise.

Nothing in the sky looked hungry.

02 / 36

Window. Tooth. Exit.

Luka and Tomaž stood beside their packed wings and studied a photograph of the wall.

Tomaž touched three points with one gloved finger.

Tomaž“Window. Tooth. Exit.”

It was not a prayer. It was a route reduced to three decisions.

03 / 36

Boring Keeps You Alive

Tomaž checked the helmet camera.

Then he checked it again.

Luka suggested a slightly lower line—better footage, closer to the Tooth.

Tomaž“No.”
Tomaž“Boring keeps you alive.”
04 / 36

Launch

By midmorning the valley had begun to breathe.

Luka raised the wing. Fabric filled, lines tightened, and the mountain released him.

For a few seconds he ran on stone.

Then there was nothing beneath his feet.

05 / 36

Allowed to Fly

The eagle entered from below.

It banked once beneath Luka's feet, found the centre of the thermal, and rose beside him.

For one impossible minute, two completely different bodies traced the same invisible column through the sky.

Luka forgot to breathe.

06 / 36

Fly With Eagles

The videos spread.

FLY WITH EAGLES

Pilots crossed Europe for the close encounters. Tourism companies sold the sky as communion. Every new clip looked more intimate than the last.

People said the birds were finally learning that humans were not a threat.

They were right.

CHAPTER 02

DISCOVERY

One hungry bird discovers the difference between the wing and the animal beneath it.

07 / 36

The Discovery

Far from the crowded launch sites, one eagle followed a lone pilot.

The wing above the man looked enormous.

The body below it did not.

The eagle had seen humans walking, shouting, throwing stones, driving machines.

It had never seen one hanging alone from strings.

08 / 36

One Small Attack

The first strike was clumsy.

The pilot threw both arms up. One brake line went slack. The canopy rolled, recovered, then rolled again.

The eagle pulled away.

The mountain did the rest.

09 / 36

The Observer

Rescuers would later find torn fabric, broken lines, and a body damaged beyond any clean explanation.

Before they arrived, the eagle returned.

On a rock above the crash site, another bird watched.

It did not feed.

10 / 36

The Flying Salami Guy

The first survivor made the mistake of describing what he had seen.

Survivor“It wasn't defending a nest. It was trying to make me fall.”

The clip became a joke before it became evidence.

THE FLYING SALAMI GUY

The warning spread faster than belief.

11 / 36

Line

Luka heard the thrashing beside the recovery path.

A raven was caught in a loop of discarded flying line. The more it fought, the tighter the filament bit into one wing.

It struck his hand hard enough to open the skin.

Luka kept cutting.

12 / 36

No Gratitude

The last loop came free.

The raven exploded out of Luka's hands, climbed once over the ridge, and vanished.

No pause.

No look back.

Luka wrapped his bleeding hand and returned to his gear.

CHAPTER 03

THE WALL

At speed, the birds do not need strength. They need timing.

13 / 36

Terrain Does the Killing

The next incident contained no strike.

A speed flyer skimmed a ridge. A bird cut across his line and he corrected by less than two metres.

His wingtip missed the rock.

The bird had not needed to touch him.

14 / 36

Mastery

Tomaž flew the Tooth Wall exactly as planned.

Window.

Tooth.

Exit.

The line was clean, fast, and almost boring.

That was why it was beautiful.

15 / 36

The Same Line

Three days later, the wind returned to the same direction.

Tomaž studied the wall. He checked the camera once.

Then again.

Tomaž“Window. Tooth. Exit.”

He stepped into the air.

16 / 36

First Bird

Before Window, an eagle crossed ahead of him.

It did not dive at Tomaž.

It did not touch him.

It occupied the place his body was about to be.

Tomaž corrected away.

17 / 36

Second Bird

The correction sent him toward the safer side of Tooth.

A second bird rose from below.

For one fraction of a second, the wall, the first bird, and the second formed a geometry nobody had intended.

Except the geometry had happened before.

18 / 36

Rock

The camera jolted.

Grey limestone filled the frame.

There was no scream.

Only wind, a sharp electronic click, and then nothing.

19 / 36

Pilot Error

CAUSE: LATE CORRECTION DURING PROXIMITY FLIGHT

The finding was reasonable.

Every wingsuit pilot knew what a late correction could do.

Luka sat through the briefing with Tomaž's recovered helmet on the table between them.

Reasonable was not the same as true.

20 / 36

Window. Tooth. Exit.

Luka replayed the flight one frame at a time.

Window.

Tooth.

The correction began before Exit.

Tomaž had not chosen a dramatic line. He had abandoned the safe line before it was lost.

Something had made him do it.

21 / 36

Steering

Luka showed Eva Kovač — the field biologist who had been logging the incidents — the first bird crossing the line.

Then the second bird already waiting on the correction side.

Luka“The first one didn't attack him.”
Luka“It moved him.”
Luka“They're learning to steer us.”
CHAPTER 04

WAITING

The most frightening sign of intelligence is not attack. It is restraint.

22 / 36

Launch Posture

On the cliffs, the birds did not need to find humans in flight.

They could watch them prepare.

Harness.

Helmet.

Feet at the edge.

Weight forward.

The launch posture was always the same.

23 / 36

City Air

At dawn, a jumper climbed onto the rim of an abandoned industrial chimney.

A few pigeons scattered.

Normal.

The distant phone camera widened.

Other birds remained on the lower ledges.

They faced inward.

When he jumped, they launched after him.

24 / 36

The Loop

Black placed the loop on the stone.

It waited until Luka looked directly at it.

Then it released the line and flew.

Luka did not decide what the gesture meant.

25 / 36

Occupied Hands

Eva found the clip by accident.

A pilot swung one arm and the raven withdrew.

It changed position.

It waited.

Only when both of the pilot's hands were buried in tangled lines did the raven approach again.

Eva stopped explaining.

26 / 36

Possible Rescue

A bad landing left Luka hanging in the upper branches above a steep ravine.

His shoulder would not move. His radio had torn away.

The raptor circled back.

Black arrived from below and struck at it again and again.

Other corvids joined the noise.

So did the rescuers.

27 / 36

Neither Do You

Eva found Luka beside the rescue vehicle, his arm in a sling.

Luka“It saved me.”
Eva“You don't know that.”
Luka“Neither do you.”

On the roof above them, Black watched neither of them for long.

28 / 36

The Shape in the Footage

They stopped asking whether one bird had intended one attack.

They mapped every approach.

Sunward side. Blind side. Occupied hands. Terrain pinch points. Return after panic.

Different birds.

Different valleys.

The same useful discoveries.

CHAPTER 05

SALAMI SEASON

The joke becomes evidence in front of the whole world.

29 / 36

Salami Season

The festival opened under perfect conditions.

Music carried across the launch meadow. Spectators pointed cameras toward the sky. Pilots joked about Salami Season and queued beneath bright wings.

When the first eagles appeared, the crowd cheered.

30 / 36

Colour in the Sky

The thermal formed above the valley and the wings found it one by one.

Red. Blue. Lime. Orange. Violet. Yellow.

Hundreds of pilots turned together, climbing through clear light.

From the ground, it looked like the sky had opened into a flower.

31 / 36

The Outer Spiral

Then the camera pulled farther back.

The coloured spiral was not alone.

Outside it—wider, darker, almost invisible against the mountains—another spiral was forming.

The birds were not centring the thermal.

They were centring the pilots.

32 / 36

Not Thermalling

Luka watched the birds hold a radius that made no sense.

He keyed the radio.

Luka“Guys… these birds aren't thermalling.”

For several seconds, nobody answered.

Unknown pilot“What are they doing?”
33 / 36

The Dive

One raptor folded its wings.

Then three more.

Pilots turned away from birds and into one another. A reserve opened too early. A canopy collapsed under another wing. Radios filled with unfinished sentences.

The birds did not become monsters.

The sky became crowded with mistakes.

34 / 36

After

By evening, every screen in the world had seen the same thing.

The birds approached.

The pilots reacted.

The formation failed.

Across the valley, coloured fabric lay where colour should never have been.

No accident report could hold all of it.

35 / 36

Calibration Noise

The feeding pattern was easy to explain. Neural tissue was rich in fat.

The behaviour was not.

Eva“They're not becoming us.”

On another monitor, seven low pulses repeated across samples that shared no instrument history.

CALIBRATION NOISE

Nobody looked at them twice.

36 / 36

The Lock

The captive raven opened the first latch.

Then the second.

It did not leave.

It waited until the technician entered the adjacent corridor.

Then it moved the mechanism again.

The corridor door locked.

The raven remained inside its own enclosure and looked through the glass.

There was no reward.
