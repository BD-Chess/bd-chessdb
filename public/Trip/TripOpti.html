TripOpti

A route optimizer that moved out of the research arena and into an ordinary browser. TripOpti keeps the simple user flow — open a page, load or paste stops, optimize — while exposing progressively deeper search: Fast, Deep, and an experimental Deep · MDL×DCC engine.

Three search depths

One interface, different compute budgets. The deeper modes do more work; MDL×DCC also changes how search effort is governed.

Fast

Immediate route ordering

For ordinary trip use where a strong answer is needed quickly. It keeps the interaction lightweight and browser-native.

Deep

Classical deeper search

Runs longer local search and keeps improving while the browser tab stays active. Useful as both a product mode and an A/B baseline.

Deep · MDL×DCC

Governed search

An experimental engine that watches search history, alternates exploration and intensification, keeps multiple operator families alive, and can use one or more CPU workers.

How the MDL×DCC loop works

The browser engine does not replace search with a single score. It uses diagnostics and history to decide how strongly to couple the search to its current basin.

01 · SEEDStart from a valid route and deterministic run state.
02 · GENERATEProduce candidate moves from several operator families.
03 · SENSERead recent progress, stagnation and compressibility/history signals.
04 · GOVERNSwitch between explore and intensify instead of holding one fixed regime.
05 · RETAINKeep better tours, checkpoints, lineage and diagnostics for continuation.
Rank, don't eliminate. TripOpti inherits a core TSP R&D lesson: a method that looks weak in one representation or phase may become useful later. The engine therefore ranks and reallocates rather than treating a temporary loser as permanently dead.

Product boundary

TripOpti is the deployment branch, not a rename of the whole TSP research program.

In the browser

Local stop-order compute

The core route-order optimization executes on the user's device. Deep runs can maintain local encrypted checkpoints and continue later. Supporting geocoding, maps, road directions or AI features may contact their respective external providers.

In the research stack

Six TSP arenas stay six

TSPES, DEV2.3, DEV2.4, BD-RPHS and the two 8zTSP-R lines remain distinct research arenas with different scientific roles. TripOpti is the research → product transfer that distills selected mechanisms into a browser-safe engine.
