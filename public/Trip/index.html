    Why 8Z-RP?

    Deterministic Results: Unlike many online solvers that produce random variations every time you click, 8Z-RP guarantees Same Input ⇒ Same Route.

    Deep Search: The "Deep Search" mode runs the optimization algorithm 12 times in parallel to find the absolute shortest path, ensuring complex routes are fully untangled.

    How to Use

    • 1. Enter Locations: Type your stops in the text box. One per line.
    • 2. Optimize: Use "Standard" for speed or "Deep Search" for complex routes.
    • 3. View Route: The map will draw the path. Use the Driving / Walking buttons to switch modes.
    • 4. Export: Click the "Open in Maps" buttons to send the route to your phone.
