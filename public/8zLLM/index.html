01

Od kompresije datotek do modelovFrom file compression to models

Izhodišče je BD-jeva zamisel: strukturna predobdelava lahko pomaga tudi že zmogljivemu kompresorju. Ali podoben princip velja za uteži in podatkovne tokove LLM-ja? Rezultati 8zC so motivacija, ne dokaz prihranka na modelih.BD’s starting idea: structural preprocessing can help even a strong existing compressor. Can the same principle help LLM weights and data flows? The 8zC results are motivation, not evidence of savings on models.

02

Kaj zaseda VRAM?What occupies VRAM?

W + QUteži in kvantizacijski podatkiWeights and quantization data
KKV-predpomnilnikKV cache
A + RAktivacije in režija izvajanjaActivations and runtime overhead

Kompresija datoteke na disku še ne zmanjša porabe VRAM-a. Raziskujemo stisnjene bloke, ki se obnovijo tik pred računanjem, brez polne dodatne kopije modela. Meriti moramo dejanski vrh pomnilnika pri prefill in decode ter hitrost generiranja.Compressing a file on disk does not reduce inference VRAM by itself. We investigate packed blocks reconstructed just before computation without a second full model copy. Actual peak memory during prefill and decoding and generation speed must be measured.

Ilustrativni račun, ne meritevIllustrative calculation, not a measurement

70 milijard parametrov pri efektivnih 4,5 bitih na parameter: približno 36,7 GiB uteži. Hipotetični 20-odstotni dodatni prihranek pomeni 29,3 GiB pred KV-predpomnilnikom in ostalo režijo.70 billion parameters at an effective 4.5 bits per parameter: about 36.7 GiB of weights. A hypothetical additional 20% saving would yield 29.3 GiB before KV cache and other overhead.

03

Raziskovalne smeriResearch directions

8zC · L0 / L1

Obrnljive predstavitveReversible representations

Bitne ravnine, ločevanje skal in indeksov, prediktorji, generatorji ter eksaktni residuali. Razlikujemo izvorne in že kvantizirane uteži.Bit planes, separate scales and indices, predictors, generators and exact residuals. Original and already quantized weights are separate contracts.

8zTSP · TEP

Geometrija tenzorjevTensor geometry

Afinitete blokov, lokalnost, dovoljena preurejanja in simetrije. Neto korist mora pokriti indekse in strošek dostopa.Block affinities, locality, legal reorderings and symmetries. Net gains must cover indices and access costs.

8zNAS · RecipeBench

Iskanje konfiguracijConfiguration search

Preverjanje kombinacij velikosti blokov, transformacij, kodirnikov in dekoderjev pri enakem proračunu.Evaluate combinations of block sizes, transforms, coders and decoders under matched budgets.

MDL × DCC

Izbira in izvajanjeSelection and execution

Celotna dolžina zapisa, hitrost GPU, predpomnilniki in prilagodljivo razporejanje računa. Osnutki žetonov so ločen poskus pospeševanja.Total encoded size, GPU speed, caches and adaptive compute allocation. Token drafting is a separate speed experiment.

MEMORY

Vdelave in kontekstEmbeddings and context

Preverimo dejansko umestitev tabel vdelav, KV-predpomnilnik ter naključni dostop. Premik v RAM ni isto kot kompresija.Measure where embedding tables and KV caches actually reside and their random access costs. Offloading to RAM is not compression.

FUTURE · L2

Nove arhitektureNew architectures

Mešana natančnost in učenje modelov s kompaktno predstavitvijo sta ločeni raziskovalni veji z merili kakovosti.Mixed precision and training models with compact representations are separate tracks with quality criteria.

04

Od hipoteze do meritveFrom hypothesis to measurement

P0

PopisInventory

P1

RecipeBenchRecipeBench

CPU preizkus obrnljivih receptov proti močnim obstoječim kodekom in preprostim izbirnikom.CPU tests of reversible recipes against strong existing codecs and simple selectors.

P2

Izvajanje na GPUGPU execution

Izmeriti vrh VRAM-a, čas prvega žetona, tok/s ter numerično in kakovostno pariteto.Measure peak VRAM, time to first token, tokens/s and numerical and quality parity.

P3

Prenos in arhitekturaTransfer and architecture

Ločeni potrditveni modeli in poznejša raziskava kompaktnejših modelov.Separate confirmation models and later exploration of more compact architectures.

Primerjalniki: Baselines: ZipNN · DFloat11 · llama.cpp. To so izhodišča za primerjavo, ne že premagani tekmeci.These are baselines to compare against, not rivals already beaten.

05

Skupno raziskovanjeCollaborative research

GCG (GPT, Claude, Gemini) oblikuje različne hipoteze in recenzije. Zbor širšega nabora modelov pomaga iskati alternative in ugovore; eksperimentalne meritve odločajo. Prispevke in avtorstvo ohranjamo ločeno.GCG (GPT, Claude, Gemini) develops distinct hypotheses and reviews. A broader model council contributes alternatives and objections; experimental measurements decide. Contributions and authorship remain distinct.
