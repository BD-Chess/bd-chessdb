ODKRITJE 1 · EXACT DR-rANS4 + QAT-ScalePalette (v0.7)

50 % bitov surovih + 8-stezni rANS4 + 10-bitna paleta skal premaga celo zlib-950% raw bits + 8-way rANS4 + 10-bit scale palette beats even zlib-9

Ko v `Q4_0` (`Gemma 4 12B QAT`) spodnja 2 bita ZigZaga ($z_{\text{lo}} = z \& 3$) pustimo kot surovo 64-bitno besedo (`0` ciklov dekodiranja!), zgornja 2 bita ($z_{\text{hi}} \in \{0..3\}$) zakodiramo z 8-steznim `rANS4` pogojenim na vzporedno razmerje skal `DR` ($\log_2(|d|/\text{mediana}(|d|))$), 16-bitne FP16 skale $d$ pa preslikamo v eksaktno 10-bitno QAT paleto (`≤ 1.142` unikatnih skal v celotni FFN matriki!), dosežemo realiziran bitno eksakten zapis `85,46–90,88 %` (`−9,12 %` do `−14,54 %`), ki premaga celo `R3/R4 zlib-9` (`85,58–91,88 %`) pri hitrosti dekodiranja `2,25–3,14 ns/utež`!Keeping the bottom 2 ZigZag bits ($z_{\text{lo}} = z \& 3$) in `Q4_0` (`Gemma 4 12B QAT`) as a raw 64-bit word (`0` decode cycles!), encoding the top 2 bits ($z_{\text{hi}} \in \{0..3\}$) with an 8-way `rANS4` conditioned on the parallel scale-ratio `DR` ($\log_2(|d|/\text{median}(|d|))$), and mapping the 16-bit FP16 scales $d$ into an exact 10-bit QAT palette (`≤ 1,142` unique scales across the entire FFN matrix!) yields a realized bit-exact size of `85.46–90.88%` (`−9.12%` to `−14.54%`), beating even `R3/R4 zlib-9` (`85.58–91.88%`) at `2.25–3.14 ns/weight` decode speed!

ODKRITJE 2 · KT_ZC2 + Bounded-ZZ v0.6 (Zero-Crossing Context za Q4_K)

Omejeni ZigZag v enem u64 registru ujame 91 % celotnega prihranka brez ovijanjaBounded-ZZ in a single u64 register captures 91% of total savings without wrap-around

V asimetričnem `Q4_K` ($w = d \cdot sc \cdot q - d_{\min} \cdot m$) vsak podblok 32 uteži premakne pravo ničlo na pol-celoštevilsko sidro $z_2 = \text{round}(2 d_{\min} m / (d \cdot sc)) \in [0..31]$. Pogojevanje `KT_ZC2` povrne zvonasti vrh (`3,751 bpw`), omejeni ZigZag `Bounded-ZZ` (`v0.6`, kjer celotna 16-elementna inverzna tabela za dani $z_2$ živi v enem samem `u64` registru `LUT_DEC[z2]`) pa odpravi modularno ovijanje repov in z le 2 kodiranima bitoma (`z_hi | z2`) ter 2 surovima bitoma (`z_lo`) doseže `3,776 bpw` (`91,0 %` celotne rezerve pod 4,0 bpw)!In asymmetric `Q4_K` ($w = d \cdot sc \cdot q - d_{\min} \cdot m$), each 32-weight subblock shifts true zero to a half-integer anchor $z_2 = \text{round}(2 d_{\min} m / (d \cdot sc)) \in [0..31]$. `KT_ZC2` conditioning restores the sharp peak (`3.751 bpw`), and `Bounded-ZZ` (`v0.6`, where the entire 16-entry inverse map for a given $z_2$ fits in a single `u64` register `LUT_DEC[z2]`) eliminates modulo wrap-around and reaches `3.776 bpw` (`91.0%` of total savings below 4.0 bpw) while keeping 50% of bits raw!

ODKRITJE 3 · Forenzika vdelav (dups == 0 & PLE baze)

Nič podvojenih vrstic v 262.144 žetonih in neporavnane baze 42 PLE plastiZero duplicate rows across 262,144 tokens and unaligned 42-layer PLE bases

Eksaktni FNV-1a pregled vseh `262.144` vrstic v `token_embd` (E4B in 12B) ter `per_layer_token_embd` (11.010.048 superblokov) je dokazal `dup_rows = 0`: prihranek `−9,73 %` do `−14,98 %` v glavni vdelavi ne izvira iz podvojenih neuporabljenih vrstic, temveč iz znotrajvrstične korelacije (`KT_PREV = 3,658 bpw`) in nasičenja skal (`100 %` superblokov ima `max(sc) == 63`). V PLE pa ima ista beseda med plastjo $l$ in $l+1$ kosinus `−0,0016`, ker vsaka plast uporablja lastno projekcijo v `per_layer_model_proj`.An exact FNV-1a scan of all `262,144` rows in `token_embd` (E4B and 12B) and `per_layer_token_embd` (11,010,048 superblocks) proved `dup_rows = 0`: the `−9.73%` to `−14.98%` main-embedding gain comes not from duplicate unused rows, but from intra-row correlation (`KT_PREV = 3.658 bpw`) and scale saturation (`100%` of superblocks hit `max(sc) == 63`). In PLE, adjacent-layer cosine is `−0.0016` because each layer applies its own `per_layer_model_proj` basis.

ODKRITJE 4 · Izrek o venčnem produktu L1-σ

Eksaktna grupa simetrij FFN nevronov brez ponovne kvantizacijeExact FFN neuron symmetry group without requantization

Ker ima `ffn_down` kvantizacijske podbloke vzdolž vmesnih nevronov (`ne[0] = M`), je maksimalna grupa permutacij nevronov, ki bitno ohrani vse kvantizirane vrednosti in skale brez tabele inverza (`L1-σ`), trinivojski venčni produkt: $S_{32} \wr S_8 \wr S_{40}$ za `Q4_K`/`Q5_K`, $S_{16} \wr S_{16} \wr S_{40}$ za `Q6_K` (16 podblokov po 16!) in $S_{32} \wr S_{M/32}$ za `Q4_0`. Empirični preizkus dokaže, da post-hoc permutacija na že kvantiziranih FFN da `< 0,01 %`, kar usmeri geometrijo `8zTSP` v pred-kvantizacijski `L2-σ` (`Peel-Then-Rotate`).Because `ffn_down` packs quantization subblocks along intermediate neurons (`ne[0] = M`), the maximal neuron permutation group preserving all quantized values and scales bitwise with zero inverse table (`L1-σ`) is the wreath product $S_{32} \wr S_8 \wr S_{40}$ for `Q4_K`/`Q5_K`, $S_{16} \wr S_{16} \wr S_{40}$ for `Q6_K` (16 subblocks of 16!), and $S_{32} \wr S_{M/32}$ for `Q4_0`. Empirical tests prove post-hoc permutation on already-quantized FFN yields `< 0.01%`, focusing `8zTSP` geometry on pre-quantization `L2-σ` (`Peel-Then-Rotate`).

ODKRITJE 5 · WarpQR na BF16 proti Q4 (G2)

3,04 % izjem na BF16 eksponentih (−29,73 % brez vej v GPU snopu)3.04% outliers on BF16 exponents (−29.73% with zero GPU warp branching)

Ubežna razporeditev `b + p · payload_bits` (kjer masko izjem v registrih izračuna `__ballot_sync`) na 4-bitnih indeksih ne prinese prihranka, ker je delež izjem izven 3-bitnega jedra previsok (`p = 21–37 %`). Na 8-bitnih eksponentih `BF16` pa le `p = 3,04 %` vrednosti pade izven 3-bitnega okna (`3,243 bita/eksponent`), kar zniža celoten `BF16` tenzor na `70,27 %` brez ene same veje v 32-nitnem snopu!The escape layout `b + p · payload_bits` (with register mask computed via `__ballot_sync`) does not compress 4-bit indices because outlier density outside a 3-bit core is too high (`p = 21–37%`). On 8-bit `BF16` exponents, however, only `p = 3.04%` fall outside a 3-bit window (`3.243 bits/exponent`), shrinking the full `BF16` tensor to `70.27%` with zero branch divergence across a 32-thread warp!

ODKRITJE 6 · Realni FFN preizkus 8z-BitSpec (G4)

Odprava +0,5·d DC pristranskosti in meja med 2-bitnim H2 in 3-bitnim H3Eliminating +0.5·d DC bias and the boundary between 2-bit H2 and 3-bit H3

Preizkus polnega FFN bloka (`gate + up + down`, `blk.20` na `Gemma 4 12B Q4_0`) na 32 aktivacijskih vektorjih je potrdil, da naivno maskiranje `(q & 0xC) | 0x2` vnese sistematični premik `+0,5·d` (`cos = 0,8266`). Unbiased midpoint reconstruction `(q & 0xC) + 1.5` lifts cosine to `0.8515` (archiving 2-bit `H2` as below the 40-layer $\alpha \ge 0.84$ threshold), while the 3-bit plane `H3` (`(q & 0xE) + 0.5`) reaches `cos = 0.9693`!
