Slovenija kot startup in investicijska nišaSlovenia as a startup and investment niche

To je majhen, ampak pomemben signal: Slovenija lahko tekmuje kot nišna država znanja, ne kot poceni država mase.Nova Gorica hosted an investment conference with more than 150 participants from 22 countries. This is a small but important signal: Slovenia can compete as a niche knowledge state, not as a cheap mass state.

KARTICA 01 · GOV.SI

The Investors Edge 2026The Investors Edge 2026

To ni samo konferencaThis is not only a conference

Za majhno državo je vsak resen mednarodni investicijski stik infrastruktura.For a small state, every serious international investment contact is infrastructure.

Uradno: konferenca The Investors Edge 2026 je 9. in 10. junija v Novi Gorici privabila več kot 150 udeležencev iz 22 držav in 27 mednarodnih govorcev.Officially: The Investors Edge 2026 took place on 9 and 10 June in Nova Gorica, attracting more than 150 participants from 22 countries and 27 international speakers.

Program je pokril investiranje, upravljanje podjetij, umetno inteligenco, izhodne strategije in trende evropskega ter globalnega podjetniškega okolja. To so teme, kjer Slovenija ne potrebuje milijonov ljudi, ampak goste mreže kakovosti.The program covered investing, company governance, artificial intelligence, exit strategies, and trends in the European and global entrepreneurial environment. These are areas where Slovenia does not need millions of people, but dense networks of quality.

Če ima država majhen trg, mora imeti večjo prepustnost: talent ven, kapital noter, znanje nazaj, podjetja globalno že od prvega dne.A small domestic market needs higher permeability: talent out, capital in, knowledge back, companies global from day one.

KARTICA 02 · BD STRATEGIJA
NIŠA
Majhna država ne sme igrati igre velikihA small state must not play the big-state game

Slovenija naj bo oster nož, ne velika lopataSlovenia should be a sharp knife, not a big shovel

Naša prednost ni masa. Naša prednost je hitro povezovanje znanja.Our edge is not mass. It is fast knowledge linking.

Slovenija ne bo premagala ZDA, Nemčije ali Kitajske po količini kapitala. Lahko pa zmaga v nišah, kjer je pomembna kombinacija inženirstva, znanosti, dizajna, medicine, AI, energetike, logistike in kakovosti življenja.Slovenia will not beat the US, Germany, or China by capital volume. It can win in niches where engineering, science, design, medicine, AI, energy, logistics, and quality of life combine.

To zahteva manj birokracije, hitrejši dostop do kapitala, boljši odnos do neuspeha in bolj praktično povezavo med univerzami, podjetji in tujino.This requires less bureaucracy, faster access to capital, a better attitude toward failure, and more practical links between universities, companies, and abroad.

KARTICA 05 · PREDLOG
RAZVOJNI STANDARD
Slovenija kot mreža, ne otokSlovenia as a network, not an island

Predlog: investicijski ledger SlovenijeProposal: Slovenia investment ledger

Ne javno razkrivanje poslovnih skrivnosti, ampak merjenje ekosistema.Not exposing business secrets, but measuring the ecosystem.

Slovenija bi morala voditi javno, anonimizirano merilno tablo startup ekosistema: število srečanj z investitorji, število pilotskih projektov, investicijski krogi, izhodi, vrnitve talentov, raziskovalni spinouti.Slovenia should maintain a public, anonymized startup ecosystem dashboard: investor meetings, pilot projects, investment rounds, exits, talent returns, research spinouts.

Tako bi razvoj merili z dejanskim tokom znanja in kapitala, ne s fotografijami s konferenc.Then development would be measured by real flows of knowledge and capital, not by conference photos.

Rezultat: Slovenija kot majhna, pametna, hitro povezana investicijska niša.Result: Slovenia as a small, smart, fast-connected investment niche.
