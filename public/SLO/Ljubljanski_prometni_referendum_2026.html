To ni strankarski članek. MOL + GOV.SI
Naknadni referendum o 4. členu prometnega odlokaSubsequent referendum on Article 4 of the traffic ordinance

Glasuje se o parkiranju v soseskahThe vote is about neighborhood parking

Majhno vprašanje, velik demokratični signal.A small question with a large democratic signal.

MOL je razpisal naknadni referendum o 4. členu odloka, ki uvaja ureditev parkiranja v območjih sosesk. Glasovanje je napovedano za 12. julij 2026.The Municipality of Ljubljana called a subsequent referendum on Article 4 of the ordinance, introducing parking regulation in neighborhood zones. The vote is scheduled for 12 July 2026.

Po uradni strani MOL je bilo na dan 11. 6. 2026 navedenih 227.376 volilnih upravičencev in 257 volišč. To ni poceni pogovor. To je velika mestna operacija.According to the official MOL page, on 11 June 2026 there were 227,376 eligible voters and 257 polling stations. This is not a cheap conversation. It is a major municipal operation.

Zato je legitimno vprašanje: ali je referendum najboljše orodje za parkirni režim ali pa znak, da javna razprava pred sprejemom ni bila dovolj prepričljiva?So the legitimate question is: is a referendum the best tool for a parking regime, or a sign that the public debate before adoption was not convincing enough?

KARTICA 02 · BD TEST
REFERENDUM
Ljudje niso prometni simulatorPeople are not a traffic simulator

Referendum je groba zavoraA referendum is a rough brake

Včasih je nujen. Pogosto pa rešuje napako prepozno.Sometimes it is necessary. Often it corrects the process too late.

Pri prometu ima vsak močno osebno izkušnjo: stanovalec, dnevni migrant, obrtnik, dostavljavec, starš, starejša oseba, invalid, kolesar. Zato je tema politično eksplozivna.Traffic policy touches everyone: residents, commuters, craftsmen, delivery workers, parents, older people, disabled people, cyclists. That is why the topic is politically explosive.

Ampak prav zaradi tega referendum pogosto postane glasovanje o jezi, zaupanju v župana ali splošnem odnosu do mestne politike, ne pa natančna odločitev o prometnem modelu.But because of that, a referendum often becomes a vote on anger, trust in the mayor, or general city politics, not a precise decision on the traffic model.

KARTICA 05 · PREDLOG
OBČINSKI STANDARD
Referendum šele po strokovni razgrnitviReferendum only after expert disclosure

Mestni standard za sporne odlokeMunicipal standard for contested ordinances

Vsaka občina bi morala imeti javni predreferendumski paket.Every municipality should have a public pre-referendum package.

Pred referendumom bi morala obstajati obvezna stran z vsemi modeli: besedilo odloka, zemljevid območij, pričakovani učinki, stroški, alternative, mnenja strokovnjakov in pripombe prebivalcev.Before a referendum, there should be a mandatory page with all models: ordinance text, area maps, expected effects, costs, alternatives, expert opinions, and resident objections.

Tako referendum ne bi bil samo “da/ne” po občutku, ampak zadnja demokratična varovalka po tem, ko so bile možnosti resno razložene.Then the referendum would not be only a gut-level yes/no, but the final democratic safeguard after the options were seriously explained.

Rezultat: referendum ostane možnost, vendar šele po javnem prometnem modelu in pilotski logiki.Result: referendum remains possible, but only after a public traffic model and pilot logic.
