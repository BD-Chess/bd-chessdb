Politična policija?Political police?

Ne gre za novo enoto Policije RS. Gre za spor o noveli Zakona o parlamentarni preiskavi: koliko moči sme dobiti politična večina in katere hitre varovalke morajo zaščititi posameznika, opozicijo, medije in javni interes.This is not a new police unit. It is a dispute about amendments to Slovenia’s Parliamentary Inquiry Act: how much investigative power a political majority should have, and which fast safeguards must protect individuals, opposition, media, and the public interest.

Kako brati ta komentarHow to read this commentary

Osnovna razlika: “politična policija” je slogan in opozorilo, ne uradno ime nove institucije. Pravo vprašanje je bolj trezno: ali novela dovolj dobro prepreči, da parlamentarna preiskava ne postane šikaniranje političnih nasprotnikov, novinarjev, civilne družbe ali zasebnikov?Basic distinction: “political police” is a slogan and warning, not the official name of a new institution. The calmer question is whether the amendment sufficiently prevents parliamentary inquiry from becoming harassment of political opponents, journalists, civil society, or private persons.

Stran povzema najino debato: najprej razčisti, kaj zakon sploh je, nato pošteno predstavi argument za učinkovite parlamentarne preiskave in nazadnje preveri, ali sedanja novela dovolj varuje posameznika.

Po preverjanju je sklep jasen: proti sedanjemu besedilu. Zakonodajni postopek je bil za tako občutljivo ureditev prehiter, naknadno sodno varstvo pa ne more izbrisati že razkritih podatkov, javne diskreditacije, stroškov ali pritiska. Boljša rešitev je nov predlog: hiter začetek preiskave, vendar tudi hitra neodvisna kontrola občutljivih posegov.

Skoči na dva odločilna razloga →

This page captures our discussion: first clarify what the law is, then fairly present the case for effective parliamentary inquiries, and finally test whether the current amendment adequately protects the individual.

After review, the conclusion is clear: oppose the current text. The legislative process was too fast for such a sensitive regime, while later judicial protection cannot erase data already exposed, public discrediting, costs, or pressure. A better solution is a new proposal: a fast start to inquiries, combined with fast independent review of sensitive intrusions.

Kartica 02 · Ustavni okvirCard 02 · Constitutional frame
DZ nadzoruje zadeve javnega pomenaParliament oversees public-interest matters
93. člen UstaveArticle 93

Preiskava ni kazenski pregonInquiry is not prosecution

Parlamentarna preiskava je orodje politične odgovornosti in nadzora, vendar uporablja močna poizvedovalna pooblastila.A parliamentary inquiry is a tool of political accountability and oversight, but it uses strong investigatory powers.

Ustava določa, da Državni zbor lahko odredi preiskavo o zadevah javnega pomena. Komisija ima pri poizvedovanju in preučevanju smiselno enaka pooblastila kakor pravosodni organi.

Zato je meja občutljiva: formalni cilj so javne funkcije, politična odgovornost in zakonodaja, praktično pa lahko preiskava zadene tudi priče, novinarje, podjetja, civilno družbo ali zasebnike.

The Constitution allows the National Assembly to order an inquiry into matters of public importance. The commission has, for inquiry and examination, powers comparable in effect to judicial bodies.

That makes the boundary sensitive: the formal target is public office, political accountability, and legislation, but in practice an inquiry can also affect witnesses, journalists, businesses, civil society, or private persons.

Zato vprašanje ni “ali smemo nadzirati politike?”, ampak “kako preprečimo, da nadzor politike ne postane politično nadlegovanje?”So the question is not “may we scrutinize politics?”, but “how do we prevent scrutiny from becoming political harassment?”

Zakaj bom glasoval protiWhy I would vote against it

Ne zato, ker verjamem sloganu o novi politični policiji, ampak zato, ker sta napačna tako hitrost postopka kot čas, ko posameznik dobi učinkovito zaščito.Not because I believe the slogan about a new political police, but because both the speed of the process and the timing of effective protection for the individual are wrong.

Parlamentarne preiskave morajo biti učinkovite in jih ni dopustno blokirati do neuporabnosti. Toda sedanji predlog je pri ustavno občutljivih pooblastilih izbral napačno ravnotežje. Hitrost je dobila prednost pred dovolj široko strokovno presojo, sodno varstvo pa se pri pomembnem delu problema seli v čas, ko je lahko nepopravljiva škoda že nastala.

Parliamentary inquiries must be effective and must not be blocked into uselessness. But for constitutionally sensitive powers, the current proposal chose the wrong balance. Speed took priority over sufficiently broad expert review, while judicial protection for a key part of the problem is shifted to a point when irreversible damage may already have occurred.

1 · PREHITER POSTOPEK1 · PROCESS TOO FAST
Predlog je bil vložen 15. maja in sprejet 26. maja 2026 po skrajšanem postopku. Enajst dni je premalo za resno primerjavo pravnih modelov, obravnavo opozoril stroke ter presojo posledic za zasebnike, novinarje in civilno družbo. Sama hitrost še ne dokazuje slabega zakona, je pa pri tako močnih pooblastilih resna rdeča zastava.The proposal was filed on 15 May and adopted on 26 May 2026 under an expedited procedure. Eleven days is too little for serious comparison of legal models, consideration of expert warnings, and assessment of consequences for private persons, journalists, and civil society. Speed alone does not prove that a law is bad, but with powers this strong it is a serious red flag.
2 · VARSTVO PRIDE PREPOZNO2 · PROTECTION ARRIVES TOO LATE
Naknadni upravni spor po končnem poročilu ni polnovredna zamenjava za hitro preventivno varstvo. Ko so podatki že pridobljeni, človek zaslišan in javno politično označen, poznejša sodna zmaga ne vrne zasebnosti, ugleda, časa, denarja ali varnosti virov.Later administrative litigation after the final report is not an equivalent replacement for rapid preventive protection. Once data have been obtained, a person questioned, and publicly labelled in political terms, a later court victory does not restore privacy, reputation, time, money, or source safety.
Meja, ki je ne smemo prestopiti:A line that must not be crossed: “Najprej te preiščemo in javno obdelamo, potem pa se lahko čez leta pritožiš” ni sprejemljiva pravna varovalka. Postopek ne sme postati kazen.“We investigate and publicly process you first; years later you may appeal” is not an acceptable legal safeguard. The process itself must not become punishment.
Zato: proti tej noveli, ne proti parlamentarnim preiskavam. Nov predlog naj omogoči hiter začetek, vendar tudi 7–15 dnevni neodvisni filter za vključitev zasebnikov in občutljive posege, posebno zaščito novinarskih virov in žvižgačev ter sankcije za politično razkrivanje zaupnih podatkov.Therefore: oppose this amendment, not parliamentary inquiries. A new proposal should allow a fast start, but also a 7–15 day independent filter for involving private persons and for sensitive intrusions, special protection for journalistic sources and whistleblowers, and sanctions for political disclosure of confidential data.
