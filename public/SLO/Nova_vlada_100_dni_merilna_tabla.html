Nova vlada naj ne dobi ne slepe vere ne avtomatske panike. Dobi naj 100-dnevno merilno tablo: obljube, prvi ukrepi, tveganja in merljivi rezultati.A new government deserves neither blind faith nor automatic panic. It deserves a 100-day scoreboard: promises, first moves, risks, and measurable results.

Zakaj je to pomembno?Why this matters

To ni strankarski članek. To je poskus, da se aktualno temo razstavi na mehanizem: kaj se je zgodilo, kje je tveganje, kaj bi merili in katera varovalka bi bila boljša od navijaškega kričanja.This is not a partisan article. It is an attempt to unpack the mechanism: what happened, where the risk is, what should be measured, and which safeguard is better than tribal shouting.

Nova vlada je dejstvo, ne projekcija željaThe new government is a fact, not wish projection

Najprej brez histerijeFirst, no hysteria

Janševa vlada naj bo merjena po rezultatih, ne po avtomatskem navijanju ali sovraštvu.The Janša government should be measured by results, not automatic cheering or hate.

Reuters je poročal, da je parlament 4. junija 2026 potrdil novo desnosredinsko vlado Janeza Janše; navedeno je bilo glasovanje 49 proti 30 in obljube o davčnih rezih, zmanjšanju birokracije, boju proti korupciji in decentralizaciji.Reuters reported that parliament approved Janez Janša’s new centre-right government on 4 June 2026; the reported vote was 49 to 30, with pledges on tax cuts, bureaucracy reduction, anti-corruption, and decentralization.

Prva napaka javnosti je, da vlado oceni po tem, koga voli. Druga napaka je, da jo oceni po tem, koga sovraži. Obe metodi sta slabi.The public’s first mistake is judging a government by who one votes for. The second is judging it by who one hates. Both methods are weak.

Boljši pristop: nova vlada dobi javno merilno tablo. Vsaka večja obljuba dobi indikator, rok, odgovorno ministrstvo in oznako: premik, zamuda, tveganje, dokazano.Better approach: the new government gets a public scoreboard. Every major promise gets an indicator, deadline, responsible ministry, and status: progress, delay, risk, proven.

Prvi dnevi niso dokaz uspeha. So zemljevid tveganj.The first days are not proof of success. They are a risk map.

Na 2. redni seji se je vlada ukvarjala z neurjem, škodo in pomočjo; opozorila je na slabo stanje pri črpanju evropskih sredstev; napovedala pregled nekaterih kadrovskih postopkov; ukinila začasni notranji nadzor na mejah s Hrvaško in Madžarsko ter spremenila nekatere sklepe glede Izraela.At its second regular session, the government handled storm damage and aid; warned about weak EU-fund absorption; announced review of some staffing procedures; ended temporary internal border checks with Croatia and Hungary; and changed some Israel-related decisions.

To so močni signali, a ne še rezultati. Pravi test je, ali bo vlada iz njih naredila merljive popravljene procese ali samo politično zgodbo o krivdi prejšnjih.These are strong signals, but not results yet. The real test is whether the government turns them into measurable process fixes or only a political story about the previous government’s blame.

KARTICA 04 · KILL-TEST
RHP FALSIFIER
Kako vemo, da gre narobe?How do we know it is going wrong?

Rdeče zastaviceRed flags

Vlada ne pade zaradi enega spornega sklepa. Pade zaradi vzorca.A government does not fail through one controversial decision. It fails through a pattern.

Najmočnejši test bo, ali lahko vlada sama sebe omeji. Oblast, ki zna postaviti merila tudi proti sebi, gradi zaupanje. Oblast, ki meri samo nasprotnike, ga ruši.The strongest test will be whether the government can constrain itself. Power that can set metrics against itself builds trust. Power that measures only opponents destroys it.

rdeča zastavica 1red flag 1
Vsak problem se razloži samo kot krivda prejšnje vlade, brez javnega načrta popravil.Every problem is explained only as the previous government’s fault, without a public repair plan.
rdeča zastavica 2red flag 2
Kadri se menjajo hitreje, kot se objavljajo strokovni kriteriji za menjave.Personnel changes move faster than public expert criteria for those changes.
BD test:BD test: Ne ocenjuj po simpatiji. Ocenjuj po tem, ali se zmanjšuje kompleksnost države za navadnega človeka.Do not judge by sympathy. Judge by whether state complexity decreases for ordinary people.
KARTICA 05 · PREDLOG
JAVNI DASHBOARD
100 dni kot javni račun100 days as a public ledger

Predlog: vladni “proof-of-work”Proposal: government proof-of-work

Vsaka vlada bi morala po 100 dneh pokazati dokazila, ne marketinškega filma.Every government should show evidence after 100 days, not a marketing film.

Predlagam preprost standard: ob 100. dnevu mora vlada objaviti tabelo 20 glavnih obljub, 20 dejanskih ukrepov, 20 nerešenih problemov in 20 merljivih rokov za naslednjih 100 dni.A simple standard: on day 100, the government publishes a table of 20 main promises, 20 actual actions, 20 unresolved problems, and 20 measurable deadlines for the next 100 days.

To ne reši politike. Ampak premakne javnost iz vere v meritev. In to je že velik napredek.This does not fix politics. But it moves the public from belief to measurement. That is already a large improvement.

Rezultat: nova vlada naj ne dobi praznega čeka. Dobi naj merilno tablo.Result: the new government should not receive a blank cheque. It should receive a scoreboard.
