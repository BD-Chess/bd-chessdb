01 · Nine Metres

Upper Wend, the north tip of the bridge · dawn, ten days before the flood

Two half-bridges stood out over the river like the hands of people who had agreed to shake and then thought better of it. Between the tips lay nine metres of brown water. A string stretched from rail to rail would also have shown the north span pointing nine centimetres left of the south, and that was the part that made the villages shout.

Illuminara walked out along Odo's side over decking so new it still smelled of larch. Odo of Upper Wend followed with a notebook held against his chest, the way a man holds a shield he expects to be asked to put down.

OdoI set my line from the Tithe Stone. The same stone Hanne Sull used. I closed it to a centimetre, twice. You are going to tell me she sighted wrong. I will carry you over the river on my back if you can show it.

IlluminaraI am not going to tell you that yet. I want to know what each of you would have to be wrong about, and I want to see it myself. Ten days to the flood. After it the ferry will not run until April. Upper Wend has eleven women due before then, and one midwife, who lives on the other bank.

OdoSeren Ashby. I have known Seren since she was nine. Do you think I need reminding?

IlluminaraNo. I think you needed someone to say it, so you would not have to say it first.

He looked at her, then at the water, the way you look at a debt.

OdoIf you say we were both right, you are saying the river lied. Rivers do not lie. Somebody sighted wrong, and it was not us.

She did not argue. Behind the anger stood a man who had spent two summers lifting beams to a line he trusted with his name, and she would not take it from him before there was something to put in its place.

IlluminaraThen show me how you sighted. Slowly, as though I were new. I will do exactly what you say.

Nobody had asked Odo to teach anything since the closing day. He set down the notebook, took the sighting vane out of his coat, and began.

02 · Two True Lines

Lower Wend, the south landing and the survey shed · midday

The ferry was a flat raft on a rope, poled by its keeper, Pell Garrow, with the patience of a man paid by the crossing. Halfway over, the rope stopped taking the weight and a length of old anchor chain took over, rattling through the sheave. Illuminara watched it pass through the block and noticed that she had noticed.

Seren Ashby was waiting on the landing with a leather bag at her feet, sixty years old and solid as a gatepost.

SerenEleven in Upper Wend before March, and I have caught nine of the last ten. If you are here to be moved by my courage, save yourself the trouble. I would rather walk.

IlluminaraThen I will try to get you something to walk on.

Hanne Sull ran her survey the way she ran her tea, strong and measured to the gram. Each page of her survey book began with the weather drawn small in ink: a sun, a cloud, one grim little rain. She taught Illuminara the chain, the plumb, the vane and the arithmetic of closing a line, and watched with folded arms while the visitor did everything wrong and then right.

By evening Illuminara had re-run both crews' work with the crews' own hands. The north line closed to eight millimetres, the south to six. She laid the two books side by side on the shed table and could find no error in either larger than a thumbnail.

HanneI told Odo that in July. He said it was impossible, which is what people say when it is not impossible.

IlluminaraIt is not. Both lines are good, so the lines are not what moved. You each began from one place. What was it?

HanneThe Tithe Stone, on the north terrace. On rock. It cannot have moved.

She stopped, pen lifted.

HanneLast April my foresight to the south pier came out nine centimetres off the stone's line. I wrote it down as chain stretch. Chains stretch.

IlluminaraThe stone is on rock, so it did not walk. What stands on something that can?

HanneThe meadows. The flats between the terrace and the water. Nobody lives on them. We only cross them.

IlluminaraIs there anything down there that belongs to neither of you?

HanneThe tithe-stones. One every hundred paces down each lane to the ford, set before either village kept a warden, and written in the parish book. The bridge is laid on the ford's line.

IlluminaraThen the stones are a tape nobody here owns.

Alone in the dusk, Illuminara laid her palm on the south footing and then, crossing, on the north. Both gave the same message, the way a rope tells you someone is pulling the other end: two great blocks either side of nothing, each drawn gently away from the other. A feeling was a hypothesis, and nobody was going to hang a bridge from her palm.

03 · The Sum

The ferry landing, Lower Wend · evening, the fourth day

The test was cheap and could embarrass her. If the ground between the rock and the river had stretched, the old stones would say so. They stood a hundred paces apart down each lane, they were written in the parish book, and they belonged to neither crew. She had asked each builder to re-chain the other's bank stone by stone, set the length against the book, and bring back one number.

Odo had gone south at dawn and been escorted through Lower Wend by a dog. Hanne had gone north at noon, set foot in Upper Wend for the first time since the closing day, and asked a child to show her the lane. Now they stood at opposite ends of Pell's raft, the only neutral ground in the valley.

OdoThree point six. From the south terrace to the water, her lane is longer than the book says by that much. Eleven cracks and steps hold some of it: the orchard wall, the lane by the well, the churchyard step. The rest is in the clay.

HanneFive point four, in yours, the same way. A door in your low lane sticks so badly the baker climbs through the window.

OdoHe enjoys it.

Nobody said the sum. It lay on the planks between them like a fish.

PellNine.

He had been leaning on the pole with the face of a man deciding whether to confess to a small crime.

PellI have pulled this raft on a hundred and eighteen metres of cable for nineteen years. At harvest it parted at the south ring. I spliced it, and the ends would not meet, so I shackled on nine metres of my father's anchor chain and told myself I had measured wrong. A ferryman who miscounts does not advertise it.

IlluminaraTaut or slack, when you measured? Spliced, or sagged?

PellTaut as a harp. Three times, mostly hoping to prove myself wrong.

Illuminara waited. Two measurements, made by people who disliked one another, against stones neither of them owned, had landed on one number, and a third, made by a man who had hoped to be wrong, had landed on it too. Had she been mistaken, the stones would have agreed with the book and the cable would have fitted.

OdoSo the river did not lie.

HanneThe valley. It opened at the harvest, while we were stacking sheaves. Each of us built to a true line, and the ground walked off from under it.

IlluminaraThrough the meadows, not through the villages.

HanneThe houses stand on rock. Nobody noticed, because nobody lives in a meadow. The bridge was the only thing we had built on one.

OdoI called you a liar in front of both villages.

HanneI called you a worse word. I will think about taking yours back after the bridge stands.

It was not forgiveness and Illuminara did not reach for it. It was the first sentence either of them had said to the other that included the word after.

04 · Three Designs, Refused

The north abutment shed · evening, day six, rain on the tarpaulin

They sat at one table, which had taken six days and a great deal of tea. Odo drew on the back of a seed catalogue, Hanne in the margin of her survey book, and Pell held the lamp and a mug of something he called medicinal.

OdoWe lengthen my span by nine metres. A new pier, a new beam. Twelve weeks.

HanneThe flood is in four days. Mine is the barges: moor the Marl and the Teasel nose to tail across the gap and call it a pontoon.

OdoThe first crest takes it. And the ice takes what the crest leaves.

HanneAnd twelve weeks takes Seren's winter, Odo.

They both turned, with something like relief, to the visitor who could have ended the argument in an afternoon.

IlluminaraI can swing a twelve-metre beam across the gap, set it on both tips and be gone by dinner. I am saying it aloud so that you can say no.

HanneNo.

OdoNo. Then it is yours, and when it cracks in March you will be somewhere three skies away, and who do we shout at?

HanneA bridge that nobody here built is a bridge nobody here can mend.

Illuminara wrote not mine on the catalogue and underlined it. She felt, absurdly, as if she had been given something.

IlluminaraYou are both designing for the valley you surveyed. Design for the one that exists. A bridge that expects to be pulled, and is not frightened when it is.

PellThen a floated span? You bring the beam in on the barges at a rising river, and you do not lift it. You settle it.

It took the evening to turn a ferryman's guess into a design. Hanne knew barges and mill sluices, where a timber rides in a slot so a frame can move without cracking. Odo knew what a bearing could stand. Hanne drew the slot; Odo made it forty centimetres at each end, because the valley had opened nine metres in a night and nine centimetres since April, and he would not be the man who built a bridge that assumed it had stopped.

HanneThe slot closes by a hand's width, we meet. Both crews. Nobody waits for the village council.

OdoAnd the stop. If the staff on Pell's landing reads one point nine, the span does not go. Either of us rings the bell twice, and the barges are cut loose.

IlluminaraThen that bell is yours, not mine. If it rings twice I go home. And this is all I can do, so plan around it: I can hold the two barges against the current for forty minutes. Not the beam. The lines carry the beam. I take the shocks. After that I am no more use than a sack of grain.

HanneForty minutes is enough if we do not argue.

OdoWe will not argue.

Pell, who had known them both for nineteen years, raised his medicinal mug and said nothing at all.

05 · The Lowering

The gap, midstream · afternoon, day nine

The river was rising at six centimetres an hour and the staff on the landing read one point six two. Sixteen people on each bank held the lines, two sets of them crossed, bow to opposite stern, so that neither barge could walk ahead of the other. The Marl and the Teasel lay side by side in the gap, nose upstream, with a hand's breadth of brown water between them, and the box beam rode across both on its cradles: twelve metres of larch and iron that two villages had built in three days, a metre and a half of it over each tip, trembling in the current like a horse that has seen a ghost.

Illuminara went into the water at the bows. It was colder than she had decided to expect. She laid a palm on each barge and felt the river lean on them, a crowd with a single bad idea, and she leaned back.

HanneValves shut, tanks dry. Freeboard?

OdoForty centimetres on the Marl, with the beam aboard.

HanneForty on the Teasel. That floats the beam twice over, so one sticking valve will not sink us. Level to the hand.

OdoCross-lines taut. Pins ready.

PellOne point six three.

It was what six an hour promised. At the tenth minute an alder came down the middle of the river with its root-plate up, turning slowly, and every head on both banks turned with it.

OdoOne bell.

He rang it once. Everyone froze. On the south bank Hanne's crew swung out a boom pole and walked the trunk off the Teasel's quarter with a long, rude, patient shove, and the bell was silent.

HanneClear.

PellOne point six six.

He said it twice, because the first time nobody wanted it.

PellThree centimetres since the tree, and four minutes of them. That is not six an hour. That is forty-five. The water is brown with rain I cannot see.

Hanne did the sum aloud, because someone had to, and Odo did it after her to see that it matched.

HanneValves at twenty-four minutes, beam seated by thirty-three, pins by thirty-five, lines off at thirty-seven. The staff will read one point eight three.

OdoSeven under the line. Nine minutes of river we do not own.

HanneThen nobody opens a valve late.

IlluminaraI have forty minutes. You are asking me for thirty-seven.

HanneI am asking you for thirty-seven.

Nobody rang. Illuminara counted minutes in the weight on her hands. Sixteen. Eighteen. At twenty, the river tried something different, a sidelong shove from the south, and she took it in the arms and the spine and gave nothing back.

PellOne point seven four.

HanneValves. Open, together, on my count. Three. Two. One.

The barges sank, very slowly, under the weight of the water they had been told to take, and the cross-lines creaked and held, and the beam went down with them with a sound like a very large animal breathing out. It took nine minutes, and nobody afterwards called them short. The north bearing met the north tip. The south bearing met the south. The slot took the first of its forty centimetres of travel with a small, clean tick.

OdoNorth home. I have a hand under the shoe: seated.

HanneSouth home. Daylight under both cradles.

OdoPin.

HannePinned.

PellOne point eight two.

HanneCross-lines off. Marl first, then the Teasel. Slack, not pull. There is nothing holding that beam up now but the stone.

At thirty-seven minutes the lines came off. The Marl came out clean from under the span, stern first, hauled by the north crew. The Teasel, pumped light as she cleared, slewed, found the current and went away downriver, riding high and empty, and Hanne watched her go and said nothing for a long time.

OdoI will build you another.

HanneYou will build me a better one. Come to my yard on Thursday.

Illuminara climbed the bank on shaking legs and sat in the mud. At dusk Seren Ashby came to the north tip with her leather bag. She waited until Odo and Hanne had walked it twice, then went out onto the span, stopped where the beam met the tip, and set one boot across the dark seam. The plank moved a hair under her. She nodded to it as one might to a horse. Then she walked into Upper Wend, and no one said anything, because nothing larger could be said.

06 · The Slot

The bridge, midspan · five weeks later, hard frost

The crest had come at two point six on the staff and gone, and the bridge had stood it with water slapping the soffit and every pin in its place. Seren had crossed six times since. The Wend ferry, retired to the bank, had a small boy asleep in it.

A week ago a tremor, smaller than the one at harvest, had walked through the valley. Pell had come out on the bridge with a bucket of hot water for his hands and a tape and found the slot a little narrower. Now, on the first morning with ice on the rails, the two crews stood at midspan with their hands in their sleeves, and Illuminara stood slightly to one side, having been asked to come and not asked to do anything.

OdoThirty-seven.

HanneThirty-seven. It started at forty. Three centimetres, in a week.

OdoThen the valley is not done.

HanneNo. And the bridge is not, either. When it reads ten, we come.

Illuminara laid her palm on the beam, out of habit. Hanne watched her do it.

HanneWe have a tape.

IlluminaraI know. I wanted to feel it.

HanneFeel it afterwards.

The book on the rail was the bridge-warden's book, which had two hands in it already, Odo's square and Hanne's flat, and a third in the margin, Pell's, who had been named warden by both villages and been paid by both, to his visible alarm. Odo read the figure aloud, Hanne wrote it, and each of them signed beneath it, once.

SerenMarta's boy came on Tuesday. I walked.

She went by them with her bag, with the plank ringing very faintly under her step. They were not friends. They were two people who had agreed to read the same tape, and over a gap that had once been nine metres, that was enough to walk on.

What the critic still says doesn't work

Illuminara discovers that Odo and Hanne measured from the same fixed truth while the ground itself moved, then helps them test and freely build a maintainable moving bridge whose safety belongs to both crews; Seren crosses before the flood, the span survives later movement and shared stewardship remains after Illuminara leaves.

Tonight's shape

Six scenes across ten days and one frosty morning: a builder's accusation at the north tip, a ferry crossing and two survey books that agree with themselves, the old stones and the cable that add up to nine, a table where three designs are refused, a lowering against a river that rises faster than forecast, and a joint that is read aloud every winter.

01 · Devet metrov

Zgornji Wend, severni konec mostu · zora, deset dni pred poplavo

Dve polovici mostu sta segali nad reko kakor roki ljudi, ki sta se dogovorila, da si bosta segla v dlan, nato pa si premislila. Med koncema je bilo devet metrov rjave vode. Vrvica, napeta od ograje do ograje, bi pokazala tudi, da je severni razpon usmerjen devet centimetrov levo od južnega, in prav zaradi tega sta vasi kričali.

Illuminara je stopala po Odovi strani čez tako sveže deske, da so še dišale po macesnu. Odo iz Zgornjega Wenda ji je sledil z beležnico, stisnjeno ob prsi, kakor človek drži ščit, za katerega pričakuje, da ga bo moral odložiti.

OdoSmer sem zakoličil od Desetinskega kamna. Od istega kamna, ki ga je uporabila Hanne Sull. Poligon sem dvakrat sklenil na centimeter natančno. Zdaj mi boš rekla, da je ona napačno merila. Če mi to dokažeš, te bom na hrbtu nesel čez reko.

IlluminaraTega ti še ne bom rekla. Vedeti hočem, v čem bi se moral motiti vsak od vaju, in to hočem videti sama. Do poplave je deset dni. Potem trajekt do aprila ne bo vozil. V Zgornjem Wendu bo do takrat rodilo enajst žensk, babica pa je ena sama in živi na drugem bregu.

OdoSeren Ashby. Seren poznam, odkar je imela devet let. Misliš, da me je treba spomniti?

IlluminaraNe. Mislim, da si potreboval nekoga, ki bo to izrekel, da ti ne bi bilo treba prvi.

Pogledal jo je, nato v vodo, kakor človek pogleda dolg.

OdoČe praviš, da sva imela oba prav, praviš, da je lagala reka. Reke ne lažejo. Nekdo je napačno zakoličil smer, mi pa nismo bili.

Ni mu ugovarjala. Za jezo je stal človek, ki je dve poletji dvigoval tramove na smer, ki ji je zaupal svoje ime, in tega mu ni nameravala odvzeti, dokler ni imela nečesa, kar bi postavila na njeno mesto.

IlluminaraPotem mi pokaži, kako si meril. Počasi, kakor da sem začetnica. Naredila bom natanko tako, kot mi rečeš.

Od dneva, ko se je gradnja ustavila, ni nihče prosil Oda, naj koga česa nauči. Odložil je beležnico, iz plašča vzel merilno vizirko in začel.

02 · Dve pravilni smeri

Spodnji Wend, južni pristan in geodetska lopa · opoldne

Trajekt je bil ravna splav na vrvi, ki jo je njegov čuvaj Pell Garrow poganjal z drogom, potrpežljivo kakor človek, plačan za prečkanje. Na polovici poti vrv ni več nosila bremena; prevzel ga je kos stare sidrne verige, ki je rožljala skozi škripec. Illuminara je gledala, kako teče skozi kolo, in opazila, da je to opazila.

Seren Ashby je čakala na pristanu z usnjeno torbo pri nogah, šestdesetletna in trdna kakor vratni podboj.

SerenPred marcem jih bo v Zgornjem Wendu enajst, jaz pa sem bila pri devetih od zadnjih desetih porodov. Če si prišla, da bi te ganil moj pogum, si prihrani trud. Raje bi šla peš.

IlluminaraPotem ti bom poskusila priskrbeti nekaj, po čemer boš lahko hodila.

Hanne Sull je geodetsko izmero vodila tako kakor pripravo čaja: močno in odmerjeno na gram. Vsaka stran njene merilne knjige se je začela z drobno risbo vremena v črnilu: sonce, oblak, en zlovoljen dež. Illuminari je pokazala verigo, grezilo, vizirko in računanje sklenjenega poligona, nato pa prekrižanih rok gledala, kako je obiskovalka najprej vse naredila narobe in potem prav.

Do večera je Illuminara z rokami obeh ekip ponovno opravila njuni izmeri. Severni poligon se je sklenil na osem milimetrov, južni na šest. Knjigi je položila drugo ob drugo na mizo v lopi in v nobeni ni našla napake, večje od nohta.

HanneOdu sem to povedala julija. Rekel je, da je nemogoče, kar ljudje rečejo takrat, ko ni nemogoče.

IlluminaraNi. Obe smeri sta dobri, zato se nista premaknili smeri. Vsak od vaju je začel na enem mestu. Katerem?

HannePri Desetinskem kamnu na severni terasi. Na skali. Ta se ni mogel premakniti.

Obmolknila je z dvignjenim peresom.

HanneLanskega aprila je bila moja vizura proti južnemu oporniku za devet centimetrov iz smeri kamna. Zapisala sem, da se je raztegnila veriga. Verige se raztezajo.

IlluminaraKamen stoji na skali, zato se ni premaknil. Kaj pa stoji na nečem, kar se lahko?

HanneTravniki. Ravnice med teraso in vodo. Na njih nihče ne živi. Samo prečkamo jih.

IlluminaraJe tam spodaj kaj, kar ne pripada nobenemu od vaju?

HanneDesetinski kamni. Po eden na vsakih sto korakov ob obeh poteh do broda; postavili so jih, še preden je katera od vasi imela oskrbnika, in vpisani so v župnijsko knjigo. Most leži v smeri broda.

IlluminaraPotem so ti kamni merilni trak, ki si ga tu ne lasti nihče.

Sama v mraku je Illuminara položila dlan na južni temelj, nato pa po prečkanju še na severnega. Oba sta sporočala isto, kakor vrv pove, da nekdo vleče na drugem koncu: dva mogočna bloka na obeh straneh ničesar, ki ju je počasi vleklo vsakega proč od drugega. Občutek je bil hipoteza, na njeni dlani pa nihče ni nameraval obesiti mostu.

03 · Vsota

Trajektni pristan, Spodnji Wend · večer, četrti dan

Preizkus je bil poceni in lahko bi jo osramotil. Če so se tla med skalo in reko raztegnila, bi to povedali stari kamni. Stali so sto korakov narazen ob obeh poteh, vpisani so bili v župnijsko knjigo in niso pripadali nobeni ekipi. Vsakega graditelja je prosila, naj kamen za kamnom ponovno premeri nasprotni breg, dolžino primerja s knjigo in prinese eno številko.

Odo je ob zori odšel na jug, skozi Spodnji Wend pa ga je spremljal pes. Hanne je opoldne odšla na sever, prvič po dnevu ustavitve gradnje stopila v Zgornji Wend in prosila otroka, naj ji pokaže pot. Zdaj sta stala na nasprotnih koncih Pellove splavi, edinih nevtralnih tleh v dolini.

OdoTri cela šest. Od južne terase do vode je njena pot za toliko daljša, kot piše v knjigi. Del tega je v enajstih razpokah in stopnicah: v sadovnjaškem zidu, poti ob vodnjaku, stopnici cerkvenega dvorišča. Ostalo je v glini.

HannePet celih štiri na vaši strani, merjeno enako. Vrata v vaši spodnji ulici se tako zatikajo, da pek pleza skozi okno.

OdoV tem uživa.

Nihče ni izrekel vsote. Ležala je na deskah med njima kakor riba.

PellDevet.

Na drog se je naslanjal z obrazom človeka, ki se odloča, ali naj prizna majhen zločin.

PellDevetnajst let vlečem to splav na sto osemnajstih metrih jeklenice. Ob žetvi se je pretrgala pri južnem obroču. Spojil sem jo, konca pa se nista več srečala, zato sem dodal devet metrov očetove sidrne verige in si dopovedoval, da sem narobe meril. Trajektar, ki se ušteje, tega ne obeša na veliki zvon.

IlluminaraJe bila med merjenjem napeta ali ohlapna? Spojena ali povešena?

PellNapeta kakor harfa. Trikrat, večinoma zato, ker sem hotel dokazati, da se motim.

Illuminara je čakala. Dve meritvi ljudi, ki si nista bila naklonjena, opravljeni po kamnih, ki niso pripadali nobenemu od njiju, sta dali isto število; tretja, ki jo je opravil človek, željan zmote, prav tako. Če bi se motila, bi se kamni ujemali s knjigo, jeklenica pa bi segla do obroča.

OdoTorej reka ni lagala.

HanneDolina. Odprla se je med žetvijo, ko smo zlagali snope. Vsak od naju je gradil po pravilni smeri, tla pa so se umaknila izpod nje.

IlluminaraSkozi travnike, ne skozi vasi.

HanneHiše stojijo na skali. Nihče ni opazil, ker nihče ne živi na travniku. Most je bil edina stvar, ki smo jo zgradili na enem.

OdoPred obema vasema sem te označil za lažnivko.

HanneJaz sem te imenovala še huje. O umiku tvojih besed bom razmislila, ko bo most stal.

To ni bilo odpuščanje in Illuminara ni posegla po njem. Bil je prvi stavek, ki ga je eden od njiju namenil drugemu in je vseboval besedo ko.

04 · Trije načrti, zavrnjeni

Lopa ob severnem oporniku · večer, šesti dan, dež na ponjavi

Sedeli so za eno mizo; zanjo so potrebovali šest dni in veliko čaja. Odo je risal na hrbtno stran kataloga semen, Hanne na rob svoje geodetske knjige, Pell pa je držal svetilko in vrček nečesa, čemur je pravil zdravilo.

OdoMoj razpon podaljšamo za devet metrov. Nov steber, nov nosilec. Dvanajst tednov.

HannePoplava pride čez štiri dni. Moj predlog sta barki: Marl in Teasel privežemo z nosom proti krmi čez vrzel in temu rečemo pontonski most.

OdoOdnesel ga bo prvi vodni val. Led pa bo odnesel, kar bo ostalo.

HanneDvanajst tednov pa bo Seren vzelo zimo, Odo.

Oba sta se z nečim, kar je bilo podobno olajšanju, obrnila k obiskovalki, ki bi lahko spor končala v enem popoldnevu.

IlluminaraČez vrzel lahko zavihtim dvanajstmetrski nosilec, ga položim na oba konca in do večerje odidem. To povem na glas zato, da lahko rečeta ne.

HanneNe.

OdoNe. Potem je tvoj, in ko marca poči, boš nekje tri neba proč. Na koga naj kričimo?

HanneMost, ki ga ni zgradil nihče od tukajšnjih, je most, ki ga nihče od tukajšnjih ne zna popraviti.

Illuminara je na katalog zapisala ni moj in besedi podčrtala. Nesmiselno se ji je zdelo, kakor da je nekaj dobila.

IlluminaraOba načrtujeta za dolino, ki sta jo izmerila. Načrtujta za tisto, ki obstaja. Most, ki pričakuje, da ga bodo vlekli, in se tega ne boji.

PellPotem plavajoči razpon? Nosilec pripeljete na barkah ob naraščajoči reki in ga ne dvignete. Spustite ga na mesto.

Ves večer so trajektarjevo domislico spreminjali v načrt. Hanne je poznala barke in mlinske zapornice, kjer les drsi v reži, da se okvir premika, ne da bi počil. Odo je vedel, kaj prenese ležišče. Hanne je narisala režo; Odo ji je na vsakem koncu določil štirideset centimetrov, kajti dolina se je v eni noči odprla za devet metrov, od aprila pa še za devet centimetrov, on pa ni hotel biti človek, ki bi zgradil most ob predpostavki, da se je ustavila.

HanneKo se reža zmanjša za širino dlani, se dobimo. Obe ekipi. Nihče ne čaka vaškega sveta.

OdoIn ustavitev. Če merilna letev na Pellovem pristanu pokaže ena cela devet, razpona ne spuščamo. Kdor koli od naju dvakrat pozvoni in barki odrežemo.

IlluminaraPotem je ta zvon vajin, ne moj. Če zazvoni dvakrat, odidem domov. In to je vse, kar zmorem, zato načrtujta temu primerno: barki lahko proti toku držim štirideset minut. Ne nosilca. Nosilec držijo vrvi. Jaz prestrezam sunke. Potem sem uporabna toliko kot vreča žita.

HanneŠtirideset minut je dovolj, če se ne prepiramo.

OdoNe bova se.

Pell, ki ju je poznal devetnajst let, je dvignil vrček svojega zdravila in ostal popolnoma tiho.

05 · Spuščanje

Vrzel sredi reke · popoldne, deveti dan

Reka je naraščala šest centimetrov na uro, merilna letev na pristanu pa je kazala ena cela dvainšestdeset. Na vsakem bregu je vrvi držalo šestnajst ljudi; dva sklopa sta bila prekrižana, premec ene barke s krmo druge, da nobena ni mogla uiti pred drugo. Marl in Teasel sta ležali druga ob drugi v vrzeli, s premcema proti toku in za dlan rjave vode med njima, škatlasti nosilec pa je na zibelkah počival čez obe: dvanajst metrov macesna in železa, ki sta ga vasi zgradili v treh dneh, poldrugi meter čez vsak konec mostu, v toku tresoč se kakor konj, ki je zagledal duha.

Illuminara je pri premcih stopila v vodo. Bila je hladnejša, kot se je odločila pričakovati. Dlan je položila na vsako barko in začutila, kako se reka naslanja nanju, množica z eno samo slabo zamislijo, sama pa se je uprla nazaj.

HanneVentili zaprti, rezervoarji prazni. Nadvodje?

OdoŠtirideset centimetrov na Marlu, z nosilcem na krovu.

HanneŠtirideset na Teaselu. To zagotavlja dvakratni vzgon za nosilec, zato nas en zataknjen ventil ne bo potopil. Izravnano na dlan.

OdoPrečne vrvi napete. Zatiči pripravljeni.

PellEna cela triinšestdeset.

Natanko toliko je obljubljalo šest centimetrov na uro. V deseti minuti je po sredini reke s koreninami navzgor priplavala jelša, ki se je počasi vrtela, in vse glave na obeh bregovih so se obrnile za njo.

OdoEn zvonec.

Pozvonil je enkrat. Vsi so otrpnili. Na južnem bregu je Hannina ekipa iztegnila odrivni drog in deblo z dolgim, neprijaznim, potrpežljivim potiskom odrinila od Teaselovega boka; zvon je molčal.

HanneProsto.

PellEna cela šestinšestdeset.

Povedal je dvakrat, ker prvič tega nihče ni hotel slišati.

PellTri centimetre od drevesa, in to v štirih minutah. To ni šest na uro. To je petinštirideset. Voda je rjava od dežja, ki ga ne vidim.

Hanne je račun opravila na glas, ker ga je nekdo moral, Odo pa ga je ponovil za njo, da se je prepričal o ujemanju.

HanneVentile odpremo v štiriindvajseti minuti, nosilec sede do triintridesete, zatiči do petintridesete, vrvi dol do sedemintridesete. Letev bo kazala ena cela triinosemdeset.

OdoSedem pod mejo. Devet minut reke, ki niso naše.

HannePotem nihče ne odpre ventila prepozno.

IlluminaraImam štirideset minut. Prosite me za sedemintrideset.

HanneProsim te za sedemintrideset.

Nihče ni pozvonil. Illuminara je minute štela v teži na svojih dlaneh. Šestnajst. Osemnajst. Pri dvajsetih je reka poskusila nekaj drugega, bočni sunek z juga, in sprejela ga je v roke in hrbtenico ter ni popustila niti za las.

PellEna cela štiriinsedemdeset.

HanneVentili. Odpremo skupaj, ko odštejem. Tri. Dve. Ena.

Barki sta se zelo počasi pogrezali pod težo vode, ki so jima jo ukazali sprejeti, prečne vrvi so škripale in držale, z njima pa se je spuščal nosilec z zvokom, kakor da izdihuje zelo velika žival. Trajalo je devet minut in nihče jim pozneje ni rekel, da so bile kratke. Severno ležišče je srečalo severni konec. Južno ležišče južnega. Reža je s tihim, čistim tleskom sprejela prvega od svojih štiridesetih centimetrov pomika.

OdoSever na mestu. Roko imam pod čevljem: sedi.

HanneJug na mestu. Pod obema zibelkama je svetloba.

OdoZatič.

HanneZavarovano.

PellEna cela dvainosemdeset.

HannePrečne vrvi dol. Najprej Marl, nato Teasel. Popusti, ne vleci. Tega nosilca zdaj ne drži nič drugega kot kamen.

V sedemintrideseti minuti so sneli vrvi. Marl je čisto zdrsnil izpod razpona, s krmo naprej, vlekla ga je severna ekipa. Teasel, razbremenjen s črpanjem, je med umikom zaneslo, ujel ga je tok in odneslo navzdol po reki; vozil je visoko in prazen, Hanne pa ga je dolgo gledala odhajati in ni rekla ničesar.

OdoZgradil ti bom drugega.

HanneZgradil mi boš boljšega. V četrtek pridi na moje dvorišče.

Illuminara je na tresočih nogah splezala na breg in sedla v blato. Ob mraku je Seren Ashby prišla na severni konec z usnjeno torbo. Počakala je, da sta ga Odo in Hanne dvakrat prehodila, nato stopila na razpon, se ustavila tam, kjer je nosilec srečal konec mostu, ter z enim škornjem stopila čez temni stik. Deska se je pod njo premaknila za las. Pokimala ji je, kakor človek pokima konju. Nato je odšla v Zgornji Wend in nihče ni rekel ničesar, ker večjega ni bilo mogoče povedati.

06 · Reža

Most, sredina razpona · pet tednov pozneje, hud mraz

Vrh poplave je prišel do dve celi šest na merilni letvi in odšel; most ga je prestal z vodo, ki je udarjala ob spodnji rob razpona, in z vsemi zatiči na mestu. Seren ga je odtlej prečkala šestkrat. Na breg umaknjeni trajekt Wend je v sebi skrival spečega dečka.

Pred tednom dni je dolino prešel potresni sunek, manjši od tistega ob žetvi. Pell je prišel na most z vedrom vroče vode za roke in merilnim trakom ter ugotovil, da je reža nekoliko ožja. Zdaj, prvo jutro z ledom na ograjah, sta obe ekipi stali na sredini razpona z rokami v rokavih, Illuminara pa nekoliko ob strani, ker so jo prosili, naj pride, ne pa tudi, naj kaj stori.

OdoSedemintrideset.

HanneSedemintrideset. Začelo se je pri štiridesetih. Trije centimetri v enem tednu.

OdoPotem dolina še ni končala.

HanneNe. In tudi most ne. Ko bo kazalo deset, pridemo.

Illuminara je iz navade položila dlan na nosilec. Hanne jo je pri tem opazovala.

HanneImamo merilni trak.

IlluminaraVem. Hotela sem ga začutiti.

HanneČuti ga potem.

Knjiga na ograji je bila mostovžarska knjiga; v njej sta bili že dve pisavi, Odova oglata in Hannina ploščata, na robu pa še tretja, Pellova. Obe vasi sta ga imenovali za mostovžarja in obe sta ga plačevali, kar ga je vidno vznemirjalo. Odo je številko prebral na glas, Hanne jo je zapisala in vsak od njiju se je pod njo enkrat podpisal.

SerenMartin deček je prišel v torek. Šla sem peš.

S torbo je odšla mimo njih, deska pa ji je zelo rahlo zazvenela pod korakom. Nista bila prijatelja. Bila sta človeka, ki sta se dogovorila, da bosta brala isti merilni trak, in nad vrzeljo, ki je bila nekoč široka devet metrov, je bilo to dovolj, da si lahko hodil.

Kaj po mnenju kritika še ne deluje

Illuminara odkrije, da sta Odo in Hanne merila od iste trdne resnice, medtem ko so se premaknila tla sama, nato pa jima pomaga preizkusiti in svobodno zgraditi vzdržljiv gibljivi most, za varnost katerega skrbita obe ekipi; Seren ga prečka pred poplavo, razpon prenese poznejše premikanje, skupno skrbništvo pa ostane tudi po Illuminarnem odhodu.

Zasnova te zgodbe

Šest prizorov v desetih dneh in enem mrzlem jutru: graditeljeva obtožba na severnem koncu, vožnja s trajektom in dve geodetski knjigi, ki sta vsaka zase skladni, stari kamni in jeklenica, katerih meritve skupaj nanesejo devet, miza, za katero zavrnejo tri načrte, spuščanje ob reki, ki narašča hitreje od napovedi, ter spoj, katerega mero vsako zimo glasno preberejo.
