What both versions share

Understanding is not the soft part of the story. It is the hardest action.

01 · Protect the pause

Keep people alive long enough to choose

Immediate harm comes first. Nara uses people, tools and procedures; Illuminara can also use AC protection. Neither mistakes a speech for rescue.

02 · Make both sides recognizable

Steel-man before persuading

Each side must hear its strongest concern stated accurately enough to correct the account rather than merely submit to it.

03 · Build, object, release

No prewritten perfect answer

The affected beings test proposals, expose failures and choose a bounded next step. Protection creates room for agency; it never replaces agency.

One alignment target · two pathologies

Goodness can fail through centrality.

Nara can move before another person has finished choosing. Illuminara can keep every option open until no choice is permitted to become real.

The comparison is the point: more power does not merely enlarge the same danger. It changes the shape of the danger.

Causal boundary: the universes do not exchange dreams, signals, artifacts, memories or power. Their relationship is an ethical comparison, not a hidden crossover mechanism.
