Two original parallel continuities

Nara / Illuminara

They share a face and one direction: good for the whole and for every distinct being within it. They do not share a world, powers, memories or a path between universes.

Grounded Nara tests that desire against finite knowledge and physics. Illuminara tests it against immense power, irreversible choice and the freedom to end even a protected conversation.

Two Naras · two failure modes

Neither is an origin form, upgrade or correction of the other.

Each continuity must earn its own emotional life, opponents, mistakes and consequences.

Grounded continuity · original IP

Nara — Near the Edge of Possible

A physically human medical-logistics worker whose attention, systems reasoning and courage can reopen a crisis. Her danger is understanding people so quickly that she moves before they have chosen.

Illuminara — The Impossible Conversation

Shared moral geometry

Understanding is not the soft part of the story. It is the hardest action.

Keep people alive long enough to choose

Nara uses people, tools and procedures; Illuminara can also use AC protection. Neither mistakes a speech for rescue.

Steel-man before persuading

Each opponent must hear the strongest version of what they protect—and retain the right to say the hero still has it wrong.

No prewritten perfect answer

The affected people test proposals, expose failures and may build a third path, refuse it or close the conversation.

Parallel, not connected: no shared dreams, signals, artifacts or secret power progression. Their rhyme is ethical, not causal.
