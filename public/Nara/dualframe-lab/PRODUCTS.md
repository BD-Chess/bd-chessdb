# DualFrame products

Concept and product direction: Bojan Dobrečevič (BD).

DualFrame Photos (DFP) is the existing photographic product at this URL. Its Gallery, Create and Compare tools retain the existing library, owner session, Sync and PWA installation identity.

DualFrame Cartoons (DFC) is a disabled product placeholder. It has no route, persisted product selection, library access, provider action or production backend. The native disabled control and localized explanation remain visible in the product selector.

Future DFC scope is its own gallery, comic reader and eventual creation workflow. The first example is `ILLUMINARA_TEZA_SVETLOBE_01`, supplied later by H1/H2 under `DFC_SHARED_CONTRACT_20261016_R1.json`. Their separate reader demo does not enable DFC here. Future integration must preserve DFP data identities and require its own reviewed release.

Public accounts, registration, billing and commercialization remain deferred. Display branding does not authorize a database, namespace, URL or account migration.
