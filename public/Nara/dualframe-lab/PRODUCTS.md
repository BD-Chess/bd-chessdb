# DualFrame products

Concept and product direction: Bojan Dobrečevič (BD).

DualFrame Photos (DFP) is the existing photographic product at this URL. Its Gallery, Create and Compare tools retain the existing library, owner session, Sync and PWA installation identity.

DualFrame Cartoons (DFC) now has a read-only sample gallery and reader for Illuminara, `ILLUMINARA_TEZA_SVETLOBE_01`: eight complete source pages in SL/EN, 39 mapped original panels, exact separately composed dialogue, remembered place, keyboard controls and rotation-safe navigation. Double tap/click a mapped panel, or use its accessible button. Source-only panels show **Original panel**, never a fabricated P/L composition. Its sample needs internet; large story media are never automatically added to the offline app shell.

BD's activated R3 APP mandate supersedes the historical disabled-Cartoons rule only for this bounded sample gallery/reader. Creation remains disabled with Coming soon. No generation, private-photo reads/writes, DFC backend, account or billing flow is introduced. Switching product retains the selected Photos tab, settings, filters, originals and ordering. The URL/install identity and existing Photos manifest remain unchanged.

DFC read state uses only `bd.dfc.reader.v1:ILLUMINARA_TEZA_SVETLOBE_01`, outside the Photos database and Sync. App defaults to Photos on restart; the explicit Cartoons choice opens its separate gallery. H1 source catalog/dialogue/release remain byte-identical provenance anchors. H2 scaffold geometry, state, navigation and bounded cache were reviewed and adapted; its fixture manifest and storage key are absent from production.

The build-time source-bound paired-art importer and continuation instructions are in `docs/dualframe/DFC_IMPORT.md`. Real artwork comes from the separate ART task. Readiness is per panel; only a validated complete pair enables deliberate P/L compositions. Until that delivery, the same source panel fits either viewport with its exact transcript. Technical integration is distinct from BD artistic review of P02–P08 and physical-device acceptance.

Public accounts, registration, billing and commercialization remain deferred. Display branding does not authorize a database, namespace, URL or account migration.
