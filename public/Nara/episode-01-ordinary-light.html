During a storm blackout, a medical-logistics worker turns one lost boot into a voluntary path out. Hours later, an altered state tempts her with the feeling of completion. When her storm-damaged home begins to fail, Nara discovers that seeing a load path is not the same as carrying it—and that real weight belongs to steel, tools and other people.Med izpadom elektrike v neurju delavka v zdravstveni logistiki iz enega izgubljenega škornja naredi prostovoljno pot ven. Nekaj ur pozneje jo spremenjeno stanje zavesti zamika z občutkom dovršenosti. Ko njen od neurja poškodovani dom začne popuščati, Nara odkrije, da videti pot bremena ni isto kot nositi ga – in da resnična teža pripada jeklu, orodju in drugim ljudem.
