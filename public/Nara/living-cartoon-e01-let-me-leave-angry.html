01

Just Rain Samo dež

Adaeze’s kitchen · ordinary rain · three hours after the orbital corridor Adaezina kuhinja · navaden dež · tri ure po orbitalnem koridorju

Three hours earlier she had held an evacuation corridor open between two orbital habitats while a hull fragment the size of a courthouse crossed where her body had been. The field had put her back. Adaeze had made stew.

Tri ure prej je držala odprt evakuacijski koridor med dvema orbitalnima habitatoma, medtem ko je kos trupa v velikosti sodišča prečkal mesto, kjer je bilo njeno telo. Polje jo je sestavilo nazaj. Adaeze je skuhala enolončnico.

Na okenski polici so se tri lončnice bazilike nagibale k temnemu steklu. AC ni bil okras. Nič ni žarelo samo zato, ker je hči sedela nasproti svoje matere.

02

Good Reasons Dobri razlogi

Two bowls on the table · the blue thermos · the sixth anniversary Dve skledi na mizi · modra termovka · šesta obletnica

Every year on the morning her father died, Adaeze took coffee in the blue thermos to the harbour wall. Last year a city had been falling. This year fourteen million lives hung inside an orbital conflict zone.

Vsako leto je Adaeze na jutro očetove smrti odnesla kavo v modri termovki na pristaniški zid. Lani je padalo mesto. Letos je bilo v orbitalnem spopadu ujetih štirinajst milijonov življenj.

LIVE PANEL · 4.5s LOOP ŽIVI KADER · 4.5s ZANKA
Adaeze
“You missed his day again.”
»Spet si zamudila njegov dan.«
Nara
“I know… I sent you a message. I couldn’t leave.”
»Vem … Poslala sem ti sporočilo. Nisem mogla oditi.«
Adaeze
“You sent me an explanation. I didn’t ask whether you could. I need you to stop interpreting me.”
»Poslala si mi pojasnilo. Nisem vprašala, ali si mogla. Hočem, da me nehaš prevajati.«

Every sentence of Nara’s explanation was true. Every sentence was unbearable. For the first time, she let the argument remain unsaid.

Vsak stavek Narinega pojasnila je bil resničen. Vsak stavek je bil neznosen. Prvič je argument pustila neizrečen.

03

The Better Argument Boljši argument

Before AC · the court interpreter’s gift and wound Pred AC · dar in rana sodne tolmačke

Before AC, Nara had been a court interpreter. She could return anyone’s position clearer than they held it themselves. Once a man had told her: “She gave me my argument back better than I had it. I have never hated anyone more.”

Pred poljem AC je bila Nara sodna tolmačka. Vsakomur je znala vrniti njegovo stališče čistejše, kot ga je imel sam. Nekoč ji je nekdo rekel: »Moj argument mi je vrnila boljši, kot sem ga imel sam. Še nikogar nisem tako sovražil.«

“You think I turned him into a scheduling conflict.”

»Ne. To je boljše od tistega, kar sem rekla jaz. Čistejše. Bolj elegantno. Vedno ti je žal natanko na ravni, kjer problem postane obvladljiv.«

Nara could survive inside a star-facing rupture. She had no defence against the possibility that her mother was right.

Nara je lahko preživela razpoko, obrnjeno proti zvezdi. Ni pa imela obrambe pred možnostjo, da ima njena mati prav.

04

Do Not Make a Room Around This Ne delaj sobe okoli tega

A coat, a doorway and ten safe minutes Plašč, prag in deset varnih minut

Adaeze carried her bowl to the sink and took her coat from the chair. Something inside Nara tightened—not fear of danger, but fear of ending. She had already imagined holding ten safe minutes in which neither could leave in anger.

Adaeze je odnesla skledo v korito in vzela plašč s stola. Nekaj v Nari se je stisnilo — ne strah pred nevarnostjo, temveč strah pred koncem. Že si je predstavljala deset varnih minut, v katerih nobena ne bi mogla oditi v jezi.

LIVE PANEL · 4.5s LOOP ŽIVI KADER · 4.5s ZANKA
Adaeze
“Nara. Do not make a room around this.”
»Nara. Ne delaj sobe okoli tega.«
Nara
“I wasn’t going to.”
»Saj nisem nameravala.«

Nara felt the lie before she finished believing it—and released the thought. The room remained an ordinary kitchen.

Nara je začutila laž, še preden ji je do konca verjela — in misel izpustila. Soba je ostala navadna kuhinja.

05

Something Wider Nekaj širšega

The first offer of the wide window · a candle beside a sun Prva ponudba širokega okna · sveča ob soncu

For less than a second, Nara touched the threshold. The air became strangely clear—not bright, clear. One surface over a vast, unclaimed presence. She held only the protective edge around the person she loved.

Za manj kot sekundo se je Nara dotaknila praga. Zrak je postal nenavadno jasen — ne svetel, jasen. Ena površina nad neizmerno, neprisvojeno navzočnostjo. Držala je le zaščitni rob okoli človeka, ki ga je imela rada.

LIVE PANEL · 4.5s LOOP ŽIVI KADER · 4.5s ZANKA
Nara
“I can open something wider than the quiet window. The field holds your boundary while your awareness opens farther. I don’t choose what you encounter. I can’t make you forgive me.”
»Lahko odprem nekaj širšega od tihega okna. Polje drži tvojo mejo, medtem ko se tvoja zavest odpre dlje. Ne izbiram, kaj boš srečala. Ne morem te pripraviti, da mi odpustiš.«
Adaeze
“What would I see? My husband? … Would I be different?”
»Kaj bi videla? Svojega moža? … Bi bila potem drugačna?«
Nara
“I don’t know. Yes—because experiences change us. A candle beside a sun: protected, not absorbed.”
»Ne vem. Da — ker nas izkušnje spremenijo. Sveča ob soncu: zaščitena, ne posrkana.«

The threshold lasted half a heartbeat. Then Nara let it close. Adaeze had not entered. Nothing had reached her.

Prag je trajal pol srčnega utripa. Nato ga je Nara zaprla. Adaeze ni vstopila. Nič se je ni dotaknilo.

Act III · The Door III. dejanje · Vrata
06

Make the Larger Room Yours Narediš večjo sobo svojo

The threshold remains private · the right to choose the door Prag ostane zaseben · pravica izbrati vrata

Adaeze opened the door and cold hallway air entered. Every part of Illuminara that had ever held a collapsing boundary wanted to intervene—to warm the corridor, to open the window just a fraction.

Adaeze je odprla vrata in vstopil je hladen zrak s hodnika. Vsak del Illuminare, ki je kdaj držal razpadajočo mejo, je hotel poseči — ogreti hodnik, odpreti okno le za drobec.

LIVE PANEL · 4.5s LOOP ŽIVI KADER · 4.5s ZANKA
Adaeze
“You decide the room needs more complexity. More perspectives. More understanding. And because you can make that room, everyone else looks unreasonable for wanting the door.”
»Ti se odločiš, da soba potrebuje več kompleksnosti. Več perspektiv. Več razumevanja. In ker ti lahko narediš takšno sobo, so vsi drugi videti nerazumni, ker hočejo vrata.«
Adaeze
“No window. No better version of what I mean. No safe extra minute. I want to leave angry.”
»Brez okna. Brez boljše različice tega, kar mislim. Brez varne dodatne minute. Hočem oditi jezna.«
Nara
“I love you.”
»Rada te imam.«
Adaeze
“I know.”
»Vem.«

Nara stepped back from the doorway. She could preserve everything except the one thing Adaeze had asked for: the right to let the damage become real.

Nara je stopila korak nazaj od vrat. Ohranila bi lahko vse, razen edine stvari, za katero je prosila Adaeze: pravice, da škoda postane resnična.

07

The Window Remained Closed Okno je ostalo zaprto

The crooked basil plant · the notebook afterward Kriva bazilika · zvezek po odhodu

The door closed. No cosmic event followed. It was harder than holding the orbital corridor. On the windowsill, one basil plant had grown crooked toward the light across the street. Nara reached to turn the pot—and stopped. She left it as it was.

Vrata so se zaprla. Noben kozmični dogodek ni sledil. Bilo je težje kot držati orbitalni koridor. Na okenski polici je ena od bazilik zrasla postrani proti luči čez cesto. Nara je segla, da bi lonček obrnila — in se ustavila. Pustila ga je takšnega, kakršen je bil.

For once she did not improve her mother’s argument. She looked at the two sentences. They were accurate. They were not enough. She let them be enough anyway.

Tokrat materinega argumenta ni izboljšala. Pogledala je stavka. Bila sta natančna. Nista bila dovolj. Vseeno jima je dovolila, da sta dovolj.
