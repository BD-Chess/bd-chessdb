2026-10-31

Mayor Jori Pell hid a failing levee so insurers would not abandon Olven's poorest quarter. With twelve hours left, Illuminara must help fourteen thousand residents organize the truth before the truth is used to discard them.Župan Jori Pell je skrival popuščajoči nasip, da zavarovalnice ne bi zapustile najrevnejše olvenske četrti. Ker je ostalo dvanajst ur, mora Illuminara štirinajst tisoč prebivalcem pomagati organizirati resnico, preden bo resnica uporabljena za to, da jih zavržejo.

01 · The Dry Gauge

Olven river wall, gauge house · 11:42

The gauge said the river was four metres below the crown. Outside the window, brown water licked the seventh rung of a ladder whose seventh rung was three metres below the crown. Rain pressed the discrepancy flat against the glass.

Illuminara set two fingers on the wall. The levee answered through stone, clay and the iron ties driven into it a century earlier. A seam under the old dye market had opened along forty-one metres. Water was already writing itself into the crack.

Mayor Jori Pell stood beside the brass recorder with his coat buttoned wrong. He was forty-seven, broad-shouldered, and had been elected by the quarter whose roofs spread below them. He watched her read the wall and did not pretend surprise.

IlluminaraWhen did you lower the gauge?

Jori PellThe first time, twenty-two months ago. Twelve centimetres. Then the insurers sent a second warning, so I changed the conversion table. The river rose. The number did not.

IlluminaraHow long until the seam opens?

Jori PellYou tell me.

IlluminaraAt the present rise, shortly after midnight. Earlier if the timber crib below the market has rotted through.

He gave a single nod, as if she had confirmed a council appointment.

Jori PellThere are fourteen thousand people below us. If the raw series reaches the insurers before we move them, household review starts at once. Mortgages are called at noon tomorrow. Landlords lock the doors before the mud is dry.

IlluminaraAnd if it does not reach them?

Jori PellThen the water does.

He opened a drawer and took out two books. One held the public numbers in a careful clerk's hand. The other held the river as it had been: crossings-out, rain stains, and twenty-two months of truth.

Jori PellMake me honest by morning and them homeless by noon. Then stay for the locksmiths and the banks and everyone who says the quarter should never have been here.

The wall shuddered. A cup walked three millimetres across the recorder shelf and stopped.

Illuminara did not tell him that fear did not excuse the lie. He already knew what judgment sounded like. She asked the question that changed where the next hour had to begin.

IlluminaraWho can bind the residents into one claimant before you disclose?

Jori looked down through the rain at the community hall.

Jori PellLera Nwan. She will hate the question. She may still answer it.

02 · One Claimant

Low Quarter community hall · 12:35

Lera Nwan had turned the hall into an evacuation desk before the mayor arrived. Red chalk marked streets on the floor. Residents moved wooden tokens for buses, oxygen bottles, wheelchairs and animals across it. Nobody waited for permission to be useful.

Lera listened to Jori's account without blinking. When he reached the changed conversion table, she took the public gauge sheet from his hand, tore it once, and laid both halves on the table rather than throwing them at him.

Lera NwanMy mother renewed her shop because you showed us that sheet.

Jori PellI know.

Lera NwanNo. You know a shop. I know the room where she keeps every receipt from forty-three years because she thinks paper can make a place stay. Do not say you know.

Jori closed his mouth.

Illuminara explained the seam, the rise and the likely midnight failure in four sentences. Then she placed the two gauge books side by side. She did not touch them again.

IlluminaraOlven's charter lets households threatened by one public work file as one claimant. If their claim vests before discovery, a supervised pool pauses household cancellation while fraud is contested.

Lera NwanHow many signatures?

Jori PellThree-fifths of insured homes. The clause was written for earthquakes.

Lera NwanAnd who appoints the claimant's committee?

IlluminaraThe residents. Not the mayor. Not me.

Lera moved six red tokens away from the river road and onto the school ridge.

Lera NwanThen the first question is not whether we forgive him. It is whether we can move fourteen thousand people and collect eighteen hundred signatures before the wall opens.

Jori PellThe insurers will say the claim is poisoned by my fraud.

Lera NwanLet them say it to all of us at once.

A deep knock came through the floor. On the chalk map, one wooden dog tipped over.

Lera stood it up and pushed a blank committee form toward the room. Hands reached for it. Not all of them.

An elderly baker returned his token to his pocket. He would evacuate, he said, but he would not join a claim that made him share a future with the man who had lied to him. Lera wrote his refusal beside his address. The pool would have to protect him if it won, without pretending he had agreed.

Illuminara felt something like relief, sharp and tender: the quarter was already more honest than its gauge.

03 · Eleven Minutes

Community hall, records room · 14:10

Sol Tann, duty officer for the River Mutual Consortium, appeared on the wall screen in a grey room three cities away. Lera had insisted that the call be public. Two hundred residents filled the hall; hundreds more listened from doorways and radios while they packed.

Sol TannIf the mayor confirms deliberate material falsification, the cancellation engine reviews every policy linked to the gauge. I cannot suspend it by discretion.

Lera NwanCan a common-cause claim suspend it by law?

Sol TannIf validly filed first. It needs three-fifths, a named resident committee and evidence that the cause is one public work. If the false data entered the claim, we contest it.

IlluminaraThe claim will use the raw gauge book, the crack map and the engineer's present measurements. Nothing derived from the public series.

Sol TannThat is clean evidence. I cannot tell you how to file it.

IlluminaraThen answer only this: if the residents form the claimant first, Mayor Pell declares second, and the raw book travels inside that declaration, does the common-cause filing control household review?

Sol TannYes, if every part arrives within the eleven-minute window after declaration. I will not advise beyond that, and the consortium will contest the stay's scope.

A murmur crossed the hall like wind through reeds.

Lera held up the first page. Seven hundred and twelve signatures had arrived. They needed two and a half times that number.

Jori PellI will not confess tonight.

Lera turned toward him slowly.

Jori PellNot until the buses are loaded and the claim is ready. If one thing fails, I will not be the hand that makes both disasters happen.

Lera NwanYou were that hand twenty-two months ago.

He flinched, but did not change his answer.

Illuminara could have argued him through the logic. Instead she looked at the map. A blue token marked Vesper House, six floors, no lift, the street nearest the dye market.

IlluminaraCome with me.

Jori PellWhere?

IlluminaraTo carry what your number left downstairs.

She waited at the door until he chose to follow.

04 · What the Number Left

Vesper House, Low Quarter · 15:05

Water had not crossed the street yet, but it had entered through the drains. In Vesper House it rose black around the basement stairs, lifting jars from shelves and turning the boiler room light into a broken yellow road.

Jori knew which flat held insulin, which woman needed the narrow evacuation chair, and which boy would not leave without two white rats named Salt and Snow. His knowledge was not innocence. It was why people had trusted him enough for the lie to work.

He and Illuminara went into the basement tied to the same rope. She could have carried every crate at once. She carried one end of each, at his speed, while water reached their waists. On the fourth trip he found the old gauge notices bundled above the caretaker's desk. They were unopened, stamped with the mayor's office seal.

Jori PellI told them not to frighten the block with notices I had already answered.

IlluminaraDid you answer them?

Jori PellI answered the insurers.

A beam cracked below the stairs. Illuminara took its weight against her shoulder long enough for Jori to drag the medicine case and the two rats through the doorway. She did not mend the building. The residents had already chosen to leave it, and the beam's failure was evidence.

Outside, river engineer Evin Ser had driven a listening rod into the street. The rod trembled visibly.

Evin SerThe crib under the dye market is gone. We have nine hours if the rise stays smooth. Seven if the upstream lock releases on schedule.

Jori PellThe lock will release. They use our public gauge.

Evin SerThen they think we have room for their water.

Jori sat on the curb with the medicine case between his knees. The child in wet socks took the rat box, held it against his coat and looked at the sealed notices under Jori's arm.

ChildMy mother said the stairs were safe because your paper said so. Was that your number?

Jori looked from the child to the emptied doorway. Nobody answered for him.

Jori PellYes. The number was mine. I answered the insurers and left you to answer the river.

Illuminara sat beside him in the rain. She did not offer absolution or ask for the confession again.

Jori PellI thought I was buying them time.

IlluminaraWhose time was it?

Across the street, Lera's volunteers chalked SAFE on an emptied doorway. Jori watched until the last resident came out.

Jori PellTheirs. I spent it without telling them. I will declare what I did after they have standing and before I resign. Show me where I sign after they do.

05 · The Order of Truth

Olven Hall, public counter · 18:52

The counter had become a river of paper. Signatures came by bicycle, boat, school van and wet hand. Lera's committee checked each against the household register. Nobody accepted a mark made by a landlord for a tenant. The baker's refusal stayed in the margin.

At 19:06 they crossed three-fifths. At 19:12 Evin filed the crack map and certified that one public work caused the common danger. At 19:14 Sol Tann appeared on the screen and read the packet number back to the hall.

Lera NwanThe claimant stands first. Jori declares. The raw book travels inside his declaration. You resign only after the receipt holds.

Jori stood at the public microphone with the real gauge book under his hand. He looked toward Lera. She did not nod.

Jori PellI altered Olven's river record for twenty-two months. I did it to prevent cancellation in the Low Quarter. That motive does not belong in the measurement and it does not excuse the danger. The figures I published were false. The book beside me is true.

The hall clock clicked once. On Sol Tann's screen, eleven minutes appeared in red.

Lera transmitted the resident committee charter. Evin transmitted the crack map. Jori scanned the raw book page by page while two residents watched his hands. At eight minutes, one page jammed. He reached for it; Lera reached first, flattened the fold, and passed it back without looking at his face.

At ten minutes and thirty-eight seconds, Sol Tann's screen went white.

Sol TannDeclaration and raw evidence received. Common-cause claim vested. Household cancellation is stayed for seventy-two hours. The consortium reserves fraud, recovery and coverage arguments. It cannot act household by household during the stay.

Jori waited until the white screen held, then returned to the microphone.

Jori PellI resign as mayor effective now and submit to the court's custody after the evacuation.

The room did not cheer. A stay was not a home, and a claim was not a wall.

Lera NwanNow tell us what we lose if we open the spill channel.

Evin unrolled the freight-yard plan. The only path that could take pressure off the levee ran through the old dye market and the municipal arcade where Lera's mother kept her receipts.

Evin SerSeventy-two buildings flood to the lintels. Without it, four thousand do, and the current reaches the hospital road.

Jori stared at the market he had tried to keep insurable.

Jori PellThe arcade is city property. Open it.

Lera NwanNot your decision anymore.

Lera put the seventy-two threatened addresses on the public screen. A roll call reached every occupied home and shop through the hall, the shelter radios and the bus stewards; four addresses were municipal stores or empty buildings.

ShopkeeperMy family lives above number sixteen. Yes to the channel. Record the flat and the loss.

ShopkeeperNo. My bakery is not a vote you may borrow because the arithmetic is large.

Lera's mother pressed both palms to the counter where she had sorted forty-three years of receipts.

Lera's motherOpen through my shop. The hospital road weighs more than my paper. Do not call that consent for anybody else.

Fifty occupants or owners said yes and eighteen said no. Lera made the terms plain before the committee voted.

Lera NwanThe roll call is a hearing, not a ballot. The committee decides, and the committee carries the blame.

The committee voted under the emergency charter: nine for the channel, two against, one abstention. Lera recorded every address, every committee name and every objection before she folded the plan and handed it to Evin.

The committee put the channel damage first in the common-cause claim, with the eighteen dissenting addresses listed before the fifty yes-voters. Evin certified the cut as a public emergency work. Sol Tann's reservation left liability contested, not erased.

06 · The Spill Channel

Old dye market and freight yard · 22:31

The last bus left the Low Quarter at 22:18. At 22:31 the upstream lock released, and the river rose against a city that had finally begun using the same numbers as the water.

Evin's crews cut the freight-yard paving along the buried drainage line. Illuminara lifted the three stone lintels they could not move before midnight and set them where Evin marked. When the first cut slumped, she held its banks apart while workers drove braces. She did not choose the route or change the vote. Her strength kept the chosen route open long enough to become real.

Jori worked the sandbag line in a borrowed coat. Some residents would not take a bag from him. He placed those bags where Lera pointed and did not ask them to reconsider.

At 23:47 the levee seam opened. The sound was less like an explosion than a long garment tearing in another room. Water crossed the dye market, struck the new cut and turned through the freight yard. Windows burst along the arcade. Forty-three years of Lera's mother's receipts floated against the ceiling of her shop.

Lera made a sound Illuminara would remember longer than the roar.

IlluminaraI can bring the cabinet out.

Lera NwanNot while the crew is in the cut.

Illuminara stayed with the braces. The cabinet disappeared under brown water.

At 00:26 the river stood forty centimetres below the hospital road. The channel carried the rest into the fallow basin north of the tracks. Behind them, seventy-two buildings filled. Beyond them, the school ridge shone with fourteen thousand shelter lights.

Evin touched the listening rod. Its trembling had slowed.

Evin SerThe wall is gone. The quarter is not.

Jori looked toward the flooded arcade.

Jori PellI saved it from the insurers until I gave it to the river.

Lera NwanYou do not get the last sentence tonight.

He lowered his head. Illuminara expected an answer. None was needed.

07 · The True Line

Low Quarter school ridge · 05:40

At dawn, every person on the evacuation register had been accounted for. Twelve injuries had gone to the field clinic; none was life-threatening. The hospital road remained open. The claim stay held. The first line of its damage annex named the seventy-two channel losses, with the eighteen dissenting addresses first. Sol Tann had sent three auditors and a note stating that the consortium would contest liability, not erase the claim.

Court officers took Jori's seal and asked him to remain on the ridge until the bridge reopened. He had resigned. Trial, restitution and the question of prison would come later, where his motive could be understood without becoming a pardon.

Lera's mother sat under a blanket with no receipts. She accepted tea from Illuminara and refused it from Jori. Both choices were honoured.

The resident committee set the two gauge books on an evidence cloth beside Jori. It had voted to require his sworn account of every warning and insurer call for the liability claim, from court custody and without any advisory power.

Lera NwanWhen the bridge opens, the court takes you. Before then, list every warning and every call. We need your truth for the claim. We do not need your office.

Jori PellYou will have it.

Children from the shelter had found blue paint. Under Evin's direction they marked the true crest of the river on the school wall. It was higher than the false gauge had ever admitted. Beside it they painted the time the spill channel opened and the number of people who had crossed the ridge gate.

Jori watched from three steps away.

Jori PellWill you put my name on it?

Lera NwanOn the falsified years and on the confession. Not on the evacuation. That belongs to the quarter.

Jori PellAll right.

Lera gave him a broom and pointed to the mud coming through the school doors.

Lera NwanYou may help without being thanked. The court can collect you when the bridge opens.

He took the broom.

Illuminara stood beneath the true blue line. On the evidence cloth, the baker's address and Lera's mother's shop sat beneath the same stay number, their opposite answers still recorded. Jori's broom left a clean strip through the mud and stopped three steps from her blanket. Illuminara felt the hard mercy of that distance.

A girl added one final mark at the bottom of the wall: the overturned wooden dog from the chalk map, upright again. Illuminara laughed softly when she saw it. The girl handed her the brush, and together they painted four wet paws pointing toward the ridge.

What the critic still says doesn't work

A mayor's hidden gauge, a refused signature and one eleven-minute window: Illuminara carries and holds while the quarter decides, and fourteen thousand people are counted at dawn.

Tonight's shape

Seven scenes across eighteen hours: a false gauge and a real crack, a confession in the rain, an insurer's automatic clause spoken aloud, one refused signature, medicine carried from a flooding basement, a pooled claim filed before the raw gauges go public, a spill channel cut through the mayor's own market, and a dawn in which safety, loss and responsibility all remain visible.

01 · Suhi vodomer

Olvenski rečni zid, hišica z vodomerom · 11:42

Vodomer je kazal, da je reka štiri metre pod vrhom. Zunaj okna je rjava voda oblizovala sedmo prečko lestve, katere sedma prečka je bila tri metre pod vrhom. Dež je neskladje sploščil ob steklo.

Illuminara je položila dva prsta na zid. Nasip ji je odgovoril skozi kamen, glino in železne vezi, zabite vanj pred stoletjem. Šiv pod staro tržnico barvil se je odprl vzdolž enainštiridesetih metrov. Voda se je že vpisovala v razpoko.

Župan Jori Pell je stal ob medeninastem zapisovalniku z napačno zapetim plaščem. Imel je sedeminštirideset let, široka ramena in izvolila ga je četrt, katere strehe so se razprostirale pod njima. Opazoval jo je, kako bere zid, in se ni pretvarjal, da je presenečen.

IlluminaraKdaj ste znižali vodomer?

Jori PellPrvič pred dvaindvajsetimi meseci. Za dvanajst centimetrov. Nato so zavarovalnice poslale drugo opozorilo, zato sem spremenil pretvorbeno tabelo. Reka je rasla. Številka ni.

IlluminaraKoliko časa, preden se šiv odpre?

Jori PellVi mi povejte.

IlluminaraOb sedanjem naraščanju malo po polnoči. Prej, če je lesena podpora pod tržnico strohnela.

Enkrat je prikimal, kakor da je potrdila sejo sveta.

Jori PellSpodaj je štirinajst tisoč ljudi. Če surova serija doseže zavarovalnice, preden jih premaknemo, se pregled gospodinjstev začne takoj. Jutri opoldne odpokličejo hipoteke. Najemodajalci zaklenejo vrata, še preden se blato posuši.

IlluminaraIn če jih ne doseže?

Jori PellPotem jih doseže voda.

Odprl je predal in vzel dve knjigi. V eni so bile javne številke, zapisane s skrbno uradniško roko. V drugi je bila reka takšna, kakršna je bila: prečrtani zapisi, dežni madeži in dvaindvajset mesecev resnice.

Jori PellDo jutra iz mene naredite poštenega človeka, do poldneva pa iz njih brezdomce. Potem ostanite zaradi ključavničarjev in bank ter vseh, ki pravijo, da četrt nikoli ne bi smela stati tukaj.

Zid se je stresel. Skodelica se je premaknila tri milimetre po polici zapisovalnika in obstala.

Illuminara mu ni rekla, da strah ne opravičuje laži. Že je vedel, kako zveni sodba. Zastavila je vprašanje, ki je spremenilo kraj, kjer se je morala začeti naslednja ura.

IlluminaraKdo lahko prebivalce poveže v enega vlagatelja zahtevka, preden razkrijete resnico?

Jori je skozi dež pogledal navzdol proti skupnostni dvorani.

Jori PellLera Nwan. Sovražila bo vprašanje. Morda bo vseeno odgovorila.

02 · En vlagatelj

Skupnostna dvorana Spodnje četrti · 12:35

Lera Nwan je dvorano spremenila v evakuacijsko središče, še preden je prišel župan. Rdeča kreda je po tleh označevala ulice. Prebivalci so po njih premikali lesene žetone za avtobuse, kisikove jeklenke, invalidske vozičke in živali. Nihče ni čakal na dovoljenje, da bi bil koristen.

Lera je poslušala Jorijevo pripoved, ne da bi trenila. Ko je prišel do spremenjene pretvorbene tabele, mu je iz roke vzela javni list vodomera, ga enkrat pretrgala in obe polovici položila na mizo, namesto da bi ju vrgla vanj.

Lera NwanMoja mati je podaljšala najem trgovine, ker ste nam pokazali ta list.

Jori PellVem.

Lera NwanNe. Vi poznate trgovino. Jaz poznam sobo, kjer hrani vsak račun iz triinštiridesetih let, ker misli, da lahko papir prisili kraj, da ostane. Ne recite, da veste.

Jori je zaprl usta.

Illuminara je v štirih stavkih razložila šiv, naraščanje vode in verjetno polnočno porušitev. Nato je položila obe knjigi vodomera drugo ob drugo. Ni se ju več dotaknila.

IlluminaraOlvenska listina gospodinjstvom, ki jih ogroža eno javno delo, dovoljuje vložitev skupnega zahtevka. Če njihova pravica nastane pred razkritjem, nadzorovani sklad začasno ustavi odpovedi posameznih gospodinjstev, medtem ko se obravnava goljufija.

Lera NwanKoliko podpisov?

Jori PellTri petine zavarovanih domov. Klavzula je bila napisana za potrese.

Lera NwanIn kdo imenuje odbor vlagatelja?

IlluminaraPrebivalci. Ne župan. Ne jaz.

Lera je šest rdečih žetonov premaknila stran od rečne ceste na šolski greben.

Lera NwanPotem prvo vprašanje ni, ali mu odpustimo. Vprašanje je, ali lahko premaknemo štirinajst tisoč ljudi in zberemo tisoč osemsto podpisov, preden se zid odpre.

Jori PellZavarovalnice bodo trdile, da je zahtevek zastrupljen z mojo goljufijo.

Lera NwanNaj to povedo vsem nam hkrati.

Iz tal je zadonel globok udarec. Na zemljevidu s kredo se je prevrnil lesen pes.

Lera ga je postavila pokonci in proti prostoru potisnila prazen obrazec za odbor. Roke so segle ponj. Ne vse.

Starejši pek je vrnil svoj žeton v žep. Evakuiral se bo, je rekel, vendar se ne bo pridružil zahtevku, zaradi katerega bi moral deliti prihodnost s človekom, ki mu je lagal. Lera je ob njegov naslov zapisala zavrnitev. Če bo sklad uspel, ga bo moral zaščititi, ne da bi se pretvarjal, da je soglašal.

Illuminara je začutila nekaj podobnega olajšanju, ostrega in nežnega: četrt je bila že bolj poštena od svojega vodomera.

03 · Enajst minut

Skupnostna dvorana, arhivska soba · 14:10

Sol Tann, dežurni uradnik Konzorcija rečnih vzajemnih zavarovalnic, se je pojavil na stenskem zaslonu v sivi sobi tri mesta stran. Lera je vztrajala, da je klic javen. Dvesto prebivalcev je napolnilo dvorano; stotine drugih so poslušale iz vrat in po radiih, medtem ko so pakirale.

Sol TannČe župan potrdi namerno bistveno ponarejanje, sistem za odpovedi pregleda vsako polico, povezano z vodomerom. Po lastni presoji ga ne morem ustaviti.

Lera NwanAli ga lahko skupni zahtevek zaradi enega vzroka ustavi po zakonu?

Sol TannČe je prej veljavno vložen. Potrebuje tri petine, imenovani odbor prebivalcev in dokaz, da je vzrok eno javno delo. Če so v zahtevku lažni podatki, ga izpodbijamo.

IlluminaraZahtevek bo uporabil surovo knjigo vodomera, zemljevid razpoke in sedanje meritve inženirja. Ničesar, izpeljanega iz javne serije.

Sol TannTo so čisti dokazi. Ne smem vam povedati, kako vložiti zahtevek.

IlluminaraPotem odgovorite samo na to: če prebivalci najprej oblikujejo vlagatelja, župan Pell poda izjavo kot drugi, surova knjiga pa potuje znotraj te izjave, ali skupna vložitev prevlada nad pregledom gospodinjstev?

Sol TannDa, če vse prispe v enajstminutnem oknu po izjavi. Dlje od tega vam ne bom svetoval, konzorcij pa bo izpodbijal obseg zadržanja.

Šumenje je prešlo dvorano kakor veter skozi trstje.

Lera je dvignila prvo stran. Prispelo je sedemsto dvanajst podpisov. Potrebovali so jih še dvainpolkrat toliko.

Jori PellNocoj ne bom priznal.

Lera se je počasi obrnila k njemu.

Jori PellNe, dokler niso avtobusi polni in zahtevek pripravljen. Če spodleti ena stvar, ne bom roka, ki bo povzročila obe nesreči.

Lera NwanTa roka ste bili pred dvaindvajsetimi meseci.

Trznil je, vendar odgovora ni spremenil.

Illuminara bi ga lahko prepričevala z logiko. Namesto tega je pogledala zemljevid. Moder žeton je označeval Vesper House, šest nadstropij, brez dvigala, ulico najbliže tržnici barvil.

IlluminaraPojdite z menoj.

Jori PellKam?

IlluminaraNositi tisto, kar je vaša številka pustila spodaj.

Pri vratih je čakala, dokler se ni odločil, da ji bo sledil.

04 · Kar je številka pustila

Vesper House, Spodnja četrt · 15:05
