01 · Item Zero

Harrow Gorge Hearing Hall · Day One, 09:10

The chair at the foot of the table was empty on purpose. Ash frame, rush seat, no nameplate. Adaeze had it carried in before the doors opened, and the clerk had entered it in the record as Item Zero.

Seven villages had stood below the old dam until the evacuation order eleven years ago. The order had also dissolved them. Their land was struck from the register, their councils with it, and under Rule Four only communities that occupied mapped ground could vote on the dam that would now be built over their graves.

Halvard RoosThe left abutment gallery is seeping at two point two litres a second. My trigger is six. I will not tell this room the old dam is safe, and I will not tell it the new one can wait for a better hearing.

Bukola FerreiraSix hundred thousand people drink from this river and the rains come in nineteen days. Every hour spent on ghosts is an hour taken from them.

Adaeze did not look up from her papers. She had chaired basin hearings for twenty years, and she had learned that the quickest way to lose a room was to answer its first insult.

AdaezeThe ghosts have names, Councillor. The clerk will read them at the end of every session until the rule changes. Witness Four, you may begin.

Illuminara had sat at the side table for an hour and no one had addressed her as anything else. That was the arrangement. Her mother had made it in the car, in four sentences, and had not asked whether it suited her.

IlluminaraThere are two workable routes for the spillway outfall. The right-bank route is straighter and cheaper, and it carries the outfall and the final spoil dump across the Pell terraces. The left-bank route takes three hundred and ten hectares of irrigated farmland in the Weir Valley and costs nine percent more. Both are safe. Neither is harmless.

Halvard RoosThe contractor says the route must be fixed today. Haul road, spoil and cofferdam are one chain.

IlluminaraThat is what the schedule says. I have not yet checked whether it is what the rock says.

Beside the long table the empty chair held the window light. Nobody sat in it. Nobody looked at it either, which was how she knew they had all been counting it.

02 · Rule Four

Chair's office, Harrow Gorge Hearing Hall · Day One, 12:40

Adaeze shut the door and took off her glasses, the only sign she gave of how the morning had gone.

IlluminaraI have read all four thousand petitions, from three countries. I can model what each village would choose within a few percent and lodge it as a sealed finding. Then the chair is not empty.

AdaezeSay the second part again, slowly.

IlluminaraI would not vote for them. I would tell the hearing what they would most probably say.

AdaezeNara.

She said it as she had said it when her daughter was twenty-four and new to the courts: not a rebuke, a hand laid flat on a table.

AdaezeYou taught me the first rule of your old trade. The interpreter carries what the speaker says. She does not improve it, and she does not supply it when the speaker is absent.

IlluminaraThey are not absent by choice.

AdaezeThat is why it is not yours to supply. You would be the third body to decide for them, and kind about it this time. I have buried that kind of kindness.

She is right. The model would be accurate, and it would still be a theft.

IlluminaraThen what do you want from me?

AdaezeThe chair holds a veto and no vote. It can stop what cannot be undone and it cannot start anything. It stays empty until the burial societies choose witnesses to sit in it, and they will need twelve days, because they live in three countries and trust nobody. The room will ask me what those twelve days cost. I need you to tell me. Not what the villages want. Which decisions on that dam are still open if we wait, and which close for ever.

IlluminaraThat is an engineering question.

AdaezeIt is the only kind I can take to Councillor Ferreira.

She had walked into the building to offer her mother an answer. She walked out holding a question, and it was the better of the two.

03 · The Chain

Contractor's site office, Harrow Gorge · Day One, 23:50

Ruta Kasprzak's schedule ran to four hundred and twelve activities; Illuminara read them by ten. She had been asking which tasks were urgent. Adaeze had asked which could be undone.

She tagged each line with three questions. Does this move rock? Bury ground? Build something that must stand on the final choice? Thirty-one activities survived the third question. Nine touched either route. The rest formed one chain because it was cheapest to haul.

Ruta KasprzakLine one hundred and eighty-four. Batching-plant pad. You put it in the middle column, reversible at a price.

IlluminaraThe slab can be broken out.

Ruta KasprzakThe forty anchor piles cross the right-bank haul alignment. Break the slab and the piles remain under whichever road loses. Pull them and I lose two days and nine hundred thousand.

Illuminara moved the row into the third column. The nine became ten.

IlluminaraThen no piles until the route is chosen. Put the mobile plant on ballast. Add two days to concrete production and the nine hundred thousand to the hearing ledger.

Ruta KasprzakYou concede quickly for someone who read all four hundred and twelve.

IlluminaraThe ledger is useful only if the person who finds me wrong can change it.

Ruta tapped the column heading with one blunt fingernail. Illuminara added a blank beside every classification: challenger, evidence, time filed. No one had asked her for that column.

Ruta KasprzakYou are still asking me to double-handle the spoil.

IlluminaraThe first two hundred and sixty-two thousand cubic metres must come out before the rains whichever route wins. The cofferdam, diversion portal and abutment bolting touch neither the terraces nor the farmland. Only the final dump and the haul road across the terraces do. Send the rock to the old quarry bench instead, and nothing is buried that cannot be dug up again.

Ruta KasprzakThe bench holds three hundred thousand on the March survey. We've been blasting beside it since.

IlluminaraThen we resurvey at dawn.

Halvard Roos came in at midnight with a flask and no intention of agreeing.

Halvard RoosYou want a delay in my safety case for a legal fiction.

IlluminaraI want a cost in it, not a delay. The temporary haul along the quarry track is slower: nine days of overtime, one point nine million, eleven days of the contractor's float. I have not found a cheaper way, and I am not claiming it is free.

Halvard RoosAnd if the rains come early? I have a gallery and a trigger. You have a spreadsheet.

IlluminaraIf the gallery crosses your trigger I can carry the shear on the left abutment for about thirty hours. Not twelve days. Thirty hours, and then I fail. The drawdown must do the rest: three point one metres starting tomorrow, which means the city's water is rationed from tomorrow.

Halvard RoosThen you are not in my safety case.

IlluminaraI did not ask to be. Log it separately.

At dawn the resurvey came back at two hundred and eighty-nine thousand cubic metres, leaving twenty-seven thousand above the excavation target, three thousand short of Halvard's minimum reserve. Ruta leased a rail-yard stockpile for the final three thousand at a further hundred and eighty thousand. Halvard read both numbers twice and wrote them into the log in his own hand.

04 · Three Countries

Harrow Gorge Hearing Hall, three video links · Day Two, 10:00

The links came up: a church hall in Corvane, a school gym in Tessaly, a shipping office in Lisk. For eleven years their burial societies had kept the seven villages' registers and sent money home for funerals when there was no home to return to.

Adaeze read the motion: an empty chair, a veto over irreversible work, no affirmative vote, a twelve-day interval and Illuminara's line-by-line ledger.

Bukola FerreiraThis hands strangers a veto over my constituents' water. Whose water do these witnesses drink?

AdaezeNot yours. The veto touches only the third column. Rationing, the cofferdam and the foundation proceed.

Mirela Haddad, from CorvaneAnd who owns the third column? Witness Four? Your safety authority?

Illuminara turned the ledger so all three cameras could see the blank beside every row.

IlluminaraThe safety authority classifies first. The chair's engineer may contest a row within twenty-four hours. The more irreversible reading governs until a reasoned ruling. Ruta found the row that proved the challenge belongs.

Mirela Haddad, from CorvaneThen our engineer, paid by the hearing, reads your numbers and surveys the bench.

Halvard RoosIf the gallery reaches its trigger while a row is disputed, protective work cannot wait.

IlluminaraOnly the work necessary to take the reading below the trigger, logged by the hour and reviewed the next day. The exception cannot choose a route.

Savitri Moyo, from the Weir ValleySame terms for us. Nobody here accepts the left bank for the valley. We meet on Day Eleven; record the dissent.

Bukola FerreiraReturn the rule's operation to public hearing after one year. I can defend bounded delay, not invent a valley choice.

AdaezePut both conditions in the motion.

Mirela Haddad, from CorvaneWitness Four. Which route buries the Pell terraces?

IlluminaraThe right-bank route covers the lower three Pell terraces. I would choose the left for that reason, but it takes this hearing's farmland. I do not weigh those losses.

Bukola FerreiraMy vote is yes, with the one-year review. The valley's vote remains its own.

The tally closed six to three. Rule Four took effect at noon. Adaeze, who did not vote, put her authorship and any blame into the record.

05 · Thirty Hours

Left abutment gallery, old Harrow Gorge dam · Day Eleven, 02:40

The storm came nine days early: a hundred and forty millimetres in nine hours. The drawdown had bought three metres of freeboard and cost the city eleven days of rationing, but the sudden rise drove reservoir pressure through the known joint in the left abutment faster than the drains could clear it. Of the two hundred and sixty-two thousand cubic metres due out before the expected rains, two hundred and forty-seven thousand lay on the quarry bench. The remaining fifteen thousand was still rock in the river-side cut; the storm had arrived before its excavation shift.

At 02:40 the gallery drain read six point one litres a second.

Halvard Roos, over the radioIt's crossed. Illuminara. Now.

She went in as far as the third joint. The concrete sweated cold. She laid both palms on the wall, found the plane along which the abutment wanted to slide, and began to carry what the rock had stopped carrying. It was not like lifting. It was like standing in a river that wished her to step aside.

She had told him thirty hours. She did not look at the clock.

Halvard Roos, over the radioFive point four. Falling. Keep going.

Not for ever. Only until the drawdown is the stronger thing.

At hour nineteen the reservoir inflow passed its crest. With the rain already gone and the reservoir three metres lower than it would otherwise have been, the drains took the load back. The reading fell to two point nine and held there. Halvard came down the gallery himself, in a rain cape with the flask in his pocket, and stood beside her until she took her hands from the wall.

Halvard RoosI can take it from here.

IlluminaraWrite it down.

Halvard RoosI already have. Nineteen hours, in a separate column. You are still not in my safety case.

She sat on the gallery floor. She could not feel her hands, and would not for six hours. Halvard unscrewed the flask and held it out, and she took it with both wrists.

06 · The Seat

Harrow Gorge Hearing Hall · Day Twelve, 10:00

The witnesses had travelled through the night from three countries. They took the chair in turn. First, an old man lowered himself with one hand on the rush seat as if it might not hold.

Their engineers had read the bench survey, challenged two ledger rows and accepted the written rulings.

Tomasz BrandlPell vetoes the right-bank route. We will not be under the dump. I carry Pell's register, not Quill's; the next witness carries theirs. We do not choose the left bank. That is the valley's land and choice.

Savitri MoyoAt four this morning, thirty-eight of fifty-one landholding households chose the left bank with full-value purchase and the levy. Nine refused. Four abstained. I voted no. I carry the result because it is ours, not because it is mine.

Bukola FerreiraMy city paid eleven rationing days. I put the levy on today's council docket. I do not cast the valley's vote.

AdaezeThen the record will show two decisions made by the people they bind, and none by anyone else.

Rule Four had stood amended since Day Two; today was its first use. Two disputed rows were settled before the storm, and Halvard's emergency log chose no route. By noon the cofferdam stood and excavation was within a day of schedule.

The Valley Courier called Adaeze a sentimentalist. The newsletter of the Lisk camp called her a bureaucrat. She read both and had them filed.

Illuminara waited at the door, her hands wrapped in gauze because Halvard had insisted.

AdaezeNara. You are late.

IlluminaraI was holding up your dam.

AdaezeI know. I read the log. And the fourth column.

Her mother crossed the hall, took the gauzed hands in both of hers, and looked at them for a moment without saying whether she thought the cost had been fair. Then she let go and went to find the carpenter. The chair, she had decided, would be fixed to the floor. The ledger would remain loose enough to turn.

What the critic still says doesn't work

A dam that cannot wait and a village that cannot vote are reconciled by a ledger of what can still be undone, a challenge column nobody asked for, and nineteen hours of one woman's hands on a wall.

Tonight's shape

Six scenes across twelve days: a hearing with an empty chair, a refusal between mother and daughter, a night spent breaking one schedule into three columns, a vote taken across three countries, an early storm in a dam gallery, and a seat that is no longer empty.

01 · Postavka nič

Dvorana za zaslišanja Harrow Gorge · prvi dan, 09:10

Stol ob vznožju mize je bil prazen namenoma. Ogrodje iz jesena, sedež iz ločja, brez tablice z imenom. Adaeze ga je dala prinesti, preden so odprli vrata, zapisnikar pa ga je v zapisnik vnesel kot postavko nič.

Sedem vasi je stalo pod starim jezom do ukaza za evakuacijo pred enajstimi leti. Z ukazom so jih tudi razpustili. Njihovo zemljo so izbrisali iz registra, z njo pa njihove svete, in po Pravilu štiri so o jezu, ki naj bi ga zdaj zgradili čez njihove grobove, lahko glasovale samo skupnosti, ki so živele na kartiranem ozemlju.

Halvard RoosGalerija levega opornika pušča dva cela dve litra na sekundo. Moj prag je šest. Tej dvorani ne bom rekel, da je stari jez varen, in ne bom ji rekel, da novi lahko počaka na boljše zaslišanje.

Bukola FerreiraŠeststo tisoč ljudi pije iz te reke in deževje pride čez devetnajst dni. Vsaka ura, porabljena za duhove, je ura, odvzeta njim.

Adaeze ni dvignila pogleda s papirjev. Zaslišanja v porečju je vodila dvajset let in se naučila, da prostor najhitreje izgubiš, če odgovoriš na njegovo prvo žalitev.

AdaezeDuhovi imajo imena, svetnica. Zapisnikar jih bo prebral na koncu vsake seje, dokler se pravilo ne spremeni. Priča štiri, lahko začnete.

Illuminara je eno uro sedela pri stranski mizi in nihče je ni nagovoril drugače. Takšen je bil dogovor. Njena mati ga je sklenila v avtomobilu, v štirih stavkih, in je ni vprašala, ali ji ustreza.

IlluminaraZa iztok prelivnega kanala sta izvedljivi dve trasi. Trasa po desnem bregu je ravnejša in cenejša, iztok in končno odlagališče izkopa pa vodi čez Pellove terase. Trasa po levem bregu vzame tristo deset hektarjev namakanih polj v Weirski dolini in stane devet odstotkov več. Obe sta varni. Nobena ni neškodljiva.

Halvard RoosIzvajalec pravi, da je treba traso določiti danes. Transportna cesta, izkop in zagatni jez so ena veriga.

IlluminaraTako pravi urnik. Nisem še preverila, ali tako pravi tudi skala.

Ob dolgi mizi je prazen stol držal okensko svetlobo. Nihče ni sedel na njem. Nihče ga tudi ni pogledal, in prav zato je vedela, da ga vsi štejejo.

02 · Pravilo štiri

Pisarna predsedujoče, dvorana za zaslišanja Harrow Gorge · prvi dan, 12:40

Adaeze je zaprla vrata in snela očala, edino znamenje, ki ga je pokazala o tem, kako je minilo dopoldne.

IlluminaraPrebrala sem vse štiri tisoč peticij iz treh držav. Z nekajodstotno natančnostjo lahko modeliram, kaj bi izbrala vsaka vas, in to vložim kot zapečateno ugotovitev. Potem stol ni prazen.

AdaezeŠe enkrat povej drugi del, počasi.

IlluminaraNe bi glasovala namesto njih. Zaslišanju bi povedala, kaj bi najverjetneje rekli.

AdaezeNara.

Izgovorila je kakor takrat, ko je bila njena hči stara štiriindvajset let in nova na sodiščih: ne kot grajo, temveč kot dlan, položeno plosko na mizo.

AdaezeNaučila si me prvega pravila svoje nekdanje obrti. Tolmačka prenese, kar govorec pove. Tega ne izboljšuje in ne dopolnjuje, ko govorca ni.

IlluminaraNiso odsotni po lastni izbiri.

AdaezePrav zato jih ne smeš nadomestiti. Bila bi tretje telo, ki bi odločilo namesto njih, tokrat prijazno. Takšno prijaznost sem že pokopala.

Prav ima. Model bi bil natančen, pa bi bil še vedno kraja.

IlluminaraKaj potem hočeš od mene?

AdaezeStol ima veto in nima glasu. Ustavi lahko tisto, česar ni mogoče razveljaviti, ne more pa ničesar začeti. Prazen ostane, dokler pogrebna društva ne izberejo prič, ki bodo sedele na njem, za to pa bodo potrebovala dvanajst dni, ker živijo v treh državah in ne zaupajo nikomur. Dvorana me bo vprašala, koliko stane teh dvanajst dni. To mi moraš povedati. Ne, kaj hočejo vasi. Katere odločitve o jezu ostanejo odprte, če počakamo, in katere se za vedno zaprejo.

IlluminaraTo je inženirsko vprašanje.

AdaezeEdino takšno lahko odnesem svetnici Ferreira.

V stavbo je vstopila, da bi materi ponudila odgovor. Odšla je z vprašanjem in to je bilo boljše od obeh.

03 · Veriga

Pisarna izvajalca, gradbišče Harrow Gorge · prvi dan, 23:50

Urnik Rute Kasprzak je obsegal štiristo dvanajst dejavnosti; Illuminara jih je do desete ure prebrala vse. Spraševala je, katere naloge so nujne. Adaeze je vprašala, katere je mogoče razveljaviti.

Vsako vrstico je označila s tremi vprašanji. Ali premakne skalo? Pokoplje zemljo? Zgradi nekaj, kar mora stati na končni izbiri? Tretje vprašanje je prestalo enaintrideset dejavnosti. Devet jih je zadevalo eno ali drugo traso. Preostale so tvorile eno verigo, ker je bilo tako najceneje odvažati.

Ruta KasprzakVrstica sto štiriinosemdeset. Ploščad betonarne. Dali ste jo v srednji stolpec, razveljavljivo za določeno ceno.

IlluminaraPloščo je mogoče razbiti.

Ruta KasprzakŠtirideset sidrnih pilotov seka traso transportne ceste po desnem bregu. Razbijte ploščo, pa piloti ostanejo pod cesto tiste trase, ki izgubi. Če jih izvlečem, izgubim dva dni in devetsto tisoč.

Illuminara je vrstico premaknila v tretji stolpec. Devet jih je postalo deset.

IlluminaraPotem brez pilotov, dokler trasa ni izbrana. Mobilno betonarno postavite na gramozno blazino. Proizvodnji betona dodajte dva dni, devetsto tisoč pa v knjigo stroškov zaslišanja.

Ruta KasprzakHitro popustite za nekoga, ki je prebral vseh štiristo dvanajst.

IlluminaraKnjiga je uporabna le, če jo lahko spremeni človek, ki dokaže, da se motim.

Ruta je s topim nohtom potrkala po naslovu stolpca. Illuminara je ob vsako razvrstitev dodala prazno polje: izpodbijalec, dokaz, čas vložitve. Tega stolpca ni zahteval nihče.

Ruta KasprzakŠe vedno hočete, da izkop pretovarjam dvakrat.

IlluminaraPrvih dvesto dvainšestdeset tisoč kubičnih metrov je treba izkopati pred deževjem ne glede na zmagovalno traso. Zagatni jez, preusmeritveni predor in sidranje opornika ne zadevajo ne teras ne polj. Samo končno odlagališče in transportna cesta čez terase. Skalo namesto tega odpeljite na polico starega kamnoloma in pokopano ne bo nič, česar se ne bi dalo znova izkopati.

Ruta KasprzakPo marčevski meritvi polica sprejme tristo tisoč. Od takrat razstreljujemo ob njej.

IlluminaraPotem jo ob zori znova izmerimo.

Halvard Roos je opolnoči vstopil s termovko in brez vsakršnega namena, da bi se strinjal.

Halvard RoosZaradi pravne fikcije hočete zamudo v mojem varnostnem primeru.

IlluminaraV njem hočem strošek, ne zamude. Začasni prevoz po kamnolomski poti je počasnejši: devet dni nadur, milijon devetsto tisoč, enajst dni izvajalčeve rezerve. Cenejše poti nisem našla in ne trdim, da je brezplačna.

Halvard RoosKaj pa, če deževje pride zgodaj? Jaz imam galerijo in prag. Vi imate preglednico.

IlluminaraČe galerija preseže vaš prag, lahko približno trideset ur nosim strižne sile levega opornika. Ne dvanajst dni. Trideset ur, potem odpovem. Preostalo mora opraviti znižanje gladine: od jutri tri cele ena metra, kar pomeni, da bo mesto od jutri racioniralo vodo.

Halvard RoosPotem vas ni v mojem varnostnem primeru.

IlluminaraNisem prosila, da bi bila. Zapišite ločeno.

Ob zori je ponovna meritev pokazala dvesto devetinosemdeset tisoč kubičnih metrov, kar je pomenilo sedemindvajset tisoč nad ciljem izkopa, tri tisoč manj od Halvardove najmanjše rezerve. Ruta je za zadnje tri tisoč najela železniško skladišče za dodatnih sto osemdeset tisoč. Halvard je obe številki prebral dvakrat in ju z lastno roko vpisal v dnevnik.

04 · Tri države

Dvorana za zaslišanja Harrow Gorge, tri videopovezave · drugi dan, 10:00

Povezave so zaživele: cerkvena dvorana v Corvanu, šolska telovadnica v Tessalyju, ladijska pisarna v Lisku. Enajst let so njihova pogrebna društva hranila registre sedmih vasi in pošiljala denar domov za pogrebe, kadar doma, kamor bi se lahko vrnili, ni bilo več.

Adaeze je prebrala predlog: prazen stol, veto nad nepovratnim delom, brez pritrdilnega glasu, dvanajstdnevni rok in Illuminarina knjiga po posameznih vrsticah.

Bukola FerreiraS tem tujcem dajemo veto nad vodo mojih volivcev. Čigavo vodo pijejo te priče?

AdaezeNe vaše. Veto se nanaša samo na tretji stolpec. Racioniranje, zagatni jez in temeljenje se nadaljujejo.

Mirela Haddad iz CorvanaKdo pa ima v lasti tretji stolpec? Priča štiri? Vaš varnostni organ?

Illuminara je obrnila knjigo, da so vse tri kamere videle prazno polje ob vsaki vrstici.

IlluminaraVarnostni organ razvrsti prvi. Inženir predsedujoče lahko vrstico izpodbija v štiriindvajsetih urah. Dokler ni obrazložene odločitve, velja bolj nepovratna razlaga. Ruta je našla vrstico, ki je dokazala, da izpodbijanje sodi sem.

Mirela Haddad iz CorvanaPotem naš inženir, ki ga plača zaslišanje, prebere vaše številke in izmeri polico.

Halvard RoosČe galerija doseže prag, medtem ko je vrstica sporna, zaščitno delo ne more čakati.

IlluminaraSamo delo, nujno za znižanje meritve pod prag, zapisano po urah in pregledano naslednji dan. Izjema ne sme izbrati trase.

Savitri Moyo iz Weirske dolineEnaki pogoji za nas. Tukaj nihče ne sprejema levega brega v imenu doline. Sestanemo se enajsti dan; zapišite nestrinjanje.

Bukola FerreiraPo enem letu vrnite delovanje pravila na javno zaslišanje. Zagovarjam lahko omejeno zamudo, ne pa izmišljene izbire doline.
