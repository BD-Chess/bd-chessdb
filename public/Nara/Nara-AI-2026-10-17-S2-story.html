The Village That Could Not SleepVas, ki ni mogla spati

01 · Fever

Nemea clinic, operating room · 14:40

The third patient began to burn forty seconds after the anaesthetic took. Dr. Corin Hale stopped the drip before the monitor had finished saying so, and the room filled with the sound of everyone breathing in. Bartek Lind, who drove timber trucks and had been dug out of the cab of one that morning, lay rigid under the lights with a temperature climbing a degree every four minutes.

Osvin Pike, seventy, and Tomas Ketter, who tended the upper kilns, had burned the same way an hour before, one after the other. Both had been packed in ice and both had lived, and neither could be put under again. Illuminara had come up the pass with the first relief convoy and had been given a stool in the corner and a promise that she would be told if she was needed. She was needed now. She watched Hale pack the man in ice, and she did not speak until his temperature turned.

Corin HaleThat is the third. Same fever, three different people, and every agent I own does it. It is malignant hyperthermia or something that wears its face. It runs in families, and I have the dantrolene for two bodies, not fourteen.

Pelagia RuskThese are not families. I have the parish books. Eleven of the fourteen are Nemea people and they came here from three different valleys across a hundred years. Nobody in this clinic is anybody's cousin.

Corin HaleThen it is bad luck three times, and I do not operate on bad luck.

Corin HaleRegional blocks avoid those agents, but the spleen and the girl's leg cannot be reached that way. For the others it is one nerve at a time, twice the work, and each patient or family must consent. I might finish eight before dawn if my hands hold.

Dr. Rusk had run the village clinic for thirty years and was wearing the same cardigan she had been wearing when the mountain came down. She laid the list on the counter: fourteen names, four who could wait a night, ten who could not. A ruptured spleen. A pelvis. A girl of nine with a femur through the skin.

Nell Brandt, on the radioNemea, Brandt. Pass closes to freezing rain at twenty hundred. I can lift at seventeen and at nineteen, three stretchers a lift. That is six of fourteen. Tell me which six.

Pelagia RuskEight stay behind with an anaesthetic that fevers them.

Nell Brandt, on the radioThen six, and I will pray for the eight.

Illuminara took the list from the counter and read it as she read a load path, looking for the place where the weight did not go where it should. It took her a long minute. Then she asked Dr. Hale for the three charts, and the times on them.

02 · The Order of the Fevers

Nemea clinic, ward corridor · 15:05

She laid the charts side by side on the floor of the corridor, where the light was best. Beside the three reactions she set two more charts from among Rusk's fourteen: Sana Moreau, one of the three non-Nemea patients, a hiker whose wrist had been splinted with a sedative at noon without fever; and Ilo Vell, fourteen, sedated at one o'clock with a fever that had come and gone on its own.

IlluminaraA family trait would not care where a person slept. Read these five as a gradient. Osvin sat beside an upper-village hearth for seventy winters; Tomas lived at the kilns; Bartek slept two nights at the inn, which burns dark cake from the upper kilns; Ilo came home yesterday; Sana has not been indoors in Nemea once. Their reactions fall in that order.

Corin HaleA gradient by hours indoors. That is a coincidence of five.

IlluminaraFive out of five, in one direction. A gene cannot count nights. Something in the houses can. What is here that is not in the valley?

Nobody answered. Down the corridor a woman in a burned shawl, who had been sitting with her injured husband for six hours, lifted her head. This was Marta Vell, Ilo's mother and keeper of the village's resin kilns, and she had not been asked for anything yet.

Marta VellResin. Every house burns it. It has always been so; my grandmother's grandmother burned it. But it is not the old resin, not since the dry autumn.

Pelagia RuskWhat do you mean, not the old resin?

Marta VellThe upper kilns ran short. We stretched the cakes with bark off the scorched slope, the one that came down today. Dark cake. The houses below the church burn the pale stock still, because they were slow to buy. The kiln houses and the upper houses burn dark.

Illuminara said aloud that she had not known it and could not have known it.

IlluminaraThen here is the working model, written before we test it: something in the dark smoke may slow the same clearance pathway the anaesthetic needs. That is a prediction, not a diagnosis.

Corin HaleAnd the blood-gas analyser does not measure enzymes.

IlluminaraNot directly. Mix each plasma drop with the clinic's clear substrate and use the oxygen sensor to read consumption. If the dark cake is involved, the rate should follow hours in its smoke. Twelve minutes, no drug given. Marta, name the fourteen houses and what each burns.

Corin HaleAnd if you are wrong, ten people wait on a hearth superstition.

IlluminaraIf I am wrong, the machine says so in twelve minutes, and we have lost less time than your next attempt would cost.

Hale looked at the three charts for a long moment. Then he went to find the plasma tubes.

03 · Twelve Minutes

Nemea clinic, laboratory corner · 15:20 to 16:15

She wrote the ranking in the clinic log in ink, with the time, and Rusk signed beneath it as witness. At the bottom: the fourteen names in the order the machine ought to give them, slowest to fastest, built from Marta's list of what each house burned and how long each patient had sat in its smoke. Sana Moreau went last, as the fastest of all.

The machine took four samples at a time. Hale loaded it with a technician's care and a sceptic's face while Illuminara stood behind the curtain and did not touch the controls. At minute twelve the first run settled: Osvin Pike, slowest by a margin that made the technician swear softly, then Bartek Lind. Run after run, the slopes came out in the order she had written. When Hale loaded the fourth run, only two tubes remained. The room went quiet with a texture it had not had at the third fever.

In the fourth run the order broke. Osvin's grand-niece Ines, her left forearm splinted from the slide, sat on a stretcher beside his bed. She lived in a pale-stock house below the church. By Marta's list and Illuminara's ranking she belonged near the fast end. She stood fourth from the slow end.

Corin HaleThere. One in fourteen. Your hearth explains thirteen and fumbles the other.

IlluminaraIt is wrong. Good. A hearth that explained all fourteen would be a story. Marta—where did Ines sit today?

Marta VellShe sat on that stretcher beside Osvin. They carried him here from the slide at nine, and she has not left his bed.

Illuminara did not move for a moment. She had been looking at the houses, and the smoke she had been looking for had been in the room she was standing in.

IlluminaraDr. Rusk. What does this clinic burn?

Pelagia RuskThe big stove in the waiting room. The upper kiln sent down a sledge of cake at dawn, to keep the wounded warm. Dark cake. It is the only stove that heats all the beds.

There was a silence in which the hum of the machine was the loudest thing in the building.

IlluminaraThen the clinic is dosing every patient while they wait. The longer we argue, the slower the clearance. We are not waiting out the danger. We are feeding it.

On the screen the last pair settled. Sana Moreau, fastest of all, as written. The only fever in the room now was in Hale's face, and he was too honest a physician to hide that it was shame.

04 · The Stove

Nemea clinic, waiting room · 16:20 to 17:46

The stove stood at the end of the room like a low black animal, and a dozen faces turned from it to her as she came in. It was nine degrees outside the blanket line. Eight patients lay in shock with their feet toward the heat.

Pelagia RuskNo. If that goes cold, I lose the spleen and the girl to the chill before any anaesthetic touches them. You have found a poison and want me to put out the only fire.

IlluminaraNot put it out. Change one grate at a time, keep the other dark grate burning until the pale side catches, and measure every bed through the change. Marta, how much pale stock is left below the church?

Marta VellForty cakes. It was to last the village until the thaw. I can put the bakery's hot soapstone under the two shock beds while the first grate catches, but the stones will not hold all night.

IlluminaraThen I am asking you for the winter, and your stones buy the swap time. I will not pretend either is small.

Pelagia RuskTemperatures every four minutes. If any bed loses half a degree before the pale side catches, the dark grate stays lit. That is my stop condition.

Marta did not answer for the time it takes to count forty cakes against a hundred houses. Behind her a man lying under a coat said something about his own hearth and what it had meant for four hundred years, and she said, without turning, that the hearth had meant it for the living.

Marta VellOpen the storehouse. But I want it written that the village gave it, and that the dark cake does not go to the lowlands to be sold. It goes in the river.

IlluminaraWritten, and I will sign it where the village can read it.

The staged swap began at 16:24. Two kiln workers banked the left grate and laid pale cake on the right while Marta slid hot soapstone beneath the two shock beds. Hale opened the high vents. At 16:36 the girl's bedside reading had fallen four tenths of a degree. Rusk ordered the dark grate held for four more minutes; Marta shifted both hottest stones beneath the child and wrapped the exposed leg above the splint. By 16:44 the reading had recovered a tenth and the pale side held. Rusk alone gave the word to empty the dark grate, and the full pale burn caught four minutes later. While it caught, Rusk clipped a sheet to each flight stretcher with the dark-smoke hours and fever histories; Hale signed a request that the receiving anaesthetist hold the general until a clearance reading, or use a cooler regional option.

Nell Brandt, on the radioThe spleen and the girl's leg are the two your blocks cannot reach. I lift at seventeen with those two. The third stretcher space stays empty; nobody else is beyond the blocks, and I will not delay them to fill it. Note that I am not waiting for your assay.

IlluminaraDo not wait. Carry them. The next readings are for the twelve who remain.

At 17:04, forty minutes after the swap began and two minutes after Brandt lifted, Hale drew the second plasma set. Twelve minutes later the slopes had begun to rise. They rose unevenly, and Osvin Pike, who had breathed the most, had risen almost not at all.

Corin HaleTold you. He is the one it is in the blood for.

IlluminaraHe is also the one who has sat longest in the smoke. Draw him again at 17:34, seventy minutes after the swap began. If I am wrong about him, I will be glad to learn it now.

At 17:34 Hale drew the sample. At 17:46 the slope came up, slow and unmistakable. Osvin's was a washout, not a flaw. Hale took the sheet in both hands.

Corin HaleThen here is what I will do, and not one thing more. The two unreachable injuries are already in the air. I will block the eight urgent patients who remain, nerve by nerve, with consent and the numbers in front of them. The four who could safely wait will stay stabilised until their curves clear my line, and only then go under the general.

Pelagia RuskEight urgent blocks, four delayed generals, and the machine decides the order, not the clock.

Marta would not sign for her son's block until Hale put the plasma curve in her hands and showed her where Ilo sat and where the line ran. She read it with a finger. Then she signed, and said that she did not understand a word of it, and that she understood the line.

05 · What the Pass Kept

Nemea clinic and landing field · 17:00 to 04:10

The helicopter lifted at 17:02 with the girl of nine and the man with the ruptured spleen, and went up into the beginning of the rain with its lights burning. Brandt radioed from the valley at 17:58 that they were down and handed over: the receiving anaesthetist had read both sheets, run clearance readings and held each general until its line rose. The second lift at nineteen hundred stood on the pad unneeded, and she logged it that way, and told Illuminara afterwards that it was the best use of a flight she had ever made.

Dr. Hale blocked the eight urgent patients who remained. He worked from the least recovered to the most, with Rusk's hands beside his, and each time the plasma line had said who should be blocked and each time the family or the patient had said yes to the number. The four who had been stable enough to wait cleared his line in the early hours, went under the general and woke without fevers. The blocks took twice as long and held. Ines, her forearm dressed after her turn, fell asleep against the wall before Osvin Pike was wheeled in last at three-forty.

At 04:10 the last suture was in. In the waiting room the stove burned pale cake with its vents open, and nobody was cold, and nobody was feverish.

Illuminara stayed through the rest of the night because there was a log to finish. She wrote the protocol herself, in a plain hand, as Hale would use it and not as she would. A bench test: plasma and a clear substrate, with the oxygen sensor reading the consumption rate, twelve minutes, no drug given. A line below which a patient was blocked, not put under. A pair of readings half an hour apart for anyone who breathed smoke of unknown stock, because the slope moved and the machine could tell. Under it she wrote the name of the village that had given the winter, and the date, and Marta Vell signed it where the village could read it.

Pelagia RuskWhat does it cost us? Say the whole of it.

IlluminaraForty cakes of pale resin, which is the village's heat until the thaw. Dark cake does not burn indoors in Nemea again, and the kilns stand idle until someone finds what in the scorched bark does this. Two of the fourteen face long recoveries far from home. And the line on the sheet is provisional. It comes from fourteen people and one machine, and I would like it checked in a laboratory before anyone trusts it twice.

Pelagia RuskChecked. But it was right tonight.

IlluminaraIt was wrong once tonight, about Osvin's niece, and that is why it was right about the stove.

Rusk looked down the ward, where the patients slept in the pale light of the new fire. Then she asked Marta Vell to bring in forty cakes from the cold storehouse, and Marta, who had been awake for twenty-two hours, said she would bring them herself.

01 · Vročica

Klinika Nemea, operacijska soba · 14:40

Tretji bolnik je začel goreti štirideset sekund po tem, ko je anestetik prijel. Dr. Corin Hale je ustavil infuzijo, še preden je monitor dokončal opozorilo, in sobo je napolnil zvok, kako so vsi hkrati zajeli sapo. Bartek Lind, voznik tovornjakov z lesom, ki so ga tisto jutro izkopali iz kabine enega od njih, je otrdel ležal pod lučmi, temperatura pa mu je naraščala za stopinjo vsake štiri minute.

Sedemdesetletni Osvin Pike in Tomas Ketter, ki je skrbel za zgornje peči, sta uro prej gorela enako, eden za drugim. Oba so obdali z ledom in oba sta preživela, vendar nobenega niso smeli znova uspavati. Illuminara je čez prelaz prišla s prvim konvojem pomoči; dali so ji stol v kotu in obljubili, da ji bodo povedali, če jo bodo potrebovali. Zdaj so jo. Gledala je, kako je Hale moža obdal z ledom, in spregovorila šele, ko se je njegova temperatura obrnila navzdol.

Corin HaleTo je tretji. Ista vročica, trije različni ljudje in povzroči jo vsak anestetik, ki ga imam. To je maligna hipertermija ali pa nekaj, kar nosi njen obraz. Pojavlja se v družinah, jaz pa imam dantrolena za dve telesi, ne za štirinajst.

Pelagia RuskTo niso družine. Imam župnijske knjige. Enajst od štirinajstih je ljudi iz Nemee, sem pa so v zadnjih sto letih prišli iz treh različnih dolin. Nihče v tej kliniki ni sorodnik nikogar drugega.

Corin HalePotem gre trikrat za nesrečno naključje, jaz pa ne operiram na podlagi nesrečnih naključij.

Corin HaleRegionalne blokade se izognejo tem anestetikom, toda tako ne morem doseči vranice in dekličine noge. Pri drugih pomeni en živec naenkrat, dvakrat toliko dela, vsak bolnik ali družina pa mora privoliti. Morda jih do zore dokončam osem, če bodo moje roke zdržale.

Dr. Rusk je vaško kliniko vodila trideset let in nosila isto jopico, ki jo je imela na sebi, ko se je utrgala gora. Na pult je položila seznam: štirinajst imen, štirje, ki so lahko počakali eno noč, deset, ki niso mogli. Počena vranica. Medenica. Devetletna deklica s stegnenico skozi kožo.

Nell Brandt, po radijski zveziNemea, Brandt. Ledeni dež bo ob dvajseti uri zaprl prelaz. Poletim lahko ob sedemnajstih in ob devetnajstih, tri nosila na polet. To je šest od štirinajstih. Povejte mi, katerih šest.

Pelagia RuskOsem jih ostane z anestetikom, ki jim povzroča vročico.

Nell Brandt, po radijski zveziPotem šest, za osem pa bom molila.

Illuminara je vzela seznam s pulta in ga brala, kakor je brala pot obremenitve, iskaje mesto, kjer se teža ni razporedila, kakor bi se morala. Potrebovala je dolgo minuto. Nato je dr. Hala prosila za tri kartoteke in za čase na njih.

02 · Zaporedje vročic

Klinika Nemea, hodnik oddelka · 15:05

Kartoteke je razgrnila drugo ob drugo na tleh hodnika, kjer je bila svetloba najboljša. Ob tri reakcije je položila še dve kartoteki izmed Ruskovih štirinajstih: Sana Moreau, ena od treh bolnic, ki niso bile iz Nemee, pohodnica, ki so ji opoldne ob pomirjevalu naravnali zapestje brez vročice; ter štirinajstletni Ilo Vell, ki so mu ob enih dali pomirjevalo, vročica pa je prišla in sama izzvenela.

IlluminaraDružinski dejavnik se ne bi menil za to, kje kdo spi. Teh pet berite kot gradient. Osvin je sedemdeset zim sedel ob ognjišču v zgornji vasi; Tomas je živel pri pečeh; Bartek je dve noči spal v gostišču, ki kuri temne pogače iz zgornjih peči; Ilo se je včeraj vrnil domov; Sana v Nemei še niti enkrat ni bila v zaprtem prostoru. Njihove reakcije si sledijo v tem zaporedju.

Corin HaleGradient po urah v zaprtih prostorih. To je naključje petih.

IlluminaraPet od petih, v eni smeri. Gen ne zna šteti noči. Nekaj v hišah jih lahko. Kaj je tukaj, česar v dolini ni?

Nihče ni odgovoril. Na drugem koncu hodnika je glavo dvignila ženska v ožganem šalu, ki je šest ur sedela ob svojem poškodovanem možu. To je bila Marta Vell, Ilova mati in skrbnica vaških smolnih peči, in doslej je še nihče ni ničesar vprašal.

Marta VellSmola. Kuri jo vsaka hiša. Tako je bilo od nekdaj; kurila jo je že mati moje prababice. Toda to ni stara smola, ne po suhi jeseni.

Pelagia RuskKako to misliš, da ni stara smola?

Marta VellZgornjim pečem je je zmanjkalo. Pogače smo podaljšali z lubjem z ožganega pobočja, tistega, ki se je danes utrgalo. Temne pogače. Hiše pod cerkvijo še kurijo svetlo zalogo, ker so jo kupovale počasi. Hiše pri pečeh in zgornje hiše kurijo temno.

Illuminara je na glas povedala, da tega ni vedela in da tega ni mogla vedeti.

IlluminaraPotem je delovni model, zapisan, preden ga preizkusimo, takšen: nekaj v temnem dimu morda upočasnjuje isto presnovno pot, ki jo potrebuje anestetik. To je napoved, ne diagnoza.

Corin HaleAnalizator krvnih plinov pa ne meri encimov.

IlluminaraNe neposredno. Vsako kapljico plazme zmešajte z bistrim substratom klinike in s senzorjem kisika odčitajte porabo. Če je vpletena temna pogača, bi morala hitrost slediti uram v njenem dimu. Dvanajst minut, brez danega zdravila. Marta, naštej štirinajst hiš in povej, kaj kuri vsaka.

Corin HaleČe se motiš, deset ljudi čaka zaradi vraževerja o ognjišču.

IlluminaraČe se motim, bo naprava to povedala v dvanajstih minutah, izgubili pa bomo manj časa, kot bi nas stal vaš naslednji poskus.

Hale je dolgo gledal tri kartoteke. Nato je šel po epruvete s plazmo.

03 · Dvanajst minut

Klinika Nemea, laboratorijski kotiček · 15:20 do 16:15

Razvrstitev je s časom vred s črnilom zapisala v dnevnik klinike, Rusk pa se je kot priča podpisala pod njo. Na dnu je bilo štirinajst imen v zaporedju, v katerem naj bi jih razvrstila naprava, od najpočasnejšega do najhitrejšega, sestavljenem po Martinem seznamu goriva vsake hiše in časa, ki ga je vsak bolnik presedel v njenem dimu. Sana Moreau je bila zadnja, najhitrejša med vsemi.

Naprava je sprejela po štiri vzorce. Hale jo je polnil s tehnikovo natančnostjo in skeptikovim obrazom, Illuminara pa je stala za zaveso in se ni dotaknila krmil. Po dvanajstih minutah se je prva serija ustalila: Osvin Pike, najpočasnejši z razliko, ob kateri je tehnik potiho zaklel, nato Bartek Lind. Serija za serijo so se nakloni razvrstili tako, kakor jih je zapisala. Ko je Hale naložil četrto serijo, sta ostali le še dve epruveti. Tišina v sobi je dobila teksturo, ki je ob tretji vročici ni imela.

V četrti seriji se je zaporedje podrlo. Osvinova pranečakinja Ines, z levo podlaktjo v opornici zaradi plazu, je sedela na nosilih ob njegovi postelji. Živela je v hiši s svetlo zalogo pod cerkvijo. Po Martinem seznamu in Illuminariini razvrstitvi bi morala biti blizu hitrega konca. Bila je četrta od počasnega konca.

Corin HaleTako. Ena od štirinajstih. Tvoje ognjišče pojasni trinajst ljudi, pri zadnji pa se spotakne.

IlluminaraNapačno je. Dobro. Ognjišče, ki bi pojasnilo vseh štirinajst, bi bilo zgodba. Marta — kje je danes sedela Ines?

Marta VellSedela je na tistih nosilih ob Osvinu. Po plazu so ga ob devetih prinesli sem, ona pa ni zapustila njegove postelje.

Illuminara se trenutek ni premaknila. Gledala je hiše, dim, ki ga je iskala, pa je bil v prostoru, v katerem je stala.

IlluminaraDr. Rusk. Kaj kuri ta klinika?

Pelagia RuskVeliko peč v čakalnici. Zgornja peč je ob zori poslala sani pogač, da bi ranjencem bilo toplo. Temne pogače. To je edina peč, ki ogreva vse postelje.

Nastala je tišina, v kateri je bilo brnenje naprave najglasnejši zvok v stavbi.

IlluminaraPotem klinika med čakanjem odmerja dim vsakemu bolniku. Dlje ko se prepiramo, počasnejše je izločanje. Ne čakamo, da nevarnost mine. Hranimo jo.

Na zaslonu se je ustalil zadnji par. Sana Moreau, najhitrejša med vsemi, kakor je bilo zapisano. Edina vročica v sobi je bila zdaj na Halovem obrazu, bil pa je preveč pošten zdravnik, da bi skrival, da gre za sram.

04 · Peč

Klinika Nemea, čakalnica · 16:20 do 17:46

Peč je stala na koncu sobe kakor nizka črna žival in ducat obrazov se je ob Illuminariinem prihodu obrnil od nje k njej. Onkraj vrste odej je bilo devet stopinj. Osem bolnikov je v šoku ležalo z nogami proti toploti.

Pelagia RuskNe. Če se to ohladi, bom zaradi mraza izgubila vranico in deklico, še preden se ju dotakne kakršen koli anestetik. Našla si strup, zdaj pa želiš pogasiti edini ogenj.

IlluminaraNe pogasiti. Menjajmo po eno rešetko, drugo temno rešetko pa pustimo goreti, dokler se svetla stran ne razgori, ter med menjavo merimo vsako posteljo. Marta, koliko svetle zaloge je še pod cerkvijo?

Marta VellŠtirideset pogač. Zadoščale naj bi vasi do odjuge. Medtem ko se prva rešetka razgoreva, lahko pod dve postelji bolnikov v šoku položim vroč milovec iz pekarne, toda kamni ne bodo držali vso noč.

IlluminaraPotem te prosim za zimo, tvoji kamni pa nam kupijo čas za menjavo. Ne bom se pretvarjala, da je eno ali drugo majhna stvar.

Pelagia RuskTemperature vsake štiri minute. Če katera koli postelja izgubi pol stopinje, preden se svetla stran razgori, ostane temna rešetka prižgana. To je moj pogoj za ustavitev.

Marta ni odgovorila toliko časa, kolikor ga je potrebno, da prešteješ štirideset pogač proti stotim hišam. Za njo je moški, ki je ležal pod plaščem, nekaj rekel o lastnem ognjišču in o tem, kaj je pomenilo štiristo let, ona pa je brez obračanja odgovorila, da je ognjišče pomenilo to za žive.
