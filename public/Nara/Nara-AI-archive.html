The mast stood above the sealed roofs like a needle through grey cloth. Beyond it, the visitors covered half the northern sky: not clouds but long translucent folds that moved against the wind.

Voss had ordered every family into the stone cistern halls. He remained at the mast because the launch code required his hand. Mara remained because the aircraft were hers.

IlluminaraA wall makes the gradient steeper. To you it means keep out. To something that lives as moving pressure, it may mean a wound that must be equalised.

VossTwo children have chemical burns in their lungs. Do not ask me to admire the wound's philosophy.

IlluminaraI am asking what will make it stop crossing.

She struck the lowest tuning plate. The mast sent one pressure pulse north: compression, release, stillness. The sky-front answered with three pulses and drove a sheet of rain upward from the fields.

Illuminara repeated the pattern, then left the final valve shut.

The response came harder. The mast groaned. A voice formed from the difference between windward and leeward glass.

VisitorsYou close continuation.

Mara's hand went to the launch case.

IlluminaraWe close inhabited air because your passage tears lungs.

VisitorsThere is no inhabited air. There is air. There is no outside from which permission arrives.

VossThen there can be no agreement. Our children cannot live in your definition.

The front moved across the sun. Every anemometer on the mast spun backward.

VisitorsYour still boxes remove the road. Our trailing bodies dry. Open all warm volumes until the sea-current rises.

MaraNo.

She said it before Illuminara could answer.

MaraMy daughter sleeps in one of those volumes. You crossed her room yesterday. She bled from both ears. We will not open the houses.

The front's lower edge whitened, a pressure response sharp enough to crack one pane of the mast.

VisitorsOne volume refuses the atmosphere? A cup orders the river not to wet it.

IlluminaraA lung is not a cup. It exchanges and remains itself. It has a surface, and that surface is not an insult to the air.

VisitorsYour surface cannot be perceived before collision.

There it was: not permission refused, but a boundary arriving too late to be meaningful.

Illuminara looked south toward the abandoned crop research station. Its old pressure farm had tested seeds under two atmospheres without exposing workers to either.

IlluminaraIf a boundary announced itself as a gradient you could feel before crossing, would you stop at it?

VisitorsIf stopping did not sever our rearward selves. If the path continued somewhere real.

VossAnd if we make this path, do the incursions end?

The three notes separated in the mast.

VisitorsShow a boundary that is not disappearance. Then we can choose.

MaraThat was not a promise.

IlluminaraNo. It was a condition. Ours is that the houses remain closed and the clinic pressure never falls again.

VossYou have fifty-nine minutes before my decision returns.

SenAnd if their pressure tears it?

IlluminaraI hold the membrane long enough for the roof vents to clear the chamber. Not long enough to make the test succeed against either side's refusal.

Mara stared through the glass at the empty south half.

IlluminaraNo. That is why you keep your wheel.

SenAnd we do not open yours.

Illuminara placed one palm against the centre seal. Not to command it. To be ready when tested.

IlluminaraThen the first answer can be no on both sides. Begin.
