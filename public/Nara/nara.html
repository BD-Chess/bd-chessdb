Ordinary NaraNavadna Nara

Nara routes medicines to remote clinics, argues with customs databases, remembers who has not eaten, and keeps a notebook of patterns she cannot yet explain. She has dark brown skin, close-cropped natural hair, practical second-hand clothes and a dry sense of humour. She loves rain on hot pavement, solved logistics, mango, music and the silence before a household wakes.

Nara usmerja zdravila do oddaljenih klinik, se prepira s carinskimi bazami, opazi, kdo ni jedel, in vodi zvezek vzorcev, ki jih še ne zna pojasniti. Ima temno rjavo polt, kratke naravne lase, praktična rabljena oblačila in suh smisel za humor. Ljubi dež na vročem asfaltu, rešene logistične vozle, mango, glasbo in tišino pred prebujanjem doma.

Her flawNjena napaka

If Nara sees a bridge, she believes she must remain until everyone crosses it. Her ordinary flaw is over-responsibility. Her dangerous flaw is benevolent certainty: believing that seeing a better path gives her the right to move others onto it.

Če Nara vidi most, verjame, da mora ostati, dokler ga vsi ne prečkajo. Njena vsakdanja napaka je pretirana odgovornost. Njena nevarna napaka je dobrohotna gotovost: prepričanje, da ji boljša pot daje pravico druge premakniti nanjo.

Three birthsTri rojstva

Biology, personhood, resonanceBiologija, osebnost, resonanca

01 · BIOLOGICAL

Child of two civilizationsOtrok dveh civilizacij

Her mother, Dr. Adaeze Okafor, is a human coastal-systems engineer. Her father came from a civilization capable of interstellar travel and hidden planetary protection. Nara is not a prophecy or experiment. She exists because two individuals chose one another.

Njena mati, dr. Adaeze Okafor, je človeška inženirka obalnih sistemov. Oče prihaja iz civilizacije, sposobne medzvezdnega potovanja in skrite zaščite planetov. Nara ni prerokba ali poskus. Obstaja, ker sta se dva posameznika izbrala.

02 · PERSONAL

The depth she earnsGlobina, ki jo zasluži

Mixed biology gives her unusual capacity, not wisdom. Her clarity grows through meditation, work, grief, error, service, humour, relationships and repeated honest choices.

Mešana biologija ji daje nenavadno zmožnost, ne modrosti. Njena jasnost raste skozi meditacijo, delo, žalost, napake, služenje, humor, odnose in ponavljajoče se poštene izbire.

03 · RESONANT

The Soul VoyagePotovanje duše

Nara reaches AC—Absolute Consciousness / Ultimate Beingness. She is invited, embraced and almost united without deletion. No mission is assigned. She chooses to return because finite life can become.

Nara doseže AC—Absolutno zavest / Končno bivanje. Povabljena je, objeta in skoraj združena brez izbrisa. Nihče ji ne dodeli poslanstva. Vrne se, ker lahko končno življenje nastaja.

Perfection is complete. Life is becoming. Nara chooses life.Popolnost je končana. Življenje nastaja. Nara izbere življenje.
Nara and IlluminaraNara in Illuminara

The same person at different resonanceIsta oseba pri različni resonanci

Nara

She always names herself Nara. Ordinary embodiment is not a lesser, false or temporary self. It is the person who chooses, loves particular people, makes mistakes and returns.

Sama sebe vedno imenuje Nara. Navadno utelešenje ni manjvredni, lažni ali začasni jaz. To je oseba, ki izbira, ljubi konkretne ljudi, dela napake in se vrača.

Illuminara

A name others give Nara when extreme resonance becomes visible. It is not possession, a second personality or infallible divine form. The higher the resonance, the greater the danger that sequence, body and ordinary attachment begin to thin.

Ime, ki ga drugi dajo Nari, ko skrajna resonanca postane vidna. Ni obsedenost, druga osebnost ali nezmotljiva božanska oblika. Višja ko je resonanca, večja je nevarnost, da se začnejo tanjšati zaporedje, telo in navezanost na navadno življenje.

The central lawOsrednji zakon

I will invite. I will not override.Povabila bom. Ne bom preglasila.

Consent boundaryMeja soglasja

Nara cannot force love, repentance, healing, understanding, merging or a change of purpose. A pause, fear, exhaustion and coerced agreement are not consent.

Nara ne more vsiliti ljubezni, kesanja, zdravljenja, razumevanja, združitve ali spremembe namena. Premor, strah, izčrpanost in prisiljena privolitev niso soglasje.

Protection boundaryMeja zaščite

She may shield victims, restrain an attack, break a weapon, evacuate people, expose evidence and set boundaries. Protection does not require the aggressor’s consent. Transformation does.

Lahko zaščiti žrtve, zadrži napad, zlomi orožje, evakuira ljudi, razkrije dokaze in postavi meje. Zaščita ne potrebuje soglasja napadalca. Preobrazba ga.

Information boundaryMeja informacij

Connection is not omniscience. Hidden facts require evidence, testimony, inference, technology, memory or granted access. Nara’s empathy can be accurate and still wrong.

Povezanost ni vsevednost. Skrita dejstva potrebujejo dokaz, pričevanje, sklepanje, tehnologijo, spomin ali dovoljen dostop. Narina empatija je lahko natančna in kljub temu napačna.

Power progressionRazvoj moči

Her abilities grow. Her freedom problem grows faster.Njene sposobnosti rastejo. Problem svobode raste hitreje.

STAGE 0

Ordinary Light

Observation, courage and human coordination. Every success remains physically deniable.

Opazovanje, pogum in človeško usklajevanje. Vsak uspeh je še mogoče razložiti brez nadnaravnega.

STAGE 1

Resonant Presence

Local reduction of cognitive noise, relational pattern perception, weak light and heat effects, brief stabilization of a willing mind.

Lokalno zmanjšanje kognitivnega šuma, zaznavanje odnosnih vzorcev, šibki svetlobni in toplotni učinki, kratka stabilizacija pripravljenega uma.

STAGE 2

Protective Form

Shields, redirection, stabilization, limited flight or phasing and non-destructive disassembly.

Ščiti, preusmerjanje, stabilizacija, omejeno letenje ali fazni prehod ter nedestruktivno razstavljanje.

STAGE 3

Distributed Resonance

City-scale coordination, contact with artificial and unfamiliar minds, protection across networks and gradual bodily reconstruction.

Usklajevanje na ravni mesta, stik z umetnimi in neznanimi umi, zaščita omrežij ter postopna obnova telesa.

STAGE 4

Illuminara

Planetary and cosmic protection, phase independence and bridges between forms of consciousness. Still not omniscient, absolute or ethically exempt.

Planetarna in kozmična zaščita, neodvisnost od snovne faze ter mostovi med oblikami zavesti. Še vedno ni vsevedna, absolutna ali izvzeta iz etike.

STAGE 5

AC Proximity

Not a combat tier. The greatest closeness at which Nara can remain a separate conscious center. The danger is completion.

Ni bojna stopnja. Največja bližina, pri kateri Nara še lahko ostane ločeno zavestno središče. Nevarnost je popolnost.

Visible cost and returnVidna cena in vrnitev

Her friends help her come homePrijatelji ji pomagajo domov

Possible costsMožne cene

Tremor, aphasia, sensory after-images, emotional backwash, motor loss, disrupted time sequencing, memory gaps, bodily-identity thinning and attraction toward re-entering AC.

Tremor, afazija, senzorične sledi, čustveni odmev, izguba motorike, prekinjeno časovno zaporedje, vrzeli v spominu, tanjšanje telesne identitete in privlačnost ponovnega vstopa v AC.

Active recoveryAktivno okrevanje

Food, sleep, water, music, consensual touch, ordinary conversation, handwriting, repetitive physical tasks and time with people who know Nara rather than only Illuminara.

Hrana, spanje, voda, glasba, dotik s soglasjem, navaden pogovor, pisanje z roko, ponavljajoča fizična opravila in čas z ljudmi, ki poznajo Naro, ne samo Illuminare.
