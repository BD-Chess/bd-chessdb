Let Me Leave AngryPusti, da odidem jezna

After returning from a conflict large enough to erase civilizations, Nara enters a rain-dark kitchen where none of her scale can repair what she missed. She can offer her mother a protected meeting with wider awareness. She cannot make the offer innocent, and she cannot make the answer yes.Po vrnitvi iz spopada, dovolj velikega, da bi izbrisal civilizacije, Nara vstopi v kuhinjo, temno od dežja, kjer nobena od njenih razsežnosti ne more popraviti tega, kar je zamudila. Materi lahko ponudi zaščiteno srečanje s širšim zavedanjem. Ponudbe ne more narediti nedolžne in odgovora ne more spremeniti v da.
