01 · The Seam

The plains of Orrel · morning, nineteen days before the Concord's predicted seal

Dray Vennick had kept the fault station for thirty-one years, and in that time the seam over the plain had done nothing but breathe: a shimmer like heat above a road, only cold, in and out once every fourteen hours. This morning it had company. Something stood in the grass on the far side of it, patient as weather, and the air around it sounded like a choir tuning up.

He had made tea for the wrong number of people. The Concord's chair arrived before sunrise in a grey coat, with two assistants she told to wait by the car. Illuminara came a quarter of an hour later, on foot, because she wanted to see the plain before she saw the problem.

Maru TessalonYou are not here to open it.

IlluminaraI'm here to listen. I can't do anything useful until I know what I'm listening to.

The answer came from the grass, from the sky, and from a point just behind her left shoulder, each part finishing the sentence the last one had begun.

Ilvane-in-manyWe are four thousand two hundred. We asked before we came, and we want that known. We have not touched your side of the seam. We will not, unless we are let.

TessalonThe Concord exists to prevent exactly this. Not you. This. A mind that arrives and changes the numbers the world is built from, because it is kind and frightened and has nowhere else to go.

She said it flatly, like someone reading out a rule she had once cried over. Illuminara did not argue. She sat down in the wet grass, a body-length from the seam, and Dray, who had never seen a person do that on purpose, brought her a cup.

02 · An Ordinary Afternoon

The plains of Orrel · the same day, noon

She asked them what was ending, and then she did the difficult part, which was to stop talking.

Ilvane-in-manyThe ordinary kind of future. Things still happen with us. They no longer lead anywhere. Tomorrow arrives as a copy of today, a little thinner each time, like a page photographed from a photograph.

IlluminaraShow me one.

Ilvane-in-manyA woman puts a kettle on. She wonders, for the length of a breath, whether to make tea or the other thing. We have had her afternoon for four hundred of our years. It is always tea. The wondering is the last live thing in it, and we can feel it going.

Illuminara asked for the whole afternoon, and they gave it to her. It took four hours. Dray carried out a second chair and then a third. Tessalon stood with her arms folded until the light changed, and at some point stopped standing.

Somewhere past the second hour Illuminara noticed that she was holding her breath each time the woman reached for the kettle. She was waiting for her to choose. When the hand came down on the tea again, she felt the small shut click of it somewhere under her ribs, and understood that this, over and over, was what they were fleeing: not death, which they could have borne, but the end of being able to want the other thing.

IlluminaraThank you. I'd like you to know that someone heard the whole of it.

Ilvane-in-manyNobody has heard the whole of it. We did not know that we wanted that.

03 · The Third Group

The fault station, back room · evening

Tessalon would not be comforted, and Illuminara had the sense not to try. She told it standing at the window, with the seam breathing behind her in the glass.

TessalonForty years ago, at Veth, a smaller fault opened and one ark came through. Dense, gentle, begging. The Concord of that day said yes, because they could not bear to say no. Everything they carried arrived at once, and the numbers slipped by a part in a million. Nobody died. The bread at Veth Quay has not risen properly since. Glass clouds. Every year someone's child asks why the harbour looks tired.

IlluminaraI'm sorry. Truly.

TessalonDon't. Answer me. Every desperate delegation is sincere. Your sympathy is exactly what the next one will use. Tell me what you will do when the third group isn't so polite.

It was a good question, and Illuminara found she did not have the answer. She said so. Then, because it was true and she was tired, she said the other thing as well.

IlluminaraI could hold the seam wide and take the shift into myself. I think I could. I think it would hold.

TessalonAnd then you would be the keeper of a universe nobody asked you to keep. It cannot consent, Illuminara. That is the whole of my office: to stand in for something that cannot say yes. You offer to carry what is not yours to carry, and you call it kindness.

There was no cruelty in it. Illuminara looked at her own offered hands, and put them away.

IlluminaraThen that's off the table. What I'd like instead is to find out what the number actually depends on.

04 · Thirty-One Years of Weather

The fault station, pantry · night

The Concord's instruments recorded peaks, because peaks were what the Concord feared. What Dray recorded was everything. In the pantry, between the flour and the kettle descaler, were thirty-one years of school exercise books in which he had written, twice a day, how the seam looked and which way the wind had gone.

DrayI never thought anyone would ask. It breathes. Fourteen hours in, fourteen out, and after it has been pushed on, it settles back. I wrote the settling down because my father's logbook did.

Illuminara read until her eyes went dry. Tessalon came in at midnight to tell her to stop, saw the columns, and read as well. By the third bell of the night they had it on the back of a seed catalogue.

TessalonIt digests. The shell takes in a fixed amount per breath of the seam and gives back what it can. Spread across the breath, it leaves no mark at all. Veth was not too many. Veth was too fast.

IlluminaraThe number depends on how quickly they arrive, not how many. They could come in as spread out as you like.

TessalonThey won't. They're a single body. They came here dense, and that is how they'll cross, in one stride, to our shame and theirs.

DrayThere's one odd page, if you want it. Eleven years back a freight shimmer from the far side leaned on the seam the whole of an afternoon. I'd have said it would tear. It went thin as a thread and would not pass a thing. I wrote sulking.

TessalonNoise. One afternoon, one shimmer.

DrayI wrote it down anyway.

TessalonThe tables say the seam seals in nineteen days.

DrayThe tables are peaks. These are days. Mine say it has been narrowing since spring. A narrowing thing isn't a closing door. It's a throat. It will be one by the nineteenth day, and it will stay one.

TessalonThen we have a wide lip for nineteen days and a narrow one for good. And the guests think they can only cross in one stride.

Illuminara went back out into the dark and asked the one question nobody had asked them.

IlluminaraWhy one stride? Why all together?

Ilvane-in-manyBecause the last one to cross crosses alone. We could come as small as you like. We could come a mind at a time over a thousand years. But whoever waits, waits in the dark with the seam closing, and nobody in the last years would see the faces of the people who had already gone. We would rather arrive together than be counted out one at a time.

They were not asking to be guests. They were asking to be a gradient, and had never been told that this was something one could ask for.

05 · The Quiet Room

The plains of Orrel · dawn, sixteen days before the throat

She said the limits first, because she had learned that a gift without its limits is a trap.

IlluminaraI can make a room. I can hold it against the inside lip of the seam so that it belongs to neither of us, and I can keep it for the sixteen days the lip stays wide, until the seam has narrowed enough to look after itself. Four hundred of you, no more, because that is as many as the lip can take at a safe pace in that time. It will cost me the use of my hands for those sixteen days, and I will not be able to leave. I will not carry any one of you into Orrel. Not one. That belongs to the Concord, a person at a time, and the Concord can say no.

Tessalon had not expected to be named in a promise. She sat down across from the grass.

Ilvane-in-manyWhy would you do that?

IlluminaraSo that nobody waits alone. So that the people who decide about you have to be in a room with you while they do it. I don't have a better reason. I would like there to be one.

A small voice detached itself from the chord, as if one string had decided to be heard on its own. It said its name was Sef. It was, as far as anyone could tell, the youngest of them.

SefI want to go first. If someone has to find out what the other side is like, let it be the one with the least to lose.

IlluminaraThat's not a thing to say lightly.

SefI'm not saying it lightly. I'm saying it small. It is the size I come in.

Tessalon looked at the youngest voice for a long moment. Then she took out her notebook, and began, a little to her own surprise, to write the terms.

06 · The Stone

The fault station, instrument bench · twelve days before the throat

They tried it first with a stone. The guests parted with a pebble of their unliving substance, no heavier than a walnut, and sent it through as dense as the whole of them would have been, to see how the world would take a lie told in miniature. The third needle on Tessalon's dial quivered by a hair, and exactly as far as Dray's old columns had said it would.

TessalonAgain. This time as fifteen, spread across one breath of the seam, evenly, a pebble about every hour.

Fifteen pebbles passed in the long breath, and the needle sat as still as a drawing of a needle. Tessalon painted a hair of red on the dial, at the mark the shift must never cross, and wrote the rule on the station door in chalk where anyone on the plain could read it.

TessalonIf the needle touches the red, the crossing stops. No appeal, no speech. Fifteen to a breath, spread across it, never more. The key to the lever is Dray's. A second key is held by whoever Orrel's farms elect. Mine does not open it. I will not be the only hand on the door.

DrayI have never voted for anyone I was not related to.

TessalonThen you will find that quite a lot of the plain is related to you.

TessalonOnce more, and ugly this time. All fifteen in one instant, as hard as you can push.

The guests pushed. Four pebbles crossed before the lip caught. The needle crept to a hair short of the red and went back, and the other eleven slid away on the guest side as if a hand had flattened them.

DraySulking.

TessalonNoise.

She heard it as she said it, and said nothing more for a while.

IlluminaraA group that isn't polite gets the first stumble and then a wall.

TessalonFour in fifteen, from a pebble, in one gulp. Spread across the breath, none. I can write a rule around a first stumble. I could never have written one around no wall at all. The red stays. A pebble is not a people.

A woman in a harbour coat had come over the plain on a cart and stood reading the chalk with her lips moving. She gave her name as Ansa Mereth, of the Veth Quay delegation, which nobody had thought to invite.

Ansa Mereth'No one is pushed on.' We were pushed on.

TessalonYes.

Ansa MerethWe will vote against your Charter.

TessalonI would think less of you if you didn't. Dray's columns are open to you, and the needle logs from every test, today's included, and from every crossing to come. Take them to your harbour and test it for the slip. If the fault was ours, I will say so on your quay, in front of the bakers.

Ansa MerethThe bread has been wrong for forty years.

TessalonThen let us find out why.

Ansa took the logs. She did not say thank you, and Tessalon did not seem to have expected it.

The guests asked what would happen to someone halfway through when the lever fell. Tessalon told them honestly: they would be held in the Quiet Room and neither pushed on nor sent back. Ilvane-in-many took the answer in silence, and at last said they would cross under that rule, and that the rule should include a door for them as well: any one of them might withdraw, at any breath, without a reason being asked.

IlluminaraThat should be in the chalk.

TessalonIt already is. I wrote it while you were talking.

07 · Tea, or the Other Thing

The plains of Orrel · the last day, dusk

The lip narrowed all that fortnight, as Dray's columns had said it would and Tessalon's tables had said it wouldn't. Four hundred crossed in the Quiet Room's keeping, fifteen to a breath, spread across it, each one standing at the lip of the world while a Concord clerk read the host's laws aloud and the traveller answered yes, and each name was written in Dray's book beside the weather. Dray called the needle at every breath, and every breath it stayed well short of the red. By the last day Illuminara had not used her hands for sixteen days, and had learned a great deal about the patience of ordinary people.

On the nineteenth day the seam did not seal. It finished narrowing into a throat, a hair-fine door whose width the passing itself kept open and whose width the shell, breathing, steadily took back, and Illuminara felt the room lose its work as the throat took it up. It would pass about one mind a month. Dray set the live curve from the slate against his oldest column, and the two lay one on the other like a hand on a hand. He laughed out loud, startled, in the dusk.

DrayIt's a gate that won't be forced. I've been writing down a gate for thirty years.

TessalonAnd the rude ones will find it just as the pebbles did. First stumble, then wall.

Tessalon signed the Gradient Charter under protest, which she had said she would do and did, and at the last clause stopped and added one of her own. The Concord's veto at every stage would be shared with Orrel's farmers, and her own would not outrank theirs. She did not look at anyone as she wrote it.

The other thirty-eight hundred would come through the throat over three centuries, at their own pace and by their own choice. Many would wait asleep together as one chord, so that nobody waited alone. The oldest of them, which had not spoken in all those days, asked only that its name be written in Dray's book before the weather, and it was. Most of their universe was already gone and would stay gone.

Ansa Mereth stood at the back with the needle logs rolled under her arm. Her delegation had voted against the Charter, and said so, and been heard. She had not changed her vote. She nodded once to Tessalon, who nodded back, and neither of them looked away.

Sef went first through the throat, at dusk, with one hand held out in front of it as if the air might be a table. It stopped on the host side and stood very still.

SefIt's loud. Everything is loud. Is that what you meant by wind?

IlluminaraThat's what I meant by wind.

Dray was waiting with two cups. He had made them in the dark, by feel, with the dignity of a man who had waited thirty-one years for company.

DrayTea, or the other thing?

Sef did not answer for a long time. Illuminara, with her hands at last her own again, watched it take every moment of it, and let it.

01 · Šiv

Orrellske ravnice · jutro, devetnajst dni pred napovedanim pečatenjem Konkordata

Dray Vennick je postajo ob prelomu vzdrževal enaintrideset let in v vsem tem času šiv nad ravnico ni počel drugega, kakor dihal: lesketal se je kakor vročina nad cesto, le da je bil hladen, ter se vsakih štirinajst ur skrčil in razširil. Tisto jutro je imel družbo. Nekaj je stalo v travi na njegovi drugi strani, potrpežljivo kakor vreme, zrak okoli njega pa je zvenel kakor zbor, ki uglašuje glasove.

Skuhal je čaj za napačno število ljudi. Predsednica Konkordata je prispela pred sončnim vzhodom v sivem plašču, z dvema pomočnikoma, ki jima je naročila, naj počakata pri avtu. Illuminara je prišla četrt ure pozneje, peš, ker je hotela videti ravnico, preden bi videla problem.

Maru TessalonNiste tukaj, da bi ga odprli.

IlluminaraTukaj sem, da poslušam. Nič koristnega ne morem storiti, dokler ne vem, kaj poslušam.

Odgovor je prišel iz trave, z neba in iz točke tik za njeno levo ramo; vsak del je dokončal stavek, ki ga je začel prejšnji.

Ilvane-in-manyŠtiri tisoč dvesto nas je. Vprašali smo, preden smo prišli, in želimo, da je to znano. Vaše strani šiva se nismo dotaknili. Ne bomo se je, razen če nam dovolite.

TessalonKonkordat obstaja prav zato, da prepreči to. Ne vas. To. Um, ki pride in spremeni števila, iz katerih je zgrajen svet, ker je prijazen in prestrašen ter nima kam drugam.

Izrekla je brezbarvno, kakor nekdo, ki bere pravilo, ob katerem je nekoč jokal. Illuminara ni ugovarjala. Usedla se je v mokro travo, za dolžino telesa od šiva, in Dray, ki še nikoli ni videl človeka, da bi to storil namenoma, ji je prinesel skodelico.

02 · Navaden popoldan

Orrellske ravnice · isti dan, opoldne

Vprašala jih je, kaj se končuje, potem pa je opravila težji del: nehala je govoriti.

Ilvane-in-manyObičajna vrsta prihodnosti. Pri nas se stvari še vedno dogajajo. Le nikamor več ne vodijo. Jutri pride kot kopija današnjega dne, vsakič nekoliko tanjša, kakor stran, fotografirana s fotografije.

IlluminaraPokažite mi enega.

Ilvane-in-manyŽenska pristavi čajnik. Za dolžino vdiha se sprašuje, ali bi skuhala čaj ali tisto drugo. Njen popoldan imamo že štiristo naših let. Vedno je čaj. To spraševanje je zadnja živa stvar v njem in čutimo, kako ugaša.

Illuminara je prosila za ves popoldan in dali so ji ga. Trajalo je štiri ure. Dray je prinesel še drugi stol in nato tretjega. Tessalon je stala s prekrižanimi rokami, dokler se svetloba ni spremenila, in nekje vmes je nehala stati.

Nekje po drugi uri je Illuminara opazila, da zadržuje dih vsakič, ko ženska seže po čajniku. Čakala je, da se odloči. Ko se je roka znova spustila na čaj, je nekje pod rebri začutila droben, zaprt klik in razumela, da je to, znova in znova, tisto, pred čimer bežijo: ne smrt, ki bi jo lahko prenesli, temveč konec zmožnosti želeti si tisto drugo.

IlluminaraHvala. Želim, da veste, da je nekdo slišal vse.

Ilvane-in-manyNihče ni slišal vsega. Nismo vedeli, da si tega želimo.

03 · Tretja skupina

Postaja ob prelomu, zadnja soba · večer

Tessalon se ni hotela pustiti potolažiti in Illuminara je bila dovolj razumna, da ni poskusila. Povedala ji je stoje ob oknu, za katerim je v steklu dihal šiv.

TessalonPred štiridesetimi leti se je pri Vethu odprl manjši prelom in skozenj je prišla ena barka. Gosta, blaga, proseča. Tedanji Konkordat je rekel da, ker ni prenesel, da bi rekel ne. Vse, kar so nosili, je prispelo naenkrat in števila so zdrsnila za milijoninko. Nihče ni umrl. Kruh v pristanišču Veth odtlej ne vzhaja pravilno. Steklo se motni. Vsako leto kak otrok vpraša, zakaj je pristanišče videti utrujeno.

IlluminaraŽal mi je. Resnično.

TessalonNe. Odgovorite mi. Vsaka obupana delegacija je iskrena. Prav vaše sočutje bo naslednja izkoristila. Povejte mi, kaj boste storili, ko tretja skupina ne bo tako vljudna.

Bilo je dobro vprašanje in Illuminara je ugotovila, da nima odgovora. To je tudi povedala. Potem pa je, ker je bilo res in ker je bila utrujena, povedala še drugo.

IlluminaraŠiv bi lahko držala široko odprt in premik vzela vase. Mislim, da bi lahko. Mislim, da bi zdržalo.

TessalonIn potem bi bili varuhinja vesolja, za katerega vas nihče ni prosil, da ga varujete. Ne more privoliti, Illuminara. To je ves namen mojega položaja: zastopati nekaj, kar ne more reči da. Ponujate se, da boste nosili nekaj, kar ni vaše, in temu pravite prijaznost.

V tem ni bilo nobene krutosti. Illuminara je pogledala svoje ponujene dlani in jih umaknila.

IlluminaraPotem tega ne bomo storili. Namesto tega bi rada ugotovila, od česa je število v resnici odvisno.

04 · Enaintrideset let vremena

Postaja ob prelomu, shramba · noč

Instrumenti Konkordata so beležili vrhove, ker se je Konkordat bal vrhov. Dray pa je beležil vse. V shrambi, med moko in sredstvom za odstranjevanje vodnega kamna iz čajnika, je ležalo enaintrideset let šolskih zvezkov, v katere je dvakrat na dan zapisoval, kakšen je bil šiv in kam je pihal veter.

DrayNikoli nisem mislil, da bo kdo vprašal. Diha. Štirinajst ur noter, štirinajst ven, in ko nekaj pritisne nanj, se spet umiri. Umirjanje sem zapisoval, ker ga je zapisoval očetov dnevnik.

Illuminara je brala, dokler se ji niso izsušile oči. Tessalon je ob polnoči prišla, da bi ji rekla, naj neha, zagledala stolpce in začela brati tudi sama. Do tretjega nočnega zvona sta odgovor zapisali na hrbtno stran kataloga semen.

TessalonPrebavlja. Ovojnica pri vsakem vdihu šiva sprejme določeno količino in vrne, kar lahko. Če jo razporedimo čez ves vdih, ne pusti nobene sledi. Pri Vethu jih ni bilo preveč. Veth je bil prehiter.

IlluminaraŠtevilo je odvisno od tega, kako hitro pridejo, ne od tega, koliko jih je. Prihajali bi lahko tako razpršeno, kakor želite.

TessalonNe bodo. Eno telo so. Sem so prišli gosti in tako bodo tudi prečkali, v enem koraku, v našo in svojo sramoto.

DrayEna nenavadna stran je, če jo želite. Pred enajstimi leti se je tovorni lesk z druge strani ves popoldan naslanjal na šiv. Rekel bi, da ga bo raztrgalo. Stanjšal se je kakor nit in ni prepustil ničesar. Zapisal sem kuja se.

TessalonŠum. En popoldan, en lesk.

DrayVseeno sem zapisal.

TessalonTabele pravijo, da se bo šiv zapečatil čez devetnajst dni.

DrayTabele so vrhovi. To so dnevi. Moji zapisi pravijo, da se oži že od pomladi. Nekaj, kar se oži, ni vrata, ki se zapirajo. Je žrelo. Do devetnajstega dne bo postalo žrelo in takšno bo ostalo.

TessalonPotem imamo devetnajst dni široko ustje in za vedno ozko. Gostje pa mislijo, da lahko prečkajo le v enem koraku.

Illuminara se je vrnila ven v temo in zastavila edino vprašanje, ki jim ga ni zastavil nihče.

IlluminaraZakaj en korak? Zakaj vsi skupaj?

Ilvane-in-manyKer zadnji, ki prečka, prečka sam. Lahko bi prihajali v poljubno majhnih skupinah. Lahko bi prihajali po en um naenkrat tisoč let. Toda kdor čaka, čaka v temi, medtem ko se šiv zapira, in v zadnjih letih nihče ne bi videl obrazov ljudi, ki so že odšli. Raje pridemo skupaj, kakor da nas enega za drugim odštevajo.

Niso prosili, da bi bili gostje. Prosili so, da bi bili gradient, in nikoli jim nihče ni povedal, da je za kaj takega mogoče prositi.

05 · Tiha soba

Orrellske ravnice · zora, šestnajst dni pred nastankom žrela

Najprej je navedla omejitve, ker se je naučila, da je dar brez svojih omejitev past.

IlluminaraLahko ustvarim sobo. Držim jo lahko ob notranjem ustju šiva, tako da ne pripada nobenemu od nas, in ohranim jo lahko šestnajst dni, kolikor ustje ostane široko, dokler se šiv ne zoži dovolj, da poskrbi sam zase. Štiristo vas, nič več, ker jih lahko ustje v tem času varno sprejme največ toliko. Šestnajst dni ne bom mogla uporabljati rok in ne bom mogla oditi. Nikogar izmed vas ne bom odnesla v Orrel. Niti enega. O tem odloča Konkordat, človek za človekom, in Konkordat lahko reče ne.

Tessalon ni pričakovala, da bo imenovana v obljubi. Usedla se je nasproti trave.

07 · Čaj ali nekaj drugega

Orrellske ravnice · zadnji dan, mrak

Ustje se je ves tisti dvotedenski čas ožilo, kakor so napovedovali Drayevi stolpci in kakor Tessalonine tabele niso.
