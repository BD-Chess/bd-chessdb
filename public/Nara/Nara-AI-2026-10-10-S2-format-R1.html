In the Tarn valley the pear trees have burst into blossom three weeks before midwinter, and the first hard frost is coming to kill them. The growers blame the glasshouses upriver; the glasshouses blame the weather. Illuminara asks which row opened first, and on which night.

01 · Blossom in the Dark

Hallam orchard and cooperative packing hall · 4 December, from 06:00

At six in the morning on the fourth of December, Aurel Brack walked the top terrace of the Hallam orchard with a hurricane lamp he did not need and found the pear trees in full flower, where six days earlier one row had been. The blossom was white in the lamplight and smelled of April. He was eighty-one and had walked that terrace on sixty-three Decembers, and on none of them had it smelled of anything but frost and wet bark. He put his bare hand under a branch and held it there until the petals touched his knuckles, as if they might be a mistake that would withdraw.

The frost would come down the valley within ten days, and a pear that flowers in December has nothing to fruit with in September. The Tarn valley grew a third of the continent's winter pears. A hundred and eighty thousand people lived on what its trees did between now and the harvest, and three hundred of the cooperative's fourteen hundred hectares had opened in six days.

In the cooperative's packing hall that afternoon, Inge Valdis, who chaired it, spread out a map with the blossoming rows shaded pink. It was a bad map and she had made it carefully. The pink ran thickest along the river road toward the glasshouse district, where the new growers lit their cucumbers and tomatoes through the dark half of the year.

IngeThey came four years ago. Last winter a few rows opened early. This winter it is everything. I am not a woman who needs a third data point.

Tomasz Rook, who spoke for the glasshouses, had brought a map of his own.

TomaszMy lamps point down. They are shielded to the standard, and the heat from the vents is a fifth of a degree at the fence line. I measured it.

He laid a second sheet over the first.

TomaszThis is every weather station in the valley. It was the warmest November on record from the bridge to the pass. Your trees are not confused by my glasshouses. They are confused by the year.

His district employed nine thousand people, which was most of the valley that did not grow pears. Neither of them was lying. That was the trouble: each held a number that cleared itself.

Illuminara had come because Inge wrote to say that the valley had two stories and both were true to the person telling them. Wren Odira had come to keep the clock, since the buds on the north terraces, the ones that had not yet opened, were next year's crop and the frost would not wait for an argument.

Illuminara read both maps without hurry. Then she asked the question neither of them had prepared for.

IlluminaraWhich row opened first, and on which night?

IngeWe did not write it down. There were three hundred hectares. We were counting what we had lost.

From a stool by the stove, Aurel Brack said.

AurelI wrote it down.

02 · What the Heat Cannot Explain

Cooperative packing hall · time unstated

He had kept a pencil almanac for fifty years, weather and bloom and pickings, in a ledger held shut with a rubber band. The page for the last week of November was crowded. Illuminara copied his dates onto the cooperative's map with Nessa Pell, Inge's foreman, row by row, while Wren read them aloud.

The first row to open was not near the glasshouses. It was on the north slope of Hallam, nine kilometres from the nearest lamp, on the coldest ground in the valley, on the night of the twenty-eighth.

IlluminaraIf heat did this, the warmest ground would open first. The first row to open was the coldest.

Tomasz said nothing, with some satisfaction, until she turned the same finger on him.

IlluminaraAnd the rows along your own fence line, a hundred metres from your lamps, are still shut.

IngeThat is because.

Inge began, and stopped.

IngeI do not know why.

IlluminaraCold is the other half of the answer. A pear does not open because the weather is kind. It opens after it has had its winter. Do you log the chill?

Nessa did, because the insurer demanded it: hours below seven degrees, terrace by terrace, since the first of October. She brought the book. The north slope of Hallam, the coldest ground, had crossed the cooperative's threshold on the fourteenth of November. The river terraces had crossed on the twenty-sixth. The rows along Tomasz's fence, low and sheltered and warm, were still forty hours short.

NessaThe cold ground finishes its winter first. That is why the north slope was ready. And the fence rows are not ready yet, whatever is shining on them.

IlluminaraThen the trees that opened were primed, and the warm November did nothing to wake them. Ready is not the same as woken. What woke them?

She laid the airfield's hourly sheet beside the ledger. The blossom had not crept across the valley. It had come in steps, three of them, on the nights of the twenty-eighth, the first and the third. November had been warm every night, and only those three nights had opened anything.

IlluminaraWhat did they share?

NessaFog.

IlluminaraNot fog. Low cloud. The airfield reports the base under six hundred metres on all three.

She ran her finger down the warmest nights of the month, which were clear, and the blossom column beside them was empty.

Then Nessa said it, because she had been frowning at the map for ten minutes.

NessaThe walnut avenue. It runs through the middle of the pink and there is not a flower on it. And the rows under the black hail net, the same.

Under net the nights were a degree warmer if anything.

NessaWhat the net and the walnuts have in common.

Nessa said slowly.

Nessais that they take the sky away.

Illuminara worked it through aloud, so that the room could take it apart as she went. Heat did not move in steps. Heat did not skip the walnut rows, or start on the coldest hill. What arrived on a low-cloud night and not on a clear one was light: the town's glow and the glasshouse district's spill, thrown back from the cloud ceiling onto every open field.

IlluminaraI would not say light wakes a pear. I would say a bud that has finished its winter is waiting for a sign that the days are lengthening, and reads that sign at dusk. It does not carry a clock. It takes the colour of the evening sky, and the sky just after sunset has a colour of its own, a good deal more far-red than red.

She turned to Tomasz.

IlluminaraWhat do your lamps make above the roof?

TomaszNothing. Three percent spill, inside the standard.

IlluminaraThree percent of a very large number, thrown at a low cloud, comes back as an evening that does not end. What colour are they?

He told her, because it was on the data sheet. Red, and a far-red channel the supplier sold to lengthen cucumber stems, switched on four winters ago and doubled last year.

IlluminaraThen your glasshouses make the best dusk in the valley.

TomaszThat is a story built on a coincidence of cloud.

Tomasz said. He did not say it unkindly. He said it like a man putting his weight on a plank he meant to test.

IlluminaraYes. So I would not ask anyone to rely on me.

It was Aurel who turned back through the ledger, a long way back, and put his thumb on a page gone brown.

AurelNineteen eighty-seven. The cement works flared off its waste gas for ten nights in the last week of November, under cloud, and the whole sky went the colour of weak tea. The lower rows opened. I wrote, sky pink after dark, trees will lie. My father had a saying for it.

Illuminara did not answer at once.

IlluminaraThat is a second instrument. It has no glasshouse in it, and it was written thirty-nine years before anyone asked. I did not have it and I needed it.

AurelYou didn't ask me for proof. You asked me what the sky looked like.

03 · Twigs in a Packing Hall

Packing hall and cold store · two nights and the fourth test day

She would not let the room agree with her. A guess that explains everything is one nobody has tried to break yet, she said, and she wrote it down as two guesses. A: any extra light on a winter night wakes a ready bud. B: only the colour of the evening does. Then she wrote what each would predict, and the card ran to a third line she added last: twigs from a row that has not finished its winter will stay shut under any light at all. If A were right, a dim red light as strong in photons as a dim far-red one would wake the buds just as well, and brightness was the lever. If B were right, only the far-red boxes would swell, and a bright red would do nothing. She folded the card and gave it to Wren.

Inge chose the test. She picked row fourteen on the Hallam terrace, the row she had been calling the glasshouses' fault for a month.

IngeIf this is a trick, I want it to be mine.

Nessa's crew cut forty twigs from the same tree and ten more from a fence-line row that was still short of its chill, and stood them in water in the cold store. They built five light boxes from what was on the shelves: dark; bright red; dim red, set to the same photon count as the dim far-red box with Tomasz's light meter; dim far-red; and bright red with a little far-red. Every box sat at twelve degrees on Nessa's loggers, within half a degree, and a probe in each stayed in until the end. Nessa put the labels in her pocket, so that no one in the room knew which box was which.

Before the lights went on, Wren measured the length of every bud with calipers and wrote it down. After two long nights under the lights every twig went to the same warm bench under the same grey lamp, so that nothing but those two nights' light could differ. Each morning she measured the buds again, tip by tip, blind.

Illuminara had put her first guess on A, and it was wrong. By the fourth day the dim far-red box had swelled, its buds fattening and greening at the tips, under a glow no brighter than a streetlamp seen through a hedge. The bright red box, ten times as bright, sat as tight as the dark one. So did the dim red box, with the same count of photons as the far-red one.

IlluminaraI was wrong about the lumen. And about the photons. I put A first.

Wren unfolded the card.

WrenYou put B second. And you wrote that the fence-line twigs would stay shut.

IlluminaraIt was a poor second.

Nessa took the labels out of her pocket. The swollen box was the far-red one. The bright box with a little far-red had swelled slightly, in proportion to its far-red and not to its brightness. The ten twigs from the fence line had stayed tight in all five boxes, even the far-red one. Nobody spoke for a time. Colour was the last switch, and only on a bud that had finished its winter. Tomasz Rook looked at the twigs for longer than anyone, and then he went outside and looked at the sky, which was low and grey and very slightly pink over his own roofs.

04 · The Channel

Cooperative packing hall · after the light-box test

TomaszYou want me to put fourteen hundred night-shift people on short hours on the strength of twigs.

he said, when he came back in.

IlluminaraI want you to switch off one channel of light, on some nights. Your red does nothing to the trees. Only the far-red, and only on nights when the cloud is low enough to throw it back. Aurel's ledger and the airfield sheets put that at about nine nights a winter.

TomaszNine nights without the channel costs the cucumbers six percent of their stem growth in the worst weeks. The supplier's controller cannot read the airfield. And then in spring, when the cloud is low and the trees are right to bloom, I am switching off for nothing.

He pulled out a chair and sat in it.

TomaszBut you are asking the wrong sensor. I bought a cloud camera for the solar panels, and every yard has a feed from it. Ceiling under eight hundred metres, channel off, and no airfield. I can wire that by tomorrow's dusk, and in spring I can narrow it when I know more.

IlluminaraThat is better than what I proposed.

TomaszIt had better be, it is my money.

He turned to Inge.

TomaszAnd you will say tested and cleared, not found guilty. The valley found this. I will not carry nine thousand people on a headline.

Inge looked at her map for a long moment.

IngeThe valley found it. And the three hundred hectares that opened are written off. I will not claim them against you, because if the frost takes them anyway it was never going to be your fault in a court. But I will put my spectrometers on your fence line, and you will let my people read them.

TomaszDone.

Illuminara asked for one more thing, and it was the one that could embarrass her.

IlluminaraOn the first cloudy night, leave the channel on in six yards nearest the Hallam road, each paired with a dark yard at the same height within two hundred metres. Tag a branch on row fourteen opposite every one, twelve in all, and have your people measure each tagged bud with calipers at dusk, before the first night. Your spectrometers read the colour and the temperature at each tag. The twigs showed their change by the fourth day, so we keep the same clock: I will write the prediction down tonight, and we read the tags on the fourth morning. The lit side at more than twice the far-red share of its dark neighbour, the two within half a degree, buds swollen against their baseline on all six lit and on none of the dark. If it is slope, or cloud in general, the pairs will match each other and I am wrong. Your people measure the tags without being told which yards were lit.

For the first time that week, Tomasz laughed.

TomaszYou want me to hurt your own trees to prove you right. I will keep the list of the six in my pocket until the tags are measured.

IngeThey are already lost. I would rather know.

05 · The Fourth Morning

Hallam and the Tarn valley · December, then late March

The ceiling came down to four hundred and eighty metres at dusk on the tenth and stayed low through the next night. Nessa's crew had measured the twelve tagged buds before the lights, and the lengths were in the tag book. Nineteen yards switched off the far-red channel on the cloud camera's say-so, and six kept it on, and the glasshouse district glowed a little less pink over the Tarn than it had for four winters. At dusk Inge's spectrometers read the lit stations at nearly three times the far-red share of their dark neighbours, and the temperatures within two-tenths of a degree. Nessa wrote the numbers in the tag book and did not say what she thought of them.

On the morning of the thirteenth, the fourth day, Aurel walked the terrace at first light with his lamp, and Nessa's crew walked behind him and measured the twelve tags blind against the dusk baseline. The buds opposite the dark yards had not moved. They were tight and brown and frost-hard in the lamplight. The six on the other side had swollen, fat and green at the tips, and by how much followed each tag's spectrometer reading, station by station. The slope and the cloud did not matter.

Tomasz took the list from his pocket and read it out. The six were the six.

Aurel looked for a long time at a swollen bud, green at its tip in the lamplight.

AurelMy father said it would take a witch to tell it. I had always thought he meant the cement works.

The frost came on the fourteenth and took everything that had opened, three hundred hectares of white that went to black in an afternoon, and the cooperative wrote off the year on those rows without arguing about whose it was. The six lit branches on row fourteen went the way of the rest; the dark six, tight and brown, came through. Inge stood in the packing hall that evening with Nessa's chill book open on the table.

IngeEleven hundred hectares are still shut. Another four cloudy nights at the rate of the last three would have opened most of them, and the January frost would have taken what opened. They will hold. Thirty-eight thousand tonnes if the spring is kind. The northern contract is thirty-two. We keep it, we keep both packing halls open through next winter, and nine hundred of my people keep their jobs, which was the thing I was afraid of on the fourth of December.

She did not say what the lost three hundred hectares were worth, and nobody asked her to.

Tomasz's cucumbers lost six percent of their stem growth in the weeks the camera held the channel off, and he put in for roof screens that would shut the leak for good, which would take a year and cost him more than he had ever agreed to spend on a sky.

In the last week of March the Hallam terrace flowered, in the right week, under a clear dark sky, with no one to see it but Aurel Brack and his lamp. He wrote the date in pencil in the ledger. Underneath it he wrote a new line, in the cramped hand he used for things that were meant to last: Sky pink after dark. Ring Tomasz.

V dolini Tarn so hruške zacvetele tri tedne pred sredino zime, prva huda slana pa jih bo pokončala. Sadjarji krivijo rastlinjake ob zgornjem toku reke, rastlinjaki krivijo vreme. Illuminara vpraša, katera vrsta se je odprla prva in katero noč.

01 · Cvetenje v temi

Sadovnjak Hallam in pakirnica zadruge · 4. december, od 6.00

Ob šestih zjutraj četrtega decembra je Aurel Brack hodil po zgornji terasi sadovnjaka Hallam z viharnico, ki je ni potreboval, in našel hruške v polnem cvetju. Še pred šestimi dnevi je cvetela samo ena vrsta. Cvetovi so bili v svetlobi svetilke beli in dišali so po aprilu. Star je bil enainosemdeset let in po tej terasi je hodil že triinšestdeset decembrov, a nobenega ni dišalo po ničemer drugem kot po slani in mokrem lubju. Golo dlan je podržal pod vejo in čakal, da so se cvetni listi dotaknili njegovih členkov, kakor da bi bili napaka, ki se bo še premislila.

V desetih dneh naj bi se po dolini spustila slana, hruška, ki cveti decembra, pa septembra nima s čim roditi. Dolina Tarn je pridelala tretjino zimskih hrušk na celini. Od tega, kaj bodo njena drevesa naredila med zdaj in trgatvijo, je živelo sto osemdeset tisoč ljudi. V šestih dneh je zacvetelo tristo od tisoč štiristo hektarjev zadružnih sadovnjakov.

Tisto popoldne je Inge Valdis, predsednica zadruge, v pakirnici razgrnila zemljevid, na katerem je cvetoče vrste obarvala rožnato. Bil je slab zemljevid in izdelala ga je skrbno. Rožnata se je najgosteje raztezala ob rečni cesti proti območju rastlinjakov, kjer so novi pridelovalci v temni polovici leta z lučmi osvetljevali kumare in paradižnike.

IngePrišli so pred štirimi leti. Prejšnjo zimo je zgodaj zacvetelo nekaj vrst. Letos cveti vse. Nisem ženska, ki potrebuje še tretjo podatkovno točko.

Tomasz Rook, predstavnik rastlinjakov, je prinesel svoj zemljevid.

TomaszMoje luči svetijo navzdol. Zasenčene so skladno s standardom in toplota iz prezračevalnikov na meji posestva dvigne temperaturo za petino stopinje. Izmeril sem.

Čez prvi list je položil drugega.

TomaszTu je vsaka vremenska postaja v dolini. Od mostu do prelaza je bil to najtoplejši november v zgodovini meritev. Vaših dreves ne begajo moji rastlinjaki. Bega jih leto.

Njegovo območje je zaposlovalo devet tisoč ljudi, torej večino doline, ki ni pridelovala hrušk. Nihče od njiju ni lagal. Prav to je bila težava: vsak je imel podatek, ki je njega samega opravičeval.

Illuminara je prišla, ker ji je Inge pisala, da dolina premore dve zgodbi in da se obe zdita resnični tistemu, ki ju pripoveduje. Wren Odira je prišla spremljat čas, saj so bili popki na severnih terasah, tisti, ki se še niso odprli, pridelek prihodnjega leta, slana pa ni nameravala čakati, da se prepir konča.

Illuminara si je brez naglice ogledala oba zemljevida. Nato je postavila vprašanje, na katero se ni pripravil nihče.

IlluminaraKatera vrsta je zacvetela prva in katero noč?

IngeTega nismo zapisovali. Tristo hektarjev jih je bilo. Šteli smo, koliko smo izgubili.

S pručke ob peči se je oglasil Aurel Brack.

AurelJaz sem zapisoval.

02 · Česar toplota ne more pojasniti

Pakirnica zadruge · čas ni naveden

Petdeset let je v zvezek s svinčnikom zapisoval vreme, cvetenje in obiranje. Zvezek je držala skupaj elastika. Stran za zadnji novembrski teden je bila popisana do roba. Illuminara je z Nesso Pell, Ingejino delovodkinjo, njegove datume prenašala na zadružni zemljevid, vrsto za vrsto, Wren pa jih je brala na glas.

Prva ni zacvetela vrsta blizu rastlinjakov. Rasla je na severnem pobočju Hallama, devet kilometrov od najbližje luči, na najhladnejših tleh v dolini, in odprla se je v noči na osemindvajseti november.

IlluminaraČe bi bila vzrok toplota, bi se najprej odprla najtoplejša tla. Prva se je odprla najhladnejša vrsta.

Tomasz je zadovoljno molčal, dokler se ni njen prst obrnil proti njemu.

IlluminaraVrste tik ob vaši ograji, sto metrov od svetilk, pa so še vedno zaprte.

IngeTo je zato, ker ...

je začela Inge in obmolknila.

IngeNe vem, zakaj.

IlluminaraMraz je druga polovica odgovora. Hruška se ne odpre samo zato, ker je vreme prijazno. Odpre se, ko je že doživela svojo zimo. Beležite ure mraza?

Nessa jih je beležila, ker je to zahtevala zavarovalnica: ure pod sedmimi stopinjami od prvega oktobra, terasa za teraso. Prinesla je knjigo. Severno pobočje Hallama, najhladnejša tla, je zadružni prag doseglo štirinajstega novembra. Rečne terase so ga dosegle šestindvajsetega. Vrste ob Tomaszevi ograji, nizke, zavetne in tople, so imele še štirideset ur premalo.

NessaMrzla tla svojo zimo opravijo prva. Zato je bilo severno pobočje pripravljeno. Vrste ob ograji pa še niso pripravljene, ne glede na to, kaj sveti nanje.

IlluminaraDrevesa, ki so se odprla, so bila torej pripravljena, topli november pa jih ni prebudil. Pripravljenost ni isto kot prebujenje. Kaj jih je prebudilo?

K evidenci je položila urni izpis letališča. Cvetenje se ni plazilo čez dolino. Prišlo je v treh skokih, v nočeh osemindvajsetega, prvega in tretjega. November je bil topel vsako noč, a samo te tri so kaj odprle.

IlluminaraKaj jim je bilo skupno?

NessaMegla.

IlluminaraNe megla. Nizka oblačnost. Letališče je vse tri noči zabeležilo spodnjo mejo oblakov pod šeststo metri.

S prstom je sledila najtoplejšim nočem meseca. Te so bile jasne, v stolpcu cvetenja pa ni bilo ničesar.

Nessa se je tedaj oglasila, ker je že deset minut mrščila čelo nad zemljevidom.

NessaOrehov drevored. Poteka skozi sredino rožnatega območja in na njem ni niti enega cveta. Enako je z vrstami pod črno mrežo proti toči.

Pod mrežo so bile noči, če sploh kaj, stopinjo toplejše.

NessaMreža in orehi imajo skupno to.

je počasi rekla Nessa.

Nessada drevesom zakrivajo nebo.

Illuminara je razmišljala na glas, da so ji lahko drugi ugovarjali sproti. Toplota ne prihaja v skokih. Toplota ne preskoči orehovih vrst in ne začne na najhladnejšem griču. V nizko oblačni noči, ne pa v jasni, je prišla svetloba: sij mesta in razpršena svetloba rastlinjakov, ki jo je oblačna plast odbijala nazaj na odprta polja.

IlluminaraNe bi rekla, da svetloba sama prebudi hruško. Rekla bi, da popek, ki je že opravil svojo zimo, čaka na znamenje, da se dnevi daljšajo, in ga bere v barvi neba ob mraku. Ure nima. Zanaša se na barvo večernega neba, nebo takoj po sončnem zahodu pa ima svojo barvo, mnogo bogatejšo z daljno rdečo kot z rdečo svetlobo.

Obrnila se je k Tomaszu.

IlluminaraKaj vaše svetilke pošiljajo nad strehe?

TomaszNičesar. Trije odstotki razpršene svetlobe, še znotraj standarda.

IlluminaraTrije odstotki zelo velike količine, odbiti od nizkega oblaka, postanejo večer, ki se noče končati. Katere barve je svetloba?

Povedal ji je, saj je pisalo na tehničnem listu. Rdeča in kanal daljne rdeče svetlobe, ki ga je dobavitelj prodajal za podaljševanje kumaričnih stebel. Vključili so ga pred štirimi zimami, lani pa mu podvojili moč.

IlluminaraPotem vaši rastlinjaki ustvarjajo najboljši mrak v dolini.

TomaszTo je zgodba, zgrajena na naključnem oblaku.

je rekel Tomasz. Ni bil neprijazen. Govoril je kakor človek, ki prenese težo na desko, da bi preveril, ali zdrži.

IlluminaraDa. Zato nikogar ne bi prosila, naj mi verjame na besedo.

Aurel je spet listal po svojem zvezku, daleč nazaj, in s palcem obstal na porjaveli strani.

AurelLeta devetnajststo sedeminosemdeset. Cementarna je konec novembra deset noči pod oblaki sežigala odpadni plin in vse nebo je imelo barvo šibkega čaja. Spodnje vrste so se odprle. Zapisal sem: nebo ponoči rožnato, drevesa bodo lagala. Oče je imel rek za to.

Illuminara ni odgovorila takoj.

IlluminaraTo je drugi instrument. Nima nič opraviti z rastlinjaki, zapis pa je nastal devetintrideset let, preden je kdo postavil vprašanje. Nisem ga imela in potrebovala sem ga.

AurelNiste me prosili za dokaz. Vprašali ste, kakšno je bilo nebo.

03 · Vejice v pakirnici

Pakirnica in hladilnica · dve noči in četrti dan preizkusa

Ni dovolila, da bi se vsi v prostoru preprosto strinjali z njo. Ugibanje, ki pojasni vse, je tisto, ki ga še nihče ni poskušal ovreči, je rekla, in na kartico zapisala dve domnevi. A: vsakršna dodatna svetloba v zimski noči prebudi že pripravljen popek. B: to stori samo barva večera. Dodala je še tretjo vrstico: vejice z dreves, ki zime še niso končala, bodo ostale zaprte pri vsaki svetlobi.
