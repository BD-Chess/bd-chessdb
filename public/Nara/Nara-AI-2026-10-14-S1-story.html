01 · Eighty-nine

Kessel Reach tribunal, hearing room two · Day One, 08:10

The Compact of the Seven Springs ran to four pages, and the whole of it turned on a single word of the Irú language. Clause Four gave Ridge Nine oruma to Harrow & Vane Minerals for the winning of ore, the springs to be rendered whole. The company read the word as lease. The people of the Irú valley read it as lend. Between the two readings lay a ridge, the aquifer under the ridge, and seven thousand people who drank from it.

The tribunal had nine days; the company’s interim licence to blast expired at 08:00 on the tenth. No dictionary could help. The last Irú school had burned with its books, and the last person who spoke the language with the work still in it sat in the front row under a grey blanket. Ilo Marrow was eighty-nine. He had been asked to testify six times and had refused six times.

Pell Ardent, the court’s translator, stood by the bench with a folder of field recordings and the look of a woman who had not slept.

Pell ArdentCounsel for the company telephoned me at home last night. She said a reading of lease would be very much in keeping with the recordings. I said I would read what they said. She said she was sure I would.

IlluminaraDid you hear a threat?

Pell ArdentI heard someone checking how much weight I could carry. If I am the only voice, I will be the weight.

She knew what the bench wanted from her, and it was a second voice. One speaker could be doubted. Two would be a record. There was no second.

She went down the aisle and knelt by Ilo’s chair so that he would not have to look up.

IlluminaraI can interpret for you. I have done it in courts. I would not change a word.

Ilo MarrowYou are kind, and you would make me a quotation. I would speak, you would speak, and the court would keep yours.

IlluminaraThen I will not ask what the word means.

His eyebrows rose a finger’s width.

IlluminaraI will ask what a person does after hearing it.

Ilo looked at his hands for a long time, the hands of a man who had lent a great many things.

Ilo MarrowThat is a question for a seed. I will speak to objects, girl. I will not speak to you about them.

Wren Odira, who kept the court’s clock because nobody else would, wrote 08:41 in her notebook and beside it, objects only. Under it she ruled a line and wrote 216 h from 08:00.

02 · What a Person Does Next

Three hamlets of the Irú valley · Days Two and Three

The language was nearly gone, but the practice had not died with it. Illuminara went to three people who lived a day’s walk apart, had never compared notes, and spoke only the common tongue: Teodora Brisk at Upper Haddow, whose cress and barley trays went from house to house each spring; Hamid Sorel, warden of the reservoir boats; and Anke Doyle, who lent the names of dead grandmothers to newborns and took them back.

She asked each the same question in the same words. What does a person do after you say oruma?

Teodora BriskYou take the measure in March. In May you bring it back with a handful over. And you say what failed.

Hamid SorelThe boat goes out leaking. It comes home caulked, with your name cut in the new plank. Nobody hands back a boat the way they found it. That is how I know they used it.

Anke DoyleThe name comes back with a line on the board. What the child did with it.

Use, change, account. Illuminara set the shape aside as a hypothesis. These three had described the practice her columns were drawn from, so they could not also be the people who proved the columns right.

At Hamid’s desk she stopped him turning a page. His boat-book, Exhibit Fourteen, had been logged as a repair ledger and nobody had read it for a verb. The oldest entry was 1727, a year after the Compact.

Skiff, oruma to Aldous Sorel for the fishing, the plank to be cut and the boat to come home. Called home in the flood of 1731. It came.

She laid Clause Four beside it. The same frame stood in both: the word, a holder, a use, and a clause saying what must happen at the end. The ferry grant Ines Calder had put in evidence herself, oruma to the Crowes for the crossing, the log to be borne up each winter, carried it too. It had never been called back in two hundred and twelve years.

Wren OdiraIt fits the company’s second reading if you squint. Keep the thing, send a token. You told me to say when something fits.

IlluminaraIt fits mine if you do not squint. That does not make it proof. The frame shows the word came with an ending-clause. It does not show that the clause was a debt, or that anyone could call it early. For that we need a case nobody on my side has built.

Hamid SorelThe Crowes carry the ferry log up to the council hall every winter. That is what coming back looks like when the thing is a river.

03 · Three Columns

Tribunal annex · Day Four, 07:30

Illuminara spread one sheet across the annex table and drew two columns. LEND: use, then the thing returned changed, with an account, and the lender free to call it back. GRANT: the thing kept, a token sent home each year. She left the third column blank and turned the sheet to face Ines Calder.

IlluminaraWrite your best reading. Not the one that is easy to beat. The one a careful lawyer would defend in her sleep.

Calder took the pen. She did not write the lease Illuminara had expected.

Ines CalderA lease allows wear. The tenant maintains, at her own cost, and keeps a written account of the days she used it. The thing comes back sound, and the lessor does not take it back before the term runs, for any reason, because that is what a term is. The springs are rendered whole when the term ends, and the account says how.

She headed the column LEASE and underlined any reason.

IlluminaraYour column and mine agree on the seed, the skiff and the name-board. A tenant mends the seam and cuts a line on the board too.

Ines CalderThen your objects cannot tell us anything.

IlluminaraThey can tell us whether the keepers recognize their own rules. They cannot tell us where we part, and we part in one place. Can the lender call it home before the term, if she has to?

Calder was silent for a breath. Then she did what Illuminara had not asked, and chose the case.

Ines CalderA sluice paddle from the reservoir office, lent for the term of the hearing. Mid-term, the lender calls it back. Twice: once because the water is rising, once for no reason at all. And the judge is not one of your three keepers, who have heard your table. Brann Crowe. His family has held the ferry for two hundred years, and he has no reason to love you.

Maren Tolliver, the company’s surveyor, who had walked Ridge Nine for eleven years, agreed to do the handing back. She asked one thing.

Maren TolliverWho decides whether I did it right?

IlluminaraNot Ilo. If this rests on one old man, it is a quotation again. Each keeper decides for the thing they keep, and Mr Crowe the paddle. If a judge cannot decide, it counts as unresolved, and it does not count for me.

At 07:52 she wrote her predictions on a card and Wren signed across the flap. The lender’s protocol would close both recalls; the tenant’s would close neither. Beneath them she wrote the line she wanted Calder to see: If Mr Crowe closes the tenant’s hold against a flood, I withdraw the claim of recall, and the company may blast Ridge Nine and answer for the springs in a ledger at the end of the term. If he closes it only against a whim, I narrow the claim to the flood. Everything on the ridge hung on that card. A spring that was fractured in the third year could not be rendered whole in the tenth.

At 09:15 Tolliver took the objects. Wren logged the minute she sowed two trays of cress, ridge soil and valley soil. Forty-eight hours, said Teodora, was a cress season.

04 · The Surveyor’s Hands

Upper Haddow seed house · Day Six, 10:00

A linen screen divided the seed house, with a slot cut low in it that showed only hands and the thing in them. On one side sat Teodora, Hamid and Anke at separate tables, each with a pencil and a card marked CLOSED and NOT CLOSED, and at a fourth table Brann Crowe, lean and unsmiling. On the other stood Maren Tolliver with her sleeves rolled, and Ilo Marrow beside a measure of seed, a skiff and a pine name-board scored with nine names. Forty-eight hours and forty-five minutes had passed since the sowing.

Each time, Ilo spoke a short phrase to the object in Irú, not to the woman, and Illuminara wrote down only the time. Tolliver did what the sheet in her hand said, and she did not know which protocol came next.

Under the tenant’s protocol the seed came back sound, measure for measure, and Teodora marked it CLOSED. Under the lender’s it came back with a handful over and a germination page, the ridge-soil tray grey with rot beside the green valley tray, and Teodora marked that CLOSED too. Under grant Tolliver kept the measure and sent home one seed, and Teodora did not turn her card at all. The skiff went the same way, and for the grant one nail that Hamid pushed back through the slot with a forefinger. At the name-board Tolliver cut a line of account into the pine, and Ilo spoke to the board for longer than the formula needed, quietly, in a voice that was not for the room. Illuminara wrote untranslated, Ilo to the board and nothing else.

Wren OdiraWhat did he say?

IlluminaraA man spoke to a board. I will not make it into something he said to me.

Anke wrote a question mark beside the grant. That was the one call the keepers could not make, and Illuminara entered it as unresolved.

At 11:30 the usher carried a notice through the slot, in Hamid’s hand. The water is rising. The gate needs its paddle. Under the lender’s protocol Tolliver returned the paddle with her log of days used, and Brann Crowe, who had said nothing since he sat down, marked it CLOSED. Under the tenant’s she said the term ran to Day Ten and offered the fee for the lost days. Crowe looked at the paddle a long moment and marked it NOT CLOSED.

At 12:10 the second notice came through. The lender wants it back. Nothing more. Tolliver returned the paddle at once, her hands slow with it. Crowe marked it NOT CLOSED. Under the tenant’s protocol she held the paddle to the term, and he marked it CLOSED.

Wren opened the envelope at one o’clock. Two of Illuminara’s four recall marks were wrong.

Ines CalderSo it is a lease. A lease holds against a whim.

Brann CroweIt holds against a whim. It does not hold against a flood. My people never called the ferry because it never stopped coming home with the log. If anyone had called it for no reason, I would have told them where to put the log.

Illuminara struck free to call it back from her own column and wrote above it, for need. She entered the miss as a miss. She had predicted that the lender could call the thing home. The case had said: for need, not for pleasure.

Maren TolliverMy hands found a difference, and not the one I expected. Holding the paddle to the term, I was doing business. Putting it down when the water rose, I was paying a debt I had not known I had. Held through a term, or returned for a need somebody shows you. That is all my hands could tell apart.

Ines CalderYou were asked to perform a word you do not speak.

Maren TolliverI was asked what a decent person does with a borrowed thing. Four people who have never met told me, and one of them was your witness. I did not need Irú for that.

05 · The Dry Shoulder

Kessel Reach tribunal, hearing room two · Day Nine, 15:20

On Day Seven the council had offered the company the Dry Shoulder, a bare saddle of rock south of the ridge, with ore a third thinner, lent on the same word and the same terms of account. Tolliver’s own 2019 cores from the saddle were already Exhibit Nine, and Calder had confirmed the grade by eight that night. Had she not, Wren’s notebook said, the tribunal would have ruled on the ridge alone at noon on Day Eight.

Judge Anselm Rhee read for forty minutes. He said a demonstration was not a dictionary, and that Ilo Marrow’s sentence to the board would stay in the record untranslated. He took the matter in three parts and said which was which. On the word, the 1727 boat-book, the 1794 ferry grant and Clause Four shared one frame: continuity was proved. On the obligation, the keepers’ practice and Mr Crowe’s marks showed a debt of account and a return changed by use. On recall, the single 1731 entry and Mr Crowe’s marks carried it for need and not for whim, a judgment under uncertainty, which the tribunal held.

Then he said what the holding was for. A lease without recall let Harrow & Vane blast through its term and answer for the springs in a ledger at the end, when Clause Four’s promise to render them whole would fall due on a fractured aquifer no ledger could mend. A loan recallable for need let the valley call the springs home while they could still be returned, and the lower spring had fallen to a third of its winter flow. The springs had called. Had Mr Crowe closed the tenant’s hold against the flood, the tribunal would have ordered the account alone, and the company would have blasted. Harrow & Vane’s claim to Ridge Nine reverted to the valley.

Ines CalderI have advised my client not to appeal. Our own surveyor’s hands are in the record, and I wrote our own column. We ask only that the account be annual and that recall for need be written out, so that no one on either side has to guess what the word means in a bad year.

At the Dry Shoulder four hundred mining jobs would never exist. Oren Basque, the council speaker, who had wanted the company gone from the whole ridge, signed with a hand that shook. The Vesper and Hale families lost the summer pasture on the saddle and left without a word.

The tribunal’s last order directed the company to fund a school. Ilo Marrow answered it from his chair, in the common tongue, before the judge had finished.

Ilo MarrowI will not be paid for it. I will lend it. Nine people, and each of them takes one thing, a seed or a boat or a name, and brings it back changed.

Pell Ardent read the order into the record without a folder. Maren Tolliver asked, from the back of the hall, whether she might be counted among the nine, and nobody laughed.

At 16:04, on the courthouse steps, Ilo put a measure of seed into the hands of the youngest of them, a girl of seven called Pia, and said the word to her, not to the seed. Pia answered in Irú, loudly and wrongly, and the old man laughed until he coughed.

Wren OdiraThat is a second speaker.

IlluminaraThat is a first one. He needs nine more, and then they need each other.

Wren wrote the time. The word had had one speaker for a long season, and by spring it would have ten.

01 · Devetinosemdeset

Sodišče Kessel Reach, razpravna dvorana dve · Prvi dan, 08:10

Dogovor o sedmih izvirih je obsegal štiri strani, ves pa je bil odvisen od ene same besede v jeziku Irú. Četrti člen je družbi Harrow & Vane Minerals dal Greben devet oruma za pridobivanje rude, izvire pa je bilo treba vrniti cele. Družba je besedo razumela kot najem. Ljudje iz doline Irú so jo razumeli kot posojo. Med obema razlagama so ležali greben, vodonosnik pod njim in sedem tisoč ljudi, ki so iz njega pili.

Sodišče je imelo devet dni; začasno dovoljenje družbe za razstreljevanje je poteklo ob 08:00 desetega dne. Noben slovar ni mogel pomagati. Zadnja šola Irú je zgorela skupaj s knjigami, zadnji človek, ki je govoril jezik z živim izročilom, pa je sedel v prvi vrsti pod sivo odejo. Ilo Marrow je imel devetinosemdeset let. Šestkrat so ga prosili, naj priča, in šestkrat je zavrnil.

Pell Ardent, sodna prevajalka, je stala ob sodni klopi z mapo terenskih posnetkov in videzom ženske, ki ni spala.

Pell ArdentOdvetnica družbe me je sinoči poklicala domov. Rekla je, da bi bila razlaga kot najem zelo skladna s posnetki. Rekla sem, da bom prebrala, kaj pravijo. Rekla je, da je prepričana, da bom.

IlluminaraSte slišali grožnjo?

Pell ArdentSlišala sem nekoga, ki je preverjal, koliko teže lahko nosim. Če sem edini glas, bom jaz ta teža.

Vedela je, kaj sodni senat hoče od nje, in to je bil drugi glas. V enega govorca je bilo mogoče podvomiti. Dva bi pomenila zapis. Drugega ni bilo.

Stopila je po hodniku in pokleknila ob Ilov stol, da mu ne bi bilo treba gledati navzgor.

IlluminaraLahko prevajam za vas. To sem že počela na sodiščih. Ne bi spremenila niti besede.

Ilo MarrowPrijazni ste in iz mene bi naredili navedek. Jaz bi spregovoril, vi bi spregovorili, sodišče pa bi ohranilo vaše besede.

IlluminaraPotem vas ne bom vprašala, kaj beseda pomeni.

Njegove obrvi so se dvignile za širino prsta.

IlluminaraVprašala bom, kaj človek naredi, potem ko jo sliši.

Ilo je dolgo gledal svoje roke, roke človeka, ki je posodil že zelo veliko stvari.

Ilo MarrowTo je vprašanje za seme. Govoril bom predmetom, dekle. O njih pa ne bom govoril vam.

Wren Odira, ki je skrbela za sodno uro, ker je ni hotel nihče drug, je v zvezek zapisala 08:41 in zraven samo predmeti. Pod tem je potegnila črto in zapisala 216 ur od 08:00.

02 · Kaj človek naredi zatem

Trije zaselki doline Irú · Drugi in tretji dan

Jezik je bil skoraj izginil, vendar praksa ni umrla z njim. Illuminara je obiskala tri ljudi, ki so živeli dan hoda narazen, nikoli niso primerjali zapiskov in so govorili le skupni jezik: Teodoro Brisk v Upper Haddowu, katere pladnji kreše in ječmena so vsako pomlad potovali od hiše do hiše; Hamida Sorela, čuvaja čolnov na zbiralniku; in Anke Doyle, ki je novorojenčkom posojala imena umrlih babic in jih jemala nazaj.

Vsakemu je z istimi besedami postavila isto vprašanje. Kaj človek naredi, potem ko mu rečete oruma?

Teodora BriskMarca vzameš mero. Maja jo prineseš nazaj s prgiščem več. In poveš, kaj ni uspelo.

Hamid SorelČoln odide, ko pušča. Domov se vrne zatesnjen, z vašim imenom, vrezanim v novo desko. Nihče ne vrne čolna takšnega, kot ga je našel. Tako vem, da ga je uporabljal.

Anke DoyleIme se vrne s črto na tabli. S tem, kar je otrok naredil z njim.

Uporabi, spremeni, položi račun. Illuminara je vzorec odložila kot hipotezo. Ti trije so opisali prakso, iz katere so izhajali njeni stolpci, zato niso mogli biti tudi ljudje, ki bi dokazali, da imajo stolpci prav.

Pri Hamidovi pisalni mizi mu je preprečila, da bi obrnil stran. Njegova ladijska knjiga, dokazni predmet štirinajst, je bila vpisana kot knjiga popravil in nihče je ni bral zaradi glagola. Najstarejši vpis je bil iz leta 1727, leto po Dogovoru.

Čoln, oruma Aldousu Sorelu za ribolov, desko je treba izrezati in čoln mora priti domov. Poklican domov v poplavi leta 1731. Prišel je.

Ob vpis je položila četrti člen. V obeh je stal isti okvir: beseda, imetnik, raba in določilo o tem, kaj se mora zgoditi na koncu. Tudi dovoljenje za trajekt, ki ga je Ines Calder sama predložila kot dokaz, oruma družini Crowe za prevoz čez reko, poleno je treba vsako zimo prinesti gor, ga je vsebovalo. V dvesto dvanajstih letih ga niso nikoli poklicali nazaj.

Wren OdiraČe zamižiš, ustreza drugi razlagi družbe. Obdrži stvar, vsako leto pošlji znamenje. Rekli ste mi, naj povem, ko kaj ustreza.

IlluminaraČe ne zamižite, ustreza moji. To še ni dokaz. Okvir kaže, da je besedo spremljalo zaključno določilo. Ne kaže pa, ali je bilo določilo dolg in ali ga je bilo mogoče odpoklicati predčasno. Za to potrebujemo primer, ki ga ni sestavil nihče na moji strani.

Hamid SorelDružina Crowe vsako zimo prinese trajektno poleno v občinsko dvorano. Tako je videti vrnitev domov, kadar je stvar reka.

03 · Trije stolpci

Sodni prizidek · Četrti dan, 07:30

Illuminara je čez mizo v prizidku razgrnila en list in narisala dva stolpca. POSOJA: uporaba, nato vrnitev spremenjene stvari z obračunom in posojilodajalčevo pravico, da jo pokliče nazaj. PODELITEV: stvar se obdrži, vsako leto pa se domov pošlje znamenje. Tretji stolpec je pustila prazen in list obrnila proti Ines Calder.

IlluminaraZapišite svojo najboljšo razlago. Ne tiste, ki jo je lahko premagati. Tisto, ki bi jo skrben pravnik zagovarjal v spanju.

Calder je vzela pero. Ni zapisala najema, ki ga je Illuminara pričakovala.

Ines CalderNajem dovoljuje obrabo. Najemnik vzdržuje stvar na lastne stroške in vodi pisni obračun dni, ko jo je uporabljal. Stvar se vrne cela, najemodajalec pa je pred iztekom roka iz nobenega razloga ne vzame nazaj, ker je to bistvo roka. Izviri se vrnejo celi, ko rok poteče, obračun pa pove, kako.

Stolpec je naslovila NAJEM in podčrtala iz nobenega razloga.

IlluminaraVaš stolpec in moj se ujemata pri semenu, čolnu in tabli z imeni. Tudi najemnik popravi šiv in vreže črto na tablo.

Ines CalderPotem nam vaši predmeti ne morejo povedati ničesar.

IlluminaraPovedo nam lahko, ali skrbniki prepoznajo lastna pravila. Ne morejo nam povedati, kje se razidemo, razidemo pa se na enem mestu. Ali lahko posojilodajalec stvar pred iztekom roka pokliče domov, če jo potrebuje?

Calder je za hip molčala. Nato je naredila nekaj, česar Illuminara ni zahtevala, in izbrala primer.

Ines CalderZaporna loputa iz urada zbiralnika, posojena za čas obravnave. Sredi roka jo posojilodajalec pokliče nazaj. Dvakrat: enkrat, ker voda narašča, drugič brez kakršnegakoli razloga. In sodnik ne bo eden od vaših treh skrbnikov, ki so slišali vašo tabelo. Brann Crowe. Njegova družina ima trajekt že dvesto let in nima razloga, da bi vas imela rada.

Maren Tolliver, geodetka družbe, ki je enajst let prehodila Greben devet, je pristala, da bo vračala stvari. Vprašala je samo eno.

Maren TolliverKdo odloči, ali sem ravnala prav?

IlluminaraNe Ilo. Če bo to slonelo na enem starcu, bo spet samo navedek. Vsak skrbnik odloči za stvar, za katero skrbi, gospod Crowe pa za zaporno loputo. Če sodnik ne more odločiti, se primer šteje za nerešenega in meni ne koristi.

Ob 07:52 je svoje napovedi zapisala na kartico, Wren pa se je podpisala čez zavihek. Posojilni protokol bi zaključil oba odpoklica; najemniški ne bi zaključil nobenega. Pod njiju je zapisala vrstico, za katero je hotela, da jo Calder vidi: Če gospod Crowe najemniško zadržanje potrdi tudi ob poplavi, umaknem zahtevek glede odpoklica, družba pa lahko razstreli Greben devet in za izvire odgovarja v knjigi ob koncu roka. Če ga potrdi le ob muhi, zahtevek zožim na poplavo. Vse na grebenu je bilo odvisno od te kartice. Izvira, ki bi bil poškodovan v tretjem letu, v desetem ne bi bilo mogoče vrniti celega.

Ob 09:15 je Tolliver prevzela predmete. Wren je zapisala minuto, ko je posejala dva pladnja kreše, enega v prsti z grebena in drugega v prsti iz doline. Oseminštirideset ur, je rekla Teodora, je ena sezona kreše.

04 · Roke geodetke

Semenska hiša v Upper Haddowu · Šesti dan, 10:00
