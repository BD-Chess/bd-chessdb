01 · A Number That Holds Still

Harrow Vale, water-board office · Monday, 16:10 · fifty-six days before the storm season

The regional authority had sent her to audit the valley's flood model, and it was a good model. Illuminara had spent two days inside it the way a locksmith spends an afternoon inside a lock. Rainfall had a spread. Soil had a spread. Only one input sat perfectly still, and it sat above six thousand roofs.

IlluminaraLeft buttress, block thirty-one. Eleven years of seam readings, one a month. Mr Prycé, water does not sit that quietly in a hillside. Every other gauge shivers when it rains on the ridge. This one is deaf.

Dovan Prycé set a cup of tea at her elbow before he answered. He was sixty-one, neat and unhurried, a man who had spent his life being the quietest person in rooms where decisions were made.

Dovan PrycéThe gauge is old and the seam is dry. I'd call that a compliment to the grouting. Harrow is a dull dam, Ms Illuminara. Most of my work is writing satisfactory in a careful hand.

She had been a court interpreter once, and she still listened for sentences that had been rehearsed. This one had been sanded. Dull, he said, gently, like a man closing a door he had oiled himself.

IlluminaraI'm not accusing anyone. I'm asking what the dam would have to look like for that series to be the honest reading. I would like to go and see. Tonight, if you'll lend me the gallery key.

The pause was shorter than half a second. It was not fear. It was the sound of a man deciding which of two truths to offer first.

Dovan PrycéThe light is best at dusk. Take the blue key. I'll follow when I've locked up.

He did not look at the shelf behind his desk, where a bicycle wheel with one spoke missing hung from a nail. She noticed that he did not look.

02 · The Pencil on the Wall

Harrow Dam, left-buttress gallery, block 31 · the same evening, 19:40

Concrete is a record of weight. Illuminara laid her palm flat on the gallery wall and read it as a set of roads along which force travelled, and a few places where the roads disagreed. At block thirty-one one road forked that should not have forked. Load was slipping sideways through a seam no thicker than a pencil line, and under her hand the seam was not still.

Interpretation, she told herself. Not evidence. Test it.

The official crack gauge, a brass tell-tale glued across the seam, had been painted over, scale and all, in fresh municipal green. Beside it, at knee height, a ruler was pinned to the wall with a bicycle spoke bent into a pointer across the crack. Down the plaster ran a column of pencil ticks, each with a date in a small, careful hand. She read them from the bottom. For nine years they crept four-tenths of a millimetre a year. Then the last two winters, a millimetre and a half each. Then, at the top, three ticks in nine days.

Dovan PrycéYou found my spoke.

IlluminaraI found your pencil. It's the most honest record in this valley.

He sat down on a cable drum as though his legs had agreed to something before he had.

Dovan PrycéFour-tenths I could live beside. Every morning now I come down before the office opens to see whether the pointer has moved, and every morning I decide not to write what it says.

IlluminaraHow long, in your judgement?

Dovan PrycéA seam like this doesn't snap. It stops giving, and on the day it stops it has nothing left to give. Another wet autumn with the melt on top? I wouldn't bet the valley on the second. I wouldn't bet it on the first.

IlluminaraThen why the paint?

He laughed once, at himself.

Dovan PrycéEleven years ago I was the junior assessor. Old Ketteridge wrote a margin note on this block: requires attention. Aldermere read it as unsafe, because their covenant has a trigger and that was the word that pulled it. They called the loan in thirty days. The Works shut. Two thousand two hundred people left in one winter and I helped load the vans. The next certificate said satisfactory, with monitoring in my hand, and I have been monitoring ever since. Your honesty is paid for out of other people's houses. Show me a repair nobody has to lose a job for, or admit you will do what I did, later, with more witnesses.

It was a good objection, and Illuminara did not hurry past it. Behind him, in the dark of the gallery, water ticked in a drain, loud as a clock.

IlluminaraI don't have the repair. I have two facts, and neither is a verdict yet: the seam is moving, and for eleven years you were the only person who knew. So here is what I can promise, and where it stops. Tonight I report nothing. Nothing with your name on it leaves this gallery, and nothing about the dam goes to the lender or the town, until there is a plan with room for you in it. But I won't leave the seam to your pencil, or to mine. Sunniva Achterberg lays a fibre-optic cable across block thirty-one at first light, so that we argue with a measurement and not with me. I read force, Mr Prycé, and I can be wrong. And teach me the valley's money first.

Dovan PrycéThen give the promise an end, and I'll give you the number. Water on the downstream face of that block. Or fifteen hundredths of a millimetre across the crack in six hours. That is what I have been afraid of since the second winter. A seam that does that is not creeping any more.

IlluminaraThen that is the line, and you drew it, not I. Until the cable is live, the spoke is read on the hour, by you and by me in turn, each figure written under the last, and the same line holds for the spoke as for the cable. If the cable crosses it, or I see water on that face, the week is over that hour. I ring the regional emergency line, the evacuation plan opens, and you hear it from me first. What you tell the board about eleven years is yours to choose. Whether people are warned if the seam goes is not. Inside that line I am asking you for a week.

He took the bent spoke between two fingers and for a while looked only at his own handwriting.

Dovan PrycéA week. Come to the house. I've a great deal of paper.

03 · The Word and the Date

Prycé's kitchen, Harrow Vale · the same night, 01:20

The loan agreement lay open on the kitchen table, a stack as thick as a brick, eleven years of his pencil in the margins. He taught her the valley's money the way a man teaches a language he has had to speak alone. At clause fourteen point three his voice went flat.

Dovan PrycéDam Condition Event. If the licensing assessor certifies the dam Class D, unsafe, the lender may call the facility within thirty days. That is the whole trap. It doesn't matter what the repair costs. It matters that I sign the word.

A trap, Illuminara thought, is built to catch one particular animal. She followed the sentence to its end, where a subordinate clause lay in its own shade.

IlluminaraUnless, at the date of such certificate, a Remedial Programme approved by the Lender's Engineer is then in progress. Have you read that?

Dovan PrycéMany times. To have a programme approved, someone must disclose the defect to the lender. It's a locked room, and the key is the same word.

IlluminaraWho discloses? Not you. You are the assessor; you certify. The Borrower discloses. Read me fourteen point five.

He turned to the riders at the back and stopped at one he had not opened in years.

Dovan PrycéThe Borrower may give written notice of a suspected condition. The Credit Officer may grant a standstill of up to ninety days for the Lender's Engineer to review a remedial programme. No such notice is a certificate or an admission as to classification, and no default is waived.

He looked at the date on the rider. It was the winter after the Works shut.

Dovan PrycéAldermere wrote that door themselves, after the vans. It is addressed to the Borrower, and I have never been the Borrower, so I filed it under administrative.

IlluminaraThen the word was never the key, and it was never the lock. It's the date. Fourteen point three asks what is happening on the day you certify, not what was said before it. So the order matters more than the courage. Voss gives notice under fourteen point five. Halloran grants the standstill. Aldermere's engineer reads a cable and approves a programme. Only then do you certify, and not an hour before. Certify first, and the proviso never attaches.

Dovan PrycéAnd until then the seam keeps moving.

He turned pages. The Works Director, for inspection, maintenance or safety, could lower the reservoir by up to twelve metres without notice to anyone.

IlluminaraThrust on a seam goes with the square of the depth. Harrow holds thirty-eight metres at full pool. Take off eleven and the push on block thirty-one is about half. A drawdown is not a programme, and the lender would be right to refuse to call it one. It is what keeps the seam alive for the days the paper takes.

Dovan PrycéEleven metres is thirty-three million cubic metres. The Works would be pouring its own stock down the river for nothing.

IlluminaraNot for nothing, if somebody downstream wants it. Who is short of water in the autumn?

He opened his mouth to say no one, and closed it. The terraces: four thousand hectares of orchard and winter wheat, and the off-stream reservoirs that Corrin Aske's irrigation union filled every March at pumping prices.

IlluminaraThen let him bite a price off it. The draw must be slow, no more than a quarter of a metre a day, or the wet shoulders of the valley slump into the pool. That is forty-four days. You have fifty-six. Start on Thursday, or the arithmetic runs out before the water does.

Prycé sat quietly for some time. Outside, somebody's dog barked at nothing.

Dovan PrycéYou've left out the strongest thing against you, and I noticed. Even if the lender permits the programme, I have certified satisfactory eleven times. That is misrepresentation, a default in its own right whatever the dam is doing. The proviso protects a man who is late. It doesn't protect a liar.

IlluminaraNo, it doesn't. That one is yours to answer, and I can't answer it for you. But you are the one who has to stand.

Dovan PrycéAnd the people below?

IlluminaraThey hear what you know on the day the lender does, and they hear the rest from you before anyone discusses your name.

Every hour until the grey came up, one of them went down the lane to the gallery, read the bent spoke against its ruler and wrote the figure under the last one. It did not move.

04 · What the Water Is Worth

Tarn Works, director's office · Wednesday, 07:30

Marguerite Voss listened to all of it standing, with her coat still on, which in Harrow Vale was a form of courtesy. When Prycé had finished she did not look at Illuminara. She looked at him.

Marguerite VossEleven years I have signed for a dam I could not see inside, on the word of a man I trusted with my children's school.

Dovan PrycéYes.

Marguerite VossI'd have done the same in your chair, which is why I can throw this chair at you. You will say it to the board yourself, Dovan, in your own words, and you won't hide behind the crack. Those are my terms. I'll sign the maintenance order for the low gates myself: dawn, Thursday. And I'll sign the notice to Aldermere under fourteen point five, as Borrower: a suspected condition, independently measured, and the assessor to give his own account in person on Monday. Your name isn't on it. It's true, and the rest is yours to say.

Dovan PrycéIt's a true sentence. I'll say the rest.

Sunniva Achterberg had driven through the night with a drum of fibre-optic cable in the back of a hatchback and spent the day lying on a gallery floor. She put a printout on the desk. It sat inside the line Prycé had drawn in the gallery, and not by much.

Sunniva AchterbergPoint three one of a millimetre across block thirty-one in eighteen hours. That is faster than your pencil, Mr Prycé. Yesterday I'd have said dull. Today I'll say it's the tail of something. Take eleven metres off and my model gives the margin back. I'll sign safer, measured. I won't sign safe. I've never seen that word survive a spring.

Marguerite VossThe water pays a third of stage one: twenty-two anchors in blocks twenty-nine to thirty-three. Halloran can release a first tranche on his own signature, up to a line, and I've priced stage one to sit under it. The notice reaches Aldermere at nine on Thursday, and at the same hour a bulletin goes on every door in the valley: the seam, the pool coming down, a public meeting on Monday. The lender won't hear one word the valley hasn't.

Corrin Aske arrived at noon in his boots and listened with a silence that grew more dangerous as it went.

Corrin AskeYou want me to buy your fear.

Marguerite VossI want you to buy thirty-three million cubic metres for the terraces at nine cents against the nineteen it costs you to pump.

Corrin AskeAt nine. In my reservoirs by Christmas, not March, with a written guarantee you can't take back. And a seat on the dam safety panel. My people sleep below that wall too. If you want me to sleep, I read the cable myself. I don't hear about it.

Marguerite VossDone. The seat, and the guarantee.

Corrin AskeAnd him?

Dovan PrycéI'll answer for the certificates. I'm not asking you to like it.

Corrin AskeGood. I don't.

05 · Residents First

Harrow town hall, Aldermere's credit committee on the screen · the following Monday, 15:00

Teodor Halloran appeared on the screen with a printed agenda in his hand, a careful man with a careful tie. He had granted the standstill on Thursday. Imre Dalca, Aldermere's engineer, had spent Friday reading Achterberg's cable himself and had approved stage one of the programme at nine that morning. The hall was full, and Illuminara said nothing.

Teodor HalloranThis is a credit review. Item one is covenant compliance.

Anneke RoweItem one is me. I nurse at the clinic nine hundred metres below the toe of that dam, and my sons' school is on the flat beside it. Before anyone talks about a loan, tell me whether my children can sleep.

Teodor HalloranMs Rowe, with respect, this is a lender's—

Anneke RoweWith respect, I'll hear it first or I'll hear nothing.

Dovan Prycé stood. His notes shook once and then did not. He had practised, Illuminara thought, for eleven years, in a mirror she had never seen.

Dovan PrycéFor eleven years I certified Harrow Dam satisfactory, with monitoring. It was not. Block thirty-one has a seam moving faster each winter, and I hid the rate. Since Thursday the pool has been lowered one point one metres, and an independent cable shows the movement has slowed by a fifth. Mr Dalca has approved stage one of a remedial programme, and so, at three o'clock, as licensing assessor, I classify Harrow Dam Class D at full pool. Ms Rowe, I will not tell you the school is safe. Ilse Bram's flood unit reran the breach map on Friday. At full pool, a breach of block thirty-one puts four metres of water on your flat in eleven minutes. At the pool we will hold by storm season, about half a metre in forty. That is a different flood. It is not no flood. If the cable shows more than fifteen hundredths of a millimetre in six hours, or the pool passes thirty-one metres, the sirens sound and the flat is cleared, whatever the sky is doing. I am the reason you had to ask.

Anneke Rowe said nothing for a while.

Anneke RoweForty minutes is a wish if it's one road and six hundred children. The flat clears in twenty-five or I want a second road. And the clinic goes on the radio net the same hour as the gallery.

Dovan PrycéThe radio I can put in the plan myself. The stopwatch at Wednesday's siren test is yours.

She sat down, which in that room was a verdict of its own.

Anneke RoweWednesday, and I'll stand under the siren myself. Go on. Say the rest to him.

Teodor HalloranMr Prycé, you have just told me you falsified eleven certificates, and my committee's first instinct will be to call the loan. My standstill covers the condition. It waives nothing. A committee will ask whether a programme was in progress on the date of your certificate, and whether approved means funded. Mr Dalca approved stage one at nine, I released the first tranche at ten on my own signature, and you certified at three. I have minuted that order. Had you certified on Thursday, I would be telling you the proviso never attached.

Dovan PrycéI know. I was made to wait.

Halloran looked, for the first time, at the woman sitting beside the assessor.

Teodor HalloranAnd you are?

IlluminaraSomeone who was sent to check a flood model. One number, if I may: if Aldermere called the facility today, on a dam with a moving seam, a valley to evacuate and a Works shut down, what does the collateral fetch?

Teodor HalloranA very small number.

He looked at the figure on his own screen for longer than a man looks who has already decided.

Teodor HalloranI can't waive a misrepresentation; that belongs to committee. I can refer it with the standstill running and a recommendation that includes that number. I'll record the facility as under active remediation, not unsafe. Mr Prycé's certificates stay reserved, the committee will rule within ninety days, and I would find a lawyer.

Dovan PrycéI have one. I should have taken her advice some years ago.

06 · Holding

Harrow Dam, crest · forty-nine days later, 02:10

The first storm of the season came off the ridge like a door thrown open. Four hundred millimetres in two days. The pool rose two point four metres in the night, and it had eleven metres of empty valley to rise into. Twenty-two anchors, drilled into blocks twenty-nine to thirty-three, held the seam in a grip it had not known since the dam was young.

Before the rain, Achterberg had written three lines on a card and taped it above the gallery radio. At a rise of two point four metres the seam would open by no more than six hundredths of a millimetre. Past the line Prycé had drawn in the gallery, the sirens would sound. At the top of every hour Aske's night man read the cable aloud over the radio. By midnight, one hundredth. By one, two. At two, three.

Dovan PrycéForty-four days of drawdown. The Works carrying new debt for ten years. Eleven families gone since September, who'd have gone anyway, they tell me, though I have never found that comfort. I'm out on the thirty-first. The committee will fine me and take my licence. My lawyer says I'll keep my house.

IlluminaraAnd Ms Rowe?

Dovan PrycéThe boys went to school on the flat on Monday. She timed the siren drill on Wednesday. Twenty-three minutes. She says she still wakes at three. I told her I do as well, and she said good.

Rain hammered the crest. Below them the valley lay dark and unevacuated, six thousand people asleep behind a wall that had, for the first time in eleven years, been described truthfully.

At three the pulse crested, and Achterberg's voice came over the radio from the gallery.

Sunniva Achterberg (radio)Crest. Four hundredths. Inside the band. The anchor cells rose with the water, so the anchors are carrying it. If the lowered pool had done this alone, they'd have read nothing. Safer, measured. I'll write safe on the card when I have seen it survive a spring.

Dovan PrycéIt moved.

IlluminaraIt was allowed to. That's the difference.

Prycé took the pencil from behind his ear. In the gallery the bent spoke stood where he had left it, and tonight he did not need to go down and read it. In the lamp of the radio shed he opened a pocket notebook, copied out the crest figure and the date beside it, and then, in the space where for eleven years he had written the careful word, wrote another.

Holding.

Illuminara stayed until the water began to fall, and by morning she was gone. The valley woke to a river running full and quiet, and the school bus came down past the terraces and stopped at the clinic, as it did every day.

What the critic still says doesn't work

A dam assessor who hid a moving seam to save his valley chooses truth when Illuminara turns his strongest objection into an ordered repair, a resident-owned safety rule and a measurable storm test that keeps both the people and their livelihood standing.

Six scenes across eight weeks: a number that holds still, a seam in the dark, a loan read for its date and a door nobody had used, a price for water, a credit committee that hears the valley first, and a storm measured against a band written beforehand.

01 · Številka, ki miruje

Harrow Vale, pisarna uprave za vode · ponedeljek, 16:10 · šestinpetdeset dni pred sezono neurij

Regionalna oblast jo je poslala preverit poplavni model doline in model je bil dober. Illuminara je v njem preživela dva dni, kakor ključavničar preživi popoldne v ključavnici. Padavine so imele razpon. Prst je imela razpon. Le en vhodni podatek je popolnoma miroval, in miroval je nad šest tisoč strehami.

IlluminaraLevi opornik, blok enaintrideset. Enajst let meritev stika, ena na mesec. Gospod Prycé, voda v pobočju ne miruje tako spokojno. Vsak drug merilnik zatrepeta, ko dežuje po grebenu. Ta je gluh.

Dovan Prycé ji je postavil skodelico čaja ob komolec, preden je odgovoril. Imel je enainšestdeset let, bil je urejen in miren, človek, ki je vse življenje preživel kot najtišja oseba v prostorih, kjer so sprejemali odločitve.

Dovan PrycéMerilnik je star, stik pa suh. Temu bi rekel pohvala injektiranju. Harrow je dolgočasen jez, gospa Illuminara. Večina mojega dela je skrbno zapisovanje besede zadovoljivo.

Nekoč je bila sodna tolmačka in še vedno je prisluškovala stavkom, ki so bili vnaprej izurjeni. Ta je bil gladko obrušen. Dolgočasen, je rekel nežno, kakor človek, ki zapira vrata, ki jih je sam naoljil.

IlluminaraNikogar ne obtožujem. Sprašujem, kakšen bi moral biti jez, da bi bila ta serija poštena meritev. Rada bi ga videla. Nocoj, če mi posodite ključ galerije.

Premor je trajal manj kot pol sekunde. To ni bil strah. Bil je zvok človeka, ki se odloča, katero od dveh resnic bo ponudil najprej.

Dovan PrycéSvetloba je najboljša ob mraku. Vzemite modri ključ. Prišel bom za vami, ko zaklenem.

Ni pogledal proti polici za svojo pisalno mizo, kjer je na žeblju viselo kolesarsko kolo z eno manjkajočo napero. Opazila je, da ni pogledal.

02 · Svinčnik na zidu

Jez Harrow, galerija levega opornika, blok 31 · isti večer, 19:40

Beton je zapis teže. Illuminara je položila dlan na steno galerije in jo brala kot sistem poti, po katerih potuje sila, ter nekaj mest, kjer se poti niso strinjale. Pri bloku enaintrideset se je razcepila pot, ki se ne bi smela. Obremenitev je drsela vstran skozi stik, ki ni bil debelejši od črte svinčnika, in stik pod njeno roko ni miroval.

Razlaga, si je rekla. Ne dokaz. Preizkusi jo.

Uradni merilnik razpoke, medeninasti kazalnik, prilepljen čez stik, je bil skupaj z lestvico prebarvan s svežo občinsko zeleno barvo. Ob njem, v višini kolena, je bilo na zid pripeto ravnilo, čez razpoko pa je kot kazalec segala upognjena kolesarska napera. Po ometu navzdol je tekla kolona črtic s svinčnikom, vsaka z datumom v drobni, skrbni pisavi. Brala jih je od spodaj. Devet let so napredovale za štiri desetinke milimetra na leto. Nato zadnji dve zimi vsakič za milimeter in pol. In potem na vrhu tri črtice v devetih dneh.

Dovan PrycéNašli ste mojo napero.

IlluminaraNašla sem vaš svinčnik. To je najpoštenejši zapis v tej dolini.

Sedel je na boben za kable, kakor da so njegove noge nekaj sprejele pred njim.

Dovan PrycéOb štirih desetinkah sem lahko živel ob njem. Zdaj vsako jutro pridem sem, preden se odpre pisarna, da pogledam, ali se je kazalec premaknil, in vsako jutro se odločim, da ne bom zapisal, kar kaže.

IlluminaraKoliko časa po vaši presoji?

Dovan PrycéTakšen stik ne poči. Neha popuščati in na dan, ko neha, nima več česa popustiti. Še ena mokra jesen, povrh pa taljenje snega? Doline ne bi zastavil na drugo. Tudi na prvo ne.

IlluminaraZakaj potem barva?

Enkrat se je zasmejal samemu sebi.

Dovan PrycéPred enajstimi leti sem bil mlajši ocenjevalec. Stari Ketteridge je ob tem bloku na rob zapisal: zahteva ukrepanje. Aldermere je to prebral kot nevarno, ker ima njihova kreditna zaveza sprožilec in ta beseda ga je potegnila. Posojilo so odpoklicali v tridesetih dneh. Obrat so zaprli. Dva tisoč dvesto ljudi je odšlo v eni zimi in pomagal sem nalagati kombije. Na naslednjem potrdilu je bilo z mojo roko zapisano zadovoljivo, ob spremljanju, in odtlej spremljam. Vašo poštenost plačujejo hiše drugih ljudi. Pokažite mi popravilo, zaradi katerega nihče ne izgubi službe, ali pa priznajte, da boste nekoč storili, kar sem jaz, le z več pričami.

To je bil dober ugovor in Illuminara ni hitela mimo njega. Za njim je v temi galerije v odtoku kapljala voda, glasna kakor ura.

IlluminaraPopravila nimam. Imam dve dejstvi, nobeno pa še ni razsodba: stik se premika in enajst let ste bili edini, ki je to vedel. Zato vam lahko obljubim tole, in tu se obljuba konča. Nocoj ne prijavim ničesar. Nič z vašim imenom ne zapusti te galerije in nič o jezu ne gre posojilodajalcu ali mestu, dokler ne obstaja načrt, v katerem je prostor tudi za vas. Toda stika ne bom prepustila vašemu svinčniku, niti svojemu. Sunniva Achterberg bo ob prvem svitu čez blok enaintrideset položila optični kabel, da se bova prepirala z meritvijo, ne z menoj. Berem silo, gospod Prycé, in lahko se motim. Najprej pa me poučite o denarju doline.

Dovan PrycéDajte obljubi konec, pa vam dam številko. Voda na dolvodni strani tega bloka. Ali petnajst stotink milimetra čez razpoko v šestih urah. Tega se bojim od druge zime. Stik, ki naredi to, ne leze več.

IlluminaraPotem je to meja in narisali ste jo vi, ne jaz.
