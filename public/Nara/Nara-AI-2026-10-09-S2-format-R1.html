A dying child has one body, two lawful identities and a medical history trapped on the wrong side of a disputed border. Illuminara cannot erase either constitution or command either court. She must show both nations that jurisdiction, identity and evidence have been mistaken for the same thing—and build a bridge narrow enough to save one life without swallowing anyone’s sovereignty.

01 · One Body, Two Children

Ilyran National Children's Hospital · nine hours remain, perhaps ten

Tavi Soren was eight years old when the border reached backward through time and divided the day of her birth.

She lay beneath a white canopy in the Ilyran National Children’s Hospital, breathing through silver mesh that brightened each time her blood forgot how to carry oxygen. One screen called her Tavi Soren, citizen of Ilyra, born at 03:14 on the east bank of the Rill. The other called her Tavi Soren, protected national of Kharad, registered at 03:22 aboard Evacuation Train Seven. Both records were lawful. That was the problem.

Floodwater had forced the train to reverse while survey drones moved the provisional boundary behind it. Ilyran law fixed citizenship at first independent breath. Kharadi law treated evacuation trains as extensions of their departure territory until a registrar sealed the birth. Tavi had breathed in Ilyra and been registered in Kharad. A later judgment upheld both rules without deciding who owned the flooded strip.

Now a dormant fungus from that flood had flowered inside her marrow. Doctor Ina Vey could stop it with a tailored immune graft, but the dose required Tavi’s neonatal exposure map, sealed in Kharad’s civil-health archive. Ilyra could use the map only inside an authenticated medical chain. Kharad could release it only to the legal person in its registry. Neither state recognized the other Tavi as the same legal subject without conceding the border.

VeyNine hours. Perhaps ten. At twelve, the graft would only prolong dying.

Kharadi registrar Dema Quill appeared by secure projection. He had opened the archive at dawn.

QuillI can prove the map exists. I cannot export its chain to an Ilyran identity. If assertion is enough, divided children can disappear from one system and reappear in another with whatever history is convenient.

Chief Justice Saren Ro of Ilyra answered from a hospital office.

RoAnd I cannot import Kharadi civil status disguised as medicine. If an executive file can overrule citizenship at breath, our constitution is theater.

Maela held her daughter’s hand.

MaelaYou are both protecting her. Which is why neither of you can touch her.

Illuminara studied the two file structures without opening either sealed body.

IlluminaraDo not give me conclusions. Give me the exact act each constitution forbids. I want to know what the states fear doing to themselves.

02 · The Doors That Looked Open

Hospital and state archives · before the hearing

The first door was mercy. Ilyra’s health minister could authorize a one-patient procedure, but not authenticate the foreign evidence needed to tune it. Justice Ro could protect Vey from immediate prosecution; she could not make an unauthenticated dose medically usable. An evidentiary order would become available only after lawful provenance existed—the missing thing, not an escape from it.

The second door was erasure. Kharad offered Maela a form renouncing Tavi’s Ilyran record. The Kharadi Tavi could then receive her map and travel under humanitarian transfer. Renunciation would also void Tavi’s inheritance from her Ilyran father and make her schooling, vaccinations and protection orders acts performed on a legal stranger.

MaelaI will not save the part of her that breathes by declaring the rest fictional.

The third door was merger. A merged identity would force one registry’s constitutional root beneath the other. Each court could call that administration only by lying about what its own database meant.

Illuminara tried a fourth door of her own: a neutral archive outside both states. Quill rejected it because Kharadi law treated custody of a sealed civil chain as jurisdiction. Ro rejected it because an external custodian could authenticate neither Ilyran citizenship nor admissibility. Neutrality had not separated authority from evidence; it had merely created a third claimant to both.

Illuminara erased the archive diagram. It was her first wrong hypothesis, and the clock had lost forty-one minutes to it.

Tavi woke and asked why the adults kept saying her name as if they were testing whether it belonged to her. Maela explained that two countries had written her birth differently and were deciding which paper could help the doctors.

TaviCan they use the paper without deciding me? Doctor Vey wants what happened to my blood. She doesn’t need to own my birthday.

Illuminara looked at the erased diagram. A neutral keeper had failed because it still held an identity. Tavi had asked for something smaller: not who might hold the record, but whether one fact could reach one body.

Illuminara, to the clerksSearch again. Not for shared custody. Find every case where your state used the other’s fact while refusing the other’s authority.

03 · What a Border May Not Break

Hospital and state archives · time unstated

The clerks returned thousands of cases. Most were temporary compromises. Illuminara rejected them. She asked which decisions allowed the foreign fact, expressly denied the foreign jurisdiction and protected a person or duty from destruction. Only two survived all three tests.

Ilyra had admitted Kharadi rescue maps for miners trapped beneath disputed ground: “A fact does not acquire sovereignty from the hand that records it.” Kharad had restored children’s schooling and inheritance from Ilyran relief lists after an earthquake: “A state may dispute another state’s jurisdiction, but it may not make a person discontinuous to preserve the dispute.” The judgments had been taught as victories over the other country. No one had placed them together.

Illuminara did.

IlluminaraYour constitutions disagree about when Tavi entered your jurisdiction. They do not require her body to become discontinuous while you disagree. You have tied identity, jurisdiction and evidentiary continuity into one knot. Each constitution already cuts that knot when refusing a fact would destroy what the law exists to protect.

Quill attacked first.

QuillA rescue map describes a tunnel. This archive describes a citizen. Release it and Ilyra can copy any Kharadi history into an invented person.

IlluminaraThen do not release the civil chain. Answer a smaller question: does the medical layer continue to the living child?

Vey refused a bare assertion. She needed sample identity, storage history and proof that the fungal sequence had not changed. Illuminara assembled three independent anchors: Maela’s emergency bracelet tied mother to infant without naming nationality; the Kharadi seal tied the neonatal vial to the bracelet’s one-time code; Tavi’s current marrow carried an immune scar the sealed map claimed to predict.

The judges allowed two rival forensic teams into separate secure rooms under temporary inspection orders. No copy could leave. Each team extracted the same three blinded features from the map and committed them before seeing the current marrow result. Only then did Vey disclose the marrow assay.

An Ilyran advocate introduced a forged bracelet with a copied code. It passed the first comparison. A Kharadi technician found that its resin lacked the flood-silt isotope embedded before the train crossed the cordon. The advocate added the isotope. The forgery passed again—until the teams revealed that the archived neonatal vial carried a time-stamped antibody ratio and the living marrow carried its rare, age-shifted scar. A forger could copy the seal, bracelet or present blood. To fabricate all three, the forger would have needed the unopened neonatal sample eight years earlier and the living child now.

Six attacks failed. The seventh remained: a future government might cite a medical continuity finding as a concealed citizenship ruling.

IlluminaraThen the permission and the prohibition must be born together. Two judgments, each binding only its own state, meeting at one fact.

04 · A Court with No Country

Reciprocal chamber negotiations · before the hearing

Illuminara proposed a reciprocal continuity chamber with no power to grant citizenship, redraw a border or command a ministry. Each state would appoint one judge and one registrar. Families entered only by consent. The chamber could certify a bounded chain of fact; each national court would separately decide what use of that fact was lawful.

For Tavi, Kharad would attest that the medical layer and storage history pertained to the bearer of the one-time birth code. Ilyra would decide whether its hospital could use the attestation without recognizing Kharadi jurisdiction or altering Tavi’s Ilyran status.

Justice Ro crossed out Illuminara’s first clause. It required preservation of every linked right until a later hearing.

RoThat imports Kharadi status before Ilyra has judged it.

She replaced it with preservation of evidence and a stay against irreversible harm—no automatic right, tax or inheritance.

Justice Hal Meret of Kharad struck the next clause. It made certification permanent.

MeretI will not let one emergency become an immortal machine.

He demanded a public audit after twelve cases, renewal by both courts after one year and a right for either registrar to challenge a new use without reopening the original fact.

Ro objected that a sunset could make Tavi discontinuous again. Meret answered that the source record would remain; only the cross-border procedure would expire. Illuminara redrafted: certified facts would remain auditable, but new legal uses after sunset required a renewed chamber. Tavi’s medical treatment, once lawful, could not be retroactively criminalized.

Maela read the terms.

MaelaThis can save her and still make her life harder.

IlluminaraYes. A bridge promising only benefits would be another false identity form.

Maela wanted Tavi’s whole history preserved but allegiance deferred until adulthood. Meret called that stateless privilege. Ro called automatic allegiance punishment for receiving medicine. Quill proposed notice at majority; Maela’s lawyer added a right to contest before any duty attached. Neither judge liked the other’s reason. Both accepted the safeguard for a different one.

With two hours left in Vey’s first nine-hour window, the text remained scarred by their resistance: audit, sunset, enumerated uses, local exclusion, notice and contest. It was stronger than Illuminara’s first design because neither sovereign had simply said yes.

The judges ordered a simultaneous adversarial hearing. Either could still reject it. Without both rulings, the map could not lawfully reach Vey in time.

05 · The Fact Between Them

Two courtrooms and one hospital room · the emergency hearing

The hearing occupied two courtrooms and one hospital room. Ilyra questioned Kharad’s seal. Kharad questioned Ilyra’s bracelet. The forensic teams disclosed every failed attack. Illuminara answered none for them. She had designed the chain; those governed by it had to prove it could survive without her authority.

The Ilyran advocate pressed the seventh attack.

The Ilyran advocateIf our court accepts that this blood belongs to Kharad’s recorded child, what prevents tomorrow’s minister from calling that a citizenship finding?

Quill surprised him.

QuillKharad prevents it. Our proposed attestation names no citizen abroad. It identifies a sample, a code and a biological continuation. If my government later adds allegiance, the audit trail will show the addition was not in the attestation.

A Kharadi advocate then attacked Ilyra’s power to reject future uses.

The Kharadi advocateReciprocity that permits rejection is not reciprocity.

Justice Ro answered before Illuminara could.

RoReciprocity preserves the statement you made. It does not compel my constitution to draw your conclusion. Procedure may be shared; sovereignty over legal effect is not.

With one hour and fourteen minutes left, Meret asked Illuminara.

MeretHow many people become two people if we approve this?

IlluminaraNone. Let the records disagree. Tavi does not have to divide with them.

RoHow many borders move?

IlluminaraNone. You limit what a border may do to evidence of one life.

The courts withdrew. Tavi’s blood oxygen fell and did not fully recover. Vey prepared an untuned graft whose odds were less than half those of the tailored dose. Maela signed the continuity petition but left consent blank until she knew whether there would be a lawful map to use.

Kharad returned first. Meret authorized a bounded medical attestation, subject to audit and sunset, and refused durable schooling or inheritance effects until separate petitions tested them. It was less than Illuminara had asked for, but enough to carry provenance.

Eleven minutes later, Ilyra returned. Ro admitted the attestation for treatment, required a separate continuity index and forbade any inference about citizenship. She rejected Kharad’s proposed right to suspend an already admitted medical fact, but accepted its right to challenge every new use.

The judgments did not mirror each other. Their overlap was one question: does this medical evidence continue to the living person who consented to its use? Both answered yes.

Maela signed. Quill released only the sealed medical layer. Vey tuned the graft and began the infusion at eight hours and twenty-nine minutes.

Tavi woke during the second bag.

TaviDid they decide my birthday?

MaelaNo. They decided your blood was allowed to remember it.

06 · The Line Afterward

Ilyran hospital and the border · the following days

The graft took. By morning, Tavi’s marrow was producing cells that recognized the fungus instead of surrendering to it. On the third day, she walked to the window and complained that the river looked smaller than the maps that had caused so much trouble.

The chamber remained narrow. It could not decide the border, grant citizenship, forgive taxes, conscript a soldier, transfer property or merge a file. It could certify that a bounded fact survived incompatible registries and preserve an auditable path to its source.

Forty-three families filed petitions in the first week. Most waited; Meret’s ruling permitted only medical continuity until the audit. One vaccination case succeeded. An inheritance claim failed because Ilyra found the foreign record authentic but legally insufficient. The failure mattered: the chamber could carry a fact without predetermining its use.

At the twelfth-case audit, both courts renewed the procedure. They added schooling and vaccination evidence, retained separate hearings for inheritance and allegiance, and published different constitutional explanations for the same narrow rule. Some families refused enrollment. Their refusal remained lawful.

Quill became Kharad’s first chamber registrar because distrust, he said, should be represented by someone who knew its strongest case. Ilyra appointed the advocate who had built the forged bracelet. The chamber’s seal showed two broken lines joined by an open circle—a crossing that did not pretend the lines had vanished.

Maela preserved Tavi’s records and deferred every allegiance claim until adulthood, when each state would have to give notice and accept a contest. Kharad retained its birth record. Ilyra retained its citizenship record. Neither became false. They no longer had to compete for the right to describe the same child’s past.

When Tavi left the hospital, reporters asked which country she belonged to. She looked at Illuminara, who did not answer for her.

TaviToday? I belong to my mother. Tomorrow I have school.

The border remained where the two nations had drawn it. It no longer ran through her body.

Umirajoči otrok ima eno telo, dve zakoniti identiteti in zdravstveno zgodovino, ujeto na napačni strani sporne meje. Illuminara ne more izbrisati nobene ustave niti ukazovati nobenemu sodišču. Obema narodoma mora pokazati, da so pristojnost, identiteto in dokaz zamenjali za isto stvar — ter zgraditi most, dovolj ozek, da reši eno življenje, ne da bi pogoltnil suverenost kogarkoli.

01 · Eno telo, dva otroka

Ilyrska državna otroška bolnišnica · ostaja devet ur, morda deset

Tavi Soren je bila stara osem let, ko je meja segla nazaj skozi čas in razdelila dan njenega rojstva.

Ležala je pod belim baldahinom v Ilyrski državni otroški bolnišnici in dihala skozi srebrno mrežo, ki se je razsvetlila vsakič, ko je njena kri pozabila prenašati kisik. En zaslon jo je imenoval Tavi Soren, državljanka Ilyre, rojena ob 03.14 na vzhodnem bregu Rilla. Drugi jo je imenoval Tavi Soren, zaščitena državljanka Kharada, vpisana ob 03.22 na evakuacijskem vlaku sedem. Oba zapisa sta bila zakonita. V tem je bila težava.

Poplavna voda je vlak prisilila, da se je obrnil, medtem ko so geodetski droni začasno mejo premikali za njim. Ilyrsko pravo je državljanstvo določalo ob prvem samostojnem vdihu. Kharadsko pravo je evakuacijske vlake obravnavalo kot podaljšek ozemlja odhoda, dokler matičar ni potrdil rojstva. Tavi je vdihnila v Ilyri in bila vpisana v Kharadu. Poznejša sodba je potrdila obe pravili, ne da bi odločila, komu pripada poplavljeni pas.

Zdaj se je v njenem kostnem mozgu razrasla mirujoča gliva iz tiste poplave. Doktorica Ina Vey bi jo lahko ustavila s prilagojenim imunskim presadkom, toda odmerek je zahteval Tavijin zemljevid izpostavljenosti ob rojstvu, zapečaten v kharadskem civilno-zdravstvenem arhivu. Ilyra je smela zemljevid uporabiti le znotraj overjene medicinske verige. Kharad ga je smel izdati le pravni osebi iz svojega registra. Nobena država ni priznavala Tavi druge države kot istega pravnega subjekta, ne da bi s tem popustila pri meji.

VeyDevet ur. Morda deset. Pri dvanajstih bi presadek le podaljšal umiranje.

Kharadski matičar Dema Quill se je pojavil prek varne projekcije. Arhiv je odprl ob zori.

QuillLahko dokažem, da zemljevid obstaja. Njegove verige ne morem izvoziti k ilyrski identiteti. Če zadošča gola trditev, lahko razdeljeni otroci izginejo iz enega sistema in se pojavijo v drugem s kakršnokoli zgodovino, ki je priročna.

Vrhovna sodnica Ilyre Saren Ro je odgovorila iz bolnišnične pisarne.

RoIn jaz ne morem uvoziti kharadskega civilnega statusa, preoblečenega v medicino. Če lahko izvršilni spis razveljavi državljanstvo ob vdihu, je naša ustava gledališče.

Maela je držala hčer za roko.

MaelaOba jo varujeta. Zato se je nobeden od vaju ne more dotakniti.

Illuminara je preučevala strukturi obeh datotek, ne da bi odprla njuno zapečateno vsebino.

IlluminaraNe dajajte mi sklepov. Povejte mi natanko, katero dejanje vsaka ustava prepoveduje. Vedeti hočem, česa se državi bojita storiti sami sebi.

02 · Vrata, ki so bila videti odprta

Bolnišnica in državna arhiva · pred obravnavo

Prva vrata so bila usmiljenje. Ilyrski minister za zdravje bi lahko odobril poseg za enega bolnika, ne pa overil tujega dokaza, potrebnega za nastavitev zdravljenja. Sodnica Ro bi lahko Vey zaščitila pred takojšnjim pregonom; neoverjenega odmerka ni mogla narediti medicinsko uporabnega. Dokazni nalog bi bil mogoč šele po vzpostavitvi zakonitega izvora — to je bila manjkajoča stvar, ne izhod mimo nje.

Druga vrata so bila izbris. Kharad je Maeli ponudil obrazec, s katerim bi se odpovedala Tavijinemu ilyrskemu zapisu. Kharadska Tavi bi nato lahko prejela svoj zemljevid in potovala v okviru humanitarnega prevoza. Odpoved bi izničila tudi Tavijino dediščino po ilyrskem očetu ter njeno šolanje, cepljenja in odredbe o zaščiti spremenila v dejanja, opravljena nad pravno tujko.

MaelaNe bom rešila dela nje, ki diha, tako da bom preostanek razglasila za izmišljenega.

Tretja vrata so bila združitev. Združena identiteta bi ustavno korenino enega registra prisilila pod drugo. Vsako sodišče bi to lahko imenovalo upravljanje le tako, da bi lagalo o pomenu lastne podatkovne zbirke.

Illuminara je poskusila s četrtimi vrati po lastni zamisli: nevtralnim arhivom zunaj obeh držav. Quill ga je zavrnil, ker je kharadsko pravo skrbništvo nad zapečateno civilno verigo obravnavalo kot pristojnost. Ro ga je zavrnila, ker zunanji skrbnik ne bi mogel overiti niti ilyrskega državljanstva niti dopustnosti dokaza. Nevtralnost ni ločila oblasti od dokaza; ustvarila je le tretjega zahtevnika za oboje.

Illuminara je izbrisala diagram arhiva. To je bila njena prva napačna hipoteza in ura je zaradi nje izgubila enainštirideset minut.

Tavi se je prebudila in vprašala, zakaj odrasli ves čas izgovarjajo njeno ime, kakor da preverjajo, ali ji pripada. Maela ji je razložila, da sta dve državi njeno rojstvo zapisali različno in zdaj odločata, kateri papir bi lahko pomagal zdravnikom.

TaviAli lahko uporabijo papir, ne da bi odločili mene? Doktorica Vey potrebuje to, kar se je zgodilo moji krvi. Ne potrebuje lastništva nad mojim rojstnim dnem.

Illuminara je pogledala izbrisani diagram. Nevtralni skrbnik je propadel, ker je še vedno držal identiteto. Tavi je prosila za nekaj manjšega: ne za odgovor, kdo sme hraniti zapis, temveč ali lahko eno dejstvo doseže eno telo.

Illuminara, pisarjemIščite znova. Ne skupnega skrbništva. Poiščite vsak primer, ko je vaša država uporabila dejstvo druge države, medtem ko je zavrnila njeno oblast.

03 · Česa meja ne sme pretrgati

Bolnišnica in državna arhiva · čas ni naveden

Uradniki so se vrnili s tisoči primerov. Večina je bila začasnih kompromisov. Illuminara jih je zavrnila. Vprašala je, katere odločbe so sprejele tuje dejstvo, izrecno zavrnile tujo pristojnost in zaščitile osebo ali dolžnost pred uničenjem. Vse tri preizkuse sta prestali le dve.

Ilyra je sprejela kharadske reševalne zemljevide za rudarje, ujete pod spornim ozemljem: »Dejstvo ne pridobi suverenosti iz roke, ki ga zapiše.« Kharad je po potresu obnovil šolanje in dedne pravice otrok na podlagi ilyrskih seznamov za pomoč: »Država sme izpodbijati pristojnost druge države, ne sme pa človeka narediti diskontinuiranega, da bi ohranila spor.« Sodbi so poučevali kot zmagi nad drugo državo. Nihče ju ni položil drugo ob drugo.

Illuminara ju je.

IlluminaraVaši ustavi se ne strinjata, kdaj je Tavi vstopila v vašo pristojnost. Ne zahtevata, da njeno telo med vajinim nesoglasjem postane diskontinuirano. Identiteto, pristojnost in dokazno kontinuiteto ste zvezali v en vozel. Vsaka ustava ta vozel že prereže, kadar bi zavrnitev dejstva uničila to, kar naj bi pravo varovalo.

Quill je napadel prvi.

QuillReševalni zemljevid opisuje rov. Ta arhiv opisuje državljana. Če ga izročimo, lahko Ilyra katerokoli kharadsko zgodovino prekopira v izmišljeno osebo.

IlluminaraPotem ne izročite civilne verige. Odgovorite na manjše vprašanje: ali se medicinski sloj nadaljuje v živem otroku?

Vey je zavrnila golo trditev. Potrebovala je identiteto vzorca, zgodovino shranjevanja in dokaz, da se glivno zaporedje ni spremenilo. Illuminara je sestavila tri neodvisna sidra: Maelina zapestnica za nujne primere je povezovala mater z dojenčkom, ne da bi imenovala državljanstvo; kharadski pečat je povezal neonatalno vialo z enkratno kodo zapestnice; Tavijin sedanji kostni mozeg je nosil imunsko brazgotino, ki jo je zapečateni zemljevid napovedoval.

Sodnika sta dvema nasprotnima forenzičnima skupinama dovolila vstop v ločene varne sobe na podlagi začasnih odredb za pregled. Nobena kopija ni smela zapustiti prostorov. Vsaka skupina je iz zemljevida izločila iste tri zaslepljene značilnosti in jih zavezala, preden je videla rezultat sedanjega testa kostnega mozga. Šele nato je Vey razkrila izvid.

Ilyrski zagovornik je predstavil ponarejeno zapestnico s prekopirano kodo. Prestala je prvo primerjavo. Kharadski tehnik je ugotovil, da njeni smoli manjka izotop poplavnega mulja, ujet vanjo, preden je vlak prečkal kordon. Zagovornik je dodal izotop. Ponaredek je znova prestal preizkus — dokler skupini nista razkrili, da je arhivirana neonatalna viala nosila časovno označeno razmerje protiteles, živi kostni mozeg pa njegovo redko, s starostjo spremenjeno brazgotino. Ponarejevalec bi lahko prekopiral pečat, zapestnico ali sedanjo kri. Za ponareditev vseh treh bi potreboval neodprti neonatalni vzorec pred osmimi leti in živega otroka zdaj.

Šest napadov je propadlo. Sedmi je ostal: prihodnja vlada bi lahko ugotovitev o medicinski kontinuiteti navedla kot prikrito odločbo o državljanstvu.

IlluminaraPotem se morata dovoljenje in prepoved roditi skupaj. Dve sodbi, vsaka zavezujoča le za svojo državo, ki se srečata pri enem dejstvu.

04 · Sodišče brez države

Pogajanja o vzajemnem senatu · pred obravnavo

Illuminara je predlagala vzajemni senat za kontinuiteto brez pooblastila, da podeli državljanstvo, prestavi mejo ali ukaže ministrstvu. Vsaka država bi imenovala enega sodnika in enega matičarja. Družine bi vstopale le s privolitvijo. Senat bi lahko potrdil omejeno verigo dejstev; vsako nacionalno sodišče bi posebej odločilo, katera uporaba tega dejstva je zakonita.
