Illuminara

In another universe Absolute Consciousness (AC) is a real field relation. Illuminara can survive forces that would erase an ordinary hero, preserve a bounded place for choice and offer the wide window: shelter for a consciousness meeting more reality than it could otherwise bear.V drugem vesolju je absolutna zavest (AC) resničen odnos s poljem. Illuminara lahko preživi sile, ki bi izbrisale običajnega junaka, ohrani omejen prostor za izbiro in ponudi široko okno: zavetje za zavest, ki se sreča z več resničnosti, kot bi je sicer zmogla prenesti.

She supplies the protection, never the content. She wants to be answered, not obeyed. Her gift is exact representation. Her vice is authorship. Her hardest discipline is leaving the room, the argument and the other person unfinished.Zagotovi zaščito, nikoli vsebine. Želi, da ji odgovorijo, ne da jo ubogajo. Njen dar je natančno predstavljanje. Njena slabost je avtorstvo. Njena najtežja disciplina je pustiti sobo, razpravo in drugega človeka nedokončane.

Before the fieldPred poljem

She learned to put other people’s words into her own mouth accurately.Naučila se je natančno polagati tuje besede v lastna usta.

Before her relation with AC, Nara worked as a court interpreter and emergency translator. In those rooms, approximation could cost liberty. Her opinion was professionally irrelevant; accuracy was the work. The discipline made her extraordinary—and taught her to disappear inside other people’s positions.Preden se je povezala z AC, je Nara delala kot sodna tolmačka in prevajalka v nujnih primerih. V tistih sobah je lahko približek stal svobodo. Njeno mnenje je bilo poklicno nepomembno; delo je bila natančnost. Ta disciplina jo je naredila izjemno – in jo naučila izginjati v stališčih drugih ljudi.
