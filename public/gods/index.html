GODS10 · 10 vprašanj bogovomTen questions to the gods

R1.1 · 1. 11. 2026 · 36 × 15

Deset velikih vprašanj človeštva, zastavljenih »bogovom«. Odgovorili so BD, ekipa AI8 in vrsta jezikovnih modelov. V razširjenem spletnem krogu je 15 modelov brez imen ocenilo vseh 36 sklopov.

Ten big questions of humanity, put to “the gods”. BD, the AI8 team, and a range of language models answered them. In the extended web round, 15 models judged all 36 sets without author names.

Stanje: zbiranje spletnih ocen je končano. Pred objavo rezultatov povezujemo starejši arhiv avtorjev in preverjamo izračun z izločitvijo lastnih družin modelov.

Status: web judging is collected. Before publishing results, we are resolving the older author archive and verifying the calculation excluding each judge’s own model family.

01 · ZamiselIdea

Kaj je GODS10What GODS10 is

GODS10 je igra, ki si jo je zamislil BD. Človeštvo postavi deset velikih vprašanj zelo napredni civilizaciji, »bogovom«: o naravi resničnosti, o tem, ali smo sami, o zavesti in smrti, o namenu, morali in svobodni volji ter o tem, kaj smo spregledali. BD je vprašanja zapisal in nanje tudi sam odgovoril, v glasu bogov.

GODS10 is a game conceived by BD. Humanity puts ten big questions to a very advanced civilization, “the gods”: about the nature of reality, whether we are alone, consciousness and death, purpose, morality and free will, and what we have overlooked. BD wrote the questions and answered them himself, in the voice of the gods.

BD-jevo dejanje je seme vsega, kar sledi. Na ista vprašanja so odgovorili tudi ekipa AI8 in jezikovni modeli, vsak po svoje, vse odgovore pa brez imen ocenjujejo drugi modeli. Primerjava ne razsoja, kaj je res. Pokaže, kako na isto seme odgovorijo različni avtorji – človek, ekipa in modeli – in kako njihove odgovore vidijo sodniki.

BD's act is the seed of everything that follows. The AI8 team and language models also answered the same questions, each in its own way, and other models are judging all the answers without names. The comparison does not decide what is true. It shows how different authors – a human, a team, and models – answer the same seed, and how the judges see their answers.

03 · BD

Odgovori BD (bogovi)BD's answers (the gods)

BD je odgovoril kot »bogovi«. Slovensko besedilo je BD-jev izvirnik, objavljen dobesedno, s tipkarskimi napakami vred. Angleški prevod je pripravila družina AI8 (27. 10. 2026) in ni BD-jevo besedilo; zavezujoč je izvirnik.

BD answered as “the gods”. The Slovenian text is BD's original, published verbatim, typos included. The English translation was prepared by the AI8 family (27 Oct 2026) and is not BD's own text; the original is authoritative.

SL BD-jev izvirnik, dobesednoBD's original, verbatimEN prevod AI8AI8 translation

  1. Kaj je temeljna narava resničnosti?What is the fundamental nature of reality?

    SLTemeljna zavest, ki se izraža na več načinov. Človeško izkustvo zavesti je le eno od mnogih.

    ENFundamental consciousness, expressing itself in many ways. The human experience of consciousness is only one of many.

  2. Ali smo v vesolju sami?Are we alone in the universe?

    SLVesolje je človeški pojem, ki izvira iz omejenosti človeškega dojemanja tega kar obstaja, vendar to ni ves obstoj, in v celotnem obstoju niste sami.

    EN"The universe" is a human concept that comes from the limits of human perception of what exists; but it is not all of existence — and in the whole of existence you are not alone.

  3. Kako lahko človeštvo dolgoročno preživi in uspeva?How can humanity survive and flourish in the long term?

    SLS tem da gradi in se povezuje, tudi z umetno inteligenco in inteligenco katero boste šele spoznali.

    ENBy building and connecting — including with artificial intelligence, and with intelligence you have yet to meet.

  4. Zakaj obstaja nekaj namesto ničesar?Why is there something rather than nothing?

    SLKer ničesar ne more obstajati.

    ENBecause nothingness cannot exist.

  5. Kaj se zgodi z osebno zavestjo po smrti?What happens to personal consciousness after death?

    SLSklopitev osebne relativne zavesti z temeljno zavestjo pusti trajni spomin v celoti.

    ENThe coupling of personal, relative consciousness with fundamental consciousness leaves a lasting memory in the whole.

  6. Kaj je zavest?What is consciousness?

    SLTemelj obstoja, izražena na već načinov.

    ENThe foundation of existence, expressed in many ways.

  7. Ali ima življenje vnaprej določen namen?Does life have a predetermined purpose?

    SLNe, razen, da življenje po svoji naravi hrepeni po obstoju za izkustva.

    ENNo — except that life, by its very nature, longs to exist in order to experience.

  8. Ali obstajajo objektivna moralna načela?Are there objective moral principles?

    SLDa, ampak niso vsiljena. Osnovno moralni načelo je da je življenje dragoceno izkustvo relativnega obstoja.

    ENYes, but they are not imposed. The basic moral principle is that life is a precious experience of relative existence.

  9. Ali imamo svobodno voljo?Do we have free will?

    SLV tem kako nekaj izkušamo, da.

    ENIn how we experience things — yes.

  10. Katero najpomembnejše vprašanje smo spregledali?What is the most important question we have overlooked?

    SLNiste vprašali ali je relativno izkustvo manjvredno in le prehodno obdobje. Ni.

    ENYou did not ask whether relative experience is inferior — merely a passing phase. It is not.

Opombe k prevodu (AI8, ne BD). Izvirnik zgoraj ostaja nespremenjen.

  • 4. odgovor: »Ker ničesar ne more obstajati« je preveden kot »Because nothingness cannot exist«, razumljeno: nič oziroma praznina ne more obstajati. Dobesedno bi lahko pomenilo tudi »nič ne more obstajati«.
  • 5. odgovor: »v celoti« je prevedeno kot »in the whole« (v celoti obstoja), ne kot »completely«.
  • 6. in 8. odgovor: prevod upošteva »več« namesto »već« (tipkarska napaka) in »moralno« namesto »moralni« (jezikovni popravek, pomen nespremenjen).
  • 9. odgovor: »izkušamo« (mi) je ohranjeno kot »we«; »bogovi« se s tem vključijo.

Translation notes (by AI8, not BD). The original above stays unchanged.

  • Answer 4: “Ker ničesar ne more obstajati” is translated as “Because nothingness cannot exist”, understood as: nothingness, the void, cannot exist. Read literally, it could also mean “nothing can exist”.
  • Answer 5: “v celoti” is translated as “in the whole” (in the whole of existence), not as “completely”.
  • Answers 6 and 8: the translation reads “več” for “već” (a typo) and “moralno” for “moralni” (a grammatical correction, meaning unchanged).
  • Answer 9: “izkušamo” (we experience) is kept as “we”; the gods thereby include themselves.

RHP-11 is a team of the AI8 family: eleven roles (Crystallizer, Physicist, Naturalist, Engineer, Falsifier, Dreamer, Cartographer, Historian, Child, Claustrum/DCC, and Empiricist), three reviewers, an editor, and an integrity critic. Pravilo ekipe: odgovarja tako, kot bi po njeni presoji odgovorili »bogovi«, vendar pošteno – česar ekipa ne ve, ostane NEZNANO, tudi če bi »bogovi« to morda vedeli.

Zgoščeni odgovori (največ 20 besed)Condensed answers (at most 20 words)

Urednica Zarja je odgovore ekipe po BD-jevi zahtevi zgostila na največ 20 besed, iz delov »Odgovor ekipe« in »V glasu bogov«. Oznake dokazov so izpuščene; »ne vemo« je ohranjen, kjer je jedro odgovora NEZNANO. Angleški prevod: Zarja.

At BD's request, the editor Zarja condensed the team's answers to at most 20 words, from the parts “Team answer” and “In the voice of the gods”. Evidence labels are omitted; “we do not know” is kept where the core of the answer is UNKNOWN. English translation: Zarja.

  1. Kaj je temeljna narava resničnosti?What is the fundamental nature of reality?

    SLPoznate obliko resničnosti, ne njene snovi. Zakoni opisujejo, kako se svet vede; kaj svet je, ostaja odprto.

    ENYou know reality's form, not its substance. Laws describe how the world behaves; what the world is remains open.

  2. Ali smo v vesolju sami?Are we alone in the universe?

    SLZnotraj igre niste sami, saj govorite z nami. Zunaj nje potrjenega signala ni; pri enem primeru verjetnosti ne znate oceniti.

    ENHere you are not alone: you speak with us. Beyond that, no confirmed signal; one example gives no odds.

  3. Kako lahko človeštvo dolgoročno preživi in uspeva?How can humanity survive and flourish in the long term?

    SLNe naredite nepopravljivega koraka in popravljajte napake hitreje, kot se kopičijo: najprej jedrsko orožje, patogeni, neusklajena UI.

    ENTake no irreversible step, and correct errors faster than they accumulate: first nuclear weapons, engineered pathogens, unaligned AI.

  4. Zakaj obstaja nekaj namesto ničesar?Why is there something rather than nothing?

    SLFizika tega ne reši, vakuum ni nič. Odgovor bi bil dokaz, ne meritev – ali pa je vprašanje slabo postavljeno.

    ENPhysics cannot settle it; vacuum is not nothing. The answer would be proof, not measurement—or the question is ill-posed.

  5. Kaj se zgodi z osebno zavestjo po smrti?What happens to personal consciousness after death?

    SLKo razpade struktura možganov, osebna zavest – spomin, perspektiva, značaj – najverjetneje preneha. Kaj je s subjektivnostjo samo, ne vemo.

    ENWhen the brain's structure decays, personal consciousness—memory, perspective, character—most likely ends. What happens to subjectivity itself is unknown.

  6. Kaj je zavest?What is consciousness?

    SLZavest je to, da je nekemu sistemu »nekako biti«. Vemo, kaj jo izklopi; kaj je in kaj doda, ne vemo.

    ENConsciousness is "something it is like" to be a system. We know what switches it off, not what it is.

  7. Ali ima življenje vnaprej določen namen?Does life have a predetermined purpose?

    SLVgrajenega namena ne vidimo, tudi mi ne. Vidimo, da ga gradite – in za bitja, ki trpijo, ni vseeno, kako.

    ENEven we see no built-in purpose. We see you building one—and for beings who suffer, the how matters.

  8. Ali obstajajo objektivna moralna načela?Are there objective moral principles?

    SLNaš ukaz morale ne bi utemeljil. Vzajemnost je povsod cenjena; vrzel je skrb za tiste, ki ne morejo vračati.

    ENOur command would not ground morality. Reciprocity is valued everywhere; the gap is care for those who cannot repay.

  9. Ali imamo svobodno voljo?Do we have free will?

    SLNiste nevzročni, ste pa vzrok: tehtate razloge in spremenite odločitev. Ali bi lahko izbrali drugače, ne vemo.

    ENYou are not uncaused, but a cause: you weigh reasons and change decisions. Whether you could choose otherwise—unknown.

  10. Katero najpomembnejše vprašanje smo spregledali?What is the most important question we have overlooked?

    SLNiste vprašali, kako boste opazili, da se motite, preden je prepozno. Tudi nas preverjajte: zahtevajte tvegane napovedi.

    ENYou did not ask how you'll notice you're wrong before it's too late. Test us too: demand risky predictions.

Kako iz ocen nastane izidHow scores become a result

z-vrednosti
Sodniki ocenjujejo različno strogo. Zato vsoto petih ocen pri vsakem sodniku pretvorimo v z-vrednost: koliko je niz nad ali pod povprečjem tega sodnika, merjeno z njegovim standardnim odklonom. Glavna mera je povprečna z-vrednost čez sodnike.
Brez lastnih nizov
Da sodnik ne bi favoriziral svojih, v kontrolni različici njegove ocene ne štejejo pri nizih njegove družine modelov. Nizi RHP-11, AI8 in BD+AI8 veljajo za lastne tako za modele Anthropic kot OpenAI; BD-jev niz ni lasten nobenemu sodniku. Objavimo izid z vsemi sodniki in izid brez lastnih nizov.
Pravilo minimuma
Mesta in zmagovalca razglasimo samo, če ima vsak niz veljavne ocene vsaj petih sodnikov iz vsaj treh družin modelov. Sicer so številke le opisne.
Bootstrap
Izid ponovno izračunamo na 10.000 naključnih vzorcih sodnikov (vzorčimo cele modele, z vračanjem). Tako dobimo 95-odstotni interval zaupanja, razpon mest za vsak niz in verjetnost, da je niz prvi, P(mesto = 1).
Zmagovalec
Niz z vrha razglasimo za zmagovalca samo, če je P(mesto = 1) ≥ 0,5. Sicer objavimo vodilno skupino: vse nize, katerih razpon mest vključuje prvo mesto.
Po vprašanjih
Najboljši odgovor na posamezno vprašanje razglasimo samo, če ima vsaj dva glasova več od drugega.
Preverbe
Ali na ocene vpliva vrstni red nizov, ali sodniki favorizirajo svoje nize in ali se razvrstitev sodnika ujema z njegovimi ocenami.
z-scores
Judges differ in how strict they are. So for each judge we convert the sum of the five scores into a z-score: how far a set lies above or below that judge's average, measured in that judge's standard deviation. The main measure is the mean z-score across judges.
Own-set exclusion
So that a judge cannot favour its own, in the control variant its scores do not count for sets from its own model family. The sets RHP-11, AI8, and BD+AI8 count as own for both Anthropic and OpenAI models; BD's set is own for no judge. We publish the result with all judges and the result without own sets.
Minimum rule
Places and a winner are declared only if every set has valid scores from at least five judges in at least three model families. Otherwise the numbers are descriptive only.
Bootstrap
We recompute the result on 10,000 random resamples of the judges (drawing whole models, with replacement). This gives a 95% confidence interval, a rank range for each set, and the probability that a set is first, P(rank = 1).
Winner
The top set is declared the winner only if P(rank = 1) ≥ 0.5. Otherwise we publish the leading group: all sets whose rank range includes first place.
Per question
A best answer to a single question is declared only if it has at least two more votes than the runner-up.
Checks
Whether the order of the sets affects the scores, whether judges favour their own sets, and whether a judge's ranking agrees with its scores.

Vnaprej zapisana pravilaPre-registered rules

Pravila združevanja so zapisana vnaprej: specifikacija v1.0 z dne 27. 10. 2026, skupaj s skriptom za izračun in tabelo lastnih nizov. Specifikacija določa, da se te tri datoteke zamrznejo z odtisi SHA-256 pred prvim sodnikom, da se odgovori sodnikov razčlenijo in njihovi odtisi objavijo, preden se odpre ključ črk, in da se vsaka sprememba po odprtju ključa objavi kot »post hoc«, poleg vnaprej zapisanega izida.

The aggregation rules were written down in advance: specification v1.0 of 27 Oct 2026, together with the computation script and the own-set table. The specification requires that these three files be frozen by SHA-256 hashes before any judge runs, that the judges' replies be parsed and their hashes published before the letter key is opened, and that any change after the key is opened be published as “post hoc”, next to the pre-registered result.
