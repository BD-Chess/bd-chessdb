The time says many things

The Story

A Rock Opera From the Edge of Europe

"The time says many things" is a concept album — every song is a chapter in a single story. Richard kills his girlfriend Sharon and her lover. His father Antonio and brother Angelo confront the aftermath: prison, execution, grief, war, and the crushing weight of choices made in greed. The narrative moves between English dialogue and Slovenian stage directions, creating something between a rock opera and a theatrical performance.

"The characters, places and incidents described in 'The time says many things' are product of SOMEONE's imagination."

About This Album

"The time says many things" is a concept album by Slovenian band SOMEONE, released in 1994 on Pan Records. Recorded at mountain Studio Femix in Bovec, Slovenia, between October 1993 and May 1994.

"The characters, places and incidents described in 'The time says many things' are product of SOMEONE's imagination."
