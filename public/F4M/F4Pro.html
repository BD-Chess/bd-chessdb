How to Play

Goal: Connect 4 tokens in a line.
Gravity: Tokens always fall DOWN. (The board rotates!)
Pro Mode:
Flood (💧): Fills board with water for 4 turns. Gravity REVERSES (tokens float up).
Firebrand (🔥): Destroys the first token it touches.
Note: If Flooded, Fire sizzles and becomes a stone.
