Clip FLAC-12 OFR OFR vs FLAC 8ZA 8ZA vs FLAC

OptimFROG — the codec with the best compression ratios on nearly every other test — consistently fails on Rammstein's industrial sound. Heavy distortion + low-frequency content defeats its multi-layer predictor. This is unprecedented in lossless audio benchmarks. 8ZA also struggles (Rammstein is v1.5's worst genre), but not as catastrophically as OFR.

Feature FLAC (23 years) 8Z-Audio (5 days) Impact
LPC order range 0–12 1–32 Captures longer correlations in tonal content
Apodization windows 1 (or subdivide_tukey) 3 (hann, tukey, none) Better autocorrelation estimates
QLP precision Fixed per preset Search 8–16 Optimal quantization per subframe
Candidates evaluated ~13 ~600 (DCC-gated) 46× more choices, MDL picks the cheapest
Signal intelligence None Scanner + DCC + CodecLearner Understands signal before compressing it
Budget adaptation None DCC (explore ↔ exploit) Spends time where it helps most
