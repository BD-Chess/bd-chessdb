8zNASIskanje nevronskih arhitektur in strukturNeural Architecture Search & Structural Search

Kako najti dobre arhitekture in konfiguracije, ne da bi morali drago ovrednotiti vsako možnost? 8zNAS raziskuje strukturo prostora rešitev in načine, kako znanje o njem usmerja naslednje iskalne korake.How can we find good architectures and configurations without expensively evaluating every option? 8zNAS studies the structure of search spaces and how knowledge about them guides the next search steps.

01

Ne samo iskanje arhitekturBeyond architecture search

Projekt izhaja iz raziskav Neural Architecture Search iz aprila 2026. Modularna arena predstavlja legalne nevronske grafe, uporablja senzorje in zakone iskanja ter primerja različne načine navigacije. Širši cilj je odkrivanje prenosljivih mehanizmov za zahtevne konfiguracijske probleme.The project builds on Neural Architecture Search research from April 2026. The modular arena represents legal neural graphs, uses sensors and search laws, and compares navigation policies. Its broader aim is transferable mechanisms for difficult configuration problems.

03

Zgodovinski rezultati in njihove mejeHistorical results and their limits

V zgodovinskem v19 preizkusu nad NAS-Bench-101 je arena analizirala prostor 423.624 arhitektur. Ugotovitve podpirajo nadaljnje raziskovanje strukturnih pogledov, vendar ne dokazujejo splošne premoči iskalnika.The historical v19 experiment used the NAS-Bench-101 space of 423,624 architectures. The findings motivate further study of structural views but do not establish general search superiority.

05

GCG in ZborGCG and the council

GPT, Claude in Gemini delujejo v ločenih raziskovalnih sejah za 8zNAS in 8zLLM. Širši Zbor pomaga razvijati alternative, iskati protiprimere in načrtovati meritve. Cilj je dokazati vrednost mehanizma, ne zmagati z imenom.GPT, Claude and Gemini work in separate research sessions for 8zNAS and 8zLLM. A broader council helps develop alternatives, find counterexamples and plan measurements. The aim is to establish the value of a mechanism, not to win by name.
