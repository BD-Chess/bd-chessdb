Iz surove baze NAS-Bench-101 smo brez uporabe pickle ekstrahirali vseh 1.270.872 zapisov (423.624 kanoničnih DAG-ov × 3 semena × epohe 54 in 108) ter do šestih decimalk natančno pojasnili zgodovinske anomalije v19: v večprocesnem načinu (Windows spawn) so delavci prvega dela Phase B ostali v privzetem 6-dimenzionalnem prostoru NATS-201 (prvih 15.625 grafov, povprečni začetek 91,251336 %, maksimum podmnožice 94,668137 %), medtem ko je enoprocesni nadaljevalni tek preiskoval celotni prostor NB101 (423.624 grafov, povprečni začetek 87,757300 %). Iz te ene same statistike reda (94,668137 % na 15.625 grafih) je RHP Krog 3 teoretično napovedal, da je v celotnem prostoru 423.624 grafov nad mejo 94,65 % le ~27,1 arhitektur (~13,94 bita lokacijske informacije). Neposredni popis vseh 423.624 grafov v Rust jedru v23 je napoved potrdil do ene same arhitekture natančno: nad 94,6500 % je natanko 26 arhitektur (13,99 bita).We extracted all 1,270,872 records (423,624 canonical DAGs × 3 seeds × epochs 54 and 108) directly from the raw NAS-Bench-101 TFRecord without pickle and resolved the historical v19 anomalies to six decimal places: under Windows spawn multiprocessing, Phase B workers defaulted to the 6D NATS-201 space (first 15,625 graphs, mean start 91.251336%, subset maximum 94.668137%), whereas single-worker resume runs searched the full NB101 space (423,624 graphs, mean start 87.757300%). From that single order statistic (94.668137% over 15,625 graphs), RHP Round 3 predicted theoretically that the full 423,624-graph space contains ~27.1 architectures (~13.94 location bits) above 94.65%. An exhaustive scan of all 423,624 graphs in Rust v23 confirmed the prediction to within a single architecture: exactly 26 architectures (13.99 bits) exceed 94.6500%.

BD-4 MORPHIC · E = 9 TUNNEL

Morfično tuneliranje čez zaporo E = 9Morphic tunneling across the E = 9 barrier

Elitni grafi v NB101 imajo polnih E = 9 robov; 1-bitni korak mora najprej izbrisati rob (padec v dolino E = 8). Atomarni 2-edit M1_EdgeSwap in Partition-Crossover presaditev motiva preskočita dolino pri stalnem proračunu robov.Elite NB101 graphs have a full E = 9 edges; a 1-bit move must first delete an edge (dropping into an E = 8 valley). Atomic 2-edit M1_EdgeSwap and Partition-Crossover motif transplants tunnel across the valley at constant edge count.

DRAIN · H-D1 & H-D1-T

Transportirano glajenje šuma (13,98×)Transported noise shrinkage (13.98×)

V kombinaciji s skupnimi naključnimi bloki (CRN) in surogatnim transportom zmanjša varianco kalibracije za 13,98-krat.Across 1,200 near-tie panels (ε = 0.10 pp), H-D1 beats POINT (+2.17 SE) and SHAM (+4.78 SE). Combined with Common Random Numbers (CRN) and surrogate control-variate transport, it reduces calibration variance by 13.98×.

Results confirm the theoretical crossover at K_c = 2 where non-additive energy exceeds 50%.
