How to Play

Goal: Connect 4 of your color (Red) in a line horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

Physics: Gravity pulls pieces down. But you can change gravity!

  • Drop: Click an empty slot to drop a piece (it falls based on gravity).
  • Turn (Rotate): Spend 1 Flip to rotate the board 90°. All unpinned pieces fall!
  • Magnet: Spend 1 Magnet to PIN a piece. It will stick to the board and never move, even if gravity changes.

Tap anywhere to close.

How to Play

Goal: Connect 4 tokens in a line (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal).
Gravity: Tokens fall towards the current gravity direction (Arrow in HUD).
Turn - Drop: Tap any cell to drop a token.
Turn - Rotate: Use ⟲ ⟳ buttons to rotate the board 90°. Costs 1 Flip Token.
Turn - Magnet: Tap the rim (edge) of the board to place a magnet. Pulls tokens to that side. Costs 1 Magnet Token. Lasts 3 rounds.
