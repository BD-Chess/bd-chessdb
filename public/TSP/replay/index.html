Two runs of the 8zTSP demo, recorded in a browser on our laptop. This page only plays them back: every tour the search found, in the order it found them, with its length and the solver time. Nothing is computed here, so it works on any phone. Open a recording below: it plays slowly, and you can pause it, go step by step and look closer.Dva teka demo različice 8zTSP, posneta v brskalniku na našem prenosniku. Ta stran ju le predvaja: vsako pot, ki jo je iskanje našlo, po vrsti, z dolžino in časom reševalnika. Tu se nič ne računa, zato deluje na vsakem telefonu. Spodaj odprite posnetek: predvaja se počasi, lahko pa ga ustavite, greste po korakih in si ga ogledate od blizu.
