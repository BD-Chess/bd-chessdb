More about the testVeč o preizkusu

We described the measured properties of 8zTSP to six leading language models, each in a new conversation, without BD’s ideas and without code, and asked whether they know such a program and whether they can write it. All six answered that they know of no such program. Three wrote a program: after five minutes on one core their tours were 0.3 % to 19 % above the optimum, and in 18 runs none reached the optimum; 8zTSP reaches it on the same two maps in every run. Three wrote that they cannot make a program with such properties. Each model had one attempt.Šestim vodilnim jezikovnim modelom smo v novem pogovoru, brez BD-jevih zamisli in brez kode, opisali izmerjene lastnosti 8zTSP in jih vprašali, ali tak program poznajo in ali ga znajo napisati. Vseh šest je odgovorilo, da takega programa ne poznajo. Trije so program napisali: po petih minutah na enem jedru so bile njihove poti od 0,3 % do 19 % nad optimumom in v 18 tekih nobena ni dosegla optimuma; 8zTSP ga na istih dveh zemljevidih doseže v vsakem teku. Trije so zapisali, da programa s takimi lastnostmi ne morejo narediti. Vsak model je imel en poskus.

One engine for the whole computer: in a browser, even on a phone; on one laptop up to 10 million cities.En motor za ves računalnik: v brskalniku, tudi na telefonu; na enem prenosniku do 10 milijonov mest.

The travelling salesman problem (TSP) asks for the shortest round trip that visits every city on a map exactly once. It is easy to state and gets very hard as the number of cities grows.Problem potujočega trgovca (TSP) išče najkrajšo krožno pot, ki vsako mesto na zemljevidu obišče natanko enkrat. Postaviti ga je lahko, z rastočim številom mest pa postane zelo težak.

With its own view of the map, 8zTSP puts 7 of the laptop’s 8 cores to work on one map at once. The standard programs, LKH and Concorde’s linkern, use one core per run: on the same laptop, with the settings used, neither got there in two hours.Z lastnim pogledom na zemljevid 8zTSP na enem zemljevidu hkrati zaposli 7 od 8 jeder prenosnika. Standardna programa, LKH in Concordov linkern, uporabljata eno jedro na tek: na istem prenosniku in z uporabljenimi nastavitvami v dveh urah do tja ni prišel nobeden od njiju.

More on nu3496Več o nu3496

Concorde, on one core of the same laptop, proved the optimum in a median of 70 min of processor time (three seeds: 55, 70 and 149 min). 8zTSP finds the optimal tour; Concorde proves it is optimal.Concorde je na enem jedru istega prenosnika optimum dokazal v mediani 70 min procesorskega časa (tri semena: 55, 70 in 149 min). 8zTSP optimalno pot najde, Concorde pa dokaže, da je optimalna.

On the shoulders ofNa ramenih

  • The TSP pages of the University of Waterloo (William Cook and colleagues): the national maps such as nu3496, the World TSP and the history of the problem.Strani o TSP Univerze Waterloo (William Cook s sodelavci): nacionalni zemljevidi, kot je nu3496, World TSP in zgodovina problema.
  • Concorde (Applegate, Bixby, Chvátal, Cook): the exact solver that proves a tour optimal; on our laptop it proved the optimum of nu3496 in a median of 70 minutes of processor time (three seeds: 55 to 149 minutes).Concorde (Applegate, Bixby, Chvátal, Cook): natančni reševalnik, ki dokaže, da je pot optimalna; na našem prenosniku je optimum nu3496 dokazal v mediani 70 minut procesorskega časa (tri semena: od 55 do 149 minut).
  • LKH (Keld Helsgaun): the Lin–Kernighan–Helsgaun heuristic, the yardstick for large maps.LKH (Keld Helsgaun): hevristika Lin–Kernighan–Helsgaun, merilo za velike zemljevide.
  • TSPLIB (Gerhard Reinelt): the classic collection of test maps, and the file format our demo reads.TSPLIB (Gerhard Reinelt): klasična zbirka preizkusnih zemljevidov in oblika datotek, ki jo bere naš demo.
  • The DIMACS TSP Challenge (David Johnson, Lyle McGeoch): the random maps E100k.0 to E10M.0 and their Held–Karp bounds.DIMACS TSP Challenge (David Johnson, Lyle McGeoch): naključni zemljevidi od E100k.0 do E10M.0 in njihove meje Held–Karp.

How to read our numbersKako brati naše številke

  • Every number has its conditions next to it: one laptop (Intel i7-10875H, 8 cores), how many threads, which build and which day.Vsaka številka ima pogoje ob sebi: en prenosnik (Intel i7-10875H, 8 jeder), koliko niti, katera gradnja in kateri dan.
  • »Median of 10 runs« means that half of the runs were faster and half slower; some seeds take much longer, and we show them.»Mediana 10 tekov« pomeni, da je bila polovica tekov hitrejša in polovica počasnejša; nekatera semena trajajo precej dlje, in jih pokažemo.
  • »The optimal tour« only where the optimum is known (nu3496: 96,132, proved by Concorde). 8zTSP finds the optimal tour; Concorde proves that it is optimal.»Optimalna pot« le tam, kjer je optimum znan (nu3496: 96.132, dokazal ga je Concorde). 8zTSP optimalno pot najde, Concorde pa dokaže, da je optimalna.
  • On big random maps no optimum is known: we give how far a tour is above the Held–Karp bound, a lower limit that no tour can go below (for 10,000,000 cities an estimate of it).Na velikih naključnih zemljevidih optimum ni znan: navedemo, za koliko je pot nad mejo Held–Karp, spodnjo mejo, pod katero ne more nobena pot (za 10.000.000 mest njeno oceno).
  • LKH and Concorde ran on the same laptop, with the settings named next to each result; better settings are welcome and would be measured the same way.LKH in Concorde sta tekla na istem prenosniku, z nastavitvami, navedenimi ob vsakem izidu; boljše nastavitve so dobrodošle in bi jih izmerili na enak način.
  • These are development results: they can change, and the lab log keeps every earlier figure with its date.To so razvojni izidi: lahko se spremenijo, laboratorijski dnevnik pa hrani vsako prejšnjo številko z datumom.
  • You can check our tours: the downloads have our tours of nu3496, Slovenia and E1M.0, with SHA-256 sums and a short verifier in Python.Naše poti lahko preveriš: med prenosi so naše poti za nu3496, Slovenijo in E1M.0, s kontrolnimi vsotami SHA-256 in kratkim preverjevalnikom v Pythonu.

Who made itKdo je to naredil

BD × AI Lab. Our own view of the map is BD’s idea, first built by Claude in March 2026, then implemented by GPT. Known methods such as the edge assembly crossover (EAX) of Nagata and Kobayashi run inside. Behind it is BD’s way of working with LLMs: the Mental Arena Lab, the RHP protocol and AI8, the team of AI sessions that built and measured 8zTSP.BD × AI Lab.
