With its zoom windows, 8zTSP puts 7 of the laptop’s 8 cores to work on one map at once. The standard programs, LKH and Concorde’s linkern, use one core per run: on the same laptop, with the settings used, neither got there in two hours.Z okni zooma 8zTSP na enem zemljevidu hkrati zaposli 7 od 8 jeder prenosnika. Standardna programa, LKH in Concordov linkern, uporabljata eno jedro na tek: na istem prenosniku in z uporabljenimi nastavitvami v dveh urah do tja ni prišel nobeden od njiju.

The optimal tour of nu3496 in about 6 secondsOptimalna pot za nu3496 v približno 6 sekundah

With 7 threads on our laptop, all 10 runs found it (median 6.1 s, build of 15 Nov). For comparison, one run on one core of the same laptop: LKH 3.0.7 did not find it in 6 hours; Concorde found the optimum after 148 min and proved it after 169 min. 8zTSP finds the optimal tour; Concorde proves it is optimal.S 7 nitmi na našem prenosniku ga je našlo vseh 10 tekov (mediana 6,1 s, gradnja 15. novembra). Za primerjavo en tek na enem jedru istega prenosnika: LKH 3.0.7 ga v 6 urah ni našel; Concorde je optimum našel po 148 min in ga dokazal po 169 min. 8zTSP optimalno pot najde, Concorde pa dokaže, da je optimalna.

Who made itKdo je to naredil

BD × AI Lab. The zoom in and out is BD’s idea; GPT implemented it as the integer bitshift pyramid. TEP, the tour-enriched pyramid, was made by Claude C1 in a session BD prepared, free of old TSP knowledge, after it saw BD’s zoom in and out. Known methods such as the edge assembly crossover (EAX) of Nagata and Kobayashi run inside. Behind it is BD’s way of working with LLMs: the Mental Arena Lab, the RHP protocol and AI8, the team of AI sessions that built and measured 8zTSP.BD × AI Lab. Zoom noter in ven je BD‑jeva zamisel; GPT jo je izvedel kot celoštevilsko bitshift piramido. TEP, piramido, obogateno s turami, je naredil Claude C1 v seji, ki jo je BD odtujil od okov starega znanja o TSP, ko je videla BD‑jev zoom noter in ven. V jedru tečejo tudi znane metode, na primer križanje s sestavljanjem robov (EAX) Nagate in Kobayashija. Za tem je BD‑jev način dela z velikimi jezikovnimi modeli: Mental Arena Lab, protokol RHP in AI8, ekipa sej umetne inteligence, ki je 8zTSP zgradila in izmerila.
