Where the engine of 8zTSP can helpKje lahko pomaga motor 8zTSP

Ten million cities is a load test, not a customer: it shows that the engine does not break under mass. What can travel to other fields is the way it spends its work: our own view of the map, our own recursive analysis of the view, and few, well-chosen steps. Below is where that could help. Each claim in a new field needs one measurement there first; none is measured yet.Deset milijonov mest je obremenilni preizkus, ne naročnik: pokaže, da se motor pod množico ne zlomi.     Our own lower bound: a certificate for every mapLastna spodnja meja: potrdilo za vsak zemljevid

    8zTSP’s own code can now show how much longer a tour is, at most, than the shortest possible one (the optimum), even where nobody knows the optimum: our best tour of Slovenia’s 6,035 settlements is at most 0.48% longer than the optimum by our own bound.Lastna koda 8zTSP zdaj zna pokazati, za koliko je pot največ daljša od najkrajše možne (optimuma), tudi kjer optimuma nihče ne pozna: naša najboljša pot skozi 6.035 naselij Slovenije je največ 0,48 % daljša od optimuma po naši lastni meji. Such a checked statement of a tour’s quality is a certificate; it rests on a lower bound, a length that no round trip on the map can go below, and our code computes one for any map of up to 20,000 cities. On wi29 and dj38 the bound equals the length of our tour, so our own code proves those tours optimal. The method is the classic Held–Karp bound (Held and Karp, 1970 and 1971), with step rules after Held, Wolfe and Crowder (1974) and Volgenant and Jonker (1982). GoalCilj

A human layman and frontier LLMs (BD × AI Lab) first developed our own ideas without the existing solvers (our own view of the map, our addition to it, MDL × DCC). We are now combining them with the best solutions in the world (LKH as the local search inside our analysis of the view, EAX and other methods from the literature) into the hybrid 8zTSP. The goal is to show that on an ordinary laptop, on uniformly random instances with 1, 3.16 and 10 million cities, the hybrid reaches a good tour several times faster than LKH 3.0.7 and Concorde's linkern on the same computer. This is a goal, not a measured result: a factor will be stated only after the full test under a locked protocol (several seeds, reserved confirmation instances, an independent review).Človeški laik in frontier LLM-ji (BD × AI Lab) smo najprej brez obstoječih rešitev razvili lastne zamisli (naš lastni pogled na zemljevid, naš dodatek k njemu, MDL × DCC). Zdaj jih združujemo z najboljšimi rešitvami sveta (LKH kot lokalno iskanje v naši analizi pogleda, EAX in druge metode iz literature) v hibrid 8zTSP. Cilj je pokazati, da na navadnem prenosniku pri enakomerno naključnih primerih z 1, 3,16 in 10 milijoni mest hibrid pride do dobre ture večkrat hitreje kot LKH 3.0.7 in Concordov linkern na istem računalniku. To je cilj, ne izmerjen izid: faktor bomo navedli šele po polnem preizkusu po zaklenjenem protokolu (več semen, rezervirani potrditveni primeri, neodvisen pregled).
