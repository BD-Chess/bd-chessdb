GA-EAX 1.0 Normal by Yuichi Nagata (MIT, gaeax/LICENSE.txt)
Source: https://github.com/nagata-yuichi/GA-EAX/tree/015dfbe9f267230f78787bd244af393ffc018900/GA_EAX_1.0/Normal
Browser glue: tests/fixtures/tsp-rivals/gaeax-web.cpp in the site repository.
Built using Emscripten 4.0.23. The adapter calls the upstream crossover, local
search, survivor selection and stopping rules with a browser deadline.
