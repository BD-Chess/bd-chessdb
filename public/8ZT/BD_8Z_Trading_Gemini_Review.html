4. Weaknesses & Hardening Points

No system is without physical friction. To complete the transition to a Tier-1 institutional stack, these realities must be addressed:

Thin-Book Slippage on Terminal Adds

While the paper trader charges a static 0.02% slippage, this model breaks down under the Leverage Paradox during a true black-swan wick. If the system scales a position to maximum capacity on Add #9 or #10, a market order executed in a depleted order book will suffer geometric slippage. Live deployment requires dynamic slippage modeling tied directly to real-time order book depth.

State Carryover and Regime Desync

As the system integrates multi-timeframe consensus, the management of state variables becomes a vulnerability. If the engines desync during a rapid regime shift (e.g., the WebSocket stutters, or the 3m array fails to align perfectly with the 1m execution loop), the logic could misfire. Absolute atomic resets are critical.
