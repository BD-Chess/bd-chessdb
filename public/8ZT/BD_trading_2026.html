How to read the result correctly

What v1.3c established

  • The real-1m replay path, matched controls, resume and extraction pipeline work.
  • A small robust frontier persisted across BTC, ETH and SOL under both cost models.
  • Re-anchor + mega-rescue + excess-close behavior can produce large simulated PnL.
  • Different risk lanes genuinely need different settings.

What it did not establish

  • Future or live profitability.
  • The minimum wallet required for every full path.
  • Rolling-window or endpoint robustness.
  • Real exchange liquidation, latency and fill behavior.
  • Winner-side pyramiding, staged exits or trailing.
Two inactive fields matter: the named sep... value was stored but not enforced by the v1.3c replay, and close_winner_on_mega was stored but not executed. They must not be presented as tested operational rules.
