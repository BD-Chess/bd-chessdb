Kaj gradimz LLM-ji in AI. What I buildwith LLMs and AI.

LLM-jev ne uporabljam predvsem kot klepetalnike, ampak kot sodelujočo raziskovalno in razvojno ekipo. Sam prispevam izvirna semena, povezave med področji, sistemsko arhitekturo, merila in končno presojo; modeli sodelujejo kot raziskovalci, programerji, kritiki, testerji in recenzenti.I do not use LLMs mainly as chatbots, but as a collaborating research and development team. I contribute the original seeds, cross-domain connections, system architecture, criteria and final judgment; models participate as researchers, programmers, critics, testers and reviewers.

Moja osrednja ideja je preprosta: več različnih predlogov naj tekmuje v poceni, merljivi areni; ohranimo tistega, ki najbolje stisne problem, pojasni ostanke in zdrži preverjanje.My central idea is simple: let several different proposals compete in a cheap, measurable arena; retain the one that best compresses the problem, explains the residuals and survives testing.

MDL×DCC

Metoda, ki problem obravnava kot kompresijo: ustvarimo več kandidatov, merimo korist in kompleksnost, pogledamo, česa ne pojasnijo, nato pa krmilnik odloči, kaj preživi. Isti vzorec uporabljam med zelo različnimi področji.A method that treats a problem as compression: generate candidates, measure utility and complexity, inspect what they fail to explain, then let a controller decide what survives. I reuse the pattern across very different domains.
