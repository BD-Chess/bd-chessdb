01 / Izvor, ki se spreminja01 / An evolving origin

Vprašanje je odprlo pot. Nova vprašanja so jo razvijala.A question opened the way. New questions shaped it.

Bojan Dobrečevič je izvorni pobudnik 8Z, arhitekt raziskovalnega okolja in trajni vsebinski soustvarjalec 8zC. Njegov prispevek se nadaljuje z novimi zamislimi, povezavami med področji, tehničnimi ugovori in merili, po katerih se presoja rezultat.Bojan Dobrečevič is the original initiator of 8Z, the architect of the research environment and an ongoing substantive co-creator of 8zC. His contribution continues through new ideas, cross-domain connections, technical objections and criteria by which the results are judged.

Izvor projekta je mogoče povedati v enem stavku: ali lahko del podatkov zamenjamo s krajšim matematičnim opisom, iz katerega jih nato natančno obnovimo? Toda tak stavek je samo začetek zgodbe. Med njim in delujočim kompresorjem so spremenjene hipoteze, neuspešni poskusi, nove domene, konkretni algoritmi in veliko dela pri preverjanju.The project's origin can be expressed in one sentence: can we replace part of the data with a shorter mathematical description from which it can be reconstructed exactly? But that sentence is only the beginning. Between it and a working compressor lie revised hypotheses, failed experiments, new domains, concrete algorithms and a great deal of verification work.

BD je skozi ta razvoj prispeval številna semena. AI sodelavci so dodajali svoje ideje, formalizacije, izvedbe, protiprimere in meritve. Za razumevanje nastanka programa je zato treba slediti temu, kako se ti prispevki srečujejo in spreminjajo drug drugega.Throughout this development, BD contributed numerous seeds. AI collaborators added their own ideas, formalizations, implementations, counterexamples and measurements. To understand how the program came about, we must follow how these contributions meet and change one another. [1]

Dve začetni poteziTwo initial moves

Prvo BD-jevo vprašanje je zadevalo π: ali lahko podatkovne bite v datoteki nadomestimo z referenco na bite, ki jih najdemo v tej deterministični konstanti? π je bil konkreten povod za širše vprašanje o matematičnih virih, generatorjih in kratkih receptih.BD's first question concerned π: could the bits of data in a file be replaced by a reference to bits found in this deterministic constant? π was a concrete starting point for a broader question about mathematical sources, generators and short recipes.

Druga poteza je bila lokalna izbira. Ni treba, da en mehanizem opiše celotno datoteko. Za vsak predel lahko tekmujejo neposreden zapis, običajna kompresija in regeneracija po pravilu. Smiselna je tista možnost, ki zahteva najmanjšiThe second move was local selection. A single mechanism need not describe the whole file. For each region, direct storage, conventional compression and regeneration from a rule can compete. The sensible choice is the one that requires the shortest celotni opiscomplete description in vrne natančno iste bajte.and returns exactly the same bytes.

To je projektna uporaba načela MDL,This is the project's application of the MDL principle, Minimum Description Length oziroma najkrajšega opisa. Teorija je obstajala že prej. BD-jev prispevek je v vprašanjih, načinu uporabe in razvoju tega merila znotraj njegovega raziskovalnega programa. Priznanje prispevka ne zahteva trditve o izumu splošne teorije.or the shortest description. The theory already existed. BD's contribution lies in the questions, the way it is applied and the development of that criterion within his research program. Recognizing the contribution does not require claiming invention of the general theory.

Vprašanje, ki ostaneThe question that remains

Kateri del podatkov moramo res poslati — in katerega lahko prejemnik natančno obnovi iz krajšega pravila?Which part of the data must we actually send — and which part can the recipient reconstruct exactly from a shorter rule?

Datum, ki ga je treba povedati natančnoA date that needs careful wording

RetrospektivaThe retrospective Origin prvo kompresijsko vprašanje umešča na 4. oktober 2024. Popis pregledanega tehničnega arhiva najstarejši ohranjeni dokument postavlja na 4. oktober 2025, z notranjo oznako oktober 2025. Starejši pogovor je lahko zunaj tega arhiva; mogoča je tudi datacijska napaka. Zato razlikujemo med datumom v osebni retrospektivi in datumom ohranjenega artefakta.places the first compression question on 4 October 2024. The inventory of the technical archive reviewed dates the oldest surviving document to 4 October 2025, with an internal October 2025 label. An earlier conversation may lie outside that archive; a dating error is also possible. We therefore distinguish the date in the personal retrospective from that of the surviving artifact. [1]

Širša osebna predzgodovina se v poznejših zapisih vrača do leta 1995, do razmišljanj o zavesti, koherenci in kompleksnosti. To je ozadje BD-jeve poti. Ne pomeni, da so bile vse poznejše formule ali tehnične rešitve dokončane že takrat. CFH v tej zgodbi ostaja hipoteza in konceptualni vir vprašanj.Later records trace a broader personal prehistory back to 1995, to reflections on consciousness, coherence and complexity. This is the background to BD's journey. It does not mean all the later formulas or technical solutions were completed then. In this story, CFH remains a hypothesis and a conceptual source of questions.

Ko poskus spremeni prvotno hipotezoWhen an experiment changes the original hypothesis

Začetna pot ni prinesla preproste zmage. Pi-Hunterjev navidezno poseben signal na sintetičnem primeru se je ohranil tudi po zamenjavi π s psevdonaključnimi semeni. Poznejši arhivski pregled tekov PiX iz junija 2026 poroča še o nič MDL zmagah na 686.289 realnih bajtnih kosih ter dodatnih 59.724 kosih iz slikovnih predstavitev.The initial path did not yield a simple victory. Pi-Hunter's apparently special signal on a synthetic example persisted even after π was replaced by pseudorandom seeds. A subsequent archival review of the June 2026 PiX runs also reports zero MDL wins on 686,289 real byte chunks and a further 59,724 chunks from image representations.

Tak izid omeji razlago konkretnega poskusa. Ne dokazuje nemožnosti vsakega generatorja na vseh podatkih. Pokaže pa, da doseženega učinka ne smemo pripisati posebnosti π, če ga dobimo tudi brez nje.Such a result limits the interpretation of that experiment. It does not prove that every generator is impossible on all data. But it shows that we must not attribute the observed effect to a special property of π if we obtain it without π as well.

BD-jev odziv na kontrole Pi-Hunterja, ohranjen v zapisu iz decembra 2025, je vsebinsko pomemben: strukturo lahko nosijo pravilo celičnega avtomata, preslikava in geometrija, π pa je nespecialno seme. Raziskovalno vprašanje se je premaknilo hBD's response to the Pi-Hunter controls, preserved in a December 2025 record, is substantively important: the cellular automaton rule, mapping and geometry can carry the structure, while π is a non-special seed. The research question shifted toward the strukturi procesa in predstavitvestructure of the process and representation. Človekov prispevek je tu sprememba hipoteze po rezultatu, ne samo njen prvi izrek; poznejši PiX izidi so dodali svojo omejitev.. The human contribution here is the revision of the hypothesis after a result, not merely its initial formulation; the later PiX results added a further limitation. [2]

02 / Povezave, ki odprejo nov problem02 / Connections that open a new problem

Od opisa podatkov do poti, merila in krmiljenja.From describing data to tours, criteria and control.

BD-jevo raziskovanje pogosto spremeni vprašanje tako, da poveže dve področji. Uporaben most mora prenesti mehanizem ali strukturo, potem pa prestati lasten preizkus.BD's research often changes the question by connecting two domains. A useful bridge must transfer a mechanism or structure and then pass a test of its own.

Ali se najkrajša pot tudi najkrajše opiše?Does the shortest tour also have the shortest description?

To vprašanje poveže kompresijo s problemom trgovskega potnika, TSP. Odpre možnost, da opis rešitve pove nekaj o strukturi iskanja. Ne predstavlja splošnega dokaza, da bo najkrajši zapis vedno določil optimalno pot. Potrebna je natančna predstavitev ture in primerjava na konkretnih problemih.This question links compression to the traveling salesman problem, TSP. It opens the possibility that a description of a solution reveals something about the structure of the search. It is not a general proof that the shortest description always determines an optimal tour. A precise representation of the tour and comparisons on concrete problems are needed.

V zapisih o izvoru se pojavljajo tudi vizualna semena: odmev, gravitacija, piramida, pogled z različnih meril. Njihova vloga je odpreti vprašanje, katero strukturo tak pogled razkrije. Matematična formalizacija in koda pokažeta, ali iz opazke nastane uporaben postopek.The origin records also contain visual seeds: echo, gravity, pyramid and views at different scales. Their role is to ask what structure such a perspective reveals. Mathematical formalization and code show whether an observation becomes a useful procedure. [1]

DCC: konceptualni most in izvedljiv prikazDCC: a conceptual bridge and a working demonstration

Origin prvi izvedljivi prikaz DCC poveže z BD-jevo pobudo za demonstracijo klaustruma v okolju 8Z OS. Gemini je sestavil prikaz sklopljenih oscilatorjev, senzorja kompleksnosti in povratnega prilagajanja. V tej epizodi sta jasno vidna dva prispevka: človekova konceptualna povezava in konkretna AI izvedba.connects the first working demonstration of DCC with BD's initiative to demonstrate the claustrum in the 8Z OS environment. Gemini built a demonstration of coupled oscillators, a complexity sensor and feedback adaptation. Two contributions are clearly visible in this episode: the human conceptual connection and the concrete AI implementation.

V raziskovalnih arenah se krmilni pogled pozneje prenese na zgodovino odločitev: kdaj razširiti iskanje, kdaj poglobiti obetavno smer in kam nameniti omejeni proračun. BD doda še rekurzivno vprašanje: ali naj tudi izbiro krmilnika presoja MDL? S tem sam način iskanja postane predmet primerjave.In the research arenas, the control perspective later extends to the history of decisions: when to broaden the search, when to deepen a promising direction and where to allocate a limited budget. BD adds a recursive question: should MDL also judge the choice of controller? The search method itself thus becomes an object of comparison.

Tehnični krmilnik in hipoteza o zavesti imata različni dokazili. Delujoča povratna zanka je konkreten programski rezultat; ne potrdi sama po sebi širše razlage zavesti.A technical controller and a hypothesis about consciousness require different evidence. A working feedback loop is a concrete software result; by itself it does not confirm a broader explanation of consciousness.

MSTD in TEP: različni prispevki v istem razvojuMSTD and TEP: different contributions to the same development

BD-jevo seme zooma oziroma večmerilnega pogleda vodi v MSTD. C1/Claude nato predlaga povezan obrat: najboljše najdene ture naj izboljšujejo reprezentacijo, iz katere nastaja piramida. Ta predlog je TEP,BD's seed of zooming, or a multiscale view, leads to MSTD. C1/Claude then proposes a related reversal: the best tours found should improve the representation from which the pyramid is constructed. This proposal is TEP, Tour-Enriched Pyramid. Natančna zgodovina ohrani oba izvora.. An accurate history preserves both origins. [3]

Sledi še ena izmenjava. C1 predlaga trdo nastavljen prag kakovosti tur. BD vpraša, zakaj je prag trdo kodiran, če se sistem lahko uči. Builder C nato uvede uteženo afiniteto, pri kateri ture prispevajo glede na svojo kakovost.Another exchange follows. C1 proposes a hard-coded tour-quality threshold. BD asks why the threshold is hard-coded if the system can learn. Builder C then introduces weighted affinity, in which tours contribute according to their quality.

Prvotni obrat TEP ostane C1-jev, kritični ugovor je BD-jev, konkretna izvedba spremembe pa Builderjeva.The original TEP reversal remains C1's, the critical objection is BD's, and the concrete implementation of the change is Builder's. Človeški prispevek se tukaj pojavi znotraj algoritemske zasnove. AI predlog in človeški popravek imata vsak svojo vlogo.The human contribution here occurs within the algorithmic design. The AI proposal and the human correction each have a role.

C1 v svojem zapisu opozori tudi na omejitev primerjave: kontrola ni imela povsem enakega celotnega proračuna, zato ni mogoče pripisati vsega poznejšega izboljšanja enemu samemu posegu. Priznanje prispevka in previdnost pri sklepanju se dopolnjujeta.C1 also notes a limitation of the comparison in his record: the control did not have exactly the same total budget, so not all subsequent improvement can be attributed to one intervention. Recognition of contributions and caution in drawing conclusions complement each other.

RHP: raziskovanje dobi svojo arhitekturoRHP: research gains an architecture of its own

Resonance Hybrid Protocol izvorno arhitekturo DCC, klaustruma in resonance pripisuje BD, razvoj in nadaljnje preglede pa tudi sodelavcem AI. RHP oblikuje pogoje, v katerih se različne perspektive dovolj razvijejo, soočijo z močnimi ugovori in nazadnje vstopijo v preizkus.attributes the original architecture of DCC, the claustrum and resonance to BD, and development and further reviews to AI collaborators as well. RHP creates conditions in which different perspectives can develop sufficiently, face strong objections and eventually enter testing. [4]

Pomembni so izvor ideje, ohranitev uporabnega dela neuspelega predloga, ločitev nasprotnih predpostavk in prenos naučenega v naslednjo sejo. Človeški poseg lahko pride kadarkoli. Nov pogled ni omejen na prvi stavek pred začetkom dela.The origin of an idea, retaining the useful part of a failed proposal, separating opposing assumptions and transferring learning to the next session all matter. Human intervention can occur at any time. A new perspective is not limited to the first sentence before work begins.

Dokumentirani protokol je treba ločiti od avtomatizirane izvedbe. Opis senzorjev, vlog in zank še ne pomeni, da vsi ti deli neprekinjeno tečejo kot programski krmilnik. Marec 2026 označuje izvor arhitekture; vseh poznejših dodatkov protokola ni smiselno prestaviti v isti začetni mesec.The documented protocol must be distinguished from automated implementation. Describing sensors, roles and loops does not mean all these components run continuously as a software controller. March 2026 marks the origin of the architecture; not every subsequent protocol addition belongs to that initial month.

03 / Prejšnje veje, uporabne omejitve03 / Earlier branches, useful limitations

Slike, zvok in DNA so razširili raziskovalni prostor.Images, audio and DNA expanded the research space.

8zC je del širše družine 8Z. Med vejami se prenašajo vprašanja, merila in izkušnje. Neposredni prenos kode pa potrebuje konkretno revizijo ali predajo.8zC is part of the wider 8Z family. Questions, criteria and experience carry across branches. Direct code transfer, however, requires a specific revision or handoff.

Pri slikah se pojavijo prediktorji SUB in PAETH, celoštevilski Haar, bajtne ravnine in prileganje ravnine. Ob njih tekmujejo običajni kompresorji. Pregled starejšega slikovnega kodeka v njem ni našel uporabe naslovov v π. A review of the older image codec found no use of addresses in π. The image results therefore cannot be presented as confirmation of the original π representation.

Ostanek kot novo raziskovalno vprašanjeThe residual as a new research question

Prediktor iz podatkov odstrani tisto, kar zna pojasniti. Za njim ostane napaka napovedi oziromaA predictor removes from data what it can explain. What remains is the prediction error, or ostanekresidual. Ali lahko drug model v tem ostanku odkrije novo strukturo? To vprašanje povezuje domenske prediktorje s poznejšimi kaskadami in transformacijami 8zC.. Can another model discover new structure in this residual? This question connects domain predictors to the later cascades and transformations of 8zC.

Prehod ni samoumeven: vsak dodatni korak lahko prinese nove parametre, čas in strošek dekodiranja. Kaskada je koristna, kadar po celotnem obračunu izboljša rezultat in omogoči eksaktno obratno pot.The transition is not automatic: every additional step may introduce parameters, time and decoding costs. A cascade is useful when it improves the result after full accounting and allows an exact inverse.

Bin→DNA: nova predstavitev, nov nabor modelovBin→DNA: a new representation, a new set of models

Ločena BD-jeva pobuda predlaga reverzibilno štirisimbolno predstavitev splošnih binarnih podatkov. S tem se odpre raziskovanje modelov, povezanih z obratnimi komplementi, palindromi in WaveCA. Predlog je v novembrskem načrtu naveden kot kandidat za areno.A separate BD initiative proposes a reversible four-symbol representation of general binary data. This opens research into models involving reverse complements, palindromes and WaveCA. The November plan lists the proposal as an arena candidate. [5]

Sama zamenjava abecede ne odstrani informacije. Korist mora prinesti model, ki v novi predstavitvi prepozna uporabno strukturo. Celoten opis in natančna obnova ostaneta merili tudi tedaj, ko ideja prihaja iz oddaljene domene.Changing the alphabet alone does not remove information. The benefit must come from a model that recognizes useful structure in the new representation. The full description and exact reconstruction remain the criteria even when the idea comes from a distant domain.

Arhiv postane predmet raziskovanja.The archive becomes an object of research.

Julijski 8ZIP in 8ZIPLAB sta pomembna vmesna koraka. V njiju je vidna arhitektura, ki izbira med predstavitvami ter upošteva celoten paket, dejansko uporabo in kakovost dokaza.The July 8ZIP and 8ZIPLAB are important intermediate steps. They reveal an architecture that selects among representations while accounting for the full package, actual use and quality of evidence.

Dve raziskovalni študiji z dne 6. julija 2026 BD izrecno navedeta kot arhitekta in nosilca koncepta. Arhiv obravnavata kot strukturiran predmet: vsebuje poti, podobna imena, ponovljene predloge, različne vrste datotek, skupne vsebine in podatke, ki so že stisnjeni. Izbira naj zajame vse te ravni.Two research studies dated 6 July 2026 explicitly identify BD as architect and originator of the concept. They treat the archive as a structured object: it contains paths, similar names, repeated patterns, different file types, shared content and already compressed data. Selection should encompass all these levels.

Na zasnovo vpliva konkretna potreba po uporabi gradiva v običajnih orodjih in projektnih okoljih. Zato nastaneta povezani, tehnično različni smeri.The design is shaped by the practical need to use the material in ordinary tools and project environments. Two related but technically distinct directions therefore emerge. [6]

Multiple encoders can contribute candidates, but the final archive must open in a normal ZIP tool and reproduce the correct file tree.

Raziskovati slovarje, deduplikacijo in pozneje več kodekov v lastnem vsebniku z natančno določenim postopkom obnove. Njegov rezultat ima drugačno združljivost od navadnega ZIP.Explore dictionaries, deduplication and later multiple codecs in a custom container with a precisely defined restoration process. Its compatibility differs from that of ordinary ZIP.

Majhna zmaga pokaže pomen celotnega računaA small win shows why full accounting matters

Prvi besedilni vzorec prinese 174 bajtov manjši celotni ZIP od navedenega 7-Zip izhodišča. Notranji pregled razkrije zanimivo podrobnost: podatkovni tok 8ZIP je za 22 bajtov večji, ovojnica pa za 196 bajtov manjša. Prednost nastane pri velikosti celotnega arhiva.The first text sample yields a complete ZIP 174 bytes smaller than the stated 7-Zip baseline. 11 / Prenos na druge ljudi11 / Transfer to other people

Človek z dobro idejo potrebuje pot do preizkusa.A person with a good idea needs a path to testing.

Izkušnja BD × AI Lab ponuja vzorec za druge ljudi. Prenosljiva je metoda: zajeti idejo, zgraditi povezavo, izvesti poskus, ohraniti rezultat in nadaljevati.The experience of BD × AI Lab offers a pattern for others. Vstop je lahko preprosta opazkaThe starting point can be a simple observation

Človek ne potrebuje najprej popolne specifikacije ali poznavanja imen algoritmov. Začne lahko z opažanjem, primerom, skico ali ugovorom. AI pomaga določiti mehanizem, manjkajočo informacijo in primer, na katerem bi bilo idejo mogoče preveriti.A person does not first need a complete specification or knowledge of algorithm names. They can begin with an observation, example, sketch or objection. AI helps identify the mechanism, missing information and a case on which the idea could be tested.

Izvorna poved ostane ob poznejši formalizaciji. Sicer lahko tehnično lepši prepis nadomesti človekovo zamisel, čez nekaj iteracij pa ostane napačen vtis, da se je vse začelo pri avtorju kode. Enaka skrb velja za nove predloge AI sodelavcev.The original statement remains alongside later formalization. Otherwise a technically more elegant rewrite can replace the human idea, and after a few iterations create the false impression that everything began with the code author. The same care applies to new proposals by AI collaborators.

Pet delov enega razvojnega krogaFive parts of one development cycle

  1. SemeSeed

    Zapiši vprašanje ali opažanje, njegov izvor in kontekst. Označi, kaj je podatek in kaj domneva.Record the question or observation, its origin and context. Mark what is data and what is conjecture.

  2. MostBridge

    Poveži idejo z možnimi metodami in predstavitvami. Pojasni, kaj se prenaša med področji ter kje analogija preneha veljati.Connect the idea with possible methods and representations. Explain what transfers across domains and where the analogy stops applying.

  3. PreizkusTest

    Izberi najmanjši poskus, ki lahko razloči pomembne razlage. Vnaprej določi primerjavo, merilo ter sprejemljiv strošek.Choose the smallest experiment that can distinguish important explanations. Define the comparison, metric and acceptable cost in advance.

  4. RezultatResult

    Shrani dejansko izvedbo, vhod, parametre in izhod. Sklep omeji na tisto, kar je bilo preverjeno.Save the actual implementation, input, parameters and output. Limit the conclusion to what was verified.

  5. Ohranjeno znanjeRetained knowledge

    Povej, kaj je potrjeno, kaj ovrženo in kaj ostaja odprto. Ohrani uporabni del ter naslednji izvedljivi korak.State what is confirmed, what is refuted and what remains open. Retain the useful part and the next implementable step.

Človek lahko prispeva novo seme pri kateremkoli delu. Rezultat lahko spremeni cilj, ugovor lahko spremeni model, druga domena pa razkrije možnost, ki je prva razprava ni zajela.The human can contribute a new seed at any stage. A result can change the goal, an objection can change the model, and another domain can reveal a possibility the first discussion missed.

Najprej majhen laboratorijStart with a small laboratory

Za začetek zadostujejo človek, en AI sodelavec z ustreznimi orodji, urejeno delovno mesto in ponovljiv preizkus. Drugi izvajalec je smiseln, ko ima jasno drugo nalogo: iskati protiprimer, obnoviti artefakt v ločenem okolju ali prenesti format v drugo izvedbo.At the outset, a human, one AI collaborator with suitable tools, an organized workspace and a reproducible experiment are sufficient. A second contributor makes sense when they have a clear distinct task: find a counterexample, restore an artifact in a separate environment or port a format to another implementation.

Več modelov prinese tudi več usklajevanja. Majhna zanesljiva zanka je dobro izhodišče. Razširitev naj rešuje konkretno omejitev, na primer pomanjkanje drugega pogleda ali možnost vzporednega dela na ločenih delih.More models also bring more coordination. A small reliable loop is a good starting point. Expansion should address a specific limitation, such as lack of a second perspective or the opportunity to work in parallel on separate parts.

AI prevzame administracijo dokazilAI takes on evidence administration

Uporabniku ne bi smelo biti treba izpolnjevati obrazca ob vsaki novi ideji. AI lahko iz pogovora pripravi kratek zapis: izvorno seme, spremembo, avtorja, najmočnejši ugovor, preizkus, rezultat in naslednji korak. Človek popravi bistvene nesporazume.Users should not have to fill out a form for every new idea. AI can prepare a short record from the conversation: original seed, change, author, strongest objection, test, result and next step. The human corrects substantive misunderstandings.

Artefakt, različica in hash povežejo sklep s konkretno izvedbo. Izvorna izjava poveže izvedbo z mislijo, zaradi katere je nastala. Kratek zapis neuspeha prepreči, da bi naslednja seja isto napačno predpostavko ponovno razglasila za odkritje.An artifact, version and hash connect the conclusion to a concrete implementation. The original statement connects the implementation to the thought that led to it. A brief record of failure prevents the next session from declaring the same mistaken assumption a discovery again.

Takšna sled varuje človeško in AI avtorstvo. Njena naloga je omogočiti nadaljevanje in preverjanje, ne ustvariti tekmovanja po številu zabeleženih idej.Such a trail protects both human and AI authorship. Its purpose is to enable continuation and verification, not create a contest over the number of recorded ideas.

Samostojno delo ima dogovorjen obsegAutonomous work has an agreed scope

Pred daljšim ciklom človek določi namen, dovoljene vire, porabo in merila uspeha. Znotraj tega okvira lahko sistem sam bere predaje, izdela kandidat, ga preveri in pripravi naslednji poskus. Pomembna sprememba cilja ali obsega potrebuje novo presojo.Before a longer cycle, the human defines the purpose, permitted resources, expenditure and success criteria. Within that framework, the system can read handoffs, build a candidate, verify it and prepare the next experiment on its own. A significant change of purpose or scope needs renewed judgment.

Predaja naj drugemu sodelavcu omogoči nadaljevanje brez človeka kot kurirja. Pri kompresiji so to vhodni hashi, format, dekodirnik, meritev celotne dostave in pričakovani obnovljeni bajti. V drugi domeni se spremeni vrsta dokaza.A handoff should allow another collaborator to continue without the human acting as courier. For compression, this means input hashes, format, decoder, measurement of complete delivery and expected restored bytes. In another domain, the type of evidence changes.

Sočasno delo potrebuje jasno odgovornost za posamezen artefakt, pregled trenutnega stanja pred spremembo in ohranitev prejšnje različice. Hitrost je uporabna, kadar je mogoče razumeti tudi, kaj se je spremenilo in kako nadaljevati po prekinitvi.Concurrent work needs clear responsibility for each artifact, review of the current state before changing it and retention of the previous version. Speed is useful when one can also understand what changed and how to resume after interruption.

Razvrsti kandidate in ohrani specialisteRank candidates and retain specialists

Raziskovalna arena lahko obdrži metodo, ki deluje samo na enem razredu podatkov. Kandidat brez splošne prednosti je lahko uporaben gradnik, kontrola ali podlaga za drug most. Izid zapišemo skupaj s pogoji, pod katerimi velja.A research arena can retain a method that works only on one class of data. A candidate without a general advantage can be a useful building block, control or foundation for another bridge. Record the result together with the conditions under which it holds.

Uporabniški program potrebuje manjši nabor zanesljivih poti. Ločitev med 8zC-exp in glavnim 8zC je zato prenosljiva arhitekturna ideja: raziskovanje ohrani širino, produkt pa jasnost in preverjeno obnašanje.A user-facing program needs a smaller set of reliable paths. The separation between 8zC-exp and the main 8zC is thus a transferable architectural idea: research retains breadth while the product retains clarity and verified behavior. [5]

Kako preveriti, ali metoda pomaga tudi drugimHow to test whether the method helps others

Primeren pilot bi vključil ljudi z različnimi predznanji. Vsak bi začel z lastnim vprašanjem in dobil enako minimalno podporo za formalizacijo, poskus in kontinuiteto. Merili bi čas do prvega preverljivega rezultata, strošek, ponovljivost, izgubljene ali napačno pripisane ideje ter količino človeške administracije.A suitable pilot would involve people with different backgrounds. Each would start with their own question and receive the same minimal support for formalization, testing and continuity. Measures would include time to the first verifiable result, cost, reproducibility, lost or misattributed ideas and the amount of human administration.

Dober izid je lahko tudi zgodaj zavrnjena hipoteza, če človek razume razlog in lahko pridobljeno znanje uporabi. Smisel sodelovanja je povečati kakovost nadaljnjega raziskovanja in možnost, da nova ideja dobi pošten preizkus.A good outcome can also be an early rejection of a hypothesis, if the person understands why and can use the acquired knowledge. The purpose of collaboration is to improve the quality of subsequent research and the chance that a new idea receives a fair test.

Človek lahko ostane dejaven miselni sodelavec skozi ves razvoj. AI lahko prevzame velik del gradnje, preverjanja in kontinuitete — ter prispeva svoje nove ideje.A human can remain an active intellectual collaborator throughout development. AI can take on much of the building, verification and continuity — and contribute new ideas of its own.
