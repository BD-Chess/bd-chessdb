Complete packages would be compared on held-out inputs with each method enabled and disabled. Accounting would include references, headers, permutations, dictionaries and the decoder, with preinstalled dependencies reported separately. Negative controls would reveal false discoveries. Every target platform would have to restore the same bytes.

Za lastni Rustovi jedri brez zunanjih izvršljivih kodekov bi merili čas priprave, stiskanja in obnove ter vršno porabo pomnilnika. The proposed native Rust engines would run without external codec executables. Measurements would separate preparation, compression and restoration time and record peak memory. Dve zgodnji vprašanji iz leta 2024Two early questions from 2024

Ali lahko del datoteke natančno obnovimo iz krajše reference na števke π oziroma drug deterministični matematični generator? In ali lahko za vsak del posebej izberemo najkrajši popolni zapis? BD prvo vprašanje v svojem zapisu o nastanku 8Z umešča na 4. oktober 2024, drugo pa neposredno za njim. To sta začetni zamisli v daljšem razvoju, med katerim je prispeval številne nadaljnje ideje.

Can a shorter reference to digits of π or another deterministic mathematical generator reproduce part of a file exactly? And can we choose the shortest complete description for each part separately? In his origin account, BD dates the first question to 4 October 2024 and places the second immediately afterwards. These are starting ideas in a longer development process to which he has contributed many further ideas.

V specifikaciji iz oktobra 2025 je BD s sodelavci opisal izbiro med izvornimi bajti, običajnim stiskanjem in determinističnim generatorjem. Pri metodi najkrajšega opisa (MDL) štejejo tudi oznaka generatorja, parametri in podatki za obnovo. In the October 2025 specification, BD and collaborators described a choice between raw bytes, conventional compression and a deterministic generator. Under minimum description length (MDL), the generator identifier, parameters and restoration data all count. In December, 8Z used 38.9% of the total input bytes on 60 TIFF images, PNG used 46.3% and JPEG XL used 31.4%. In February 2026, 8Z Audio v1.5 produced smaller files than FLAC-12 on 7 of 15 clips.
