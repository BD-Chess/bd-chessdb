Kako je nastal prihranekWhere the savings came from

R1 shrani en skupni zapis povezav. R2 iz koordinat natančno obnovi 776.323 shranjenih dolžin. R3 spremeni zapis zaporedja povezav in prihrani dodatnih 24.723 B. Paketi 8zC vsebujejo dekoder in podatke za obnovo. Primerjava uporablja enonitni solidni 7‑Zip 26.05, LZMA2 Ultra.

R1 stores one copy of the shared adjacency data. R2 reconstructs 776,323 stored distances exactly from coordinates. R3 changes the representation of the adjacency order and saves another 24,723 B. The 8zC packages include the decoder and restoration data. The comparison uses single-threaded solid 7-Zip 26.05 with LZMA2 Ultra.

Koliko prostora potrebuje obnova?How much space does restoration require?

Za to datoteko potrebujemo še 15 B (R + H) in enkratni dekoder (L_once) velikosti 26 248 B. Če celoten dekoder pripišemo eni datoteki, veja PAQ -3 po tem obračunu porabi 33 899 B. Če je dekoder že v zbirki, dodatna datoteka porabi 7 651 B.

This file also needs 15 B (R + H) and a 26 248 B decoder (L_once). Charging the entire decoder to one file brings the PAQ -3 residual path to 33 899 B under this accounting. If the collection already contains the decoder, the additional file accounts for 7 651 B.

Ločeni posnetek korpusa R3.1 za PAQ -10LE potrjuje 472/479 bitno točnih poslov. V seštevek spada 236/240 datotek (235 popolnih parov in ena brez preostanka). Neposredno stiskanje je zasedlo 8 118 458 B, pot 8zC z vštetimi R + H in enim dekoderjem pa 7 773 608 B. Neto prihranek na teh datotekah je 344 850 B.

A separate R3.1 corpus checkpoint for PAQ -10LE verifies 472/479 byte-exact jobs. The totals cover 236/240 files (235 complete pairs and one file without a residual). Direct compression used 8 118 458 B. The 8zC path used 7 773 608 B including R + H and one decoder, saving 344 850 B net on those files.

Vir in obseg meritevSource and measurement scope

Workova matrika kodekov z dne 23. novembra 2026 vsebuje 42 uporabnih nastavitev PAQ in 17 nastavitev 7z/ZIP. Izločili smo tri 9-bajtne izhode PAQ, pri katerih je manjkal slovar. Navedena časa PAQ merita trajanje stiskanja že pripravljenih vhodov. Surovi zapisi ne vsebujejo časa predobdelave, procesorskega časa, meritev RAM, ločenih časov za cmix ali dokazil SHA-256 o obnovi za vsako nastavitev matrike. Ločeni zapisi R3.1 potrjujejo bitno točno razširitev obeh tokov -10LE iz tabele.

The Work codec matrix of 23 November 2026 contains 42 usable PAQ settings and 17 settings for 7z/ZIP. We excluded three 9-byte PAQ outputs produced with a missing dictionary. The PAQ timings measure elapsed compression time on prepared inputs. The raw records omit preprocessing time, CPU time, RAM measurements, separate cmix timings and per-setting SHA-256 restore receipts. Separate R3.1 records verify byte-exact decoding of both -10LE streams in the table.
