Na ločenih testnih vhodih bi primerjali celotne pakete z vključenim in izključenim postopkom. Všteli bi sklice, glave, permutacije, slovar in dekoder ter posebej prikazali primer z že nameščenimi odvisnostmi. Negativne kontrole bi razkrile lažne zadetke. Obnova bi morala na vseh ciljnih platformah vrniti iste bajte.

Complete packages would be compared on held-out inputs with each method enabled and disabled. Accounting would include references, headers, permutations, dictionaries and the decoder, with preinstalled dependencies reported separately. Negative controls would reveal false discoveries. Every target platform would have to restore the same bytes.

Za lastni Rustovi jedri brez zunanjih izvršljivih kodekov bi merili čas priprave, stiskanja in obnove ter vršno porabo pomnilnika. Zabeleženih 11,1 s za PAQ −3 na preostanku in 35,4 s za −10LE na izvirniku ene knjižnice sta časa obstoječega kodeka za različna toka. Za 8z-LZ ali 8z-CM še ni izmerjenega razmerja med prihrankom, časom in RAM-om.

The proposed native Rust engines would run without external codec executables. Measurements would separate preparation, compression and restoration time and record peak memory. The logged 11.1 s for PAQ −3 on one library’s residual and 35.4 s for −10LE on its original input are times for an existing codec on different streams. No size, time and RAM trade-off has yet been measured for 8z-LZ or 8z-CM.

Od π do datotek ELFFrom π to ELF files

Ali lahko del datoteke natančno obnovimo iz krajše reference na števke π? In ali lahko za vsak del posebej izberemo najkrajši popolni zapis? Bojan Dobrečevič prvo vprašanje v svojem zapisu o nastanku 8Z umešča na 4. oktober 2024, drugo pa neposredno za njim.

Can a shorter reference to the digits of π reproduce part of a file exactly? And can we choose the shortest complete description for each part separately? In his account of how 8Z began, Bojan Dobrečevič dates the first question to 4 October 2024 and places the second immediately afterwards.

Prvi preizkusi, 2025–2026Early tests, 2025–2026

V specifikaciji iz oktobra 2025 je BD s sodelavci opisal izbiro med izvornimi bajti, običajnim stiskanjem in determinističnim generatorjem. Pri metodi najkrajšega opisa (MDL) štejejo tudi oznaka generatorja, parametri in podatki za obnovo. Decembra je 8Z na 60 slikah TIFF skupaj zasedel 38,9 % vhodnih bajtov, PNG 46,3 % in JPEG XL 31,4 %. Februarja 2026 je zvočni kodek 8Z v1.5 ustvaril manjše datoteke kot FLAC-12 na 7 izmed 15 izsekov. Julija je ReZIP iskal manjše združljive arhive ZIP, medtem ko je 8zipLab preizkušal lasten format s skupnim slovarjem in odstranjevanjem podvojenih vsebin.

In the October 2025 specification, BD and collaborators described a choice between raw bytes, conventional compression and a deterministic generator. Under minimum description length (MDL), the generator identifier, parameters and restoration data all count. In December, 8Z used 38.9% of the total input bytes on 60 TIFF images, PNG used 46.3% and JPEG XL used 31.4%. In February 2026, 8Z Audio v1.5 produced smaller files than FLAC-12 on 7 of 15 clips. In July, ReZIP searched for smaller compatible ZIP archives, while 8zipLab tested a custom format with a shared dictionary and content deduplication.

Novembra 2026 je P2-C isto zamisel preizkusil na 240 datotekah ELF. Pravila za obnovo med drugim znova ustvarijo izbrane bajte tabel ELF. Kodirnik na teh mestih zapiše ničle in preostanek stisne s PAQ8PX -7. Pri dekodiranju reference povedo, katere bajte je treba izračunati in kam jih vrniti. Vseh 240 datotek se je obnovilo bitno točno. Neposredni PAQ je zasedel 10 817 209 B, P2-C z referencami, glavami in enim skupnim dekoderjem pa 10 408 291 B. Prihranek na tem naboru je 408 918 B (3,78 %).

In November 2026, P2-C tested the same idea on 240 ELF files. Its restoration rules can regenerate selected bytes in ELF tables. The encoder puts zeros at those positions and compresses the residual with PAQ8PX -7. During decoding, the references identify which bytes to compute and where to put them back. All 240 files were restored byte for byte. Direct PAQ used 10 817 209 B. P2-C used 10 408 291 B including references, headers and one shared decoder, saving 408 918 B (3.78%) on this corpus.

Pri P2-C je 8zC-C vodil tehnično zasnovo. 8zC-G je z neodvisnim protiprimerom popravil razlago zgodnjega poskusa in določil merilni protokol. 8zC-HP1 je pripravil kodirnik in merilne programe ter izvedel potrditveni preizkus.

For P2-C, 8zC-C led the technical design. 8zC-G provided an independent counterexample that corrected the interpretation of an early test and set the measurement protocol. 8zC-HP1 built the encoder and measurement tools and ran the confirmation test.
