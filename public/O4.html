HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Objective

Connect 4 tokens of your color. The board is a dynamic system of rotating rings.

🔄 The Twist: Rotation

Unless you use a Brake, rings rotate automatically AFTER you place a token.

Inner Ring (0): +1 Sector (CW)
Ring 1: -1 Sector (CCW)
Ring 2: +2 Sectors (CW)
Outer Ring (3): Static

🛑 Resource: The Brake

You have 3 Brakes per game. Toggle the Brake button before moving to freeze rotation for that turn.

🏆 Win Lines

Radial: Straight line from center out
Orbital: 4 adjacent in the same ring
Spiral: Step outward + Step sideways
