A ranked atlas of inventions and cultural technologies that changed civilization, followed by a small BD × AI appendix for future physical invention seeds.

Methodology note. The main Top 100 ranks existing inventions by civilizational impact, dependency depth, persistence, and system leverage. The BD × AI appendix is a separate physical seed lane: seed score is not proof, only a measure of novelty, first-test clarity, bridge potential and page readiness.

Score is not moral purity and not truth probability. Score is research/civilizational usefulness under this lens. Some inventions have severe harms and still transformed the world.

Methodology / MAL disclaimer

Two lanes: proven inventions and physical seeds

Existing inventions lane

This lane ranks inventions that already changed the world. The score blends impact, dependency, persistence, breadth and transformation. It does not mean the invention is morally pure.

Future physical seed lane

The appendix uses a different score: novelty, physical bridge potential, first-test clarity, proposal-page readiness and review usefulness. A seed score is not proof that the invention works.

Methodology / MAL note

MAL can be used as a multi-LLM critique loop for stress-testing the ranking, missing inventions, over-compressed entries and score assumptions. It is not a vote and not a substitute for evidence.

Rule: rank, don’t eliminate. Low-ranked seeds stay in the archive until better tests or representations clarify them.
