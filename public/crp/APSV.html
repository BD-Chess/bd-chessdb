APSVActive Pressure-Skin Vehicle.Aktivno tlačno-kožno vozilo.

A research program exploring whether a vehicle body can become an active fluid organ rather than a passive shell: moving air or water through and along its surface, shaping pressure and wake, reducing drag, improving stability and safety, and — where external momentum exchange is explicit — contributing to propulsion.Raziskovalni program preverja, ali lahko trup vozila postane aktiven fluidni organ namesto pasivne lupine: vodi zrak ali vodo skozi in ob površini, oblikuje tlak in vrtinec, zmanjšuje upor, izboljšuje stabilnost in varnost ter — kjer je izrecna zunanja izmenjava gibalne količine — prispeva k pogonu.

Across more than 24.6 million v0.5.3 Weekly evaluations, the strongest repeatable signal remains water and underwater operation. The clearest near-term path is a quiet, controllable inspection or rescue vehicle; a second high-value path is active aerodynamic assistance for trucks, vans and electric vehicles. The long-term maximum is a hybrid multi-medium specialty craft whose skin helps conventional subsystems work better.Po več kot 24,6 milijona v0.5.3 Weekly evaluacijah ostaja najmočnejši ponovljiv signal voda in podvodno delovanje. Najjasnejša kratkoročna pot je tiho in natančno vodljivo vozilo za pregledovanje ali reševanje; druga zelo vredna pot je aktivna aerodinamična pomoč tovornjakom, kombijem in električnim vozilom. Dolgoročni maksimum je hibridno večmedijsko specialno vozilo, pri katerem aktivna koža pomaga konvencionalnim podsistemom delovati bolje.

What APSV isKaj je APSV

Not a claim that a flying submarine already exists. APSV is a structured way to keep many propulsion and flow-control ideas alive long enough to measure, rank, and falsify them.Ni trditev, da leteča podmornica že obstaja. APSV je strukturiran način, da veliko pogonskih in flow-control idej ostane živih dovolj dolgo, da jih izmerimo, rangiramo in falsificiramo.

Core ruleJedrno pravilo

Rank, don’t eliminate.Rangiraj, ne eliminiraj. A weak result today is not deletion. It becomes a low-ranked row, a watch lane, a failure mode, or a resurrection-sweep candidate under the next representation.Slab rezultat danes ni izbris. Postane nizko rangirana vrstica, watch veja, failure mode ali kandidat za resurrection sweep pod naslednjo reprezentacijo.

Baselines are references, not guillotines. APSV ideas must pay for momentum, energy, mass, cavitation, sound, control, and maintenance — but they are not killed merely because a conventional solution wins one narrow axis.Baseline-i so reference, ne giljotine. APSV ideje morajo plačati moment, energijo, maso, kavitacijo, zvok, kontrolo in vzdrževanje — niso pa ubite samo zato, ker konvencionalna rešitev zmaga na eni ozki osi.

Physical framingFizikalni okvir

APSV is not anti-gravity, not reactionless propulsion, and not free energy. The active skin may create drag reduction, attachment, circulation, control, lift, or thrust, but net thrust requires explicit external momentum exchange.APSV ni antigravitacija, ni pogon brez reakcije in ni prosta energija. Aktivna koža lahko ustvarja manjši upor, attachment, cirkulacijo, kontrolo, vzgon ali potisk, toda neto potisk zahteva izrecno zunanjo izmenjavo momenta.

APSV = Active Pressure-Skin Vehicle.

What we are doing — and what success could look likeKaj delamo — in kako bi lahko izgledal uspeh

The project is no longer only a question about a “flying car.” It is a search for useful active-skin technologies that can improve ordinary vehicles as well as enable new ones.Projekt ni več samo vprašanje »letečega avtomobila«. Iščemo uporabne tehnologije aktivne kože, ki lahko izboljšajo običajna vozila in hkrati omogočijo nova.

The central idea is simple: the body should participate. Instead of merely resisting the surrounding fluid, the surface can sense it, guide it, recover part of its energy, reduce harmful pressure and wake, and provide distributed control.Osrednja ideja je preprosta: trup naj sodeluje. Namesto da okoliškemu fluidu samo nasprotuje, ga lahko površina zaznava, vodi, povrne del njegove energije, zmanjša škodljiv tlak in vrtinec ter zagotavlja porazdeljeno kontrolo.

Most realistic first productNajbolj realističen prvi izdelek

A quiet underwater inspection / rescue robot.Tih podvodni robot za pregledovanje ali reševanje.

M1 traveling-wave skin, porous flow control, cilia and slot-sheet concepts repeatedly score best in slow underwater missions where silence, fine maneuvering, station keeping and safety matter.M1 potujoča valovna koža, porozna kontrola toka, cilije in slot-sheet koncepti se ponavljajo kot najboljši v počasnih podvodnih nalogah, kjer so pomembni tišina, natančno manevriranje, station keeping in varnost.

Most commercial assist pathNajbolj komercialna assist pot

Active wake and drag reduction for trucks, vans and EVs.Aktivno zmanjševanje vrtinca in upora tovornjakov, kombijev in EV.

Roof/front air routing, adaptive rear surfaces and pressure-stiffened fairings may reduce the low-pressure wake behind a vehicle. A clean 5% net energy saving would already be interesting; 10% would be commercially important; 20% would be a major breakthrough. These are success thresholds, not results already achieved.Zajem in vodenje zraka s strehe ali sprednjega dela, prilagodljive zadnje površine ter tlačno ojačani aerodinamični dodatki bi lahko zmanjšali podtlak za vozilom. Čist 5-odstotni neto prihranek bi bil že zanimiv; 10 % komercialno pomemben; 20 % velik preboj. To so merila uspeha, ne že doseženi rezultati.

Strong success scenarioMočan scenarij uspeha

A family of licensable subsystems.Družina licenčnih podsistemov.

APSV could produce separate products for underwater propulsion, safer propeller-free control, road-aero assistance, active sealing/morphing, stability and transition control. The project does not need one single “miracle vehicle” to become valuable.APSV lahko ustvari ločene izdelke za podvodni pogon, varnejšo kontrolo brez izpostavljenega propelerja, cestno aerodinamično pomoč, aktivno tesnjenje in morphing, stabilnost ter kontrolo prehodov. Projekt ne potrebuje enega samega »čudežnega vozila«, da postane vreden.

Maximum physically realistic ambitionNajvečja fizikalno realistična ambicija

A hybrid specialty or rescue vehicle that drives on roads, operates efficiently on and under water, and — in its most ambitious form — uses conventional distributed fans for limited flight. The active skin would not replace all propulsion; it would reduce drag, manage water flow, stabilize transitions, seal and reshape the body, and lower the burden on the conventional engines.Hibridno specialno ali reševalno vozilo, ki se vozi po cesti, učinkovito deluje na vodi in pod njo ter — v najbolj ambiciozni obliki — uporablja konvencionalne porazdeljene ventilatorje za omejen let. Aktivna koža ne bi nadomestila vsega pogona; zmanjšala bi upor, urejala vodni tok, stabilizirala prehode, tesnila in preoblikovala trup ter razbremenila konvencionalne motorje.

Why the current evidence mattersZakaj so sedanji dokazi pomembni

Different arena generations keep returning to the same water-side families even after stricter momentum, power, mass, cavitation, control and baseline checks. That repetition does not prove a product, but it is exactly the kind of stable signal that justifies moving from broad search to focused engineering.Različne generacije arene se tudi po strožjih preverjanjih gibalne količine, moči, mase, kavitacije, kontrole in baseline-ov vedno znova vračajo k istim vodnim družinam. To še ne dokazuje izdelka, je pa prav tista vrsta stabilnega signala, ki upraviči prehod iz širokega iskanja v ciljno inženirstvo.

What the arena actually doesKaj arena dejansko počne

It creates and evaluates millions of combinations of mechanisms, shapes, vehicle classes, media and missions. Candidates must pay for momentum, pump or actuator power, mass, drag, cavitation, noise, control, maintenance and failure modes. Raw surprises are kept, but separated from clean engineering evidence.Ustvarja in ocenjuje milijone kombinacij mehanizmov, oblik, razredov vozil, medijev in misij. Kandidati morajo plačati gibalno količino, moč črpalk ali aktuatorjev, maso, upor, kavitacijo, hrup, kontrolo, vzdrževanje in načine odpovedi. Surova presenečenja ohranimo, vendar jih ločimo od čistih inženirskih dokazov.

What comes nextKaj sledi

The best families now need a higher-fidelity bridge: explicit wave amplitude and frequency, real membrane and actuator models, 2D/3D wake behavior, matched conventional controls, then CFD/FSI and a small water-tank or road-aero prototype. That is the path from a promising arena signal to measurable engineering.Najboljše družine zdaj potrebujejo višjeredni bridge: izrecno amplitudo in frekvenco vala, realne modele membrane in aktuatorja, 2D/3D obnašanje vrtinca, ujemajoče konvencionalne kontrole, nato CFD/FSI ter majhen vodni ali cestno-aerodinamični prototip. To je pot od obetavnega signala arene do merljivega inženirstva.

Mechanism families now under studyDružine mehanizmov v sedanjem raziskovanju

v0.5.3 separates propulsion, drag reduction, flow control, safety, stability, transition and frontier roles. A mechanism can be weak in one role and valuable in another.v0.5.3 ločuje pogon, zmanjšanje upora, kontrolo toka, varnost, stabilnost, prehode in frontier vloge. M1 traveling-wave + porous control

The strongest retained clean engineering combination: an undulating water-side skin provides momentum while porous suction manages attachment and pressure without being counted as free thrust.Najmočnejša ohranjena čista inženirska kombinacija: valovita vodna koža zagotavlja gibalno količino, porozno sesanje pa ureja oprijem toka in tlak, ne da bi ga šteli kot brezplačni potisk.

Slot-sheet + Coandă + cilia

A persistent water-side family for distributed thrust, attachment and fine control. It remains close to the top, but direct jet momentum and induced pressure must stay separately accounted.Vztrajna vodna družina za porazdeljeni potisk, oprijem toka in fino kontrolo. Ostaja blizu vrha, vendar morata biti neposredni moment curka in inducirani tlak vedno ločeno obračunana.

M13 Surface Jet Loop

A recirculating wall-jet and collector loop with optional rear bleed. Weekly evidence strengthened it as a pressure/control/energy-recovery partner for M1 and related water mechanisms, not as reactionless closed-loop thrust.Recirkulacijski površinski curek z zbiralnikom in opcijskim zadnjim bleed izpuhom. Weekly dokazi so ga okrepili kot partnerja M1 za tlak, kontrolo in povračilo energije, ne kot brezreakcijski zaprtozančni potisk.

M15 through-body pumpjet skin

Front intake, internal duct/plenum and distributed rear exhaust. Its best current value is safety, redundancy, vectoring, docking, station keeping, debris tolerance and limp-home capability rather than pure energy dominance.Sprednji zajem, notranji kanal/plenum in porazdeljeni zadnji izpuh. Njegova največja sedanja vrednost je varnost, redundanca, vektoriranje, pristajanje, station keeping, odpornost na smeti in limp-home, ne čista energetska prevlada.

Road-aero wake control

M20L, M21, M21H and M22 explore integrated tails, rear-wake refill, roof/front ram-air routing and adaptive rear surfaces for trucks, vans, buses and cars. These are energy-assist branches, not road propulsion claims.M20L, M21, M21H in M22 raziskujejo integrirane zadke, polnjenje zadnjega vrtinca, dovod zraka s strehe/spredaj ter prilagodljive zadnje površine za tovornjake, kombije, avtobuse in avtomobile. To so energetske assist veje, ne trditve o cestnem pogonu.

M30 / M31 pressure skin

Pressure-stiffened and pneumatic morphing skins are being tested for shape change, sealing, trim, buoyancy, deployable fairings and control. They are promising subsystems, not standalone propulsion miracles.Tlačno ojačane in pnevmatsko spreminjajoče se kože testiramo za spremembo oblike, tesnjenje, trim, vzgon, raztegljive aerodinamične dodatke in kontrolo. So obetavni podsistemi, ne samostojni pogonski čudeži.

Cilia + MHD anomaly

The current global raw top combines active cilia with seawater MHD. It survives several accounting gates but fails clean promotion on local robustness, making it a high-value falsifier target rather than a public winner.Trenutni globalni raw vrh združuje aktivne cilije z MHD v morski vodi. Preživi več računovodskih vrat, vendar ne doseže clean promocije zaradi lokalne robustnosti, zato je dragocen cilj za falsifier test, ne javni zmagovalec.

Conventional baselinesKonvencionalni baseline-i

Marine screw, pump-jet, waterjet, oscillating foil, multirotor and ducted-fan controls remain inside the evaluator. APSV earns attention by surviving comparison with them, not by removing them.Ladijski propeler, pump-jet, waterjet, oscilirajoča plavut, multirotor in ducted-fan kontrole ostajajo v evaluatorju. APSV si pozornost zasluži tako, da preživi primerjavo z njimi, ne tako, da jih odstranimo.

Ordinary road vehicles firstNajprej običajna cestna vozila

APSV can be valuable without propelling a car. A rear-wake or underbody system that saves more drag power than it consumes could lower fuel use or extend EV range. Trucks and vans are especially attractive because their wake losses and annual mileage are large.APSV je lahko vreden, ne da bi poganjal avtomobil. Sistem za zadnji vrtinec ali podvozje, ki prihrani več moči upora, kot je porabi, lahko zmanjša porabo goriva ali poveča doseg EV. Tovornjaki in kombiji so posebej zanimivi zaradi velikih izgub v vrtincu in velike letne kilometrine.

Air remains a hybrid subsystemZrak ostaja hibridni podsistem

For limited flight, the likely lift source remains a conventional distributed fan or rotor system. APSV contributes through flow control, drag reduction, sealing, stability, fault tolerance and transition management rather than claiming uncharged lift.Za omejen let ostaja verjeten vir vzgona konvencionalni porazdeljeni ventilatorski ali rotorski sistem. APSV prispeva s kontrolo toka, zmanjšanjem upora, tesnjenjem, stabilnostjo, odpornostjo na odpovedi in upravljanjem prehodov, ne z brezplačnim vzgonom.

Universal value is mission valueUniverzalna vrednost je misijska vrednost

A rescue craft need not beat the best car, boat, submarine and helicopter separately. It must be good enough across the required modes to reach places that several separate vehicles cannot reach quickly or safely. Costs and compromises remain explicit.Reševalno vozilo ne rabi ločeno premagati najboljšega avtomobila, čolna, podmornice in helikopterja. Dovolj dobro mora delovati v zahtevanih načinih, da hitro ali varno doseže kraje, ki jih več ločenih vozil ne more. Stroški in kompromisi ostanejo izrecni.
