APSVActive Pressure-Skin Vehicle.Aktivno tlačno-kožno vozilo.

A research seed and Python-arena program exploring whether a vehicle can use its surface as an active fluid-processing skin: moving air or water along the body, shaping pressure, reducing drag, improving control, and sometimes producing thrust through explicit momentum exchange.Raziskovalno seme in Python-arena program, ki preverja, ali lahko vozilo uporablja svojo površino kot aktivno fluidno kožo: vodi zrak ali vodo po trupu, oblikuje tlak, zmanjšuje upor, izboljša krmiljenje in včasih ustvari potisk z izrecno izmenjavo momenta.

What APSV isKaj je APSV

Not a claim that a flying submarine already exists. It is a structured way to keep many propulsion and flow-control ideas alive long enough to measure them.Ni trditev, da leteča podmornica že obstaja. Je strukturiran način, da veliko pogonskih in flow-control idej ostane živih dovolj dolgo, da jih izmerimo.

Core ideaJedrna ideja

Instead of treating the vehicle hull as passive, APSV treats it as an active organ: suction slots, tangential sheet jets, Coandă caps, cilia-like skins, MHD/plasma branches, traveling waves, and recirculating surface loops compete in an arena.Namesto da je trup pasiven, ga APSV obravnava kot aktiven organ: sesalne reže, tangencialni ploskovni izpihi, Coandă kapice, ciliarne/dlakaste kože, MHD/plazemske veje, potujoči valovi in recirkulacijske površinske zanke tekmujejo v areni.

Rule:Pravilo: nothing is deleted early; every seed is ranked, tagged, and tested against its mission.nič se ne izbriše prezgodaj; vsako seme se rangira, označi in testira glede na misijo.

NameIme

APSV = Active Pressure-Skin Vehicle.

For air/water it means pressure and flow-control skin. For the future space branch, APSV-S shifts the interpretation toward an active momentum-skin interacting with photons, plasma, solar wind, or artificial beams.Za zrak/vodo pomeni tlačno in flow-control kožo. Za prihodnjo vesoljsko vejo APSV-S pomen razširi v aktivno momentum-kožo, ki deluje s fotoni, plazmo, sončnim vetrom ali umetnimi žarki.

Mechanism familiesDružine mehanizmov

The arena separates pressure, thrust, drag reduction, control, energy, and warnings instead of merging everything into one magical score.Arena loči tlak, potisk, zmanjšanje upora, kontrolo, energijo in opozorila, namesto da vse zlepi v eno magično oceno.

Slot-sheet + Coandă

Thin tangential sheet jets over curved or capped surfaces; currently the strongest clean underwater signal.Tanki tangencialni ploskovni curki po ukrivljenih površinah; trenutno najmočnejši čist podvodni signal.

M13 Surface Jet Loop

Bojan seed: slot injector → surface wall-jet → collector/suction slot → flat duct recirculation, with optional rear bleed for net thrust.Bojanovo seme: režni vpih → površinski wall-jet → zajem/sesalna reža → ploski recirkulacijski kanal, z opcijskim zadnjim bleed potiskom.

M1 / traveling-wave skin

A frontier water branch inspired by active undulation and biological locomotion. Strong raw signal, still under audit.Frontier vodna veja, navdihnjena z aktivnim valovanjem in biološkim gibanjem. Močan raw signal, še pod auditom.

Cilia / hair skin

Dense active fibers or cilia-like motion; more plausible in water than in air and useful for fine flow-control.Gosta aktivna vlakna ali ciliarno gibanje; bolj verjetno v vodi kot v zraku in koristno za fin flow-control.

MHD / EHD / plasma

Electromagnetic branches: MHD for conductive water, EHD/plasma/ion wind for air. Real relatives exist, but scale and efficiency matter.Elektromagnetne veje: MHD za prevodno vodo, EHD/plazma/ionski veter za zrak. Sorodne tehnologije obstajajo, a merilo in izkoristek sta ključna.

Conventional baselines

Rotor, ducted fan, marine screw and pump-jet are not executioners. They are measuring sticks and mission-specific references.Rotor, ducted fan, ladijski propeler in pump-jet niso rablji. So merilni trakovi in misijsko odvisne reference.

Why it matters even if parts already existZakaj je pomembno, tudi če deli že obstajajo

APSV is not a novelty claim about every component. It is an integration and arena program.APSV ni trditev, da je vsak del nov. Je integracijski in arenski program.

The useful novelty questionKoristno vprašanje novosti

Boundary-layer suction, Coandă blowing, pump jets, synthetic jets, MHD and active skins have relatives in the real world. The APSV question is different: which combination, in which mission, with which energy, control, safety and multi-medium tradeoff, survives measurable arena testing?Sesanje mejne plasti, Coandă vpih, pump-jeti, synthetic jeti, MHD in aktivne kože imajo sorodnike v realnem svetu. APSV vprašanje je drugo: katera kombinacija, v kateri misiji, s kakšnim energetskim, kontrolnim, varnostnim in multi-medium kompromisom, preživi merljivo arensko testiranje?

Air and universal vehiclesZrak in univerzalna vozila

A universal vehicle should not be killed for failing to beat a helicopter in pure hover or a boat in pure water. Baselines are references, not judges.Univerzalnega vozila ne smemo ubiti, ker v čistem hoverju ne premaga helikopterja ali v vodi čolna. Baseline je referenca, ne sodnik.

Likely air architectureVerjetna zračna arhitektura

For air, APSV probably begins as a hybrid: distributed ducted fans / many protected small fans for lift, plus active surface flow-control for stability, safety, drag reduction, and transition behavior.Za zrak APSV verjetno začne kot hibrid: porazdeljeni ducted fani / veliko zaščitenih malih ventilatorjev za vzgon, plus aktivna površinska kontrola toka za stabilnost, varnost, manjši upor in prehode.

APSV-S / Space backlog

A future extension: active momentum-skin in photon, plasma, solar-wind, exhaust-plume, or artificial beam flows.Prihodnja razširitev: aktivna momentum-koža v tokovih fotonov, plazme, sončnega vetra, raketnega izpuha ali umetnega žarka.

Near-Sun probe

A probe near the Sun could interact with stronger photon pressure and solar-wind/plasma streams. This belongs to APSV-S, not the current air/water scoring.Sonda blizu Sonca bi lahko delovala z močnejšim fotonskim pritiskom in tokovi sončnega vetra/plazme. To spada v APSV-S, ne v trenutni zrak/voda scoring.

Plume / beam rider

A trailer/pod could interact with a directed plasma or ion beam, but never reactionlessly: momentum and energy must still close.Prikolica/pod bi lahko deloval z usmerjenim plazemskim ali ionskim žarkom, a nikoli brez reakcije: moment in energija morata ostati zaprta.

Clean signal

The strongest clean signal is still water / underwater / inspection. The leading family is tangential slot-sheet + Coandă on teardrop / capsule-like underwater pods.

Frontier signal

M1 traveling-wave water scout is the strongest raw/anomaly branch and needs deeper audit. It is promising, but not yet a clean final winner.

M13 status

M13 recirculating slot-suction loop is alive. Closed-loop is only slightly better than open slot in current matched tests; semi-closed M13 shows a more interesting small advantage.

Air / universal status

Air hover and universal air+water have no clean winner yet. Baselines are references, not guillotines: future scoring must include mission value, safety, redundancy, and multi-medium capability.
