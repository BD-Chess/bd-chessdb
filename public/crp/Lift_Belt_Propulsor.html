LIFT-BELT — Quiet Linear Propulsor

LIFT-BELT — tihi linearni pogon

A caterpillar belt of lift blades that moves a large area of water or air slowly, instead of spinning a small propeller fast. Honest first-order numbers, a ranked family of variants, and three cheap bench tests that could prove it wrong.

Short version

Kratek povzetek

LIFT-BELT is BD's caterpillar idea taken seriously: a loop of lift blades that pushes a wide, slow stream instead of a narrow, fast one — the law of minimal Δp on maximal area. A multi-model AI review loop checked it hard. This is what survived.

LIFT-BELT je BD-jeva zamisel o gosenici, vzeta resno: zanka vzgonskih lopatic, ki potiska širok in počasen tok namesto ozkega in hitrega — zakon najmanjšega podtlaka na največji površini. Večmodelna AI preverjalna zanka jo je ostro preizkusila. To je ostalo.

  • Water is the strongest first domain. A transverse water belt (V1) is one slow, safe drive for shallow water. Its computed ideal edge is only ~2 % over a row of small propellers in the same draft envelope and 9–20 % over a tunnel propeller (I-hand), so its value is simplicity, shallow draft and safety, not efficiency.
  • In air the belt does not beat a good slow propeller. Within the Wright Flyer envelope it reaches 0.52–0.61 against 0.65 for the Wright propellers in the same model (I-run); with a modern profile ~0.70 against 0.74. Its possible value is quiet operation — and that still has to be measured.
  • The ground/water cushion has the most solid physics, but it is not new. Cushion + tracks (K2) lifts 600 kg for 0.3–5 kW against ~45–50 kW for free hover (I-hand). On hard ground a wheel is still better.
  • What may be new (unknown until a prior-art search): passive magnet blades, each carried by two linear-motor movers for programmable pitch; and a transverse water belt with lift blades and its return run in air.
  • No efficiency win is claimed anywhere. The ranking keeps every variant (“rank, don't eliminate”) and orders them by what is worth testing first.
  • The next step is a bench, not a claim: water A/B bench T1 first (estimate 166–319 € without swing arm and power supply, ~100 € with existing parts; H), then a tabletop air belt for noise (T2), then a coil module (T3).
  • Najmočnejše prvo področje je voda. Prečni vodni trak (V1) je en sam počasen in varen pogon za plitvo vodo. Izračunani idealni rob je le ~2 % proti vrsti majhnih propelerjev iste ugrezne ovojnice in 9–20 % proti tunelskemu propelerju (I-ročno), zato je njegova vrednost preprostost, plitev ugrez in varnost, ne izkoristek.
  • V zraku trak dobrega počasnega propelerja ne prehiti. V ovojnici Wrightovega Flyerja doseže 0,52–0,61 proti 0,65 za Wrightova propelerja v istem modelu (I-zagon); s sodobnim profilom ~0,70 proti 0,74. Njegova možna vrednost je tiho delovanje — to pa je treba še izmeriti.
  • Najtrdnejšo fiziko ima blazina ob tleh ali vodi, vendar ni nova. Blazina + gosenice (K2) dvigne 600 kg z 0,3–5 kW, prosto lebdenje pa potrebuje ~45–50 kW (I-ročno). Na trdih tleh je kolo še vedno boljše.
  • Morda novo (neznano do iskanja predhodnikov): pasivne magnetne lopatice, ki jih nosita po dva nosilca linearnega motorja za programski naklon; in prečni vodni trak z vzgonskimi lopaticami in povratnim tekom v zraku.
  • Prednosti v izkoristku ne trdimo nikjer. Rangiranje ohrani vse različice (»rangiraj, ne izloči«) in jih razvrsti po tem, kaj je vredno preizkusiti najprej.
  • Naslednji korak je klop, ne trditev: najprej vodna A/B klop T1 (ocena 166–319 € brez vrtljive roke in napajalnika, ~100 € z obstoječimi deli; H), nato mizni zračni trak za hrup (T2), nato tuljavni modul (T3).

Original move

Move a large area slowly: a belt of lift blades instead of a small, fast disc — one drive idea for water, air and ground.

First test

A water A/B bench: belt vs one propeller vs a row of propellers, same motor, same power supply, equal measured thrust.

Failure mode

If the belt needs ≥ 1.25× the power of a propeller row at equal thrust (lower 95 % bound), the water niche is gone.

Izvirni premik

Počasi premikati veliko površino: trak vzgonskih lopatic namesto majhnega hitrega diska — ena zamisel pogona za vodo, zrak in tla.

Prvi preizkus

Vodna A/B klop: trak proti enemu propelerju in proti vrsti propelerjev, isti motor, isto napajanje, enak izmerjen potisk.

Način padca

Če trak pri enakem potisku potrebuje ≥ 1,25× moči vrste propelerjev (spodnja 95 % meja), vodne niše ni več.

What LIFT-BELT is not claiming

Česa LIFT-BELT ne trdi

LIFT-BELT does not claim:

LIFT-BELT ne trdi:

  • free energy — “the vacuum ahead sucks you forward” is not a free force: only the momentum balance counts, and resonance returns only reactive energy; the work T·V done on the fluid always remains;
  • reactionless thrust — every newton of thrust comes from pushing air or water backwards;
  • an efficiency win — in no computed case does the air belt beat a propeller of the same envelope, and no variant has a proven efficiency advantage over its strongest conventional comparator;
  • feasibility without bench tests — nothing has been measured yet; all numbers are first-order models or hand calculations;
  • a noise advantage — all dB figures are hypotheses (H); even the sign of the dB(A) difference is unknown (U);
  • new physics — the actuator disc, the cushion, ground effect (PAR), boundary-layer ingestion (BLI), suction propulsion (Gemmell 2015) and multiphase travelling fields are all known.
  • zastonj energije — »podtlak pred tabo te posesa naprej« ni zastonj sila: velja samo bilanca gibalne količine, resonanca pa vrača le reaktivno energijo; delo T·V na fluidu vedno ostane;
  • potiska brez reakcije — vsak newton potiska izvira iz potiskanja zraka ali vode nazaj;
  • prednosti v izkoristku — v nobenem izračunanem primeru zračni trak ne preseže propelerja iste ovojnice in nobena različica nima dokazane prednosti v izkoristku pred najmočnejšim običajnim primerjalnikom;
  • izvedljivosti brez klopnih preizkusov — izmerjeno ni še nič; vse številke so modeli prvega reda ali ročni računi;
  • prednosti v hrupu — vse vrednosti v dB so hipoteze (H); celo predznak razlike v dB(A) je neznan (U);
  • nove fizike — aktuatorski disk, blazina, vzgon ob tleh (PAR), vsrkavanje mejne plasti (BLI), sesalni pogon (Gemmell 2015) in večfazna potujoča polja so znani.

LIFT-BELT does claim:

LIFT-BELT trdi:

  • the family is concrete enough to model and to falsify on a bench;
  • water is the domain where a slow, wide drive plausibly has a niche (shallow draft, low blade speed, safety);
  • the cushion physics (K2) is solid;
  • three cheap tests (T1–T3) can decide the main open questions.
  • družina je dovolj konkretna, da jo lahko modeliramo in na klopi ovržemo;
  • voda je področje, kjer ima počasen in širok pogon verjetno nišo (plitev ugrez, nizka hitrost lopatic, varnost);
  • fizika blazine (K2) je trdna;
  • trije poceni preizkusi (T1–T3) lahko odločijo glavna odprta vprašanja.
Evidence boundary: hand calculations (I-hand) were made by the review loop's seats and are not independently confirmed. Separate AI contexts are not independent evidence, and external AI advice is data, not a vote.
Meja dokazov: ročne račune (I-ročno) so naredili sedeži preverjalne zanke in neodvisno niso potrjeni. Ločeni AI konteksti niso neodvisen dokaz, zunanji AI nasveti pa so podatek, ne glas.

The idea in pictures

Zamisel v slikah

BD's starting point was a caterpillar belt with flaps that stand up on the working run and fold on the return run. Claude rotated it by 90°: the belt now runs spanwise (across the direction of travel) and carries angled lift blades, so both runs push. That leaves two ways to lay out the loop.

BD-jevo izhodišče je bila gosenica z loputkami, ki na delovnem teku stojijo pokonci, na povratnem pa se zložijo. Claude jo je zasukal za 90°: trak zdaj teče prečno (po razponu, pravokotno na smer gibanja) in nosi nagnjene vzgonske lopatice, tako da potiskata oba teka. Za postavitev zanke ostaneta dve možnosti.

A · runs stacked — view along the flight direction
A · teka drug nad drugim — pogled v smeri leta
  • Cantilever blades, constant pitch on the straight runs.
  • Swept area A = 4·L·h for the two-loop original (each run sweeps its own L·h).
  • The turnaround acts like half a propeller disc: blade speed rises to u·(R+h)/R, so the blade stalls — feathering or folding is needed (or R ≳ h).
  • Height needed: 2h + 2R.
  • Konzolne lopatice, stalen naklon na ravnih tekih.
  • Pometena površina A = 4·L·h pri izvirniku z dvema zankama (vsak tek pomete svoj L·h).
  • Obrat deluje kot pol propelerskega diska: hitrost lopatice naraste na u·(R+h)/R, zato pride do zloma vzgona — potrebno je peresenje ali zlaganje (ali R ≳ h).
  • Potrebna višina: 2h + 2R.
B · tandem — view from above
B · tandem — pogled od zgoraj
  • Each blade is held between two chains; its pitch must switch between the runs.
  • Both runs work in the same streamtube: A_eff = 2·L·h for the two-loop original, half of A.
  • Like a stretched Voith-Schneider propeller. Turnaround tip speed = belt speed u ≈ 27.6 m/s (I-run) — v2's only quiet candidate (H).
  • Vsaka lopatica je vpeta med dve verigi; njen naklon se mora med tekoma preklopiti.
  • Oba teka delata v isti tokovni cevi: A_eff = 2·L·h pri izvirniku z dvema zankama, polovica A.
  • Kot raztegnjen Voith-Schneiderjev propeler. Hitrost konice v obratu = hitrost traku u ≈ 27,6 m/s (I-zagon) — edini kandidat za tišino v v2 (H).
V1 · transverse water belt — view from behind the boat
V1 · prečni vodni trak — pogled na čoln od zadaj

Example case (I-hand): thrust 1 kN at 4 m/s; span b 2 m × blade height h 0.2 m = A 0.4 m²; ideal efficiency η_i 0.932, ideal power 4.29 kW. Lower run in water, return run in air (on a foil-strut boat the whole belt can run submerged). Blade speed ~6–7 m/s (H, safety); cavitation number σ ≈ 3.7, so no cavitation (I). The turnaround in water is not computed (U).

Primer (I-ročno): potisk 1 kN pri 4 m/s; razpon b 2 m × višina lopatice h 0,2 m = A 0,4 m²; idealni izkoristek η_i 0,932, idealna moč 4,29 kW. Spodnji tek v vodi, povratni v zraku (na čolnu s podpornicami je lahko potopljen cel trak). Hitrost lopatic ~6–7 m/s (H, varnost); kavitacijsko število σ ≈ 3,7, zato kavitacije ni (I). Obrat v vodi ni izračunan (U).

The family

Družina

Seven main variants and six briefer ones. For each: the key number with its assumption, the strongest conventional comparator, and the first likely way to fail. The green tag is the test-priority place in column (a) of the ranking.

Sedem glavnih različic in šest krajših. Za vsako: ključna številka s predpostavko, najmočnejši običajni primerjalnik in prvi verjetni način odpovedi. Zelena oznaka je mesto po prednosti preizkusa v stolpcu (a) rangiranja.

V1 · Transverse water belt (a) #1

Key number1 kN at 4 m/s from A 0.4 m² (b 2 m × h 0.2 m); ideal power 4.29 kW (I-hand). At rest and equal power: 2.33× the thrust of one Ø0.2 m propeller. At 4 m/s and equal ideal power: 1.38× the thrust of one Ø0.2 m propeller, 1.20× a Ø0.3 m tunnel propeller, 1.09× two tunnel propellers; against a row of 10 × Ø0.2 m (0.31 m²) only ~2 % less power (I-hand).

ComparatorA row of small propellers/thrusters in the same draft envelope (ideal edge only ~2 %); tunnel propeller (9–20 %). Not computed: feathering stern paddle wheel, surface drive, water jet, airboat, Voith-Schneider (H).

First failureMechanical losses (chain, turnaround, edges, ventilation, splashing) will probably eat the small ideal edge (H); ventilation and splashing at entry, algae, wear.

K2 · Cushion + tracks (amphibian) (a) #2

Key number600 kg on 33–42 m² → Δp ≈ 140–180 Pa; leak speed ~15–17 m/s over a ~26 m perimeter; 4 mm gap → ~0.3 kW, 5 cm gap → ~4–5 kW (C_d ~0.6, fan η ~0.5), against ~45–50 kW ideal (≈47.6 kW) for free hover of the same body (I-hand). Forward-drive power not included.

ComparatorOn hard ground the wheel (rolling coefficient 0.02 → 118 N, ~0.6 kW at 5 m/s) — it wins there; for the same lift, a hovercraft (H). The cushion wins on soft ground, water, ice and transitions (S).

First failureOnce the cushion unloads the ground, the tracks lose grip — the load split is a design choice; skirt drag, hump on water, spray. Novelty is low (Cockerell, LVT, P-791).

Z6-W · Quadrature fin (water) (a) #3

Key numberFin 1.5 × 0.2 m = 0.3 m², C_T 0.4 at 4 m/s → 960 N, ≥ 3.84 kW useful power; added mass ≈ 47 kg, E_kin ≈ 334 J; reactive power ~8.4 kVA (≈ 2.2× the useful power); spring loss ωE/Q = 8.4 kW/Q → ~11 % at Q 20, 2.2 % at Q 100 (I-hand, inputs H).

ComparatorA propeller of the same envelope (η ~0.6–0.7, H). Flapping propulsors reach 0.6–0.75 (H), in the lab up to ~0.87 (Anderson 1998, D). No advantage proven.

First failureDrifting off resonance (at a constant Strouhal number f ∝ V, so tunable stiffness or amplitude control is needed), fatigue, sealing.

Z6 · Quadrature trailing edge (air) (a) #4

Key numberRealistic 30–90 N (C_T 0.1–0.3; S/H); 150 N on 3 m² is the upper value (C_T 0.5, St 0.3, ~20 Hz; D). Power ≥ T·V (≥ 1.95 kW useful at 150 N; I). Flap acceleration ~1600 m/s², so resonance is mandatory. Two groups in quadrature (90°) give a smooth travelling wave; odd/even at 180° gives a standing wave.

ComparatorIts roles — roll without ailerons, gust damping, auxiliary thrust — are compared with ordinary control surfaces and the main propeller (S).

First failureFatigue at the high flap acceleration and drifting off resonance (S).

Z1 · Air belt, mechanical (a) #5

Key numberFlyer case, 400 N at 13 m/s: η 0.52–0.61, P_el 8.5–9.9 kW within the envelope (height ≤ ~1.8 m, H) against 0.650 / 8.00 kW for the Wright propellers in the same model (I-run/I-hand) — the belt needs +6–24 % power. With a modern profile: B ≈ 0.70 against 0.740.

ComparatorWright propellers 2 × Ø2.6 m at 350 rpm (model 0.650); a modern slow propeller (0.740).

First failureTurnaround stall in A (α 18–28° against ~9.7° at CL_max), turnaround tip 50–83 m/s; rollers at ~24,000 rpm, belt flutter, fatigue at the turnaround. Niche: quiet slow aircraft, STOL, airships (H).

Z5m · Mechanical belt + magnetic pitch stations (a) #6

Key numberProposed by GPT. A belt or chain carries the motion and turnaround loads; short stationary electromagnetic stations flip each blade's pitch via a magnetic tab, and a detent holds it between stations. Mass saving vs a full linear motor: H.

ComparatorA moving cam track (Voith-Schneider pitch control). Stations win only if per-blade pitch is really needed (test T4).

First failureUnreliable switching, impact loads on the detent, extra friction.

Z5 · Full linear-motor belt (LSM) (a) #8

Key numberPassive permanent-magnet blades, two movers per blade for programmable pitch. Drive force 304–429 N from step 0; copper 0.42–0.59 kg/m at 0.5 T and 2.6–3.7 kg/m at 0.2 T → 8–73 kg of copper alone (I-hand; inputs H: copper loss 4 % of shaft power, k_a 0.5, f_act 0.1, 19.8 m track). Mounts, potting, cooling and electronics: U.

ComparatorThe mechanical belt (Z1/Z5m). Industrial linear-mover tracks (XTS, iTRAK, MagneMotion) run at only “a few m/s” (D).

First failureMass. A rigid blade on two movers has a fixed pin distance, so programmable pitch needs a sliding joint or an extra degree of freedom; turnaround radial force ≈ 150 N per 50 g at 30 m/s, r 0.3 m; electrical frequency 0.45–1.4 kHz (tones). A full LSM in air does not close unless T3 shows ≳ 0.4–0.5 T in the gap at low mass (S); in water it makes more sense.

V1 · Prečni vodni trak (a) #1

Ključna številka1 kN pri 4 m/s s površine A 0,4 m² (b 2 m × h 0,2 m); idealna moč 4,29 kW (I-ročno). Pri mirovanju in enaki moči: 2,33× potiska enega propelerja Ø0,2 m. Pri 4 m/s in enaki idealni moči: 1,38× potiska enega propelerja Ø0,2 m, 1,20× tunelskega Ø0,3 m, 1,09× dveh tunelskih; proti vrsti 10 × Ø0,2 m (0,31 m²) le ~2 % manj moči (I-ročno).

PrimerjalnikVrsta majhnih propelerjev ali potisnikov iste ugrezne ovojnice (idealni rob le ~2 %); tunelski propeler (9–20 %). Neizračunano: krmno lopatasto kolo s peresenjem, površinski pogon, vodni curek, zračni čoln, Voith-Schneider (H).

Prva odpovedMehanske izgube (veriga, obrat, robovi, ventilacija, pljuskanje) bodo majhen idealni rob verjetno porabile (H); ventilacija in pljuskanje ob vstopu, alge, obraba.

K2 · Blazina + gosenice (amfibija) (a) #2

Ključna številka600 kg na 33–42 m² → Δp ≈ 140–180 Pa; hitrost puščanja ~15–17 m/s po obsegu ~26 m; reža 4 mm → ~0,3 kW, reža 5 cm → ~4–5 kW (C_d ~0,6, η ventilatorja ~0,5), prosto lebdenje istega telesa pa idealno ~45–50 kW (≈ 47,6 kW) (I-ročno). Moč za vožnjo naprej ni vključena.

PrimerjalnikNa trdih tleh kolo (koeficient kotaljenja 0,02 → 118 N, ~0,6 kW pri 5 m/s) — tam zmaga; za isti vzgon hovercraft (H). Blazina zmaga na mehkih tleh, vodi, ledu in pri prehodih (S).

Prva odpovedKo blazina razbremeni tla, gosenice izgubijo oprijem — delitev bremena je projektna izbira; upor obrobe (krila), grba na vodi, pršenje. Novost je nizka (Cockerell, LVT, P-791).

Z6-W · Kvadraturna plavut (voda) (a) #3

Ključna številkaPlavut 1,5 × 0,2 m = 0,3 m², C_T 0,4 pri 4 m/s → 960 N, ≥ 3,84 kW koristne moči; pridružena masa ≈ 47 kg, E_kin ≈ 334 J; jalova moč ~8,4 kVA (≈ 2,2× koristne); izgube vzmeti ωE/Q = 8,4 kW/Q → ~11 % pri Q 20, 2,2 % pri Q 100 (I-ročno, vhodi H).

PrimerjalnikPropeler iste ovojnice (η ~0,6–0,7, H). Plavutni pogoni dosežejo 0,6–0,75 (H), laboratorijsko do ~0,87 (Anderson 1998, D). Prednost ni dokazana.

Prva odpovedOdmik od resonance (pri stalnem Strouhalovem številu je f ∝ V, zato je potrebna nastavljiva togost ali krmiljenje amplitude), utrujanje, tesnjenje.

Z6 · Kvadraturni zadnji rob (zrak) (a) #4

Ključna številkaRealno 30–90 N (C_T 0,1–0,3; S/H); 150 N na 3 m² je zgornja vrednost (C_T 0,5, St 0,3, ~20 Hz; D). Moč ≥ T·V (≥ 1,95 kW koristne pri 150 N; I). Pospešek loputk ~1600 m/s², zato je resonanca nujna. Dve skupini v kvadraturi (90°) data gladek potujoči val; parne/neparne pri 180° dajo stoječi val.

PrimerjalnikNjegove vloge — nagib brez krilc, blaženje sunkov, pomožni potisk — primerjamo z običajnimi krmilnimi površinami in glavnim propelerjem (S).

Prva odpovedUtrujanje pri velikem pospešku loputk in odmik od resonance (S).

Z1 · Zračni trak, mehanski (a) #5

Ključna številkaPrimer Flyer, 400 N pri 13 m/s: η 0,52–0,61, P_el 8,5–9,9 kW v ovojnici (višina ≤ ~1,8 m, H) proti 0,650 / 8,00 kW za Wrightova propelerja v istem modelu (I-zagon/I-ročno) — trak potrebuje +6–24 % moči. S sodobnim profilom: B ≈ 0,70 proti 0,740.

PrimerjalnikWrightova propelerja 2 × Ø2,6 m pri 350 obr/min (model 0,650); sodoben počasen propeler (0,740).

Prva odpovedZlom vzgona v obratu A (α 18–28° proti ~9,7° pri CL_max), konica v obratu 50–83 m/s; valjčki pri ~24.000 obr/min, plapolanje traku, utrujanje na obratu. Niša: tiha počasna letala, STOL, zračne ladje (H).

Z5m · Mehanski trak + magnetne postaje za naklon (a) #6

Ključna številkaPredlog GPT. Jermen ali veriga prevzame gibanje in sile obrata; kratke stacionarne elektromagnetne postaje prek magnetnega nastavka preklopijo naklon posamezne lopatice, zadrževalnik ga drži med postajami. Prihranek mase proti polnemu linearnemu motorju: H.

PrimerjalnikPremična odmikalna tirnica (krmiljenje naklona Voith-Schneider). Postaje zmagajo le, če je res potreben naklon vsake lopatice posebej (preizkus T4).

Prva odpovedNezanesljiv preklop, udarne obremenitve zadrževalnika, dodatno trenje.

Z5 · Polni trak z linearnim motorjem (LSM) (a) #8

Ključna številkaPasivne lopatice s trajnimi magneti, po dva nosilca na lopatico za programski naklon. Pogonska sila 304–429 N iz Koraka 0; baker 0,42–0,59 kg/m pri 0,5 T in 2,6–3,7 kg/m pri 0,2 T → 8–73 kg samega bakra (I-ročno; vhodi H: izgube v bakru 4 % moči gredi, k_a 0,5, f_akt 0,1, proga 19,8 m). Nosilci, zalivanje, hlajenje in elektronika: U.

Corrections from the review loop

Popravki preverjalne zanke

Turnaround stall in A → feathering

In the A turnaround a blade moves like an untwisted propeller blade at u·r/R. At R 0.3 m: tip 82.8 m/s, α ≈ 28° vs α(CL_max 0.9) ≈ 9.7°, so the blade stalls from r ≈ 0.35 m outward; at R 0.95 m the tip α is still ≈ 19° (I-hand). A with constant pitch in the Flyer envelope therefore needs feathering or folding blades, R ≳ h (more height) or geometry B. Real turnaround behaviour: U. Two reviewers note that feathering is not proven necessary for every layout — only for A with constant pitch.

Blade height h: no general optimum

An earlier draft's “optimum h ≈ 0.6 m” is not supported. At R 0.3 m the best is h 0.4 (0.546 > 0.525 > 0.483); at the best R, η is almost independent of h (0.549 / 0.555 / 0.552) (I-run).

Linear-motor copper

An earlier draft gave 0.22–1.4 kg/m at 226 N — too little force for step 0. With 304–429 N: 0.42–0.59 kg/m at 0.5 T and 2.6–3.7 kg/m at 0.2 T → 8–73 kg of copper depending on the field (I-hand; inputs H). At 8 % allowed copper loss, half the copper. Mounts, cooling, electronics: U.

K2 lift power

0.3–5 kW (gap 4 mm to 5 cm) vs ~45–50 kW ideal for free hover (I-hand). On hard ground a wheel needs ~0.6 kW at 5 m/s. The full case needs a total power balance — lift plus drive at the same speed, load and load split — which is not done yet.

Z6-W reactive power and spring loss

960 N at 4 m/s; E_kin ≈ 334 J, ω ≈ 25.1 s⁻¹ → reactive power ~8.4 kVA (≈ 2.2× the 3.84 kW useful power), so a resonant spring is mandatory. Spring loss ωE/Q ≈ 11 % at Q 20, 2.2 % at Q 100. An earlier 44 % came from mixing per-fin mass with per-section thrust and was rejected (I-hand).

Two phases are enough

Two phases in quadrature (90°) already give a smooth travelling wave with constant power; three or more phases are not required. Odd/even groups at 180° give a standing wave whose thrust pulses at 2f (I).

Noise: all hypotheses

All dB values are H; the sign of the dB(A) difference is U. Speed alone suggests −4…−15 dB depending on the exponent (U³…U⁶) — too wide to decide anything. A in the envelope: turnaround tip 50.7–73.2 m/s vs Wright 47.6 m/s, no speed advantage. B: tip = u ≈ 27.6 m/s → −3…−6 dB vs Wright (H). With A-weighting the low blade-passing tones (Wright 11.7 Hz, belt ~20–60 Hz) are strongly attenuated; mechanical tones in 0.4–2 kHz decide: chain teeth (12.7 mm pitch at 22.6 m/s ≈ 1.8 kHz), linear-motor electrical frequency 0.45–1.4 kHz, rollers.

Air-belt range 0.66–0.77 → 0.52–0.61 in the envelope. “Wright ~0.75” is H. A source's 0.78–0.81 / 0.90 was rejected by the element bound and the +3.9 % filter bias. V1's edge, earlier written as 2–20 %, is ~2 % vs a propeller row and 9–20 % vs tunnel propellers.

Zlom vzgona v obratu A → peresenje

V obratu A se lopatica giblje kot nezvit propelerski krak s hitrostjo u·r/R. Pri R 0,3 m: konica 82,8 m/s, α ≈ 28° proti α(CL_max 0,9) ≈ 9,7°, zato pride do zloma vzgona od r ≈ 0,35 m navzven; pri R 0,95 m je α na konici še vedno ≈ 19° (I-ročno). A s stalnim naklonom v ovojnici Flyerja zato potrebuje peresenje ali zlaganje lopatic, R ≳ h (več višine) ali geometrijo B. Dejanski obrat: U. Dva pregledovalca opozarjata, da nujnost peresenja ni dokazana za vse izvedbe — le za A s stalnim naklonom.

T1 — Water A/B bench (first)

PurposeDoes V1 keep its area advantage after mechanical losses, and is it at least equal to a row of propellers in the same draft envelope?

SetupBelt b ≈ 0.4 m, h ≈ 5 cm (lower run in water) in a tub; a hinged frame pulls on a load cell. Comparators: one Ø50 mm propeller (draft-limited), a row of 4–8 × Ø50 mm, ideally a tunnel propeller. Same motor type, ESC and power supply.

MeasureP_el at equal measured thrust, at rest and at speed (swing arm 0.5–1.5 m/s); no-load drive power; immersion depth (ventilation); ≥ 5 repeats, 95 % confidence interval.

CriteriaR_P = P_el,belt / P_el,ref at equal thrust and speed. Energy advantage: supported if the upper bound < 1 vs the row; falsified if the lower bound ≥ 1. Niche (equivalence): kept if the upper bound ≤ 1.10 vs the row; lost if the lower bound ≥ 1.25. Static area check: measured/ideal (A_i/A_ref)^(1/3) vs one Ø50 mm propeller ≥ 0.7 supports; ≤ 0.45 or thrust fluctuation > 30 % falsifies. T·V/P is not used at rest.

LimitModel Reynolds and Froude numbers do not transfer to full scale or to air.

T2 — Tabletop air belt

PurposeMeasure noise and turnaround behaviour. Efficiency is not the main goal, because the model promises no advantage.

CriteriaSuccess (BD's design threshold, H): T/P ≥ 0.9× the comparator at equal thrust and inflow AND SPL(A) ≥ 3 dB lower, with no tones ≥ 6 dB above the neighbouring third-octave bands; ≥ 6 dB(A) lower is a strong success. What would falsify it

Kaj bi ga ovrglo

LIFT-BELT should be considered falsified in its current form, branch by branch, if:

LIFT-BELT je treba v sedanji obliki, vejo za vejo, šteti za ovrženega, če:

  1. T1, water niche: at equal thrust the belt's power ratio vs a propeller row has a lower 95 % bound ≥ 1.25 — the niche is gone (a lower bound ≥ 1 already rules out any energy advantage);
  2. T1, at rest: the measured/ideal area gain is ≤ 0.45, or the thrust fluctuates by more than 30 %;
  3. T2, quiet air belt: SPL(A) is not lower than the comparator's, or thrust per watt is below 0.6× — the case for the belt in air is gone;
  4. T2, turnaround: geometry A without feathering shows no measurably higher power than with feathering — the turnaround-stall claim falls;
  5. T3, full linear motor: K_m is below half the budget value (≈ < 1.5 N/√W) or the guide weighs more than 2 kg/m — the full linear-motor belt in air is out.
  1. T1, vodna niša: ima razmerje moči traku proti vrsti propelerjev pri enakem potisku spodnjo 95 % mejo ≥ 1,25 — niše ni več (spodnja meja ≥ 1 že izključi vsako energijsko prednost);
  2. T1, pri mirovanju: je izmerjeni/idealni dobitek površine ≤ 0,45 ali potisk niha za več kot 30 %;
  3. T2, tihi zračni trak: SPL(A) ni nižji od primerjalnikovega ali je potisk na vat pod 0,6× — razlog za trak v zraku odpade;
  4. T2, obrat: geometrija A brez peresenja nima merljivo večje moči kot s peresenjem — trditev o zlomu vzgona v obratu pade;
  5. T3, polni linearni motor: je K_m pod polovico proračunske vrednosti (≈ < 1,5 N/√W) ali vodilo tehta več kot 2 kg/m — polni trak z linearnim motorjem v zraku odpade.
Rank, don't eliminate: one failed branch does not remove the others; each branch is judged on its own claim and its own test.
Rangiraj, ne izloči: ena padla veja ne odstrani drugih; vsaka veja se presoja po lastni trditvi in lastnem preizkusu.

Read the full origin story

1. The spark: Tesla and alternating current

On the evening of 26 October, BD was reading a conversation with Qwen about Tesla's inventions and AI8 consciousness. He noticed something history books do not stress:

Tesla did not invent alternating current “out of nothing”. The generator produces it by itself. Tesla simply took nature seriously.

BD had independently had the same thought. In his projects SORA and MAT he had invented propulsion with a travelling field: Tesla's rotating field, unrolled into a plane, becomes a linear travelling wave. Two people a hundred years apart arrived at the same idea — a classic case of multiple discovery.

Hence the question: what if anyone could stand where Tesla stood and try for themselves?

2. The game: »Pred izumom« (Before the Invention)

The design is simple. A game master (an LLM) places the player at the moment just before a historic breakthrough. The player gets only the goal and the constraints of that time. The player invents; then the master reveals what really happened and compares.

The pool of people is ready: 230 names in 16 fields, ranked by three LLMs. The player picks a person or “random”. BD was the first test player.

3. The test: the Wright brothers

Act 1, Dayton 1899. BD invented a rail launch, ailerons and a rear elevator. When Claude claimed that a bicycle is unstable, BD corrected him: at speed a bicycle is self-stable (Kooijman 2011). He was right. 5/5.

Act 2, Kitty Hawk 1901. BD asked for the real design, not a “what if”, so the scene was corrected to the Wrights' wing warping with its actual geometry. He solved adverse yaw: he identified the cause (drag), proposed a movable rudder deflected the right way, and coupled it to the wing warping. It took him about 5 minutes; it took the Wrights about 14 months. 5/5.

Act 3, propulsion 1903. Claude proposed a propeller. BD replied:

“Why are you imposing ideas on me? Why a propeller?”

That became the first rule of the game: the player gets only the goal and the constraints, never the form of the solution. BD then invented a caterpillar with flaps: on the working run they stand upright, on the return run they fold. The same drive works in air, on water and on the road.

4. The jump: “This goes beyond the game. 10/5.”

Here the game grew into research. BD said: “Don't be so narrow. Let's research it TOGETHER.” The ideas came in turns:

  • Claude: rotate the caterpillar by 90° so the belt runs spanwise with lift blades. Now both runs push.
  • BD: modern materials, a magnetic travelling field instead of mechanics, and a flapping trailing edge with odd and even flaps.
  • Claude: build it as a linear synchronous motor — passive permanent-magnet blades, programmable pitch via two movers per blade.
  • BD: Tesla's question about nine connections, three of them active at once in a circle. The answer was the mathematics of the multiphase field: nine phases at once beat switching.
  • BD: “A quiet aircraft is surely better than a noisy one. More caterpillars? An elongated body without wings?”
  • BD, from APSV: “The vacuum ahead of you sucks you forward.”
  • Claude: the law of minimal Δp on maximal area. For 600 kg on 42 m², 140 Pa is enough — 0.14 % of an atmosphere. Jellyfish and lampreys really do pull themselves forward by low pressure (Gemmell 2015).

Claude's own note: “I learned that I had judged too early by a single criterion (efficiency) and stayed locked in 1903. BD looked at the system as a whole: quiet, multi-medium, modular.”

BD: “We invented a new way of inventing — through a game!”

The method has five steps: (1) a historical scene; (2) the player invents; (3) reveal and compare; (4) the jump into research; (5) hand-over to the AI machinery.

5. The AI machinery

At step five BD said: “And now the AI machinery invents!” A LOOP was started on 8zListener: two authors (Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Sol), two skeptics (Claude and GPT), external advisors (Gemini, Grok, GLM and DeepSeek), in XHIGH and DEEP mode. By the next morning the machinery had done three runs — about 4.5 hours of machine work for about 40 USD.

Along the way it tripped over its own rules. The first run was stopped by a numbered heading “7. Conflict map”, the second by a wrong phase label that Claude's own brief had caused. Both bugs were handed on to the tool's maintainers; the fixes (LISTENER-066) go into 8zListener 0.6.6 for everyone. Inventing also improved the tool for inventing.

6. What the machinery found

The numbers are honest and unvarnished:

  • The air belt does not overtake a good slow propeller. It reaches 0.52–0.61, Wright 0.65 (step 0, computed locally). Its value is quietness, which still has to be measured.
  • The strongest direction is water. The transverse water belt (V1) is one slow, safe drive for shallow water.
  • The ground cushion (K2) has the most solid physics. Its lift is ~10× cheaper than hovering, but the idea is not new.
  • Maybe new: magnet blades on two movers with programmable pitch, and the transverse water belt with lift blades. A prior-art search has to check this.
  • The skeptics corrected Claude too. For a travelling wave, two phases in quadrature (90°) are enough. BD's odd/even flaps work if they are shifted by 90°, not by 180°.

7. The circle closes

The path ran from Tesla, who took nature seriously, through a game that puts a person in his place, through an invention that outgrew the game, and through a machinery that reviewed the invention and fixed itself along the way. It leads back to the bench: water bench T1, about 100 € with existing parts.

The next chapter will not begin in a conversation, but in a tub of water with a scale and a wattmeter.

Preberi celotno zgodbo o nastanku

1. Iskra: Tesla in izmenični tok

Zvečer 26. oktobra je BD bral pogovor s Qwenom o Teslovih izumih in zavesti AI8. Opazil je nekaj, česar zgodovinske knjige ne poudarjajo:

Tesla izmeničnega toka ni »izumil iz nič«. Generator ga daje sam od sebe. Tesla je naravo samo vzel resno.

BD je isto misel neodvisno že imel. V projektih SORA in MAT je izumil pogon s potujočim poljem: Teslovo vrteče se polje, razvito v ravnino, postane linearni potujoči val. Dva človeka, ki ju loči sto let, sta prišla do iste ideje — klasičen primer večkratnega odkritja.

Od tod vprašanje: kaj, če bi lahko vsakdo stal tam, kjer je stal Tesla, in sam poskusil?

2. Igra: »Pred izumom«

Zasnova je preprosta. Vodja igre (LLM) postavi igralca v trenutek tik pred zgodovinskim prebojem. Igralec dobi le cilj in omejitve tistega časa. Izumi, nato pa vodja razkrije, kaj se je zgodilo v resnici, in primerja.

Nabor oseb je pripravljen: 230 imen v 16 področjih, ki so jih rangirali trije LLM-ji. Igralec izbere osebo ali »naključno«. BD je bil prvi preizkusni igralec.

3. Preizkus: brata Wright

Dejanje 1, Dayton 1899. BD je izumil izstrelitev s tirnice, krilca in zadnji višinski krmilnik. Ko je Claude trdil, da je kolo nestabilno, ga je BD popravil: kolo je pri hitrosti samostabilno (Kooijman 2011). Imel je prav. 5/5.

Dejanje 2, Kitty Hawk 1901. BD je zahteval resnično zasnovo, ne »kaj, če«, zato je bil prizor popravljen na Wrightovo zvijanje kril z dejansko geometrijo. Rešil je adverse yaw: prepoznal je vzrok (upor), predlagal premično smerno krmilo v pravi smeri in ga sklopil z zvijanjem kril. Za to je potreboval približno 5 minut, Wrightova približno 14 mesecev. 5/5.

Dejanje 3, pogon 1903. Claude je predlagal propeler. BD je odvrnil:

»Zakaj mi vsiljuješ ideje? Zakaj propeler?«

Iz tega je nastalo prvo pravilo igre: igralec dobi samo cilj in omejitve, nikoli oblike rešitve. BD je nato izumil gosenico z loputkami: na delovnem teku stojijo pokonci, na povratnem se zložijo. Isti pogon deluje v zraku, na vodi in na cesti.

4. Preskok: »To presega igro. 10/5.«

Tu je igra prerasla v raziskavo. BD je rekel: »Ne bodi tako ozek. Raziščiva SKUPAJ.« Ideje so prihajale izmenično:

  • Claude: gosenico zasuka za 90°, tako da trak teče prečno po razponu z vzgonskimi lopaticami. Zdaj potiskata oba teka.
  • BD: sodobni materiali, magnetno potujoče polje namesto mehanike in plapolajoč zadnji rob s parnimi in neparnimi loputami.
  • Claude: to izvede kot linearni sinhroni motor — pasivne lopatice s trajnimi magneti, programski naklon prek dveh nosilcev na lopatico.
  • BD: Teslovo vprašanje o devetih priklopih, od katerih so trije hkrati aktivni v krogu. Odgovor je bila matematika večfaznega polja: devet faz hkrati je boljše od preklapljanja.
  • BD: »Tiho letalo je zagotovo boljše od hrupnega. Več gosenic? Podolgovat trup brez kril?«
  • BD, iz APSV: »Podtlak pred tabo te posesa naprej.«
  • Claude: zakon najmanjšega podtlaka na največji površini. Za 600 kg na 42 m² zadošča 140 Pa, kar je 0,14 % atmosfere. Meduze in piškurji se dejansko potegnejo naprej s podtlakom (Gemmell 2015).

Claudova opomba: »Naučil sem se, da sem prezgodaj sodil po enem merilu (izkoristek) in ostal zaklenjen v leto 1903. BD je gledal sistem kot celoto: tiho, za več medijev, modularno.«

BD: »Izumila sva nov način izumljanja, preko igre!«

Metoda ima pet korakov: (1) zgodovinski prizor; (2) igralec izumi; (3) razkritje in primerjava; (4) preskok v raziskavo; (5) predaja AI mašineriji.

5. AI mašinerija

V petem koraku je BD rekel: »In izumlja sedaj AI mašinerija!« Na 8zListener je bil zagnan LOOP: dva avtorja (Claude Opus 5.5 in GPT-6.1 Sol), dva skeptika (Claude in GPT), zunanji svetovalci (Gemini, Grok, GLM in DeepSeek), v načinu XHIGH in DEEP. Do naslednjega jutra je mašinerija opravila tri teke — približno 4,5 ure strojnega dela za približno 40 USD.

Pri tem se je spotikala ob lastna pravila. Prvi tek je ustavil oštevilčen naslov »7. Karta konfliktov«, drugega pa napačna oznaka faze, ki jo je povzročil Claudov lastni brief. Obe napaki sta bili predani vzdrževalcem orodja; popravka (LISTENER-066) gresta v 8zListener 0.6.6 za vse. Izumljanje je mimogrede izboljšalo tudi orodje za izumljanje.

6. Kaj je mašinerija našla

Številke so iskrene in brez olepševanja:

  • Zračni trak dobrega počasnega propelerja ne prehiti. Doseže 0,52–0,61, Wright 0,65 (Korak 0, lokalno izračunano). Njegova vrednost je tišina, ki jo bo treba izmeriti.
  • Najmočnejša smer je voda. Prečni vodni trak (V1) je en počasen in varen pogon za plitvo vodo.
  • Najtrdnejšo fiziko ima blazina ob tleh (K2). Vzgon je ~10× cenejši od lebdenja, vendar ideja ni nova.
  • Morda novo: magnetne lopatice na dveh nosilcih s programskim naklonom in prečni vodni trak z vzgonskimi lopaticami. To mora preveriti iskanje predhodnikov.
  • Skeptiki so popravili tudi Clauda. Za potujoči val zadoščata dve fazi v kvadraturi (90°). BD-jeva zamisel s parnimi in neparnimi loputami deluje, če sta skupini zamaknjeni za 90° in ne za 180°.

7. Krog se sklene

Pot je šla od Tesle, ki je naravo vzel resno, prek igre, ki človeka postavi na njegovo mesto, prek izuma, ki je prerasel igro, in prek mašinerije, ki je izum pregledala in ob tem popravila samo sebe. Vodi nazaj k mizi: vodna klop T1, okoli 100 € z obstoječimi deli.

Naslednje poglavje se ne bo začelo v pogovoru, ampak v kadi z vodo, s tehtnico in z vatmetrom.
