Grand Challenge Arenas

A single map of the formal, benchmark, and practical arenas where MDLxDCC will be tested: from the Clay Millennium Prize Problems to ARC, Hutter, SAT/SMT, CASP, DREAM, XPRIZE, and already-working practical domains like TSP, DNA/RNA, NAS, Sudoku, chess, compression, trading, and RH prime gaps.
Version 0.2 · May 3, 2026 · BD × AI · public working page · linked hub
Core distinction. MDLxDCC.html explains the kernel. MDLxDCC-arenas.html tracks where we test it. Deep-dive arena papers such as MDLxDCC-arena-millennium.html and MDLxDCC-arena-rh.html document individual high-value arenas.

1. Arena model

Each arena follows the same pattern:

problem → trace → encoding → MDL sensors → null controls → DCC search/governance → invariant target → practical output or proof bridge

Formal frontier

proof-facing

Problems where the endpoint is a formal invariant, theorem, or proof bridge. Clay Millennium problems belong here.

Benchmark frontier

metric-facing

Competitions and open benchmarks with clear scoring: ARC, Hutter, SAT/SMT/PACE, CASP, DREAM, MLPerf, Kaggle-like arenas.

Practical frontier

use-facing

Domains where value comes from working solutions now: TSP/trips, routing, compression, trading, drug discovery, protein folding, cyber, logistics.
