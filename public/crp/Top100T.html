What this page is doing

The page separates cosmic history from existence source. A model can score high at describing the early observable universe and still score lower as an explanation of being. The goal is not to kill strange seeds, but to place them honestly.

Important correction

Big Bang / ΛCDM is not treated as a theory of why existence exists. It is ranked as a strong observable-history model and a weak absolute-origin model. That is why it appears in the Top 20 but does not lead the map.

ACR usage

ACR is the single umbrella item for our consciousness/source framework: AC as ground, CFH as coupling bridge, AC-WFP as whole-first physicalization. It is not split into three items here.

Scoring methodology

Score is research priority, not truth probability

Each item receives a 0–100 rank-don’t-eliminate score from source power, world mechanism, empirical contact, consciousness-contact and testability. After the MAL v1.5.4 review, this score is explicitly firewalled from a separate science-first control lens: a theory can be strong physics and weak source, or strong source seed and still physically unverified.

Leading box

Top 5 source-layer candidates

These lead because they try to answer the deeper question, not merely what happened after an early hot dense state already existed.

#1
84

ACR — Absolute Consciousness–Resonance Framework

Existence is not produced from nothing; relative universes are resonant physicalizations of Absolute Consciousness.

Why it leads

ACR merges AC, CFH and AC-WFP into one ladder: absolute ground, resonance, field/coupling, whole-first physicalization, and local worlds. The MAL v1.5.4 council usefully sharpened the status: ACR can lead the source-layer lens while still being physics-bridge unverified. Its next serious gate is a neutral-twin / label-ablation test: keep the same target fixed, strip the ACR label, and ask whether the ACR version gives better compression, clearer predictions, or fewer derivation steps than the neutral mechanism. So ACR stays #1 here as a source-depth seed, not as a claimed finished physics theory.

#2
80

Holographic / Quantum-Information Universe

Spacetime and matter may be encoded through boundary information or deeper quantum-information structure.

Why it leads

The holographic principle is one of the strongest physics-side substrate ideas because it links geometry, entropy, black holes and information. It does not by itself say why existence exists, but it may describe the compression layer by which a universe becomes spatial. Strong bridge to AC-WFP: parts and volume may be emergent rather than primitive.

#3
78

Quantum Cosmology — Universe Wavefunction

The Universe is treated as a quantum object with a wavefunction, boundary condition, or transition amplitude.

Why it leads

This family tries to speak before ordinary spacetime, so it is much closer to an origin theory than standard Big Bang history. The problem is that a wavefunction, path integral, or law-space is already something; it still needs an ontological ground. Strong as a bridge between physics and source-layer questions.

#4
76

Penrose CCC — Conformal Cyclic Cosmology

The Big Bang is a crossover from a previous aeon, not absolute creation from nothing.

Why it leads

CCC is conceptually powerful because it directly refuses the naive “universe from nothing” reading. The far future of one aeon is conformally joined to the Big Bang of the next. Its evidence claims are disputed, but as a lens it is essential: Big Bang becomes transition surface, not origin of existence.

#5
74

Cyclic / Ekpyrotic / Brane-Bounce Universe

The Big Bang is a bounce, collision, or phase change in a larger pre-existing cosmic process.

Why it leads

This family keeps the seed alive that our hot early universe is not first cause. Brane collision and ekpyrotic smoothing offer alternatives to inflation. Weakness: model details are difficult and observational separation from inflation can be hard. Strength: it removes the worst “nothing produced everything” intuition.
