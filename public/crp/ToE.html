01 / THE WHOLE PICTURE

One programme. Different kinds of claim.

En program. Različne vrste trditev.

This is the wider question already developed by AC–WFP and Relational Spectral Emergence (RSE). The former ToE v0.4 page explored a narrower information-and-computation branch. That work survives as Predictive Compression & Access; it is not relabelled as a derivation of the universe.

The leading candidate spine is Ω–MSP: Modular–Spectral Physicalization. Its scientific version uses a neutral Whole, Ω. AC is an additional interpretation of fundamental presence; CFH discusses its proposed field-like availability; CCH asks separate biological questions. These layers share questions, not automatic evidence.

Candidate, not completion. The proposal below specifies objects, dependencies, rival branches and calculations that could move it forward. It does not yet provide one closed physical law yielding quantum theory, gravity, the observed particle spectrum and cosmology.

02 / CANDIDATE KERNEL

Ω–MSP: modular–spectral physicalization.

Ω–MSP: modularno-spektralna fizikalizacija.

The most developed spine is a modular–spectral path from one relational state to recoverable sectors, clocks, geometry and matter.

Let Ω denote the neutral Whole. For each candidate stable part α, associate a local algebra 𝒜α and a faithful state ωα. Under appropriate mathematical assumptions, Tomita–Takesaki theory supplies a modular operator Δα and modular flow σαt. The conjectural move is to ask whether the spectral data of this flow can organize what later appears as physical time, effective locality and matter.

AC0: Ω / fundamental presence → distinction
AC1: persistent subsystem α → (𝒜α, ωα)
AC2: modular data Δα, σαt
AC3: compatibility graph Γ(α,β) from mutual recoverability
AC4: geometry from graph/operator spectrum
AC5: matter from stable excitations / superselection sectors
AC6: observers from persistent information-bearing subsystems

The point is not that modular theory already derives gravity or particles. It is that it gives an exact mathematical object where the programme can start asking physical questions without inventing a fundamental background time.

What is missing: a state-selection principle, a derivation of the physical Hamiltonian and units, the Born rule and definite outcomes, Lorentzian geometry, Einstein dynamics, gauge groups, particle masses and constants. These are research targets, not hidden assumptions.

04 / GEOMETRY & MATTER

A bridge is not a derivation.

Most še ni izpeljava.

Several established research programmes show that geometry and gravity are deeply tied to quantum information: entanglement entropy, relative entropy, modular Hamiltonians and recovery all carry geometric content. Jacobson's thermodynamic argument and holographic relative-entropy results are strong bridge precedents. They do not prove AC–WFP.

A useful candidate metric between local parts is built from symmetric recoverability or information distance. For example, one may compare α and β through relative entropies or recovery fidelities and then infer graph distances. The exact construction is open: different monotone quantities can yield inequivalent geometry.

candidate edge: w(α,β) = F[ D(ωα || Rα←βωβ), D(ωβ || Rβ←αωα) ]
candidate geometry: d(α,β) = shortest/spectral distance on Γ

In the matter branch, stable excitations and superselection sectors are the cleanest known analogy. A real derivation must recover the Standard Model representation content and observed mass hierarchy rather than merely naming "stable patterns" particles.

[S10] [S11] [S12]

Več uveljavljenih raziskovalnih programov kaže globoko zvezo med geometrijo, gravitacijo in kvantno informacijo: entropija prepletenosti, relativna entropija, modularni Hamiltoniani in obnovitev nosijo geometrijsko vsebino. Jacobsonov termodinamski argument in holografski rezultati relativne entropije so močni precedensi za most. Ne dokazujejo AC–WFP.

Uporaben kandidat za metriko med lokalnimi deli lahko gradi na simetrični obnovljivosti ali informacijski razdalji. Na primer, α in β lahko primerjamo z relativnimi entropijami oziroma zvestobami obnovitve, nato pa iz tega sklepamo grafne razdalje. Natančna konstrukcija je odprta: različne monotone količine lahko dajo neekvivalentne geometrije.

kandidatna povezava: w(α,β) = F[ D(ωα || Rα←βωβ), D(ωβ || Rβ←αωα) ]
kandidatna geometrija: d(α,β) = najkrajša/spektralna razdalja na Γ

V veji snovi so stabilne vzbuditve in superselekcijski sektorji najčistejša znana analogija. Prava izpeljava mora dobiti reprezentacijsko vsebino Standardnega modela in opaženo hierarhijo mas, ne zgolj poimenovati "stabilnih vzorcev" za delce.

05 / ONE EXACT BRIDGE

From a quantum channel
to a well-defined coarse world.

Od kvantnega kanala
do dobro določene grobe ravni.

A concrete result survives from the broader AC–WFP work. Let Φ be a fine-grained quantum channel and C a coarse-graining map into a smaller description. A coarse channel Q exists exactly when

C ∘ Φ = Q ∘ C.

Operationally: two fine states that look identical after coarse-graining must continue to look identical after evolve-then-coarse. This is an exact projectability condition, not a metaphysical claim.

If exact projectability fails, one can ask for the best approximate Q and measure the defect, for example by relative entropy, trace distance or task-specific prediction loss.

Coarse predictability toy

p(A | microstate 1) 0.8
p(A | microstate 2) 0.8
mismatch 0

The coarse future is well-defined: both microstates give A probability 0.8.

This theorem-like bridge matters because emergent "parts" should not merely be visually recognizable. Their coarse state should support autonomous prediction to some tolerance. That gives Ω–MSP a falsifiable subsystem criterion to compare with spectral persistence and recovery.

Iz širšega dela AC–WFP ostaja konkreten rezultat. Naj bo Φ kvantni kanal na fini ravni in C grobozrnata preslikava v manjši opis. Grobi kanal Q obstaja natanko tedaj, ko velja

C ∘ Φ = Q ∘ C.

Operativno: dve fini stanji, ki sta po grobozrnjenju enaki, morata ostati enaki tudi po evoluciji in nato grobozrnjenju. To je natančen pogoj projektabilnosti, ne metafizična trditev.

Če natančna projektabilnost odpove, lahko poiščemo najboljši približni Q in merimo napako, na primer z relativno entropijo, sledno razdaljo ali izgubo napovedne naloge.

Groba napovedljivost — primer

p(A | mikrostanje 1) 0,8
p(A | mikrostanje 2) 0,8
neskladje 0

Groba prihodnost je dobro določena: obe mikrostanji pripišeta A verjetnost 0,8.

Ta most je pomemben, ker emergentni "deli" ne smejo biti zgolj vizualno prepoznavni. Njihovo grobo stanje mora znotraj tolerance omogočati samostojno napoved. S tem dobi Ω–MSP ovrgljiv kriterij podsistema, ki ga lahko primerjamo s spektralno trajnostjo in obnovljivostjo.

07 / QUANTUM CELL CANDIDATE

A dimensionless budget,
not a particle’s hidden bit count.

Brezdimenzijski proračun,
ne skrito število bitov delca.

For a chosen spatial region of radius R with mean energy budget Ē, the Bekenstein inequality gives the dimensionless upper bound

S(ρ) ≤ 2πRĒ/(ℏc),

under the assumptions of the bound. This does not assign an intrinsic entropy to a named particle. The information depends on a state, a region and an operational question. In continuum quantum field theory, the vacuum-subtracted quantity with the cleanest local meaning is relative entropy, not an absolute bit inventory attached to a point.

In the coherent-state example used in the review notes, the von Neumann entropy may equal the vacuum entropy even though the state is distinguishable from vacuum. The displacement then appears in a relative-entropy / modular-energy quantity such as Δ⟨K⟩, while accessible classical information belongs to a separately specified discrimination or communication task.

Q := 2πRĒ/(ℏc)
Q = 2π/ln 2 × [ RĒ/(ℏc) ] bits
if RĒ/(ℏc)=1, then Q = 9.06472 bits

The value 9.06472 bits is therefore a conditional coefficient of the chosen dimensionless budget RĒ/(ℏc)=1. It is not a measured universal electron, photon or quark memory capacity.

A better bridge question is whether a nested family of local algebras and states exhibits a reproducible crossover scale where distinguishability, recovery, modular-spectrum structure and approximate projectability change together. If such a scale exists without being chosen to force Q≈9, it could become evidence for a physical cell candidate. Until then, "~9 bits per particle" remains withdrawn as a universal claim.

[R17] [R18] [R19] [R20] [R21] [R22]

Za izbrano prostorsko območje polmera R s povprečnim energijskim proračunom Ē poda Bekensteinova neenačba brezdimenzijsko zgornjo mejo

S(ρ) ≤ 2πRĒ/(ℏc),

ob predpostavkah meje. To ne pripiše lastne entropije poimenovanemu delcu. Informacija je odvisna od stanja, območja in operativnega vprašanja. V kontinuumski kvantni teoriji polja ima med lokalnimi, vakuumsko odštetimi količinami najčistejši pomen relativna entropija, ne absolutni inventar bitov, pripet na točko.

V primeru koherentnega stanja iz preglednih opomb je von Neumannova entropija lahko enaka vakuumski, čeprav se stanje razlikuje od vakuuma. Premik se tedaj pokaže v relativni entropiji oziroma modularno-energijski količini, kot je Δ⟨K⟩, medtem ko dostopna klasična informacija pripada posebej določeni nalogi diskriminacije ali komunikacije.

Q := 2πRĒ/(ℏc)
Q = 2π/ln 2 × [ RĒ/(ℏc) ] bitov
če RĒ/(ℏc)=1, potem Q = 9,06472 bitov

Vrednost 9,06472 bitov je torej pogojni koeficient izbranega brezdimenzijskega proračuna RĒ/(ℏc)=1. Ni izmerjena univerzalna kapaciteta spomina elektrona, fotona ali kvarka.

Boljše povezovalno vprašanje je, ali gnezdena družina lokalnih algeber in stanj pokaže ponovljivo prehodno skalo, kjer se hkrati spremenijo razločljivost, obnovljivost, struktura modularnega spektra in približna projektabilnost. Če taka skala obstaja, ne da bi jo izbrali zato, da dobimo Q≈9, bi lahko postala dokaz za kandidatno fizikalno celico. Do takrat ostaja "~9 bitov na delec" umaknjena univerzalna trditev.

10 / SAME-CODE TEST

A cross-domain claim should survive a shared executable object.

Meddomenska trditev mora preživeti skupni izvršni objekt.

A useful discipline inherited from MDL×DCC is the same-code principle: if a proposed structure is genuinely the same across two domains, specify one formal object, one transformation rule and one verifier that both domains instantiate. Do not accept vocabulary alone as a bridge.

representation → invariant → operation → verifier
domain A ↘ same formal object ↙ domain B

For Ω–MSP, a future same-code test could ask whether recovery/projectability selection and a proven MDL×DCC control rule are instances of the same constrained coarse-graining functional. This is currently a proposal, not an established identity.

Uporabna disciplina iz MDL×DCC je načelo iste kode: če naj bi bila struktura v dveh domenah res ista, določimo en formalni objekt, eno pravilo transformacije in en preverjevalnik, ki ga obe domeni instancirata. Samo besedna podobnost ni most.

reprezentacija → invarianta → operacija → preverjevalnik
domena A ↘ isti formalni objekt ↙ domena B

Za Ω–MSP bi prihodnji same-code test lahko preveril, ali sta izbor po obnovljivosti/projektabilnosti in dokazano pravilo MDL×DCC primer istega funkcionala omejenega grobozrnjenja. To je trenutno predlog, ne potrjena identiteta.

12 / WHAT AC ADDS

Meaning is not smuggled
into the equations.

Pomen ni pretihotapljen
v enačbe.

The neutral physical programme can be read without AC: Ω is simply the global relational structure/state. AC adds the interpretation that fundamental presence is not generated by the physical description but expressed through it. That can guide questions about actuality and experience, but it is not allowed to certify a physics result.

The same separation protects CFH and CCH. They may motivate experiments on integration, accessibility and biological organization; they do not become true because the modular–spectral programme is mathematically interesting.

Nevtralni fizikalni program lahko beremo brez AC: Ω je preprosto globalna relacijska struktura/stanje. AC doda interpretacijo, da fundamentalna prisotnost ni ustvarjena s fizikalnim opisom, ampak se skozi njega izraža. To lahko usmerja vprašanja o aktualnosti in izkustvu, ne sme pa potrjevati fizikalnega rezultata.

Enaka ločitev ščiti CFH in CCH. Lahko motivirata eksperimente o integraciji, dostopnosti in biološki organizaciji; ne postaneta resnična zato, ker je modularno-spektralni program matematično zanimiv.
