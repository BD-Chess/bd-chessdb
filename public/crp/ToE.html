A research programme about structure, predictive compression, the physical cost of information, the computational cost of access, and the possible experiential interpretation proposed by CFH.

8Z × CFH Teorija vsega?

Ni potrjena Theory of Everything. Vprašaj je bistven: seme ostane ambiciozno, vsak most pa si mora zaslužiti status.

01

The original seed stays visible

The programme started from a deliberately strong intuition. v0.3 does not quietly replace it with weaker claims and then call the seed solved. It decomposes each part into what is already ruled out, what survives as a sharper question, and what would have to be proved.

Izvirno seme ostane vidno

Program se je začel z namerno močno intuicijo. v0.3 je ne zamenja neopazno s šibkejšimi trditvami in nato razglasi, da je seme rešeno. Vsak del razstavi na to, kar je že ovrženo pri sedanjem pomenu, na to, kar preživi kot natančnejše vprašanje, in na to, kar bi bilo treba dokazati.

compression = ↓ entropy  ·  i = mc⁴  ·  P = NP through compression
literal form corrected

Compression ↔ entropy

Lossless recoding does not lower the total Shannon entropy of the same source. The surviving question is whether discovered dependence removes the cost of falsely treating correlated degrees of freedom as independent — and when that structure has a physical free-energy value.

Brezizgubno prekodiranje ne zniža skupne Shannonove entropije istega vira. Preživi vprašanje, ali odkrite odvisnosti odstranijo ceno napačno predpostavljene neodvisnosti — in kdaj ima ta struktura tudi fizikalno vrednost proste energije.

active physical question

i = mc⁴

If i means bits, the bare equation is dimensionally incomplete. v0.3 keeps the mass–energy–information question active: define the measurable quantity, the missing dimensional factor, the regime and a discriminating prediction.

Če i pomeni bite, je gola enačba dimenzijsko nepopolna. v0.3 ohrani aktivno vprašanje masa–energija–informacija: določiti moramo merljivo količino, manjkajoči dimenzijski faktor, režim in razlikovalno napoved.

open mathematical target

P = NP via compression

A short description is not automatically a fast computation. The target survives as a precise construction problem: find exact representations or witness generators that are both compact and uniformly fast, with discovery and decoding costs counted.

Kratek opis ni avtomatično hiter izračun. Cilj preživi kot natančen konstrukcijski problem: najti točne predstavitve ali generatorje prič, ki so hkrati kompaktni in uniformno hitri, pri čemer štejeta tudi cena odkritja in dekodiranja.

8Z principle

Do not dismiss it. Decompose it. A failed literal equation can still expose a better variable, a sharper theorem target or a useful experiment. A good correction is not a surrender; it is a more expensive standard of survival.

Ne zavrzi ideje. Razstavi jo. Neuspela dobesedna enačba lahko še vedno razkrije boljšo spremenljivko, ostrejši cilj izreka ali uporaben eksperiment. Dober popravek ni predaja; je zahtevnejši standard preživetja.

02

Four connected layers — without proof transfer

The synthesis becomes useful only if the layers remain distinguishable. Each can fail without automatically destroying the others.

Štiri povezane ravni — brez prenosa dokaza

Sinteza je uporabna le, če ravni ostanejo ločljive. Vsaka lahko odpove, ne da bi avtomatično porušila vse druge.

1 · Physical relations

States, constraints, interactions, energy and geometry. A structure is physical only when a carrier and dynamics are specified.

Stanja, omejitve, interakcije, energija in geometrija. Struktura je fizikalna šele, ko sta določena nosilec in dinamika.

2 · Predictive representation

Compress history while preserving distinctions needed for specified future predictions or interventions.

Stisniti zgodovino, vendar ohraniti razlike, potrebne za določene prihodnje napovedi ali posege.

3 · Adaptive access

Search, cost, scale, representation and reopening. This is where 8Z, MDL and DCC become an engineering programme.

Iskanje, cena, merilo, predstavitev in ponovno odpiranje. Tu 8Z, MDL in DCC postanejo inženirski program.

4 · Experiential interpretation

CFH asks whether some organised dynamics are local expressions of a more fundamental experiential ground. This remains ontology, not a consequence of solver performance.

CFH sprašuje, ali so nekatere organizirane dinamike lokalni izrazi temeljnejše izkustvene podlage. To ostaja ontologija, ne posledica uspeha solverja.

Firewall: success in MDL×DCC does not prove CCH, CFH, AC or artificial consciousness. A positive neuroscience result would not prove the ontology. The project keeps lineage without transferring proof.

Požarni zid: uspeh MDL×DCC ne dokazuje CCH, CFH, AC ali umetne zavesti. Pozitiven nevroznanstveni rezultat ne bi dokazal ontologije. Projekt ohrani rodovnik brez prenosa dokaza.

03

Three mathematical bridges that are real — and bounded

v0.3 does not claim these identities as new physics. Their value is that they give the seed precise places where coding, dependence, thermodynamics and prediction genuinely meet.

Trije matematični mostovi, ki so resnični — in omejeni

v0.3 teh identitet ne razglaša za novo fiziko. Njihova vrednost je, da dajo semenu natančna mesta, kjer se kodiranje, odvisnosti, termodinamika in napovedovanje dejansko srečajo.

1. Model mismatch = excess ideal code length

Eₚ[−log₂ q(X)] = H₂(p) + DKL,2(p || q)

KL divergence measures the ideal coding penalty for using model q when the source is p. Improving the model can lower this penalty without changing the source itself.

KL-divergenca meri idealni kodirni pribitek zaradi uporabe modela q, ko je vir p. Izboljšanje modela lahko zmanjša ta pribitek, ne da bi spremenilo sam vir.

2. Nonequilibrium free energy uses the same divergence

F[p] − F[π] = kBT ln2 · DKL,2(p || π)

For a specified finite classical system coupled to a heat bath, relative entropy to the Gibbs state equals excess free energy after the physical factor is restored. Same mathematics, different process: this does not turn arbitrary LZ savings into joules.

Za določen končen klasični sistem v stiku s toplotno kopeljo relativna entropija do Gibbsovega stanja po vključitvi fizikalnega faktorja meri presežek proste energije. Ista matematika, drugačen proces: to ne pretvori poljubnega LZ-prihranka v joule.

3. Correlation is the cost of false independence

𝒯(X₁,…,Xd) = Σ H₂(Xj) − H₂(X) = DKL,2(p || ∏ pj)

Total correlation is the ideal coding saving of a joint model over a model that knows each part but incorrectly treats the parts as independent. Under an additive Hamiltonian and equal marginals, the same quantity also appears in the free-energy difference between correlated and product states.

Skupna korelacija je idealni kodirni prihranek skupnega modela proti modelu, ki pozna vsak del, vendar dele napačno obravnava kot neodvisne. Pri aditivnem Hamiltonianu in istih marginalah se ista količina pojavi tudi v razliki proste energije med koreliranim in produktnim stanjem.

Important limit

Interaction energy can change the accounting. Correlation is not universally convertible into extractable work, and a compressed file is not a thermodynamic state transition merely because its representation is shorter.

Interakcijska energija lahko spremeni račun. Korelacije niso univerzalno pretvorljive v dostopno delo, stisnjena datoteka pa ni termodinamični prehod samo zato, ker je njen zapis krajši.

04

The stronger target: accessible predictive compression

A description is valuable only if it preserves the consequences we care about and can actually be discovered and used within the stated resource budget.

Močnejši cilj: dosegljiva napovedna kompresija

Opis je vreden le, če ohrani posledice, ki nas zanimajo, in ga je mogoče dejansko odkriti ter uporabiti v deklariranem proračunu virov.

I(Y ; Ht | Zt) ≤ ε    with    Zt = C(Ht)

The summary Zₜ may discard history, but only if the discarded history adds little information for the declared future target Y. Change the target and the sufficient summary may change.

Povzetek Zₜ sme zavreči zgodovino le, če zavržena zgodovina za deklarirani prihodnji cilj Y doda malo informacije. Če spremenimo cilj, se lahko spremeni tudi zadosten povzetek.

Multiscale consistency

Cℓ(UΔx) ≈ Ûℓ,Δ(Cℓ(x))

A coarse representation earns legitimacy when evolving the detailed system and then compressing agrees, within declared error, with evolving the compressed state itself. This turns “multi-scale compression” from a metaphor into a testable commuting condition.

Groba predstavitev si zasluži legitimnost, ko razvoj podrobnega sistema in nato kompresija znotraj deklarirane napake sovpadata z razvojem že stisnjenega stanja. Tako »večskalna kompresija« iz metafore postane preverljiv komutativni pogoj.

05

Mass, information and geometry: keep the question, fix the equation

The original i = mc⁴ intuition stays as an active research task, not as a universal bit law. Physics already supplies rigorous nearby relations — but they include additional scales and constants.

Masa, informacija in geometrija: ohranimo vprašanje, popravimo enačbo

Izvirna intuicija i = mc⁴ ostane aktivna raziskovalna naloga, ne univerzalni zakon o bitih. Fizika že pozna stroge bližnje zveze — vendar vsebujejo dodatne skale in konstante.

Bekenstein-type bound

Ient ≤ 2πER / (ħc ln2)

With E = mc²: Ient ≤ 2πmcR / (ħ ln2). Geometry matters.

Schwarzschild black hole

IBH = 4πGm² / (ħc ln2)

For this system the entropy capacity scales with m², not with a bare mc⁴ bit law.

Active contract for i = mc⁴

To survive as physics, a successor relation must define what i measures, derive any dimensional factor independently, state its domain, and make a prediction that differs from existing bounds or identities.

Da bi naslednja različica zveze preživela kot fizika, mora določiti kaj meri i, neodvisno izpeljati dimenzijski faktor, določiti domeno in dati napoved, ki se razlikuje od že znanih mej ali identitet.

Correction to older project material: the former “3.13 Å matching scale” obtained by treating dimensional constants as naked numbers is not unit-invariant and is not retained as evidence. The older bridge on the Demon page is historical; this page supersedes that subclaim.

Popravek starejšega projektnega gradiva: nekdanja »ujemajoča skala 3,13 Å«, dobljena z obravnavo dimenzijskih konstant kot golih števil, ni invariantna na izbiro enot in ni več dokaz. Starejši most na strani Demon je zgodovinski; ta stran ta podsklep nadomešča.

06

P vs NP: compression survives as a construction target

The central warning is simple: short description ≠ short computation. But that does not close every compression route. It tells us what a successful route must additionally prove.

P proti NP: kompresija preživi kot konstrukcijski cilj

Osrednje opozorilo je preprosto: kratek opis ≠ kratek izračun. To pa ne zapre vsake kompresijske poti. Pove nam, kaj mora uspešna pot dodatno dokazati.

RouteEnough for polynomial solving if…Missing universal step
A · witness setA polynomial-size, polynomial-time constructible set is guaranteed to contain a valid witness whenever one exists.Construct such a set for every SAT instance.
B · structural widthAn equivalent representation and decomposition of width O(log n) are found in polynomial time, with exact DP over the width.Find that representation for every instance without hiding exponential work.
C · short + fast witness programsEvery satisfiable instance has a witness-generating program of O(log n) bits with one uniform polynomial runtime bound; programs can be dovetailed and outputs verified.Prove the bound for all satisfiable instances and preserve correct UNSAT decisions.

A constructive positive control: linear constraints over GF(2)

For affine linear systems, Gaussian elimination efficiently reveals rank, inconsistency, free variables and a compact description of all solutions. This is a real example of structure → compact representation → efficient decision. It is not general SAT, but it shows what a successful structural programme looks like when the theorem is true.

Pri afinih linearnih sistemih Gaussova eliminacija učinkovito razkrije rang, neskladnost, proste spremenljivke in kompakten opis vseh rešitev. To je resničen primer struktura → kompakten opis → učinkovita odločitev. Ni splošni SAT, pokaže pa, kako je videti uspešen strukturni program, ko izrek drži.

Complexity barriers are information, not a stop sign

Known kernelization/compression barriers matter because they tell us what kind of universal compression would have major complexity-theoretic consequences. They do not by themselves prove that every compression-inspired route to P = NP is impossible.

Znane ovire za kernelizacijo in kompresijo so pomembne, ker povedo, kakšna univerzalna kompresija bi imela velike posledice za teorijo zahtevnosti. Same po sebi pa ne dokazujejo, da je vsaka kompresijsko navdihnjena pot do P = NP nemogoča.

8Z → MDL → DCC: where the engineering lane enters

The ToE branch does not redefine the working method. It asks whether the same distinction — discover structure, price it honestly, govern access to it — can be made mathematically sharper across domains.

8Z → MDL → DCC: kjer vstopi inženirska veja

Veja ToE ne predefinira delujoče metode. Sprašuje, ali lahko isto razliko — odkrij strukturo, pošteno obračunaj njeno ceno, upravljaj dostop do nje — matematično izostrimo med domenami.

8Z · Generator

Alternative representations, operators, model families, scales and routes.

MDL · Judge

Model + residual + decoder + exceptions + declared search/implementation cost.

DCC · Governor

Budget, focus, reopening, scale and representation change under measured progress and failure.

“Thermostat” is one candidate model of DCC, not a privileged explanation. Budget allocation, representation switching, scale selection, minority preservation and stagnation detection remain competing or complementary mechanisms.

»Termostat« je en kandidatni model DCC, ne privilegirana razlaga. Razporejanje proračuna, menjava predstavitev, izbor merila, ohranjanje manjšinskih kandidatov in zaznavanje stagnacije ostajajo konkurenčni ali dopolnjujoči mehanizmi.

09

A theory branch earns value by losing cleanly

v0.3 treats tests as claim-specific pressure, not as a single referendum on the entire worldview. A positive result promotes only the mechanism actually tested.

Raziskovalna veja si zasluži vrednost tudi tako, da lahko jasno izgubi

v0.3 obravnava teste kot pritisk na posamezne trditve, ne kot en sam referendum o celotnem svetovnem nazoru. Pozitiven rezultat napreduje samo mehanizem, ki je bil dejansko testiran.

T1 · Predictive value

Does reachable compression predict held-out consequences better than simple baselines after discovery cost is counted?

T2 · Shuffled feedback

REAL / OFF / SHUFFLED under matched compute. Distinguish useful signal, equivalence, harm and insufficient data — not “ToE true/false”.

T3 · Mechanism

Thermostat schedule versus richer DCC control: budget, representation, scale and reopening.

T4 · Joules

Measure physical energy of execution. Never infer energy savings from code-length savings alone.

T5 · Exact search boundaries

For SAT/TSP/Sudoku, measure representation size, discovery time, boundary width and correctness certificates.

T6/T7 · CCH + correlation

Separate organisation from learning progress; test whether correlation savings are relevant to prediction and control, not merely present.

What would justify v0.4?

Not more prose. A new derivation that survives counterexamples, a bounded causal experiment, a new exact complexity result, or a physical relation with independently fixed units and a discriminating prediction.

Ne več besedila. Nova izpeljava, ki preživi nasprotne primere; omejen kavzalni eksperiment; nov točen rezultat zahtevnosti; ali fizikalna zveza z neodvisno določenimi enotami in razlikovalno napovedjo.

What this page claims today

  • Established mathematics used: Shannon entropy, KL divergence, Gibbs free-energy relations under stated assumptions, total correlation, standard black-hole information bounds, and known polynomial structure for affine linear systems.
  • Project-supported engineering: 8Z/MDL/DCC as a working research architecture with domain-bounded evidence and explicit controller comparisons.
  • Open hypotheses: whether accessible predictive compression is a useful cross-domain explanatory variable; whether a deeper mass–information law exists; whether compression-inspired constructions can produce new complexity results; and the CFH ontology.
  • Not claimed: an established Theory of Everything, P = NP, a universal i = mc⁴ law, a compression-to-joules conversion, or proof of consciousness from engineering success.

Kaj ta stran danes trdi

  • Uporabljena uveljavljena matematika: Shannonova entropija, KL-divergenca, Gibbsove/prostoenergijske zveze pri navedenih predpostavkah, skupna korelacija, standardne meje informacije črnih lukenj in znana polinomska struktura afinih linearnih sistemov.
  • Projektno podprt inženiring: 8Z/MDL/DCC kot delujoča raziskovalna arhitektura z domensko omejenimi dokazi in eksplicitnimi primerjavami kontrolerjev.
  • Odprte hipoteze: ali je dosegljiva napovedna kompresija uporaben meddomenski pojasnjevalni parameter; ali obstaja globlji masno-informacijski zakon; ali kompresijsko navdihnjene konstrukcije lahko dajo nove rezultate zahtevnosti; in ontologija CFH.
  • Ni trditev: potrjena Theory of Everything, P = NP, univerzalni zakon i = mc⁴, pretvorba kompresije v joule ali dokaz zavesti iz inženirskega uspeha.
