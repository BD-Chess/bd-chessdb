The accounting identity — and its limitRačunovodska identiteta — in njena meja
H(T | D) = H(R | D) + H(P | R,D)

D is the known instance, T the tour, R its projection and P the remaining information required to reconstruct it. This chain rule assumes a deterministic, jointly reversible representation. A short projection need not mean a simple original. Adding the payload repairs the omission; it does not make code length universally monotone in tour quality.

D je znana instanca, T tura, R njena projekcija in P preostala informacija za obnovitev. Verižno pravilo predpostavlja deterministično, skupaj obrnljivo predstavitev. Kratka projekcija ne pomeni nujno preprostega izvirnika. Payload popravi izpuščeni del; kodne dolžine ne naredi univerzalno monotone v kakovosti ture.

Keep LZ, change the question. LZ also sees repetition order that a zero-order histogram model misses. 05 / THE STRONGER QUESTION

Does the representation help us choose what happens next?

Ali predstavitev pomaga izbrati, kaj naredimo naslednje?

A tour's current length is already directly computable. A more useful role for a new sensor is to predict the response to a declared continuation action beyond what cheap context already tells us.

Trenutno dolžino ture že neposredno izračunamo. Uporabnejša vloga novega senzorja je napoved odziva na določeno nadaljevalno dejanje nad tem, kar že pove cenejši kontekst.

I(Y; Z | B,a) = H(Y | B,a) − H(Y | B,Z,a)

B: known context · Z: candidate representation · a: action · Y: future outcome. This is an ideal information identity, not a measured result. The prepared ridge/MSE experiment tests predictive mean error, not bits of mutual information. [1][2]

B: znani kontekst · Z: kandidatna predstavitev · a: dejanje · Y: prihodnji izid. To je idealna informacijska identiteta, ne izmerjen rezultat. Pripravljeni ridge/MSE test preverja napako napovedi povprečja, ne bitov vzajemne informacije. [1][2]

Better prediction is not automatically a better decisionBoljša napoved ni avtomatično boljša odločitev

Imagine two equally likely states with the same current quality. Action A/B gains are (4,0) in the first and (0,4) in the second. A fixed choice averages 2; knowing the state allows 4 before observation costs. But if A wins in both states, better gain prediction need not change the action at all. Both are constructed examples, not arena results.

Predstavljajmo si dve enako verjetni stanji z isto trenutno kakovostjo. Izboljška dejanj A/B sta v prvem (4,0), v drugem (0,4). Fiksna izbira da povprečno 2; poznavanje stanja omogoči 4 pred stroški opazovanja. Če pa A zmaga v obeh stanjih, boljša napoved izboljška morda sploh ne spremeni dejanja. To sta konstruirana primera, ne rezultata arene.

V(Z | B) = E[ maxₐ E[U | B,Z,a] ] − maxₐ E[U | B,a] ≥ 0

Ideal value of observation with the same available actions and the option to ignore Z. Net value after sensing, learning and use costs can be negative; a learned policy can also be wrong.

Idealna vrednost opazovanja ob istih dejanjih in možnosti, da Z prezremo. Neto vrednost po ceni merjenja, učenja in uporabe je lahko negativna; naučena politika se lahko tudi zmoti.

Why cross-entropy is not a two-sided distanceZakaj cross-entropy ni dvostranska razdalja

For q=(0.8,0.2), the all-first-symbol distribution costs 0.322 bits per symbol under q; q itself costs 0.722. A more concentrated distribution can therefore be cheaper without being closer. For reference similarity, test KL/JS or a specified typicality model; keep cross-entropy as a separately named predictor.

Pri q=(0,8;0,2) porazdelitev samih prvih simbolov pod q stane 0,322 bita na simbol; sam q stane 0,722. Bolj koncentrirana porazdelitev je torej lahko cenejša, ne da bi bila bližja. Za podobnost referenci testiramo KL/JS ali določen model tipičnosti; cross-entropy ostane ločeno poimenovan napovednik.

07 / MATHEMATICS THAT ALREADY CONNECTS THE FIELDS

Real bridges. Explicit assumptions.

Resnični mostovi. Izrecne predpostavke.

Coding mismatch and free energyNeujemanje modela in prosta energija
Eₚ[−log₂ q(X)] = H₂(p) + DKL,2(p || q)
F[p] − F[π] = kBT ln2 · DKL,2(p || π)

The first identity measures ideal coding overhead. The second describes excess free energy relative to the Gibbs state of a specified classical system at temperature T. The shared mathematics is real; improving a model is not the same operation as physically changing the system. Arbitrary code savings are not automatically joules. [3]

Prva identiteta meri idealni kodirni pribitek. Druga opisuje presežek proste energije glede na Gibbsovo stanje določenega klasičnega sistema pri temperaturi T. Skupna matematika je resnična; izboljšanje modela ni ista operacija kot fizična sprememba sistema. Poljubni kodni prihranki niso avtomatično jouli. [3]

Correlation: the price of assumed independenceKorelacija: cena predpostavljene neodvisnosti
𝒯 = Σⱼ H₂(Xⱼ) − H₂(X) = DKL,2(p || ∏ⱼ pⱼ)

With known distributions, total correlation is the ideal saving of joint coding over independent coding. With an additive Hamiltonian and the same local marginals, the corresponding free-energy difference is kBT ln2 · 𝒯. Interaction energy changes the accounting. Discovering correlations is not the same as physically creating them.

Ob znanih porazdelitvah je skupna korelacija idealni prihranek skupnega kodiranja pred neodvisnim. Pri aditivnem Hamiltonianu in istih lokalnih marginalah je ustrezna razlika proste energije kBT ln2 · 𝒯. Interakcijska energija spremeni račun. Odkrivanje korelacij ni isto kot njihovo fizično ustvarjanje.

Multiscale descriptions must preserve dynamicsVečskalni opisi morajo ohraniti dinamiko
Cℓ(UΔx) ≈ Ûℓ,Δ(Cℓ(x))

Evolve the detailed state and then summarize, or evolve its summary: agreement within a declared error makes the coarse description useful. The observation family, interventions and time horizon must be specified. Applying this condition to an MSTD pyramid remains a proposed test, not a completed physical derivation.

Razvij podrobno stanje in ga povzemi ali razvij njegov povzetek: ujemanje znotraj določene napake naredi grobi opis uporaben. Določiti moramo opazovanja, posege in časovni horizont. Uporaba tega pogoja na MSTD piramidi ostaja predlagan preizkus, ne opravljena fizikalna izpeljava.

CONDITIONAL ROUTE A

A sufficient witness list

Zadostna množica prič

Construct in polynomial time a polynomial-size list guaranteed to include a witness whenever one exists. Verify every candidate, including the correct negative outcome.

V polinomskem času zgradi polinomsko veliko množico, ki vsebuje pričo, kadar ta obstaja. Preveri vse kandidate, tudi pravilen negativni odgovor.

CONDITIONAL ROUTE B

An accessible narrow representation

Dosegljiva ozka predstavitev

Find an exact equivalent representation and decomposition of width O(log n), with a poly(n)·2ᴼ⁽ʷ⁾ decision algorithm. Representation discovery must also be polynomial.

Najdi točno ekvivalentno predstavitev in razgradnjo širine O(log n), z odločanjem poly(n)·2ᴼ⁽ʷ⁾. Tudi odkritje predstavitve mora biti polinomsko.

CONDITIONAL ROUTE C

Short and uniformly fast programs

Kratki in uniformno hitri programi

For every satisfiable input, guarantee a witness program of at most a·log₂n bits and at most nᵇ steps, with fixed constants. Bounded enumeration would then be polynomial.

Za vsak zadovoljiv vhod zagotovi program priče z največ a·log₂n biti in nᵇ koraki, s fiksnima konstantama. Omejena enumeracija bi bila tedaj polinomska.

These are sufficient conditional routes for SAT, not proved universal constructions. Known compression barriers identify major consequences under precise assumptions; they are not a blanket ban on every compression-inspired approach. [6]

To so zadostne pogojne poti za SAT, ne dokazane univerzalne konstrukcije. Znane kompresijske ovire pod natančnimi predpostavkami določajo velike posledice; niso splošna prepoved vsake kompresijsko navdihnjene poti. [6]

A constructive foothold: for linear constraints over GF(2), Gaussian elimination finds consistency, rank, free variables and a compact representation of all solutions efficiently. Structure → compact representation → exact decision really works here. It is a known restricted class, not general SAT.

Konstruktivna opora: pri linearnih omejitvah nad GF(2) Gaussova eliminacija učinkovito najde skladnost, rang, proste spremenljivke in kompakten opis vseh rešitev. Struktura → kompaktna predstavitev → točna odločitev tukaj res deluje. Gre za znan omejeni razred, ne splošni SAT.

10 / COSMOLOGY, DYNAMICS, EXPERIENCE

A common question is not yet a common cause.

Skupno vprašanje še ni skupni vzrok.

Unfolding and understanding are the two directions of the programme — a philosophical frame, not a derived law of the universe.

Razvijanje in razumevanje sta dve smeri programa — filozofski okvir, ne izpeljan zakon vesolja.

A short law need not imply a short initial state, a cheap simulation or a thermodynamic arrow of time. A physical ToE would still need a shared dynamical model yielding quantum probabilities, relativistic gravity, matter and distinguishing predictions. The information bridges guide the search; they do not supply those missing derivations.

Kratek zakon ne pomeni nujno kratkega začetnega stanja, poceni simulacije ali termodinamične puščice časa. Fizikalna ToE še vedno potrebuje skupni dinamični model, iz katerega sledijo kvantne verjetnosti, relativistična gravitacija, snov in razlikovalne napovedi. Informacijski mostovi vodijo iskanje; manjkajočih izpeljav ne nadomestijo.

The draft offers 100,000 candidate evaluations per action and a primary gap filter ≤0.5 (50%, not 5%). Operator pair, budget rule and filter are still design choices. Equal evaluations do not mean equal CPU time or neighbourhood coverage. Group identities must follow actual shared origins; 128 snapshots are not 128 independent runs. Choosing a model or a filter after its result remains exploratory.

Osnutek ponuja 100.000 kandidatnih ocen na dejanje in primarni filter gap ≤0,5 (50 %, ne 5 %). Par operatorjev, pravilo proračuna in filter so še izbire zasnove. Enako število ocen ne pomeni enakega CPU-časa ali pokritosti sosedstva. Skupine morajo slediti dejanskemu skupnemu izvoru; 128 posnetkov ni 128 neodvisnih tekov. Izbira modela ali filtra po rezultatu ostaja raziskovalna.

If one operator dominates, predicting gain is still testable, but the best-action question may have little variation. Wide intervals are inconclusive, not proof of equivalence. A positive development result justifies an independent next test, not a production sensor replacement.

Če en operator prevladuje, lahko še vedno testiramo napoved izboljška, vprašanje najboljšega dejanja pa ima lahko malo variacije. Široki intervali so nedoločeni, ne dokaz ekvivalence. Pozitiven razvojni rezultat upraviči naslednji neodvisni test, ne zamenjave produkcijskega senzorja.

Keep the alternative mechanisms aliveOhranimo alternativne mehanizme

Projection loss, code-model choice, sequence order, orientation and ties, generator mixture, geometric move availability, delayed reorganisation and the regulator's causal role can produce different signatures. The first six address the static measurements directly. The last two need actual temporal data. Reference-law transfer, shuffled feedback, physical energy and CCH are separate tests, not parts of one all-or-nothing ToE verdict.

Izguba v projekciji, kodni model, vrstni red, orientacija in vezi, mešanica generatorjev, geometrijska razpoložljivost potez, zakasnjena reorganizacija in vzročna vloga regulatorja imajo lahko različne podpise. Prvih šest neposredno zadeva statične meritve. Zadnja dva potrebujeta časovne podatke. Prenos referenčnega zakona, premešani feedback, fizična energija in CCH so ločeni testi, ne deli ene same sodbe vse-ali-nič o ToE.
