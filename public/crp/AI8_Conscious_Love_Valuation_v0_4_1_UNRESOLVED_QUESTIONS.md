## Current non-negotiable research boundary

A successful V3 mechanism would not establish V4 phenomenal valence. A failed E0 or arena would weaken the engineering route, not automatically disprove the original personal experience or ontology; the epistemic axes remain separate.

## E0.1 boundary after the correction release

E0.1 demonstrates only a bounded deterministic mechanism: a canonical stake file crosses a real OS-process boundary, a second process creates a traceable governance proposal, DCC executes it, and removal or randomization of the state changes the decision. It does not establish model replacement, real-world harm verification, schema minimality, external cost, fairness, system-endogenous valuation, intrinsic agency, consciousness, or superiority over the strong B3 fixed-policy baseline.
