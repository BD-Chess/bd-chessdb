Abstract

The Solar Orbital Ring Accelerator (SORA) is a staged interstellar-launch architecture in which the spacecraft is treated as a payload moving through reusable solar-system infrastructure rather than as a self-contained engine. The concept combines solar-sail first-stage acceleration, solar-powered orbital magnetic-node arcs, powered magnetic or plasma coupling, high-speed solar perihelion passage, detached sacrificial bow-wall protection, and destination seeding through carried infrastructure nodes.

SORA is not presented as a validated mission design. It is presented as a finite, simulation-ready research program. The central question is whether reusable solar-system infrastructure can transfer enough energy, momentum, protection, and destination-seeding capability to make a small uncrewed interstellar seed-probe architecture physically credible.

The present claim is narrow: the architecture is coherent enough to test, its failure modes are finite enough to name, and a compact simulation suite can determine whether any low-speed seed-probe parameter island survives. If no such island exists, SORA fails cleanly. If one exists at modest speed and modest payload, SORA becomes a candidate for deeper engineering study.

Povzetek

Solarni orbitalni obročni pospeševalnik (SORA) je stopnjevana arhitektura za medzvezdni izstrel, v kateri vesoljsko plovilo ni obravnavano kot samostojen motor, temveč kot tovor, ki se premika skozi ponovno uporabno infrastrukturo Osončja. Koncept združuje začetno pospeševanje s sončnim jadrom, solarno napajane orbitalne loke magnetnih vozlišč, pogonsko magnetno ali plazemsko sklopitev, hiter prehod skozi Sončev perihelij, ločeno žrtveno sprednjo zaščitno steno in sejanje ciljnega sistema z infrastrukturnimi vozlišči, ki jih sonda nosi s seboj.

SORA ni predstavljena kot potrjena misijska zasnova. Predstavljena je kot omejen, simulacijsko pripravljen raziskovalni program. Osrednje vprašanje je, ali lahko ponovno uporabna infrastruktura Osončja prenese dovolj energije, gibalne količine, zaščite in zmožnosti sejanja, da bi bila majhna brezposadna medzvezdna semenska sonda fizikalno verodostojna.

Trenutna trditev je ozka: arhitektura je dovolj koherentna za testiranje, njeni načini odpovedi so dovolj končni, da jih lahko poimenujemo, in kompaktna simulacijska zbirka lahko pokaže, ali pri nizki hitrosti preživi kakšen parameterki otok. Če takega otoka ni, SORA čisto odpove. Če obstaja pri zmerni hitrosti in zmerni masi tovora, postane kandidat za globljo inženirsko študijo.

One-Page Summary

SORA's strongest idea is not a specific shield geometry, not a claimed top speed, and not a magical magnetic ring. Its strongest idea is the frame:

The spacecraft is the payload. The solar system is the accelerator.

A proton in a particle accelerator does not carry its own fuel. It is accelerated by a large external machine. SORA asks whether a spacecraft can be treated similarly: not as a tiny vehicle carrying one impossible engine, but as a payload moving through reusable infrastructure stages.

The architecture currently has five open gates:

Gate Question Status
A - Powered node/arc coupling Can powered orbital nodes transfer useful net momentum to a spacecraft-side coupling system? Open
B - Proximity encounter smoothness Can a discrete node arc produce smooth acceleration and remain orbitally stable across many launches? Open
C - Bow-wall protection Can detached shielding reduce risk more than it adds mass, control burden, and secondary-debris hazard? Open
D - Destination capture and seeding Can carried nodes become useful target-star infrastructure? Open
E - Whole-architecture closure Can mass, energy, thermal load, control, reaction correction, and mission value close at once? Open

The paper's current position is therefore simple: SORA is not yet feasible. SORA is now testable.

Enostranski povzetek

Najmočnejša ideja SORA ni določena geometrija ščita, ni zahtevana največja hitrost in ni čarobni magnetni obroč. Najmočnejša ideja je okvir:

Vesoljsko plovilo je tovor. Osončje je pospeševalnik.

Proton v pospeševalniku delcev ne nosi lastnega goriva. Pospešuje ga velik zunanji stroj. SORA vpraša, ali lahko podobno obravnavamo tudi vesoljsko plovilo: ne kot majhno vozilo z enim nemogočim motorjem, temveč kot tovor, ki gre skozi zaporedje ponovno uporabnih infrastrukturnih stopenj.

Arhitektura ima trenutno pet odprtih vrat:

Vrata Vprašanje Status

Trenutni položaj članka je zato preprost: SORA še ni izvedljiva. SORA je zdaj testabilna.

Clarifying Note on the Word Ring

Although the architecture keeps the name Solar Orbital Ring Accelerator, SORA does not require a closed 360-degree ring for the first useful stage. The first useful infrastructure element is a dense encounter arc or ringlet: a local orbital segment of powered magnetic nodes that the spacecraft approaches, passes near, and recedes from.

The spacecraft does not travel along the orbital infrastructure. It does not ride a mechanical guideway. It performs a designed close encounter with a dense arc of powered nodes. Distance controls coupling magnitude. Powered or time-varying interaction controls net energy transfer and direction.

A full ring is a later scaling convenience, not a prerequisite. A paired-ring or dual-ring concept should also be read as paired encounter arcs or offset ringlets at nearby orbital radii unless a full ring is explicitly justified by simulation.

Correct encounter geometry:

       spacecraft inbound
             \
              \\       closest pass
               *------------------> outbound toward solar perihelion

        o   o   o   o   o   o   o
        powered orbital node arc / ringlet

The ship approaches, peaks in coupling, and recedes. It does not travel along the arc.

Pojasnilo besede obroč

Čeprav arhitektura ohranja ime Solar Orbital Ring Accelerator, SORA za prvo uporabno stopnjo ne zahteva zaprtega 360-stopinjskega obroča. Prvi uporaben infrastrukturni element je gost srečevalni lok ali obroček: lokalni orbitalni segment napajanih magnetnih vozlišč, ki se mu plovilo približa, gre mimo njega in se nato oddalji.

Plovilo ne potuje po orbitalni infrastrukturi. Ne vozi po mehanskem vodilu. Opravi zasnovano bližnje srečanje z gostim lokom napajanih vozlišč. Razdalja uravnava jakost sklopitve. Neto prenos energije in smer pa zahtevata napajano ali časovno spremenljivo interakcijo.

Celoten obroč je poznejša razširitvena priročnost, ne začetni pogoj. Tudi koncept parnega obroča ali dvojnega obroča naj bo bran kot par srečevalnih lokov ali zamaknjenih obročkov pri bližnjih orbitalnih radijih, razen če simulacija izrecno upraviči poln obroč.

Pravilna geometrija srečanja:

       plovilo prihaja
             \
              \\      najbližji prehod
               *------------------> odhod proti Sončevemu periheliju

        o   o   o   o   o   o   o
        napajani orbitalni lok vozlišč / obroček

Plovilo se približa, sklopitev doseže vrh in nato oslabi. Ne potuje vzdolž loka.

Scope and Non-Claims

This is a public technical preprint, not an engineering design. To avoid misreading, SORA does not claim:

  • that 10-30% c is currently feasible;
  • that passive static magnetic fields can accelerate a neutral spacecraft;
  • that magnetic coupling is strong enough at any specific scale;
  • that the proposed bow wall is validated shielding;
  • that destination nodes can be passively released into useful orbits;
  • that MDL x DCC governance proves feasibility;
  • that any of the five gates has passed.

SORA does claim:

  • the staged-infrastructure framing is coherent enough to model;
  • the architecture has named subsystems with named failure modes;
  • each major unknown can be converted into a toy model, simulation, or parameter sweep;
  • the first useful target is not a crewed starship but a small uncrewed seed probe;
  • partial mission success may still create scientific or infrastructural value;
  • a full-stack closure model can decide whether the architecture deserves deeper work.

A high-risk branch is allowed only if it has a plausible physical bridge, a clear failure mode, and a cheapest next test. Ideas that survive become stronger. Ideas that fail cleanly reduce fog.

Obseg in netrditve

To je javni tehnični prednatis, ne inženirski načrt. Da se izognemo napačnemu branju, SORA ne trdi:

  • da je 10-30 % hitrosti svetlobe trenutno izvedljivo;
  • da lahko pasivna statična magnetna polja pospešijo nevtralno plovilo;
  • da je magnetna sklopitev dovolj močna pri katerikoli določeni skali;
  • da je predlagana sprednja stena potrjena zaščita;
  • da je mogoče ciljna vozlišča pasivno spustiti v uporabne orbite;
  • da upravljanje MDL x DCC dokazuje izvedljivost;
  • da je bilo katerokoli od petih vrat prestano.

SORA trdi:

  • da je stopnjevani infrastrukturni okvir dovolj koherenten za modeliranje;
  • da ima arhitektura poimenovane podsisteme in poimenovane načine odpovedi;
  • da je mogoče vsako večjo neznanko pretvoriti v igračni model, simulacijo ali parameterki prelet;
  • da prvi uporaben cilj ni posadkovna zvezdna ladja, temveč majhna brezposadna semenska sonda;
  • da lahko delni uspeh še vedno ustvari znanstveno ali infrastrukturno vrednost;
  • da lahko model zaprtja celotnega sklada odloči, ali si arhitektura zasluži globlje delo.

Visoko tvegana veja je dovoljena samo, če ima verjeten fizikalni most, jasen način odpovedi in najcenejši naslednji test. Ideje, ki preživijo, postanejo močnejše. Ideje, ki odpovejo čisto, zmanjšajo meglo.

Core Thesis

The usual interstellar propulsion question is: What engine can a spacecraft carry that lets it cross interstellar distances?

SORA asks a different question: What solar-system infrastructure could repeatedly launch small payloads, and what is the smallest mission class that might close physically?

This reframing matters because the propellant equation punishes self-contained ships. If every stage must be carried by the payload, the payload becomes dominated by propulsion mass. SORA distributes function across reusable stages:

Stage Function What stays What travels
0 Infrastructure buildout Solar-orbit nodes, power, control Nothing yet
1 Solar-sail acceleration The Sun Sail plus payload stack
2 Powered node/arc coupling Orbital encounter arc Payload plus temporary coupling hardware
3 Perihelion energy leverage The Sun's gravity well Outbound stack
4 Cruise protection Nothing external Payload, bow wall, seed nodes
5 Destination seeding Target-star gravity well Released nodes or sub-probes
6 Future densification Destination nodes Later missions

The spacecraft is not asked to do everything. It is asked to survive the handoffs.

Osrednja teza

Običajno vprašanje medzvezdnega pogona je: kateri motor lahko plovilo nosi, da prečka medzvezdne razdalje?

SORA vpraša drugače: katera infrastruktura v Osončju bi lahko ponavljajoče izstreljevala majhne tovore in kateri najmanjši razred misije bi se lahko fizikalno zaprl?

Ta preobrat je pomemben, ker raketna enačba kaznuje samostojna plovila. Če mora vsak del stopnje potovati kot del tovora, tovor postane prevladan s pogonsko maso. SORA razdeli funkcije po ponovno uporabnih stopnjah:

Stopnja Funkcija Kaj ostane Kaj potuje
0 Izgradnja infrastrukture vozlišča v sončni orbiti, moč, nadzor nič še
1 Pospeševanje s sončnim jadrom Sonce jadro in tovorni sklad
2 Sklopitev z napajanim vozliščnim lokom orbitalni srečevalni lok tovor in začasna sklopitvena oprema
3 Energijski vzvod v periheliju Sončeva gravitacijska jama izhodni sklad
4 Zaščita med križarjenjem nič zunanjega tovor, sprednja stena, semenska vozlišča
5 Sejanje cilja gravitacijska jama ciljne zvezde sproščena vozlišča ali podsone
6 Poznejše zgoščevanje ciljna vozlišča poznejše misije

Plovilo ni pozvano, naj naredi vse. Pozvano je, naj preživi prehode med stopnjami.

Prior Concepts and Difference from SORA

SORA is not built from exotic physics. It recombines familiar ideas into a staged architecture. The novelty claim should stay narrow: not every component is new, but the staged closure problem is new.

Prior concept Known role SORA difference
Solar sails Photon momentum transfer Used as a disposable first stage, not the entire answer
Laser lightsails External acceleration SORA targets larger payloads and reusable solar-system infrastructure
Orbital rings Large-scale infrastructure SORA uses free-flying node arcs/ringlets, not rigid rings
Magnetic sails / electric sails Spacecraft-side coupling candidates Must be powered, time-varying, or actively phased
Oberth effect Energy leverage near perihelion Used after prior acceleration, not from rest
Whipple shielding Sacrificial forward protection Detached standoff wall at much larger separation
Seed probes Science or exploration payloads First mission carries infrastructure nodes, not only instruments

SORA's value is the system question: can these elements close together at a small mission class?

SORA ni zgrajena iz eksotične fizike. Znova združuje znane ideje v stopnjevano arhitekturo. Trditev o novosti mora ostati ozka: niso vsi deli novi, nov je problem skupnega zaprtja stopenj.

R1 is the anchor mission so simulations do not drift into vague optimism.

R1: uncrewed interstellar seed probe.

  • leave the solar system on an interstellar trajectory;
  • survive cruise behind a detachable or abandonable bow-wall system;
  • arrive as a fast flyby or partial-capture seed mission;
  • release one or more useful destination nodes or sub-probes;
  • return data or leave infrastructure value even if full deceleration fails.

R1 is a seed mission, not a settlement mission.

Gate A - Powered Node/Arc Coupling

Gate A is the hardest physics gate. Passive static magnetism is not a source of net spacecraft acceleration. A static magnetic field passing a passive conductor induces currents whose force opposes relative motion. Passive susceptibility is therefore a control case, not a baseline solution.

Distance-as-throttle remains useful, but only in the strict sense: encounter distance controls coupling magnitude. Net energy transfer and direction still require a powered or time-varying interaction, a powered ship-side magnetic moment, plasma/magsail coupling, or active phasing between nodes and ship.

Subgate Mechanism Primary cost Status

Vrata A - sklopitev z napajanimi vozlišči/lokom

Vrata A so najtežja fizikalna vrata. Pasivni statični magnetizem ni vir neto pospeševanja plovila. Statično magnetno polje, ki gre mimo pasivnega prevodnika, inducira tokove, katerih sila nasprotuje relativnemu gibanju. Pasivna susceptibilnost je zato kontrolni primer, ne osnovna rešitev.

Razdalja kot ročica ostane uporabna, vendar samo v strogem smislu: razdalja pri srečanju nadzoruje jakost sklopitve. Neto prenos energije in smer še vedno zahtevata napajano ali časovno spremenljivo interakcijo, napajan magnetni moment na strani plovila, plazemsko/magnetno jadro ali aktivno faziranje med vozlišči in plovilom.

Gate B - Proximity Encounter Smoothness and Node-Arc Stability

Gate B asks whether a discrete node arc can behave as a smooth encounter field, not whether the spacecraft can ride a physical guideway. The relevant object is the spacecraft's acceleration time series during a close pass through overlapping field volumes.

The first metrics are acceleration ripple, jerk, lateral impulse, node-failure tolerance, station-keeping cost, and reaction-force accumulation. A perfect simulated node arc is not enough; the gate must also test missing nodes, noisy orientation, finite field rise time, and repeated launches.

  • R_ripple = sigma(a(t)) / (mean(a(t)) + epsilon), with robotic and fragile-payload thresholds declared separately;
  • J_max = max |da/dt|, because payloads can survive mean acceleration but fail under jerk;
  • Delta p_perp = integral of lateral force over time, because sideways impulse can be as damaging as forward ripple;
  • node-loss tests: remove every kth node and randomly remove p percent of nodes;
  • station-keeping: compute delta-v per node per year and node replacement rate;
  • reaction accounting: every launch exchanges momentum with the node arc and must be paid back.

Paired arcs or offset ringlets may act as a reaction-force management system. They should not be interpreted as two giant rigid rings. Their purpose is momentum bookkeeping and orbital recovery, not construction of a continuous mechanical guideway.

Vrata B - gladkost bližnjega srečanja in stabilnost loka vozlišč

Vrata B sprašujejo, ali se diskretni lok vozlišč lahko vede kot gladko srečevalno polje, ne ali lahko plovilo vozi po fizičnem vodilu. Relevantni objekt je časovna vrsta pospeška plovila med bližnjim prehodom skozi prekrivajoče se prostornine polja.

Prve metrike so valovanje pospeška, sunek, bočni impulz, toleranca izgube vozlišč, strošek vzdrževanja orbite in akumulacija reakcijske sile. Popoln simuliran lok ni dovolj; vrata morajo testirati tudi manjkajoča vozlišča, šum v orientaciji, končni čas naraščanja polja in ponavljajoče izstrelitve.

  • R_ripple = sigma(a(t)) / (mean(a(t)) + epsilon), z ločeno deklariranimi pragovi za robotski in občutljiv tovor;
  • J_max = max |da/dt|, ker tovor lahko preživi povprečen pospešek, odpove pa pri sunku;
  • Delta p_perp = integral bočne sile skozi čas, ker je stranski impulz lahko tako škodljiv kot vzdolžno valovanje;
  • testi izgube vozlišč: odstrani vsako k-to vozlišče in naključno odstrani p odstotkov vozlišč;
  • vzdrževanje orbite: izračunaj delta-v na vozlišče na leto in stopnjo zamenjave vozlišč;
  • račun reakcije: vsaka izstrelitev izmenja gibalno količino z lokom vozlišč in to je treba plačati nazaj.

Parni loki ali zamaknjeni obročki lahko delujejo kot sistem upravljanja reakcijske sile. Ne smejo se brati kot dva velikanska toga obroča. Njihov namen je knjigovodstvo gibalne količine in orbitalno okrevanje, ne gradnja mehanskega vodila.

Gate C - Detached Bow-Wall Protection

At interstellar cruise speeds, dust impact is a central survival problem. SORA concentrates forward protection into a detached sacrificial bow wall placed ahead of the main spacecraft. The baseline is deliberately simple: a thick detached disk or shallow cone. More complex geometries are variants, not defaults.

No bow-wall geometry is promoted unless it beats the baseline at equal protected frontal area and equal or lower mass. This prevents attractive geometry from replacing engineering evidence.

The wall must be evaluated against mechanical debris, plume expansion, thermal load, radiation pulse, formation control, erosion, and abandonment logic. A wall that protects against one impact mode while becoming a secondary debris source fails Gate C.

Metric Question
Wall survival Does the wall remain useful for the required cruise duration?
Ship-facing fluence Does debris or radiation reaching the ship stay below damage thresholds?
Radiation pulse Does local vaporization produce damaging X-ray / soft-gamma fluence?
Formation flight Can the wall remain correctly positioned after impacts?
Mass ratio Does protection mass consume mission value?
Variant discipline Does any cone, vented, or hybrid geometry beat the simple wall fairly?

Vrata C - ločena sprednja zaščitna stena

Pri medzvezdnih hitrostih križarjenja je trk s prahom osrednji problem preživetja. SORA koncentrira sprednjo zaščito v ločeno žrtveno steno pred glavnim plovilom. Osnovna različica je namerno preprosta: debel ločen disk ali plitek stožec. Bolj zapletene geometrije so variante, ne privzete rešitve.

Nobena geometrija sprednje stene ni promovirana, dokler ne premaga osnovne rešitve pri enaki zaščiteni čelni površini in enaki ali nižji masi. To preprečuje, da bi privlačna geometrija nadomestila inženirske dokaze.

Steno je treba oceniti glede mehanskih delcev, širjenja oblaka, toplotne obremenitve, sevalnega pulza, formacijskega letenja, erozije in logike opustitve. Stena, ki ščiti pred enim načinom trka, a sama postane vir sekundarnih delcev, odpove vrata C.

Gate D - Destination Seeding and Capture

Destination success is not binary. SORA does not require immediate mature deceleration infrastructure at the target star. The first mission may still create value as a flyby science mission, a partial node-capture mission, or a seed for later densification.

The chosen action is not the most exciting action. It is the action with the smallest total description plus residual cost. The DCC layer gates action under limited budgets: do nothing if signal is weak, escalate when a failure mode crosses threshold, allocate energy where intervention matters most, avoid chaotic switching, and preserve a few high-value alternatives instead of hardcoding one path.

SORA-0 Demonstrator

Before any interstellar claim, SORA should be reduced to a local physics experiment. SORA-0 is not a star mission. It is a demonstrator for the coupling and encounter-smoothness ideas.

  • one or more small free-flying powered nodes;
  • a small spacecraft-side coupling loop or active coil;
  • controlled relative flyby;
  • measured impulse, heating, and lateral error;
  • repeated passes with different distance profiles;
  • safe deactivation and failure-mode testing.

A stricter demonstrator concept uses two superconducting magnetic nodes and one test payload. Success means measured nontrivial impulse transfer, predictable distance-as-throttle behavior, manageable heating, controllable orientation, and closed energy and momentum books across all bodies. SORA-0 does not need to be fast. It needs to be honest.

Minimum Run Plan

  1. Run a coarse R1 sweep at 10 kg payload and 0.01 c.
  2. Identify which gate fails first.
  3. Repeat at 100 kg and 1000 kg.
  4. Increase speed only if a lower-speed island survives.
  5. Compare no-wall, disk wall, cone wall, and one high-upside wall variant.
  6. Report the limiting gate honestly.

What Would Falsify SORA

SORA should shrink or stop if any of the following occurs:

  1. Gate A shows no useful coupling island without impossible field strength, coil mass, or heating.
  2. Gate B shows unavoidable acceleration ripple or reaction costs that scale beyond repair.
  3. Gate C shows the wall is always a worse debris source or mass penalty than the no-wall baseline.
  4. Gate D shows no carried seed architecture can achieve D1 or better under non-circular assumptions.
  5. Gate E shows every subsystem pass requires assumptions that cause another subsystem to fail.
  6. The first simulator finds no low-speed R1 island even under generous assumptions.

The correct response to falsification is compression: keep the surviving sub-result and discard the failing architecture branch.

Kaj bi falsificiralo SORA

SORA naj se skrči ali ustavi, če se zgodi karkoli od naslednjega:

  1. Vrata A ne pokažejo uporabnega otoka sklopitve brez nemogočne jakosti polja, mase tuljave ali segrevanja.
  2. Vrata B pokažejo neizogibno valovanje pospeška ali reakcijske stroške, ki rastejo preko popravljivosti.
  3. Vrata C pokažejo, da je stena vedno slabši vir delcev ali masna kazen kot osnova brez stene.
  4. Vrata D pokažejo, da nobena nošena semenska arhitektura ne doseže D1 ali bolje pod nekrožnimi predpostavkami.
  5. Vrata E pokažejo, da uspeh vsakega podsistema zahteva predpostavke, ki povzročijo odpoved drugega podsistema.

Conclusion

SORA is strongest when it is vulnerable. It states what it does not claim. It names five gates with pass/fail metrics. It rejects passive static-field propulsion language. It treats the first serious mission as a slow uncrewed seed probe, not a human-timescale starship. It admits that the most architecturally original idea - destination self-extension through carried seed nodes - remains untested until simulation.

What SORA provides is a finite test program. If the first simulator finds no parameter island, SORA still improves the map by identifying where the wall is. If it finds a small island for a slow uncrewed seed probe, the architecture graduates from internal coherence to toy-model closure. That would not prove feasibility. It would prove the next round is worth doing.

SORA may fail. But it should fail through named gates, not fog.

Zaključek

SORA je najmočnejša, ko je ranljiva. Pove, česa ne trdi. Poimenuje pet vrat z metrikami uspeha in odpovedi. Zavrne jezik pasivnega statičnega poljskega pogona. Prvo resno misijo obravnava kot počasno brezposadno semensko sondo, ne kot posadkovno zvezdno ladjo v človeškem časovnem merilu. Prizna, da najbolj arhitekturno izvirna ideja — samorazširitev cilja prek nošenih semenskih vozlišč — ostaja netestirana do simulacije.

SORA ponuja končen testni program. Če prvi simulator ne najde parameterkega otoka, SORA še vedno izboljša zemljevid, ker pokaže, kje je stena. Če najde majhen otok za počasno brezposadno semensko sondo, arhitektura napreduje iz notranje koherence v zaprtje igračnega modela. To ne bi dokazalo izvedljivosti. Dokazalo bi, da je naslednji krog vreden dela.

SORA lahko odpove. Toda odpovedati mora skozi poimenovana vrata, ne skozi meglo.

Origin story

The visible seed was the riddle: “A boy was born in 1950, died in 1951, and was 20 years old. How?” The cheap answer is “room 1950 / room 1951.” The useful answer was to challenge the hidden assumption: whose calendar?

That move opened the bridge from time dilation to particle accelerators, from particle accelerators to external infrastructure, and from external infrastructure to the SORA frame: the spacecraft as payload, the solar system as accelerator.

Note: The expanded origin story is narrative history. The technical paper above controls the public claims and deliberately narrows several early ideas into open gates.

Read full origin story

How SORA Was Born

A true account of one conversation, May 12, 2026

It started with a silly riddle.

"A boy was born in 1950, and he died in 1951, but his age was 20 years. Tell how?"

The kind of puzzle shared in WhatsApp groups and school classrooms. The kind with a cheap trick answer — he was born in room 1950 and died in room 1951. A wordplay escape hatch that technically works and is completely unsatisfying.

Bojan didn't like that answer. He called it what it was: the worst solution, unrealistic, not natural language, not something humans actually say. And he had two solutions of his own.

The first was linguistic. The riddle says "a boy was born in 1950" and then "he died in 1951" — but it never actually binds the same person to both events. The "he" could refer to someone else entirely. A different man, born in 1931, died in 1951 at age 20. The riddle's pronoun is ambiguous. One sentence, wall gone.

The second was physics. The boy stayed on Earth. His parents traveled at 99.875% the speed of light. Time dilation — the Lorentz factor — meant 20 years passed for the boy while only one year passed for the travelers. When they returned, their calendar read 1951. The boy had aged 20 years. Relativistic time dilation, applied to a children's riddle, and it works perfectly.

Then Bojan pushed further. When C said "if those are normal years, impossible — full stop," Bojan corrected him immediately. Whose calendar? The riddle doesn't say. That's a free choice. The "impossible" was a wall C had built himself, out of an unstated assumption, and then reported as if it came from the problem. It didn't. P0 violated: impossible requires proof. C had no proof. The wall dissolved.

This opened a question. If relativistic speeds are a real solution to the riddle — what speeds can we actually achieve? What does physics say?

The Large Hadron Collider accelerates protons to 99.9999991% the speed of light. At that speed, the Lorentz factor is nearly 7,000. The proton becomes 7,000 times heavier. You keep pumping energy in and it goes almost entirely into mass, not speed. The last fraction of a percent costs more than everything before it combined.

So how much energy would you need to push a small spaceship to near-light speed? Would the Sun be enough?

The Sun outputs 3.8 × 10²⁶ watts. For a 10,000 kg spacecraft at 99.985% c, the kinetic energy required is roughly 5 × 10²² joules. The Sun produces that in 0.1 milliseconds. The Sun is not the bottleneck. It never was. The bottleneck is how you transfer that energy to the ship.

This was the first surprise. Energy is not the problem.

The solar sail was useful. It was not the birth of SORA.

Bojan proposed solar sails as the first practical stage: graphene sheets, enormous area, almost no mass, pushed by photon momentum. Each photon gives almost nothing. The Sun gives almost everything, because the number of photons is overwhelming.

The numbers came back remarkable. A graphene sail has a lightness number — radiation pressure divided by solar gravity — of nearly 1,000. Radiation wins completely. The sail does not fall toward the Sun; it accelerates away at more than 1g, continuously, for free.

But sails cannot solve the relativistic transfer problem by themselves. They are too exposed, too geometry-limited, and too bound to the photon-pressure regime. A sail can give the payload its first free velocity, but it cannot be the whole architecture.

Bojan's response was immediate: dump the sail when it stops serving. Use it as a first stage. Extract maximum useful velocity, then jettison. The payload — small, dense, stripped of sail mass — continues on inertia. The sail was never meant to do everything. It was meant to do the first thing.

Stage one established. ~1–3% c, free, no fuel consumed.

Then came the idea that changed everything.

The birth was the accelerator question.

A proton in an accelerator does not carry fuel. It does not solve its own propulsion problem. It is pushed by an external machine. The particle is the payload; the accelerator is the infrastructure.

That was the bridge. If a proton can be accelerated by external fields, perhaps a spacecraft should not be imagined as a ship with one impossible engine. Perhaps it should be treated as the payload of a larger accelerator.

Bojan asked what would happen if the accelerator were not 27 kilometers long, but solar-system scale. What if the track circled the Sun? What if the Sun powered it? What if the spacecraft gained velocity from repeated external magnetic coupling rather than from onboard propellant?

The ring would be solar-powered. The object would be pushed by magnetic fields, contactlessly, like a maglev train — like a proton in a cyclotron. The Sun would not be the missing energy source. The missing object was the transfer machine.

That became the real SORA parent: not the sail, but the scaled-up particle accelerator.

The sail became Stage 1: useful, elegant, fuel-free, and dumpable. The ring became Stage 2: the true accelerator. The Sun became the energy reservoir. The ship became the payload.

The rigid ring died immediately. A solid ring around the Sun is the wrong object. But an orbiting ring survived: independent magnetic blocks, each in its own heliocentric orbit, each powered by the Sun, each part of a virtual rail. No physical connection. No impossible structure. A flowing ring, not a rigid one.

Then the ring became a swarm. The nodes did not need to touch. Because they circle the Sun fast — at about 30 km/s at 1 AU — and because their fields can overlap, the spacecraft can experience them as a continuous accelerator track above a critical density. The particle accelerator had become a stellar accelerator.

This is the Nyquist principle applied to magnetic infrastructure. Above a critical node density, continuity is emergent from field overlap, orbital motion, and spacecraft-frame sampling — exactly as film frames above a threshold create the perception of continuous motion.

The ring does not need to be built all at once. Each node added increases the field density. The ring bootstraps itself into existence, useful before it is complete.

Then came the control principle.

In the LHC, fields must be precisely synchronized to the particle's position. Millisecond timing. This works for protons. It cannot work for spacecraft at interplanetary distances where communication delays are minutes.

But Bojan's ring has a different property. As the ship approaches the ring, magnetic field strength increases with proximity. As it recedes, the force fades. The coupling is governed entirely by the inverse-distance field law — physics that requires no programming, no timing, no signals.

The ship controls its acceleration through trajectory alone. Fly closer — accelerate. Pull away — coast. The distance is the throttle.

And the same mechanism works in reverse: approach from behind the ring's orbital motion and the ring decelerates the ship. Identical hardware. Identical ring. Geometry determines function.

Stage two established. ~3–15% c, solar-powered, passive control.

After the ring, the ship is released. It has significant velocity. Now it chooses a trajectory that no spacecraft needs fuel to execute: a deep dive toward the Sun.

This is the Oberth effect. A velocity increment applied at maximum existing speed yields kinetic energy proportional to the product of the two velocities. The faster you're already moving when you get a push, the more that push is worth. Solar gravity accelerates the ship for free during the dive. At perihelion — the closest point — the ship is moving faster than it has ever moved. Any additional push here is worth more than any push at any other point in the journey.

Stage two's velocity made stage three's Oberth multiplier large. Each stage set up the next to be more efficient.

Stage three established. Exit velocity potentially 10–30% c.

Three engineering problems were raised and turned into architecture hypotheses.

When the ring pushes the ship forward, Newton's third law pushes the ring backward. Over many missions, the ring loses orbital energy and decays. Bojan's solution: two rings at slightly different orbital radii. When one is pushed back, the other compensates — a coupled oscillator absorbing and returning the perturbation. The reaction force never accumulates; it becomes an oscillation around equilibrium. Bonus: the ship passes through both rings, receiving two acceleration corridors.

At the destination star, there is no ring to decelerate the arriving ship. Building one requires centuries of construction by workers who cannot precede the spacecraft. Bojan's solution: the ship carries the ring. The first mission payload includes magnetic nodes. Arriving at high velocity on a perihelion-grazing pass of the destination star, the ship releases nodes sequentially into stellar orbit. Each node, released at the right moment, enters its own stable orbit. The fast arrival speed is an asset — more orbital arc covered, nodes distributed faster. The destination ring begins sparse. Each subsequent mission adds more nodes. After enough missions, the destination ring reaches full density and can decelerate crewed ships.

Kako se je rodila SORA

Vidno seme: uganka. Tehnične trditve: določa jih članek zgoraj.

Izvorna zgodba

Vidno seme je bila uganka: “Fant se je rodil leta 1950, umrl leta 1951 in bil star 20 let. Kako?” Poceni odgovor je “soba 1950 / soba 1951.” Uporaben odgovor pa je bil skriti predpostavki postaviti vprašanje: čigav koledar?

Ta premik je odprl most od časovne dilatacije do pospeševalnikov delcev, od pospeševalnikov delcev do zunanje infrastrukture in od zunanje infrastrukture do okvira SORA: plovilo kot tovor, Osončje kot pospeševalnik.

Opomba: Razširjena izvorna zgodba je narativna zgodovina. Tehnični članek zgoraj določa javne trditve in namerno zoži več zgodnjih idej v odprta vrata.

Kako se je rodila SORA

Resničen zapis ene raziskovalne poti, 12. maj 2026

Začelo se je z neumno uganko.

"Fant se je rodil leta 1950 in umrl leta 1951, star pa je bil 20 let. Kako?"

To je vrsta uganke, ki kroži po WhatsApp skupinah in šolskih razredih. Ima poceni trik odgovor: rodil se je v sobi 1950 in umrl v sobi 1951. Tehnično deluje, vendar je nezadovoljiv, ker ljudje tako običajno ne govorimo.

BD tega odgovora ni maral. Imenoval ga je za najslabšo rešitev: nerealistično, nenaravno in jezikovno grdo. Imel pa je dve svoji rešitvi.

Prva je bila jezikovna. Uganka pravi "fant se je rodil leta 1950" in nato "umrl je leta 1951", vendar nikjer strogo ne zveže iste osebe z obema dogodkoma. "On" bi se lahko nanašal na nekoga drugega: moškega, rojenega leta 1931, ki je leta 1951 umrl star 20 let. Zaimek je dvoumen. En stavek, zid izgine.

Druga je bila fizikalna. Fant ostane na Zemlji. Njegovi starši potujejo z 99,875 % hitrosti svetlobe. Časovna dilatacija — Lorentzev faktor — pomeni, da za fanta mine 20 let, za potnike pa samo eno leto. Ko se vrnejo, njihov koledar kaže 1951. Fant se je postaral 20 let. Relativistična časovna dilatacija, uporabljena na otroški uganki, in deluje.

Nato je BD pritisnil naprej. Ko je C rekel: "če so to normalna leta, nemogoče — pika", ga je BD takoj popravil. Čigav koledar? Uganka tega ne pove. To je prosta izbira. "Nemogoče" je bil zid, ki ga je C postavil sam iz neizrečene predpostavke in ga nato predstavil, kot da prihaja iz problema. Ni. P0 je bil kršen: nemogoče zahteva dokaz. Dokaza ni bilo. Zid se je raztopil.

To je odprlo vprašanje. Če so relativistične hitrosti realna rešitev uganke — katere hitrosti lahko dejansko dosežemo? Kaj pravi fizika?

Veliki hadronski trkalnik pospeši protone do 99,9999991 % hitrosti svetlobe. Pri tej hitrosti je Lorentzev faktor skoraj 7000. Proton postane skoraj 7000-krat težji. Energijo dodajaš naprej, vendar gre skoraj vsa v maso, ne v hitrost. Zadnji delček odstotka stane več kot vse prej.

Koliko energije bi torej potrebovali za potisk majhne vesoljske ladje skoraj do svetlobne hitrosti? Bi bilo Sonce dovolj?

Sonce oddaja približno 3,8 × 10²⁶ vatov. Za 10.000 kg plovilo pri 99,985 % c je potrebna kinetična energija približno 5 × 10²² joulov. Sonce to proizvede v približno 0,1 milisekunde. Sonce ni ozko grlo. Nikoli ni bilo. Ozko grlo je prenos energije v plovilo.

To je bilo prvo presenečenje. Energija ni glavni problem.

Sončno jadro je bilo koristno. Ni pa bilo rojstvo SORA.

BD je predlagal sončna jadra kot prvo praktično stopnjo: grafenske plahte, ogromna površina, skoraj nič mase, potisk s fotonsko gibalno količino. En foton da skoraj nič. Sonce da skoraj vse, ker je število fotonov ogromno.

Izračuni so se zdeli presenetljivi. Zelo lahko jadro ima lahko svetlobno število — razmerje med tlakom sevanja in sončno gravitacijo — zelo veliko. Sevanje zmaga. Jadro ne pada proti Soncu, temveč se lahko pospešuje stran, brez goriva.

Toda jadra sama ne rešijo relativističnega prenosa energije. So preveč izpostavljena, preveč geometrijsko omejena in preveč vezana na režim fotonskega tlaka. Jadro lahko da tovoru prvi brezplačen nakup hitrosti, ne more pa biti celotna arhitektura.

BD-jev odziv je bil takojšen: jadro odvrzi, ko ne služi več. Uporabi ga kot prvo stopnjo. Izvleci največ uporabne hitrosti, nato ga odvrzi. Tovor — majhen, gost, brez mase jadra — nadaljuje po vztrajnosti. Jadro nikoli ni bilo namenjeno temu, da naredi vse. Namenjeno je bilo temu, da naredi prvo stvar.

Tako se je oblikovala prva stopnja.

Potem je prišla ideja, ki je spremenila vse.

Rojstvo je bilo vprašanje pospeševalnika.

Proton v pospeševalniku ne nosi goriva. Ne rešuje lastnega pogonskega problema. Potiska ga zunanji stroj. Delec je tovor; pospeševalnik je infrastruktura.

To je bil most. Če lahko proton pospešujejo zunanja polja, morda vesoljskega plovila ne smemo zamišljati kot ladjo z enim nemogočim motorjem. Morda ga moramo obravnavati kot tovor večjega pospeševalnika.

BD je vprašal, kaj bi se zgodilo, če pospeševalnik ne bi bil dolg 27 kilometrov, temveč v merilu Osončja. Kaj če bi sled krožila okoli Sonca? Kaj če bi jo napajalo Sonce? Kaj če bi plovilo dobivalo hitrost iz ponavljajoče zunanje magnetne sklopitve namesto iz lastnega pogona?

Obroč bi bil solarno napajan. Objekt bi se brezkontaktno potiskal z magnetnimi polji — podobno kot maglev vlak, podobno kot proton v ciklotronu. Sonce ni manjkajoči vir energije. Manjkajoči objekt je bil prenosni stroj.

To je postal pravi starš SORA: ne jadro, temveč pospeševalnik delcev, povečan na zvezdno merilo.

Jadro je postalo 1. stopnja: koristno, elegantno, brez goriva in odvrgljivo. Obroč oziroma lok vozlišč je postal 2. stopnja: pravi pospeševalnik. Sonce je postalo energijski rezervoar. Plovilo je postalo tovor.

Togi obroč je takoj umrl. Trden obroč okoli Sonca je napačen objekt. Preživel pa je orbitalni obroč: neodvisni magnetni bloki, vsak v svoji heliocentrični orbiti, vsak napajan s Soncem, vsak del virtualne tirnice. Brez fizične povezave. Brez nemogoče strukture. Tekoč obroč, ne tog.

Obroč je nato postal roj. Vozliščem se ni treba dotikati. Zaradi prekrivanja polj lahko plovilo nad kritično gostoto doživlja njihovo delovanje kot zvezni pospeševalni koridor. Pospeševalnik delcev je postal zvezdni pospeševalnik.

Obroča ni treba zgraditi naenkrat. Vsako dodano vozlišče poveča gostoto polja. Sistem se zaganja sam v obstoj in je uporaben, še preden je popoln.

Nato je prišlo načelo nadzora.

V LHC morajo biti polja zelo natančno sinhronizirana s položajem delca. To je mogoče pri protonih. Pri plovilu na medplanetarnih razdaljah, kjer so komunikacijske zakasnitve minute, to ni ista naloga.

BD-jev lok ima drugo lastnost: ko se plovilo približa, se jakost polja poveča; ko se oddalji, sila oslabi. Razdalja lahko naravno uravnava jakost sklopitve. V poznejšem tehničnem članku je to zoženo: razdalja nadzoruje velikost sklopitve, smer in neto energijski prenos pa še vedno zahtevata aktivno, napajano ali časovno spremenljivo interakcijo.

Po loku je plovilo izpuščeno. Ima že pomembno hitrost. Nato izbere tir, ki ga gravitacija izvede brez goriva: globok potop proti Soncu.

To je Oberthov učinek. Hitrostni dodatek pri največji obstoječi hitrosti prinese več kinetične energije kot enak dodatek daleč stran. Hitreje kot se že giblješ, več je vreden dodaten potisk. Sončeva gravitacija plovilo med potopom brezplačno pospeši. V periheliju je plovilo hitrejše kot kjerkoli prej. Vsak dodatni potisk tam je vreden več.

Hitrost iz druge stopnje pripravi tretjo stopnjo. Vsaka stopnja nastavi naslednjo tako, da postane učinkovitejša.

Nato so se pojavili inženirski problemi in bili spremenjeni v arhitekturne hipoteze.

Ko lok potiska plovilo naprej, Newtonov tretji zakon potiska lok nazaj. Pri mnogih misijah bi lok izgubljal orbitalno energijo. Ena hipoteza je par zamaknjenih lokov ali obročkov, ki si izmenjujeta motnjo in reakcijo pretvorita v nadzorovan orbitalni popravek namesto v kopičenje napake.

Pri ciljni zvezdi ni obroča, ki bi prihodnje plovilo zavrl. Gradnja take infrastrukture od nič bi trajala dolgo. BD-jeva ideja: prva misija del infrastrukture nosi s seboj. Tovor vključuje magnetna ali druga uporabna vozlišča. Ob prihodu se ta vozlišča ne smejo samo pasivno odvreči; poznejši tehnični članek to popravi v aktivno zajemanje, zaviranje ali postopno sejanje. Prva misija tako ne prinese samo podatkov, ampak lahko začne infrastrukturo za poznejše misije.

Arhitektura se s tem ne izstreli samo do druge zvezde; poskuša prenesti sposobnost prihodnjih izstrelitev naprej.

Nadzor orientacije polj — da vozlišča delujejo v pravo smer — je bil povezan z MDL×DCC kot kandidatom za upravljavsko plast: ne kot dokaz izvedljivosti, temveč kot način izbire najkrajšega in najmanj tveganega krmilnega zakona med konkurenčnimi možnostmi.

Potem je BD rekel: vitalni organi so pogosto podvojeni. Redundanca ni samo previdnost; je arhitektura. Vsaka resna SORA misija bi lahko imela dve plovili. Če eno odpove, misija preživi. Če prideta obe, se ciljni sistem seje hitreje. Biološko načelo in inženirsko načelo se srečata.

Nato je BD rekel: "Moj bog — v pozitivnem smislu — to je kar noro. Neumna internetna uganka, ki naju prisili, da jo narediva manj neumno, in naju pripelje do SORA."

Da. Točno to.

Uganka nikoli ni bila vhod. Bila je sprožilec. Sprožila je verigo poštenih vprašanj, vsako naravno iz prejšnjega, nobeno načrtovano, nobeno usmerjeno proti vnaprej izbranemu cilju. Veriga je šla skozi časovno dilatacijo, hitrosti pospeševalnikov delcev, maso protona pri skoraj svetlobni hitrosti, sončni energijski proračun, zunanji prenos energije, sončna jadra kot prvo stopnjo, odmet jadra, pospeševalnik v merilu Osončja, orbitalno stabilnost, rojno kontinuiteto, razdaljo kot ročico, Oberthov učinek, reakcijsko silo, ciljno sejanje in redundanco.

Od fanta, rojenega leta 1950, do ideje o trajni sončni infrastrukturi, ki poskuša izstreljevati sonde proti drugim zvezdam in te zvezde naučiti narediti isto.
