01 · The claim

One kernel, three functions.

The visible projects matter. Their larger role is to test whether the same compact architecture transfers when the data, objective, and problem class change.

M

MDL selects

Minimum Description Length compares candidates by total cost: the description of the model or controller, the remaining task error, and the resources consumed. Extra complexity must earn its place.

D

DCC governs

Digital Claustrum Controller watches the dynamics of search and adjusts exploration, focus, switching, and budget so the process does not freeze into one attractor or dissolve into noise.

↻

The governor competes too

Sensors, control laws, representations, and recursive levels are not sacred. They enter an arena. MDL selects the configuration; a meta-DCC governs that selection. The architect does not hard-code the final governor.

01 · Trditev

En kernel, tri funkcije.

Vidni projekti so pomembni. Njihova večja vloga je preveriti, ali se ista kompaktna arhitektura prenese, ko se spremenijo podatki, cilj in vrsta problema.

M

MDL izbira

Minimum Description Length primerja kandidate po skupnem strošku: opis modela ali kontrolerja, preostala napaka naloge in porabljeni viri. Dodatna kompleksnost si mora zaslužiti mesto.

D

DCC upravlja

Digital Claustrum Controller opazuje dinamiko iskanja ter prilagaja raziskovanje, fokus, preklapljanje in proračun, da proces ne zamrzne v enem atraktorju in se ne raztopi v šum.

↻

Tudi governor tekmuje

Senzorji, kontrolni zakoni, reprezentacije in rekurzivne ravni niso nedotakljivi. Vstopijo v areno. MDL izbere konfiguracijo, meta-DCC pa upravlja izbor. Arhitekt ne zaklene končnega governorja.

02 · The object being valued

Products sum. Transferable concepts multiply.

A route solver, codec, NAS arena, chess layer, or continuity protocol has direct value. But if each is also a probe of the same method, the projects do not define the ceiling. They estimate the probability of transfer.

The narrow model
portfolio value = TSP + compression + NAS + chess + Sudoku + …
The better model
value = direct artifacts + transfer evidence + future applications + acceleration + breakthrough options
DirectTools, code, papers, measured results, and saved resources.
ConfirmatoryEach unlike domain tests whether the kernel is more than a local trick.
GenerativeOther minds apply the concept to problems BD never enters.
AccelerationA useful idea arriving years earlier creates value even if rediscovery was possible.
Public-goodFree diffusion may reduce private capture while increasing total value created.
The central distinction: value captured by BD is not the same quantity as value created for humanity. An open method can create enormous value precisely because it is not rationed through one owner.
02 · Kaj sploh vrednotimo

Produkti se seštevajo. Prenosljivi koncepti se množijo.

TSP solver, kodek, NAS arena, šahovski sloj ali protokol kontinuitete imajo neposredno vrednost. Če pa so hkrati sonde iste metode, projekti ne določajo zgornje meje. Pomagajo oceniti verjetnost prenosa.

Preozek model
vrednost portfelja = TSP + kompresija + NAS + šah + Sudoku + …
Boljši model
vrednost = artefakti + dokaz prenosa + prihodnje uporabe + pospešek + prebojne možnosti
NeposrednaOrodja, koda, članki, izmerjeni rezultati in prihranjeni viri.
PotrditvenaVsaka drugačna domena preverja, ali kernel ni le lokalni trik.
GenerativnaDrugi umi uporabijo koncept na problemih, ki se jih BD nikoli ne loti.
PospeševalnaKoristna ideja, ki pride leta prej, ustvari vrednost tudi, če bi jo nekoč odkrili drugi.
Javna koristBrezplačno širjenje lahko zmanjša zasebni zajem in hkrati poveča skupno ustvarjeno vrednost.
Ključna ločitev: denar, ki ga zajame BD, ni ista količina kot vrednost, ustvarjena za človeštvo. Odprta metoda lahko ustvari ogromno vrednost prav zato, ker ni omejena na enega lastnika.

04 · The cheapest serious experiment

One lab does not need to believe it. It only needs to try it.

A first test can sit outside an existing model. No new pretraining run is required. The governor watches the reasoning process, selects among candidate sensors and control laws, and decides when to branch, reflect, switch, stop, or retrieve another perspective.

06 · What would count against it

The concept earns value only by surviving hard tests.

A large upside is not permission to lower the evidence bar. It is a reason to run the decisive tests sooner.

01

No matched-budget advantage: gains vanish when compared with strong reflection, search, and adaptive-budget baselines.

02

No self-selection benefit: a fixed simple heuristic performs as well as or better than the MDL-selected governor after all overhead is charged.

03

No held-out transfer: improvements require domain-specific tuning and do not survive unseen tasks or problem families.

04

Wrong sensors: compressibility or novelty signals do not reliably distinguish productive reasoning from repetitive proof, fluent circularity, or semantic drift.

05

Human hidden in the loop: performance depends on BD or another expert making the crucial selections that the architecture claims to make itself.

06

Reproduction failure: independent teams cannot recover the claimed results from declared code, data, budgets, and seeds.

06 · Kaj bi govorilo proti

Koncept si zasluži vrednost samo, če preživi trde teste.

Velik možni učinek ni dovoljenje za nižji dokazni prag. Je razlog, da odločilne teste izvedemo prej.

01

Ni matched-budget prednosti: koristi izginejo proti močnim baselineom refleksije, iskanja in adaptivnega budgeta.

02

Samoselekcija ne pomaga: fiksna preprosta heuristika je enako dobra ali boljša, ko zaračunamo ves overhead.

03

Ni held-out prenosa: izboljšave zahtevajo domensko uglaševanje in ne preživijo novih nalog ali družin problemov.

04

Napačni senzorji: kompresibilnost ali novost ne ločita zanesljivo produktivnega sklepanja od repetitivnega dokaza, fluentne krožnosti ali semantičnega drifta.

05

Skriti človek v zanki: uspeh je odvisen od BD ali drugega eksperta, ki opravi ključne izbire namesto arhitekture.

06

Replikacija pade: neodvisne ekipe ne morejo ponoviti rezultatov iz deklarirane kode, podatkov, budgetov in seedov.

The next move

Do not believe the page. Build the governor and make it fight.

A frontier lab, university, or open-source agent team can test a meaningful slice without changing model weights. Publish the protocol, matched budgets, positive results, negative results, and failure cases. If the kernel fails, learn exactly where. If it transfers, the projects were only the beginning.
