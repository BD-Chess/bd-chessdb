0.2 Algorithmic information, MDL and MML foundation#

The compression principle used here has a precise lineage. Solomonoff induction and Kolmogorov complexity define an ideal relation between short generative descriptions and prediction, but the universal quantities are not computable in general [18,19]. Minimum Message Length and Minimum Description Length turn the same broad insight into explicit statistical coding disciplines: a model must pay for itself by shortening the complete message, not merely by fitting observations [20–23].

MPRC therefore uses operational code lengths, never an oracle claim about exact algorithmic complexity. Every code must declare its alphabet, prefix or normalization convention, parameter precision, residual distribution, ordering rule and codebook cost. A practical prequential form is

$$ \mathcal L_{\rm preq}(M;D) = L(M)+L(\mathcal C_M) +\sum_{t=1}^{n}-\log_2 p_M(x_t\mid x_{<t}), $$

where 𝒞M denotes the executable codebook and modelling machinery. The exact implementation may differ, but it must be finite, reproducible and fixed before the confirmation split is opened.

Scope: MDL is a sensor for generalizing structure. It is not a metaphysical proof that reality “is compression,” and a short model can still be wrong outside its declared data and intervention domain.

1. The core proposition#

The parts-first picture assumes local constituents and attempts to build the universe from them. AC–WFP tests the inverse explanatory order:

Locality, particles, fields and spacetime may be stable effective structures generated within a globally constrained Whole.

This is not the claim that large spatial objects create smaller spatial objects. If spacetime is emergent, the Whole is not larger in metres. It is more inclusive and ontologically prior to any particular factorization into local pieces.

The strongest neutral statement is:

A physical world is a stable relational regime in which compatible restrictions of Ω acquire predictive closure, recoverable records, causal reach, geometry, energy–momentum and persistent modes.

The AC interpretation adds:

Ultimate actuality is not wholly devoid of presence. Physical organization localizes, stabilizes and enriches bounded relative consciousness rather than creating phenomenal presence from absolute non-presence.

2. Undivided is not featureless#

In an algebraic Stage-II representation, a trivial algebra cannot generate nontrivial internal structure:

$$ \mathcal M=\mathbb C\mathbf1 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \operatorname{Der}(\mathcal M)=0, \qquad \operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal M)=\{\mathrm{id}\}, \qquad \operatorname{Proj}(\mathcal M)=\{0,1\}. $$

Therefore

$$ \boxed{ \text{undivided Whole} \neq \text{featureless mathematical object}. } $$

This is a representation-level no-go, not a proof that operator algebra is the final ontology. It says that a generative representation must possess at least constrained latent relational capacity.

The open foundational question is:

What is the least structure sufficient for differentiation, while still too weak to encode our observed world by hand?

2.1 Global capacity / local compression inversion#

V4-DRAFT retains the apparent reversal between the Whole and its effective parts:

The global actuality must carry enough irreducible relational capacity to permit genuine differentiation, while successful local restrictions must become conditionally compressible under short predictive models.

This is the Global Capacity / Local Compression Inversion (GCLI). It blocks two easy but sterile extremes.

Trivial Whole#

Too little relational capacity: no nontrivial distinctions, modes, automorphisms or effective parts.

Random Whole#

High raw incompressibility but no stable reusable restrictions; apparent structure fails held-out and perturbation tests.

Overwritten Whole#

The desired geometry or partition is encoded directly in D, the search grammar or the oracle. Recovery is decoding, not explanation.

Viable window#

Nontrivial global capacity plus robust local lawfulness after total model and machinery cost.

A finite arena operationalizes only the second half of the inversion. With a domain-specific nontriviality measure 𝒩, it asks for

$$ \mathcal N(\Omega)>0, \qquad \exists\,\mathcal P\notin\{\text{whole},\text{singletons}\}: \Delta L_{\rm test}(\mathcal P)\ge\delta_{\rm abs}, \qquad r_{\rm FP}\le\alpha. $$

𝒩 is not presented as exact Kolmogorov complexity. The deeper question—why Ω has this capacity at all—remains open.

5.2 Co-emergence as testable MDL synergy#

Co-emergence is not a poetic synonym for simultaneity. It means that one jointly learned mechanism predicts the held-out physical package more economically than a pipeline of separately fitted modules.

$$ \Delta_{\rm co}^{\rm test} = L_{\rm seq}^{\rm test} - L_{\rm joint}^{\rm test} - \Delta L_{\rm machinery}. $$

A joint mechanism receives the label CO_EMERGENT only when all three pre-registered conditions pass:

$$ \Delta_{\rm co}^{\rm test}\ge\delta_{\rm co,abs}, \qquad \frac{\Delta_{\rm co}^{\rm test}}{L_{\rm seq}^{\rm test}} \ge\delta_{\rm co,rel}, \qquad \operatorname{LCB}_{1-\alpha}(\Delta_{\rm co})>0. $$

Removing one component must worsen at least one other independently scored component. Otherwise the proposal is a convenient bundle, not a mutually constraining phase. The sequential and joint architectures remain a bifurcation pair until the arena decides.

Δco and the later centre-level quantity Δcentre belong to the same MDL family at different scales. They are not identical to the heuristic score S=√(C·X).

Anti-tautology: joint models often fit better simply because they are larger. ΔLmachinery, the pre-registered margins and the confirmation split are mandatory.

Part II — Stage I and Stage II#

6. Stage I: whole-first reconstruction of quantum structure#

AC–WFP may not begin with Hilbert space and then declare quantum mechanics derived. Stage I begins with a causal operational framework containing:

  • states;
  • effects;
  • transformations;
  • composition rules;
  • probabilities;
  • no primitive spacetime geometry.

The current whole-first principles are:

W1 — compatible local facts admit a Whole#

Every jointly admissible family of perspective-indexed local facts belongs to at least one compatibility class.

W2 — whole-first purification#

Every mixed local state has a pure completion, essentially unique up to reversible transformations on the complement, under the assumptions of the reconstruction theorem used.

W3 — no privileged classical distinction#

Pure alternatives of the same operational type are reversibly connected unless an independently stated conserved structure distinguishes them.

W4 — causal composition#

Present outcome probabilities cannot depend on later freely chosen operations.

W5 — local distinguishability or an explicit replacement#

35. Hard kill conditions#

Demote or repair the mechanism if:

  • its parameters do not alter candidate behaviour;
  • its gain is reproduced by random energy or shuffled controls;
  • a classical baseline matches or beats it at lower total cost;
  • it succeeds only on a generator built from the same latent family;
  • the oracle target is systematically penalized by the frozen objective;
  • score parity fails;
  • naturality fails under exact representation change;
  • null false positives are uncontrolled;
  • resume changes scientific outputs;
  • synthetic replay reaches the scientific score;
  • the mechanism needs held-out or hidden metadata to work.

Part VIII — Development sequence, later sidecars and verdict#

37. Research sequence#

Phase V4-0 — instrument repair#

Implement the temporal candidate object, independent prequential scorer, genuine generator laws, explicit machinery ledger, production/reference/audit parity and operational resilience.

Phase V4-1 — wake-only challenger tournament#

Run MPRC2, MSR2, proposal-only LEC, LEC ablations and strong classical baselines on development systems. Mine objective misses and disagreement cases rather than repeating easy seeds.

Phase V4-2 — unseen-law transfer#

Freeze a subset of complete generator laws as development holdouts. A mechanism that succeeds only on aligned latent-Markov systems is not promoted.

Phase V4-3 — final contract selection#

Retain only mechanisms and controls that pass kill-tests. Freeze exact thresholds, codebooks, budget matching, approximation tolerances, statistics and complete failure rules.

Phase V4-4 — fresh confirmation#

Generate a new sealed split after the final contract hash is fixed. Only a compatible signed pass from the final qualification instrument may open Arena 0A.

Phase V4-5 — evidence-driven paper freeze#

Final V4 records what survived. Failed mechanisms are demoted explicitly; they are not preserved by changing interpretation after the data.

38. Deferred wake–sleep experiment#

Wake–sleep is scientifically interesting but not part of the first alpha score.

Promotion prerequisites are:

  1. wake-only LEC has a specific held-out advantage;
  2. exact small-case energy calibration passes;
  3. synthetic data are permanently tainted;
  4. the sleep phase is compared against NO_SLEEP, SHUFFLED_SLEEP and SYNTHETIC_NO_UPDATE;
  5. no synthetic observation enters held-out scientific scoring;
  6. model, negative-phase and sampling costs are fully charged.

A sleep phase that improves only its own recognition accuracy but not real held-out closure discovery is rejected.

40. Later scientific sidecars#

40.1 Arena 0X-I — intervention-robust closure#

Introduce bounded internal perturbations and require a candidate to predict what changes, what remains invariant and whether the effective boundary recovers. A pass can support INTERVENTION_ROBUST_CLOSURE, not spacetime.

40.2 Multiscale fixed-point closure#

Test whether repeated

model → parts → effective model → parts

converges to a stable hierarchy with measurable basin size, perturbation recovery and representation covariance.

40.3 Disagreement Forge#

Search development domains where MPRC2, MSR2, LEC and classical baselines disagree while exact evaluation remains possible. Forge outputs are curriculum and debugging evidence, never confirmation evidence.

40.4 Physics gates#

Only after a robust closure mechanism is earned should the programme activate finite causal-cone, dimension-attractor, geometry–matter, measurement and embodied-RC arenas.
