      Abstract#

      AC–WFP begins with a reversal of the usual parts-first ontology:

      The physical Whole is not assembled from independently existing local pieces. Effective parts are stable, mutually compatible, predictive and reconstructible restrictions of a deeper relational actuality.

      The ontological interpretation calls the ultimate actuality AC — Absolute Consciousness. The neutral scientific placeholder is Ω, so the mathematical and physical programme can be tested without assuming consciousness. This paper keeps that separation strict: a successful Ω theory would support whole-first physics, but would not by itself establish that Ω is conscious.

      The paper is organized as a staged research architecture rather than a pre-time production story. Before physical time is recovered, arrows mean dependence, admissibility, reconstruction or fixed-point consistency, not chronological succession. The central dependency graph is

      The framework adopts a two-layer representation-resistant kernel. A concrete Stage-II presentation is

      $$ K_\alpha = (\mathcal M_\alpha,\omega_\alpha,D_\alpha; J_\alpha,\Gamma_\alpha,\mathcal C_\alpha), $$

      while the scientific kernel is the family of admissible presentations, exact equivalences and declared invariants,

      $$ \boxed{ K_\Omega = (\mathsf{Pres}_\Omega,\mathfrak D_\Omega,\operatorname{Inv}) . } $$

      No one wavefunction, algebra, bulk geometry, boundary theory, graph or spectral triple is identified automatically with the Whole.

      The primary candidate physicalization mechanism is Minimal Predictive–Reconstructive Closure (MPRC). It searches for overlapping effective algebras whose intrinsic projected dynamics are compressible and predictive, whose content can be recovered where the claim requires it, and whose discovery is invariant under exact changes of presentation. Its update is

      $$ \boxed{ \mathcal P_{n+1} = F_{\rm MPRC,\alpha} (\mathcal P_n\mid K_\alpha), \qquad \mathcal P_\alpha^* = F_{\rm MPRC,\alpha} (\mathcal P_\alpha^*\mid K_\alpha). } $$

      MPRC is the primary mechanism because its objective is general predictive and reconstructive closure, not a direct reward for spacetime, Lorentz symmetry, Einstein gravity, the Standard Model, life or consciousness.

      The paper does not blend every attractive mechanism into MPRC. Modular–Spectral Recovery (MSR) is retained as a matched, explicit challenger based on the intrinsic channel

      $$ \boxed{ \Phi_{\eta,s}^{\omega,D} = \exp(\eta\mathcal G_D)\circ\sigma_s^\omega, \qquad \mathcal G_D(A)=-\tfrac12[D,[D,A]]. } $$

      MPRC and MSR use the same inputs, model-cost accounting, naturality tests, controls and held-out evaluation. MPRC asks which parts best compress and predict intrinsic relational data; MSR asks which algebras are most recoverable and persistent under a declared modular–spectral channel. Their separation is deliberate: if they are merged by default, their common assumptions can hide circularity and the information already stored in D.

      Physics is evaluated after discovery. Candidate fixed points are tested for independent distance reconstruction, operational influence, a quasi-local modular causal cone, one low-energy cone across protected sectors, Lorentz recovery, dimensional stability, geometry–matter response, stable matter sectors, chirality and anomaly cancellation. The Modular Causal Cone Condition remains a downstream phase criterion, not the fundamental law.

      The result presented here is an improved theory architecture and an executable experiment specification, not yet a derivation of nature. The first unearned step after the Stage-II representation is the generic emergence of nontrivial stable effective parts. If MPRC or MSR passes that gate, the next decisive unearned step is the emergence of a robust finite quasi-local cone. AC remains at AC0: an ontological interpretation that changes no earned physical equation. The next result must come from matched computation or theorem, not from another unrestricted layer of prose.

      Plain-language statement#

      1. Reality may be whole-first rather than made from independently existing local objects inside pre-existing space.
      2. “Whole” does not mean physically gigantic. It means that local descriptions are constrained by a more complete actuality.
      3. An undivided Whole cannot be mathematically featureless if it is to support distinctions, transformations or modes.
      4. Quantum structure must be earned in Stage I; operator algebras and Hilbert-space machinery are not allowed to appear at the first line and then be called derived.
      5. Once quantum structure is justified, a useful representation consists of an observable algebra, a state and a relational/spectral operator.
      6. MPRC searches for effective parts by prediction, compression, recovery, stability and presentation invariance—not by asking the search to manufacture known physics.
      7. MSR is a serious matched competitor, not an ingredient silently fused into MPRC.
      8. Spacetime is promoted only if discovered parts independently support distance, finite influence, a common cone, stable clocks and a Lorentzian low-energy regime.
      9. Matter, geometry and physical information may become jointly well-defined as one stable physicalization phase.
      10. AC says that ultimate actuality is not devoid of presence. RC remains conditional on sufficiently organized local relation/resonance; a stone is AC-grounded but is not thereby an embodied conscious centre.

      Part I — Claim discipline and the central reversal#

      0. Claim-status vocabulary#

      Every load-bearing statement should carry one of the following statuses.

      Status Meaning
      Established Standard theorem, formal construction or experimentally supported result in a stated domain
      Reconstruction result Known formalism follows from an explicit axiom package
      Adapted Established mathematics is assigned a new role, with the adaptation declared
      Conjectured New mechanism or bridge proposed in AC–WFP
      Conditional recovery Desired physics follows if listed assumptions and earlier gates pass
      Interpretive AC/RC meaning assigned to neutral mathematics
      Open Required step with no adequate derivation yet
      Rejected / demoted A stronger earlier claim no longer survives the evidence discipline

      No philosophical interpretation repairs a failed neutral model. No famous thinker counts as evidence. No equation counts as progress unless its types, assumptions, inputs, domain, model cost and failure conditions are stated.

      0.1 Scientific and AC ladders#

      The scientific ladder is

      L0  coherent whole-first question
L1  explicit neutral mathematical kernel
L2  executable nontrivial mechanism
L3  stable generic relational structures on held-out systems
L4  controlled recovery of a known physical regime

      The AC ladder is separate:

      AC0  AC is an ontological interpretation of Ω; no changed physics
AC1  AC selects admissible factorizations, centres or phases
AC2  AC changes dynamics, probabilities or physicalization

      A theory may reach high scientific value while remaining at AC0. AC receives no promotion merely because the whole-first picture is compatible with it.

      1. The core proposition#

      The parts-first picture assumes local constituents and attempts to build the universe from them. AC–WFP tests the inverse explanatory order:

      Locality, particles, fields and spacetime may be stable effective structures generated within a globally constrained Whole.

      This is not the claim that large spatial objects create smaller spatial objects. If spacetime is emergent, the Whole is not larger in metres. It is more inclusive and ontologically prior to any particular factorization into local pieces.

      The strongest neutral statement is:

      A physical world is a stable relational regime in which compatible restrictions of Ω acquire predictive closure, recoverable records, causal reach, geometry, energy–momentum and persistent modes.

      The AC interpretation adds:

      Ultimate actuality is not wholly devoid of presence. Physical organization localizes, stabilizes and enriches bounded relative consciousness rather than creating phenomenal presence from absolute non-presence.

      2. Undivided is not featureless#

      In an algebraic Stage-II representation, a trivial algebra cannot generate nontrivial internal structure:

      $$ \mathcal M=\mathbb C\mathbf1 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \operatorname{Der}(\mathcal M)=0, \qquad \operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal M)=\{\mathrm{id}\}, \qquad \operatorname{Proj}(\mathcal M)=\{0,1\}. $$

      Therefore

      $$ \boxed{ \text{undivided Whole} \neq \text{featureless mathematical object}. } $$

      This is a representation-level no-go, not a proof that operator algebra is the final ontology. It says that a generative representation must possess at least constrained latent relational capacity.

      The open foundational question is:

      What is the least structure sufficient for differentiation, while still too weak to encode our observed world by hand?

      5.1 Proto-information and physical information#

      Part II — Stage I and Stage II#

      6. Stage I: whole-first reconstruction of quantum structure#

      AC–WFP may not begin with Hilbert space and then declare quantum mechanics derived. Stage I begins with a causal operational framework containing:

      • states;
      • effects;
      • transformations;
      • composition rules;
      • probabilities;
      • no primitive spacetime geometry.

      The current whole-first principles are:

      W1 — compatible local facts admit a Whole#

      Every jointly admissible family of perspective-indexed local facts belongs to at least one compatibility class.

      W2 — whole-first purification#

      Every mixed local state has a pure completion, essentially unique up to reversible transformations on the complement, under the assumptions of the reconstruction theorem used.

      W3 — no privileged classical distinction#

      Pure alternatives of the same operational type are reversibly connected unless an independently stated conserved structure distinguishes them.

      W4 — causal composition#

      Present outcome probabilities cannot depend on later freely chosen operations.

      W5 — local distinguishability or an explicit replacement#

      Global relational states must be operationally reconstructible from an adequate family of local tests, or the theory must state precisely why a weaker composition rule is used.

      Known operational reconstructions show that quantum theory can follow from suitable informational principles, with purification playing a central role [1,2]. AC–WFP has not yet derived the complete axiom package from one smaller Whole-first postulate.

      Stage-I status: a disciplined reconstruction programme, not a completed derivation of QM.

      7. Stage II: typed quantum-relational presentations#

      These structures are scientifically fertile but already quantum/operator-theoretic. They belong only after the Stage-I firewall.

      8.1 Naturality of physicalization#

      Each presentation carries a candidate map

      $$ F_\alpha:\mathcal X_\alpha\to\mathcal X_\alpha. $$

      For a valid presentation map Uαβ with induced transport Uαβ^X, define

      $$ E_{\rm nat}^{\alpha\beta}(X) = d_\beta\!\left( U_{\alpha\beta}^{X}F_\alpha(X), F_\beta U_{\alpha\beta}^{X}(X) \right). $$

      Exact-equivalence target:

      $$ E_{\rm nat}^{\alpha\beta}=0 $$

      up to numerical error. Approximate maps require a predeclared error budget. Analogy-only maps cannot support a naturality claim.

      Naturality itself can be overfit. The framework therefore requires held-out composition: construct or tune with maps Uab and Ubc, then test the unseen direct/composite relation Uac without adjustment.

      Part III — Physicalization mechanisms#

      9. Primary mechanism: Minimal Predictive–Reconstructive Closure#

      The broad fixed-point architecture requires an explicit, auditable update rule. MPRC is adopted as the primary candidate because it is lean, testable and comparatively low-bias, with explicit coding, naturality and held-out rules.

      9.1 Candidate effective cover#

      Let

      $$ \mathcal P = \{(\mathcal A_i,E_i)\}_{i=1}^{m} $$

      be an overlapping cover of candidate subalgebras 𝓐i⊂𝓜. Ei:𝓜→𝓐i is a conditional expectation, restriction or controlled finite approximation.

      Overlaps are allowed because physical parts need not be disjoint tensor factors. Holographic/code-like locality, gauge constraints and perspective-indexed facts can require overlapping algebras.

      Candidate moves come from a predeclared low-complexity library:

      • add or remove a generator;
      • merge or split candidate algebras;
      • add or remove an overlap;
      • add or remove a declared recovery relation;
      • rotate only within a declared degenerate invariant sector;
      • change a model class only through a coded, auditable move.

      The move library, thresholds and search policy are charged to model description length.

      9.2 Intrinsic relational data#

      MPRC may use only predeclared relational data generated from the Stage-II presentation:

      $$ \mathscr D_{\rm rel}(K_\alpha) = \{ \text{operator correlations}, \text{projected intrinsic evolution}, \text{intervention responses}, \text{restriction/recovery data}, \text{perturbation responses} \}. $$

      It may not use target coordinates, target dimension, a known light cone, Einstein’s equation, Standard Model labels, observer viability, life, AC or RC rewards.

      9.3 Projected intrinsic dynamics#

      For a suitable algebra/state pair, Tomita–Takesaki modular theory supplies the state-relative flow [3]

      $$ \sigma_s^\omega(A) = \Delta_\omega^{is}A\Delta_\omega^{-is}. $$

      In a finite matrix factor with faithful density matrix ρ,

      $$ \sigma_s^\omega(A)=\rho^{is}A\rho^{-is}. $$

      For candidate part i, define projected evolution

      $$ T_{i,s} = E_i\circ\sigma_s^\omega\circ\iota_i, $$

      where ιi embeds 𝓐i into 𝓜.

      A candidate part earns predictive closure when T_{i,s} is represented compactly by a local completely positive unital map Φi,s on held-out operators and modular intervals:

      $$ \epsilon_{\rm pred}(i) = \sum_{s\in S_{\rm test}} \sum_{A\in\mathcal B_i^{\rm test}} \left\| T_{i,s}(A)-\Phi_{i,s}(A) \right\|_\omega^2, \qquad \|X\|_\omega^2=\omega(X^\dagger X). $$

      The modular parameter s is not yet physical time. It is intrinsic relational evolution data.

      Alternative intrinsic dynamics—declared physical channels, automorphism flows or causal-history updates—must be tested as matched challengers rather than silently merged.

      9.4 Reconstructibility#

      For an authorized domain B, a recovery channel \mathcal R_{B\to i} attempts to reconstruct the logical content of 𝓐i:

      $$ \epsilon_{\rm rec}(i\leftarrow B) = \min_{\mathcal R_{B\to i}} \left\| \mathcal R_{B\to i}\circ E_B-E_i \right\|_\omega. $$

      Recovery is claim-relative. Redundant recovery is mandatory for a holographic or distributed-encoding claim, not for every ordinary subsystem.

      The recovery map itself is charged. An arbitrarily powerful optimizer that hides the desired answer inside \mathcal R is model bloat, not explanation.

      9.5 Predictive description length#

      The discovery objective is a two-part, preferably prequential code:

      $$ \boxed{ \mathcal L_{\rm MPRC}(\mathcal P) = L(\mathcal P,\{E_i\},\{\Phi_i\},\{\mathcal R_i\}\mid K_\alpha) + L_{\rm preq}(\mathscr D_{\rm rel}\mid \mathcal P,\{\Phi_i\},\{\mathcal R_i\}). } $$

      The first term encodes the model. The second encodes held-out or sequential residuals. If practical weights are used, they must come from the declared residual/noise code rather than post-hoc preference.

      The full cost includes the kernel and search machinery whenever models with different kernels are compared:

      $$ L_{\rm total} = L(\mathcal M)+L(\omega)+L(D)+L(F) +L(\mathcal P,E,\Phi,\mathcal R) +L(\text{calibration}) +L(\text{residuals}). $$

      9.6 Deterministic update#

      Let 𝒩(𝒫) be the finite neighborhood of allowed coded modifications. Define

      $$ \mathcal Q_{\min} = \operatorname*{argmin}_{\mathcal Q\in\mathcal N(\mathcal P)} \mathcal L_{\rm MPRC}(\mathcal Q), $$

      with a canonical deterministic tie-breaker. Then

      $$ \boxed{ F_{\rm MPRC,\alpha}(\mathcal P) = \begin{cases} \mathcal Q_{\min}, &\mathcal L(\mathcal Q_{\min})<\mathcal L(\mathcal P),\\[4pt] \mathcal P,&\text{otherwise.} \end{cases} } $$

      A local fixed point satisfies

      $$ \boxed{ \mathcal P_\alpha^* = F_{\rm MPRC,\alpha}(\mathcal P_\alpha^*). } $$

      In a finite candidate space with discrete code lengths, strict monotone descent terminates. This earns finite termination only. It does not prove physicality, uniqueness, global optimality or relevance to our universe.

      9.7 Genericity and basin structure#

      One hand-built success is insufficient. Report:

      • fixed-point residual;
      • number and structure of attractors;
      • basin size across randomized initial covers;
      • sensitivity to search order and tie-breaking;
      • held-out generalization across seeds, perturbations and sizes;
      • model cost of the move library;
      • whether trivial full-algebra or singleton covers dominate.

      A physically interesting mechanism should produce nontrivial fixed points in a broad, non-fine-tuned region, not only after a curated initialization.

      9.8 Naturality of MPRC#

      For an exact state/spectrum-preserving isomorphism U, the target is

      $$ \boxed{ F_{UK}(U\mathcal P) = U F_K(\mathcal P) } $$

      up to canonical relabeling. At fixed points,

      $$ E_{\rm fp\text{-}dual}^{\alpha\beta} = d_\beta\! \left(U_{\alpha\beta}^{\mathcal P}(\mathcal P_\alpha^*), \mathcal P_\beta^*\right). $$

      A mechanism that finds different physical parts after a harmless basis change fails immediately.

      10. Matched challenger: Modular–Spectral Recovery#

      The strongest explicit alternative is Modular–Spectral Recovery. MSR is kept separate and compared at matched complexity.

      10.1 Intrinsic MSR channel#

      For faithful ρ and self-adjoint D,

      $$ \sigma_s^\omega(A) =\rho^{is}A\rho^{-is}, $$
      $$ \mathcal G_D(A) =DAD-\tfrac12(D^2A+AD^2) =-\tfrac12[D,[D,A]], $$
      $$ \mathcal N_{\eta,D} =e^{\eta\mathcal G_D}, \qquad \boxed{ \Phi_{\eta,s}^{\omega,D} =\mathcal N_{\eta,D}\circ\sigma_s^\omega. } $$

      σω supplies reversible state-relative flow. Nη,D supplies dephasing or coarse-graining relative to D. Neither contains a target dimension or Einstein tensor, but D may still contain substantial geometric information.

      10.2 Recoverable-algebra objective#

      For candidate 𝓐i, restriction Ei and recovery Ri,

      $$ \epsilon_{\rm MSR}(i;\eta,s) = \sup_{A\in\mathcal A_i,\|A\|\le1} \|R_iE_i\Phi_{\eta,s}^{\omega,D}(A)-A\|. $$

      MSR selects a nontrivial Pareto or MDL set minimizing:

      • recovery error;
      • persistence/leakage error;
      • perturbation sensitivity;
      • full description length of channel, algebra, restriction and recovery.

      For a fair arena, convert this to a comparable coded objective:

      $$ \mathcal L_{\rm MSR} = L(\eta,s,\mathcal A,E,R,D) + L_{\rm preq}(\text{recovery and persistence residuals}). $$

      10.3 Why MPRC and MSR are not merged by default#

      MPRC is a broad discovery principle. MSR is a specific channel hypothesis. A default fusion would risk:

      1. rewarding the same modular structure twice;
      2. hiding physical content already encoded in D;
      3. making failure uninterpretable;
      4. converting a matched challenger into an unablated internal component.

      The arena may include MPRC+MSR as a separate ablation arm, but it is not the baseline.

      10.4 The D-debt#

      Both mechanisms are vulnerable if D encodes the answer. Mandatory controls:

      • preserve the spectrum and randomize eigenvectors;
      • preserve eigenvectors and perturb the spectrum;
      • replace D with matched graph/Laplacian operators;
      • remove D;
      • compare compactly generated D with hand-designed D;
      • charge the generator and representation of D in full.

      A model that reconstructs geometry only because geometry was inserted into D has decoded, not explained.

      11. Effective parts: five core gates plus one conditional gate#

      A mathematical subalgebra is promoted to an effective part only if it passes the gates relevant to the claim.

      Gate 1 — restriction#

      There is a well-defined Ei:𝓜→𝓐i, with assumptions stated.

      Gate 2 — predictive persistence#

      The part supports compact prediction of its projected intrinsic dynamics over held-out intervals and survives a nontrivial perturbation range.

      Gate 3 — recovery or justified autonomy#

      There is an explicit recovery map with a declared domain and error, or a justified local-autonomy criterion when recovery is not part of the claim.

      Gate 4 — robustness and nontriviality#

      The part survives perturbations, coarse-graining and unseen sizes, and beats full-algebra, scalar, singleton, frozen and arbitrary-factor baselines.

      Gate 5 — covariance#

      Under every declared exact-equivalence map, the part and its restriction/prediction/recovery data transport consistently.

      Conditional Gate 6 — redundancy#

      If the claim is holographic or redundantly encoded locality, more than one authorized domain must recover overlapping logical content. This is not imposed on every physical subsystem.

      The residual vector is reported without hiding mandatory failures in a weighted average:

      $$ \mathbf E_{\rm part}(i) = (E_{\rm restrict},E_{\rm predict},E_{\rm recover}, E_{\rm robust},E_{\rm cov};E_{\rm redundant}). $$

      Distance must be reconstructed independently of the commutator-spreading profile used to score a cone.

      Part VIII — Discriminators, roadmap and verdict#

      36. Pre-registered candidate discriminators#

      These are research targets, not claims that nature already satisfies them.

      38. Current verdict#

      GO WITH ONE PRIMARY MECHANISM, ONE MATCHED CHALLENGER, AND EVIDENCE FIRST#

      The framework does not average incompatible ideas. It assigns each surviving component a precise role:

      • representation-resistant Ω kernel: the neutral scientific foundation;
