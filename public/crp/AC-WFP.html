1. The core proposition#

The parts-first picture assumes local constituents and attempts to build the universe from them. AC–WFP tests the inverse explanatory order:

Locality, particles, fields and spacetime may be stable effective structures generated within a globally constrained Whole.

This is not the claim that large spatial objects create smaller spatial objects. If spacetime is emergent, the Whole is not larger in metres. It is more inclusive and ontologically prior to any particular factorization into local pieces.

The strongest neutral statement is:

A physical world is a stable relational regime in which compatible restrictions of Ω acquire predictive closure, recoverable records, causal reach, geometry, energy–momentum and persistent modes.

The AC interpretation adds:

Ultimate actuality is not wholly devoid of presence. Physical organization localizes, stabilizes and enriches bounded relative consciousness rather than creating phenomenal presence from absolute non-presence.

2. Undivided is not featureless#

In an algebraic Stage-II representation, a trivial algebra cannot generate nontrivial internal structure:

$$ \mathcal M=\mathbb C\mathbf1 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \operatorname{Der}(\mathcal M)=0, \qquad \operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal M)=\{\mathrm{id}\}, \qquad \operatorname{Proj}(\mathcal M)=\{0,1\}. $$

Therefore

$$ \boxed{ \text{undivided Whole} \neq \text{featureless mathematical object}. } $$

This is a representation-level no-go, not a proof that operator algebra is the final ontology. It says that a generative representation must possess at least constrained latent relational capacity.

The open foundational question is:

What is the least structure sufficient for differentiation, while still too weak to encode our observed world by hand?

2.1 Global capacity / local compression inversion#

V4-DRAFT.2 retains the apparent reversal between the Whole and its effective parts:

The global actuality must carry enough irreducible relational capacity to permit genuine differentiation, while successful local restrictions must become conditionally compressible under short predictive models.

This is the Global Capacity / Local Compression Inversion (GCLI). It blocks two easy but sterile extremes.

Trivial Whole#

Too little relational capacity: no nontrivial distinctions, modes, automorphisms or effective parts.

Random Whole#

High raw incompressibility but no stable reusable restrictions; apparent structure fails held-out and perturbation tests.

Overwritten Whole#

The desired geometry or partition is encoded directly in D, the search grammar or the oracle. Recovery is decoding, not explanation.

Viable window#

Nontrivial global capacity plus robust local lawfulness after total model and machinery cost.

A finite arena operationalizes only the second half of the inversion. With a domain-specific nontriviality measure 𝒩, it asks for

$$ \mathcal N(\Omega)>0, \qquad \exists\,\mathcal P\notin\{\text{whole},\text{singletons}\}: \Delta L_{\rm test}(\mathcal P)\ge\delta_{\rm abs}, \qquad r_{\rm FP}\le\alpha. $$

𝒩 is not presented as exact Kolmogorov complexity. The deeper question—why Ω has this capacity at all—remains open.

A record becomes internally available when it is:

  • stable over a relevant interval;
  • available to multiple internal processes;
  • linked to a self/world model;
  • counterfactually able to alter prediction, report, planning or control;
  • comparable with later records;
  • causally integrated without requiring an infinite regress of internal observers.

This is a functional criterion for knowledge-like access. It does not by itself derive phenomenal subjectivity.

21.1 Two directions of the AC–RC shorthand#

The framework uses both RC = AC·R and AC = RC×R as two conceptual directions, not arithmetic identities.

Local expression#

$$ \boxed{ RC_i:=\mathcal R_i(AC) } $$

Global reconstruction#

which is the disciplined meaning of AC = RC × R: the Whole is not a sum of isolated minds, but the globally compatible actuality reconstructed through their relations and all other admissible restrictions.

Neither equation is currently a laboratory law.

For a physical centre i, let Ri remain a typed vector rather than a mystical scalar. Candidate components include recurrent integration, memory continuity, self/world modelling, counterfactual access, causal autonomy, global availability, world coupling, perturbation recovery and cross-time identity stability.

A secondary screening score is

$$\boxed{S_i=\sqrt{C_iX_i},}$$

where Ci is coherence and Xi is complexity understood as information diversity. S rejects two simple false positives—noise with low coherence and rigidity with low complexity—but it is not consciousness, not IIT’s Φ, and not yet a validated measure of a centre.

Its relation to existing programmes is explicit:

  • IIT adjacency: AC–WFP shares the interest in irreducible integration, but does not identify a scalar with phenomenal quantity and does not inherit IIT’s axioms by default [24,25].
  • Global Workspace / Global Neuronal Workspace adjacency: knowledge-like closure requires information to become available to multiple specialized functions, but broadcast or ignition is treated as an operational organization property, not automatic proof of phenomenal RC [26,27].

21.3 Centre-level MDL synergy and the expander control#

The formal centre-level analogue of co-emergence is

$$ \Delta_{\rm centre}^{\rm test} = L_{\rm separated}^{\rm test} - L_{\rm integrated}^{\rm test} - \Delta L_{\rm integration\ machinery}. $$

Δcentre asks whether one integrated model predicts memory, access, intervention and recovery data more economically than separately fitted modules. It is the centre-level member of the same MDL family as Δco. S is only a low-cost diagnostic that may help propose candidates for the full test.

The complexity term X does not by itself defeat the expander-graph objection. A random or expander network can be highly connected, diverse and difficult to partition while lacking memory, self-model, endogenous control or persistent identity. It may even receive a larger S. Aaronson’s “unconscious expander” critique therefore becomes an explicit adversarial test rather than a footnote [28].
