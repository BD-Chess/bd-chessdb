Q00 · Ground check

Are black holes real, and what is gravity?

Ali so črne luknje realne in kaj je gravitacija?

physics boundaryfoundation

Physics pressure: before asking whether black holes destroy information, we must ask whether black holes and gravity are understood at all.

Round‑1 answer: Black holes are very likely real astrophysical compact objects. What remains open is the ultimate interior description and whether singularities are physical or model failure. General relativity remains the best local geometric description: gravity behaves as spacetime curvature. CFH/AC does not replace this; it asks whether curvature is an RC-level expression of deeper differentiation and coupling inside AC/AE.

Smallest test: keep GR predictions untouched in ordinary domains; use AC/RC only as an ontology layer above already successful equations.

Boundary: No claim that AC explains measured gravity better than GR.

Fizikalni pritisk: preden vprašamo, ali črne luknje uničijo informacijo, moramo vprašati, ali sploh razumemo črne luknje in gravitacijo.

Odgovor 1. kroga: Črne luknje so zelo verjetno realni astrofizični kompaktni objekti. Odprto ostaja, kaj se res zgodi v notranjosti in ali so singularnosti fizične ali le zlom modela. Splošna relativnost ostaja najboljši lokalni geometrijski opis: gravitacija se obnaša kot ukrivljenost prostora-časa. CFH/AC tega ne nadomešča; vpraša, ali je ukrivljenost RC-nivojski izraz globlje diferenciacije in couplinga znotraj AC/AE.

Najmanjši test: GR napovedi pustimo nedotaknjene v navadnih domenah; AC/RC uporabimo le kot ontološko plast nad že uspešnimi enačbami.

Meja: Ne trdimo, da AC razloži merjeno gravitacijo bolje kot GR.

Q01 · Black-hole information

What is actually lost?

Kaj se dejansko izgubi?

integrated seedreverse prism

Physics pressure: if Hawking radiation is purely thermal, exact information about what fell into a black hole seems lost, conflicting with quantum unitarity.

Round‑1 answer: Information is stable differentiation inside RC/RE. A black hole is not a cosmic delete key; it is a reverse-prism / recompressor. Rich, locally readable RC structure becomes horizon-bound, externally unreadable, MDL-inaccessible code. If black holes do not have an ordinary independent interior, horizon encoding becomes even more natural: the horizon is the compressed interface, not a sticker surface.

Smallest test: finite-state bulk → horizon ledger → radiation; compare deletion, boundary ledger, reversible recompression, and noisy release.

Boundary: No derivation of Hawking correlations, Page curves, or a black-hole S-matrix.

Fizikalni pritisk: če je Hawkingovo sevanje čisto termalno, se zdi, da natančna informacija o tem, kar pade v črno luknjo, izgine, kar se tepe s kvantno unitarnostjo.

Odgovor 1. kroga: Informacija je stabilna diferenciacija znotraj RC/RE. Črna luknja ni kozmična tipka delete; je reverse-prism / rekompresor. Bogata, lokalno berljiva RC struktura postane horizontno vezana, od zunaj neberljiva, MDL-nedostopna koda. Če črne luknje nimajo navadne neodvisne notranjosti, postane horizontno kodiranje še bolj naravno: horizont je stisnjen vmesnik, ne nalepka na površini.

Najmanjši test: finite-state bulk → horizontni ledger → sevanje; primerjamo izbris, boundary ledger, reverzibilno rekompresijo in šumni izpust.

Meja: Ne izpeljemo Hawkingovih korelacij, Page curve ali black-hole S-matrix.

Q02b · Quantum entanglement

Can distant things remain one state?

Ali lahko oddaljene stvari ostanejo eno stanje?

nonseparabilityMDL bridge

Physics pressure: entangled systems can be spacetime-separated while still being described by one joint quantum state. Bell tests pressure simple local-hidden-variable stories, while no-signaling prevents faster-than-light messaging.

Round‑1 answer: Entanglement is RC nonseparability: apparent distance inside spacetime does not always equal full ontological separateness. The correct move is not “particle A sends a signal to B,” but “A and B were never fully independent at the deeper state level.” MDL favors the shorter joint-state description over two forced independent descriptions.

Smallest test: toy systems where one shared latent generator compresses correlations better than independent variables, while forbidding any signal channel.

Boundary: This does not derive Bell inequalities or replace quantum mechanics. It is a conceptual bridge: relation can be deeper than local separation.

Fizikalni pritisk: prepleteni sistemi so lahko ločeni v prostoru-času, a jih še vedno opisuje eno skupno kvantno stanje. Bellovi testi pritiskajo na preproste lokalne skrite spremenljivke, no-signaling pa prepreči hitrejše-od-svetlobe sporočanje.

Odgovor 1. kroga: Prepletenost je RC neločljivost: navidezna razdalja znotraj prostora-časa ni vedno popolna ontološka ločenost. Pravilni premik ni “delec A pošlje signal B,” ampak “A in B na globlji ravni nikoli nista bila popolnoma neodvisna.” MDL daje prednost krajšemu skupnemu opisu pred dvema prisilno ločenima opisoma.

Najmanjši test: toy sistemi, kjer en skupni latentni generator bolje stisne korelacije kot neodvisne spremenljivke, brez dovoljenega signalnega kanala.

Meja: To ne izpelje Bellovih neenačb in ne nadomesti kvantne mehanike. Je konceptualni most: relacija je lahko globlja od lokalne ločenosti.

Q03 · Quantum gravity

Why do micro and macro theories resist marriage?

Zakaj se mikro in makro teorije ne poročijo čisto?

hardestconceptual resolution

Physics pressure: quantum mechanics governs microscopic possibilities; general relativity governs macroscopic spacetime geometry. Directly combining them keeps failing or becoming incomplete.

Round‑1 answer: The conflict may be a category error: physics tries to quantize spacetime as if spacetime were the deepest container. In AC→RC, spacetime is not the absolute stage. It is an early RC differentiation — the stable geometry of here/there, before/after, causal access, and relational distance. Quantum mechanics and GR may be two regimes of the same deeper differentiation process.

Smallest test: toy models where relational graph geometry emerges from lower-level constraints, then compare micro update rules with macro geodesic-like behavior.

Boundary: This is not solved quantum gravity. It is a conceptual reframing of why the conflict exists.

Fizikalni pritisk: kvantna mehanika ureja mikroskopske možnosti; splošna relativnost ureja makroskopsko geometrijo prostora-časa. Neposredno združevanje se stalno lomi ali ostane nepopolno.

Odgovor 1. kroga: Konflikt je lahko kategorijska napaka: fizika poskuša kvantizirati prostor-čas, kot da je ta najgloblji vsebnik. V AC→RC prostor-čas ni absolutni oder. Je zgodnja RC diferenciacija — stabilna geometrija tu/tam, prej/potem, kavzalnega dostopa in relacijske razdalje. Kvantna mehanika in GR sta lahko dva režima istega globljega procesa diferenciacije.

Najmanjši test: toy modeli, kjer relacijska graf-geometrija nastane iz nižjih omejitev, nato primerjamo mikro pravila z makro geodezijskim vedenjem.

Meja: To ni rešena kvantna gravitacija. Je konceptualni preokvir, zakaj konflikt nastane.

Recommended claim

CFH × MDL×DCC is not a replacement for physics. It is a consciousness-and-information framework used as a pressure map: where black holes, quantum reality, gravity, cosmology, and quantum biology strain our current descriptions, we ask whether AC→RC, R-coupling, MDL, and DCC can generate smaller testable bridges.

New theories and observations show pressure, not proof. Massive gravity does not confirm AC. JWST has not proven galaxies older than the universe. Microtubules do not prove quantum consciousness. Deutsch does not prove our future-history branch. The stronger move is precise: map pressure, build tests, update weights.
