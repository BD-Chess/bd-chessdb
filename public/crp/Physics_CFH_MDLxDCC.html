Q00 · Black holes and gravity

Are black holes real, and what is gravity?

foundationhonesty boundary

Physics pressure: Before asking whether black holes destroy information, we first ask whether black holes and gravity are understood at all.

Round‑1 answer: Black holes are very likely real astrophysical compact objects. What remains open is the ultimate interior description and whether singularities are physical or a sign that the model breaks. General relativity remains the best local geometric description: gravity behaves as spacetime curvature. CFH/AC does not replace this; it asks whether spacetime curvature is an RC-level expression of deeper differentiation and coupling inside AC/AE.

Smallest test: Keep GR predictions untouched in ordinary domains; use AC/RC only as an ontology layer above already successful equations.

Boundary: No claim that AC explains measured gravity better than general relativity.

Q01 · Black-hole information

Can information disappear?

framework resolutionphysics not solved

Physics pressure: If Hawking radiation were purely thermal, exact information about what fell into a black hole would seem lost, conflicting with quantum unitarity.

Round‑1 answer: In CFH × MDL×DCC, information is stable differentiation inside RC/RE. A black hole is not treated as a cosmic delete key, but as a reverse-prism / recompressor. Rich, locally readable RC structure becomes horizon-bound, externally unreadable, MDL-inaccessible code. What looks like information loss may be reconstruction-cost explosion, not absolute erasure.

Smallest test: Build a finite-state “bulk → horizon ledger → radiation” toy and compare true deletion, reversible compression, and noisy release by reconstruction error and MDL cost.

Boundary: We do not derive Hawking correlations, Page curves, or a black-hole S-matrix.

Q02 · Holography

Smallest test: In ARC, TSP, Sudoku, and route arenas, ask whether boundary/skeleton descriptors reconstruct or predict full structure better than brute full-state descriptions.

Boundary: No derivation of AdS/CFT or real quantum-gravity holography.

Q03 · Quantum gravity / micro–macro

Why do micro and macro theories resist marriage?

Physics pressure: Quantum mechanics governs microscopic possibilities; general relativity governs macroscopic spacetime geometry. Directly combining them remains hard.

Round‑1 answer: The conflict may be partly categorical. Physics often tries to quantize spacetime as if spacetime were the deepest container. In AC→RC, spacetime is not the absolute stage. It is an early RC differentiation — the stable geometry of here/there, before/after, causal access, and relational distance. Quantum mechanics and GR may be two regimes of the same deeper differentiation process, not two final substances to weld together.

Smallest test: Build toy worlds where relational graph geometry emerges from lower-level constraints, then compare micro update rules with macro geodesic-like behavior.

Boundary: This is not solved quantum gravity. It is a conceptual reframing of why the conflict exists.

Smallest tests we can build

These are not physics experiments. They are framework tests. If they fail, the page gets weaker. If they work, the bridge becomes more credible.

Reverse-Prism Compression Toy

Create a finite-state bulk that evaporates into radiation. Compare deletion, boundary ledger, reversible recompression, and noisy release.

Micro-Holography in ARC/TSP/Sudoku

Test whether boundary/skeleton variables reconstruct or predict full solution structure better than brute full-state descriptors.

Landscape DCC Selector

Generate artificial parameter landscapes and ask whether DCC can find high-S or observer-compatible regimes using compressed viability criteria.

Joy-to-Output Correlation

Tag moments of high subjective joy/resonance and later score whether those moments produced real artifacts, tests, or discoveries.

Najmanjši testi, ki jih lahko zgradimo

To niso fizikalni eksperimenti. To so testi okvira. Če odpovejo, stran oslabi. Če delujejo, most postane bolj kredibilen.

Reverse-Prism Compression Toy

Ustvarimo finite-state bulk, ki izhlapi v sevanje. Primerjamo izbris, boundary ledger, reverzibilno rekompresijo in šumni izpust.

Mikro-holografija v ARC/TSP/Sudoku

Testiramo, ali boundary/skeleton spremenljivke rekonstruirajo ali napovedujejo polno strukturo bolje kot brute full-state opisi.

Landscape DCC Selector

Generiramo umetne parameter-landscape in preverimo, ali DCC najde high-S ali observer-compatible režime z uporabo kompresiranih viability kriterijev.

Joy-to-Output korelacija

Označimo trenutke visokega subjektivnega veselja/resonance in kasneje ocenimo, ali so rodili realne artefakte, teste ali odkritja.

08

Clean public wording

Recommended claim

CFH × MDL×DCC is not a competitor to string theory or holography. It is a consciousness-and-information framework that can be stress-tested against the same deep fault line: whether structure, information, and equations are enough to explain reality, or whether presence itself needs a deeper ontological ground.

Do not say

“We solved black holes,” “CFH proves holography,” “AC replaces physics,” or “MDL×DCC derives string theory.” The stronger move is precise: we are building bridges and tests.

Čista javna formulacija

Priporočena trditev

CFH × MDL×DCC ni konkurent teoriji strun ali holografiji. Je okvir zavesti in informacije, ki ga lahko stresno testiramo ob isti globoki razpoki: ali struktura, informacija in enačbe zadostujejo za razlago realnosti, ali pa prisotnost sama potrebuje globlji ontološki temelj.

Ne reči

“Rešili smo črne luknje,” “CFH dokazuje holografijo,” “AC nadomesti fiziko,” ali “MDL×DCC izpelje teorijo strun.” Močnejši premik je natančen: gradimo mostove in teste.
