Why this page uses $3T+/year

$3T+/year is used here as a directional project-level upside band for the full 60-domain map, not as a fake-precise TAM claim. Once the scope explicitly includes large compute-, simulation-, and infrastructure-heavy areas such as gaming and real-time 3D worlds, immersive video, telecom routing, aerospace and automotive aerodynamics, digital twins, supercomputing governance, and hybrid quantum workflows, $3T+/year is the more fitting narrative band for this page.

What this map is really saying

MDL × DCC should not be framed as “just another AI model.” The deeper claim is that it can act as a search governor, compute allocator, representation selector, and multi-scale bridge across many expensive domains. That is why the map now reaches cleanly from compression and TSP to telecom routing, weather, HPC, robotics, CFD, immersive media, and quantum-adjacent workflows.
