ABSTRACT

DNA_Math uses a Python research stack to compare 1,000-bp genomic windows with synthetic null sequences. In the inspected Detective runs, confirmation uses 160 Markov-2 nulls generated from each window itself. High scores identify within-window repeat structure that this null does not reproduce.

The strongest annotated yeast regions are tandem repeats within FLO1 and FLO9. Comparable Detective results across individual, eight-genome and size-matched inputs do not establish a general amplification trend. The numerical score is specific to its transform, window and estimated null distribution; it is not an extreme-tail probability or a universal false-positive guarantee.

The collection, merge utilities and scanner remain useful research tools. Biological interpretation, generator profiles, degrees-of-freedom hypotheses and cross-domain generalization require separate controls and held-out validation.

1Why This Matters▸

The useful empirical question is whether a specified representation detects sequence organization beyond a specified null model. The inspected examples answer that question for repeats in particular windows; they do not establish a universal law of genomic organization.

  • Detection: localize repeat-rich windows and inspect their sequence and annotation.
  • Comparison: keep the tool, transform, window, locus and null procedure fixed.
  • Validation: separate discovery from independent controls and held-out sequence tests.

The project connects compression research with biological sequence analysis. That connection motivates experiments; it is not itself evidence of a biological mechanism or an origin-of-life explanation.

The yeast regions contain approximately 135-bp tandem-repeat units. Other inspected E. coli peaks include repeat arrays. These annotations support a repeat-detection interpretation; the yeast peaks are not evidence of a special chromosome-boundary mechanism.

What existing controls establish

The audit's repeat controls also produce high scores, while its five Markov-2 synthetic chromosomes produced no threshold hits. This is a limited control set. It does not establish a near-zero false-positive rate for arbitrary genomes, windows, transforms or multiple-testing procedures.

A z-score estimates separation from the sampled null distribution. With 160 null draws it does not justify a numerical probability in an unobserved extreme tail.

Degrees of freedom

The proposed relationship between coding constraints and sequence organization is an unsupported hypothesis in these comparisons. The available results neither establish nor refute it. Testing it requires predefined loci, comparable procedures and controls for repeats and genome composition.

5 Generator Search and Validation Requirements ▸

The scanner explores candidate mathematical generators as descriptions of sequence structure. A match may reflect a repeat, composition, transform artifact or a useful representation. Generator selection and threshold tuning on the same data cannot establish generalization.

  • Compare repeat controls, composition-matched and Markov controls.
  • Keep tuning sequences separate from held-out evaluation sequences.
  • Include all model, parameter and decoder costs in compression comparisons.
  • Account for the number of generators, transforms and windows searched.

The generator suite and its tuning remain exploratory here. No count of “validated generators” follows from the repeat audit.

6 Exploratory Multi-Domain Characterization ▸

The wider suite explores generator agreement, principal components, binary and sonic representations, superpositions and reverse-engineered fingerprints. Those appendices were not independently checked in the source/output audit used for this page.

Profiler fits and fingerprint similarity are exploratory outputs, not validation of a biological mechanism. Their interpretation requires reproducible settings, null controls, sensitivity checks, complete cost accounting and held-out tests. They are not used as evidence for the repeat finding above.

8What Is Confirmed, What Is Open, What Is Speculative▸

Supported by the inspected records

  • Repeat-rich windows differ from the sampled within-window Markov-2 null.
  • The annotated yeast peak regions lie within FLO1/FLO9 tandem repeats.
  • Comparable Detective merge maxima and Synthia counts are available with their procedural limits.

Still open

  • False-positive behavior across larger, independent control sets and multiple-testing corrections.
  • Fixed-locus comparisons with identical transforms and null settings.
  • Generator and profiler generalization on held-out sequences.
  • The proposed degrees-of-freedom relationship and any biological mechanism beyond known repeats.

The records do not establish diagnostic validity, an extreme-tail probability or a universal detection guarantee.

9 The Role of MDL × DCC ▸

MDL can compare complete sequence descriptions, while DCC could allocate search effort among candidate representations. These are methodological proposals to evaluate against fixed-budget baselines.

LayerEvidence required
RepresentationComplete stored-byte cost including model and decoder
Search allocationHeld-out quality/cost comparison against a fixed policy
Biological interpretationAnnotation, repeat controls and independent genomic validation

The current repeat result does not validate every component of the larger MDL × DCC program. Physical, computational and biological claims need their own evidence.

10Next Bridges▸

The immediate research priority is a reproducible repeat benchmark: retain exact source coordinates, use comparable null procedures, preregister thresholds and evaluate held-out genomic and synthetic controls.

Sequence-to-structure and protein-folding ideas remain possible later research directions. A repeat signal in DNA does not establish a folding mechanism or justify a performance claim in another biological domain.

"A genomic signal needs a specified window, null model and biological annotation."
