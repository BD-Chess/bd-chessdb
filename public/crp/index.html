Engineering Concepts

Applied technical concepts and hardware directions

These sit closer to concrete engineering. They belong here because they show the same habit of cross-domain building, but in harder physical systems.

Energy system
DGTE
Double-Gravity Thermoelectric Engine — the flagship hardware concept in this branch. It explores low-grade waste-heat recovery through gravity-assisted thermal cycling, with the main value proposition being access to temperature ranges that ordinary power systems usually cannot exploit economically.

Fluid / pressure concept
HTH
Horizontal Trompe Hybrid — a fluid/pressure architecture that extends old trompe logic into a terrain-aware horizontal system. Its strongest idea is not one isolated component, but the use of deeper flows, natural terrain curvature, and simple pressure mechanics as a practical energy and air-compression path.

MGHB / HTH-WGPB
Marine Gravity Hybrid Breakwater / Horizontal Trompe Hybrid + Wave Gravity Piston Breakwater. This is the coastal-infrastructure branch: a smart breakwater that protects a harbor while trying to harvest part of the wave, hydrostatic, tidal, and pneumatic energy it already absorbs.
