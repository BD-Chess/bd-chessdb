Engineering Concepts

Applied technical concepts and hardware directions

These sit closer to concrete engineering. They belong here because they show the same habit of cross-domain building, but in harder physical systems. The bottom set is intentionally arranged as two rows: DGTE + HTH first, then the hybrid synthesis + the new marine breakwater branch.

Originality by architecture, not components

These devices are not claimed to be original because heat, gravity, pipes, air, water, waves, or generators are new. They are original in the way known elements are arranged into purpose-built architectures, with specific operating niches and commercial positions.

DGTE — Low-grade waste heat architecture

DGTE targets low-temperature waste heat that many competing systems cannot use cheaply or simply enough. The commercial point is not “heat exists”; it is a capsule-based double-gravity DC engine intended for low-grade sources where ORC, Stirling, or TEG solutions can become too expensive, too complex, or too weak.

The architecture is specific: HOT and COLD columns, sealed capsules, two gravity-driven power strokes, direct electromagnetic harvesting, recuperation/regeneration, and a maintenance-light path that avoids turbines and refrigerants.

HTH — Horizontal/adaptive trompe architecture

HTH targets deeper flows, hydrostatic pressure, river/ocean current, and natural terrain geometry instead of requiring a classical waterfall or ideal turbine site. It is designed to read the river or coast: deep pools, gradients, bends, fast shallows, and natural routes become part of the machine.

The originality is the horizontal/adaptive trompe architecture: a terrain-following pipe and channel system that combines flow, depth, entrainment, separation, and compressed-air use under MDL/DCC selection of the cheapest useful path.

DGTE × HTH — Thermal core plus hydraulic-pneumatic support

DGTE × HTH is not just two devices placed beside each other. The intended architecture keeps DGTE as the ordered thermal core while HTH acts as the hydraulic-pneumatic support organ around it.

That support can provide compressed air, pressure smoothing, priming, flow assist, storage, and channel arbitration. The key is not to mix working bodies into chaos, but to couple control layers and let DCC/MDL decide which channel earns its complexity.

MGHB — Energy-positive coastal infrastructure

MGHB turns a breakwater from passive coastal protection into energy-positive infrastructure. The device is not only a wave-energy converter; it is a modular smart breakwater that must protect a harbor first and harvest useful energy second.

The architecture combines guided wave pistons, HTH-style pneumatic buffering, compressed-air smoothing, storm/bypass logic, and modular serviceability. Its commercial position is dual-use: protection plus local electricity, especially where a port or coastal project must already pay to absorb wave energy.

The commercial test is simple: can the architecture harvest value from places where existing systems are too costly, too complex, or too maintenance-heavy?

Inženirski koncepti

Uporabni tehnični koncepti in strojne smeri

Ti so bližje konkretnemu inženiringu. Sem sodijo zato, ker kažejo isto navado prenašanja idej med domenami, vendar v trših fizičnih sistemih. Spodnji sklop je namenoma urejen v dve vrstici: najprej DGTE + HTH, nato hibridna sinteza + nova morska valolomna veja.

Originalnost po arhitekturi, ne po komponentah

Te naprave niso predstavljene kot originalne zato, ker bi bile toplota, gravitacija, cevi, zrak, voda, valovi ali generatorji novi. Originalne so v tem, kako so znani elementi urejeni v namensko zgrajene arhitekture, s konkretnimi operativnimi nišami in komercialnimi položaji.

DGTE — arhitektura za nizkotemperaturno odpadno toploto

DGTE cilja nizkotemperaturno odpadno toploto, ki je številni konkurenčni sistemi ne morejo izkoristiti dovolj poceni ali dovolj preprosto. Komercialno bistvo ni “toplota obstaja”, ampak kapsulni dvojni gravitacijski DC motor za nizkokakovostne vire, kjer so ORC, Stirling ali TEG pogosto predragi, prekompleksni ali prešibki.

Arhitektura je specifična: HOT in COLD koloni, zaprte kapsule, dva gravitacijska delovna hoda, neposreden elektromagnetni zajem, regeneracija/recuperacija in lažje vzdrževanje brez turbin in refrigerantov.

HTH — horizontalna/adaptivna trompe arhitektura

HTH cilja globlje tokove, hidrostatični tlak, rečni/morski tok in naravno geometrijo terena, namesto da zahteva klasičen slap ali idealno turbinsko lokacijo. Zasnovan je tako, da bere reko ali obalo: globoki tolmuni, gradienti, krivine, hitri plitvi deli in naravne trase postanejo del stroja.

Originalnost je horizontalna/adaptivna trompe arhitektura: cevni in kanalski sistem, ki sledi terenu ter združuje tok, globino, vnos zraka, separacijo in uporabo stisnjenega zraka pod MDL/DCC izbiro najcenejše uporabne poti.

DGTE × HTH — termalno jedro plus hidro-pnevmatska podpora

DGTE × HTH ni samo postavitev dveh naprav eno ob drugo. Predvidena arhitektura ohrani DGTE kot urejeno termalno jedro, HTH pa deluje kot hidro-pnevmatski podporni organ okoli njega.

Ta podpora lahko zagotovi stisnjen zrak, glajenje tlaka, priming, pomoč pretoka, shranjevanje in arbitražo kanalov. Ključno je, da se delovna telesa ne zmešajo v kaos, ampak se povežejo kontrolne plasti in DCC/MDL odloči, kateri kanal si zasluži svojo kompleksnost.

MGHB — energetsko pozitivna obalna infrastruktura

MGHB spremeni valolom iz pasivne obalne zaščite v energetsko pozitivno infrastrukturo. Naprava ni samo pretvornik valovne energije; je modularni pametni valolom, ki mora najprej zaščititi pristanišče in šele nato zajeti uporabno energijo.

Arhitektura združuje vodene valovne bate, HTH-slog pnevmatskega bufferja, glajenje s stisnjenim zrakom, storm/bypass logiko in modularno servisiranje. Komercialni položaj je dvojna raba: zaščita plus lokalna elektrika, posebej tam, kjer mora pristanišče ali obalni projekt že tako plačati za absorpcijo valovne energije.

Komercialni test je preprost: ali lahko arhitektura zajame vrednost tam, kjer so obstoječi sistemi predragi, prekompleksni ali pretežki za vzdrževanje?
