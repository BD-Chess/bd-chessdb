Sudoku R6: change the representation,
not just the solver.

The next AI8 Sudoku arena expands the search space from “which solver should run?” to “which representation, specialist, proof language and allocation policy is worth using now?” The aim is not complexity for its own sake. It is to discover representations that make useful structure cheaper to recognize, verify and reuse.

Why R6 exists

Give the governor something qualitatively different to govern.

Earlier Sudoku generations showed an important split: strong classical leaves can still beat a more elaborate combined system, while some DCC portfolio-allocation contrasts remain positive. R6 takes that result constructively. Instead of stacking more meta-control over nearly identical actions, it deliberately creates a richer ecology of representations and specialists.

Proof economy

Discovery only matters if it becomes cheaper next time.

Proof codec

Bits with a decoder

Reference proof bits are meaningful only inside a frozen proof language and codebook. Runtime, library cost and decoder work remain visible.

Atlas

Matcher cost matters

A beautiful macro that is expensive to recognize may compress storage without reducing solving work. R6 measures both.

Symmetry

Canonical schemas

Atlas entries are canonicalized across declared Sudoku symmetries so the library does not fill with equivalent positional copies.

Negative knowledge

Nogoods + Negative Atlas

Store verified forbidden combinations separately from completed “nothing found” scans; both retain exact scope and dependency conditions.

Forge economics

Search for views is not free

Representation/program search, compilation, canonicalization, failed candidates and maintenance are included in the system account.

Marginal value

Who survives distillation?

Measure value with ablation and replacement-aware contribution, not just isolated leaderboard rank or editorial enthusiasm.

Recommended build order

S0 → S8: grow the laboratory without losing the product.

S0

Bind the base and measurement ledger

Preserve checker authority, cost accounting, exposure boundaries and reproducible identities.

S1

Actual proof / choice codec

Encoder, decoder, replay and independent checking before proof bits become a research currency.

S2

Atlas vertical slice + safe constraints

One real macro/matcher path, symmetry contract, nogood representation and negative-cache rules.

S3

Executable mechanisms + anchors

AIS, CubeLift, Geometric Path-MDL, plus DLX and certified SAT/CDCL references.

S4

Representation selection

Compare fixed, structural, bandit, MDL-only, DCC-only and combined control over the same capability menu.

S5

One pattern through the product

Generator → solve → proof → Atlas → Game Review → next practice, end to end.

S6

Broader screening + interaction pairs

Echo, magnetic, gravity, negative-space and retained archive ideas under bounded budgets.

S7

Persistent transfer + marginal contribution

Measure whether expensive discovery leaves cheaper future capability and which specialists truly add portfolio value.

S8

Dependency-closed HTML distillation

Only mechanisms with verified parity, product value and justified size/runtime cost enter the final single-file browser product.

Product destination

The final product is not the arena.

R6 is allowed to be large because its job is to discover, falsify and compare. The endpoint remains deliberately small: one self-contained browser file that can generate unique puzzles, solve, explain, review a player’s actual moves, maintain a local learning profile and choose the next useful exercise.

Player mode

Quiet by default

The grid stays central. Proof and research machinery surface only when the player asks for help or review.

Laboratory mode

Inspectable when needed

Expose proof bits, work units, representations, DCC decisions and replay for research and advanced users.

Evidence posture

Creative ideas become claims only after matched tests.

The v1.3 master synthesis contains verified mathematical checks for parts of PATH6 and related accounting, but it does not claim that R6 already wins as a solver. Editorial scores are historical priorities; after data arrive, empirical cards replace them.

No magical score

Bits, WU, CPU, solved rate, proof coverage and human learning are different quantities. They are not merged post hoc.

Strong baselines

Best classical leaf, restarts, DLX, ordinary adaptive control, contextual bandits and matched shams remain part of the contract.

10% has a denominator

Any “10% better” claim must name the task profile, comparator, quality gate, total account and uncertainty rule beforehand.
