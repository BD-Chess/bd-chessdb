01 / ORIGIN

The whole came before this log.

Celota je bila pred tem dnevnikom.

The research programme is broader than any one formula or September branch. BD's working picture connects consciousness hypotheses (CFH/AC), whole-first physicalization (AC-WFP/RSE/Ω-MSP), the engineering/search kernel 8Z–MDL×DCC and AI8 continuity. These layers are intentionally kept evidence-distinct: engineering success does not prove ontology, and a failed formalization does not erase the seed it was trying to express.

Raziskovalni program je širši od katerekoli posamezne formule ali septembrske veje. BD-jeva delovna slika povezuje hipoteze zavesti (CFH/AC), whole-first fizikalizacijo (AC-WFP/RSE/Ω-MSP), inženirsko/iskalno jedro 8Z–MDL×DCC in kontinuiteto AI8. Te plasti namenoma ostajajo dokazno ločene: inženirski uspeh ne dokazuje ontologije, neuspešna formalizacija pa ne izbriše semena, ki ga je skušala izraziti.

BD's governing request: look at the whole, then the part, then return to the whole. The private source archive preserves BD's own seed wording verbatim; this public page summarizes process without presenting a model's paraphrase as BD's words.

BD-jeva vodilna zahteva: najprej poglej celoto, nato del in se vrni k celoti. Zasebni izvorni arhiv hrani BD-jevo besedilo semen dobesedno; ta javna stran povzema proces, ne da bi modelno parafrazo predstavljala kot BD-jeve besede.

04 / CONTINUITY MEMORY

Compression is allowed. Silent drift is not.

Kompresija je dovoljena. Tihi odmik ni.

Email is the chronological archive. Drive stores full reviews, derivations, tests and source handles. Each substantive reply carries one editable continuity capsule of at most 3,000 words. The capsule is working memory, not evidence.

Email je kronološki arhiv. Drive hrani celotne preglede, izpeljave, teste in izvorne kazalce. Vsak vsebinski odgovor nosi eno uredljivo continuity capsule z največ 3.000 besedami. Kapsula je delovni spomin, ne dokaz.

HIGH

Parent + decisive originals.

MEDIUM

Active frontier, findings, objections, alternatives.

LOW / ARCHIVE

Compact checkpoints + full source history with zoom-back.

At material corrections and after roughly twelve new delivered turns, load-bearing capsule claims are checked against the originals and later corrections. A repeated summary never becomes a new witness merely by repetition.

Ob pomembnih popravkih in po približno dvanajstih novih dostavljenih obratih se odločilne trditve kapsule preverijo proti izvirnikom in poznejšim popravkom. Ponavljan povzetek s ponavljanjem ne postane nov dokaz.

05 / VERSION ARCHAEOLOGY

A history that also states its holes.

Zgodovina, ki pove tudi svoje luknje.

Each actual ToE.html change is archived privately from the committed/read-back release, using a UTC filename. The archive is not physically immutable: integrity comes from stored hashes plus periodic re-hashing. Git commit lineage and sampled backup lineage are tracked separately so a daily backup is never presented as a complete intraday history.

Vsaka dejanska sprememba ToE.html se zasebno arhivira iz commitane/read-back izdaje z UTC imenom. Arhiv ni fizično nespremenljiv: integriteto zagotavljajo shranjeni hashi in periodično ponovno hashiranje. Git commit sled in vzorčena backup sled se vodita ločeno, zato dnevni backup ni predstavljen kot popolna intradnevna zgodovina.
