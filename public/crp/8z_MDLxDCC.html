8z–MDL×DCCSearch better before searching longer.

A research architecture for generating representations and procedures, measuring what they cost to describe and use, allocating compute adaptively, verifying domain outcomes, and turning expensive discoveries into reusable capability.

The central hypothesis: useful structure is not only in the final answer. It can also live in the representation, the search trajectory, the proof, the specialist ecology and the way compute is allocated. The goal is not to force one algorithm to win everywhere — it is to discover which transferable mechanisms create measurable value across different domains.

01 · Architecture

Generate possibilities. Price them. Govern work. Verify reality.

8z–MDL×DCC is best understood as an open architecture rather than a sacred three-module recipe. The current triad gives three different jobs; domain specialists and future mechanisms may be added, replaced or demoted when evidence says they should be.

8z

Expand the language of search

The strongest version of 8z is not “compression everywhere.” It is the deliberate creation and recombination of candidate representations, views, operators and programs.

MDL

Make explanations pay rent

A short label is not a short model if its decoder, library or hidden table is free. MDL is useful only when the encoded object and side information are explicit.

DCC

Intervene only when useful

A controller is valuable only when its signal changes a decision and the changed trajectory repays sensing and control overhead. “Do nothing” must remain an allowed action.

02 · What “cross-domain” means

Universal does not mean #1 on every benchmark.

The research target is a transferable architecture that repeatedly creates useful effects across heterogeneous domains. A specialist may still be best at the final step. A negative result in one domain does not erase a verified result in another — and a wide portfolio does not, by itself, prove one universal kernel.

1 · Artifact

Does it work?

A real codec decodes. A tour is valid. A verifier accepts the right program. A Sudoku proof replays.

2 · Comparative result

Does it beat a named alternative?

Same target, stated budget, explicit metric and honest denominator.

3 · Mechanism

Why did it improve?

Matched controls isolate a sensor, representation, controller, proof language or scheduler.

4 · Transfer

Does the same mechanism travel?

Fresh domains, adapters and baselines test whether value survives outside the original problem.

Research rule

Cross-domain success ≠ universal dominance. The stronger claim is repeated, measurable utility of a transferable architecture, with domain specialists allowed to win and with failures kept visible.

04 · Sudoku as a stress test

A small game became a serious laboratory.

8zSudoku began as a single browser file: generator, multiple solving strategies and process sensing. That small product grew into a Python proof-and-control laboratory, then into R5 AdaptiveProof/Game Review. The intended final direction loops back to one standalone HTML product.

Product-first discovery

HTML → arena

A compact browser artifact exposed useful questions about solving traces, logic, proof, difficulty and human play.

Research expansion

Arena → evidence

Matched controls, root-stuck cohorts, stateful policies, proof checking and protected confirmation separated useful mechanisms from favored explanations.

Distillation

Evidence → better HTML

The final user should not inherit the laboratory. The user should inherit its verified gold: generation, explanation, review, practice and local continuity.

Product thesis

The long-term 8zSudoku goal is not merely “beat every classical solver.” It is a standalone Game Review and learning system that can evaluate an actual move against verifiable alternatives, explain why, track a local skill profile and generate the next useful exercise.

05 · R6 research direction

From selecting procedures to learning reusable capability.

R6 changes the problem the governor receives. Instead of another larger scheduler over nearly the same menu, it introduces qualitatively different representations and specialists — then asks whether expensive discovery can be compiled into cheap future recognition.

PATH6 / GLOBAL9

Digit paths as a testable representation

One digit has 46,656 valid row-order patterns under the formal PATH6 decomposition. A fixed 16-bit index is a compact record, but it cannot rank patterns by itself; useful signal must come from a conditional model, joint structure or partial state.

CubeLift / AIS

Equivalent truth, different work

The 9×9×9 candidate cube and algebraic invariant compilation do not create new Sudoku truth. Their value must come from cheaper recognition, stronger bounded propagation or a useful specialist regime.

R6 claim boundary

R6 is a research architecture, not a solved benchmark. Its strongest current contribution is a sharper experimental question: can a system turn expensive verified reasoning into a reusable detector that saves work on fresh problems?

06 · Proof Atlas

Memory should store capability, not just history.

A short proof is useful. A reusable detector can be more useful. Proof Atlas treats learned knowledge as an executable object with a matcher and a cost model, not merely a remembered label or solved instance.

Schema

What was discovered?

Premise pattern, representation, effect program, proof language and scope.

Detector

Can we recognize it cheaply?

Matcher cost, false positives, false negatives and dependencies decide whether reuse is practical.

Economics

Did reuse repay discovery?

Library bits, proof bits, discovery work, lookup work, verification and future work saved remain separate accounts.

AI8 bridge

This is a concrete route from “remember what happened” to “retain a verified skill.” The same principle can later connect 8zCoding and 8zReasoning: difficult discoveries should leave behind versioned, testable, reusable operators rather than only prose summaries.

07 · AI8

A candidate first research instrument for many domains.

A reusable architecture may be most valuable at the beginning of an unfamiliar problem: create several views, establish a verifier, run strong controls, find a first qualified signal, preserve what was learned, then hand the task to the best specialist if that is the right answer.

Continuity

State survives sessions

Project goals, artifacts, claims, evidence, tests, open tensions and next actions live outside any single model call.

MAL / RHP

Generate better possibilities

Multiple reasoning and design perspectives widen the candidate space before executable tests decide.

8zCoding

Turn proposals into evidence

Build, run, test, benchmark and repair code under deterministic oracles when possible.

new domain → first valid run → first qualified signal → verified decision → reusable skill → specialist handoff or continued search
A measurable hypothesis

The AI8 claim is not “our framework is magically fastest everywhere.” The experiment is whether a reusable instrument lowers the total cost and time from a new question to a useful, verified research decision compared with strong alternative workflows.

08 · Research principles

Open architecture. Hard verification.

Rank, don't eliminate

Value depends on representation and regime

A method that is weak today can become a specialist when a new representation or scale reveals its niche. Preserve defeat reasons, future tests and resurrection conditions.

No brand privilege

Names do not earn compute

If a contextual bandit, classical leaf or simple scheduler wins under equal information and cost, it should be used. MDL, DCC and ss layers remain hypotheses that must repay their overhead.

Verifier outside the governor

Truth is not a control variable

A controller proposes work. A domain checker decides whether the result is valid. The governor cannot redefine correctness to make its own policy look better.

Less describes more

Distill after discovery

Large laboratories are allowed to be complex. The final product should carry only the mechanisms that earned their cost and survived verification.
