Three jobs.
Not three locked boxes.Tri naloge.
Ne trije zaprti predali.

8z creates alternatives. MDL evaluates description economics. DCC governs a trajectory of work. Specialists, checkers and memory close the loop; components may be added, replaced or omitted where justified.8z ustvarja alternative. MDL presoja ekonomiko opisa. DCC upravlja potek dela. Is this one algorithm or an architecture?Je to en algoritem ali arhitektura?

An architecture with concrete algorithm families. Some projects reuse code or state-update laws. Others share a feedback contract or research organization. The transfer table says which, instead of treating all similarity as identical code.Arhitektura s konkretnimi družinami algoritmov. Nekateri projekti ponovno uporabljajo kodo ali zakone posodobitve stanja. Drugi delijo povratni kontrakt ali raziskovalno organizacijo. Tabela prenosa pove, kaj velja, namesto da bi vso podobnost obravnavala kot identično kodo.

Must all three named components be present everywhere?Morajo biti povsod vse tri poimenovane komponente?

No. The framework is open to additions, replacements and simpler specialists. A partial implementation is labeled partial. Finding that a component is unnecessary in one task is useful design knowledge, not a reason to rename every successful alternative as MDL×DCC.Ne. Okvir je odprt za dodatke, zamenjave in preprostejše specialiste. Delno izvedbo označimo kot delno. Ugotovitev, da je komponenta pri neki nalogi odveč, je uporabno projektno znanje, ne razlog za preimenovanje vsake uspešne alternative v MDL×DCC.

What is distinctive, and what comes from prior work?Kaj je značilno za projekt in kaj izhaja iz predhodnih del?

MDL, algorithm portfolios, adaptive selection and reusable libraries have substantial prior work. The project explores their particular recurrent, multi-level integration across representations, process signals and external verification. The novelty question belongs at the level of specified mechanisms and combinations; this page does not certify a “first in history” claim.MDL, portfelji algoritmov, prilagodljiva izbira in ponovno uporabne knjižnice imajo obsežno predzgodovino. Projekt raziskuje njihovo konkretno povratno, večnivojsko povezavo skozi predstavitve, procesne signale in zunanje preverjanje. Novost se presoja pri določenih mehanizmih in kombinacijah; ta stran ne potrjuje trditve »prvi v zgodovini«. Zapis predhodnih del ↗

Does a matching controller prove a performance gain?Ali ujemajoči se krmilnik dokazuje boljšo zmogljivost?

No. Conformance shows what transferred. A comparative experiment shows whether it helped: same candidate/operator access, comparable tuning, common budgets and all relevant overhead. Both matter, and neither should be substituted for the other.Ne. Skladnost pokaže, kaj se je preneslo. Primerjalni poskus pokaže, ali je pomagalo: isti dostop do kandidatov/operatorjev, primerljivo nastavljanje, skupni proračuni in vsi pomembni dodatni stroški. Oboje šteje in eno ne sme nadomestiti drugega.

What would strengthen the next cross-domain result?Kaj bi okrepilo naslednji meddomenski rezultat?

Keep the shared part explicit, separate adapters, then compare it with strong simple alternatives on held-out tasks. Measure total useful work, not only controller activity. A modest repeatable system-level gain can matter; no component needs to win everywhere.Ohrani izrecni skupni del, loči adapterje in ga primerjaj z močnimi preprostimi alternativami na ločenih nalogah. Meri celotno koristno delo, ne le aktivnosti krmilnika. Tudi zmeren ponovljiv sistemski napredek je lahko pomemben; nobeni komponenti ni treba zmagati povsod.

Does a short description prove truth, intelligence or consciousness?Ali kratek opis dokazuje resnico, inteligenco ali zavest?

No. A description is judged within a specified language and task. Domain correctness, generalization and claims about experience need their own criteria. A controller can guide a search without being authorized to redefine what counts as a valid answer.Ne. Opis ocenjujemo znotraj določenega jezika in naloge. Domenska pravilnost, posploševanje in trditve o izkustvu potrebujejo lastna merila. Krmilnik lahko vodi iskanje, nima pa pravice na novo določiti, kaj šteje kot pravilen odgovor.

What does 8z mean?Kaj pomeni 8z?

The name began with the compression idea: keep strong conventional codecs, add mathematical or structural descriptions where their full cost wins. It is not an official successor to 7-Zip. The wider architecture extends that candidate-forge role to views, operators, programs and hypotheses.Ime izvira iz kompresijske ideje: ohrani močne klasične kodeke in dodaj matematične ali strukturne opise tam, kjer zmaga njihov celotni strošek. Ne gre za uradnega naslednika 7-Zip. Širša arhitektura to vlogo ustvarjanja kandidatov prenese na poglede, operatorje, programe in hipoteze.

What does DCC stand for here?Kaj tukaj pomeni DCC?

Dynamic Complexity Controller. Some source generations use Dynamic Compression Controller for a compression-signal implementation. “Digital Claustrum Controller” is reserved for a future, separately specified and tested architecture—not a description of current biological or conscious capability.Dynamic Complexity Controller. Nekatere izvorne generacije uporabljajo Dynamic Compression Controller za izvedbo s kompresijskim signalom. »Digital Claustrum Controller« je rezerviran za prihodnjo, ločeno opredeljeno in testirano arhitekturo, ne opis sedanjih bioloških ali zavestnih sposobnosti.
