How it worksKako deluje

+

The proposed mechanismPredlagani mehanizem

Heating must increase displaced capsule volume relative to bath density until upward buoyancy exceeds weight and all drag/load forces. Cooling must reverse that condition. The two transfers close a cycle at approximately the same two elevations.Segrevanje mora povečati izpodrinjeni volumen kapsule glede na gostoto kopeli, dokler vzgon ne preseže teže ter vseh uporov in obremenitev. Hlajenje mora pogoj obrniti. Dva prehoda skleneta cikel na približno istih dveh višinah.

The co-axial baseline uses a central HOT riser, an annular COLD return and a full-height heat-exchange wall. The wall couples counter-current fluid streams without routing capsules through a separate regenerator. External source/sink loops and heat exchangers are necessary interfaces.Koaksialna osnova uporablja osrednji topli dvižni kanal, hladni povratni obroč in izmenjevalno steno po celotni višini. Ta poveže protitočna tokova tekočine, kapsule pa ne potujejo skozi ločen regenerator. Zunanja kroga vira in ponora ter izmenjevalniki ostajajo nujni vmesniki.

A measured temperature profile must show that recovery preserves the required buoyancy reversals rather than short-circuiting the driving gradient. Pickup placement, magnetic shielding and eddy-current losses in the co-axial geometry also remain engineering questions.Izmerjeni temperaturni profil mora pokazati, da vračanje toplote ohrani potrebna obrata vzgona in ne izniči pogonske razlike. Položaji tuljav, magnetno zaslanjanje in vrtinčne izgube v koaksialni geometriji prav tako ostajajo inženirska vprašanja.

Forces and loaded motionSile in gibanje pod obremenitvijo

Fdrag ≈ ½ CD ρbath A v²;   Fdrive = Fdrag + Fgenerator + Fother   (steady stroke)

Free-fall or unloaded terminal speed cannot be combined with arbitrary generator power. Taking electricity adds opposing electromagnetic force and changes the speed. The stroke work and generator output must be solved or measured together.Hitrosti prostega oziroma neobremenjenega gibanja ne smemo kombinirati s poljubno močjo generatorja. Električni odvzem doda nasprotno elektromagnetno silo in spremeni hitrost. Delo giba in izhod generatorja je treba računati ali meriti skupaj.

The more serious issue: work per capsulePomembnejše vprašanje: delo posamezne kapsule

Wstrokes,ideal = g H (ρh Vh − ρc Vc);   ρh = ρc: W = ρ g H ΔV

This is the pre-loss translation budget for a fixed-mass capsule with constant states on each leg in stationary baths. Capsule gravitational potential returns to its starting value; two strokes do not provide 2mgH as net work. With varying states, use g∫[ρup(z)Vup(z) − ρdown(z)Vdown(z)]dz and close the pressure, heat and fluid-circulation balance.To je razpoložljivo delo premikanja pred izgubami za kapsulo s stalno maso in stalnima stanjema na obeh poteh v mirujočih kopelih. Gravitacijska potencialna energija kapsule se vrne na izhodišče; dva giba ne dasta 2mgH neto dela. Pri spremenljivih stanjih uporabi g∫[ρgor(z)Vgor(z) − ρdol(z)Vdol(z)]dz ter skleni tlačno, toplotno in pretočno bilanco.

A generous illustration: 1 L cold capsule volume, 12.5% external expansion, ρ = 850 kg/m³ and H = 3 m give only 3.13 J/cycle before losses. An illustrative 0.8 kg RT82 fill stores about 136 kJ over its stated 70–85 °C interval: work/heat is about 0.0023% before recovery. These nominal material values are not a measured capsule equation of state, and RT82 is not selected for a 110 °C system.Velikodušen primer: 1 L hladnega volumna kapsule, 12,5-odstotno zunanje raztezanje, ρ = 850 kg/m³ in H = 3 m dajo le 3,13 J/cikel pred izgubami. Ponazoritvena polnitev 0,8 kg RT82 shrani okoli 136 kJ v navedenem območju 70–85 °C: razmerje delo/toplota je okoli 0,0023 % pred vračanjem toplote. Nazivni podatki materiala niso izmerjena enačba stanja kapsule; RT82 tudi ni izbran za sistem pri 110 °C.

Using the early maximum capsule dimensions (40 × 120 mm), 40 capsules and 0.4 m/s on both legs, the same generous 12.5% expansion scenario gives about 1.26 W total ideal mechanical power, with zero dwell, transfer time, drag or generator losses. The historical Prototype C estimate of 200–600 W electrical is not supported by this capsule-stroke scenario. This is not a universal bound on gas/vapour engines or a separately driven fluid cycle.Pri zgodnjih največjih merah kapsule (40 × 120 mm), 40 kapsulah in 0,4 m/s na obeh poteh isti velikodušni scenarij 12,5-odstotnega raztezanja da okoli 1,26 W skupne idealne mehanske moči, brez čakanja, časa prehodov, upora ali generatorskih izgub. Zgodovinske ocene prototipa C 200–600 W električno ta scenarij kapsulnih gibov ne podpira. To ni splošna meja za plinske/parne motorje ali ločeno gnani tekočinski cikel.

Even hypothetical recovery of 90% of all required capsule heat raises the 1 L example only to about 0.023% before losses. Reaching 4% arithmetically would require about 99.94% recovery while retaining the same work; no such thermodynamically feasible recovery is established. Wall-HX effectiveness is not whole-cycle regeneration, especially for latent heat.Tudi hipotetično vračanje 90 % vse potrebne toplote kapsule bi primer z 1 L dvignilo le na okoli 0,023 % pred izgubami. Za aritmetični doseg 4 % bi bilo ob istem delu potrebno okoli 99,94-odstotno vračanje; taka termodinamsko izvedljiva regeneracija ni dokazana. Učinkovitost stenskega izmenjevalnika ni regeneracija celotnega cikla, zlasti latentne toplote.

What the arena does not closeČesa arena ne sklene

In the v0.6b force/throughput core, hot and cold density differences are sampled independently and applied to one fixed geometric capsule volume. A common capsule mass, external V(T,p), PCM enthalpy and coupled loaded speed are not derived together. Taking min(thermal power, stroke power) does not repair incompatible states. The engineering-pass count is a model-filter result, not evidence that these capsules can exist.V jedru sil in pretoka v0.6b se topla in hladna gostotna razlika vzorčita neodvisno in uporabita pri enem stalnem geometrijskem volumnu kapsule. Skupna masa kapsule, zunanji V(T,p), entalpija PCM in hitrost pod obremenitvijo niso izpeljani skupaj. Minimum toplotne in gibne moči ne popravi nezdružljivih stanj. Število prehodov inženirskega filtra je rezultat modela, ne dokaz, da take kapsule lahko obstajajo.

Heat recovery: the corrected NTU relationVračanje toplote: popravljena povezava NTU

ε = [1 − e−NTU(1−Cᵣ)] / [1 − Cᵣ e−NTU(1−Cᵣ)];   Cᵣ → 1: ε = NTU / (1 + NTU)

At Cᵣ = 1, NTU = 2.3 gives ε ≈ 0.697; ε = 0.90 requires NTU = 9. The newer paper addendum corrects the earlier “NTU ≥ 2.3 ⇒ ε ≈ 0.90” shortcut. Fouling, pressure drop and actual temperature profiles still need measurement.Pri Cᵣ = 1 da NTU = 2,3 vrednost ε ≈ 0,697; za ε = 0,90 je potreben NTU = 9. Novejši dodatek popravi zgodnjo bližnjico »NTU ≥ 2,3 ⇒ ε ≈ 0,90«. Obloge, tlačni padec in dejanski temperaturni profili še zahtevajo meritve.

Energy closureEnergijska bilanca

Pnet = PDC,gross − Paux;   ηnet = Pnet / Q̇in;   ηC = 1 − Tc/Th   (kelvin)

At 110/25 °C, the Carnot ceiling is 22.18%; it is not a prediction for DGTE. The archived α·ηC·fHX·φ·ηgen·χ model is a scenario model with assumed factors. Recomputing its displayed values (0.30, 0.96, 0.86, 0.85, χ = 1, parasitics 0.25 percentage points) gives 4.67% gross and 4.42% net, not proof of either value.Pri 110/25 °C je Carnotov strop 22,18 %; to ni napoved za DGTE. Arhivirani model α·ηC·fHX·φ·ηgen·χ je scenarijski model s privzetimi faktorji. Ponovni izračun prikazanih vrednosti (0,30; 0,96; 0,86; 0,85; χ = 1; odbitek lastne rabe 0,25 odstotne točke) da 4,67 % bruto in 4,42 % neto; nobena vrednost s tem še ni potrjena.

For a simple non-thermal two-fluid loop, apparent stroke work and reversible pressure-displacement work both scale as ΔρgHV. A better door or lock can reduce losses; it cannot supply the missing energy. A complete thermal cycle must restore the capsule and baths and include all boundary work.Pri preprosti netoplotni zanki dveh tekočin sta navidezno delo giba in reverzibilno tlačno-izpodrivno delo oba reda ΔρgHV. Boljša vrata ali zapora zmanjšajo izgube; ne dovajajo manjkajoče energije. Popoln toplotni cikel mora obnoviti stanje kapsule in kopeli ter vključiti vse delo na meji.

IDLE → PREHEAT / PRIME → RAMP → RUN ↔ DE-RATE → STOP;   fault → LATCH → controlled recovery

Spacing control, flow control and a thermal-recovery guard should expose their own electrical costs. Distinguish capsule spacing time from stroke time; distinguish coil occupancy from productive cycle-time fraction.Krmiljenje razmikov, pretoka in toplotnega vračanja mora razkriti tudi lastno električno porabo.
