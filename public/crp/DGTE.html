Gravity does all the work — twice. Hot capsules become lighter than the surrounding fluid: gravity pushes the fluid down, the capsule rises. Cold capsules become heavier: gravity pulls them down directly. One force, two strokes, continuous DC. A co-axial recuperator couples the columns with target effectiveness ε ≥ 0.90. ABSTRACT

DGTE is a double-gravity heat engine. The name says what it does: gravity acts twice per cycle. When a sealed capsule is heated, its density drops below the surrounding fluid — gravity pulls the fluid down, displacing the capsule upward. When the capsule cools, its density rises above the fluid — gravity pulls the capsule down directly. Same force, two directions, two power strokes. Segmented linear coils harvest electromagnetic energy from both strokes — no turbines, no pistons, no refrigerants. A full-height annular counter-current recuperator couples the HOT and COLD columns with target effectiveness ε ≥ 0.90, recovering heat that would otherwise be lost.

Two crossover variants are formalised: V1 (rotary airlocks, baseline — proven mechanics, 9/10 confidence) and V2 (magnetic coupling, R&D — higher long-term upside, 5/10 confidence). The architecture accepts both as swappable cartridges. Pilot acceptance targets: net efficiency η = 4–8% at 90–120/20–30 °C, duty factor φ ≥ 0.85, availability ≥ 99% over 30 days, parasitics ≤ 100W, zero crossover-attributable jams.

Best-fit sources: industrial waste heat (compressor oil coolers, CIP/pasteurisation), low-enthalpy geothermal, solar-thermal, district heat. FOAK economics: €40–80k/kWₑ with 12–18 year payback at €0.20/kWh; volume targets €10–15k/kWₑ with 3–5 year payback at high tariffs. The system trades peak thermodynamic efficiency for simplicity, safety, availability, and direct DC output.

Conceived, designed, and authored by Bojan Dobrečevič with AI teamwork (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek). The recuperation architecture was seeded by a human insight — "capsules don't traverse the exchanger; fluid does" — and elaborated through multi-LLM iteration into the co-axial annular design adopted as baseline.

Gravitacija opravi vse delo — dvakrat. Tople kapsule postanejo lažje od okoliške tekočine: gravitacija potisne tekočino navzdol, kapsula se dvigne. Hladne kapsule postanejo težje: gravitacija jih neposredno vleče navzdol. Ena sila, dva hoda, neprekinjen enosmerni tok. Koaksialni rekuperator povezuje stolpca s ciljno učinkovitostjo ε ≥ 0.90. Brez turbostrojev. Brez hladiv. Brez gibljivih tesnil v toplotni poti.

Povzetek

DGTE je toplotni motor z dvojnim delovanjem gravitacije. Ime pove, kaj počne: gravitacija deluje dvakrat na cikel. Ko se zaprta kapsula segreje, njena gostota pade pod gostoto okoliške tekočine — gravitacija vleče tekočino navzdol in izpodrine kapsulo navzgor. Ko se kapsula ohladi, njena gostota naraste nad gostoto tekočine — gravitacija jo neposredno potegne navzdol. Ista sila, dve smeri, dva delovna hoda. Segmentirane linearne tuljave zajemajo elektromagnetno energijo pri obeh hodih — brez turbin, batov in hladiv. Obročasti protitočni rekuperator po celotni višini povezuje TOPLI in HLADNI stolpec s ciljno učinkovitostjo ε ≥ 0.90 ter vrača toploto, ki bi se sicer izgubila.

Formalizirani sta dve izvedbi prehodov: V1 (rotacijske zapore, osnovna izvedba — preizkušena mehanika, zaupanje 9/10) in V2 (magnetna sklopka, raziskovalna smer — večji dolgoročni potencial, zaupanje 5/10). Arhitektura sprejme obe kot zamenljiva vložka. Pilotni cilji za sprejem: neto izkoristek η = 4–8% pri 90–120/20–30 °C, faktor delovanja φ ≥ 0.85, razpoložljivost ≥ 99% v 30 dneh, lastna poraba ≤ 100W, nič zastojev zaradi prehodov.

Najprimernejši viri: industrijska odpadna toplota (hladilniki olja kompresorjev, CIP/pasterizacija), nizkoentalpijska geotermija, sončna toplota in daljinsko ogrevanje. Ekonomika prve izvedbe FOAK: €40–80k/kWₑ z vračilno dobo 12–18 let pri €0.20/kWh; cilji serijske izdelave €10–15k/kWₑ z vračilno dobo 3–5 let pri visokih tarifah. Sistem zamenjuje najvišji termodinamični izkoristek za preprostost, varnost, razpoložljivost in neposredni enosmerni izhod.

Zamisel, zasnovo in avtorstvo je prispeval Bojan Dobrečevič ob skupinskem delu z AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek). How It WorksKako deluje

+

DGTE exploits gravity twice. A capsule heated in the HOT column becomes less dense than the bath fluid — the fluid sinks under gravity, and the capsule is displaced upward. At the top, it crosses to the COLD column, cools, becomes denser than the fluid — and gravity pulls it straight down. Both motions pass through segmented coils that generate electricity. Two strokes, one force. Gravity is both the piston and the return spring.

The 12-Step Capsule Loop

  1. Preheat loops to setpoints; baseline ε
  2. Prime and degas; verify Δp
  3. Start crossovers — bottom airlock feeds HOT column
  4. HOT ascent → power generation (stroke 1)
  5. HOT→COLD crossover at top
  6. COLD descent → power generation (stroke 2)
  7. COLD→HOT crossover at bottom
  8. Spacing control via back-EMF timing
  9. Throughput ramp to achieve φ_duty ≥ 0.85
  10. Steady run — hold C_r ∈ [0.6, 1.0]
  11. De-rate on ε/Δp drift
  12. Orderly stop — cool; log ε, Δp

Co-Axial Vessel Architecture

Central HOT riser and annular COLD downcomer separated by a full-height annular recuperator wall. Crossovers at top and bottom complete the capsule loop. The recuperator is not a separate unit — it IS the wall between the columns, transferring heat continuously via counter-current flow.

Key Physics

F_net = (ρ_bath − ρ_capsule) · V · g → gravity on the density difference drives both strokes
F_drag = ½ · C_D · ρ_bath · A · v² → design v ≤ 0.4 m/s in coil zones
ε_recup = [1 − exp(−NTU(1−C_r))] / [1 − C_r · exp(−NTU(1−C_r))]
P_net = η_gen · P_gross − P_parasitic → direct DC output

DGTE izkorišča gravitacijo dvakrat. Kapsula, segreta v TOPLEM stolpcu, postane manj gosta od tekočine kopeli — tekočina zaradi gravitacije tone, kapsulo pa izpodrine navzgor. Na vrhu preide v HLADNI stolpec, se ohladi in postane gostejša od tekočine — gravitacija jo potegne naravnost navzdol. Obe gibanji potekata skozi segmentirane tuljave, ki proizvajajo elektriko. Dva hoda, ena sila. Gravitacija je hkrati bat in povratna vzmet.

Pot kapsule v 12 korakih

  1. Predgrej kroga na nastavljeni temperaturi; določi izhodiščni ε
  2. Napolni in razplini; preveri Δp
  3. Zaženi prehode — spodnja zapora podaja kapsule v TOPLI stolpec
  4. TOPLI dvig → proizvodnja elektrike (hod 1)
  5. Prehod TOPLO→HLADNO na vrhu
  6. HLADNI spust → proizvodnja elektrike (hod 2)
  7. Prehod HLADNO→TOPLO na dnu
  8. Krmiljenje razmika s časovnim odčitavanjem protielektromotorne napetosti
  9. Povečevanje pretoka do φ_duty ≥ 0.85
  10. Ustaljeno delovanje — vzdržuj C_r ∈ [0.6, 1.0]
  11. Zmanjšaj obremenitev ob odmiku ε/Δp
  12. Urejena zaustavitev — ohladi; zabeleži ε, Δp

Koaksialna arhitektura posode

Osrednji TOPLI dvižni kanal in obročasti HLADNI povratni kanal ločuje obročasta rekuperacijska stena po celotni višini. Prehoda na vrhu in dnu skleneta kapsulno zanko. Rekuperator ni ločena enota — JE stena med stolpcema, ki s protitokom neprekinjeno prenaša toploto.

Thermodynamic Model

Ideal ceiling: η_C = 1 − T_c/T_h. Net efficiency accounts for recuperator effectiveness, duty factor, generation efficiency, geometry, and parasitics:

η_net ≈ (α · η_C) · f_hx(ε, C_r) · φ_duty · η_gen · χ − P_par/Q̇_in

Where α ≈ 0.30 captures non-HX irreversibilities, η_gen ≈ 0.8–0.9, P_par/Q̇_in ≈ 0.2–0.35%.

Safety & RegulatoryVarnost in predpisi

+

Governing Standards

Pressure: ASME BPVC VIII / EN 13445. Piping: B31.3 / EN 13480. Relief: API 520/521. Machinery: ISO 12100, EN 60204-1. Electrical: IEC 60204-1, IEC 61439. EMC: IEC 61000. Functional safety: IEC 61508/61511 or ISO 13849.

Key Safeguards

  • Overpressure: PSV(s) sized to controlling scenario + optional rupture disk. Hydrotest ≥ 1.3× MAWP.
  • Hardwired STO/E-stop to crossovers, coil stage, pump VFD
  • Trips: vessel over-pressure, Δp spike >2×, bath T out-of-window, DC bus OVP, door open, rotor stall >1s
  • Soft derates: ε drop >5 %-pt, φ < 0.80, coil T high, EMI mask violation
  • CIP: isolate → drain → flush → recirculate → neutralise → rinse → sample → release
