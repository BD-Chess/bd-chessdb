Strengths & weaknesses

What is genuinely strong

  • An original, falsifiable core hypothesis — MDL as quality sensor, DCC as governance.
  • Cross-domain transfer demonstrated across many domains — including physical engineering, not just software.
  • Epistemic discipline above field norm — claim ladders, named gates, failure ledgers, confidence maps.
  • AIM³ — a real methodological contribution to human–AI work.
  • Premium, distinctive, consistent design; reading aids; bilingual EN/SL.
  • ~260 substantive pages, real demos, real Python/Rust — in 18 months.

What is holding it back

  • Near-zero discoverability — zero indexed pages. The ceiling.
  • No external validation — no peer review, citations, or independent reproduction; AI reviews are self-commissioned.
  • Everything self-reported; some code locked.
  • Heavy internal duplication (same page in three folders) + archive/variant sprawl + exposed private pages.
  • The boldest framing taxes the honest work — $3T/yr and "100% win-rate / +448%" read as crank-signal before a skeptic reaches the genomics.
  • Creative/misc pages in the main map cost credibility on first contact. (The physical inventions are not this — they're core.)
