What this page is

A fan-made, cross-universe ranking of high-end comic entities, grouped into broad power tiers. Tiers are coarse (big buckets); ordering within a tier is a best-effort estimate, not a claim of canon truth.

  • #Rank is the global position in the list.
  • Tier is the bucket (SSS → D). Unknown forms, amps, and “in-domain” states are noted explicitly.
  • Universe is the source continuity label used in this list (DC / Marvel / Image / Vertigo, etc.).

What the figures mean

Tier %
Tier score (within-tier)
A relative dominance indicator vs. characters in the same tier bucket (higher = stronger within that tier).
Overall %
Overall score (global)
A relative dominance indicator vs. all entities on the page (higher = stronger overall).
[STRAT]
Strategic / prep-tag (optional)
Use when a character “punches above their weight” via planning, artifacts, timelines, or narrative leverage.

#1 The One-Above-All Marvel
Supreme Being

Marvel’s ultimate “author-level” creator figure. In most depictions it stands outside the entire Marvel cosmology: multiverse, abstracts, time, and even the rules that bind beings like Eternity or the Living Tribunal. It rarely acts directly; when it does, it’s usually through quiet interventions, avatars, or moments of moral/creative revelation. For crossover storytelling, TOAA works best as the unreachable boundary condition: it can’t be “beaten,” only reflected, interpreted, or invoked as a final constraint on what reality is allowed to become.

#6 The Great Darkness DC
