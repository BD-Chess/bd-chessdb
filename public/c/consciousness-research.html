The research ladderRaziskovalna lestev

R1 · TESTIMONY

Soul Voyage

What BD reports experiencing. Preserved verbatim. The occurrence and structure of the report are data; its external interpretation is open.Kaj BD poroča, da je doživel. Ohranjeno dobesedno. Obstoj in struktura poročila sta podatek; zunanja interpretacija ostaja odprta.

R2 · ONTOLOGY

CFH / AC sandbox

The possibility that consciousness is fundamental and local systems organize conditions for bounded expression. Speculative, not public fact.Možnost, da je zavest temeljna in lokalni sistemi organizirajo pogoje za omejen izraz. Spekulativno, ne javno dejstvo.

R3 · BIOLOGY

CCH

Testable questions about coherence, complexity, control, claustrum-related dynamics, loss and recovery of consciousness.Testabilna vprašanja o koherenci, kompleksnosti, nadzoru, dinamiki povezani s klaustrumom ter izgubi in povratku zavesti.

R4 · ENGINEERING

Functional AI8

Persistent stake, context control, initiative, standing, consequence updates and self-selecting governance—valuable even without consciousness.Trajni stake, nadzor konteksta, pobuda, standing, posodobitve posledic in samoselektivno upravljanje—koristno tudi brez zavesti.

R5 · EVIDENCE

Discriminating testsRazlikovalni testi

Only a test earns movement between layers. Similar vocabulary, eloquent self-report or council consensus does not.Samo test zasluži premik med plastmi. Podobno besedišče, lep self-report ali soglasje sveta ne.

External challenge: BrentZunanji izziv: Brent

Brent Rehmel's evolving cognition theory is used here as an external pressure source: context centering, impetus × control, dynamic abstraction, embodied value precursors and possible new hardware. It is not the governing ontology of AI8, and Brent had no role in the origin of MDL×DCC/DCC.Razvijajoča se Brent Rehmelova teorija kognicije se tukaj uporablja kot zunanji pritisk: centriranje konteksta, impetus × control, dinamična abstrakcija, telesni predhodniki vrednosti in morebitna nova strojna arhitektura. Ni vodilna ontologija AI8 in Brent ni sodeloval pri nastanku MDL×DCC/DCC.

No cross-layer promotionBrez promocije med plastmi

A functional AI result does not establish V4. A personal experience does not establish CFH. A brain correlation does not by itself establish causation. A failed hypothesis does not erase the original testimony or seed.Funkcionalni AI rezultat ne vzpostavi V4. Osebna izkušnja ne vzpostavi CFH. Možganska korelacija sama ne vzpostavi vzročnosti. Padla hipoteza ne izbriše izvornega pričevanja ali semena.
