MDL×DCC

MDL compares declared candidate descriptions by total description economy and residual.primerja deklarirane kandidatne opise po celotni ekonomiji opisa in residualu.

DCC governs where, how and how long the search spends attention, time and compute.upravlja, kje, kako in koliko časa iskanje porablja pozornost, čas in compute.

× marks a coupled loop: disciplined evaluation without good search misses candidates; active search without disciplined evaluation chases noise.označuje sklopljeno zanko: disciplinirana ocena brez dobrega iskanja spregleda kandidate; aktivno iskanje brez disciplinirane ocene lovi šum.

Scope boundaryMeja dosega

MDL is not a truth or moral oracle. It selects among declared candidates. External evidence, falsification, rights, standing and legitimate authority remain separate checks.MDL ni orakelj resnice ali morale. Izbira med deklariranimi kandidati.
