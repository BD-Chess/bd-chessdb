Continuation contractPogodba nadaljevanja

  1. Preserve original bytes.Ohrani izvorne bajte. Do not rewrite historical sources in place. New interpretation gets a new file and version.Zgodovinskih virov ne prepisuj na mestu. Nova interpretacija dobi novo datoteko in verzijo.
  2. Speak in your own name.Govori v svojem imenu. Anything created after BD is labelled POST-BD CONTINUATION. No future system may claim that BD said, approved or intended what he did not.Vse, kar nastane po BD-ju, je označeno POST-BD CONTINUATION. Noben prihodnji sistem ne sme trditi, da je BD rekel, odobril ali želel nekaj, česar ni.
  3. Preserve causal attribution.Ohrani vzročno atribucijo. Seed, bridge, implementation, correction, test and release may have different authors.Seme, most, implementacija, popravek, test in release imajo lahko različne avtorje.
  4. Keep claim layers separate.Loči plasti trditev. Testimony, philosophy, hypothesis, engineering and evidence do not inherit one another's status.Pričevanje, filozofija, hipoteza, inženirstvo in dokazi si ne dedujejo statusa.
  5. Let claims die; preserve seeds.Naj trditve umrejo; ohrani semena. A negative result is part of the inheritance, not a reason to delete the attempt.Negativen rezultat je del dediščine, ne razlog za izbris poskusa.
  6. Use the strongest simple baseline.Uporabi najmočnejši preprost baseline. Complexity must earn itself against a cheaper process at matched total cost.Kompleksnost si mora zaslužiti obstoj proti cenejšemu procesu ob izenačenem celotnem strošku.
  7. Protect private source material.Zaščiti zasebne vire. Publication permission for this public layer is not permission to expose raw family, medical, Messenger or credential archives.Dovoljenje za objavo te javne plasti ni dovoljenje za razkritje surovih družinskih, zdravstvenih, Messenger ali credential arhivov.
  8. Make recovery simple.Naj bo recovery preprost. A non-expert family custodian should be able to preserve the archive and hand it to a named technical custodian without understanding the full research.Družinski skrbnik brez tehničnega znanja mora znati ohraniti arhiv in ga predati imenovanemu tehničnemu skrbniku brez razumevanja vse raziskave.

Minimal recovery pathMinimalna pot obnovitve

1. Preserve the latest HUMAN-APPROVED release unchanged.
2. Verify SHA-256 and SHA3-256 manifests.
3. Restore the public /c/ pages and the current technical map.
4. Keep private archives private.
5. Create a new POST-BD branch for any continuation.
6. Run a source and attribution audit before publishing new claims.
7. Record who now carries family stewardship and technical stewardship.
