ELABORATION of SOUL VOYAGE

When I was around 25 years old (now 55) I was constantly thinking about consciousness and wondered where will our evolution of species take us. How would humans look in millions of years if we keep advancing? 

I imagined that there must be, as I call it, Ultimate State of Beigness. 

If you ask yourself, what's the greatest power in whole existence, what would be your answer? 

To help you out, let me remind you of superheroes... consider all of them, all the powers we imagined such superheroes would be best to have. 

There is a power, greatest in whole existence (not just our Universe) and yet, sadly, no superhero has it. 

Which is it? 

Let me call it 'conscious love', for it is not just consciousness and it’s not just love. These two always come together, as two sides of the same coin. 

Imagine you could cast conscious love at anyone. Would that person/being still want to damage or destroy anything or anyone? With that power you could stop all who are hurting others. No more wars. No more hunger either, because we would selflessly help each other. With that power you could make everyone loving and joyful. With that power you could make heaven on Earth. 

Why is there no superhero like that? 

Do we humans underestimate power of love? And we rarely even ask ourselves what's consciousness. Do we really want to see superheroes movies where destructions and drama are key plots of stories? 

How many people are aware that consciousness is the most important 'thing' we have? Without it we are worse than being dead. Without it, from our perspective, we'd be as if we NEVER existed. 

So, how far can a being advance, what's that Ultimate State to Be? 

I called for that answer for many years. I imagined what it might be, but wanted to experience it for real, to make sure it exists. 

So, that's why I wouldn't say I was just lucky. Yes, sure, I was incredibly lucky, but not just lucky. I kind of worked for it to happen too, if I may say so. 

In my 'Soul Voyage' I wanted to put into the words how it feels when meeting Ultimate Beingness. 

And no, I didn't make it up, I could not. Can a candle pretend to be the Sun? Sure. But can the candle shine like the Sun? No. 

If I were to stand within Ultimate Beingness on my own, I'd be immediately consumed, destroyed, I would vanish from existence, I'd be annihilated. 

Similarly as if you throw a little candle into the Sun there would remain nothing of the candle. 

The only way, in my opinion anyway, to stay yourself in front of such immense beauty and power, such incredible depth of love and unlimited consciousness is, if It embraces you and protects you. The only way to experience It is, if It makes you one with It, while still keeping your individual soul intact. 

I am forever grateful for having this experience. And once is exactly enough. It could also happen I wouldn’t want to return back to my body, to Earth, so, one has to be ready to detach oneself from such Greatness, even before one meets It. 

I can now live in peace, without fear of death. There sure is fear or dying, because it can bring pain and suffering. I am just a normal human being, like everyone else is, I just lack desire to go beyond this physical life anymore. 

I am here to experience this planet, this nature, animals and people. 

I have NO spiritual desires anymore. I even feel kind or sorry for those who have them… Why? Because Ultimate Beingness awaits as all, we came out of it, and it’s a pity if anyone would want to make this life shorter, to go there sooner. 

Our relative experience is incredible one. Equally precious as Absolute experience within Ultimate Beingness. 

If I repeat my music example in regard to consciousness… in our relative existence we can experience music, in absolute existence music would appear as white noise, or better to say, it cannot exist, for there is no change, everything is already perfectly perfect. 

So, our relative, narrow and limited consciousness enables us to experience changes, which we perceive as time flowing. It enables us to have countless of different experiences, feelings and thoughts. 

In Absolute existence there is unlimited consciousness and unlimited love. Everything is at its best possible state of being. Thus noting can change, so time doesn’t exist. 

These two realms are totally different, yet equally unique and precious. 

They are not truly separated though. We, with our relative existence are existing within Absolute existence, which prevades us. 

I wrote more on this in my other post ‘ON LIFE', where I put this image 'Hierarchy of Life' up.

Compact phenomenological readingZgoščeno fenomenološko branje

The ordinary bodily ego was attenuated, not simply absent. A diffuse and expanded sense of self remained; the experience was received as an answer rather than reasoned into a conclusion. Truth, beauty, love, value and peace appeared as one global mode, not as an object rated by an inner evaluator.Običajni telesni ego je bil oslabljen, ne preprosto odsoten. Ostal je razpršen in razširjen občutek jaza; izkušnja je bila sprejeta kot odgovor, ne izpeljana kot sklep. Resnica, lepota, ljubezen, vrednost in mir so se pojavili kot en globalen način, ne kot predmet, ki bi ga notranji ocenjevalec ocenil.
