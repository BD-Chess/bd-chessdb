Kaj gradimz LLM-ji in AI. What I buildwith LLMs and AI.

LLM-jev ne uporabljam predvsem kot klepetalnike, ampak kot sodelujočo raziskovalno in razvojno ekipo. Sam prispevam izvirna semena, povezave med področji, sistemsko arhitekturo, merila in končno presojo; modeli sodelujejo kot raziskovalci, programerji, kritiki, testerji in recenzenti.I do not use LLMs mainly as chatbots, but as a collaborating research and development team. I contribute the original seeds, cross-domain connections, system architecture, criteria and final judgment; models participate as researchers, programmers, critics, testers and reviewers.

Moja osrednja ideja je preprosta: več različnih predlogov naj tekmuje v poceni, merljivi areni; ohranimo tistega, ki najbolje stisne problem, pojasni ostanke in zdrži preverjanje.My central idea is simple: let several different proposals compete in a cheap, measurable arena; retain the one that best compresses the problem, explains the residuals and survives testing.

Kaj je že tu — in kaj še niWhat exists—and what does not yet

Že obstajaAlready exists

Javna koda, delujoči spletni programi, Python arene, rezultati, članki, primerjalni protokoli, razvojne sledi in konkretni fizični testni načrti. To kaže, da ne gre le za zbirko promptov ali pogovorov z modeli.Public code, working web programs, Python arenas, results, papers, comparison protocols, development traces and concrete physical test plans. This shows that the work is more than a collection of prompts or model conversations.

Še zahteva dokazStill requires proof

AI8 ni dokazana AGI; fizični izumi potrebujejo neodvisne bench teste; trgovanje nima javno revidiranega dokaza živega donosa; AC/CFH/CCH niso dokazana fizika. Te meje so del metode, ne opomba pod črto.AI8 is not proven AGI; physical inventions need independent bench tests; trading has no publicly audited live-return proof; AC/CFH/CCH are not proven physics. These boundaries are part of the method, not a footnote.

Z več LLM-ji spreminjam nenavadna semena v kodo, merljive arene, uporabna orodja, fizične testne načrte in jasno omejene znanstvene hipoteze.I am an independent systems inventor and human–AI collaboration researcher. With multiple LLMs, I turn unusual seeds into code, measurable arenas, useful tools, physical test plans and clearly bounded scientific hypotheses.
