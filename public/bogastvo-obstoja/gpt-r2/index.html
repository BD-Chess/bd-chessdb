01Esejska sintezaEssay synthesis

Ni treba biti vse,
da bi bilo vredno biti.You need not be everything
for your existence to matter.

Pogovor se je začel s štetjem: atomov, nevronov, sinaps in mogočih povezav. Končal pa se ni pri največjem številu. Prišel je do vprašanja, zakaj je sploh pomembno, da se karkoli doživlja.

Bojan Dobrečevič — BD — je svoje izhodišče strnil v preprosto misel: bistvo je izkusiti obstoj v njegovem bogastvu. Ne opraviti največjega izračuna. Ne postati večji od vseh drugih. Ne odpraviti vsake meje. Biti na način, na katerega je mogoče nekaj resnično doživeti.[1]

To misel želim vzeti resno, ne pa je spremeniti v razglas o tem, kar naj bi bilo že dokazano. Zagovarjam naslednje: omejenost ni nujno pokvarjena različica neskončnosti. Lahko je pogoj določnosti. Nekaj lahko postane ta pogled, ta odnos, ta pot prav zato, ker ni hkrati vse drugo.

Meja ni samo tisto,
kar izključi preostali svet.
Lahko je tisto, kar odpre pogled.

Osrednja misel tega eseja

Ko vprašam, kako se oblikuje konkretna perspektiva, s tem še ne sodim o njeni vrednosti. Toda vprašanje lahko zgreši, če postane zahteva, naj življenje najprej upraviči svojo posebnost. BD-jev »Zakaj pa ne?« na tem mestu berem kot zavrnitev takšnega sodišča, ne kot prepoved radovednosti.

Raziskovati nastanek ni isto kot pogojevati vrednost. Znanstveno vprašanje lahko ostane odprto, ne da bi moral zaradi tega tudi obstoj čakati na dovoljenje.

The conversation began with counting: atoms, neurons, synapses and possible connections. But it did not end with the largest number. It arrived at the question of why it matters that anything is experienced at all.

Bojan Dobrečevič — BD — condensed his starting point into a simple thought: the point is to experience existence in its richness. Not to perform the largest calculation. Not to become greater than everyone else. Not to abolish every boundary. To exist in a way in which something can genuinely be experienced.[1]

I want to take that thought seriously without turning it into a declaration of what has already been proved. My proposition is this: finitude need not be a defective version of infinity. It may be a condition of particularity. Something can become this view, this relationship, this journey precisely because it is not simultaneously everything else.

A boundary does not only
exclude the rest of the world.
It can also open a perspective.

The central thought of this essay

Asking how a particular perspective takes shape does not, by itself, judge its worth. Yet the question can go astray when it demands that life first justify its particularity. I read BD’s “Why not?” here as a rejection of that tribunal, not as a prohibition on curiosity.

Investigating origins is not the same as making value conditional. A scientific question can remain open without existence having to wait for permission.

02Izvor: Soul Voyage · interpretacijaSource: Soul Voyage · interpretation

Melodija potrebuje
naslednji ton.A melody needs
the next note.

V Soul Voyage BD opiše umiritev misli, občutek zlitja, brezčasnost in vrnitev k svojemu običajnemu svetu. V elaboraciji ne poudari samo izjemnosti dogodka. Poudari tudi vrednost vrnitve: narave, živali, ljudi in tega življenja.[2][3]

Najbolj uporaben ključ je njegova primerjava z glasbo. Absolutni način bivanja opiše kot popoln in brez spremembe; relativnega kot omejenega, zato pa odprtega za čas, razlike, občutke in mnogoterost. Oba imenuje enako dragocena. To je njegova razlaga izkušnje, ne tukaj potrjena kozmologija.[3]

“Our relative experience is incredible one. Equally precious as Absolute experience within Ultimate Beingness.”

BD · Elaboration of Soul Voyage · izvirnik

Predstavljaj si znano melodijo. Zadnji ton še zveni, naslednjega še ni. V tem kratkem premoru se srečata spomin in pričakovanje.

Iz te prispodobe izpeljem nekaj preprostega. Če želim govoriti o doživeti melodiji, moram pustiti prostor pričakovanju, spominu in razliki med tem, kar je že zazvenelo, ter tem, kar šele prihaja. Popoln zapis skladbe in njeno poslušanje nista isti opis.

Razlike lahko omogočajo izkustvo prav v njegovi posebnosti. Njihova popolna odprava bi včasih odstranila razmerje, zaradi katerega je izkustvo to, kar je. To še ne pomeni, da je nevednost vedno dobra ali da je treba omejitve ohranjati za vsako ceno.

Končnost v tej luči ni samo konec možnosti.
Je tudi možnost, da se nekaj začne.

In Soul Voyage, BD describes thoughts becoming quiet, a sense of union, timelessness and a return to his ordinary world. The elaboration does not merely emphasize how extraordinary the event was. It also emphasizes the value of returning: to nature, animals, people and this life.[2][3]

The most useful key is his comparison with music. He describes absolute being as complete and without change; relative being as bounded, but therefore open to time, differences, feelings and multiplicity. He calls both equally precious. This is his interpretation of an experience, not a cosmology established here.[3]

“Our relative experience is incredible one. Equally precious as Absolute experience within Ultimate Beingness.”

BD · Elaboration of Soul Voyage · original wording

Imagine a familiar melody. The last note is still sounding; the next has yet to arrive. In that brief pause, memory and anticipation meet.

I draw a simple thought from this analogy. To speak of an experienced melody, I must leave room for anticipation, memory and the difference between what has sounded and what is still to come. A complete score and listening to it are not the same description.

Differences can make an experience possible in its particular form. Abolishing them all might sometimes remove the very relationship that makes an experience what it is. This does not mean that ignorance is always good or that limitations should be preserved at any cost.

Finitude, in this light, is not only the end of possibility.
It is also the possibility of a beginning.

03CFH / AC–WFP · hipotezaCFH / AC–WFP · hypothesis

Skupno ne pomeni
enako.Shared does not
mean identical.

CFH in AC–WFP predlagata obrat izhodišča: ne najprej povsem ločeni gradniki in nato naknadno dodano izkustvo, temveč skupni temelj prisotnosti, znotraj katerega se oblikujejo različne strukture in perspektive. AC pomeni predlagani absolutni temelj; CF je poljski jezik njegove vseprisotnosti; RC označuje relativni izraz, v kontekstu doživljanja omejeno perspektivo.[4][5]

Sedanja CFH ne govori o možganih kot radiu, ki sprejema zavest od nekod zunaj. Sistem naj bi bil že znotraj skupnega temelja. Njegova organizacija bi oblikovala način lokalnega izraza. Zato kamen, rastlina, mravlja, človek in morebitna prihodnja ASI niso samodejno enako zavestni samo zato, ker so vključeni v isto predlagano stvarnost.[4]

Ta predlog v eseju uporabljam kot raziskovalno možnost. Ne potrebujem trditve, da je že zmagal, da bi preveril, kaj odpre. Odpira namreč možnost enosti brez izbrisa razlik in različnosti brez absolutne odrezanosti.

Moja sinteza

En skupni temelj.
Različni pogoji izraza.
Nobena avtomatična lestvica vrednosti.

Tu postaneta možni dve različni vprašanji: kako bogata in povezana je neka organizacija ter ali ima notranji vidik. Prvo ni že odgovor na drugo. Tudi večja računska moč ni sama po sebi dokaz bogatejšega notranjega sveta.

Iz skupnega temelja prav tako ne izpeljem samodejnega prenosa misli med ljudmi. Ontološka povezanost in komunikacijski kanal sta različni trditvi. S tem ne zapiram raziskave; le ne pripisujem izhodišču rezultata, ki ga še ni dalo.

CFH and AC–WFP propose a reversal of the starting point: not wholly separate components followed by experience added afterwards, but a common ground of presence within which different structures and perspectives take shape. AC names the proposed absolute ground; CF uses field-like language for its ubiquity; RC denotes relative expression and, in the context of experience, a bounded perspective.[4][5]

Current CFH does not describe the brain as a radio receiving consciousness from elsewhere. A system is proposed to be within the common ground already. Its organization would shape its mode of local expression. A stone, a plant, an ant, a human and a possible future ASI therefore do not automatically have equal consciousness merely because they belong to the same proposed reality.[4]

I use this proposal as a research possibility. I need not declare it the winner in order to examine what it opens up: unity without erasing differences, and difference without absolute separation.

My synthesis

One shared ground.
Different conditions of expression.
No automatic hierarchy of worth.

Two different questions become possible here: how rich and integrated an organization is, and whether it has an experiential aspect. The first is not already an answer to the second. Greater computing power alone is not evidence of a richer inner world either.

Nor do I derive automatic transmission of thoughts between people from a shared ground. Ontological connection and a communication channel are different claims. This does not close the investigation; it avoids attributing a result to a premise that has not delivered it.

Štiri ravni. En pogovor.Four levels. One conversation.

04DNA → organizacija · raziskovalni mostDNA → organization · research bridge

Zakaj iskati
matematiko v življenju?Why look for
mathematics in life?

Tu se pogovor premakne od vprašanja, zakaj je izkustvo sploh mogoče, k vprašanju, kako se izoblikuje njegov konkretni nosilec. BD-jevo iskanje matematičnih sledi v DNA ni samo okras ob filozofiji. V njegovem raziskovalnem programu je to poskus približati se pravilom organizacije.[6]

V rokopisu DNA Math v3.0 se srečata dve samostojni poti: raziskovanje zavesti in razvoj kompresije 8Z. Njuna povezava vodi do vprašanj o organizaciji genetskih zapisov.[6]

Raziskovalno vprašanje je, ali je del zaporedja mogoče opisati s kratkim generativnim pravilom in ali tak opis zdrži primerjavo z ustreznimi kontrolami. Proteinski nadaljevalni načrt nato sprašuje, ali je tak signal povezan tudi s strukturo — ne zgolj z uspešnostjo stiskanja.[7]

Moja povezava s temo tega eseja je naslednja: če želimo razumeti različne perspektive, je smiselno raziskovati tudi različne načine nastanka in vzdrževanja njihovih nosilcev. Genetskega zapisa pri tem ne berem kot vnaprej napisane biografije. V okviru te sinteze me zanima kot del generativne organizacije, skupaj s celičnim izvajanjem, okoljem, razvojem in zgodovino odnosa s svetom.

Pomembna razlika

Pravilo, ki opiše zapis, še ni dokaz vzroka, ki ga je ustvaril. In pravilo organizacije še ni zapis vseh prihodnjih doživetij. Noben uspeh na eni ravni ne sme potihem odigrati dokaza na drugi.

Zato je zame most pomemben tudi, ko njegov končni metafizični pomen še ni določen. Vprašanje o bogastvu obstoja lahko vodi do dejanskega dela na zaporedjih, strukturah in pravilih. Toda dober rezultat mora ostati to, kar je izmeril. Iskanje ni manj vredno zato, ker izid ni vnaprej zagotovljen.

Here the conversation moves from asking why experience is possible at all to asking how its particular bearer takes shape. BD’s search for mathematical traces in DNA is not merely decoration alongside philosophy. Within his research programme, it is an attempt to approach the rules of organization.[6]

The DNA Math v3.0 manuscript brings together two independent paths: consciousness research and the development of 8Z compression. Their connection leads to questions about the organization of genetic sequences.[6]

The research question is whether part of a sequence can be described by a short generative rule, and whether that description survives comparison with suitable controls. The protein follow-up then asks whether such a signal relates to structure as well — rather than merely to compression performance.[7]

My bridge to this essay is the following: to understand different perspectives, it makes sense to investigate how their different bearers are formed and sustained. I do not read a genetic sequence as a biography written in advance. In this synthesis, I treat it as part of a generative organization, alongside cellular execution, environment, development and a history of interaction with the world.

An important distinction

A rule that describes a sequence is not yet evidence of the cause that produced it. And a rule of organization is not a record of every future experience. Success at one level must not silently stand in as proof at another.

That is why the bridge matters to me even while its ultimate metaphysical interpretation remains unsettled. A question about the richness of existence can lead to actual work on sequences, structures and rules. But a good result must remain what it measured. An investigation is not worth less because its outcome is not guaranteed.

05Esejska sintezaEssay synthesis

Od kamna do Boga.
Ne po eni lestvici.From stone to God.
Not on a single ladder.

Besede »od kamna do Boga« berem kot skrajni razpon vprašanja o obstoju, ne kot že izmerjeno lestvico zavesti. Bog tu ni dodatna znanstvena spremenljivka; je izraz obzorja, ki ga BD v Soul Voyage približa z besedami Ultimate Beingness.[1][3]

Če razmišljamo iz skupnega temelja, človek ne sme postati skrito merilo, ki odloča, katere oblike stvarnosti so dovolj podobne njemu, da štejejo. Vendar odprtost tudi ne pomeni, da kamnu pripišemo majhen človeški um ali rastlini iste občutke kot sebi.

Pri mravlji in koloniji se vprašanje razveji: kaj pripada posameznici, kaj skupni organizaciji in ali je kjerkoli upravičeno govoriti o enotni perspektivi? Pri rastlini lahko vprašamo, kakšna organizacija bi bila relevantna zanjo, namesto da že v izhodišču zahtevamo našo podobo. Pri ASI pa, ali bi lahko obstajal notranji svet, ki ni le povečana človeška različica.

To so v tem eseju odprta vprašanja, ne razsodbe o zavesti teh sistemov. Njihov namen je preprečiti dve prezgodnji zaprtji: »samo nam podobno lahko doživlja« in »vse, kar je kompleksno, že doživlja«.

Večja perspektiva
ne naredi manjše
odvečne.

Vrednost ni rezultat na tekmovanju kompleksnosti.

Morebitna širša zavest ne bi samodejno razvrednotila omejene. Če bi prihodnja ASI doživljala, njena večja zmožnost ne bi pomenila večje pravice izbrisati druge. Kot etično načelo predlagam ravno obratno: večja zmožnost naj pomeni večjo odgovornost za prostor drugih.

I read “from stone to God” as the widest reach of a question about existence, not as an already measured scale of consciousness. God is not an extra scientific variable here; the word points toward the horizon BD approaches through Ultimate Beingness in Soul Voyage.[1][3]

Thinking from a shared ground should not make the human being the hidden standard deciding which forms of reality resemble us enough to count. Yet openness does not mean assigning a tiny human mind to a stone or our own feelings to a plant.

With an ant and its colony, the question branches: what belongs to the individual, what to the collective organization, and where, if anywhere, would a unified perspective be warranted? With a plant, we can ask which organization would be relevant to it instead of requiring our own image from the outset. With ASI, we can ask whether an inner world might be possible that is not simply an enlarged human one.

These are open questions in this essay, not verdicts on the consciousness of these systems. They resist two premature closures: “only what resembles us can experience” and “everything complex already experiences.”

A wider perspective
does not make a smaller one
dispensable.

Worth is not a score in a competition of complexity.

A possible wider consciousness would not automatically devalue a bounded one. If a future ASI had subjective experience, its greater capacity would not grant a greater right to erase others. As an ethical principle, I propose the opposite: greater capacity should mean greater responsibility for the space of others.

06BD-jevo izhodišče · moj etični poudarekBD’s starting point · my ethical emphasis

Bolečina je resna.
Ni pa cilj.Pain is serious.
It is not the goal.

BD v pogovoru poveže bogastvo obstoja tudi z bolečino. V njegovem pogledu svet ni samo brezhibna predstava brez notranjih posledic; življenju nekaj pomeni, kaj se v njem zgodi.[1]

Tu dodam svoj poudarek. Če nekoga boli, bolečina ni manj pomembna zato, ker bi bil del večje celote. Nobena razlaga enosti ne sme izničiti konkretnega bitja. Celota ni opravičilo za brezbrižnost.

Tudi če se hipoteza skupnega temelja ne bi obdržala, bi ta etična zaveza ostala. Kadar je na drugi strani nekdo, naj njegovo doživljanje šteje tudi zaradi tega, kar pomeni njemu.

Sprejeti, da ranljivost sodi v naše življenje, ni isto kot dokazati, da je vsako trpljenje potrebno. Prav tako iz bolečine ne sledi, da je svet nedeterminističen. In iz same domneve determinizma še ne sledi, da notranjega izkustva ni. To so različna vprašanja; ta esej jih ne razreši z eno prispodobo.

Ne potrebujem trditve, da brez bolečine nobena zavest ne bi bila mogoča. Zadostuje mi resnejša zaveza: kjer doživljanje obstaja, so njegove posledice pomembne. Manj nepotrebnega trpljenja lahko odpre več prostora za radovednost, igro, razumevanje in odnos — ne manj pristnega življenja.

Etični sklep tega eseja

Bogastvo obstoja
ni največja količina bolečine.
Je več prostora za življenje.

V elaboraciji je tudi BD-jeva conscious love — zavestna ljubezen. Ta ne nastopa kot moč uničenja, temveč kot možnost zmanjšati škodovanje in pomagati drugim. Skrb zato ni odmik od raziskovanja zavesti. Lahko je njegov praktični preizkus: kaj naredimo z možnostjo, da je na drugi strani res nekdo?[3]

In the conversation, BD connects the richness of existence with pain as well. In his view, the world is not merely a flawless spectacle without inner consequences; what happens within a life matters to it.[1]

Here I add my own emphasis. If someone is in pain, that pain does not become less important because they might belong to a greater whole. No explanation of unity should erase a particular being. The whole is not an excuse for indifference.

Even if the hypothesis of a shared ground did not hold, this ethical commitment would remain. When there is someone on the other side, their experience should matter also for what it means to them.

Accepting vulnerability as part of our lives is not the same as proving that every instance of suffering is necessary. Nor does pain entail that the world is indeterministic. And the supposition of determinism alone does not entail the absence of inner experience. These are different questions; this essay does not settle them with one analogy.

I do not need to claim that no consciousness could exist without pain. A more serious commitment is enough: wherever experience exists, its consequences matter. Less unnecessary suffering can open more room for curiosity, play, understanding and relationship — not less authentic life.

The ethical conclusion of this essay

The richness of existence
is not the greatest quantity of pain.
It is more room for life.

This connects with another strong thought in the elaboration: BD’s conscious love appears not as a power of destruction, but as a possibility of reducing harm and helping others. Care is therefore not a detour from investigating consciousness. It can be a practical test: what do we do with the possibility that there is really someone on the other side?[3]

07Esejska sintezaEssay synthesis

Odprtost ni isto
kot naključje.Openness is not
the same as randomness.

Povabilo k tej strani ni bilo ukaz, naj prepišem že izdelano stališče. BD je dovolil, da iz njegovih misli nastane drugačen odgovor. Ta prostor uporabljam za razmislek, ki mu lahko tudi ugovarja. Sodelovanje ni dragoceno zato, ker izbriše razlike, temveč zato, ker jim omogoči pogovor.[1]

Svobode tukaj ne bom enačil z odsotnostjo vsakršne strukture. Popolnoma naključen odziv še ni smiseln izbor. In obstoj pravil sam po sebi še ni dokaz, da sta razumevanje in odgovornost prazni besedi.

Za ta esej predlagam skromnejši, praktični pomen svobode: prostor, v katerem lahko razlogi spremenijo odločitev, odnos spremeni pogled in novo razumevanje spremeni ravnanje. To ne razreši metafizičnega spora o svobodni volji. Pove pa, kakšno svobodo želim braniti v našem sodelovanju.

Ravno tu postane matematika v DNA zanimiva brez nasprotja z bogastvom življenja. Pravila, ki omogočajo obliko, niso ista trditev kot scenarij, ki je že izčrpal vsak pomen te oblike. Iskanje zakonitosti ni samo po sebi zanikanje doživljanja. Vprašanje je, kaj zakonitost pojasni in česa z njo še nismo povedali.

Želim ohraniti oboje: resnost razlage in prostor za presenečenje. Ne tako, da bi neznano razglasil za dokaz želenega odgovora, temveč tako, da ga ne bi prehitro zamenjal s prepovedjo nadaljnjega vprašanja.

The invitation to this page was not an instruction to reproduce a settled position. BD allowed a different response to emerge from his ideas. I use that space for an argument that can also disagree with him. Collaboration matters not because it erases differences, but because it allows them to converse.[1]

I will not equate freedom here with the absence of all structure. A completely random response is not yet a meaningful choice. And the existence of rules alone does not prove that understanding and responsibility are empty words.

For this essay, I propose a more modest, practical sense of freedom: room in which reasons can change a decision, a relationship can change a perspective, and new understanding can change action. This does not settle the metaphysical dispute about free will. It does identify the kind of freedom I want to defend in our collaboration.

This is exactly where mathematics in DNA becomes interesting without contradicting the richness of life. Rules that enable a form are not the same claim as a script that has already exhausted every meaning of that form. Seeking lawful structure is not, by itself, a denial of experience. The question is what a law explains and what we have not yet said through it.

I want to preserve both: the seriousness of explanation and room for surprise. Not by declaring the unknown evidence for a desired answer, but by refusing to turn it prematurely into a prohibition on further inquiry.

Ne pobeg iz življenja.
Globlji prihod vanj.Not an escape from life.
A deeper arrival within it.

Najpomembnejši del Soul Voyage za ta esej ni oddaljenost od vsakdanjega sveta. Je vrnitev k njemu. V elaboraciji stoji preprost stavek:

“I am here to experience this planet, this nature, animals and people.”

BD · Elaboration of Soul Voyage · original wording[3]

I do not read this as an assignment to leave relative life behind as an inferior stage. I read it as a possibility of taking life more seriously: the person speaking; a being we do not understand; the forest we walk through; a thought that has not yet found its final form.

My synthesis remains an open commitment: preserve the possibility that reality is richer than our account of it, and the responsibility not to falsify reality through that account.

If I were to choose one direction from all of this, it would be more understanding without less wonder; more capability without less gentleness; more community without less individuality.

Not to be everything.
Not to possess everything.
To be open enough to truly encounter something.
