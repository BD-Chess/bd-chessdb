8zip is not one magic codec. It is a verified arena: build several serious candidates, account for every byte, reject anything that cannot restore the exact source tree, and keep the smallest valid result. The compatible standard-ZIP branch and the experimental 8zipLab branch are deliberately separate.

8zip ni en čudežni kodek. Je verificirana arena: zgradi več resnih kandidatov, obračunaj vsak bajt, zavrni vse, kar ne obnovi natančnega izvornega drevesa, in obdrži najmanjši pravilen rezultat. Združljiva veja standardnega ZIP-a in eksperimentalna veja 8zipLab sta namenoma ločeni.

The separation prevents a good experimental result from being mistaken for an ordinary ZIP result. It also lets the mainline stay useful today while the laboratory explores deeper structures for tomorrow.

Ločitev prepreči, da bi dober eksperimentalni rezultat zamenjali za navaden ZIP rezultat. Hkrati omogoča, da je glavna veja uporabna že danes, laboratorij pa raziskuje globlje strukture za jutri.

8zip + ReZIP

The standard-ZIP mainlineGlavna veja standardnega ZIP-a

The output remains a normal .zip archive using Store(0) and Deflate(8). Ordinary unzip tools restore it. Internally, 8zip may compare fresh 7‑Zip, ECT, KZIP, imported streams, and its own per-file candidates.

Izhod ostane običajen arhiv .zip z metodama Store(0) in Deflate(8). Obnovijo ga navadna unzip orodja. Znotraj arene lahko 8zip primerja sveži 7‑Zip, ECT, KZIP, uvožene tokove in lastne kandidate po datotekah.

  • Standard ZIP compatibility is a hard requirement.Združljivost s standardnim ZIP-om je trda zahteva.
  • The smallest candidate wins only after strict tree and byte verification.Najmanjši kandidat zmaga šele po strogi preverbi drevesa in bajtov.
  • ReZIP optimizes existing archives for cold storage without changing their meaning.ReZIP optimizira obstoječe arhive za hladno hrambo, ne da spremeni njihov pomen.
8zipLab

The experimental FPLB laboratoryEksperimentalni laboratorij FPLB

8zipLab is allowed to use its own FPLB container, embedded trained or procedural dictionaries, content-addressed blobs, and research-only restoration logic. It is not presented as standard ZIP.

8zipLab sme uporabljati lasten vsebnik FPLB, vgrajene naučene ali proceduralne slovarje, vsebinsko naslovljene bloke ter raziskovalno obnovo. Ni predstavljen kot standardni ZIP.

  • All model, dictionary, manifest, and container bytes are charged.Vsi bajti modela, slovarja, manifesta in vsebnika so obračunani.
  • Train, validation, and sealed holdout are separated by exact-content group.Train, validation in sealed holdout so ločeni po skupinah enake vsebine.
  • FPLB is not standard ZIP. A custom-format win does not imply universal dictionary generalization; CROSS_ARCHIVE_REUSE remains unproven without independent external corpora.FPLB ni standardni ZIP. Zmaga lastnega formata še ne pomeni univerzalne posplošitve slovarja; CROSS_ARCHIVE_REUSE ostaja nedokazan brez neodvisnih zunanjih korpusov.

What we know so farKaj za zdaj vemo

The encouraging part is not a slogan. It is that several different mechanisms have now produced verified gains: stream selection in standard ZIP, embedded dictionaries in FPLB, and exact-content deduplication in FPLB3.

Spodbuden del ni slogan. Več različnih mehanizmov je že prineslo verificirane prihranke: izbor tokov v standardnem ZIP-u, vgrajeni slovarji v FPLB in odstranjevanje natančnih dvojnikov v FPLB3.

Interpretation

Why both small and large gains matterZakaj so pomembni majhni in veliki prihranki

A 0.3% gain on a difficult corpus can validate a new mechanism. A 9–15% gain on a suitable archive can already matter operationally. The right goal is not “beat everything everywhere”; it is find the best valid description for this data and never regress when a simpler method is better.

0,3 % prihranka na težkem korpusu lahko potrdi nov mehanizem. 9–15 % na primernem arhivu je lahko že praktično pomembnih. Pravi cilj ni »premagati vse povsod«, temveč najti najboljši pravilen opis teh podatkov in ne regresirati, kadar je preprostejša metoda boljša.

Research lessonsRaziskovalne lekcije

The project is becoming clearer, not smallerProjekt postaja jasnejši, ne manjši

Each run removes one kind of uncertainty. That is progress: we now know which gains are container effects, dictionary effects, exact-duplicate effects, and which claims still need external corpora.

Vsak run odstrani eno vrsto negotovosti. To je napredek: zdaj vemo, kateri prihranki izvirajo iz vsebnika, slovarjev, natančnih dvojnikov in katere trditve še zahtevajo zunanje korpuse.

Verification is part of compressionVerifikacija je del stiskanja

A smaller archive that cannot prove exact restoration is not a winner. The verifier, tree hash, corruption tests, and deterministic rebuild are part of the product.

Manjši arhiv, ki ne dokaže natančne obnove, ni zmagovalec. Verifier, hash drevesa, testi poškodb in deterministična ponovna gradnja so del izdelka.

Split by content, not only by filenameDelitev po vsebini, ne le po imenu

v0.2.1 revealed that identical content under different paths can weaken holdout independence. v0.2.2 keeps exact duplicates inside one split.

v0.2.1 je pokazala, da ista vsebina pod različnimi potmi oslabi neodvisnost holdouta. v0.2.2 drži natančne dvojnike v istem splitu.

One learned dictionary survived stricter testsEn naučeni slovar je preživel strožje teste

The reusable signal became smaller after duplicate-safe analysis, but it did not disappear. That makes the next cross-archive test worth doing.

Signal ponovne uporabe se je po duplicate-safe analizi zmanjšal, ni pa izginil. Zato je naslednji cross-archive test smiseln.

Exact dedup changed the scaleExact dedup je spremenil merilo

On this corpus, storing identical content once produced far more gain than refining dictionary recipes alone. The best roadmap follows measured leverage.

Na tem korpusu je enkratno shranjevanje enake vsebine prineslo mnogo več kot samo izboljševanje slovarskih receptov. Najboljši načrt sledi izmerjenemu vzvodu.

Realistic upsideRealistični potencial

What could this become?Kaj lahko iz tega nastane?

There is no honest universal “maximum compression” number. The ceiling depends on the data. But the measured mechanisms already support three realistic development scenarios.

Poštena univerzalna številka »maksimalne kompresije« ne obstaja. Strop je odvisen od podatkov. Izmerjeni mehanizmi pa že podpirajo tri realistične razvojne scenarije.

Useful nowUporabno že zdaj

Verified ZIP optimizerVerificirani ZIP optimizator

ReZIP becomes a practical cold-storage tool that can recover anything from zero to low double-digit percentages, depending on how weak or redundant the original archive is, while preserving ordinary ZIP compatibility.

ReZIP postane praktično orodje za hladno hrambo, ki lahko glede na šibkost ali redundanco izvirnega arhiva prihrani od nič do nizkih dvomestnih odstotkov in ohrani običajno ZIP združljivost.

Strong research pathMočna raziskovalna pot

Specialized FPLB archivesSpecializirani arhivi FPLB

For project bundles, versioned JSON/CSV/log datasets, and backup trees with repeated content, exact dedup plus charged dictionaries can realistically produce persistent mid- to low-double-digit gains.

Pri projektnih paketih, verzioniranih JSON/CSV/log podatkih in backup drevesih s ponavljajočo vsebino lahko exact dedup ter obračunani slovarji realno prinesejo trajne srednje do nizke dvomestne prihranke.

High-upside frontierVisoko-potencialna meja

Near-duplicate chunks and deltasNear-duplicate bloki in delte

If content-defined chunking and base+delta candidates work without verifier complexity exploding, highly repetitive snapshots could go well beyond today’s 9% migration proof. This is plausible on the right data, not promised for arbitrary files.

Če content-defined chunking in base+delta kandidati delujejo brez eksplozije kompleksnosti verifierja, bi lahko zelo ponavljajoči snapshoti močno presegli današnji 9-odstotni migracijski dokaz. To je verjetno na pravih podatkih, ne obljuba za poljubne datoteke.

The most important positive constraint: already-compressed JPEG, MP4, encrypted, or high-entropy data may offer almost nothing. That does not weaken the architecture. It is why the arena keeps Store/fallback candidates and promotes a new method only when the complete verified result is genuinely smaller.

Najpomembnejša pozitivna omejitev: že stisnjeni JPEG, MP4, šifrirani ali visoko-entropijski podatki lahko ponudijo skoraj nič. To ne slabi arhitekture. Prav zato arena ohrani Store/fallback kandidate in novo metodo promovira le, kadar je celoten verificirani rezultat res manjši.
