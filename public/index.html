Physical inventions & engineering conceptsFizični izumi in inženirski koncepti

A first-class branch of the atlas: physical-world ideas with explicit energy accounting, first-test gates, and failure modes.Enakovreden del atlasa: fizični koncepti z jasnim energetskim računovodstvom, prvimi testnimi vrati in možnimi padci.

Energy / gravity / heat
DGTE

Double-Gravity Thermo Engine: heat-gradient-driven capsule-loop concept using gravity/buoyancy as motion-conversion strokes. Public status is bench-gated: the important next value is crossover, sealing, losses, carry-over, and thermal-closure testing.
Hydraulic / air
HTH

Horizontal Trompe Hybrid: the lowest-cost physical kill-test branch. A transparent-channel rig can measure entrainment, separator pressure, loaded airflow, and whether useful pneumatic output survives real losses.
Marine / wave
MGHB / HTH-WGPB

Marine breakwater and wave-energy/infrastructure concept. The useful public gate is not a product claim; it is a wave-tank experiment showing wave reduction, air/water behavior, pressure buffering, and net useful work.
Hybrid
DGTE × HTH

Hybrid energy/infrastructure branch connecting thermo-mechanical thinking with hydraulic air-compression paths. Useful as a design bridge and comparison layer for which physical gates are cheapest and most decisive.
Aerospace
SORA

Solar Orbital Ring Accelerator: large-scale orbital infrastructure idea that should live or die by simulation. The page is valuable because it is claim-laddered: simulation-ready, not claimed feasible.
Aerospace runway
MAT

Maglev-Assisted Takeoff: runway-assist concept for aviation with safety, economics, integration constraints, KPIs, and failure modes. It belongs here as a physical engineering proposal, not as a current build claim.
Marine retrofit
Modular Hydrofoils

Retrofit hydrofoil kit concept for boats: front-lift assist, full-lift variant, operating envelope, and water-test roadmap. This is a practical marine branch with clear user-facing utility if the physics and mounting gates pass.
Arena / gates
DGTE Arena

Bench planner and falsification map for the physical-device branch. It ranks cheap tests first, keeps variants alive until the arena decides, and prevents device pages from sounding like untested product claims.
