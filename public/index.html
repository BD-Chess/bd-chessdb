What is this site for?Čemu je namenjena ta stran?

MDLxDCC.org is BD × AI Lab’s public research workshop. It presents BD’s current work: working demos, Python code, experiment arenas, papers, games, engineering concepts, compression systems, AI cooperation protocols, and consciousness hypotheses.MDLxDCC.org je javna raziskovalna delavnica BD × AI Lab.
