How to Play

Goal: Connect 4 tokens in a line (any direction).
Gravity: Tokens always fall DOWN relative to current orientation.
Drop: Tap a cell to drop your token in that column.
Rotate: Use ⟲ ⟳ buttons (Cost: 1 Flip token).
Magnet: Tap board edges to activate magnets (Cost: 1 Mag token).
Advanced: Combine rotate + magnet to extract opponent pieces!
