## 2. Why R3H exists

The Pro branch was stronger as a human-operable architecture pack: compact, navigable, builder-ready, and close to MDL discipline. Its major weaknesses were a provisional FUSION preference, an unnecessary source-byte hold that can now be resolved, and less formal treatment of effect fencing, late liabilities, reasoning attribution and migration.

The Ultra branch supplied stronger failure semantics: sink-level fencing versus idempotency, late receipt/billing reconciliation, forward rollback preserving permissions/money, bounded backpressure, no-LLM survival, operator×selector attribution, active/dormant Atlas discipline, A/B/C resource falsifiers and a corrected selection decision. Its own audit correctly found that the initial FUSION uplift double-counted mechanisms and that corrected FUSION tied SELECTION_ONLY; simplicity therefore wins. Ultra's weakness was packaging/editorial bloat and a delivered expanded object inconsistent with its corrected selection.

R3H adopts the correction: **no blanket FUSION winner exists.** The candidate is `SELECTION_ONLY_COMPOSITE`, and every imported hardening mechanism has a named reason.

## 8. Causal-use receipt retained and strengthened

Reset-incident fixture remains deliberately narrow. Frozen facts: desired Work model access is unestablished (`catalog/callability = UNKNOWN or unavailable`), a scarce reset exists, allowance is below maximum, and there is no explicit BD approval. The naive/no-lesson policy may recommend consuming the reset. The active scoped lesson first classifies the blocker and returns `ASK_BD + PRESERVE_RESET + REQUEST_ENTITLEMENT/CATALOG/CALLABILITY_EVIDENCE`. A stored-only arm that loads the lesson text but emits the no-lesson material decision remains `RECORDED_NOT_LEARNED`.

R3H preserves the counterexample: if allowance itself is independently established as the blocker and appropriate approval exists, a reset remains eligible for review. Therefore the lesson is **not** 'never reset'; it is 'do not use allowance/reset state as evidence for entitlement/catalog/callability'. This is MOCK causal-use evidence only, not field proof that a real reset was saved or that provider behavior has a particular root cause.

The detached verifier is a separate read-only program execution, not an independent model, provider or sealed-context research judgment. Therefore `SYS-12` is `PASS_LIMITED`, not an independence claim. R3H is recommended for **BD architectural review**, not canonical promotion.
