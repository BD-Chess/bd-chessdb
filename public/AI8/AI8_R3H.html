BD acceleration amendment

Distill repeatable intervention classes; preserve human surprise.

BD currently contributes unusually high-leverage trajectory changes: seeds, cross-domain bridges, reframing, mission correction, value judgments and exception authority. R3H treats gradual system-level capture of repeatable intervention classes as a bounded improvement target. This is a candidate direction, not a claim of completed internalization or model-weight learning.

BD pospeševalni amandma

Ponovljive razrede posegov destiliraj; ohrani človeško presenečenje.

BD trenutno prispeva posebej visoko-vzvodne spremembe trajektorije: semena, meddomenske mostove, reframing, popravljanje misije, vrednostne presoje in avtoriteto izjem. R3H obravnava postopno sistemsko zajemanje ponovljivih razredov posegov kot omejen cilj izboljševanja. To je kandidatna smer, ne trditev o dokončani internalizaciji ali učenju uteži modela.

Cross-cutting contracts

Rank, don't eliminate

Viable alternatives survive until evidence earns pruning, merging, rejection or parking.

Learn from mistakes

Incident → failed assumption → impact → lesson candidate → regression fixture → controlled causal use. Stored prose alone remains RECORDED_NOT_LEARNED.

Claim boundary

R3H is recommended as a candidate for BD review. Publication is not canonical promotion, field validation, AGI evidence, consciousness evidence or unrestricted self-improvement.
