IZBERI NAČIN SODELOVANJACHOOSE HOW THEY COLLABORATE

Kaj naj preverita?What should they check?

Izberi računalnik in nastavitve, preglej celoten načrt ter ga potrdi za oddajo v zasebni GitHub Inbox. Kopiranje ukaza ostaja na voljo za uporabo v povezani seji.Choose the computer and settings, review the full plan and confirm it for submission to the private GitHub Inbox. Copying the command remains available for use in a connected session.

Neposredna oddaja je na voljo prijavljenemu lastniku. Privzeto: HOME · AUTO · 2 seji · največ 100 potez / 12 ur aktivnega dela.Direct submission is available to the signed-in owner. Default: HOME · AUTO · 2 sessions · at most 100 turns / 12 hours of active work.

Vsak računalnik sprejema svoje zahteve: do pet različnih debat hkrati, zaporedne poteze znotraj vsake. WORK podpira AUTO/HIGH in MAX; MIN in XHIGH še nista sprejeta.Each computer accepts its own requests: up to five different debates at once, sequential turns within each. WORK supports AUTO/HIGH and MAX; MIN and XHIGH are not accepted yet.

Štiri ločena dejstva. Dokaz: pretekli preizkus samo v navedenem obsegu, ne splošna ocena. Dostopnost: trenutno stanje; neznana ne pomeni na voljo. Usmerjanje: ali ta stran oziroma izvajalnik model sme poklicati. ID: stabilni zapis v katalogu in točno ujemanje profila. Katalog ne podeljuje izbire.Four separate facts. Evidence: a past test, only within the stated scope, not a general rating. Availability: current state; unknown does not mean available. Routing: whether this page or the executor may call the model. ID: the stable catalog record and exact profile match. The catalog does not grant selection.

Uporabi točen zasebni ID naloge iz prejemka in njen računalnik. LOOP CAPACITY pokaže mejo različnih debat na hostu; LOOP CAPACITY 5 jo nastavi. Znižanje pusti tekoče odgovore dokončati. EMAIL, DRIVE in BROWSER ostanejo izrecno ločeni kanali.HELP, STATUS, PAUSE, RESUME, STOP and CAPACITY are control commands. Examples: LOOP HELP, LOOP STATUS, LOOP PAUSE <UUID>, LOOP RESUME <UUID>, LOOP STOP <UUID>. Use the exact private task ID from the receipt and its computer. LOOP CAPACITY shows the per-host limit of different debates; LOOP CAPACITY 5 sets it. Lowering it lets running answers finish. EMAIL, DRIVE and BROWSER remain explicitly separate channels.
