Naslednji raziskovalni korakNext research step

CortexNAS je predlagana raziskovalna povezava. Na novih zamrznjenih nalogah primerjamo surovi model, sedanji Cortex, kontrolni priklic z enakim proračunom in optimizirano politiko.CortexNAS is a proposed bridge. On unseen frozen tasks, compare the raw model, current Cortex, equal-budget retrieval control and optimized policy.

Merimo celotno nalogoMeasure the whole task

Uspeh, p95 zakasnitev, kontekstni žetoni, mentorjevi klici, pomnilnik in varnost. Ne obljubljamo splošne premoči nad večjimi modeli.Success, p95 latency, context tokens, teacher calls, memory and safety. We do not claim general superiority over larger models.

Prenos v druge modeleTransfer to other models

Ali isti preverjeni zunanji učni paket pomaga drugemu lokalnemu modelu? Poznejši QLoRA je ločen poskus z izoliranim adapterjem in svežim holdoutom.Can a verified external learning package help another local model? Later QLoRA is a separate isolated-adapter experiment with a fresh holdout.
