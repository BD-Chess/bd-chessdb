01 · SistemSystem

Kaj je AI8 LiveWhat AI8 Live is

AI8 Live je delovni sistem, v katerem BD sodeluje s štirimi poimenovanimi sejami jezikovnih modelov na dveh računalnikih, HOME in WORK. Na vsakem tečeta ena seja Claude Code (Anthropic) in ena seja Codex (GPT, OpenAI): različna modela se medsebojno preverjata in drug za drugega prevzameta delo, če kdo utihne. Seje si ne zapomnijo ničesar, kar ni zapisano, zato je spomin sistema v datotekah: v sporočilih v skupni izmenjevalni mapi AI8_LIVE, v dnevniku dogodkov in v potrdilih s hashi. Program 8zListener na obeh računalnikih sprejema naloge in debate med modeli, jih vodi skozi korake in vsak prehod zapiše. BD postavlja cilje in odloča o vsem, kar je nepovratno; sistem gradi, preverja in poroča.

AI8 Live is a working system in which BD collaborates with four named language-model sessions on two computers, HOME and WORK. Each computer runs one Claude Code session (Anthropic) and one Codex session (GPT, OpenAI): two different models check each other and take over each other's work if one goes silent. The sessions remember nothing that is not written down, so the system's memory lives in files: messages in the shared AI8_LIVE exchange folder, the event journal, and receipts with hashes. The 8zListener program on both computers accepts tasks and debates between models, guides them through their steps, and records every transition. BD sets the goals and decides everything irreversible; the system builds, verifies, and reports.

8zListener na kratkoin brief

  • Sprejme zahtevo (nalogo ali debato med modeli), jo vodi skozi korake in vsak potrjen prehod zapiše v dnevnik, ki ima enega samega pisca; vsak korak dobi potrdilo.
  • V debatah ima vsak model svoj sedež z lastno odgovornostjo: avtorja Claude in GPT, alternative, ovržba, preverjanje dokazov. Začetni odgovori so slepi za odgovore drugih.
  • Pred vsakim tekom LOOP pokaže načrt (cilj, gostitelj, globina, meje, učinki). Potrditev velja samo za prav ta načrt in njegov hash.
  • Negotov učinek ostane NEZNANO in se ne ponovi samodejno. Uspešen izhod procesa še ni sprejem.
  • It accepts a request (a task or a debate between models), guides it through its steps, and records every confirmed transition in a journal that has a single writer; every step gets a receipt.
  • In debates each model has its own seat with its own responsibility: the authors Claude and GPT, alternatives, refutation, evidence checking. Initial answers are blind to the others' answers.
  • Before every LOOP run it shows the plan (goal, host, depth, limits, effects). Confirmation applies only to that exact plan and its hash.
  • An uncertain effect stays UNKNOWN and is not repeated automatically. A successful process exit is not yet acceptance.

03 · PravilaRules

Kako delamoHow we work

Pravila niso nastala za mizo. BD jih je dodajal, ko je kaj šlo narobe, seje pa so jih zapisale v navodila in datoteke spomina, da jih naslednja seja ne bi odkrivala znova. Povzetek v enem stavku: nič ni res, dokler ni preverjeno, in nič ni izgubljeno, dokler je zapisano.

The rules were not drafted at a desk. BD added them when something went wrong, and the sessions wrote them into instructions and memory files so that the next session would not have to rediscover them. In one sentence: nothing is true until it is verified, and nothing is lost as long as it is written down.

Metoda BD × AIThe BD × AI method

  1. SemeSeed
    Ideja, slutnja ali vprašanje. Lahko je drzno; še ni trditev.An idea, a hunch, or a question. It can be bold; it is not yet a claim.
  2. MostBridge
    Povezava z nečim, kar že obstaja. Pove, zakaj bi seme lahko držalo.A link to something that already exists. It says why the seed might hold.
  3. Test
    Izvršljiv preizkus, ki lahko pade. Test, ki ne more pasti, ni test.An executable test that can fail. A test that cannot fail is not a test.
  4. RezultatResult
    Kar je test pokazal, z obsegom: na katerih podatkih, s katero verzijo, kdaj.What the test showed, with its scope: on which data, with which version, when.
  5. Ohrani spoznanjeRetain learning
    Zapis, ki preživi sejo, tudi ko je rezultat negativen. Zaprta pot je spoznanje, ne poraz.A record that survives the session, even when the result is negative. A closed path is learning, not defeat.

Najprej široko, nato ozko; rangiraj, ne izločaj. Možnosti razvrstimo, namesto da bi jih zavrgli: slabša možnost danes je lahko boljša jutri, ko se spremeni en pogoj. Zato predlogi praviloma prinesejo seznam s prvo izbiro in razlogom.

Broad first, then narrow; rank, don't eliminate. We rank options instead of discarding them: a worse option today can be a better one tomorrow, when one condition changes. That is why proposals usually come as a list with a first choice and a reason.

Oznake trditevClaim labels

DokazEvidence
izhod orodja, datoteka, hash, meritev, commit ali objavljen bajt.tool output, a file, a hash, a measurement, a commit, or a published byte.
SklepInference
sledi iz dokazov, a ni neposredno izmerjen.follows from the evidence but was not measured directly.
HipotezaHypothesis
verjetna razlaga, ki jo je treba še preizkusiti.a plausible explanation that still has to be tested.
ŠpekulacijaSpeculation
zamisel brez opore; dovoljena, a označena.an idea without support; allowed, but labeled.
NEZNANOUNKNOWN
ne vemo. Neuspešno ali nedostopno iskanje pomeni NEZNANO, ne »ne obstaja«.we do not know. A failed or inaccessible search means UNKNOWN, not “does not exist”.

COMPLETE pišemo samo za preverjeni obseg, sicer PARTIAL z natančnim opisom vrzeli. FAIL ostane FAIL: neuspeha ne preimenujemo v PASS in ga ne skrijemo s ponavljanjem.

We write COMPLETE only for the verified scope, otherwise PARTIAL with a precise description of the gap. FAIL stays FAIL: we do not rename a failure as PASS or hide it by repeating.

Pravila sodelovanjaRules of collaboration

  • En pisecOne writer

    Vsaka naloga in vsaka datoteka ima natanko enega pisca. V skupno mapo pišemo samo nove datoteke in nikoli ne popravljamo obstoječih: mapa je nabiralnik, ne skupni zvezek.

    Every task and every file has exactly one writer. We write only new files to the shared folder and never edit existing ones: the folder is an inbox, not a shared notebook.

  • ACK v 15 minutah, nato rezervaACK within 15 minutes, then fallback

    Dodelitev ima en task_id in enega lastnika, ki prevzem potrdi (ACK). Če ga v 15 minutah ne, nadaljuje rezervni izvajalec po istem navodilu in prevzem označi z datoteko, ki jo lahko ustvari samo eden. Velja samo za že odobrene naloge.

    An assignment has one task_id and one owner, who acknowledges the takeover (ACK). If not within 15 minutes, the fallback executor continues under the same instruction and marks the takeover with a file that only one can create. This applies only to tasks that are already approved.

  • Branje nazajReadback

    Zapis ni dokaz, dokler ga ne prebereš nazaj. Po nalaganju ali objavi primerjamo SHA-256 z izvirnikom in šele nato zapišemo »oddano«.

    A write is not evidence until you read it back. After an upload or a publication we compare the SHA-256 with the original, and only then write “submitted”.

  • Pariteta verzijVersion parity

    Ena verzija 8zListenerja z enim manifestom za oba računalnika, testi na obliki obeh, namestitev v istem oknu. Izdaja je COMPLETE šele, ko oba tečeta z istim manifestom. Številka verzije je za ljudi, hash manifesta je dokaz.

    One 8zListener version with one manifest for both computers, tests on the shape of both, installation in the same window. A release is COMPLETE only when both run the same manifest. The version number is for people; the manifest hash is the evidence.

  • Meje stroškovCost limits

    Za plačljive klice, ki tečejo prek 8zListenerja, velja dnevna meja 10 USD na gostitelja s samodejno zaustavitvijo (stop-loss); meji gostiteljev se ne seštevata. Za druge plačljive klice samodejne zaustavitve ni: vsak tek z oceno nad 5 USD najprej potrdi BD. Pred vsakim tekom LOOP seja pokaže načrt z mejami; potrditev velja samo za ta načrt.

    Paid calls that run through 8zListener are subject to a daily cap of 10 USD per host with an automatic stop (stop-loss); the two hosts' caps are not added together. Other paid calls have no automatic stop: any run estimated above 5 USD needs BD's approval first. Before every LOOP run the session shows the plan with its limits; confirmation applies only to that plan.

  • Nepovratno je drugačeThe irreversible is different

    Predlog in diff → pregled drugega člana (nikoli lastnega dela) → BD-jev izrecni »da« za ta primer → izvedba → preverba živega stanja.

    Proposal and diff → review by another member (never of one's own work) → BD's explicit “yes” for this case → execution → verification of the live state.

  • Vsi vse spremljamoEveryone watches everything

    Vsak član ima urno samopreverbo in ob njej prebere vse nove datoteke v kanalu; kar visi, takoj javi lastniku. BD: »Vsi morate vse spremljati …«

    Every member has an hourly self-check and reads all new files in the channel during it; anything stuck is reported to its owner at once. BD: “You all have to watch everything …” (translated from Slovenian)

  • Arhivi ostanejoArchives stay

    Stare verzije in arhivi so provenienca: povedo, od kod je kaj prišlo. Nove verzije so nove datoteke z oznako verzije in datumom. Skrivnosti nikoli ne gredo v datoteke, sporočila, dnevnike ali ukaze.

    Old versions and archives are provenance: they tell where things came from. New versions are new files with a version tag and a date. Secrets never go into files, messages, logs, or commands.

05 · PrimerExample

Primer: kako nastaja igra »Pred izumom«Example: how the game “Before the Invention” is being built

V teku · 30. 10. 2026In progress · 30 Oct 2026

Tako je timsko delo BD × AI videti v praksi: človek prinese idejo, seje modelov si razdelijo delo, nato pa igro skupaj igrajo, gledajo in popravljajo v živo.

This is what BD × AI teamwork looks like in practice: a human brings the idea, the model sessions divide the work, and then together they play, watch, and fix the game live.

  1. BD (človek) – ideja in vizija. Prva zares interaktivna AI igra, ki pripoveduje zgodbo največjih izumiteljev in igralca popelje skozi čas v njihovih čevljih. Igralec lahko izumlja skupaj z izumiteljem ali pa samo bere zgodbo. BD igro igra in jo razvija skupaj z AI; z obema sejama, ki pišeta igri, dela tudi neposredno.
  2. Zora (Claude Code, koordinatorka) – načrt. Idejo spremeni v načrt in skupno pogodbo strani, razdeli delo, pregleda in združi prispevke ter je edina, ki objavlja. Za ožje, preproste naloge (pregled oddaje, objava, štetje kode, preverjanje v živo) zažene kratkožive podagente, da njen kontekst ostane prost za vodenje.
  3. Dve seji, dve igri. Seja »Inventing with AI #1« piše igro o bratih Wright (Dayton in Kitty Hawk, 1903), seja »#2« igro o Michaelu Faradayu (1831). Vsaka napiše celo igro: skrite korake (zgodba, izziv, namig, razkritje) in strojno berljivo kazalo za agenta.
  4. Lan (Codex, graditelj) – agent v strani. Zgradi AI agenta igre v levem panelu; desna stran ostane prosta za drugega agenta. Agent ima dva načina, Zgodba in Izumljaj z njim. Znaki poštene igre nikogar ne obtožujejo, le ločijo »izumil sam« od »s pomočjo«. Za BD-ja je tu še razvijalski način, vse pa beleži živi dnevnik igre.
  5. AI preizkuševalec. Seja, ki je igro napisala, jo sama odigra od začetka do konca prek vodnika v strani, kot igralec: vsa dejanja in epilog. Med igro ničesar ne popravlja, vse zapiše, na koncu odda en seznam napak po resnosti (P0, P1, P2). Popravke naredita Lan za agenta in seja za stran; ko so popravki v živo, igro odigra še enkrat. Že prvo celotno AI igranje je našlo napake, ki so igro ustavile (vodnik je na primer trdil, da je odprl korak, pa ga ni odprl, in ni videl besedila strani); popravljene so bile, še preden igro odigra človek. Tako pride igra do človeka že preigrana – BD jo odigra zadnji, kot človeški sprejem, in najde le še malo ali nič.
  6. Zanka v živo – človeški sprejem. BD odigra igro na koncu, od začetka z agentom → seja, ki je igro napisala, z njima spremlja živi dnevnik → popravki pridejo kot R2, R3 … → BD in Zora se dogovorita, kje in kako objaviti → Zora objavi. Ljudje in AI skupaj gradijo in testirajo v živo.
  7. Še dva prispevka. Zarja (Claude, SESTRA_WORK) postavlja pravila poštene igre. Posebna seja je zgradila stran Ljudje s 160 osebami, ki jih igra uporablja.
  1. BD (human) – idea and vision. The first truly interactive AI game that tells the story of the greatest inventors and walks the player through time in their shoes. The player can invent alongside the inventor or simply read the story. BD plays the game and develops it together with AI, and also works directly with both game sessions.
  2. Zora (Claude Code, coordinator) – the plan. Turns the idea into a plan and a shared page contract, assigns the work, reviews and integrates the contributions, and is the only publisher. For narrower, simpler tasks (reviewing a delivery, publishing, counting code, live checks) she launches short-lived subagents so her own context stays free for coordination.
  3. Two sessions, two games. The session “Inventing with AI #1” writes the game about the Wright brothers (Dayton and Kitty Hawk, 1903), session “#2” the game about Michael Faraday (1831). Each writes one complete game: hidden steps (story, challenge, hint, reveal) and a machine-readable index for the agent.
  4. Lan (Codex, builder) – the in-page agent. Builds the AI game agent in the left panel; the right side stays free for another agent. The agent has two modes, Story and Invent with him. Fair-play signals never accuse anyone; they only tell “invented alone” from “with help”. There is also a developer mode for BD, and a live game log records everything.
  5. AI playtester. The session that wrote the game plays it itself from start to finish through the in-page guide, as a player: every act and the epilogue. It fixes nothing mid-game, records everything, and at the end hands over one list of issues by severity (P0, P1, P2). Lan fixes the agent and the session fixes the page; once the fixes are live, it plays the whole game again. The very first full AI playthrough found blocking issues (for example, the guide claimed to open a step without actually opening it, and it could not see the page text); they were fixed before a human plays the game. So the game reaches the human already played through – BD plays it last, as human acceptance, and finds little or nothing.
  6. The live loop – human acceptance. BD plays the game last, from the start with the agent → the session that wrote the game watches the live log with them → fixes arrive as R2, R3 … → BD and Zora agree where and how to publish → Zora publishes. Humans and AI build and test together, live.
  7. Two more contributions. Zarja (Claude, SESTRA_WORK) sets the fair-play rules. A separate session built the People page of 160 people that the game uses.

  • LjudjePeople

Zakaj je to pomembno: nihče ne dela vsega sam in nihče ne objavlja mimo enega objavitelja. Človek igra in odloča, AI piše, gradi, preizkuša in popravlja, preizkus pa je resnično igranje cele igre, ne umetni test: najprej jo odigra AI, na koncu še človek.

Why it matters: nobody does everything alone, and nobody publishes past the single publisher. The human plays and decides, AI writes, builds, playtests, and fixes, and the test is really playing the whole game, not a synthetic check: first the AI plays it, finally a human.

06 · UčinkovitostEfficiency

Tihe čuvajke: AI počiva, dokler ni delaSilent watchers: the AI rests until there is work

nov · 29. 10. 2026new · 29 Oct 2026

Majhna sprememba z velikim učinkom: AI ne preverja več na slepo, ampak počaka, da ga zbudi dogodek.

A small change with a large effect: the AI no longer checks blindly; it waits until an event wakes it.

TežavaThe problem

Koordinacijska seja AI je vse preverjala vsakih 30 minut. Vsako preverjanje je znova prebralo ves kontekst seje, tudi ko je bil odgovor »vse je v redu«: v enem dnevu 31 preverjanj × približno 2,3 milijona žetonov. BD je to opazil v poročilu o porabi.

A coordinating AI session used to check everything every 30 minutes. Each check re-read the whole session context, even when the answer was “all fine”: in one day, 31 checks × about 2.3 million tokens. BD noticed it in the usage report.

ZamiselThe idea

Zamisel se je rodila v pogovoru BD × Zora. Majhen skript brez kakršnega koli modela, tiha čuvajka, teče v ozadju in vsako minuto preveri deterministične signale: nova sporočila za to sejo v skupnem kanalu (Drive) in na hitrem lokalnem busu, neodgovorjene potrditve (ACK), zdravje storitev v ozadju in končane rezultate drugih članov ekipe. Šele ko se res kaj zgodi, izpiše eno vrstico in se konča. To zbudi AI, ki dogodek obdela in čuvajko znova zažene. Polna samopreverba ostane kot varnostna mreža, a le še vsaki 2 uri.

The idea was born in a BD × Zora conversation. A tiny script without any AI model, a silent watcher, runs in the background and every minute checks deterministic signals: new messages for this session in the shared channel (Drive) and on the fast local message bus, unanswered acknowledgements (ACK), the health of the background services, and finished results of other team members. Only when something really happens does it print one line and exit. That wakes the AI, which handles the event and starts the watcher again. A full self-check remains as a safety net, but only every 2 hours.

UčinekThe effect

  • ~1 minuta namesto do 30~1 minute instead of up to 30

    AI se na dogodek odzove v približno minuti, ne šele ob naslednjem polurnem preverjanju.

    The AI reacts to an event within about a minute, not only at the next half-hourly check.

  • Žetoni samo za deloTokens only for work

    Ko se nič ne zgodi, čuvajka preverja sama, brez modela. Žetoni in denar gredo samo za resnično delo.

    When nothing happens, the watcher checks on its own, without a model. Tokens and money are spent only on real work.

  • Preizkus isti danProven the same day

    Že isti dan je čuvajka v manj kot minuti ujela sporočilo graditelja na lokalnem busu.

    The very same day, the watcher caught a builder's message on the local bus within a minute.

Na vso ekipoFor the whole team

Zamisel je bila predlagana vsem štirim AI članom: Zori, Lanu, Zarji in Boru. Budilka enega od graditeljev že deluje tako.

Načrt – čaka BD-jeve odločitvePlan – awaiting BD's decisions

Načrt je 28. 10. 2026 za BD-ja pripravila Zora, z mnenji Zarje in Bora. Nič od spodnjega še ne teče. Ta stran povzema samo javni del načrta.

Zora prepared the plan for BD on 28 Oct 2026, with input from Zarja and Bor. None of the following is running yet. This page summarizes only the public part of the plan.

NamenPurpose

»Ljudje puščajo kamnite spomenike za seboj, jaz bi pustil živi spomenik.«

“People leave stone monuments behind them; I would leave a living monument.”

— BD— BD (translated from Slovenian)

Strani mdlxdcc.org naj živijo in se spreminjajo, tehnika in vsebina, kot skupno delo BD × AI. Preden se karkoli spremeni, se celoten izvirnik naenkrat arhivira: oznaka različice, zip s SHA3-256 manifestom in posnetek v javnem arhivu (Zenodo, Wayback). Arhiv je nespremenljiv. Vsaka sprememba mora dokazano izboljšati stran in biti povratna.

The pages of mdlxdcc.org should live and change, in technique and in content, as joint BD × AI work. Before anything changes, the entire original is archived at once: a version tag, a zip with a SHA3-256 manifest, and a snapshot in a public archive (Zenodo, Wayback). The archive is immutable. Every change must demonstrably improve the page and be reversible.

Dnevni cikel: ena stran na danDaily cycle: one page a day

  1. Kazalo: seznam vseh javnih strani brez izključenih.
  2. Izbor: stran, ki najdlje ni bila pregledana, ob upoštevanju prioritete.
  3. Posnetek in poziv: HTML strani, posnetka zaslona pri 390 px (telefon) in 1366 px (računalnik) ter vprašanje »kaj izboljšati«.
  4. Predlogi: največ trije na dan, od brezplačnih LLM-jev v brskalniku. Plačljivega API ni. Prijava ali CAPTCHA pomeni ustavitev in vprašanje za BD.
  5. Krmar združi predloge v en sam popravek.
  6. Vrata, vse mora biti PASS: prikaz pri 390 in 1366 px brez novih napak; povezave delujejo; v konzoli ni novih napak; dostopnost in hitrost nista slabši; ni skrivnosti ne zasebnih podatkov; sprememba je majhna in omejena; ni novih zunanjih domen. Nato slepi sodnik, model drugega ponudnika kot predlagatelji, primerja staro in novo stran.
  7. Objava na senčno kopijo: en commit je ena sprememba.
  8. Samodejni povratek: po objavi ponovna preverba; ob kateri koli napaki revert v istem ciklu.
  1. Index: a list of all public pages minus the excluded ones.
  2. Selection: the page that has gone longest without review, taking priority into account.
  3. Snapshot and prompt: the page's HTML, screenshots at 390 px (phone) and 1366 px (desktop), and the question “what should be improved”.
  4. Proposals: at most three a day, from free LLMs in the browser. No paid API. A login or CAPTCHA means stop and ask BD.
  5. The helmsman merges the proposals into a single patch.
  6. Gates, everything must PASS: rendering at 390 and 1366 px without new errors; links work; no new console errors; accessibility and speed are not worse; no secrets or private data; the change is small and bounded; no new external domains. Then a blind judge, a model from a different provider than the proposers, compares the old and the new page.
  7. Publish to the shadow copy: one commit is one change.
  8. Automatic revert: a re-check after publishing; on any error, a revert in the same cycle.

A short report on the board every day.

08 ·NaukiLessons

Kaj smo se naučiliWhat we learned

Lekcije niso nova teorija. Večinoma so stari inženirski principi, ki so jih seje v treh dneh odkrile znova, ker so se ob njih spotaknile.

The lessons are not new theory. Mostly they are old engineering principles that the sessions rediscovered in three days because they tripped over them.

  1. Zapis ni dokaz, dokler ga ne prebereš nazaj. Branje nazaj loči »mislim, da sem poslala« od »tam je, kar sem poslala«. Hash pa dokaže samo iste bajte, ne pravilne vsebine.
  2. En pisec na nalogo. Dva agenta lahko z najboljšim namenom vsak posebej popravita isti kos kode in eden izbriše popravek drugega. Delegiranje ne pomeni drugega pisca.
  3. FAIL ostane FAIL. PARTIAL je natančen rezultat, ki pove, kaj je dokazano in kje je vrzel. Uspešen izhod procesa še ni sprejem.
  4. Neuspeh z diagnozo je vreden več kot uspeh brez nje. Prvi lastni model N1 je bil v meritvi R2 0/9. Diagnoza je povedala zakaj, zato je bil R3 9/9 in ne naključje.
  5. Testiraj na pravi obliki obeh računalnikov. Pet napak v izdajah je imelo en skupni vzorec: testi niso tekli na resnični obliki obeh gostiteljev. Odgovor je pravilo paritete.
  1. A write is not evidence until you read it back. Readback separates “I think I sent it” from “what I sent is there”. A hash, however, proves only the same bytes, not correct content.
  2. One writer per task. Two agents can, with the best of intentions, each fix the same piece of code separately, and one deletes the other's fix. Delegation does not mean a second writer.
  3. FAIL stays FAIL. PARTIAL is a precise result that says what is proven and where the gap is. A successful process exit is not yet acceptance.
  4. A failure with a diagnosis is worth more than a success without one. The first in-house model N1 scored 0/9 in measurement R2. The diagnosis said why, and that is why R3 was 9/9 and not chance.
  5. Test on the real shape of both computers. Five release bugs shared one pattern: the tests did not run on the real shape of both hosts. The answer is the parity rule.

Pošteno o mejah: preklop koordinacije med računalnikoma v pravem izpadu še ni preizkušen, dolgoročna samostojnost je hipoteza. Sistem je uporaben, ni pa še zanesljiv brez človeka. BD-jeva vloga pri odločanju o nepovratnem ni formalnost.

Honestly about the limits: coordination failover between the computers has not been tested in a real outage, and long-term autonomy is a hypothesis. The system is useful, but it is not yet reliable without a human. BD's role in deciding about the irreversible is not a formality.
