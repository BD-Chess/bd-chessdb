Real fail-closed suite

Why this matters for Bounded-Ridge

A prepared worker may merely reach a better point on the same abstraction ridge—or may become more likely to challenge and rebuild the representation. D2 adds a second hypothesis: differently prepared workers may cover different ridges and create a valid bridge only after controlled interaction.

No solution is assumed. If Prepared Single, Prepared Team and Team Genesis fail matched held-out representation-shift tests, the Bounded-Ridge objection survives.

Promotion boundary

Current status: validator-hardened and experiment-ready, not evidence-backed.

Promotion into an ArenaLoop candidate architecture should require replicated, cost-adjusted downstream gain and independently adjudicated team-only bridge evidence on more than one held-out task family. Until then, Architecture Spec v0.2.2 remains immutable.
