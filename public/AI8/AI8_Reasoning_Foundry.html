Strong uncued test

A frozen signature extractor, opaque operator IDs, decoys and a lexical firewall separate explicit-cue replay from genuine trigger recognition. Cross-domain adaptation is measured separately from retrieval.

Residency and non-erasure

Active Atlas, Dormant Archive and Quarantine are separate owner-local states. Dormancy lowers active retrieval cost without deleting useful or negative provenance. Contaminated/injection-class artifacts default to Quarantine.
