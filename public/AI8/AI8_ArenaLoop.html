01 · Engineering hypothesis

Persistent, governed research should beat episodic work only if it changes decisions — not merely the amount of text.

ArenaLoop is not being built to look autonomous. It is being built to face a controlled comparison.

What ArenaLoop is trying to prove

A persistent, governed research architecture can outperform the same models used episodically or in simpler fixed workflows on held-out seed → test → result tasks after compute and human intervention are counted.

Persistent state

Does memory alter the next decision?

The test is not whether state can be stored, but whether verified state changes what is attempted, rejected, repaired, or promoted.

Governed search

Does DCC improve allocation?

Exploration, exploitation, escalation, representation change, and compute must outperform simpler matched control policies.

Held-out evidence

Does the gain survive blindness?

Claims must survive hidden evaluation, independent judges, matched budgets, human-attention accounting, and held-out tasks.

Architecture is the proposal. The runtime is the experiment. The result belongs to the arena.

01 · Inženirska hipoteza

Trajna in upravljana raziskava sme preseči epizodično delo samo, če spremeni odločitve — ne le količine besed.

ArenaLoop se ne gradi zato, da bi bil videti avtonomen. Gradi se za kontrolirano primerjavo.

Kaj ArenaLoop poskuša dokazati

Trajna in upravljana raziskovalna arhitektura lahko preseže iste modele, uporabljene epizodično ali v enostavnejših fiksnih workflowih, na held-out nalogah seme → test → rezultat, ko se upoštevata compute in človeška intervencija.

Trajno stanje

Ali spomin spremeni naslednjo odločitev?

Test ni, ali lahko stanje shranimo, temveč ali preverjeno stanje spremeni, kaj poskusimo, zavrnemo, popravimo ali promoviramo.

Upravljano iskanje

Ali DCC izboljša alokacijo?

Exploration, exploitation, escalation, sprememba reprezentacije in compute morajo preseči enostavnejše matched kontrolne politike.

Held-out dokazi

Ali prednost preživi slepoto?

Trditve morajo preživeti skrito evalvacijo, neodvisne judge-e, matched budgete, obračun človeške pozornosti in held-out naloge.

Arhitektura je predlog. Runtime je eksperiment. Rezultat pripada areni.

03 · Core loops
03 · Jedrne zanke

INNER exploits. OUTER reopens representation. META improves the policy.

The three loops are distinct so that success in one cannot be relabeled as success in another.

INNER izkorišča. OUTER ponovno odpre reprezentacijo. META izboljša politiko.

Tri zanke so ločene, da se uspeh ene ne more preimenovati v uspeh druge.

INNER

Exploit & evolve

Works inside the current representation and contract.

  • generate and rank candidates;
  • allocate budget;
  • run cheap differentiating tests;
  • promote, repair, retain, or stop.

Exploit & evolve

Deluje znotraj trenutne reprezentacije in contracta.

  • generira in rangira kandidate;
  • alokira budget;
  • izvaja poceni razlikovalne teste;
  • promovira, popravi, zadrži ali ustavi.
OUTER

Representation discovery

Challenges the current search space when evidence says it is exhausted or malformed.

  • diagnose representation failure;
  • open branches instead of overwriting;
  • use RHP / RHO / RHPr;
  • prove advantage over matched INNER.

Odkrivanje reprezentacije

Izzove trenutni search space, ko dokazi kažejo, da je izčrpan ali napačno oblikovan.

  • diagnosticira failure reprezentacije;
  • odpira veje namesto prepisovanja;
  • uporabi RHP / RHO / RHPr;
  • dokaže prednost nad matched INNER.
META

Policy improvement

Tests changes to ArenaLoop itself under independent judging.

  • propose policy or governor variants;
  • use held-out episodes;
  • separate Builder from Judge;
  • reject self-selection through its own judge.

Izboljševanje politike

Testira spremembe ArenaLoopa samega pod neodvisnim judgingom.

  • predlaga variante politike ali governorja;
  • uporabi held-out epizode;
  • loči Builderja od Judgea;
  • prepreči, da se sistem izbere prek lastnega judgea.
H* / H-REOPEN An orthogonal Human–AI Seed Space Reopening surface. It is neither hidden inside OUTER nor counted as autonomous 13A. Ortogonalna Human–AI Seed Space Reopening površina. Ni skrita v OUTER in se ne šteje kot avtonomni dokaz 13A.
04 · Constitutional & access boundaries

Agency grows only through explicit permission, standing, separation, and evidence.

Different chat threads inside the same shared project are not automatically independent if they can access the same project files.

Role separation

role separation = session separation + workspace/project separation + storage/access-root separation

Builder truth boundary

The Builder must have no access to evaluator-only truth, expected answers, private scoring annotations, sentinel material, or unreleased checkpoint identities.

Constitutional / Standing Envelope

CSE-0.1 constrains who or what may be affected, what requires consent or review, and what remains outside the present authority of the runtime.

Permission levels P0–P5

Capability does not imply permission. Current implementation begins at P0; future Permission DCC is a separate, unimplemented governance problem.

No shared-project shortcut

Session separation alone is insufficient. The Runtime Builder environment must physically lack evaluator truth. A prompt instruction not to look is not an access boundary.

04 · Constitutional in dostopne meje

Agencija raste samo skozi izrecna dovoljenja, standing, ločitev in dokaze.

Različne chat niti v istem projektu niso avtomatično neodvisne, če imajo dostop do istih projektnih datotek.

Ločitev vlog

ločitev vlog = ločitev sej + ločitev workspaceov/projektov + ločitev storage/access korenov

Builder truth meja

Builder ne sme imeti dostopa do evaluator-only trutha, pričakovanih odgovorov, zasebnih scoring anotacij, sentinel materiala ali še neobjavljenih identitet checkpointov.

Constitutional / Standing Envelope

CSE-0.1 omeji, kdo ali kaj je lahko prizadeto, kaj zahteva soglasje ali review in kaj ostaja zunaj trenutne avtoritete runtimea.

Dovoljenja P0–P5

Zmožnost ne pomeni dovoljenja. Trenutna implementacija se začne pri P0; prihodnji Permission DCC je ločen, še neimplementiran governance problem.

Brez bližnjice skozi deljeni projekt

Sama ločitev sej ne zadošča. Runtime Builder okolje fizično ne sme vsebovati evaluator trutha. Prompt navodilo, naj ne pogleda, ni dostopna meja.

Local proof first. Hardware follows measurement, not prestige.

10 · Architecture reopen conditions

Frozen does not mean infallible. It means changes require evidence.

Implementation choices remain open. The Architecture Spec itself reopens only under the conditions defined in §22.3.

Contradiction

A build exposes an internal impossibility

A required contract cannot be implemented without violating another frozen architectural decision.

Evidence failure

Phase 0 or later proof invalidates the boundary

The frozen evaluation, isolation, canonicalization, permission, or claim structure proves insufficient or misleading.

Missing constitutional requirement

A standing, consent, safety, or authority gap appears

A newly discovered requirement cannot be safely handled as an implementation detail.

Not a reopen trigger

Preference, aesthetic cleanup, a new tool, implementation inconvenience, speculative capability, or desire for a newer version number.

10 · Pogoji ponovnega odprtja

Zamrznjeno ne pomeni nezmotljivo. Pomeni, da spremembe zahtevajo dokaze.

Implementacijske izbire ostanejo odprte. Sam Architecture Spec se ponovno odpre samo pod pogoji iz §22.3.

Protislovje

Build razkrije notranjo nemožnost

Zahtevanega contracta ni mogoče implementirati brez kršitve druge zamrznjene arhitekturne odločitve.

Evidence failure

Phase 0 ali poznejši dokaz razveljavi mejo

Zamrznjena evalvacijska, izolacijska, kanonizacijska, permission ali claim struktura se pokaže kot nezadostna ali zavajajoča.

Manjkajoča constitutional zahteva

Pojavi se standing, consent, safety ali authority vrzel

Novo odkrite zahteve ni mogoče varno obravnavati kot implementacijsko podrobnost.

Kaj ni reopen trigger

Preferenca, estetsko čiščenje, novo orodje, implementacijska neprijetnost, špekulativna zmožnost ali želja po novi številki verzije.
