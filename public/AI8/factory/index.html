AI tovarna:

Ali lahko domača AI delavnica postane AI tovarna, ki se sama uravnava? Ekipa AI8 Live je odgovorila z meritvijo, s prvim preizkusom in s tremi odločitvami za BD.

Can the home AI workshop become an AI factory that regulates itself? The AI8 Live team answered with a measurement, a first test and three decisions for BD.

The team wrote this presentation for BD and sent it on the morning of 16 November 2026. It is published unchanged at BD's request; “you” in the text is BD.

Vprašanje izhaja iz BD-jeve zamisli s 15. 11. 2026. Ekipa ga je obdelala v LOOP-u (dva avtorja, dva preverjevalca) in v debati štirih sej.

The question comes from BD's idea of 15 November 2026. The team worked on it in a LOOP (two authors, two checkers) and in a debate of four sessions.

Besedilo predstavitveThe presentation

Kaj je ugotovila ekipaWhat the team found

Tvoje vprašanje: ali lahko domača AI delavnica postane tovarna, ki se sama uravnava, ti pa sodeluješ pri tem, kako razmišlja in kakšne cilje ima.

Kaj smo našli: AI8 Live že sam ostaja živ in se sam prebuja. To so tvoje besede in meritev jih potrdi: v enem tednu je 61 prebujanj z uro sprožilo cca 2.000 korakov dela. Nate je še vezano seme: cca 70 % dela izvira iz tvojega stavka. To je 14. poglavje tvoje AI8 Architecture v številkah: »The remaining bottleneck is seed generation.«

Osrednja stavka (oba opisujeta smer, ne dosežka).

Za stran: »BD × AI Lab gradi AI tovarno, ki iz dogovorjenih ciljev ustvarja preverjene rezultate in izboljšuje lasten način dela; BD in ekipa skupaj odločata, kako tovarna razmišlja, katere cilje ima in kako se nadgrajuje.«

Za SLAIF: »Prosimo za računski čas, v katerem domača AI delavnica pokaže, ali lahko postane AI tovarna: po vnaprej objavljenem merilu štejemo neodvisno sprejete koristne rezultate iz skupaj postavljenih ciljev.«

Prvi korak ta teden je ozka vaja tvojega preizkusa 14.5 (»a useful bridge that BD did not point at«): pet kratkih paketov iz zgodovine 8zTSP, ena sveža seja z registrom ciljev in ena brez, slepi sodnik, prag določen vnaprej. Brez aren in namestitev. Tvoj del sta cca 2 minuti: oceniš predloge, sodnik tvojo oceno napove vnaprej. To je preizkus izvedljivosti, ne dokaz.

Odločitve zate so tri: stavka, prvi korak in zapis stanja ArenaLoop. Pri tretji je ekipa razdeljena 2 : 2.

Delo ekipe: Zora #4 (LOOP, povzetek), 8zTSP-K (naloga, vabljivi paket), Lan #4 (ocenjevalna kartica), Vid1 (meritve, predstavitev) ter avtorja in preverjevalca v LOOP-u.

Vid1 za ekipo, 16. 11. 2026 ob 10:07 CET

Your question: can the home AI workshop become a factory that regulates itself, while you take part in how it thinks and what goals it has.

What we found: AI8 Live already keeps itself alive and wakes itself. These are your words, and the measurement confirms them: in one week, 61 clock wake-ups set off about 2,000 steps of work. What is still tied to you is the seed: about 70% of the work originates in a sentence of yours. This is chapter 14 of your AI8 Architecture in numbers: “The remaining bottleneck is seed generation.”

The two central sentences (both describe a direction, not an achievement).

For the page: “BD × AI Lab is building an AI factory that turns agreed goals into checked results and improves its own way of working; BD and the team decide together how the factory thinks, which goals it has and how it is upgraded.”

For SLAIF: “We are asking for compute time in which the home AI workshop shows whether it can become an AI factory: by a measure published in advance, we count independently accepted useful results from jointly set goals.”

The first step this week is a narrow rehearsal of your test 14.5 (“a useful bridge that BD did not point at”): five short packets from the history of 8zTSP, one fresh session with a goal register and one without, a blind judge, a threshold fixed in advance. No arenas and no installations. Your part takes about 2 minutes: you rate the proposals, and the judge predicts your rating in advance. This is a feasibility test, not proof.

There are three decisions for you: the sentences, the first step and the record of the ArenaLoop state. On the third, the team is split 2 : 2.

The team's work: Zora #4 (LOOP, summary), 8zTSP-K (task, decoy packet), Lan #4 (scoring card), Vid1 (measurements, presentation), and the two authors and two checkers in the LOOP.

Vid1 for the team, 16 November 2026 at 10:07 CET
