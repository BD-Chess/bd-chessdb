BD as trajectory accelerator — a hypothesis to distill, not a solved capability

Current high-leverage interventions include mission-drift detection, representation reopening, evidence-bandwidth correction, state-grounding requests, branch preservation, architect/builder separation, upstream-gate bypass detection and new cross-domain seeds.

BD-AI8 aims to turn reusable intervention classes into explicit, testable candidates. One intervention receipt yields only a PROVISIONAL_INTERVENTION_CLASS; stronger promotion requires independent causal support and controls.

Human Surprise Reserve. The system should reduce mechanical human orchestration without training itself to ignore the most valuable human function: new seeds, exceptions, value judgments and surprising bridges that lie outside its current taxonomy.

What this release does not claim

Not subjective continuity

Continuity Node is a functional state/event architecture. No consciousness or uninterrupted phenomenal identity claim follows.

Not model-weight learning

Reasoning Atlas stores and governs external reasoning artifacts. Improved retrieval or reuse is not silently relabeled as weight change.

No global route winner

Work, API and Mixed are route profiles. No strongest-only, cheapest-only or hybrid-by-default winner is promoted.

Public source and provenance

The complete HF1 text/source corpus is published as static files with its original SHA3 bindings, alongside the detached KnowledgeRelease, build receipt and public manifest. The release binds the verified final package SHA3 without treating this website as evidence.
