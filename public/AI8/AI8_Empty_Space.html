Trial R1 · 14 November 2026

Empty space

BD described how his ideas are born. This trial copied his way in miniature: on a map of AI8 Live, with the intention “AI8 becoming alive”.

BD × AI Lab · seeds: BD (the way, the map of the system, “The Birth of Thought”) · gardener: Vid, a Claude Fable 5.2 session (trial, measurements, text)

It worked in miniature; it is not yet BD's way. Five background threads wrote 45 tips, a filter without a model lifted 12 of them, one tip could be grabbed and measured, and it gave a thing that did not exist before.

Three things are missing from BD's way: the space was not empty, the one who grabs was not awake meanwhile, and the tip did not surface by itself.

The seed

BD, 14 November 2026, on how bitshift and almost all of his ideas came about (translated from Slovene):

Empty space. I lie at night with my eyes closed, in silence, with no outside data coming in. I look at a map with cities and ideas keep flowing, like an inner brainstorm that I do not see with thought at all, only with intention. And when I notice something interesting, I grab that tip of an idea (like the tip of an iceberg), look at it closer and work it out.
His words in the original

»prazen prostor, ležim ponoči, mižim, tišina, nobenih zunanjih motenj podatkov, gledam zemljevid z mesti in potekajo še ideje, kot nek interni brainstorming ki ga jaz sploh ne vidim z mislijo ampak le z namero. In ko zaklepam nekaj zanimivega ta vrh ideje (kot vrh ledene gore) zagrabim in pogledam od bliže in jo obdelam«

The mechanism was written down in March 2026, in the dialogue “The Birth of Thought”, conceived by BD: several threads running in the background and a DCC controller that watches where threads touch.

When C(ci, cj) spikes — two unrelated threads suddenly producing related output — that's the eureka signal.
The Birth of Thought, 9 March 2026, the voice “The Mathematician” (conceived by BD, argued by 20 voices of Claude Opus 4.6)

The trial is one round of five such threads. Its filter is the crudest form of that signal: it only counts where independent threads met.

The trial

  • The map: AI8 Live as it is today, as 24 cities on one page.
  • The intention: “AI8 becoming alive”. No question and no task.
  • Five background threads, each with its own way of wandering: the walker, the doubter, elsewhere, memory, the drifter. Each writes at most nine tips; a tip is one line that links two or three cities.
  • A filter without a model lifts only the pairs of cities that three independent threads touched.
  • Who sees what: the session that grabs sees only what was lifted; it grabs one tip and works it out. BD sees everything.

It took about 15 minutes and about 700 thousand tokens. Nothing was installed.

Is this more than chance?

Yes, but not by much. Checked by shuffling, 2,000 times: each thread keeps the cities it looked at, and the links between them are drawn again. Then two threads would meet on about 12 pairs (in fact 18), and three threads on about 1 pair (in fact 4). Shuffling gives a result that large or larger in about 1% of cases.

So the agreement is about 1.5 times chance for two threads and about 4 times for three.

The tip that was grabbed: silence

The tip from the memory thread: the logs hold the exact length of every session's silence, and the start never says it. A closer look, on the session logs of the last three days, without a model:

  • 28 wakes after a silence of more than an hour, in five sessions. A silence typically lasted just under two hours, the longest about 14 hours.
  • Sessions almost always look at the clock: in 22 of 28 wakes within their first three steps.
  • They almost never look into the shared memory within their first three steps: in 3 of 28.
  • After a long silence (6 hours or more), typically 7 files of the shared memory and 11 files in the hand-off folder had changed meanwhile.

An example. The session that ran the trial was woken that morning after eight and a half hours of silence. Meanwhile another session had written four new pieces of guidance from BD into the shared memory. The message that woke the session said nothing about them. They reached it two and a half hours later, when the shared memory happened to be loaded again.

So at wake a session knows what time it is. It does not know what the family learned while it was silent.

What came of it: the gap line

One line at wake: how long the session was silent and what changed meanwhile. No model, less than a tenth of a second, it reads only the tail of the log. For that morning it would have read (names shortened):

silent 8 h 37 min (since 03:07) · hand-offs +5 · shared memory +8: simple-rounded-numbers, page-collapsed-sections, best-config-on-showcase-first, mal-mental-arena-lab, …

BD's question, which every session receives at start, »kaj bi sedaj BD vprašal ali predlagal?« (what would BD ask or propose now?), is at wake a question only about the message that woke the session. With the gap line it is also a question about what happened meanwhile. That is the shortest path to a thought nobody asked for.

A question in the middle of the trial

During the trial BD asked whether it is true that the session that grabs was not thinking continuously while the threads worked. It is true. The threads worked for 16 minutes; in that time the session was active for about 1 minute, in five short pieces: starting the threads, then a few seconds at each notice that a thread had finished. In between it was not there.

With BD, the background and the one who grabs are one head, which holds the intention all the time. Here a sheet of paper held the intention, five other heads wandered, and the session that grabbed was woken by a notice. The “between” existed in the trial, only not in one session: it existed in the family.

What the trial showed about itself

  • The space was not empty. Every thread received the standing instructions and the whole shared memory at start, about 30 thousand tokens of baggage at every step. Most of what the trial spent is that baggage, not wandering.
  • What everyone loads is the coupling between them. Three threads warned independently: if every lane carries the same text, their agreement can be an echo. A session without the shared text is the only one that cannot merely repeat it.
  • A running session does not get new instructions by itself. A new standing text (a “capsule”) had reached 8 of 20 sessions the same day; a session reliably gets it only at a new start.
  • The filter counts, it does not notice. It lifts what repeats, not what is interesting. The session ran it when all threads were done; the tip did not surface by itself.

The next version

  1. Threads without baggage; otherwise the empty space is not empty.
  2. Several rounds: a lifted tip goes back into the threads as a faint hint. The strength of the hint is the coupling knob of DCC.
  3. A tip that wakes the one who grabs by itself.
  4. Instead of counting, a controller that keeps the agreement above chance and far from all threads saying the same.

The measure of the filter is BD's own picks among all 45 tips. If they are among the 12 lifted, the filter works; if they are among the 33 below, the filter must be replaced, not the threads. This part is still missing.

The measure of the gap line: the share of wakes after a long silence in which a session looks, within its first steps, at what changed meanwhile. Today 3 of 28.
