Claim boundary / Meja trditve
This is a proposed engineering architecture. It is not evidence of autonomous AGI, an autonomous scientist, consciousness, or recursive self-improvement advantage. To je predlagana inženirska arhitektura. Ni dokaz avtonomne AGI, avtonomnega znanstvenika, zavesti ali prednosti rekurzivnega samoizboljševanja.

The idea

Ideja

BD gives a seed: “try materials discovery with MDL×DCC.” The system should preserve the mission, open multiple architectures, build, test, run, retrieve evidence, criticize itself and decide whether to promote, mutate, branch, park or reopen the representation.

BD poda seme: »poskusimo odkrivanje materialov z MDL×DCC«. Sistem naj ohrani namen, odpre več arhitektur, gradi, testira, poganja, pridobi dokaze, kritizira rezultat in odloči: promoviraj, mutiraj, razvejaj, parkiraj ali ponovno odpri reprezentacijo.

The failure that matters

Napaka, ki je pomembna

An arena may compile, pass tests and run for hours while still failing its research mission. Arena Foundry therefore treats mission failure as distinct from implementation failure. If the supposedly central mechanism contributes 0% under a matched ablation, the correct response may be “reopen the architecture,” not “run longer.”

Arena se lahko prevede, prestane teste in teče ure, pa vseeno zgreši raziskovalni namen. Arena Foundry zato loči neuspeh misije od neuspeha implementacije. Če domnevno osrednji mehanizem pri izenačeni ablacijski primerjavi prispeva 0 %, je pravilen odgovor lahko »ponovno odpri arhitekturo«, ne »poganjaj dlje«.

Where the human stays

Kje ostane človek

The goal is not to remove BD. It is to remove mechanical orchestration. Human attention should move upward toward seeds, surprising cross-domain bridges, mission choices, high-risk permissions and judgments that are difficult to reduce to a metric.

Cilj ni odstraniti BD-ja. Cilj je odstraniti mehansko orkestracijo. Človeška pozornost naj se premakne navzgor: k semenom, presenetljivim prenosom med domenami, izbiri misije, tveganim dovoljenjem in presojam, ki jih težko skrčimo na metriko.
