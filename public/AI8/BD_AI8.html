BD Research Governor · candidate direction

Human acceleration should become less mechanical, not less human.

BD is currently the strongest trajectory accelerator in this research system: seeds, unusual cross-domain bridges, reframing, value judgments, exception handling and “this is the wrong question” corrections can redirect whole programs. BD-AI8 aims to distill repeatable classes of such interventions into explicit, testable operators, guards and policies so routine transport/orchestration decreases.

This is not a claim that the process is already internalized in model weights. The v0.x architecture is external, evidence-bound and reversible. BD remains mission, permission, exception and final promotion authority; human surprise and value input remain first-class.

BD Research Governor · kandidatna smer

Človeško pospeševanje naj postane manj mehansko, ne manj človeško.

BD je trenutno najmočnejši pospeševalnik trajektorije v tem raziskovalnem sistemu: semena, nenavadni meddomenski mostovi, reframing, vrednostne presoje, izjeme in popravki »to je napačno vprašanje« lahko preusmerijo celoten program. BD-AI8 želi ponovljive razrede takih posegov pretvoriti v eksplicitne, preverljive operatorje, garde in politike, da se zmanjša rutinski transport in orkestracija.

To ni trditev, da je proces že internaliziran v utežeh modela. Arhitektura v0.x je zunanja, dokazno vezana in reverzibilna. BD ostaja avtoriteta misije, dovoljenj, izjem in končne promocije; človeško presenečenje in vrednostni input ostajata prvovrstna.
