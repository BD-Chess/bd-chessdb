CANDIDATEBounded self-improvement program

Bounded improvement, not unrestricted self-improvement

BD-AI8 values candidate changes under mission, permission, evidence, cost, counter-metrics and rollback. Builders, evaluators and owners remain separable; BD remains final mission, exception and high-impact authority.

Human Acceleration Distillation Loop

Repeated human interventions can become explicit research objects: detect a candidate class, define its trigger and anti-trigger, compare matched outcomes, test held-out surfaces, and only then promote a reusable policy.

  • Mission drift detected / mission reopen
  • Evidence too compressed / state grounding required
  • Preserve old branch / separate architect and builder
  • Upstream-gate bypass detected
  • Seed a new cross-domain bridge

Current boundary: these are valuable observed intervention classes and architecture targets, not a field-proven autonomous replacement for BD.

Human Surprise Reserve

Mechanizing frequent interventions must not erase low-frequency high-value human novelty. Seeds outside the current taxonomy receive bounded PLAY opportunity, provenance remains append-only, and anti-capture cases escalate to ASK_BD.

Learning requires causal use

A lesson note, memory entry or policy text is not learning by itself. The ordinary downstream consumer must behave materially differently on valid controls for the right reason before a learning claim is earned.
