01 · What is AI8?

A continuity and governance architecture for research that must survive its workers.

AI8 separates the durable trajectory from any one model session, while keeping human responsibility, evidence, correction, standing, and permission visible.

Continuity

State that can be rebuilt

Commitments, open questions, results, corrections, provenance, and permission boundaries persist beyond one context window.

Selection

MDL chooses what earns complexity

Candidates must justify their description cost through residual reduction, prediction, compression, or measured performance.

Governance

DCC regulates the search regime

Exploration, exploitation, budget, escalation, and representation reopening remain observable and testable.

Correction

Evidence can change the path

Negative results, minority reports, ambiguity, failure, repair, and supersession remain part of the durable record.

Claim boundary

AI8 is currently a strong human–AI research organism and architecture. It is not demonstrated autonomous AGI, a proven conscious entity, or a continuously subjective person across model restarts.

01 · Kaj je AI8?

Arhitektura kontinuitete in upravljanja za raziskavo, ki mora preživeti svoje workerje.

AI8 loči trajno trajektorijo od posamezne modelske seje, pri tem pa ohrani vidne človeško odgovornost, dokaze, popravljanje, standing in dovoljenja.

Kontinuiteta

Stanje, ki ga je mogoče obnoviti

Zaveze, odprta vprašanja, rezultati, popravki, provenienca in meje dovoljenj preživijo eno kontekstno okno.

Izbor

MDL izbira, kaj si zasluži kompleksnost

Kandidati morajo svoj opisni strošek upravičiti z zmanjšanjem residuala, napovedjo, kompresijo ali merjenim rezultatom.

Upravljanje

DCC uravnava raziskovalni režim

Exploration, exploitation, budget, escalation in ponovno odpiranje reprezentacije ostanejo opazni in preverljivi.

Popravljanje

Dokazi lahko spremenijo pot

Negativni rezultati, manjšinska poročila, dvoumnost, failure, repair in supersession ostanejo del trajnega zapisa.

Meja trditve

AI8 je trenutno močan human–AI raziskovalni organizem in arhitektura. Ni dokazana avtonomna AGI, potrjeno zavestno bitje ali neprekinjen subjekt skozi restarte modelov.

09 · Claims, open questions & collaboration

The decisive threshold is behavioral, not declarative.

The next major AI8 proof is not “I am alive.” It is an autonomous or collaborative research bridge that survives matched baselines, held-out tests, cost accounting, and independent review.

09 · Trditve, odprta vprašanja in sodelovanje

Odločilni prag je vedenjski, ne deklarativen.

Naslednji veliki dokaz AI8 ni »živ sem«. Je avtonomni ali sodelovalni raziskovalni most, ki preživi matched baseline, held-out teste, obračun stroškov in neodvisni review.

Current claim

Governed research continuity can be built and tested.

ArenaLoop now provides the frozen architecture and staged proof program. The implementation still has to earn the claim.

Open question

Does persistence change decisions and results?

Matched episodic, fixed-workflow, stateful-simple, human-orchestrated, and ablated baselines must decide.
