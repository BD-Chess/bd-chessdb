ChessBest · private iPhone candidate

Bundled board, game library, Studies, DCC tools and Stockfish 18 Lite work locally. ChessDB uses HTTPS when online and may receive the position being analysed and your IP address. Its actual provider label is shown; unavailable CDB positions fall back to local SF.

Gemini, Lichess and Anthropic are unavailable in this candidate. No provider keys are bundled. There are no ads, account, analytics or automatic cloud backups.

Your APP data is separate from Safari/PWA. Transfer explicitly: use Game library or Study import to open PGN/JSON from Files; export opens the iOS share sheet. Browser tokens are never imported. Check the saved file before deleting anything.

Studies retain their variations, A/B comparisons and recovery data in Study JSON. PGN carries chess moves and annotations; it is not a full app or simulation backup. Simulation and evidence each have their own JSON export/import.

The app pauses analysis in the background. Resume local games and simulations explicitly; training stops with the position retained. iPhone and native Files/Share acceptance are pending.
